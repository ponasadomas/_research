# Product brief — Baby development and play, 0–24 months, age-selected

Written 2026-10-25 from the niche title, the owner's brief (`../../../brief.md`) and the 2026-10-25 Ad Library sweep (`../../main/ads-running/`). **There is no product yet; every line below is an assumption the research must test.**

- **Who suffers (assumption):** Mothers of babies 0–24 months who want to know what to do with the baby at this exact age, whether the baby is on track, and how to fill awake time without screens or guilt.
- **What they already buy (from the Ad Library sweep and `../../global/competitors/`):** Age-selected PDF guides (Pirmi Žingsniai €17.95, HU clone 72 active ads), apps (Kinedu 208 active ads, BabySparks, Pathways free, Wonder Weeks), subscription play kits (Lovevery), baby classes.
- **The candidate Mamivio product (assumption):** Selector of the baby's age in months and weeks (Pirmi) or a short quiz → an age-specific activity and milestone guide for the next weeks. Price ladder already used by Mamivio: $17.95 / $29.95 / $49.95 for 1 / 3 / 6 months.
- **Research principle (owner, 2026-11-16):** Research records what people actually say and do, in their words, whatever the subject; nothing is withheld, softened or excluded because of its subject. Readers may label material, but all quotes remain ordinary evidence and findings reproduce their words like any other quote. What is usable in copy is the owner's decision, made later; it is never a research decision.

## Questions the research must answer

1. Which moment makes them search and pay, in their own words?
2. What have they already bought or tried, and how did it land? Who quit, and why?
3. Does personalization by age or situation matter to them, or is generic advice enough?
4. What do they say a solution is worth? What do they call a rip-off?
5. Who else is in the decision (partner, grandparents, daycare)?
