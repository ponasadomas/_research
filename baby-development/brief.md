# Brief: baby-development

Written 2026-11-18. It replaces the October brief (`_work/2026-10/brief.md`), which named a
candidate product and so shaped what was selected and read.

## The problem

Mothers of babies 0 to 24 months who want to know what to do with the baby at this age and
whether the baby is on track.

This is a starting description, in our words. The first reading tests it: what she writes
about, how she says it and where the problem's edges are come from her posts.

## What this brief leaves out, on purpose

No product, no price, no brand, no funnel. A reader who knows what we might sell finds
quotes about it.

## The questions

The four in `../README.md`: who she is, what hurts and what she wants and how she says it,
what she buys and how it went, who sells to her. Question 3 is answered from what she says
she tried and paid for, never by asking whether she would buy a particular thing.

## Method

1. First reading: a random sample of every post with text, no keyword. Each post is marked
   on or off the niche and described in a line.
2. A word list built from her words in that reading, tested on that sample for what it
   catches and misses.
3. Deep reading: a random sample of the matched posts across the whole period. Quotes are
   cut and each post is tagged against one shared list of patterns.
4. Counts are of posts, not quotes.
