# statistics

- What: published US figures on mothers of babies and young children (age at first birth, work, leave, child care, time use, media, developmental worry and screening, spending), from NCHS, BLS, Census, USDA, Pew, ASPE/OPRE, America's Health Rankings (National Survey of Children's Health), Mott Poll, ZERO TO THREE (RAPID), New America, and others. Notes: `us-first-time-mothers.md`.
- When: 2026-11-17. Tool: web search and page download by an agent (no scraping scripts kept).
- Question answered: question 1, "who is she, and what is her life like?", as national figures to stand beside the forum quotes. Almost all figures cover all mothers or all parents of young children, not first-time mothers (see "Cautions" in the notes).
- `raw/`: 17 text files, one per source page, holding the exact paragraphs or table rows the figures come from, with the URL and fetch date at the top. Every number in the notes appears word for word in one of them.
- Not found: marital status and college degree of first-time mothers; household income of families with a baby under 1 or 3; typical return-to-work week from a federal source; trust in each information source; use of baby-tracking apps; use of the CDC milestone checklists or app; well-child visit attendance for ages 0 to 2; first-year spending; spending on apps, books, classes or toys.
- Gaps in access: bls.gov, acf.gov (OPRE) and ihpi.umich.edu returned 403; BLS releases were read from the St. Louis Fed archive, and the 2025 BLS release (April 2026) was not read.
