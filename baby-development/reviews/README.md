# Reviews

What buyers say about the apps sold to this niche.

- **Collected:** 2026-10-25, with `_tools/history/collect_app_reviews.py` and
  `extract_play_reviews.py`. App Store reviews (US, UK, Canada, Australia) and Google Play
  reviews for nine apps, among them Kinedu, BabySparks, The Wonder Weeks, Lovevery, Sprouty.
- **Question:** what does she pay for, and was she satisfied?
- **`reviews.md`:** what was collected per app, how it was read, the counts.
- **`not-harvested.md`:** what could not be collected.
- **`raw/`:** the saved pages and review texts, 165 files.
- **Quotes:** 159 from apps, in `../quotes/reviews-rv1.jsonl` (that file also holds the 5
  book-review quotes).
