### They ask whether the screens that make daily life manageable are too much.

6 of 38 posts.

These parents use screens for lunch, the end of a working day, chores, pregnancy, a move or recovery after birth. Children are two or nearly three, with some older siblings; stated use ranges from under an hour to three-hour weekends or television on all day during postpartum recovery. They describe other play, outings or reading alongside screens and ask for comparisons, reassurance or a less harmful way to get a break.

- "But when she’s not here, the tv is on 24/7. I feel terrible. I’m overwhelmed, I’m in pain, I’m still going through the baby blues, I’m only getting 2 hour increments of sleep, and part of me is spiraling wondering if adding another kiddo was a good idea." [ar023-0072]
