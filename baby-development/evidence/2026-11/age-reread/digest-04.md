### The parent keeps repeating instructions, but the child refuses and ordinary routines become power struggles.

30 of 190 posts.

These parents describe repeated refusals across dressing, leaving daycare, cleaning up, play and bedtime; most children are two or three, including several nearly four. Choices, explanations, toy removal, timeouts and spanking are reported as ineffective or short-lived, and some parents ask for exact scripts or a course they can watch. Comparisons with more cooperative siblings coexist with accounts of a recent change in a previously cooperative child.

- "He is never following my rules without some whining, I have to repeat everything 10 times, and each time he makes up a distraction that he needs to do before proceeding." [ar003-0001]
- "It doesn’t even matter if it’s something fun or positive! It’s like a switch is flipped and it’s automatic pushback." [ar005-0002]
