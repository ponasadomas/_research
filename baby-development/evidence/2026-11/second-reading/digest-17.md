All 161 supplied posts were read whole; none remain unread or were excluded. These are shares of one main pattern from the stated random sample of 20,000 posts, not estimates about all parents. Counts are posts, not quotes or people: apparent cross-posts retain their separate supplied post IDs, and repeated quote text within a post adds no count. Forms use “the parent” without inferring sex; unknown stages and speaker identities remain unknown. The near section also includes explicitly identified professional and other speakers.

Tags record explicit statements: tried means an action already taken toward the concern, including attending, seeking or arranging care/classes; a plan alone is no. Others means relevant actions or statements by other people, including caregivers and other children; mere attendance or the focal child’s behaviour alone is no. Bought requires an explicit completed purchase or payment, not an assumed fee for attendance; feeling requires the speaker to name a feeling. Per-post yes/no tags appear in Markdown comments below each Posts line.
