### The parent rejects the claim that comforting or holding a baby teaches manipulation or bad habits.

5 of 63 posts.

Parents of babies from ten days to seven months resist being told that picking up, breastfeeding or responding to crying will teach the baby to demand attention. One grandmother puts a ten-day-old down to make her learn; another repeatedly proposes crying as the answer to feeding and holding difficulties. Parents describe responding differently to fussing and full crying rather than picking up at every sound.

- "My bf says I am teaching her to cry to get what she wants but.. she isn't even old enough to understand that I dont think? I am all for listening to that when she is a little bit older.  But all she knows is me. Right? Idk, I also think she has gas but, once she gets going with the crying nothing helps, except the boob." [sr050-0012]
