# baby-development: what hurts, what does she want, and how does she say it?

**Written:** 2026-11-18 from `~/GitHub/_research/baby-development/evidence/evidence.md`.
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

The evidence file sorts 2,970 quotes and cites 1,086 of them: 2,806 are from Reddit
posts written between April and October 2026, in twelve subreddits, and 164 are from
public reviews of nine apps and a few books. They were collected to learn what hurts,
what parents of babies 0 to 24 months want and what they say about products. The readers
chose which posts to read and which sentences to keep, so a count is a count of quotes,
never of people, and never shows how common something is. The last 1,182 quotes come from
a re-reading of 1,169 posts picked because they had first been left out for their
subject, so those subjects weigh more here than a fair sample would give them.

What the evidence cannot tell us:

- How common anything is: 0 sampled respondents. People post more when things are bad,
  and many who post never buy.
- Whether the people quoted buy a development guide, or what one is worth to them: 0
  Reddit quotes report buying or pricing one. The evidence file counts 0 of 603, 0 of 511
  and 0 of 505 in the first three waves of reading. Nothing shows which of them buy.
- Whether what a quote reports happened, or caused what followed: 0 quotes were checked
  against a medical or other outside record. A diagnosis, a doctor's words, the effect of
  a treatment, an activity or a screen, an injury and abuse are each the speaker's report
  or belief.
- How many people are behind the quotes: the Reddit quotes come from 1,314 posts, and a
  post is not a person. Crossposts, follow-ups and one account written down twice are all
  in the counts.
- What the unread posts say: 4,302 posts on the first search list have had no full read,
  and two further lists of 4,220 and 2,019 posts were never read in full. The lists
  overlap and cannot be added.
- Anything said outside Reddit and the app stores: 0 quotes from other forums, Facebook
  groups, YouTube or TikTok comments, or any seller's customers. Only 5 quotes are from
  book reviews.
- How fathers and working parents differ from her: 119 quotes have a partner as the
  speaker and 9 come from r/workingmoms.
- Whether the app reviews are independent: each store showed about 10 reviews. One
  reviewer was promised an e-book for a review, and one app asked its users for reviews.

Other standing limits:

- How to read the counts: the evidence file gives almost no count for a whole pattern.
  "Quotes cited" is the number of different quotes it cites in the passages a finding is
  drawn from. It is a floor, not a total. Findings share quotes, so the counts cannot be
  added. Where the evidence file gives a tag count, it covers one wave of reading and is
  given in the sentence after the count; the waves are never added.
- Who is counted as her: mothers, and parents who do not state their gender (1,384 of the
  2,970 quotes). Where a quoted line is a father's, a relative's or a reviewer's, the
  finding says so. Ids that start with `dvr1` are reviews.
- Not independent: five quotes are from an app builder's promotional post (dv05-0051 to
  dv05-0055), one is a possible promotion (dv23-0208) and one is a story its author
  marks as fiction (dv25-0179).
- Nothing is left out because of its subject. Rage, self-harm, harm to a baby, disability
  and suspected abuse are given in the speakers' words like everything else.
- The evidence file also tests a planned product against these quotes and lists the
  objections to expect. That is about a brand, not about her; the coverage table says
  where each of those sections went.
- Four small gaps remain in the reading itself: one post read with no quote kept, and
  three mentions of a price or a bet that were never written down as quotes.

## Her day as the baby grows

### Once the feeding, the books, the walk and the tummy time are done, she still has hours to fill

32 quotes cited. Tagged `what_to_do_all_day`: 68 quotes in the first wave of reading, 42 in the second, 54 in the third.

- "Today it got to 1pm and I realised I had 5 hours to fill before I could begin bedtime-adjacent things." [dv02-0322]
- "We have HOURS of the day where I’m clenching my fists bc she’s just begging for something. I run around with her. We play with her toys. We watch a little tv. And that’s 20 min of the day lol." [dv02-0002]

**But:** some babies do keep themselves busy. "In the mornings we get up and he usually spends 30mins playing by himself with various toys." [dv06-0050]

### In the first weeks feeding, changing and settling take the whole day, and she asks what the day is for

5 quotes cited.

- "I constantly see online people posting what they do during wake windows - go for walks, play on mat, but how am I supposed to do that when I feel like changing, feeding and then settling takes up the entire time?" [dv02-0148]
- "I guess the part I’m confused by is what is the day so full with. Does giving them bottles and changing their diapers take all day?" [dv02-0158]

**But:** within a few months the opposite arrives. "Days used to fly by, but gradually as feeds have got quicker and naps have got shorter there suddenly seems to be so much TIME to fill" [dv02-0320]

### From about two to nine months the baby is awake, easily bored and cannot yet do much, and she performs for him through every wake window

25 quotes cited. The second wave of reading put this at two to six months; the third found it running on to nine.

- "It’s really exhausting to put on this fake smile and parentese voice for her all the time." [dv01-0090]
- "What I dread, and feel like a failure for, is his wake time." [dv02-0182]
- "He’s at that awkward stage where he can’t really do much or interact much with anything, just lays there playing a little bit but he mostly just whines unless I’m talking to him and interacting." [dv01-0116]

**But:** one mother decides that boredom is good for the baby. "it’s good for her to learn how to play independently and handle potential boredom." [dv03-0099]

### From about four to twelve months the baby wants to move before he can, and screams about it

16 quotes cited.

- "He must be moving at all times but he cannot crawl/wont crawl. So he face plants himself on the floor and then cries because he’s stuck but won’t move." [dv01-0144]
- "At first we thought it was frustration from learning to roll. Then because he was learning to crawl. Well now he can do both but he’s furious he can’t walk." [dv05-0281]

**But:** for one parent crawling brought some relief. "It got better since he's crawling and can play a little bit by himself." [dv03-0231]

### She is told it gets easier once he sits, crawls or walks, and for some it does not

14 quotes cited.

- "Everyone kept telling me, “It gets easier when he sits,” then “when he crawls,” then “when he pulls to stand.” He does all of those things now and cruises along furniture, so apparently we’re at the “once he walks” stage.." [dv02-0008]
- "So what used to be a fun time is now me just constantly chasing her around trying to prevent her from eating things, or having to hold her while she wriggles and cries to get down." [dv05-0296]

**But:** for some it did get better. "It's not linear, but things are consistently trending toward better for us at 10.5 months." [dv05-0067]

### By 18 to 24 months she asks for things that need no setting up, because anything elaborate is over in seconds

4 quotes cited.

- "I need some simple, low prep activities I can have on hand for him so that when I need to lay on the sofa for 10 minutes I can do so without feeling guilty cause he wants me to play with him." [dv02-0162]
- "he has a 2 second attention span and it takes me more time to set these things up, than him be interested." [dv02-0200]

**But:** one mother wants more preparation, not less. "I yearn to be an activity mom or weekly theme (educational topic) mom." [dv24-0047]

### Her day can also be too full: the care she is told to give does not fit into the baby's waking hours

5 quotes cited.

- "I’ve been working on the stretches but it’s hard to do that consistently along with feeding, tummy time, reading, talking, changing, etc in such a small wake window." [dv22-0175]
- "HOW DO YOU DO IT ALL? I am finding it hard to figure out what to sacrifice and just want to be an amazing mom (I am a perfectionist st heart)." [dv22-0177]

**But:** others describe the reverse, a day with too little in it. "We hit the same stores, same play museums, same parks but that’s 1-2 hours of the day." [dv02-0004]

### A skill the child already had can disappear, which is a different fear from waiting for a first one

7 quotes cited.

- "And then, one day, a little after a year old, his speech all but stopped. Weeks would go by and he wouldn't even say mama. He slowly stopped using some of his gestures." [dv22-0041]
- "Previously she had signed more, milk, and was slowly learning some other signs along with a handful of other vocab but in the past 2 months it seems like she’s regressed to only saying those 3 words. She doesn’t sign anymore at all." [dv25-0192]

**But:** other follow-ups report the skill arriving after all. "Hi! He started babbling at 9 months 3 weeks. He is progressing great and trying to say cat!" [dv21-0179]

### The date she dreaded passes and the skill arrives, sometimes with nothing changed

17 quotes cited. Two pairs of ids are one account written down twice (dv21-0234 with dv06-0068, dv25-0120 with dv25-0123).

- "I don’t know what happened, we didn’t change anything and we’d regularly done what everyone advises you know about peek-a-boo etc. it just seems to be clicking." [dv21-0234]
- "Well, a few days after her evaluation she has started using the M, B, and sometimes G sounds. Even a “mamama”. Previously she had no consonant sounds or repetitive “a a a” sounds. There are definitely things I am still concerned about but this feels like such a big step and I’m so excited." [dv21-0157]

**But:** progress can also stall after a doctor has called the child age appropriate. "Fast forward to today— he is 18 months old and significantly delayed." [dv22-0098]

### She is bored herself: every day is the same

14 quotes cited.

- "As much as I love this time with him, I’m starting to feel like I’m losing my mind because every day feels exactly the same." [dv03-0171]
- "its my first summer as a mom and im not used to the slowness and repetitiveness of motherhood. i used to be so spontaneous." [dv05-0225]

**But:** one parent says keeping the baby busy is no trouble. "I have no problem entertaining baby" [dv01-0004]

### Heat, cold, rain, no car and nap times keep her indoors, so being told to get out of the house does not help

14 quotes cited.

- "Everyone says get out and about… but seriously, and do what? I go for a daily walk to get a coffee but that only takes an hour or so." [dv02-0321]
- "I keep getting advice like go for a walk or to a cafe. How?? He'll fuss the entire time we do that. Its not a break for any of us." [dv06-0166]

**But:** one mother who made herself go out says it changed her days. "I put my big girl pants on, bought the things I needed to leave the house and let me tell you, the days went so quickly and I actually enjoyed myself!" [dv21-0241]

## What other people say to her

- "We had our 8-12 month developmental review today with a nurse and both my partner left the appointment feeling like completely inadequate parents." [dv01-0053]
- "I mentioned that today and was told we need to get her speech evaluated. I was completely shocked. I didn’t even ask a question because that was the last thing I expected." [dv06-0203]
- "I brought it up to his doctor who said she isn’t concerned and he’ll crawl when he’s ready but of course I’m starting to get concerned as he’s getting closer to a year." [dv06-0033]

**But:** reassurance does land for some. "it's a relief to be assured that I shouldn't be so worried about things." [dv04-0009]

### She is told to do more of something she already does every day

8 quotes cited.

**But:** one therapist's handout gave a family things they had not tried, and the child loved them. "Another idea was wrapping them in a blanket and swinging and she LOVED that. Kept saying more." [dv22-0085]

### Some professionals blame her to her face, and she cannot always afford to go elsewhere

8 quotes cited. Two of the eight quotes are a stay-at-home father's.

- "I don’t know what to do as this place is the only place my insurance accepts and I don’t want my son to be behind." [dv22-0024]

**But:** the second mother's own pediatrician disagreed with the therapist each time. "Every single time, our pediatrician has reassured me that she isn’t concerned and he looks great." [dv22-0021]

### Relatives check the baby against other babies and against milestones, and she hears it as blame

16 quotes cited.

- "my grandma says im hurting his development because im always carrying him and i never let him cry it out :/" [dv01-0149]

**But:** one mother grants that they mean well. "My in laws live with us (cultural reasons) and they mean well" [dv03-0190]

### She compares her baby with cousins, twins, friends' babies and babies online, and telling herself not to does not stop it

44 quotes cited.

- "His cousin just turned one and she can say all kinds of things and walk and climb. Ik we aren’t supposed to compare but I’m like why doesn’t he say ANY words at all." [dv02-0098]

### A stranger's passing remark can carry her home or send her out in tears

3 quotes cited.

### Wanting help does not get her help: she is refused, told to wait for months, loses her cover or cannot pay

### Some are afraid of what accepting help will bring: child protection at the door, or a label on the child

7 quotes cited. The third quote is about a programme with autism in its name, offered to a child who has no diagnosis.

- "I’m embarrassed, and have a fear in the back of my head the ECI people are actually CPS in disguise and want to take her away." [dv23-0026]
- "Do we have to worry about a cps call if our house isn’t spotless?" [dv24-0193]
- "I told him I’m going to enroll him in the more extensive program and the name intimidated him. He’s skeptical as well and was really questioning if we need our son in the program." [dv21-0140]

**But:** the first and the third go ahead anyway. "I’m not going to let that fear stop me from keeping the appointment." [dv23-0026] "I said yes, why start with less when I have the option to start with more?" [dv21-0140]

### What her child does in an assessment can differ from what she sees at home

5 quotes cited.

- "It seemed like he failed to do so many things that I know he can do in other contexts and he just was not interested or capable of complying with their requests/intent of the activity." [dv24-0106]

**But:** she does not reject the test itself. "I know it's a standardized, validated evaluation but I'm still just so shocked he did so poorly." [dv24-0106]

## What she tries and how it lands

27 quotes cited.

15 quotes cited.
