# Baby development: what hurts, what does she want, and how does she say it?

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from `~/GitHub/_research/baby-development/evidence/2026-11/second-reading/counts.md` and the thirteen sections in `~/GitHub/_research/baby-development/findings/2026-11/patterns/`.
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

The posts are a random draw of 20,000 (seed 20261119, no keyword) from all 122,021 posts with text in twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April to 24 October 2026. Agents read every one and marked it yes (on the niche: a child of 0 to 24 months, and development, abilities, behaviour, play or the day), near or no: 2,471 yes, 5,534 near, 11,995 no. Each yes and near post got one main pattern out of 13; digest readers sorted each pattern's posts into forms, section writers merged the forms, and this page joins forms that say the same kind of thing. A pattern's count is out of the 2,471 on-niche posts, a form's is out of its pattern's posts, and a count under "Next to the niche" is out of the 5,534 near posts. "Parents" means whoever wrote the post.

- How common anything is among mothers or buyers: Reddit is not the market. People post more when things are bad, and many never buy.
- How many people: a count is a number of posts, each tagged by one reader. First-round coders agreed on the main pattern for 21 of 30 shared posts, and readers' yes rates ran from 5.3% to 20.8%. Small differences mean nothing: patterns a few points apart are equal, here 245 and 226 (what to get, the day), 129 and 127 (prefers someone else, delight), 107 and 98 (known delay, daycare).
- How firm the forms are: they were named by different readers and joined by judgement twice, so a form's count is softer than a pattern's.
- Whether the writer is a mother: 2,014 of the 2,471 (81.5%) do not say.
- What the poster wrote later: 14,640 of the 20,000 posts were read without the poster's own replies.
- What others answered: every reply by someone other than the poster is unread.
- The rest of the pool: 97,521 posts (10 April to 24 October 2026) are unread, and 33,030 posts whose text was gone were never in the pool.
- What things cost: 82 of 2,471 posts mention paying, mostly without a price. The sections' "bought or paid" counts were tagged by other readers, sum to 102 and do not reconcile with the 82; each number is given with its source.
- How the four "across" counts of a group relate: they overlap, and "how they feel" counts any named feeling, pride and no worry included.
- What a quote proves: diagnoses, causes and a doctor's words are the poster's account. Where a section or a reader's note marks a quoted passage as AI-assisted, promotional or second-hand, it is marked here too.
- Her vocabulary: no jargon glossary has been made for this round.
