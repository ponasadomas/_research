# baby-development: pattern 11, Potty readiness and learning

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/second-reading/digest-19.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

The random, keyword-free sample covered 20,000 Reddit posts across twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026; 2,471 were on-niche. This pattern contains 38 on-niche and 50 near posts, all read; each count is posts tagged by one reader, not people or market prevalence. Forms from different digest readers would be merged by judgement; this single digest’s forms remain separate, with overlapping cross-post tags.

## On-niche posts

### They want gentle potty practice before full training.

7 of 38 posts.

They consider brief sits and bathroom routines without demanding quick results.

- "if it takes 2 years, also great." [sr058-0067]
- "just being very relaxed but proactive when it comes to potty training." [sr048-0083]

**But:** Comfort is uncertain: "he’s really been struggling with it." [sr048-0082].

### They cannot tell whether early signs mean readiness.

5 of 38 posts.

At 8–20 months, dryness, diaper aversion, unclear cues and early-training claims prompt questions.

- "isn’t 13m a bit early to potty train" [sr046-0024]
- "I have no idea when my 20 month old is peeing" [sr012-0150]

**But:** These signs do not establish readiness here; nothing found against their uncertainty.

### They weigh training against a new baby and other transitions.

4 of 38 posts.

Divided attention, bed changes and weaning complicate timing.

- "So should I wait until he’s closer to like 22 months" [sr019-0113]
- "how I pay attention to him 100% while also taking care of my 2 month old." [sr122-0029]

**But:** Waiting has costs: "he is consistently peeing through diapers" [sr122-0028].

### They find childcare conflicts with their potty routine.

3 of 38 posts.

Classrooms differ in diaper rules, toilet access, equipment and adult help.

- "her daycare classroom does not take kids to the potty." [sr025-0160]
- "they have insisted on no diapers or pull-ups, training undies only." [sr107-0032]

**But:** Support also occurs: "Daycare was supportive" [sr045-0070].

### They struggle to turn cues into timely toileting.

3 of 38 posts.

Late announcements and unproductive sits persist despite reminders.

- "He is giving me the cue right after he pees." [sr048-0030]
- "how can we encourage him to actually communicate that he needs to go potty?" [sr130-0035]

**But:** Prompting sometimes works: "we didnt have a single accident all morning" [sr132-0075].

### They question continuing when training brings accidents or resistance.

3 of 38 posts.

Early success gives way to distress, refusal or withholding worries.

- "Do I persevere? Do I take a break?" [sr007-0080]
- "Should we stop?" [sr098-0141]

**But:** One concern is narrower: "It’s not going bad at all but she hasn’t pooped yet" [sr127-0078].

### They question other people’s instructions about starting age.

2 of 38 posts.

Relatives discourage early practice; a reported health-visitor instruction urges it. Guidance is unverified.

- "my family says that I’m forcing him to grow up too fast." [sr021-0011]
- "we should start potty training our 11 month old" [sr103-0210]

**But:** Starting need not impose a deadline: "We aren’t on any strict timeline" [sr021-0017].

### They struggle to fit training around outings and interruptions.

2 of 38 posts.

Vacations, driving and daycare diapers interrupt planned methods.

- "A bathroom will not be quickly accessible to us at all times." [sr009-0034]
- "wait for a few weeks until I have a full weekend" [sr016-0070]

**But:** Continuing is considered: "then I was going to switch to the undies." [sr009-0036].

### They hear potty requests but meet resistance to sitting.

2 of 38 posts.

Announcements bring screaming or delay; one speaker is a caregiver.

- "constantly tell me they have to use the potty then scream" [sr019-0010]
- "he has to poop but then won’t do it for an hour." [sr121-0068]

**But:** Some sitting is voluntary: "occasionally he’ll sit on it while clothed." [sr121-0069].

### They ask whether training underwear is worth buying.

2 of 38 posts.

They ask about UpAiry, alternatives and overnight protection; neither reports buying it.

- "I wondered if it was worth the investment." [sr033-0002]
- "I had a terrible time potty training my last daughter." [sr115-0135]

**But:** Earlier training was not always difficult: "pretty much out of diapers by 24m." [sr033-0001].

### They want potty requests to stop consuming the day.

1 of 38 posts.

Frequent trips disrupt church and bedtime despite almost-dry days.

- "he will tell me he needs the potty every couple minutes." [sr047-0021]
- "Should I just keep taking him every time he asks?" [sr047-0023]

**But:** Requests include urination: "It's only a little tinkle" [sr047-0021].

### They find underwear disrupts otherwise successful toileting.

1 of 38 posts.

The almost-two-year-old toilets independently naked but wets underwear without warning.

- "just going to the bathroom herself." [sr065-0059]
- "doesn’t say anything 80% of the time when she pees her pants" [sr065-0059]

**But:** Poop signalling remains: "she ALWAYS tells us when she has to poop no problem" [sr065-0059].

### They want bath-time pooping to stop becoming routine.

1 of 38 posts.

After two incidents, the parent wants less sanitizing and repetition.

- "has only happened twice but I’m worried a trend is taking off" [sr084-0045]
- "Sanitizing the bathtub and toys is getting old fast" [sr084-0045]

**But:** nothing found against it.

### They feel unsettled as potty learning changes the routine.

1 of 38 posts.

Underwear and scheduled sits make growing up feel sudden.

- "But, its got me feeling odd, I guess." [sr121-0051]
- "And the fact she's growing up so fast." [sr121-0051]

**But:** The start feels successful: "Overall, I'd say it went good for the first time." [sr121-0050].

### They need separate help with pooping despite successful peeing.

1 of 38 posts.

Soft stools bring freezing, crying and floor accidents; the parent considers stickers.

- "nailing the pee in the potty at home thing" [sr131-0101]
- "she freezes like a deer in the headlights and starts crying" [sr131-0102]

**But:** Distress extends beyond the toilet: "She now also cries if she happens to poop in a pull up or underwear too" [sr131-0105].

## Near posts

### They ask what readiness means and when others trained.

5 of 50 posts.

They compare ages, walking, earlier attempts and school deadlines; most ages are unknown.

- "a 10-11 month old that isn’t walking yet" [sr036-0045]
- "how old were they when they were actually potty trained?" [sr067-0070]

**But:** One early start had a deadline: "she had to be fully trained by 2" [sr064-0072].

### They want a workable way to start or motivate training.

5 of 50 posts.

They ask about rewards and avoiding nakedness; one account includes self-reported resource-making.

- "I honestly don't even know where to start with potty training." [sr012-0033]
- "I tried rewards but didn't work." [sr020-0046]

**But:** Preparation is underway: "I have gotten one of those tiny toilets" [sr012-0033].

### They seek a way back after potty progress reverses.

4 of 50 posts.

Contexts include a sibling, moving or returning nappies; one regression later resolves.

- "Suddenly he was refusing to go potty" [sr060-0073]
- "We did the chart for both." [sr004-0042]

**But:** Change is not always reported: "ZERO routine or life changes?" [sr109-0044].

### They celebrate progress after finding something that helped.

4 of 50 posts.

They celebrate poops, approaching the potty, clothing help or easier restarting; causes remain uncertain.

- "Today on day 5 he officially pushed out a huge turd!" [sr031-0075]
- "same parents, same house, same approach… completely different kids." [sr036-0053]

**But:** Approaching need not mean trained: "Even getting near it has been Run away ! (Screaming)." [sr068-0053].

### They need a plan for toileting away from home.

4 of 50 posts.

They ask about toilets, accidents and fathers helping daughters; one scenario is hypothetical.

- "what do we do if he pees in public?" [sr050-0085]
- "Should the father take his daughter to the men’s or women’s restroom" [sr051-0029]

**But:** Precautionary pull-ups may stay dry: "he doesn’t pee in the pull-up" [sr050-0084].

### They need clothing and toilet equipment that work practically.

4 of 50 posts.

Poop cleanup, drawstrings, larger diapers and urine shields pose distinct problems.

- "would it be easier to get pants with snaps for daycare?" [sr013-0033]
- "there's not a pee shield high enough" [sr111-0042]

**But:** Equipment trouble coexists with success: "he's pooped in successfully a few times." [sr111-0041].

### They question continuing amid accidents and resistance.

3 of 50 posts.

They try reminders, rewards and distractions while worrying about fear and frustration.

- "so much pee on the floor and couch" [sr040-0036]
- "I don’t want to become an angry parent" [sr125-0094]

**But:** Sitting is not always resisted: "We sit on the potty every single time." [sr125-0093].

### They need separate help with poop after pee improves.

3 of 50 posts.

They describe diapers, fear and discomfort; one reports a first potty poop.

- "he hasn't pooped on the potty even once" [sr062-0007]
- "no daddy, I scared" [sr055-0045]

**But:** Relief may begin in a diaper: "Our plan is to let him diaper-poo for a bit" [sr112-0077].

### They weigh training before birth against managing a newborn.

2 of 50 posts.

They consider regression, grandparents’ care and limited help after birth.

- "Should I try to start potty training now" [sr005-0121]
- "will potty training with a newborn be too much to juggle" [sr071-0135]

**But:** Child interest matters too: "our son is very interested in the potty." [sr071-0133].

### They want limits on potty trips without denying bodily needs.

2 of 50 posts.

Requests delay transitions or bedtime, according to the parents.

- "He’s also learned to strategically ask to potty" [sr039-0032]
- "Anyway, it makes bedtime maddening" [sr102-0051]

**But:** Bedtime requests produce urine: "She always does actually pee" [sr102-0050].

### They still rely on reminders or get unproductive sits.

2 of 50 posts.

Ages are unknown; one parent worries about preschool after moving.

- "But just never pees or poops." [sr020-0053]
- "the only reason she’s potty trained or eats is because mommy and daddy are on top of her" [sr088-0088]

**But:** nothing found against it.

### They vent about training feeling hard and messy.

2 of 50 posts.

Ages and methods are unknown; they use war language and sarcasm.

- "Potty Training Day 2; War is Hell" [sr055-0110]
- "Didn’t know I’d be getting more pee on me in the toddler age than the new born stage" [sr113-0065]

**But:** nothing found against it.

### They want less cleanup after toileting.

2 of 50 posts.

One faces lingering urine smell; another suspects incomplete emptying behind repeated poops.

- "The smell is very unpleasant." [sr112-0053]
- "It's a pain because I'm cleaning out the potty chair each time" [sr124-0106]

**But:** Aiming has improved: "the boy is better about aiming" [sr112-0053].

### They want to teach toileting with limited speech.

1 of 50 posts.

The newly two-year-old uses about twelve words; the parent also has a newborn.

- "while not completely verbal just yet" [sr043-0039]
- "Immediately after he pees he’ll begin pulling on the crotch of his diaper." [sr043-0042]

**But:** Understanding is stronger: "Despite not speaking much, he’s very smart." [sr043-0041].

### They find underwear undoes pantsless success.

1 of 50 posts.

Independent toileting gives way to wetting and resisting removal of wet underwear.

- "once the underwear is on she goes back to treating it like the diaper." [sr066-0076]
- "she spreads the legs, fights pulling down the wet underwear" [sr066-0077]

**But:** The child eventually approaches: "eventually making it to the potty chair" [sr066-0076].

### They need training to allow errands and outdoor play.

1 of 50 posts.

Appointments require diapers; constant prompting also fails to suit the child.

- "I can't leave the house for more than 20 minutes and get lucky." [sr113-0184]
- "He also can't stand the every 20 minute method." [sr113-0185]

**But:** They still want training: "I don't want to give up potty training" [sr113-0185].

### They worry that dry nights never seem closer.

1 of 50 posts.

Age is unknown; nightly wet pull-ups and leaks prompt pediatrician questions.

- "a wet pull-up every. single. night." [sr046-0038]
- "Something worth mentioning to the pediatrician?" [sr046-0039]

**But:** The older sibling became dry: "eventually grew out of it about a year ago." [sr046-0037].

### They question starting training during illness and antibiotics.

1 of 50 posts.

Possible bowel irregularity makes a planned holiday-weekend start feel uncertain.

- "would being on an antibiotic affect ability to potty train?" [sr033-0121]
- "I’m wondering if it’s a bad idea to potty train while maybe getting stopped up" [sr033-0122]

**But:** nothing found against it.

### They want to know when to stop potty rewards.

1 of 50 posts.

After a month without accidents, stickers redeemable for toys still matter.

- "he is still realllllllly focused on getting a sticker every time." [sr080-0106]
- "did the toddler eventually lose interest in the rewards?" [sr080-0106]

**But:** This feels manageable: "It’s not the end of the world" [sr080-0106].

### They also recount infant diarrhea or dispute a removed meme.

2 of 50 posts.

One bowel-history account includes suspected dairy intolerance; the other concerns moderation. Ages are 14 months and unknown.

- "she would projectile diarrhea across a room." [sr002-0218]
- "when I asked why they said it was “low effort”" [sr076-0032]

**But:** nothing found against it.

## Across the on-niche posts

### They have tried practice, methods or preparation for potty learning.

32 of 38 posts.

This includes completed actions, information-seeking and previous-child experience; plans alone do not count.

- "I started with 3 days of the naked method at home" [sr045-0070]
- "I usually put him on it once/twice a day" [sr048-0084]

**But:** Accidents persist: "yet hes still managed to have multiple accidents." [sr132-0076].

### They receive conflicting advice and help from other people.

17 of 38 posts.

Relatives, partners, health visitors and childcare influence timing and routines.

- "putting a lot of pressure on us to potty train him." [sr009-0032]
- "they freaked out about him being to young" [sr021-0013]

**But:** Others also help: "they've started having her just sit on a potty a couple times a day" [sr019-0128].

### They report buying potty equipment and protective underwear.

3 of 38 posts.

Three posts report seats, miniature toilets or leak-shield boxers. Prices are unknown; gifts and plans do not count.

- "I recently bought the Baby Bjorn potty seat" [sr048-0082]
- "I was able to get a little mini toddler toilet, the one that looks just like a small version of a normal toilet, on sale so I grabbed it. […]" [sr057-0026]
- "I did buy some boxers that have a shield so pee won’t leak through" [sr085-0028]

**But:** Buying need not ensure comfort: "he’s really been struggling with it." [sr048-0082].

### They feel unprepared, pressured or overwhelmed by potty learning.

17 of 38 posts.

Feelings include nervousness and sadness about growing up; the tag also includes hope.

- "Potty training is something I feel the least prepared for!" [sr058-0030]
- "why did I agree to this? Why didn’t I just say no?" [sr009-0035]

**But:** Hope appears too: "we had a successful pee on the potty so we had high hopes." [sr098-0138].
