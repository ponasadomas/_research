## Limits

The random, keyword-free sample covers 20,000 posts from twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026; this pattern contains 93 on-niche and 638 near posts, all read. Counts are posts tagged by one reader, not people or market prevalence; forms were merged whole by judgement across digest readers. Cross tags overlap; definitions differ between readers and unreported actions remain unknown.
