# baby-development: pattern 10, Other people interfere

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/second-reading/digest-18.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

A random, keyword-free sample of 20,000 posts from twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026, yielded 2,471 on-niche posts; this pattern has 63 on-niche and 64 near posts, all read. Each count is posts tagged by one reader, not people or market prevalence; forms from different readers would be merged by judgement, but this pattern has one digest. Cross-tags overlap; unstated demographics are unknown.

## On-niche posts

### Parents want relatives to respect tired cues.

6 of 63 posts.

Stimulation disrupts comfort and sleep.

- "she will go get baby from cot and start singing loudly and clapping" [sr040-0021]
- "making my daughter uncomfortable or self-conscious." [sr093-0018]

**But:** One grandmother stopped: "she accepted it and stopped." [sr093-0021]

### Parents feel blamed for crying or avoiding interaction.

6 of 63 posts.

Relatives exaggerate crying, blame depression or resent refusal.

- "it's affecting her via my breast milk." [sr094-0099]
- "you broke grandmas heart today and she wants to jump off a cliff" [sr078-0026]

**But:** One also reports: "a lot of very big tantrums" [sr048-0088]

### Parents reject claims that comforting teaches manipulation.

5 of 63 posts.

They distinguish fussing from crying.

- "all she knows is me. Right?" [sr050-0012]
- "He says he doesnt want to condition baby into being picked up when he starts crying." [sr079-0107]

**But:** One allows this later: "when she is a little bit older." [sr050-0012]

### Parents resent developmental labels and comparisons.

5 of 63 posts.

Speculation causes doubt.

- "today she made a comment that my son seems "behind" hers." [sr019-0110]
- "My mom keeps labeling my 10 month old calling her anxiety ridden and stressed out" [sr024-0248]

**But:** One welcomes autism: "I do not care if my son has autism" [sr076-0117]

### Parents want caregivers to respect screen limits.

5 of 63 posts.

Others push television for speech.

- "they do it anyway because they think it’s funny." [sr093-0095]
- "that’s why he’s not talking yet." [sr113-0103]

**But:** One permits: "some screen time with Ms Rachel, Sesame Street, and blues clues" [sr113-0102]

### Parents want harsh treatment stopped.

4 of 63 posts.

Reports include flipping while crying, isolation, spanking and punishment for not eating.

- "put his fingers over the baby’s mouth and told him to be quiet" [sr037-0002]
- "I do not like him spanking them/slapping their hands" [sr102-0101]

**But:** The flipping parent allows: "it’s possible I swooped in too soon." [sr018-0067]

### Parents want the child’s refusal of contact respected.

3 of 63 posts.

Adults insist on kisses, walking or water play.

- "force kisses on her even if she is crying." [sr125-0026]
- "He was happy and has no obligation to perform fun for anyone." [sr068-0116]

**But:** Other relatives are: "fantastic, respectful, and babysit often with no issues." [sr068-0118]

### Parents reject gender restrictions on play and temperament.

3 of 63 posts.

Boys face toy restrictions; girls must be calm.

- "he can’t be playing with crayons and colouring because that’s for girls." [sr109-0116]
- "calm quiet and behaved." [sr060-0028]

**But:** One objection is selective: "this is the first time he’s brought it up." [sr005-0168]

### Parents want feeding and safety instructions followed.

3 of 63 posts.

Grandmothers override cup-weaning, exploratory solids and choking warnings.

- "my MIL proudly told me she only used bottles while we were away." [sr071-0055]
- "She said I’m wrong" [sr125-0089]

**But:** Help coexists: "She’s been cooking for us" [sr077-0035]

### Parents want agreement about toddler behaviour.

3 of 63 posts.

Some defend current practice; another seeks joint change.

- "I said to ignore him" [sr024-0093]
- "help us blend our parenting styles and be a united front" [sr084-0126]

**But:** Discussion is possible: "If someone wants to know, they can ask." [sr024-0099]

### Parents question others’ judgement about safe play.

3 of 63 posts.

Sitting, barefoot walking and bubbles prompt doubt or amusement.

- "He thinks I was totally irresponsible." [sr020-0174]
- "this cracked me up." [sr070-0112]

**But:** A stranger’s meaning is uncertain: "My fiance says that it may be a creep" [sr111-0033]

### Parents want affection and progress enjoyed without dismissal.

3 of 63 posts.

Smiles, kisses and achievements draw diminishing reactions.

- "there’s zero reason for you to rain on my parade about it." [sr006-0008]
- "Being proud of my kid doesn’t mean yours is behind" [sr098-0046]

**But:** One initially: "shrugged it off" [sr063-0036]

### Parents feel worn down defending everyday care.

3 of 63 posts.

Newborn care attracts repeated, sometimes contradictory instructions.

- "I carry him wrong. Change him wrong. Feed him wrong." [sr089-0046]
- "me and the baby against the world" [sr126-0135]

**But:** Explanations can work: "all of the people around me agree with me at the end and respect everything" [sr126-0135]

### Parents want size comments stopped.

2 of 63 posts.

They fear body-image harm.

- "before he begins to understand what they’re saying and absorbs this identity." [sr005-0131]
- "He already did enough damage on me." [sr088-0086]

**But:** nothing found against it.

### Parents leave professional feedback doubting their care.

2 of 63 posts.

Nursery and review comments undermine confidence.

- "I feel like I am having to prove myself as a parent" [sr029-0101]
- "We both feel like shit parents" [sr098-0064]

**But:** One accepts a concern: "The only thing she found issues with, that was warranted, was his gross mobility skills." [sr098-0062]

### Parents want grandparents to leave the parental role intact.

2 of 63 posts.

Grandmothers claim names or take over care.

- "My mil keeps referring to herself as mum to him" [sr049-0142]
- "she needs to raise him like her own" [sr091-0128]

**But:** One grandmother is: "a lovely woman and so kind" [sr049-0144]

### Parents want separation choices respected.

2 of 63 posts.

One wants contact naps; another needs nursery for work.

- "I am quite content with this choice" [sr062-0150]
- "life reality tells me this is how it has to be." [sr119-0071]

**But:** The contact-napping parent acknowledges: "ultimately there will be a deadline on feeding to sleep." [sr062-0153]

### Parents question who should intervene with other children.

2 of 63 posts.

They want warning or intervene themselves.

- "Is it ok to gently ask parents to cue you first?" [sr076-0065]
- "their parents showed up and asked me not to parent their kids." [sr081-0112]

**But:** Safety permits touch: "if it’s needed for safety or to protect your kid." [sr076-0063]

### Parents want unwanted toy gifts stopped.

1 of 63 posts.

Requests have failed.

- "I don't trust the safety of any of it" [sr121-0028]
- "Both grandmas have a history of throwing pity parties when mildly confronted" [sr121-0031]

**But:** The parent reconsiders: "I wrote this when I was angry." [sr121-0033]

## Near posts

### Parents want frightening adult behaviour stopped.

6 of 64 posts.

Reports include screaming, insults, mockery, threats and teaching hitting.

- "He has already called me cunt and bitch in front of our son." [sr058-0130]
- "BE A MAN DONT CRY" [sr121-0021]

**But:** One partner changes: "he appears to be avoiding it now." [sr047-0053]

### Parents feel undermined by unsolicited advice.

6 of 64 posts.

Strangers, relatives and a postpartum therapist supply corrections.

- "worsening my postpartum anxiety and overall feeling of imposter syndrome." [sr014-0222]
- "had to quit because my therapist kept giving me stupid unsolicited baby advice" [sr126-0097]

**But:** A critic also helps: "my mom truly has been such a help to us in MANY ways" [sr085-0121]

### Parents resist pressure to stop comforting.

5 of 64 posts.

They defend contact naps, rocking and bedsharing.

- "to limit dependency." [sr003-0158]
- "For now I'm just enjoying the snuggles while I can." [sr060-0029]

**But:** The partner otherwise: "does more than any mom could hope for." [sr059-0108]

### Parents want control over access to their baby.

5 of 64 posts.

Reports include confusing names, forced handovers, blocked access and custody threats.

- "please no and please don’t" [sr006-0185]
- "told me I’d never see him again" [sr057-0033]

**But:** Names were initially accepted: "we were able to weigh in at the time, I think." [sr060-0048]

### Parents want sleep advice to fit their baby.

5 of 64 posts.

Advice brings blame or worse sleep; some want solidarity.

- "The naps have been broken and short and the mornings have got earlier." [sr065-0056]
- "Not more decisions to make." [sr080-0175]

**But:** Others have tried helping: "They do come and try… and nothing helps" [sr080-0157]

### Parents want feeding decisions to fit the child.

4 of 64 posts.

Conflicts concern choking, pacing, breastfeeding duration and NICU discharge.

- "he doesn’t pace her like he should." [sr094-0021]
- "he has decided the schedule is too strict" [sr134-0034]

**But:** The NICU parent also wants: "her to come home as soon as possible" [sr134-0035]

### Parents want work and family choices understood.

4 of 64 posts.

Pressure concerns another baby, work, weaning, separation and childcare.

- "the only reason I would have a second is to give HER a sibling." [sr033-0050]
- "you two are codependent" [sr073-0094]

**But:** A friend may worry: "she sometimes is witness to the hard days." [sr089-0022]

### Parents question others’ expectations in shared spaces.

4 of 64 posts.

Conflicts concern sharing, throwing, running and seat-kicking.

- "expected to give up something they were happily playing with" [sr037-0039]
- "She continued to throw them at me for the next 30 minutes" [sr047-0049]

**But:** One acknowledges: "I don't want to overstep" [sr047-0051]

### Parents want caregiving work recognised and shared.

4 of 64 posts.

Partners dismiss information while family prescribe duties and independence.

- "I tell him what the child's physio advised" [sr010-0054]
- "Suck a dick, Patty. My boy is fine." [sr103-0113]

**But:** One reassesses the partner: "he’s trying and doing pretty good tbh." [sr024-0045]

### Parents want safe movement arrangements respected.

3 of 64 posts.

They challenge missing straps, furniture barricades and seated-walker pressure.

- "oh well, nothing happened. She's fine." [sr027-0248]
- "he didn't use the safe already-set-up pack and play" [sr107-0092]

**But:** One holds their boundary: "i don’t let these kinds if things this influence my parenting decisions" [sr102-0048]

### Parents want childcare sleep expectations to fit their child.

3 of 64 posts.

Parents challenge reduced naps and early collection despite full-day payment.

- "I have told her multiple times no he needs 2." [sr008-0055]
- "I’m paying for a full day’s worth of childcare." [sr129-0060]

**But:** One centre still offers: "he’ll still be offered 3 naps" [sr117-0095]

### Parents want settling and naps protected.

2 of 64 posts.

Noise and missed sleep cost rest.

- "we get a screaming, cranky baby after we get back up." [sr028-0069]
- "I'm not angry with my husband, I'm just so tired." [sr081-0095]

**But:** The newborn’s issue is: "more timing than noise level" [sr028-0073]

### Parents want bodily boundaries respected.

2 of 64 posts.

They question finger-sucking play and block unwanted touch.

- "I definitely don’t think my mother was being sexual AT ALL" [sr045-0160]
- "quand elle veut pas qu’on la touche, bah on la touche pas" [sr091-0015]

**But:** The finger-sucking baby: "Baby loved it, I did not." [sr045-0159]

### Parents challenge negative behaviour and sleep labels.

2 of 64 posts.

They counter comparisons or judgement from one bad night.

- "get her checked out for autism and adhd because this isn’t normal behavior" [sr010-0011]
- "one bad night doesn't mean the whole year of sleep has been bad." [sr010-0205]

**But:** One allows uncertainty: "maybe she very well could have it" [sr010-0016]

### Parents feel judged when sharing celebratory moments.

2 of 64 posts.

Food-grabbing humour and a six-month party meet criticism.

- "I feel like I’ve failed my baby" [sr046-0043]
- "being called a nutter for throwing a halfway to one party with family????" [sr121-0010]

**But:** One acknowledges: "the message doesn’t seem rude or harsh" [sr046-0043]

### Parents want sleep changes agreed while carrying wake-ups.

2 of 64 posts.

Opposition compounds exhaustion; one says they no longer want motherhood.

- "six hours of unbroken sleep versus me getting shifts of two hours sleep" [sr005-0115]
- "I don't want to be a mom anymore." [sr049-0063]

**But:** Capping naps still left: "another 2 hour wake up" [sr005-0111]

### Parents disagree about when children need help.

2 of 64 posts.

One wants responsibility; another challenges delayed education and intervention.

- "she continually does things for my kids rather than make them do it for themselves." [sr005-0024]
- "Not wanting to do early intervention therapies" [sr055-0050]

**But:** Alternatives remain possible: "I can accept homeschool if it’s structured" [sr055-0051]

### Parents feel insecure about strangers’ size comments.

1 of 64 posts.

Low percentiles attract remarks.

- "aww she’s so tiny, like a little bird" [sr048-0087]
- "does this also make others insecure?" [sr048-0087]

**But:** The parent recalls: "she’s meeting milestones and is healthy and happy" [sr048-0087]

### Parents want screen content selected carefully.

1 of 64 posts.

An analogy changes the grandfather’s approach to AI videos.

- "hours long AI videos that hold his attention" [sr081-0028]
- "It worked" [sr081-0032]

**But:** The platform is accepted: "I say YouTube is fine" [sr081-0028]

### Parents gain conditional agreement about a boy’s doll.

1 of 64 posts.

Discussion and research change his position.

- "he's no longer telling me that we need to return the doll." [sr100-0073]
- "it's so cute watching him take care of it" [sr100-0073]

**But:** Restrictions remain: "He doesn't want him to get any more dolls" [sr100-0073]

## Across the on-niche posts

### Parents have tried care changes, explanations and boundaries.

53 of 63 posts.

Efforts include feeding changes, speech practice, corrections and time apart.

- "We work on sign language and speaking with tips from a speech therapist." [sr113-0105]
- "I then sent her guidelines from our government health service" [sr125-0091]

**But:** Effort need not work: "it keeps not working." [sr012-0114]

### Parents report others overriding their judgement.

63 of 63 posts.

The tag includes actions and advice, from scrutiny to harsh handling.

- "Her solution is always to let her cry." [sr028-0017]
- "they do it anyway because they think it’s funny." [sr093-0095]

**But:** Not every action is hostile: "they express their affection by constantly bringing presents over." [sr121-0028]

### Parents report purchases supporting or conflicting with care.

6 of 63 posts.

Purchases include relatives’ gifts, books, dolls, pacifiers, DVDs and a bassinet; prices are unknown.

- "the Montessori Toddler, The Whole Brain Child, and How to Talk so Little Kids Will Listen." [sr019-0044]
- "She also bought pacifiers and used them even though I told her she wasn’t taking pacifiers" [sr071-0051]

**But:** More toys can disappoint: "our girl loses interest in within a few minutes." [sr121-0028]

### Parents describe anger, shame, guilt and exhaustion.

58 of 63 posts.

Pleasure, amusement and pride also count.

- "I feel shame and defeated from the judgement" [sr019-0009]
- "I feel so guilty." [sr085-0097]
- "I’m so miserable and angry at the world" [sr084-0031]

**But:** There is joy too: "I love the contact napping so much" [sr062-0157]
