# baby-development: pattern 4, What should I get for this age?

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/second-reading/digest-10.md baby-development/evidence/2026-11/second-reading/digest-11.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

A random, no-keyword sample covered 20,000 posts across twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026; 2,471 were on-niche. This pattern contains 245 on-niche and 165 near posts; each count is posts tagged by one reader, not people or prevalence in the market. Forms named independently by digest readers were merged by judgement; cross-post tags overlap.

## On-niche posts

### They want gifts the child will use or keep.

31 of 245 posts.

They seek toys, experiences, practical gifts and keepsakes.

- "small, easily stored, practical, consumable, or outdoors!" [sr025-0027]
- "a personalised gift that he can keep forever" [sr106-0033]

**But:** Some consider saving: "Saving the money as they’re too little to understand?" [sr131-0070]

### They need safe space as the child becomes mobile.

21 of 245 posts.

They seek mats, barriers and locks.

- "figured out how to open his door" [sr094-0101]
- "almost our entire living room" [sr128-0081]

**But:** Some need no containment yet: "haven't really had issues yet." [sr046-0108]

### They need feeding equipment the child can use comfortably.

20 of 245 posts.

They compare cups, utensils and seating.

- "it has 7 different parts!" [sr006-0096]
- "a good, reachable footrest for good posture." [sr069-0013]

**But:** Some skills already work: "LO drinks water great out of straws." [sr067-0043]

### They want books and learning resources suited to this stage.

20 of 245 posts.

They seek developmental value and parenting guidance.

- "is it better to prioritize visual contrast" [sr035-0096]
- "I read all the pregnancy and infant books" [sr060-0023]

**But:** More can hinder engagement: "he actually does better when he has less options" [sr121-0078]

### They need shoes that fit and allow comfortable movement.

18 of 245 posts.

They weigh grip, fit, weather and development.

- "I cannot get his feet in any shoe" [sr073-0106]

**But:** Soft soles are not always best: "shoes with a harder sole which work a little bit better actually" [sr002-0248]

Grabbing, chewing, movement and boredom shape requests.

### They ask when to change the sleep space.

14 of 245 posts.

**But:** Some see no immediate need: "She hasn’t tried climbing out of it yet" [sr051-0123]

### They want safe items satisfying chewing and sensory interests.

14 of 245 posts.

They seek teethers, pacifiers, wire substitutes and screen-free stimulation.

- "satiate his appetite for delicious human flesh?" [sr002-0123]
- "**screen-free and battery-free**" [sr128-0092]

**But:** Pacifiers divide them: "your baby DOESNT need one." [sr105-0084]

### They want movement equipment that fits now and lasts.

13 of 245 posts.

Ability, space and price constrain choices.

**But:** Some buy ahead: "this feature would not be used to closer to three" [sr096-0003]

### They need seats the baby can safely use and tolerate.

12 of 245 posts.

Sitting, weight and bath behaviour change needs.

- "pee without a baby crying in the background." [sr030-0006]

**But:** Waiting is also considered: "It seems too early now to utilize" [sr068-0001]

### They need outing equipment that fits their growing child.

11 of 245 posts.

### They ask whether audio players and stories suit this child.

9 of 245 posts.

They weigh independence, content and readiness.

**But:** Existing equipment may suffice: "from my phone or a speaker?" [sr085-0020]

### They want equipment letting the child join everyday routines.

**But:** Potty access need not mean training: "Not potty training, just potty exploring" [sr093-0046]

### They question whether another purchase will be worth it.

They weigh usefulness, clutter and ignored purchases.

4 of 245 posts.

## Across the on-niche posts

### They have tested gear, adaptations and ordinary alternatives.

185 of 245 posts.

Attempts include secondhand searches, practice and household changes.

- "We have tried those cooling gels and tablets" [sr002-0125]
- "pulling the lid out with the straw and spilling water everywhere." [sr027-0167]

**But:** Some trials succeed: "handled the night like a champ." [sr128-0127]

### Other people influence what they acquire and when.

63 of 245 posts.

Family, daycare, professionals and ads influence choices.

- "they’ve asked us to bring in stronger ones." [sr128-0123]
- "I been getting ads for Lovevery, Kiwico and some others." [sr034-0057]

**But:** Influence need not produce agreement: "My partner and I can't agree" [sr125-0045]

### They report buying toys and practical developmental gear.

21 of 245 posts.

Purchases range from secondhand bargains to unsuccessful preparations.

- "at a flea market today for $40." [sr012-0123]
- "I bought teethers early because I thought I was being prepared. Lol nope." [sr037-0031]

**But:** Purchases do not ensure use: "things that I’ve never used again for baby 🥲" [sr004-0134]

### They express overwhelm, worry and frustration about choosing gear.

58 of 245 posts.

They also describe guilt, disappointment, pride and excitement.

**But:** Feelings also include joy: "my baby is finally getting a nursery (I am ecstatic lol)" [sr009-0056]
