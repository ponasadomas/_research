# baby-development: pattern 2, What do I do with the child all day?

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/second-reading/digest-07.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

The brief describes 20,000 randomly sampled Reddit posts, without keywords, from twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026: 2,471 were on-niche, with 226 yes and 85 near posts in this pattern. Counts are posts tagged by one reader, not people or market prevalence; merging forms across digest readers requires judgement, but this single digest’s forms remain separate. Cross tags overlap; some ages are unknown.

## On-niche posts

### Parents run out of ideas for ordinary days.

21 of 226 posts.

Solo care exhausts familiar options.

- "[…] I wake up thinking I've got a plan and then somehow it’s 10:30 and I'm out of ideas" [sr020-0071]
- "[…] Is it possible to play with him yet or is he too young? […]" [sr016-0043]

**But:** Babies can be content. "[…] He’s definitely an “easy” baby during the day." [sr030-0001]
