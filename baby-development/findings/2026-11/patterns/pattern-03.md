# baby-development: pattern 3, Hard behaviour

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/second-reading/digest-08.md baby-development/evidence/2026-11/second-reading/digest-09.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

A keyword-free random sample of 20,000 Reddit posts from twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026, yielded 2,471 on-niche posts; this pattern has 420 on-niche and 143 near posts. Each count records posts tagged by one reader; forms were merged across digest readers by judgement, and counts do not estimate market prevalence. Both digests were read; two records repeat wording, unknown ages remain unknown, one near-post age is inconsistent, and cross-tags overlap; absent means unstated.

## On-niche posts

### They want painful rough touch to stop.

59 of 420 posts.

Injuries affect adults, children and pets.

- "I would do anything for a gentle touch." [sr079-0026]
- "My 21 month old keeps bitting kids at daycare" [sr028-0022]

**But:** Intent varies: "He doesn’t hurt me because he’s angry" [sr079-0023]

### They want relief and explanations for persistent crying.

42 of 420 posts.

They seek causes and an end date.

- "We have literally never had a break." [sr106-0107]
- "I’m starting to dread night time" [sr016-0086]

**But:** Severity varies: "Some nights it's just a quick cry" [sr090-0063]

### They want to handle tantrums without worsening them.

42 of 420 posts.

Outbursts sometimes include self-injury.

- "Do they actually stop eventually?" [sr012-0172]
- "she hits her own head on the floor" [sr067-0041]

**But:** Some are brief: "Luckily it’s momentary" [sr025-0076]

### They want care routines without a fight.

42 of 420 posts.

Care raises fears about restraint.

- "restrain him still poop flying all over the place." [sr028-0056]
- "I just feel like I’m waterboarding her every night." [sr131-0077]
- "Clothes? They are prison." [sr004-0099]

**But:** Cooperation varies: "day care says he doesn’t act that way when we’re not there." [sr041-0019]

### They want manageable outings and car journeys.

35 of 420 posts.

Crying and containment battles restrict errands and holidays.

- "I'm fighting for my life" [sr023-0032]
- "I can’t sit in the back to comfort him." [sr109-0143]

**But:** One objects to stops: "Driving is totally fine but better not stop." [sr090-0044]

### They need to put their child down.

31 of 420 posts.

Contact demands displace meals, work and recovery.

- "eating with both hands and going to the bathroom by myself." [sr002-0080]
- "My house is a wreck" [sr105-0021]

**But:** Caregivers experience differences: "The baby is chill for hours, will play independently" [sr003-0144]

### They want children to share space and toys safely.

25 of 420 posts.

They seek gentleness, turn-taking and protection.

- "whatever intervention we use literally only works for about 5 seconds." [sr133-0015]
- "She will come home with large bruises on her arms." [sr110-0053]

**But:** Sharing differs by partner: "he happily gives it to us." [sr074-0139]

### They want limits their young child can understand.

24 of 420 posts.

They fear repeating harsh parenting.

- "I also don’t know if it’s realistic to discipline this early." [sr059-0054]
- "I don’t want to parent like that at all." [sr011-0220]

**But:** One reports benefit: "setting those boundaries early has definitely made things easier for us." [sr076-0074]

### They need relief from battles and their reactions.

24 of 420 posts.

Accounts include regret, lost enjoyment, yelling, spanking and therapy.

- "I crossed the line. I snapped" [sr030-0020]
- "I’m not enjoying being a mother." [sr014-0065]

**But:** Restraint can remain: "And I’ve stayed patient." [sr112-0014]

### They need to keep an exploring child safe.

22 of 420 posts.

Climbing, escapes and chewing defeat supervision and barriers.

- "she climbed over the living room gate" [sr132-0051]
- "My 1 year old eats everything… foam, plastic, paper, silicone…" [sr061-0079]

**But:** Cooperation varies: "He also stayed by us when we told him to." [sr107-0046]

### They connect frustration with emerging skills.

18 of 420 posts.

Movement, communication and tasks become frustrating.

- "She can do all the actions for crawling but isn't strong enough" [sr119-0016]
- "those skill go out the window the second he’s frustrated????? SOS!!!!" [sr008-0146]

**But:** Signs help one family: "using sign language has helped tremendously." [sr046-0124]

### They want meals without battles.

16 of 420 posts.

They balance limits and intake.

- "he will be having 1 bite of his meal 3 times a day???" [sr114-0057]
- "I've resorted to chasing him around and popping bites of food in his mouth" [sr118-0012]

**But:** Settings differ: "When he is at daycare he eats 3 meals a day" [sr132-0036]

### They want clean teeth without forcing the brush.

11 of 420 posts.

They fear aversion but need clean teeth.

- "im hoping im making contact with her teeth." [sr012-0001]
- "The only way to do it properly is to pin him down" [sr022-0029]

**But:** Resistance can pass: "she just grew out of it." [sr002-0010]

### They need relief from screaming.

6 of 420 posts.

Volume hurts even when the child is happy.

- "he's not upset, just finding his voice" [sr010-0135]
- "I have developed tinnitus in my right ear" [sr097-0004]

**But:** Quiet periods exist: "He can be quiet and content as well" [sr102-0003]

### They link harder behaviour to a major change.

6 of 420 posts.

Moves, siblings, daycare and weaning prompt difficult adjustments.

- "Since our move a month ago I’ve noticed more and more tantrums." [sr024-0017]
- "My 16 m/o is struggling with the transition" [sr054-0035]

**But:** Daycare itself can go well: "he seems to do fine and enjoy himself when I’m not there!" [sr087-0128]

### They want head-banging and hair-pulling to stop.

4 of 420 posts.

Blocking does not reliably prevent another movement.

- "She doesn’t care if it’s grass hardwood or concrete." [sr033-0013]
- "a large spot on the top of his head that was extremely thin." [sr117-0135]

**But:** Episodes are not inevitable: "sometimes he’s fine and just goes off and play" [sr118-0085]

### They question stories about toddler temperament.

4 of 420 posts.

They challenge stereotypes, frightening forecasts and idealised portrayals.

- "toddler temperament seems way more personality-based than gender-based." [sr028-0101]
- "an entirely different genre of motherhood." [sr133-0135]

**But:** The humorous rant allows calm experiences: "I'm not saying your peaceful little routines aren't real." [sr133-0135]

### They need help with difficult behaviour before sleep.

3 of 420 posts.

Bedtime screaming or missed naps make evenings difficult.

- "She takes her bottle, finishes it, screams, and then passes out." [sr034-0036]
- "around 4pm the tantrums start." [sr053-0079]

**But:** Nights can go well: "Luckily she sleeps through the night." [sr034-0036]

### They question managing another baby.

2 of 420 posts.

An attached or sensitive first child consumes available energy.

- "I don’t know if I’m even the slightest capable to care for one more child" [sr052-0103]
- "too overwhelmed and distressed being separated at daycare." [sr053-0166]

**But:** Some relief exists: "I’ve had some success with a toddler tower lately" [sr052-0100]

### They ask whether behaviour will worsen.

2 of 420 posts.

They seek others’ experience before assuming escalation.

- "Couple times a day? Couple times an hour?!" [sr001-0058]
- "Did they mellow out sooner" [sr128-0003]

**But:** Prediction remains uncertain: "I know expected behavior is a broad range" [sr128-0004]

### Other parents describe swearing or moving a diaper aside.

2 of 420 posts.

Separate concerns include embarrassment and urine-soaked bedding.

- "Our 14 month old won’t stop running around yelling shit." [sr097-0044]
- "he is still managing to pee out of that." [sr071-0058]

**But:** Diaper-shifting paused: "he actually stopped altogether for a week or two." [sr071-0057]

## Near posts

### They want settling without prolonged distress.

48 of 143 posts.

Waking, contact dependence and changing comfort habits dominate.

- "unless we lie on the floor next to her." [sr002-0117]
- "How do I provide that comfort while keeping her feed at the start of the routine?" [sr002-0136]

**But:** One seeks bedsharing instead: "He loves going to sleep in his bed." [sr118-0122]

### They feel depleted and fear their own reactions.

24 of 143 posts.

Accounts include regret, isolation, harm thoughts and substance-use fears.

- "I find myself having thoughts about hurting her" [sr006-0200]
- "I used to have issues with substances" [sr106-0050]

**But:** Thoughts do not establish acts: "I have never hurt her" [sr006-0204]

### They want safe contact between children.

10 of 143 posts.

They navigate sibling injuries, daycare disputes and friends’ children.

- "Today’s bite broke skin and left a big bruise." [sr032-0111]
- "she would rather leave the activity all together than share." [sr063-0018]

**But:** Daycare can differ: "She is in daycare and social at daycare. No concerns." [sr063-0020]

### They want workable limits without harmful punishment.

10 of 143 posts.

They question consequences, affection withdrawal and caregiver disagreement.

- "Smack her hand, put her in the corner" [sr119-0022]
- "withholding affection from him as a punishment." [sr066-0050]

**But:** Playful techniques sometimes help: "It doesn't work all the time" [sr004-0193]

### They need safety as exploration outgrows containment.

7 of 143 posts.

Crib escapes, road danger and climbing require protection.

- "started lifting his legs to climb out of the crib!" [sr010-0070]
- "he can learn from these things and change his behavior." [sr070-0083]

**But:** Exploration also delights: "I love the creativity of my toddler." [sr117-0074]

### They want tantrums to become manageable.

6 of 143 posts.

They seek reassurance and alternatives to spanking.

- "without spanking her like my dad immediately tells me to do." [sr075-0174]
- "His calm down time is getting longer and longer" [sr003-0155]

**But:** Practice sometimes helps: "the winning the game is it's own reward." [sr016-0160]

### They want painful roughness to stop.

5 of 143 posts.

They report failed scripts, punishment and daycare exclusion.

- "I also don’t want to be a punching bag." [sr040-0106]
- "they had to disenroll." [sr083-0085]

**But:** Immediate relief may fade: "he just hits me again later that day." [sr080-0099]

### They question whether another child is manageable.

4 of 143 posts.

They weigh attention, capacity and another difficult postpartum period.

- "1 is stressful already, 2 doesn’t equal less stress." [sr005-0103]
- "I'm barely surviving right now with all tantrums and teething." [sr059-0021]

**But:** Sibling connection offers hope: "I've got some brief glimpses of them playing well together" [sr005-0152]

### They want outings without distress or restraint battles.

4 of 143 posts.

Costs, crying and buckling struggles make outings stressful.

- "so worried about how much this all cost" [sr027-0017]
- "doesn’t seem worth it 🤣" [sr052-0073]

**But:** Journeys can improve after buckling: "The trip is almost always smooth." [sr040-0052]

### They need changes and clothes to stay manageable.

4 of 143 posts.

Nappy removal, mess and dressing refusals defeat familiar tactics.

- "We used tape and tried everything backwards and she figured it out" [sr014-0219]
- "without physically restraining him." [sr020-0163]

**But:** Difficulty is intermittent: "This isn't every day" [sr020-0165]

### They want food eaten rather than thrown.

3 of 143 posts.

They balance intake, consequences and utensil practice.

- "making meals just feels so discouraging" [sr069-0056]
- "I do want her to learn how to use them" [sr018-0136]

**But:** Prevention sometimes works: "it works about 30% of the time" [sr134-0060]

### They link regression to losing familiar comfort.

2 of 143 posts.

A new sibling or removed pacifier brings distress.

- "Managing the toddler has been more difficult than the newborn" [sr012-0122]
- "She tosses any stuffy we give her." [sr133-0004]

**But:** nothing found against it.

### They want repetitive questions and demands to stop.

2 of 143 posts.

Repetition strains patience alongside other caregiving.

- "Me telling him that he needs to wait doesn’t stop him" [sr005-0175]
- "My husband had to take over before I snapped." [sr043-0074]

**But:** Politeness is understood: "we do require him to ask politely before he gets something." [sr005-0177]

### They gain patience by seeing behaviour as learning.

2 of 143 posts.

Reframing growth and repetition makes care easier.

- "immediately correct that to the terrific twos" [sr046-0083]
- "toddlers do not learn through explanations alone they learn through repetition" [sr053-0038]

**But:** Difficulty remains: "It’s absolutely hard, but terrific too." [sr046-0083]

### They want screaming to give way to words.

1 of 143 posts.

One two-year-old also demands standing cuddles.

- "Evey question you ask he just screams." [sr084-0053]
- "I have to stand." [sr084-0054]

**But:** One change initially helps: "Tried no TV today and its so far going better!" [sr084-0057]

### They hope skill-related frustration will ease.

1 of 143 posts.

One parent connects screaming to rapid developmental changes.

- "But MAN have the last 3 weeks been tough." [sr010-0072]
- "screaming all day out of frustration is killer 😭" [sr010-0080]

**But:** nothing found against it.

### They need adult brushing despite independent enthusiasm.

1 of 143 posts.

One two-year-old enjoys brushing but cleans ineffectively.

- "he just kinda brushes the front two really lightly" [sr011-0148]
- "If I try and just do it for him, it turns into a big meltdown." [sr011-0148]

**But:** Modelling partly helps: "this sort of works" [sr011-0148]

### They seek hope about a demanding child’s future.

1 of 143 posts.

They ask about adolescence and adulthood.

- "What do they grow up to be like?" [sr065-0065]
- "I guess I'm just looking for ... Hope?" [sr065-0067]

**But:** nothing found against it.

### Other caregivers describe distinct practical problems.

8 of 143 posts.

Concerns include mess, piercing distress, congestion, repairs, animals, sleeplessness and a nasal rock; one account is second-hand.

- "he hasn't gained any weight over the last 3–4 months" [sr095-0114]
- "trying to drill out a two millimeter screw while my kid screams." [sr081-0090]

**But:** Mess need not be malicious: "I know she’s just playing and not doing it maliciously" [sr077-0110]

## Across the on-niche posts

### They have tried changing responses and routines.

370 of 420 posts.

Attempts include soothing, redirection, restraint, physical punishment and professional help.

- "then he started calculating it was worth it and doing it anyway." [sr021-0025]
- "I lost it and slapped his hand." [sr129-0036]

**But:** Some approaches help: "and she calms." [sr085-0094]

### They feel depleted, guilty or afraid.

285 of 420 posts.

They describe lost identity and distress.

- "I don’t feel like a person anymore sometimes." [sr029-0026]
- "I feel rage building inside me as if I’m being physically/ sexually attacked" [sr090-0042]

**But:** Love remains explicit: "I can love this child with every molecule of my existence" [sr133-0137]

### They receive conflicting advice and help.

201 of 420 posts.

Advice conflicts across caregivers and professionals.

- "when I asked my dad he said "bite her back," which is insane" [sr022-0002]
- "oh my kids know better" [sr030-0148]

**But:** Support also exists: "Our daycare has been wonderful throughout all of this." [sr044-0097]

### They report buying practical items, meals and outings.

10 of 420 posts.

Items include covers, stroller, visor, potty supplies and a book; prices are mostly unknown. A humorous rant reports $14 produce.

- "We bought a book called "teeth are not for biting"" [sr133-0026]
- "I pay to go to indoor play places" [sr016-0123]

**But:** Buying does not ensure use: "Haven’t used it yet." [sr052-0027]
