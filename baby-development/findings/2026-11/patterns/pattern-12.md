# baby-development: pattern 12, The child has a known delay or is in therapy

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/second-reading/digest-20.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

A keyword-free random sample of 20,000 Reddit posts from twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026, yielded 2,471 on-niche posts. This pattern has 107 on-niche and 51 near posts; each count is posts tagged by one reader, not people or market prevalence. Forms from different digest readers would be merged by judgement; this single digest’s forms remain separate, and overlapping tag counts cover any mention, not every example named.

## On-niche posts

### Parents need workable movement and positioning practice at home.

10 of 107 posts.

Exercises and handling can provoke distress.

- "She will not let us hold her hands to practice." [sr019-0170]
- "asking for remedial baby handling advice." [sr116-0075]

**But:** "He has made a great deal of progress in other areas" [sr077-0113]

### Parents wait for delayed speech or understanding to catch up.

9 of 107 posts.

Signs or comprehension can coexist with missing words.

- "But no words. Not even mama or dada." [sr018-0020]
- "I don’t think she really understands anything we say." [sr037-0074]

**But:** "His language exploded around 16 months" [sr078-0083]

### Parents notice additional missing skills during existing care.

9 of 107 posts.

Concerns span gestures, movement and fine-motor play.

- "He was waving but now he hasn’t recently." [sr073-0109]
- "she doesn't do things she should be able to like use a utensil while eating" [sr101-0012]

**But:** "He assured me he thinks my child is fine" [sr121-0008]

### Parents wonder whether delays and other behaviours mean autism.

7 of 107 posts.

Fear and openness to assessment coexist.

- "Is arm flapping always an autism sign?" [sr006-0031]
- "I’m terrified it’s in my genetics and I never should have had a baby." [sr062-0054]

**But:** "early services can give him a real advantage" [sr080-0086]

### Parents blame themselves or fear blame for the delay.

7 of 107 posts.

Effort coexists with guilt and family criticism.

- "I didn’t talk to her “right” or “enough”." [sr074-0088]
- "blamed for it by my husband and mil" [sr084-0122]

**But:** "He's jumped from the 4th percentile for weight to the 87th" [sr050-0231]

### Parents question helmet necessity and worry about wearing one.

7 of 107 posts.

They weigh incentives, discomfort and babyhood memories.

- "the company that sells the helmets to be assessed for said helmet." [sr078-0089]
- "All memories I make with him at this age will include the helmet." [sr049-0012]

**But:** "We’re willing to do it and pay for it if it’s necessary" [sr078-0090]

### Parents need solids to become a workable source of nutrition.

7 of 107 posts.

- "There’s no way she’s even close to being able to consume what she needs with solids" [sr117-0014]

**But:** "now she’ll put a cookie or cracker in her own mouth." [sr117-0013]

6 of 107 posts.

Unexpected scores or unmentioned labels unsettle them.

- "the EI assessors found severe receptive communication delay (4th percentile)." [sr002-0305]
- "not going to be representative of what his actual abilities are" [sr084-0079]

**But:** One parent recognises the concern: "the eye thing is def true." [sr085-0080]

### Parents want stories of delayed movement eventually improving.

**But:** "We’ve been seeing pt/ot and have seen improvements" [sr024-0011]

5 of 107 posts.

3 of 107 posts.

Insurance, eligibility and exhausted funds limit care.

**But:** Progress preceded lost eligibility: "EI helped a TON" [sr108-0082]

3 of 107 posts.

Peers make differences painfully visible.

- "question my parenting abilities" [sr035-0102]

**But:** "she was climbing around and happy." [sr002-0207]

### Parents want care and friendships to include their child.

3 of 107 posts.

They worry about participation and belonging.

- "I just want to make sure he has friends in this life." [sr076-0059]

**But:** "daycare doesn't seem to have an issue with it." [sr024-0001]

### Parents ask whether hearing or oral conditions explain delayed speech.

3 of 107 posts.
