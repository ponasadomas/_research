# baby-development: pattern 1, Is this normal, or is something wrong with my child?

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/second-reading/digest-04.md baby-development/evidence/2026-11/second-reading/digest-05.md baby-development/evidence/2026-11/second-reading/digest-06.md

## Limits

A random, no-keyword sample covered 20,000 posts from twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026; this pattern contains 595 on-niche and 186 near posts. Counts are posts tagged by one reader, not people or market prevalence; different digest readers’ forms were merged by judgement, whole. Digest readers read all supplied posts; unknown ages remain unknown and medical explanations remain speakers’ accounts.

## On-niche posts

### Parents ask whether movement and practical skills are late.

73 of 595 posts.

Concerns span head control, walking and hand tasks.

- "[…] He just doesn’t have the strength to turn his head. […]" [sr109-0075]
- "[…] But independently she won’t unless we coax her to do it. […]" [sr111-0014]

**But:** Not always alarm. "[…] I’m not particularly worried." [sr039-0099]
