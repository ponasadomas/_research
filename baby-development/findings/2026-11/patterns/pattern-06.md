# baby-development: pattern 6, The child prefers someone else

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/second-reading/digest-14.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

A random, no-keyword sample of 20,000 Reddit posts from twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026, yielded 2,471 on-niche posts; this pattern has 129 on-niche and 27 near posts. Counts are posts tagged by one reader, not people or market prevalence; all 156 were read, and cross tags overlap. Merging forms across digest readers requires judgement; this sole digest’s distinct forms are retained.

## On-niche posts

### Parents feel rejected when the other parent receives the affection they want.

14 of 129 posts.

They want affection and feel humiliated or resentful.

- "[…] STARVED for my baby to show me the tiniest crumb of affection." [sr046-0061]
- "Sometimes she will even go down the list of all the people she loves, but not me." [sr091-0081]

**But:** It can pass: "[…] this was a phase that ultimately has passed for us now." [sr053-0030]

### Parents cannot get a break when the child demands their presence.

12 of 129 posts.

Eating, washing, working and caring for siblings become difficult without screaming or clinging.

- "I can’t shit, brush my teeth, sleep in, shower, leave for work […]" [sr071-0008]
- "[…] I’m not able to give proper time or attention to my 2 year old and 4 year old :(" [sr003-0057]

**But:** It varies: "Sometimes he's perfectly fine with my husband watching him, other times not […]" [sr074-0104]

### Caregivers feel displaced or caught between adults when a relative is preferred.

10 of 129 posts.

Parents fear losing their role; a caregiving cousin feels guilty about being chosen.

- "He probably thinks I'm just the bitch that lives with him. […]" [sr032-0057]
- "[…] she’ll run to me instead (mind you, i don’t tell her to come to me)." [sr080-0095]

**But:** Parents still receive affection: "He is also very affectionate with me and my husband when we’re alone." [sr042-0024]

### Parents want to support a partner who feels rejected.

10 of 129 posts.

They seek reassurance, routines and resources to protect the partner’s confidence.

- "I would do anything for mom to be the favorite just for her sanity." [sr023-0077]
- "Does anyone know any resources to help teach dad to be a little more nurturing?" [sr016-0142]

**But:** One questions whether preference is the issue: "This feels more like self-doubt than an actual issue." [sr065-0046]

### Parents want to share bedtime or night waking again.

10 of 129 posts.

Preferences change despite established routines, including with formula feeding.

- "[…] Middle of the night, apparently dad is a wild animal coming to hurt him." [sr089-0052]
- "[…] baby is formula fed so it isn't a breastfeeding thing." [sr022-0064]

**But:** Presence can matter: "If he leaves the house, she’s totally fine with me […]" [sr104-0069]

### Parents fear that a difficult start or shared care damaged attachment.

9 of 129 posts.

They blame birth, feeding, depression or divided attention; these are feared causes.

- "I had a c-section and I couldn’t breastfeed […]" [sr087-0044]
- "[…] I couldn’t be caregiving 100 percent of the time." [sr095-0122]

**But:** Some report affection: "She smiles when she sees me […]" [sr087-0044]

### Parents feel unable to comfort a baby whom others can soothe.

9 of 129 posts.

Crying with them and calm with others can feel like incompetence.

- "[…] being calmed, yet not being calmed in my arms." [sr016-0128]
- "[…] I find embarrassing in front of our parents and friends with their own kids." [sr078-0066]

**But:** Night settling can differ: "[…] I don’t have as hard of a time getting her back to sleep during the night." [sr051-0114]

### Parents feel overlooked when their baby smiles or performs skills for others.

7 of 129 posts.

Skills shown to others can replace developmental worry with hurt.

- "When I try to do the same things he does and bring the same energy, she just doesn't react the same." [sr028-0162]
- "So now I'm not worried about her development anymore […]" [sr018-0180]

**But:** They still receive smiles: "I get smiles from her […]" [sr028-0163]

### Parents want to preserve or rebuild their bond across absences.

7 of 129 posts.

Work, trips and custody bring fears of replacement or difficult reunions.

- "Will my little one forget me? […]" [sr009-0054]
- "[…] I used to be his center now I am not. […]" [sr003-0110]

**But:** Distress can end after handover: "Once his mom is gone and he settles down, he is completely normal with me. […]" [sr112-0030]

### Parents want their baby to accept the other parent’s comfort.

7 of 129 posts.

Proposed bonding feeds can conflict with pumping anxiety.

- "[…] let me comfort him, even if it’s once every 5 times?" [sr013-0087]
- "[…] even pumping is too anxiety inducing for her." [sr015-0024]

**But:** Play may already work: "He plays with him happily […]" [sr096-0101]

### Caregivers want the child to accept a familiar relative they reject.

5 of 129 posts.

They consider contact, play, language and adult anxiety; two sampled posts may repeat one account.

- "[…] he cried the WHOLE day […]" [sr001-0021]
- "[…] my mom wants nothing more than to feel loved from my daughter." [sr015-0028]

**But:** Interest can follow rejection: "[…] then kept asking where she is once she left. […]" [sr054-0060]

### Parents say care goes well until the preferred parent appears.

5 of 129 posts.

Seeing or hearing that parent interrupts happy care.

- "[…] the second LO sees or hears mom, starts crying until in her arms." [sr001-0062]
- "[…] don’t walk in the room if she wants me to watch him […]" [sr040-0002]

**But:** Solo care can go well: "[…] we play, have fun, eat, wash, sleep, etc. totally fine" [sr130-0014]

### Parents fear leaving because separations bring prolonged distress.

5 of 129 posts.

They cut outings short or fear future separations.

- "[…] scream cried for 3+ hours straight until I left early to come home." [sr126-0106]
- "[…] makes me feel shit like I’ve abandoned him" [sr111-0081]

**But:** Some fear is anticipatory: "[…] she is by all means an "easy baby"." [sr084-0116]

### Nursing parents feel wanted for milk while missing other affection.

4 of 129 posts.

They want affection without offering a breast.

- "[…] all he sees me is as a pair of boobs lol" [sr023-0106]
- "[…] DAD GETS KISSES BEFORE I DO????!!!’n" [sr059-0003]

**But:** Some occasions feel different: "[…] I am just over the moon if he does want me on the rare occasion." [sr114-0048]

### Parents are unsure when to intervene in the other parent’s soothing.

4 of 129 posts.

They weigh attachment worries against letting their partner try.

- "[…] letting baby cry in my arms until she tires herself out is a form of Cry It Out?" [sr050-0024]
- "[…] And then we argue." [sr086-0180]

**But:** Solo caregiving may already be routine: "[…] we often parent alone" [sr086-0176]

### Parents feel displaced when their child appears to prefer childcare staff.

4 of 129 posts.

They value childcare but fear not being needed.

- "I feel invisible? […]" [sr032-0119]
- "[…] he just went up to the nanny and gave her a leg hug. […]" [sr121-0049]

**But:** Parents are also sought: "[…] And stops crying when i get him." [sr091-0124]

### Parents find affection or soothing success after feeling rejected.

3 of 129 posts.

Time, bubbles or patient holding bring changes.

- "Since she’s turned one, I see how much she loves her mama." [sr004-0159]
- "I feel so damn proud of myself." [sr063-0013]

**But:** Preference can remain: "She’s still totally a daddy’s girl." [sr004-0158]

### Parents worry about their own connection to a child who prefers others.

3 of 129 posts.

They fear loving less, favouring a sibling or merely performing parenthood.

- "[…] i feel like i love him less and less." [sr088-0100]
- "I've felt sometimes like I'm role-playing a dad […]" [sr106-0089]

**But:** Commitment remains: "[…] I’m committed to being a good dad to him." [sr092-0091]

### A parent questions whether a new nanny can bond while family intervene.

1 of 129 posts.

They weigh adjustment against a possible mismatch in energy.

- "every time the baby fusses or cries with the nanny, they immediately step in to comfort her." [sr045-0148]
- "[…] a much lower, more passive energy level […]" [sr045-0149]

**But:** They allow adjustment time: "I know transitions take time […]" [sr045-0147]

## Near posts

### Parents want to share bedtime or night waking again.

10 of 27 posts.

They try sleep changes, sometimes including extinction crying; others rule sleep training out.

- "[…] 2 nights in a row he cried til he threw up." [sr040-0077]
- "[…] hold mama’s hand while dad rocks you to sleep" [sr006-0111]

**But:** Absence can help: "[…] once I was away overnight she settled no problem!" [sr115-0076]

### Parents feel unable to settle a baby whom others settle faster.

4 of 27 posts.

Sleep struggles include worsening depression and passive suicidality despite treatment.

- "I spend over 6 hours trying to make him sleep yesterday […]" [sr079-0055]
- "[…] I cry a lot and get irritated, and passively suicidal. […]" [sr033-0064]

**But:** Faster settling does not establish preference: "he gets super upset to have to go to his dad […]" [sr056-0107]

### Parents cannot get a break when the child demands their presence.

3 of 27 posts.

They want relief and resent the other parent’s ability to step away.

- "[…] it's so hard to get anything done or have a moment to myself […]" [sr067-0055]
- "[…] he gets to just check out when he's not feeling well." [sr114-0015]

**But:** One enjoys weekend relief: "[…] my husband will watch him upstairs while I cook or bake […]" [sr114-0017]

### Fathers miss affection when their child chooses mom.

2 of 27 posts.

Ages are unknown; rejection and newly learned words feature.

- "“I don’t like daddy”" [sr092-0082]
- "Was trying to comfort him and he was just screaming out mama" [sr105-0002]

**But:** One expects fluctuation: "I know it goes back and forth […]" [sr105-0003]

### A mother fears that birth and feeding difficulties damaged attachment.

1 of 27 posts.

She reports failures despite treatment for postpartum anxiety and depression.

- "[…] I’m terrified my baby isn’t bonded with me. […]" [sr042-0092]
- "[…] we missed this huge bonding experience again. […]" [sr042-0090]

**But:** She also reports closeness: "Baby currently is fast asleep on my chest […]" [sr042-0093]

### A parent feels shut out when offering help with evening crying.

1 of 27 posts.

Her partner interprets offers as distrust and refuses to hand the baby over.

- "[…] They take it as me being a control freak or not trusting them." [sr058-0012]
- "[…] feels like I’m being punished […]" [sr058-0013]

**But:** Overnight differs: "[…] overnight wakes aren’t terrible […]" [sr058-0015]

### A mother wants to manage her emotions when her child calls for dad.

1 of 27 posts.

She describes shutting down and depressive moments with bipolar II.

- "[…] It was heartbreaking for me, and I shut down." [sr070-0017]
- "How do I learn to just set that shit aside when I'm with her […]" [sr070-0018]

**But:** She regularly participates: "I do usually take over baby duty when I get home […]" [sr070-0017]

### A parent fears a trip because the child screams for her at bedtime.

1 of 27 posts.

She fears separation, while trusting dad.

- "[…] I am absolutely filled with anxiety […]" [sr072-0111]
- "[…] he will scream and cry until I come in to sit with him." [sr072-0110]

**But:** She trusts dad: "[…] he’s an amazing dad who 1000% can do everything I do with probably less stress" [sr072-0112]

### A parent considers ending night-nanny care to receive nighttime affection.

1 of 27 posts.

Comfort-seeking or longer sleep might end the arrangement.

- "I want him to hug me and my husband at night." [sr024-0113]
- "[…] It's a monthly minimum thing." [sr024-0116]

**But:** They already report preference: "[…] prefers me because I take care of him in the daytime." [sr024-0115]

### A parent wonders whether attachment will allow working from home with shared care.

1 of 27 posts.

Two virtual teachers consider alternating care or arranging daycare.

- "Is it going to be possible for us to tag team it? […]" [sr034-0055]
- "[…] she's pretty attached to me […]" [sr034-0056]

**But:** The later arrangement remains untested: "[…] will it be enough for me to able to get work done?" [sr034-0056]

### A father waits for his daughter to recognise and appreciate him.

1 of 27 posts.

He assumes greater maternal bonding without describing rejection.

- "I have a 2-year-old daughter" [sr033-0035]
- "when did your daughter begin to "feel" or "know" or "appreciate" you as a father?" [sr033-0035]

**But:** nothing found against it.

### A father misses sex and intimacy while struggling to bond with the baby.

1 of 27 posts.

He hopes greater interaction will help; his partner describes exhaustion.

- "[…] I really miss intimacy and sex." [sr035-0075]
- "[…] she last gave me a blowjob shortly after childbirth and nothing since then. […]" [sr035-0084]

**But:** He reports improvement: "[…] I do feel it’s slowly getting better." [sr035-0080]

## Across the on-niche posts

### Parents try shared care, soothing and repeated contact to build comfort.

116 of 129 posts.

They use routines, feeding changes, exposure, reassurance and boundaries.

- "[…] bedtime routines, diaper changes, baths, even wrapping her in one of my shirts." [sr017-0013]
- "Until now they've gone to the park every week and that helped" [sr054-0059]

**But:** Attempts can fail: "[…] Same result." [sr112-0029]

### Other adults’ help and comments shape parents’ experience of preference.

124 of 129 posts.

They report support, interference, dismissiveness and gloating.

- "I could also handle it if he wasn’t a fucking dick and gloated about it…" [sr038-0115]
- "[…] who have offered to watch her so we have can have an evening to ourselves" [sr017-0014]

**But:** Explanations are contested: "[…] we both do everything with him […]" [sr130-0045]

### Parents mention nanny care, purses or unspecified purchases.

5 of 129 posts.

Amounts are unknown; two nanny payments are inferred from the role, and the fifth purse purchase is proposed.

- "We hired a new nanny for our 1-year-old […]" [sr045-0147]
- "[…] buy my 5th secondhand Coach purse this week bc I deserve dopamine too goddamnit!!!n!!" [sr059-0005]

**But:** Some spending is unspecified: "[…] buying stuff […]" [sr106-0089]

### Parents describe rejection, resentment or exhaustion alongside love and pride.

112 of 129 posts.

They also name shame, loneliness and fears of loving less.

- "I feel embarrassed and unappreciated […]" [sr053-0014]
- "I am a weird level of tired that I feel in my bones […]" [sr129-0009]
- "If I died tomorrow he would be none the wiser." [sr032-0059]

**But:** Success also brings pride: "I feel so damn proud of myself." [sr063-0013]
