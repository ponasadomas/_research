# baby-development: pattern 3, Hard behaviour (children of 2 and 3 years)

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/age-reread/digest-04.md baby-development/evidence/2026-11/age-reread/digest-05.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

Posts came from a keyword-free random sample of 20,000 Reddit posts in twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026. This pattern contains 379 yes and 41 near posts, all read; counts are posts tagged by one reader, with forms merged across digest readers by judgement. Counts are not market rates; supplied classifications remain intact.

## On-niche posts

### The parent cannot get cooperation through repeated rules and choices.

54 of 379 posts.

Refusals include contradictory demands that granting a wish cannot resolve.

- "He is never following my rules without some whining, I have to repeat everything 10 times, and each time he makes up a distraction that he needs to do before proceeding." [ar003-0001]

**But:** "We ​ were up a little late, but boy it looks tidy in here for a change!" [ar011-0106]

### The parent wants to prevent painful contact and harm to people or pets.

41 of 379 posts.

Accounts include unwanted soothing touch, serious injuries and animal risks.

- "What has led me to reach out for help is the head butt I received yesterday at nap time that literally broke my nose. This kid has given me a black eye before…." [ar037-0021]
- "My daughter seems to think she has to hold the kitten tighter so she won't escape, which obviously makes the situation worse." [ar005-0098]

**But:** "It’s not because he’s angry, most of the time he’s playing or showing love." [ar062-0108]

### The parent wants to know why meltdowns intensified and when they ease.

38 of 379 posts.

Causes remain unestablished.

- "My son is 2.5 years old and every evening for the past two weeks he tantrums from around 6pm-when he goes to bed around 7:30/8:30pm." [ar063-0020]

**But:** "The only thing that reliably works is her blankie. It regulates her and she calms down." [ar017-0057]

### The parent struggles to complete care without distress or force.

34 of 379 posts.

Parents question restraint and bodily autonomy.

- "Every day that toothbrush comes out and the kid instantly covers his mouth with a death grip and forces his mouth shut." [ar056-0096]

**But:** "[…] She LOVES brushing her teeth with the clear training toothpaste but won't even try colored toothpaste without a fight. […]" [ar082-0011]

### The parent wants siblings safe without rejecting the older child.

32 of 379 posts.

Rough affection, jealousy and attacks compete with another child’s needs.

- "Last night he was kicking his brother and when we gently told him no kicking, he head butted the baby." [ar025-0043]

**But:** "ever since then she has gotten more aggressive towards us (not him)." [ar052-0050]

### The parent wants safe outings without chasing or physically removing the child.

28 of 379 posts.

Running, climbing, escapes and refused restraints make trips difficult.

- "Just now I pulled her back from walking in front of a school bus." [ar019-0005]

**But:** "he is a great kid and extremely well behaved, no pulling throwing kicking none of that. He absolutely hates social gatherings. Infact when he is in a clean and less noisy set up he seems to do okay. And even when shopping." [ar002-0113]

### The parent wants to stop reacting angrily while keeping limits and affection.

24 of 379 posts.

Regret coexists with love; one parent fears returning to substances.

- "I just don’t want to yell. I want to be his safe space." [ar052-0009]

**But:** An aunt reports calmer reactions. "But our home is much calmer now, and I feel far more in control of my reactions than I did before" [ar016-0048]

### The parent wants the child to respect peers and remain included.

21 of 379 posts.

Sharing, aggression, private-part touching and childcare expulsion all appear.

- "My son is two years old. He recently started touching other kids private parts." [ar035-0138]

**But:** "Although the director reassured us that it isn’t anything major and they just want to get ahead of it, I’m mortified. […]" [ar068-0056]

### The parent wants relief from loud, rude or hurtful speech.

20 of 379 posts.

Parents question noise and copied language.

- "His scream is so high pitched. It’s not just annoying but hurts our ears and his twin." [ar066-0063]
- "“everyone looks so lovely! Except the black girls. I don’t like the black girls”" [ar021-0056]

**But:** "He obviously has absolutely no idea what either word means. He’ll just randomly say them because I think he remembers the sound and knows it gets a reaction." [ar081-0128]

### The parent wants exact words and a repeatable response.

18 of 379 posts.

Two writers promote their own resources; their claims lack independent reviews.

- "Someone (okay instagram reels) told me that using If statements are like threatening your child. Is that what I’m doing?! I thought I was outlining the consequence." [ar055-0013]

**But:** A resource promoter describes partial change. "[…] Not instantly fixing anything, but he engages with it in a completely different way. […]" [ar004-0006]

### The parent wants other adults to change their response.

10 of 379 posts.

Adults disagree, model behaviour or fail to intervene.

**But:** "When I parent their kid in front of them they never have a problem with it. So they know the behavior isn’t ok… I think?" [ar066-0077]

### The parent needs a break from constant activity and attention demands.

10 of 379 posts.

Movement, interruptions and rigid games consume time.

**But:** "[…] I was able to spend a solo afternoon with him (he has a 10m old sister) and it was wonderful. He was sweet as pie. He wasn't climbing all over me and making crazy noise and being absolutely off the walls. We went on a hike and had ice cream and it was truly so great to be there with him. […]" [ar040-0073]

### The parent wants repeated licking, spitting and throwing to stop.

**But:** "Do you guys have any recommendations to discourage this behavior? Any advice on how to react? It’s driving me nuts! He’s a very easygoing and obedient kid otherwise." [ar069-0116]

### The parent wants professional help beyond their current tools.

6 of 379 posts.

Evaluations or reassurance can leave concerns unresolved.

**But:** One hospitalized child is unusually aggressive. "He's never usually aggressive? He has been, especially with doctors, but never to this extent. I'm hoping it's just the sedation." [ar028-0045]

### The parent struggles when the child rejects one caregiver and demands another.

6 of 379 posts.

**But:** "[…] She is an insanely smart girl, is very observant and well-spoken, all around a wonderful kid." [ar081-0014]

### The parent wants adults to prevent repeated childcare injuries.

5 of 379 posts.

**But:** "Aside from this, the daycare is amazing. First time we have encountered an issue." [ar041-0053]

### The parent wants fewer screen demands after screens helped them cope.

5 of 379 posts.

Removal distress and persistent demands can divide caregivers.

**But:** "After school we divert his TV request to a list of other fun things we can do together. We think the tantrums are already getting better… but we’ll see." [ar035-0101]

### The parent wants comfort without painful touching or pacifier dependence.

4 of 379 posts.

Removal and substitutes can bring distress.

**But:** "I would just absolutely love to hold and cuddle her but she just wants my damn hands and I NEED this to stop." [ar062-0005]

**But:** "[…] I don’t hate my motherhood. I’m a SAHM by choice, I love being a mum. Dare I even say it’s come pretty easy to me from the start and I don’t find it overly hard even though I have a hard child. […]" [ar018-0121]

## Across the on-niche posts

### The parent has already tried ways to change behaviour.

336 of 379 posts.

Parents try calm choices, routines, timeouts, restraint and spanking.

- "I believe in time outs, putting them in the corner, taking toys, having talks about why certain behavior is wrong, and yes, even spankings. None of it seems to matter with her." [ar071-0029]

**But:** "Today I finally realised I needed to remind her that she was going to see it and that if she wanted to she could close her eyes.  So we “closed our eyes together” and I took her pajama pants off and viola no meltdown." [ar013-0055]

### The parent hears conflicting responses from relatives, partners and professionals.

181 of 379 posts.

Others reassure, criticise, advise punishment or report disruption.

- "My family keeps telling me we need to bite her back but I just cannot bring myself to do that." [ar003-0076]

**But:** "Everything that I see and that my pediatrician has told me says this is just the normal two year old life." [ar054-0047]

### The parent reports spending on care, alternatives and ordinary purchases.
