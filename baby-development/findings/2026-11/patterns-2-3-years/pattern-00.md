# baby-development: pattern 0, None of these (children of 2 and 3 years)

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/age-reread/digest-01.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

From 20,000 randomly sampled, keyword-free Reddit posts (twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026), this pattern contains 56 on-niche and 80 near posts. Counts are posts tagged by one reader, not people or market prevalence; cross-reader forms would be merged by judgement, but only one digest is assigned. On-niche forms retain two young-infant accounts despite the age label; near posts include other and unknown ages.
