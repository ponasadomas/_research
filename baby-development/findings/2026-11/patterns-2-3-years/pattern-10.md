# baby-development: pattern 10, Other people interfere (children of 2 and 3 years)

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/age-reread/digest-12.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

A random sample of 20,000 posts from twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026, was read without a keyword filter: 3,811 were on-niche, including 1,340 about children of two and three. This pattern has 27 on-niche and 13 near posts, all read; counts are posts tagged by one reader, not people or market prevalence. Forms from different digest readers would be merged by judgement; only one digest supplies this pattern, and its distinct forms remain separate.

## On-niche posts

### The parent wants relatives to stop shaming normal toddler behaviour.

6 of 27 posts.

Parents resist relatives treating crying, impulsivity or slow warming as wrongdoing or rejection; some end visits early.

- "My sister then accused me of letting her “get her way” and eventually called my daughter a “fucking cry baby” while she was standing right there." [ar001-0134]
- "Once, I was in the room when my mom said “aw don’t cry, you’re going to make grandma cry too!”" [ar009-0110]
- "While I was trying to calm her, my FIL got frustrated, stormed toward the boat and said, “Just throw her in.”" [ar006-0187]

**But:** Firm correction can be welcome: "They correct her behaviour but still make her feel safe and loved." [ar001-0135]

### The parent wants grandmothers to respect what the family teaches.

3 of 27 posts.

Disputes concern Catholic teaching, taking museum toys and an accidentally rude hand gesture.

- "She also asked to take my daughter to her church group and I said no." [ar074-0025]
- "While we were my MIL basically stole one of the toys from the play area. She pulled it out of her pocket while I was buckling my child into her seat and gave it to her." [ar016-0039]

**But:** Learning about religion is acceptable: "I’m fine with my daughter learning about religion at this age, but I’d rather it be in the context that there are many different belief systems." [ar074-0027]

3 of 27 posts.
