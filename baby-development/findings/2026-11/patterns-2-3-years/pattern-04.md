# baby-development: pattern 4, What should I get for this age? (children of 2 and 3 years)

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/age-reread/digest-06.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

A random, keyword-free sample of 20,000 posts from twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026, yielded 3,811 on-niche posts, 1,340 about two- and three-year-olds. This pattern has 97 on-niche and 27 near posts; counts are posts tagged by one reader, not market prevalence. Merging forms across digest readers requires judgement; this single digest’s forms remain separate, including infant and unknown-age material.

## On-niche posts

### Parents want toys that stay interesting.

10 of 97 posts.

Compatibility and size also matter.

- "I'd rather have fewer things that actually stick around in his play." [ar015-0090]
- "What brand tracks also work with other brands ?" [ar005-0092]

**But:** Toys may lose out: "She’s MUCH more interested in what I’m doing." [ar044-0040]

### Parents want activities ready to use.

7 of 97 posts.

- "I work FT and have a first grader as well, and I have zero creativity to create the activities myself." [ar007-0053]
- "Sketchpens and sheets of paper work great." [ar061-0001]

**But:** Adaptation is needed: "Some of the activities are above her head but we make them fun anyway." [ar003-0114]
