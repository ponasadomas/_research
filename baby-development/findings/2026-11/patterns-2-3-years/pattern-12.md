# baby-development: pattern 12, The child has a known delay or is in therapy (children of 2 and 3 years)

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/age-reread/digest-14.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

A random, keyword-free sample of 20,000 Reddit posts from twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026, yielded 3,811 on-niche posts; 1,340 concern children of two and three years. This pattern has 34 on-niche and 16 near posts, all read; counts are posts tagged by one reader, not people or prevalence among children or buyers. Forms from different digest readers would be merged by judgement; only digest-14 is supplied, and its distinct forms remain separate.

## On-niche posts

### They want to know when their child’s speech will catch up.

8 of 34 posts.

They seek hopeful timelines, from first words to understandable sentences.

- "If you had a 2 year old with a speech delay and received early intervention -when did they have a word explosion and catch up on speech?" [ar084-0071]
- "I’m wondering for those of you who also have/had late talkers/speech delays, when their pronunciation got better and were more understandable with their sentences/words? When did they start speaking in more sentences and able to really communicate efficiently lol?" [ar006-0278]

**But:** Progress is already reported: "My daughter turned 2 in June and her speech has really started “exploding” the past few months, shortly after she started preschool. She’s started combining a few words and making small 2-3 word sentences." [ar006-0277]

### They want daily life with their child to feel less exhausting.

4 of 34 posts.

Support has not resolved communication difficulties, refusal, aggression, or tantrums.

- "And it makes spending 1:1 time with him a chore, something we should look forward to but often can’t." [ar053-0060]
- "The problem is that I cannot get her to buy in at home - she will NOT do them." [ar022-0062]

**But:** One parent reports brief improvement: "These are the best 2 days we’ve had in a while! I don’t want to speak too soon and say I’ve “ fixed” the problems." [ar069-0128]

### They want to know which therapy to choose and what happens there.

4 of 34 posts.

Questions cover providers, settings, coverage, waits, and the child’s participation.

- "What should I be looking for in a provider, and how long did it take you to actually get an appointment?" [ar048-0014]
- "So im concerned if i take him he won't sit still long enough to be engaged with the therapist. What does speech therapy actually consist of?" [ar018-0048]

**But:** "I wouldn't agree to more than 25 hrs of therapy per week." [ar058-0021]

### They wonder whether new troubling behavior needs another assessment.

4 of 34 posts.

Regression, mouthing, self-injury, or repetitive behavior prompts questions, not established diagnoses.

- "She put a coin in her mouth and said “you need to get this so I don’t put it in my mouth.” She said “you need to take this toy so I don’t eat it. I’m worried I’ll eat it.”" [ar069-0175]
- "He tested low risk on the MCHAT at 18 months and 24 months but the early intervention assessor wants to re-test in 6 months due to some repetitive behaviors she noticed." [ar029-0100]

**But:** Some settings are reassuring: "At daycare, they tell me how great she does with her friends." [ar001-0118]

### They feel they are failing when home speech practice does not work.

3 of 34 posts.

Their self-blame does not establish what caused the delay.

- "My husband and I dropped the ball, horribly, when it comes to talking to our daughter (almost 3). Because of this she has a speech delay and I’m struggling with feeling inadequate/a bad mom." [ar071-0055]
- "We do flash cards, books, it still doesn’t seem like he progresses." [ar029-0003]

**But:** Visits can still be enjoyable: "They come to our house every 2 weeks or so, and he loves the visits." [ar042-0123]

### They want to ease changes in preschool or family routines.

3 of 34 posts.

**But:** Distress can end after drop-off: "After I'm gone, he is content to go about his day. Its just the whole transitioning to me leaving that is hard for him." [ar076-0151]

### They want a speech explanation without making childhood entirely therapy.

1 of 34 posts.

### They want an accurate assessment without holding back their child’s skills.

**But:** "I do want him to get support if he needs it. He has a speech delay, but he’s making progress." [ar035-0141]

## Near posts

3 of 16 posts.

**But:** "My son has some delays and daycare is so good for him, spending more time there will only be more beneficial. (Both of his therapists words!)" [ar042-0117]

### They want sleep to become less distressing or fit new care.

3 of 16 posts.

Two caregivers seek relief; a future stepmother seeks an earlier schedule.

- "My husband and I are incredibly stressed out and wake up each day riddled with anxiety." [ar064-0026]

**But:** An earlier improvement was reported: "No more wakes, no more frantic screaming for us to be with him, he could be rocked for a few minutes and set down in bed and then he’d put himself to sleep while we sat across the room." [ar030-0141]

## Across the on-niche posts

### They have tried home activities, professional support, or changes in care.

33 of 34 posts.

Attempts also include sensory tools, rewards, medical care, and transition preparation.

- "We read with him, play, and constantly model language. He imitates words, but not consistently." [ar023-0004]
- "He currently receives speech therapy and OT, and we’ve also worked with developmental/play-based providers. We’re specifically looking into PROMPT/motor-speech approaches because of the concern about planning the movements needed for speech." [ar044-0110]

**But:** Some report marked improvement: "My wife finally took her to a family nurse practitioner and she referred her out. My daughter received tubes and her entire life turned around." [ar035-0070]

### They receive professional advice that can conflict with their own observations.

25 of 34 posts.

Others’ actions include dismissal, eligibility advice, referrals, assessments, and family screen use.

- "One of his therapists told me I shouldn’t practice fine motor skills with him anymore because he needs to qualify for services." [ar035-0140]
- "His speech therapist has raised concerns about motor planning, and we’re starting to explore that more seriously." [ar044-0105]

**But:** Reassurance is also reported: "I did tell them about my concerns with school and the evaluator said he scored on the high end of average for communication and gross motor, and still within the average range for everything else." [ar013-0108]

### They pay for therapy or materials, sometimes stopping over cost and usefulness.

3 of 34 posts.

Purchases also include private speech therapy and sensory items; attendance alone is not payment.

- "We stopped doing OT because it was $50 per session and while they success getting her to do the exercises, I couldn’t get her to do them at home and the office didn’t like parents participating in the sessions." [ar022-0063]
- "We got him books (Daniel Tiger) and a baby doll to interact with." [ar026-0078]

**But:** Private speech therapy helps despite cost: "We are doing public funding for SLP as well but the public honestly sucks compared and it was only on webcam once a month so we decided to start paying private on top of it. And it has helped it’s just expensive we try to do as much as possible and our standard is 2x a month." [ar023-0006]

### They describe guilt, exhaustion, and fear about helping enough.

27 of 34 posts.

Feelings also include heartbreak and milestone anxiety; seven posts have no feeling tag.

- "I feel pathetic even writing this, but my 3yo son is autistic and speech delayed." [ar029-0001]
- "It's just mentally exhausting it feels like she's never going to talk and my wife looks like she's ready to mentally breakdown over it every day." [ar046-0005]

**But:** Hope coexists with being behind: "Big hugs to you as well. I am hopeful things will speed up. I noticed more words and more sounds after 2.5 it’s just that we are still behind 😭. We got this! We are all doing the best we can !" [ar023-0009]
