# baby-development: pattern 9, Classes, groups, other children, daycare as a place to grow (children of 2 and 3 years)

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/age-reread/digest-11.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

A keyword-free random sample covered 20,000 Reddit posts from twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April–24 October 2026; 3,811 were on-niche, including 1,340 about children of two and three. This pattern has 140 on-niche and 38 near posts, all read; counts are posts tagged by one reader, not people or market prevalence. Merging forms across readers requires judgement; this single digest’s distinct forms remain separate.

## On-niche posts

### Parents want easier daycare goodbyes.

21 of 140 posts.

Distress sometimes persists.

- "He can walk into the building by himself, excitedly exclaim “friends!” while walking towards his classroom, but when it comes to crossing the threshold, he won’t do it." [ar037-0054]
- "Parents always say "oh it will get better in a few weeks" i turn and say "its been going on 2 years"." [ar012-0086]

**But:** "Twice this week he ran ahead of me." [ar012-0060]

15 of 140 posts.

**But:** "My son is happy in the morning and asks to go see his new provider." [ar028-0115]
