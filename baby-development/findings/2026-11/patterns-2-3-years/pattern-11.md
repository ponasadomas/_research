# baby-development: pattern 11, Potty readiness and learning (children of 2 and 3 years)

**Written:** 2026-11-20 from baby-development/evidence/2026-11/age-reread/digest-13.md
**Reviewed by the owner:** not yet

## Limits

The source is a random sample of 20,000 Reddit posts from twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April to 24 October 2026, chosen without keywords; 3,811 were on-niche, including 1,340 about children of 2 and 3 years. This pattern contains 179 on-niche and 12 near posts, all read; each count is posts tagged by one reader, not people or market prevalence, and the four tags overlap. Merging forms across digest readers requires judgement; this single-digest section retains its distinct forms, with fictional and professional accounts labelled.

## On-niche posts

### They need potty learning to work across caregivers.

18 of 179 posts.

Success varies by caregiver or setting; prompts, pull-ups and adults following the routine also matter.

- "But he will not tell other people at all in the moment." [ar022-0068]
- "He doesn’t get upset whenever they take him to the potty like he does at home." [ar026-0047]

**But:** Some schools are supportive. "His school is wonderful and supportive but he’s so stubborn!" [ar055-0059]

### They want to recover lost potty progress.

17 of 179 posts.

They seek a way back after lost progress, with changes or painful experiences sometimes implicated and sometimes absent.

- "I’m DONE. If I have to clean up another accident I’m leaving her at an orphanage." [ar013-0064]
- "Is it normal to go backwards like this even if there are ZERO routine or life changes?" [ar018-0177]

**But:** A reason to go can still work. "But when its a "Hey use the potty so we can go to x" its right to the potty like it's his job." [ar072-0048]

### They struggle to fit starting potty training around family life.

15 of 179 posts.

Pregnancy, work, travel and another child complicate starting or restarting.

- "I honestly don't even know where to start with potty training." [ar077-0001]
- "However this morning he was given antibiotics for a newly developed ear infection." [ar032-0044]

**But:** Some already see success. "M son has used the potty a few random times. He wakes with dry diapers and is showing signs of readiness." [ar081-0002]

### They face a separate hurdle with potty poops.

15 of 179 posts.

They weigh allowing a poop diaper against painful holding, fear and losing pee progress.

- "he now consistently only poops while asleep." [ar065-0076]
- "Should we give him the diaper to poop in?" [ar049-0069]

**But:** Some willingly sit. "He will tell me when he feels poop coming & he’s happy to sit, but very little to no action." [ar026-0040]

### They keep meeting refusal despite trying several methods.

14 of 179 posts.

Rewards, equipment, naked time and breaks have not overcome fear, diaper preference or indifference.

- "But it’s getting discouraging that it’s been so long and so many attempts with literally no progress at all!" [ar028-0157]
- "I bought her an incentive basket with just a bunch of Target dollar tree items. She wants the items but won’t go potty." [ar079-0047]

**But:** Some already manage poop. "She is pretty good for poops and will usually poop in the potty." [ar075-0079]

### They cannot get their child to release on the potty.

13 of 179 posts.

Awareness, sitting and hours of holding do not ensure release.

- "After trying a new method that didn't work she now knows that she can hold her pee/poop from when she wakes up until she gets a nighttime diaper 12 hours later." [ar050-0007]
- "The problem I’m running into, is she will sit on the potty for 2-3 minutes, not go, and then pee during the 10 minute break." [ar066-0146]

**But:** One restart succeeded. "Dont lose hope for some reason the 3 month break just made something click in her brain!" [ar002-0096]

### They face school deadlines for toilet independence.

12 of 179 posts.

School or care requires independence, sometimes including clothing and wiping.

- "The school requires kids to be “fully potty trained” and we got an email today saying if things don’t improve she might get kicked out." [ar007-0113]
- "We’re really trying to stay calm because she absolutely reads our stress, but the August deadline is real and some days it’s hard not to feel like we’re running out of time." [ar030-0024]

**But:** Some manage poops. "This time around he's actually pooping on the potty when he has to go!" [ar052-0049]

### They question whether to pause an overwhelming training attempt.

10 of 179 posts.

They question whether distress warrants persistence, a different method or a break.

- "I’m losing my patience and we have to go back to normal life soon—can’t just stay at home naked." [ar016-0036]
- "I don't want to give up potty training but am not sure this is the method." [ar003-0116]

**But:** Some see progress. "But today shes communicated that she has had to go to the potty" [ar005-0024]

### They keep cleaning poop from clothes or nap diapers.

9 of 179 posts.

Better pee use has not ended soiling or late poop announcements.

- "Recently she’s started waiting until it’s nap time and then poos in her diaper, takes the diaper off and shakes the poo out onto the floor." [ar080-0066]
- "Im at my wits end, and I don't know how to deal with this situation without yelling." [ar058-0103]

**But:** One finishes when caught. "When we do catch him before it all comes out we'll usher him to the bathroom and he'll happily finish up on the toilet and loves to show off his poo in the toilet." [ar020-0051]

### They want toileting to fit outings and daily routines.

8 of 179 posts.

Outings complicate toileting; potty requests can also delay transitions.

- "If he doesn’t want to do something, he has learned he can delay it by up to half an hour by sitting on the potty." [ar034-0045]
- "It's not uncommon for stretches where we're unable to exit the freeway and would feel unsafe pulling over." [ar039-0039]

**But:** Training can go well. "Just started potty training with our 2.5 year old. It’s going pretty well." [ar057-0080]

### They struggle with painful holding and repeated cleanup.

7 of 179 posts.

Pain, leaks and repeated wiping dominate daily life; medical treatment has helped some and left others struggling.

- "It’s now affecting her mood, our routines, daycare, outings — basically our entire daily life." [ar030-0128]
- "My Son (3y.o) has held his poo in for 6 days as he didn't want to go in the potty." [ar019-0115]

**But:** Softening need not remove fear. "He’s also prescribed a laxative which is making her poops loose but isn’t helping with the fear itself." [ar017-0087]

### They want dry sleep without exhausting night routines.

7 of 179 posts.

They question readiness and night training while weighing wet bedding, preschool rules and disrupted sleep.

- "I’ve heard controlling it overnight is a developmental thing and then I’ve also heard people train overnight like it’s nothing." [ar041-0110]
- "It was hell for everyone, kid was miserable and exhausted and I was too." [ar037-0173]

**But:** Some had dry nights. "My now 3 year old, fully potty trained and has not wet the bed in over 5 months, has been bed wetting over the last 3 days." [ar028-0088]

### They worry their reactions or delays hurt potty learning.

6 of 179 posts.

They describe anxiety, delay or anger, including yelling and spanking in wider parenting.

- "I lost my cool and screamed at him that he was being a baby and that if he wants to poop in his underwear then it’s time to go back to diapers. I took his ruined underwear and threw in the trash in front of him." [ar007-0061]
- "I don’t want to fail him." [ar052-0080]

**But:** Spanking is not necessarily for accidents. "I do want to clarify that I don’t spank my child over potty accidents." [ar080-0024]

### They lose naked potty success when clothes go on.

5 of 179 posts.

They cannot carry naked success into underwear or trousers despite practice and help.

- "I’ve also tried the commando method in loose shorts. Same story, instantly pees in them ." [ar061-0101]
- "I take them half off and leave the other half for her to do but she just walks around with her pants at her ankles like it’s fun for her 🙃" [ar059-0134]

**But:** Practice is not always enough. "I have demonstrated pulling down pants/underwear and we practice pulling them up and down so not sure it is an understanding issue or something else." [ar017-0021]

### They want their child to stop playing before accidents.

5 of 179 posts.

They want their child to recognise the urge and interrupt play without relying on adult prompts.

- "The goal is intrinsic body awareness and self-initiation — not just compliance with external prompting." [ar080-0034]
- "If he's trying to do a puzzle, watching a show, playing a game, he will absolutely pee himself rather than stop to.go potty." [ar051-0067]

**But:** Some achieve substantial success. "He’s peeing in the toilet about 70% of the time." [ar038-0158]

### They want public toilet visits without panic.

5 of 179 posts.

Noise or a new fear makes public bathrooms difficult despite success elsewhere.

- "She says they are too noisy - fair enough, I understand the acoustics bother their little ears." [ar012-0032]
- "No amount of bribing or encouraging will work." [ar030-0085]

**But:** Home toilets can be acceptable. "She uses toilets happily in any home including ones she's never seen before, as well as at nursery." [ar041-0013]

### They question how much toileting help to give.

4 of 179 posts.

They ask how much wiping or nighttime bathroom access they should still manage.

- "He wants to wipe his own bum. Which like…great. But it grosses me out and is so messy lol" [ar084-0022]
- "now it’s more relaxed and she’s pooping at school but coming home with very dirty underwear." [ar083-0056]

**But:** One knows the steps. "Yea I showed him how. He knows what to do. But gets a little wild with the toilet paper once it has poop lol" [ar084-0031]

### They want potty teaching that suits their child’s communication.

4 of 179 posts.

They ask how to teach toileting with limited speech or autism, without assuming diaper awareness proves readiness.

- "I’m worried about how not communicating in full sentences yet would affect potty training?" [ar066-0092]
- "He has autism, and doesn’t speak." [ar028-0152]

**But:** Some already understand. "he has an extensive vocabulary and is now starting to put two words together, he has knowledge and understanding." [ar066-0092]

### They share progress after adapting their approach.

3 of 179 posts.

Reducing pressure, changing equipment or playful stories brought progress.

- "We then switched to wearing pull-ups and sitting on the potty every 30 mins this worked way better for all of us." [ar057-0104]
- "By himself, he took himself and pooped ON THE POTTY." [ar055-0114]

**But:** One still has a poop hurdle. "I will say we do still need to figure out pooping on the potty. He's pretty freaked out about that." [ar076-0101]

### Other: they offer satire or professional criticism about potty learning.

2 of 179 posts.

One account is explicit satire with fictional spending, sexist jokes and AI dialogue; the other is a preschool worker’s criticism.

- "My wife is a woman after all, and women are notoriously bad at science." [ar020-0038]
- "I’m talking about parents who don’t even think to or put actual effort into potty training until their kid is pushing 4 and able to fully tell them that they pooped and need changed." [ar027-0100]

**But:** The worker names exceptions. "Disclaimer: I KNOW some kids are just stubborn and/or have developmental delays or disabilities. I’m not talking about those cases!!!" [ar027-0099]

## Near posts

### They want sleep back during toileting and other changes.

3 of 12 posts.

Sleep disruption accompanies bottle removal, travel or toilet learning; these accounts do not establish that training caused it.

- "Ever since taking the bottle away two weeks ago he has been waking up every single night at around 1-2 am." [ar019-0059]
- "At 4am my daughter always woke up for pee, well it's actually great but on the other side, we are walking like a zombie lol." [ar004-0116]

**But:** Early waking can accompany success. "Everything is good now but there is still something that me and my wife have to struggle a bit." [ar004-0115]

### They promote expert potty advice and support resources.

2 of 12 posts.

An announcement and professional AMA promote Jamie Glowacki’s resources; children’s ages are unknown.

- "This is your opportunity to ask questions directly to Jamie and get her insights on potty training, toddlers and surviving early parenting!" [ar007-0075]
- "When asking, please include your child’s age, how long you’ve been training, and what you think the main issue is." [ar002-0142]

**But:** Difficulty does not establish bad parenting. "Quick reminder: potty training is not a reflection of your parenting." [ar002-0143]

### They want bowel relief before potty learning.

2 of 12 posts.

Bowel problems postpone potty learning and constrain family life despite treatment, with hypotonia described in one account.

- "We won't ever get this time back with our sweet girl, and we're spending so much of it at home because being out is a nightmare so often." [ar009-0074]
- "Pooping = hurt = diaper changes = rash." [ar009-0078]

**But:** Pain is not universal. "She doesn't seem to be in any pain, it is honestly just a quality of life issue at this point" [ar012-0141]

### Fathers question which public restroom to enter with daughters.

2 of 12 posts.

They want safe help and to avoid conflict over restroom etiquette.

- "My wife suggested that I start taking her into the women's toilets and I want to be able to, I'm just not entirely sure if there's an etiquette to be followed when doing so" [ar047-0069]
- "But I don't want any confrontation from other women." [ar037-0194]

**But:** One situation remains anticipated. "This hasn't happened yet, but it likely will at some point." [ar037-0193]

### A mother seeks reassurance about a male teacher’s toileting duties.

1 of 12 posts.

A mother in trauma therapy links her fear to childhood sexual abuse.

- "Due to my past history of childhood SA, I immediately felt anxious about this. I started imagining the worst." [ar051-0054]
- "My mom says I need to switch daycares, although, I know her reaction is based on her own trauma and mine." [ar051-0057]

**But:** She reports no daycare complaints. "I’ve had no complaints. A lot of the employees have their kids enrolled so it always felt like a safe environment." [ar051-0051]

### A parent wants overnight protection against soaked bedding.

1 of 12 posts.

A parent seeks overnight protection after repeated soaked mornings and hopes for future potty readiness.

- "I’m so tired of washing the sheets and mattress cover." [ar061-0103]
- "I’m using Millie moon overnight diapers. I’ve tried honest and those are even worse…." [ar061-0103]

**But:** nothing found against it.

### A parent fears retrying after misjudging potty readiness.

1 of 12 posts.

A parent fears another failed attempt after a book led them to misjudge readiness.

- "Let me make myself clear: the anxiety I’m experiencing over potty training is about ME, not my son." [ar003-0107]
- "My gut tells me my sweet boy is fully capable, but I am so worried/scared/anxious that I will fail him. 😓" [ar003-0108]

**But:** They now see new readiness signs. "I am seeing signs of readiness in my son now at 2.5 that I did not see when we attempted training back in the spring." [ar003-0108]

## Across the on-niche posts

### They try prompts, rewards and changes of approach.

171 of 179 posts.

Attempts include preparation, play, diapers, medical treatment and night routines; some also report angry responses.

- "We’ve tried just about everything: reward-based/incentivized training, and taking him to the bathroom on a schedule (every 20, 30, 60, even 90 minutes)." [ar012-0002]
- "For us, it was a "clean out" day of mirolax and exlax." [ar037-0105]

**But:** One changed approach felt better. "We then switched to wearing pull-ups and sitting on the potty every 30 mins this worked way better for all of us." [ar057-0104]

### They receive conflicting advice and demands about potty learning.

92 of 179 posts.

School, family and clinicians influence timing, methods and expectations.

- "daycare insists he's ready to potty train. They even said it would be easy because he doesn't like being dirty." [ar035-0116]
- "They said they haven’t seen signs he’s ready yet and they recommended waiting until Christmas break." [ar052-0078]

**But:** Advice can also help. "One person mentioned having a poop party and that was the route that I decided to go with and I’m glad I did." [ar055-0108]

### They buy potty equipment, clothing, incentives and diapers.

15 of 179 posts.

Fourteen report purchases; one tagged satirical post has fictional prices and spending.

- "I am sick of changing poopy diapers. I am sick of spending tons of money on these things." [ar071-0011]
- "I ordered a toilet seat to put on ours, boxers and pull ups for night time." [ar066-0091]

**But:** Equipment can go unused. "We’ve bought a potty watch with a timer, but we never got the chance to get in a good rhythm with it before we accidentally lost it (still looking)." [ar021-0104]

### They name frustration, anxiety and guilt alongside some optimism.

100 of 179 posts.

They fear harm or lost progress and feel relief when something works.

- "I feel shitty tbh because she is 3 now and feel like im doing her a disservice by not training her." [ar002-0095]
- "I feel trapped in the house fighting with him to use the potty." [ar032-0038]

**But:** Some feel optimistic. "I’m feeling really optimistic about it!" [ar048-0051]
