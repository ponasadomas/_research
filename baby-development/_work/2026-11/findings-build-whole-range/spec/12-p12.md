## The child has a known delay or is in therapy
@pattern 12

### One post in twenty-seven is by a parent whose child has a known delay or condition, or is already in therapy
@count pattern

Under 2: {U2} posts ({U2P}). At 2 and 3: {A23} ({A23P}). Under 2 the body counts as much as speech: head shape, a tight neck, late movement, feeding; at 2 and 3 it is mostly speech, and living with therapy.

- "I love my little boy so damn much but it wrecks me because I don’t know how to make this better." [sr026-0063]
- "I’m trying to make sure I’m supporting him without turning his entire childhood into therapy." [ar044-0113]

**But:** some report a turn, as after ear tubes.

@run u2

### Under 2, the known problem is often of the body: late movement, a tight neck, a helmet, feeding that does not work, serious medical news
@forms u2
Parents need workable movement and positioning practice at home.
Parents question helmet necessity and worry about wearing one.
Parents need solids to become a workable source of nutrition.
Parents want stories of delayed movement eventually improving.
Parents face uncertain futures after serious medical news.
Parents want clearer instructions and reassurance about torticollis.
Parents need milk feeding to work despite oral difficulties.
Other concerns leave parents seeking explanations or next steps.
@end

- "All memories I make with him at this age will include the helmet." [sr049-0012]
- "She is in physical therapy but it hasn’t helped." [sr070-0089]

**But:** progress is uneven, not absent.
