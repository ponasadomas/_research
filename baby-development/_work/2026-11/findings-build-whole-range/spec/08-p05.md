## Am I harming the child's development by what I do?
@pattern 05

### One post in eighteen asks whether the parent's own choices are harming the child's development
@count pattern

Under 2: {U2} posts ({U2P}). At 2 and 3: {A23} ({A23P}). Under 2 the fear is of not doing enough for a baby, and of damage from crying; at 2 and 3 it is screens, angry words and spoiling.

- "Have I messed it up for him? I feel like I failed him." [sr126-0085]
- "I don’t want to mess it up." [ar011-0044]

**But:** one answers the fear herself. "The truth is that we don't control our children's development, we support it." [sr041-0054]

@run u2

### Under 2, parents fear lasting harm from what they did: crying left unanswered, anger, time apart, too much comforting, a seat, a feeding choice
@forms u2
They fear crying, scares or anger leave lasting harm.
They fear separation or changed care damages their bond.
They fear positioning or soothing harms physical development.
They fear comforting their child has created dependence.
They fear their words and example teach unwanted habits.
They fear feeding or health choices could damage development.
They fear multiple languages confuse or delay speech.
@end
