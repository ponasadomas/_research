## Limits

The niche is the development of a child from birth to 48 months. The posts are a random draw of 20,000 (seed 20261119, no keyword) from the 122,021 posts with text in twelve parenting subreddits, 1 April to 24 October 2026. Agents read each one and marked it yes (on the niche), near or no. The first reading wrongly stopped at 24 months and found 2,471 yes posts; on 2026-11-20 the 12,534 posts it had marked off, or near for age, were read again and gave 1,340 more: 3,811 yes, 8,476 near, 7,713 no. Each yes post has one main pattern out of 13. A pattern's count is out of the 3,811; a form's is out of its pattern's posts in one reading; a count under "Next to the niche" is out of the 8,476. "Parents" means whoever wrote the post.

- How common anything is among mothers or buyers: Reddit is not the market. People post more when things are bad, and many never buy.
- How many people: a count is a number of posts, each tagged by one reader. First-round coders agreed on the main pattern for 21 of 30 shared posts, and readers' yes rates ran from 5.3% to 20.8%. Patterns or bands a few points apart are equal.
- Whether a step at 24 months is real: "Under 2" is the first reading's 2,471 posts (46 give no age) and "At 2 and 3" is the second reading's 1,340 (1,317 about a child of 2 or 3, 19 about a younger child, 4 other). The readings had different readers and briefs, so a jump exactly at 24 months may be partly the reading. Percentages in a pattern's group are shares of these two sets.
- How firm the forms are: they were named by different readers and joined by judgement three times (digests, sections, this page), separately for the two ages. A form's count is softer than a pattern's; compare kinds across ages, not exact numbers.
- Children older than 48 months: 1,976 posts on the same subjects were marked near and not sorted into forms.
- Whether the writer is a mother: 3,117 of the 3,811 do not say.
- What the poster wrote later: 14,640 of the 20,000 posts were read the first time without the poster's own replies; the second reading kept no such list.
- What others answered: every reply by someone other than the poster is unread.
- The rest of the pool: 97,521 posts of the same subreddits are unread.
- What things cost: 138 of 3,811 posts mention paying, mostly without a price. The sections' "bought or paid" tags, set by other readers, sum to 173 and do not reconcile with the 138.
- How the four "across" counts of a group relate: they overlap, and "how they feel" counts any named feeling, pride and no worry included.
- What a quote proves: diagnoses, causes and a doctor's words are the poster's account. Posts a section marks as AI-assisted, promotional, fictional or second-hand are marked here too.
- Her vocabulary: no jargon glossary has been made for this niche.
