## Hard behaviour
@pattern 03

### One post in five is about hard behaviour: hitting, biting, screaming, meltdowns, refusing, fights over care
@count pattern

Under 2: {U2} posts ({U2P}). At 2 and 3: {A23} ({A23P}). Under 2 much of it is crying and a child who cannot yet be gentle; at 2 and 3 it is a child who refuses.

- "I would do anything for a gentle touch." [sr079-0026]
- "I feel like I’m constantly battling with my 3 year old lately." [ar071-0029]

**But:** many say the child means no harm and cannot yet be gentle.

@run u2

### Under 2, parents want crying, screaming and tantrums to ease, to be able to put the child down, and ask whether so young a child can take in a limit
@forms u2
They want relief and explanations for persistent crying.
They want to handle tantrums without worsening them.
They need to put their child down.
They want limits their young child can understand.
They connect frustration with emerging skills.
They need relief from screaming.
They want head-banging and hair-pulling to stop.
They need help with difficult behaviour before sleep.
@end

Crying and being unable to put the child down are 82 of the 170; that form has no match at 2 and 3.

- "We have literally never had a break." [sr106-0107]
- "Do they actually stop eventually?" [sr012-0172]

**But:** a new skill, such as signing, can ease it.

### Under 2, parents want nappy changes, washing, dressing, teeth, meals and car journeys without a fight
@forms u2
They want care routines without a fight.
They want manageable outings and car journeys.
They want meals without battles.
They want clean teeth without forcing the brush.
@end

- "I just feel like I’m waterboarding her every night." [sr131-0077]
- "I’m a single mum I can’t sit in the back to comfort him." [sr109-0143]

**But:** at daycare the same child may not resist.

### Under 2, parents want the child to stop hitting, biting and grabbing, at home and among other children
@forms u2
They want painful rough touch to stop.
They want children to share space and toys safely.
@end

- "When I pick him up he pinches me every single time." [sr079-0023]
- "She will come home with large bruises on her arms." [sr110-0053]

**But:** with adults the same child may share happily.

### Under 2, smaller forms of hard behaviour: the parent's own reactions, a child who climbs and eats everything, a big change
@small u2
They need relief from battles and their reactions. => frightened by their own reactions
They need to keep an exploring child safe. => a climbing, escaping, chewing child
They link harder behaviour to a major change. => after a move, a sibling, daycare or weaning
They question stories about toddler temperament. => doubting what toddlers are said to be like
They question managing another baby. => whether they could manage another baby
They ask whether behaviour will worsen. => whether it will get worse
Other parents describe swearing or moving a diaper aside. => swearing; a nappy pushed aside
@end

- "I crossed the line." [sr030-0020]

**But:** some hold their temper.

@run a23

### At 2 and 3, the child refuses: rules repeated ten times, and teeth, washing, dressing, meals and outings only by force or chase
@forms a23
The parent cannot get cooperation through repeated rules and choices.
The parent wants exact words and a repeatable response.
The parent struggles to complete care without distress or force.
The parent wants safe outings without chasing or physically removing the child.
The parent wants meals without constant prompting or destruction.
@end

Eighteen ask for exact words to say; two of those promote the writer's own resource.

- "It’s like a constant push and pull, roller coaster. EXHAUSTING." [ar003-0001]
- "Just now I pulled her back from walking in front of a school bus." [ar019-0005]

**But:** it sometimes works.

### At 2 and 3, parents want the child to stop hurting the parent, the baby sibling, pets and children at daycare, or want their own child kept safe there
@forms a23
The parent wants to prevent painful contact and harm to people or pets.
The parent wants siblings safe without rejecting the older child.
The parent wants the child to respect peers and remain included.
The parent wants adults to prevent repeated childcare injuries.
@end

- "Last night he was kicking his brother and when we gently told him no kicking, he head butted the baby." [ar025-0043]
- "He recently started touching other kids private parts." [ar035-0138]

**But:** as under 2, it is often not anger.

The parent wants relief from loud, rude or hurtful speech.
The parent needs a break from constant activity and attention demands.

### At 2 and 3, smaller forms of hard behaviour: other adults, habits, screens, wanting professional help
@small a23
The parent wants other adults to change their response. => wanting another adult to respond differently
The parent wants repetitive habits to stop damaging the child’s body or clothing. => nail biting and picking (one post is about a 5-month-old)
The parent wants repeated licking, spitting and throwing to stop. => licking, spitting, throwing
The parent wants professional help beyond their current tools. => wanting professional help
The parent struggles when the child rejects one caregiver and demands another. => the child refuses one parent
The parent wants fewer screen demands after screens helped them cope. => demands for a screen
The parent wants comfort without painful touching or pacifier dependence. => comfort habits that hurt the parent
Other: the parent questions illness, a disputed hitting account or hidden supplies. => other
The parent wants to reassure a child frightened of flooding after a cartoon. => fear of flooding after a cartoon
@end

- "He constantly tells me, in front of our kids, that my job is to be loving and nurturing and it's his job to be the disciplinarian." [ar047-0003]

**But:** nothing found against it.
