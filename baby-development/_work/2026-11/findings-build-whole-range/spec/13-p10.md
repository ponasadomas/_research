## Other people interfere
@pattern 10

### One post in forty-two is mainly about other people interfering in how the child is raised
@count pattern

Under 2: {U2} posts ({U2P}). At 2 and 3: {A23} ({A23P}). Under 2 others overrule how a baby is soothed, fed and put to sleep; at 2 and 3 they shame the child's behaviour and dispute discipline and what the family teaches.

- "My sister then accused me of letting her “get her way” and eventually called my daughter a “fucking cry baby” while she was standing right there." [ar001-0134]

**But:** the same relative is often called kind and helpful.
