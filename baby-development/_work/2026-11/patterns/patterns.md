# Patterns, draft 1 (2026-11-18)

Drafted by the supervisor from the first reading's one-line descriptions of 192 on-niche
posts. Each pattern is what the post is about, from the parent's side.

| # | Pattern | Belongs here | Does not belong here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is this normal, or is something wrong with my child? | One or more specific things the child does or does not do yet (moving, talking, responding, body, behaviour), and the parent asks whether it is normal, late or a sign of a condition | The child already has a diagnosis or is in therapy (12) |
| 2 | What do I do with the child all day? | Boredom, long awake hours, running out of ideas, activities, outings, play at home, how to fill the day | Asking which thing to buy (4) |
| 3 | Hard behaviour | Tantrums, meltdowns, hitting, biting, screaming, scratching, throwing, climbing, testing limits, and how to respond | The parent asks whether the behaviour means a condition (1) |
| 4 | What should I get for this age? | Choosing or asking about toys, books, walkers, shoes, furniture, gear or gifts for the child's stage | |
| 5 | Am I harming the child's development by what I do? | Guilt or fear that the parent's own choices (holding, contact naps, letting cry, screens, too much or too little play, daycare, work) damage the child | Worry about the child with no link to the parent's own doing (1) |
| 6 | The child prefers someone else | Preference for the other parent, a grandparent or a nanny; rejection; worry about the bond | |
| 7 | Learning to eat and drink as a skill | Chewing, finger foods, self-feeding, cups, cutlery, gagging while learning | Nutrition, amounts, allergies, milk, weight |
| 8 | Delight or pride in what the child now does | Sharing a new skill, a funny habit, joy in the child's growth | |
| 9 | Classes, groups, other children, daycare as a place to grow | Baby groups, swimming, playdates, shyness with other children, what daycare does for the child | Daycare as logistics or cost |
| 10 | Other people interfere | Grandparents, partners or others pushing, criticising or undermining on screens, toys, milestones, play or routines | |
| 11 | Potty readiness and learning | When to start, whether the child is ready, setbacks | Nappy products, rashes |
| 12 | The child has a known delay or is in therapy | Diagnosis, early intervention, therapy, and the search for answers or progress | |
| 0 | None of these | Say what it is about in `other_what` | |
