All 150 assigned files were read in order, including the author's archived replies where supplied. The ledger has 23 yes, 41 near and 86 no posts. One no row (1ta3vyq) is unavailable: only a Reddit removal notice survives, so its subject cannot be judged. Thus 149 files contain substantive post text. Nothing assigned remains unread. In 49 files, the archive explicitly says replies were not fetched; those missing replies were unavailable to this reading. There are 192 quotes from 37 posts: all 23 yes posts and 14 near posts explicitly tied to development or skills.

Hard behaviour recurred most: pattern 3 appears in 20 posts, including eight yes posts. Parents described repeated crying, biting, hitting, head banging and correction that seemed ineffective. Filling time or activities appeared in 14 posts, and choosing stage-related gear or play equipment in 13; each includes four yes posts. Specific normality or condition concerns appeared in six posts, five yes. These are overlapping counts of posts in this assigned share of the supplied random sample, not counts of quotes or estimates about parents generally.

The range of experiences stood out. One parent described spanking and regretting it; another described self-hitting during a sleep regression. Both are retained. There were also first drawings, playful crawling chases and three motor skills arriving within a day. Buying or constructing something did not ensure the expected play: one child preferred balancing on the playground boundary wall.

Age was the main uncertainty: 18 near posts give no age, and five say only two years old. School and preschool wording can suggest an older child without establishing months; those uncertainties are recorded. Other near borders were sleep (6), parent state (5), feeding (3), logistics (3) and other (1). Ordinary sleep-only, medical-only and equipment-only posts were excluded unless they also supplied relevant abilities, behaviour or activities. Ownership, booking or enrollment alone was not treated as explicit payment. Two near posts describing an app or vitamins may be promotional, but that provenance is unverified and they remain included with notes.
