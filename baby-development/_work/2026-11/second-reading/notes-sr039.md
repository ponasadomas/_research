# Reader 039 — second reading

Read all 150 assigned files in share order, including every archived author reply present. This share comes from the supervisor’s random sample; I did not select by topic. All files were available; none remain unread. They date from 21–29 May 2026. Results: **16 yes, 49 near, 85 no; 113 quotes from 28 posts** (all 16 yes and 12 near with developmental ties).

Among yes posts, normality questions appeared in 7; filling the day and hard behaviour each in 5; worries about the parent’s own choices in 4. Patterns overlap. Parents described lost independent play, outings curtailed by crying, inconsolable newborns, eye-contact changes, body exploration, chewing and heavier babies’ motor milestones. Two yes posts used pattern 0: babyproofing before crawling and coping with loud noises. One yes post centred on delight in smiles, babbling and playful greetings.

Sleep was a recurring boundary: 13 near posts were mainly about sleep, including regressions, rolling-related swaddle decisions and milestones disrupting sleep. Other near reasons: parent_state 17, age_unknown 10, age_over_24 4, other 3, logistics 2. Only one included post explicitly described payment: babysitting. Owning gear or seeing a therapist alone did not establish payment.

The contrasts stood out: progress made sleep harder; time away coexisted with enjoying new smiles; affectionate rituals appeared alongside severe distress. Eligible distress and body-exploration passages were retained without softening.

Uncertainty concerned age and incidental details. Sick-child play, a toddler naming a tattoo and a younger sibling colouring were kept near. “Two years old” and “not even 4” without exact ages were kept near; the harness child may be older. Motherhood required the author’s wording; “new_mother” for an identified infant’s mother does not establish first-time parenthood. The final prematurity post was marked no: corrected age and general outcomes were given, but no specific developmental subject. That exclusion merits supervisor review.
