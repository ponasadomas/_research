# Reader 010 — age reread

Read all **150 posts**, in share order, including their authors' archived replies. Nothing remains unread or unavailable. This is one share of the supplied random sample from twelve parenting subreddits, April–October 2026. No network or additional niche material was used.

**13 yes, 35 near, 102 no; 142 quotes from 22 posts.** All yes posts and nine near posts with an explicit development/skills link are quoted. Counts below count posts, not quotes; patterns overlap.

Every yes post concerns a two- or three-year-old: seven and six respectively. Hard behaviour occurs in five; social opportunities or learning with other children in three. Potty learning, parent preference, delight in abilities, and filling the day each occur in two; normality worries in one. One post falls outside the numbered patterns: genital touching and public/private boundaries. The pride posts concern storybook empathy and standing up to bullies. No yes post concerns eating skills or a known developmental delay. Surprisingly, none concerns an infant, despite many infant posts about sleep, medical symptoms, feeding amounts and postpartum life.

Near reasons: **24 age_over_48, 3 age_unknown, 3 parent_state, 3 logistics, 2 sleep**. Older-child posts include friendship, literacy, screen rules, anxiety and school behaviour. These are separate from the on-niche count.

Uncertain boundaries are documented in the ledger. “Four years old” reaches the fourth-birthday cutoff. “3–4 year old” crosses it without identifying the exact age, so is near/age_unknown. Second grade establishes an older child without a numerical age. Some mixed-age households have no dominant child. Genital touching is coded zero rather than assumed to be a diagnostic worry. Trying or possessing something does not establish payment. An invented bedtime-story passage is retained and labelled fictional.
