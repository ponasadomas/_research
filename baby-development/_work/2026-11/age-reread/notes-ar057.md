# Reader 057 — age reread

Read all 150 assigned post files, including every saved author reply, in share order. This is one share of the random sample of posts with text from twelve parenting subreddits, April–October 2026; no keyword selection was applied by this reader. Results: **14 yes, 38 near, 98 no; 123 quotes**. Nothing remains unread or unavailable at the post-file level. Some source files say author replies were not fetched; those replies were not available to read.

All 14 yes posts concern two-year-olds (9 posts) or three-year-olds (5). Hard behaviour appeared in 10, worry about the effects of the parent's choices in 6, peer/group experiences in 5, others interfering in 4, and potty learning in 4. These tags overlap. Tantrums around transitions, demands for help followed by refusal, and uncertainty about responding without teaching the wrong lesson recur. Parents describe warnings, distraction, consequences and physical removal; some report better results with a visible timer or a less stressful potty method. Drawing and imaginative play appear alongside delight and, in one post, severe tantrums and disrupted sleep attributed to a possible leap.

The 38 near posts comprise 21 over-age, 4 age-unknown, 4 feeding, 3 parent-state, 3 other-topic, 2 logistics and 1 sleep case. Seven near posts explicitly connect to skills, growth, neurodivergence, brain effects or attachment and have quotes. The adult sibling caring for a ten-year-old and the education-research solicitation were unexpected; the latter's reported parental concerns are labelled second-hand. The nail-biting post explicitly reports buying deterrent and coloured polish. Product possession, enrollment and professional contact alone were not treated as proof of payment.

Borderline calls: four-year-olds were placed near/age_over_48 because the cutoff is the fourth birthday; no exact birthday was supplied. Shared posts about older and younger siblings use the older child's age when neither dominates. Second grade establishes an older child, but its numerical-age stage remains unknown. Speech-therapy information in an iron-food post made it developmental despite its feeding focus. Constipation, teething, rashes and ordinary sleep complaints stayed no without a relevant skill or behaviour issue. Generic descriptions such as an “easy” baby, daycare going well, or guilt about a younger daycare start did not alone establish a developmental subject. Counts describe sampled posts, not people or market prevalence.
