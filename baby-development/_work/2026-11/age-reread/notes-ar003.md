# Reader 003 — age reread

Read all 150 assigned post files, including every archived author reply supplied in each file. This share belongs to the random, non-keyword sample of text posts from twelve parenting subreddits, April–October 2026. Nothing in the share remains unread. These are counts of posts in this share, not people or market prevalence: **16 yes, 31 near, 103 no**. Extracted **123 quotes from 22 posts**: every yes post and six near posts explicitly tied to development or skills.

Hard behaviour recurred most among the yes posts: **11 of 16** showed pattern 3. Parents described hitting, biting, pushing, refusing directions, running away and screen-related tantrums. Several had already tried multiple responses without success: separating children, removing toys, timeouts, time-ins, naming emotions, redirecting, withholding screens and, in some accounts, spanking. One parent described a successful boundary-plus-alternatives approach. These are reported experiences, not evidence that an approach works generally. Four yes posts showed activities/day-filling, four groups or peer settings, three normality concerns and two potty learning; tags overlap.

What surprised me was how often apparently practical requests contained a larger problem: screen demands followed simultaneous family medical emergencies, potty accidents confined an outdoor-loving child indoors, and peer aggression complicated plans to return to work. Positive material included affectionate and funny toddler behaviour and a well-used gifted magazine subscription. Only **two yes/near posts explicitly said they bought something**: toy cars, and books/toys/Yoto intended to replace screens. Reading a book, receiving a gift or saying “got” without explicit buying was not counted as payment.

The 31 near posts comprise **19 age_over_48, three parent_state, two sleep, two logistics, two age_unknown, two other and one feeding**. Stated four-year-olds were treated as past the 48-month boundary unless an exact qualifying age was supplied. Mixed-age posts follow their main child; joint discussions are noted where that choice is uncertain. K–12 and intended adoption ages 4–8 were retained as older-child material. The youngest stated child supplies the stage for the joint eight-/ten-year-old holiday-preference post.

Judgment calls: public-tantrum shame (1v2iy67) and potty-training anxiety (1w59htv) centre on the parent, so are near; the latter explicitly discusses readiness and capability and therefore has quotes. Risky playground supervision (1urdqao) centres on the older children described. The shared Gibran poem (1uk463n) is labelled literary material, not firsthand child evidence; the financial-learning project (1vmb2yw) has stated commercial intent. Age-appropriate clothing alone, tooth eruption incidental to toothpaste choice, health symptoms and sleep-only posts were no. Development ties were not inferred merely from “normal,” a parenting question or a child's age.
