# Reader 041 — age reread

Read all 150 assigned post files, including all available author replies, in share order. This is one share of the supplied random sample of text posts from twelve parenting subreddits, April–October 2026; no keyword screening was used. Nothing in the share remains unread. Some files say replies were not fetched: these results cover the archived text available.

Marks: **15 yes, 43 near, 92 no**. Extracted **150 quotes from 33 posts**: all 15 yes posts and the 18 near posts with explicit developmental or skill links. The 43 near reasons were 24 age_over_48, 10 age_unknown, 3 sleep, 2 feeding, 2 parent_state, 1 logistics and 1 other.

Potty learning and difficult behaviour recurred: each appeared in 4 yes posts. Worry about the parent's own choices appeared in 3; normality concerns, home activities and peer interaction each appeared in 2. These are overlapping post counts, not quote counts. Four yes posts mainly concerned potty learning; two each mainly concerned normality, activities and hard behaviour. The remaining main subjects were equipment, peer interaction, delight, others' interference and emotional processing after witnessing physical punishment.

What surprised me was that all 15 yes posts concerned two-year-olds (9) or three-year-olds (6). Infant posts in this share mainly concerned health, milk, sleep, travel or parents' circumstances. The qualifying material included pleasure and humour as well as distress: a requested train song, a grandmother's unintended rude gesture, and a rock up a toddler's nose. The witnessed-hitting post described reenactment in play and nightmares; it is retained under pattern 0 because none of the named patterns adequately describes processing witnessed punishment.

The least certain age decisions were the first-bike question (1u617ot), which asks about two-year-olds without explicitly stating the author's child's age, and mouth breathing in children aged two and four (1vhwtx8), with no main child identified. I marked these near/age_unknown and near/age_over_48 respectively. Fourth birthdays already passed were treated as outside yes. Where several children had equal emphasis, the ledger notes this. Fictional TV behaviour and second-hand accounts are labelled.

No included author explicitly said they bought or paid for a relevant intervention or item. Owning equipment, reading books or attending daycare alone was not treated as purchase evidence. These counts describe this share's posts, not people or market prevalence.
