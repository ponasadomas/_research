# Reader 050 — age reread

Read all 150 assigned post files in share order, including every archived original-poster reply supplied in those files. This is one share of the supervisor's random sample of text posts across twelve parenting subreddits, April–October 2026; I did not select by keywords. No files were unavailable and none remain unread. Marks: **14 yes, 46 near, 90 no**. Pulled **105 quotes**, retaining original wording, spelling and punctuation.

Among the 14 yes posts, potty readiness/training appeared in 5, groups/daycare/school as a place to grow in 4, hard behaviour in 4, and normality/condition questions in 3. These overlap and count posts, not quotes. Parents repeatedly wanted help deciding what to do next: start or pause potty training, respond to a daycare report, prepare for preschool, or handle repeated scratching, nose picking and throwing. The preschool-skills post was unusually concrete about toilet independence, lunchboxes, language, utensils and emotional regulation. One parent's question about colourblindness also stated that the almost-three-year-old was nonverbal, without stating a diagnosis or therapy.

The 46 near posts divide into 25 age_over_48, 7 age_unknown, 6 parent_state, 4 logistics, 2 feeding and 2 sleep. Older children's accounts included gifted learning, swimming fears, emotional regulation and domino play. Parent exhaustion often appeared alongside a child's activities or growing mobility, but 90 posts concerned adult matters, pregnancy, health symptoms, feeding equipment or sleep without qualifying developmental content. These share counts describe posts here, not parents or market prevalence.

The main uncertainties were the fourth-birthday cutoff, unspecified ages in school posts, and symptoms versus behaviour. I treated stated four-year-olds as beyond the birth-to-fourth-birthday span. Middle school and kindergarten without numerical ages remain age_unknown; I did not assign ages from school level. Repeated thigh scratching is yes because stopping the behaviour is central, while noting eczema as a possible cause. Ordinary night waking and teething relief are no. Financial capacity to attend Montessori and acquiring clothing do not establish explicit payment; the domino purchase does. The wedding post states a prospective age, recorded as such. School-website curriculum passages reproduced by an author are labelled second-hand in quote context.
