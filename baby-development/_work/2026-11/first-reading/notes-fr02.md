## What surprised me

Joy accompanies worry: a movement delights its parent, delayed rolling resolves, independent play increases, and pronunciation is funny. Development also crosses into family logistics: screens replace an adult's response, mobility creates sleep-safety worries, and limited speech complicates understanding distress or potty readiness.
