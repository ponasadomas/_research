# Reader 04: first reading of baby-development

Read all **300** files in `reader-04.tsv`, including every supplied author reply. **31 yes, 67 near, 202 no; 597 quotes from 98 posts.** Nothing remains unread or unavailable. This is one share of the supplied random 1,500-post sample of text posts, April–October 2026, without keyword selection. Counts below are posts, not quotes or people; they describe this share, not the parenting market. The 67 near posts are an uncertainty set, not 67 additional confirmed niche posts.

The repeated tension is whether the parent is doing enough, doing too much, or interpreting the child correctly. Across yes and near, **20 posts** have a `parent_exhaustion` tag, **18** `parent_guilt`, and **11** `comparison_pressure`. These overlapping counts deduplicate post ids across quotes. Even among the 31 yes posts alone, eight contain parent guilt. Comparison includes children seen at classes, relatives, friends’ posts and online videos. Some parents blame their own interaction, feeding pressure or lack of activities for a difficulty; others wonder whether boredom explains behavior.

**Twelve posts** chiefly concern activities, play, sources for ideas or outings. Their practical problem is often keeping the child interested while also cooking, cleaning or recovering some personal time. Independent exploration can itself provoke guilt. One seven-month activity post lists many unsuccessful attempts and later reports a small success with sofa cushions (`1u6y1tp`). Another describes plentiful movement and shared games yet worries about missing directed teaching (`1w700bb`). Use or consideration of toys, apps, classes and equipment is common; it must not be silently treated as a purchase. The clearly stated buggy purchase also describes its abandonment.

Development is not only worry about delay. Parents enjoy reciprocal cooing, speech, affection, independence and children learning from one another. Pride in advanced abilities can coexist with uncertainty about meeting the child’s needs. At the other end, a fourteen-month child’s multiple delays, limited therapy progress and difficulty obtaining further investigation are central (`1uk78al`). Feeding learning produced four principal posts, including cutlery and the move to solids; movement produced five, including walking alignment, crawling surfaces and falls. These are primary-subject counts; related details appear elsewhere.

Among the **202 no posts**, the largest primary groups were **47 about explicitly older children**, **25 about sleep without a clear developmental connection**, **23 about parental wellbeing**, **19 medical concerns**, **15 product/travel logistics**, and **15 relationships**. The other 58 cover pregnancy, family size, employment and childcare, general parenting, feeding, routine care and unrelated matters. One very terse post (`1tojpwc`) supplies insufficient context to establish relevance. A friend’s child report (`1sioicy`) does not establish that the author cares for that child.

The difficult boundary was sleep, feeding, health and parental distress. **Twenty-one near posts** chiefly concern sleep or sleep equipment but explicitly connect to development, movement, independence or separation. Ten near posts give only an unqualified age of two years; exact months could put them outside 24 months. Other uncertainties include unstated ages, future age only, broad education questions and mixed-age siblings. Newly turned two was included; an explicitly older child was excluded even when infancy was recalled. Reasons are in `unsure_why`. Exact stated ages and self-described roles are retained in the ledger; unknown details stay unknown.

Provenance matters: `1x1fej6` is commercial self-promotion, retained and labelled; `fr04-0290` reports advice from Claude and is labelled second-hand AI advice. Medical explanations and vaccine fears are the authors’ claims. Distress, regret, head banging, rough handling and other difficult material were retained when in scope. Routine thanks, empty headings and bare links were not extracted as substantive passages. This reading covers the supplied text, not unseen attachments.

Coverage, ids, allowed values, per-post quote totals, exact short phrases and every quote passage were checked by script against the assigned files. No network, git, other readers’ outputs or earlier niche research were used. Only the three assigned output files were written.
