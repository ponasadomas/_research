# Reader 03 — first reading

Read all 300 assigned post files, including every archived reply by the original author. Nothing unavailable or left unread. This is one share of the brief’s random 1,500-post sample from twelve parenting subreddits, April–October 2026; no keyword selected these posts. I used no network, older niche material, product framing, or other readers’ work.

**Result:** 42 yes (14.0%), 80 near (26.7%), 178 no (59.3%); 782 quote records. These are post counts, not people or market prevalence. The near posts are a separate boundary group, not another 80 definite matches. Ages as stated are included in `wants`, since the requested ledger has no age column. `stage` stays unknown where only an unspecified toddler age or future age is given. Mother/experience labels require explicit supporting statements; subreddit, partner, pregnancy or breastfeeding alone did not determine them.

What recurred among yes and near posts (overlapping counts from the quote themes, counting each post once):

- **Sleep, naps and settling: 46 posts, 40 near and 6 yes.** Parents ask whether to help or wait for maturation, fear creating habits, and compare with age-based expectations. “behind in "independent" sleep” and “sleep is apart of development” make the developmental framing explicit. Most remain near because sleep dominates rather than waking skills or play.
- **Guilt, overwhelm, burnout, exhaustion, distress or self-doubt: 29 posts, 9 yes and 20 near.** Time with baby competes with housework, work, recovery and rest. Some parents describe a choice in which resting, chores or independent play each produces guilt. This counts those named themes, not every negative feeling.
- **Movement and motor abilities: 23 posts, 11 yes and 12 near.** Walking, head control, crawling, rolling, climbing and reflexes appear as worries, pleasures and practical changes. Some near posts mention a skill only incidentally. A reluctant 20-month-old walker has assessments and PT but limited local access; a seven-week-old’s head-control concern intensifies after a health visitor’s referral.
- **Caregiver preference or bonding: 10 posts, split 5 yes and 5 near.** Affection can feel like a judgment on caregiving effort. Daycare, grandparents and nannies can be support or feared competition. Fathers and a teenage brother also ask about connection; these are not all mothers’ posts.

Ordinary activities were as revealing as formal milestones: reading choices, cooking toys, chores, bath play, library groups and keeping an alert baby interested. I was struck by how mobility could make a baby happier while making outings or sleep harder. Pleasure appears in unprompted helping, funny words, kisses and exploration. Buying intentions are distinguished from completed purchases; having equipment does not establish its price or that the author paid.

The 178 no posts were primarily about older children (63), adult relationships/wellbeing/employment/household strain (52), pregnancy/birth or future-family planning (23), and child illness/injury/safety (13). The remaining 27 covered purchases, finances, birthdays, general reflections, jokes and other topics. These are mutually exclusive descriptive groups. Older-child posts stayed no even when recalling infancy. Sensitive subjects occurred in both included and excluded posts; fit and age determined the mark.

The main uncertainties are sleep, feeding acceptance versus developmental feeding skills, equipment or outings without an explicit developmental aim, and toddler ages possibly above 24 months. Nineteen near posts have unknown stage, and one looks back without ages. Each near row explains its reason. Standalone illness and adult relationship complaints stayed no; an explicit link to skills, interaction or daily time could make a similar post near. The first-Christmas toy post is yes with unknown stage: birth month and future infant age establish a baby, but current age is not directly stated.

**Checks:** all 300 share IDs appear exactly once; no extra posts; quote counts agree with the ledger; all 782 IDs are unique and sequential; every quote part occurs verbatim in its assigned raw file, with matching source metadata. All `her_words` phrases also match exactly. No usernames were added. This is a first reading for review, not finished findings.
