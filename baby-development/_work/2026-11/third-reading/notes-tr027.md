# Reader 027 — third reading

Read all 150 assigned post files, including every archived author reply supplied in them, in share order. Nothing remains unread or unavailable. These are one reader's share of the stated random sample of text posts from twelve parenting subreddits, April–October 2026; I made no keyword selection. Marks: **17 yes, 48 near, 85 no**. Extracted **183 quotes from 43 posts**: all 17 yes posts and 26 near posts with an explicit development or skill link.

Among the 17 yes posts, hard behaviour and normality questions each appeared in 5 posts; worries about the parent's own choices and daycare/social experiences each appeared in 3. Toy/book choice, caregiver preference, delight in growth, and known delay/therapy each appeared in 2; interference and activity ideas each appeared in 1. Tags overlap. Recurring experiences included a baby protesting chores, biting after becoming mobile, crying through daycare integration, watching speech and walking milestones, and feeling rejected despite doing much of the care. The five-week-old's parent directly feared insufficient interaction would delay milestones. Two posts described physical or occupational therapy, including daycare pressure to practise sitting despite ongoing PT.

The near group was dominated by sleep (22 posts), followed by age unknown (11), feeding (7), parent state (4), logistics (2), possibly over 24 months (1), and other (1). Across all 65 yes/near posts, 24 needed pattern 0, often for sleep changes linked to rolling, crawling, self-settling or a named regression. One included post explicitly reported paying: the dissatisfied sleep-trainer customer. Owning gear, using branded products, daycare attendance and treatment were not counted as purchases without a purchase/payment statement.

The main uncertainties were unstated toddler ages and the boundary between bottle refusal and a feeding skill: ordinary refusal stayed near without a development link; explicitly restoring bottle-feeding ability after tie revision was near with a skill link. Bare “two year old” stayed near; clearly older children stayed no. The weight-comparison post was yes under pattern 1's body-normality provision, not a feeding-amount question. Adjusted age determined stage for the premature twins. Attachment or guilt alone did not establish a development link. One quoted post says “Reposted with permission”; its uncertain original authorship is labelled. The French articulation/hearing-loss post was preserved in French. These are post counts within this share, not counts of people or market prevalence.
