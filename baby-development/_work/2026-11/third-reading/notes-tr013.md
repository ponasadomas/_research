# Reader 013 — third reading

Read all 150 assigned post files, including every supplied author reply: **16 yes, 40 near, 94 no**. Kept **79 quotes from 27 posts**: all 16 yes posts and 11 near posts explicitly mentioning development, abilities, milestones, learning, or regression. Nothing remains unread in this share. Some files state that replies were not fetched; those missing replies were unavailable, not skipped. This is one share of the supplied random sample, not a separate estimate of market prevalence.

Among the 16 yes posts, hard behaviour, learning to eat, and delight/funny habits each appeared in 3 posts; normality worries, stage-related purchases, and known delay/therapy each appeared in 2. Patterns overlap. Product ownership, app use, workshops, and treatment were not assumed to establish payment.

The 40 near posts divide into 10 age unknown, 9 age possibly over 24 months, 7 feeding, 6 sleep, 3 logistics, 3 parent state, and 2 other. Repeated age ambiguity matters: “two years old,” even recently turned two, was kept near. Earlier ages in anecdotes did not establish current age. Sleep-only, medical-only, relationship-only, and clearly older-child posts were excluded; sleep posts describing separate preference, clinginess, play, or regression were kept near. Feeding distress without a learning issue was kept near rather than treated as a developmental delay. These boundaries, bath screaming as hard behaviour, and safety teaching as a skill remain judgment calls flagged in the ledger.

Surprises included the scarcity of explicit purchases and the number of funny accounts of ordinary baby habits.
