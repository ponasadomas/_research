
## Is this normal, or is something wrong with my child?
@pattern 01

### The most common post asks whether something the child does, or does not yet do, is normal
@count pattern

- "I just don’t know what’s developmentally appropriate at this age." [sr072-0053]
- "Could these things indicate a bigger problem?" [sr088-0135]

**But:** some ask without worry. "I want to add that I’m not worried or concerned." [sr061-0056]

### Parents ask whether a repeated movement or a habit is harmless or a sign
@forms
Parents question whether unusual movements signal problems.
Parents question unusual play, attention and habits.
Parents want comfort and exploration without injury.
@end

Shaking, stiffness and toe-walking are 60 of these; rubbing, sucking and odd ways of playing are 44.

- "Do these seem like infantile spasms? I’m really really scared." [sr125-0020]
- "I’m worried about it but I feel bad taking away his comfort." [sr121-0026]

**But:** some habits do injure. "He will yank/pull and has even left his poor balls bleeding." [sr012-0034]

### Parents ask whether the child is late to roll, sit, crawl, walk or use the hands
@forms
Parents ask whether movement and practical skills are late.
Parents seek reassurance while movement skills emerge.
@end

- "He just doesn’t have the strength to turn his head." [sr109-0075]
- "But independently she won’t unless we coax her to do it." [sr111-0014]

**But:** some say the child is well within the normal range and they are not worried.

### Parents ask whether babbling and words are enough, and worry when sounds or skills stop
@forms
Parents question what counts as enough speech.
Parents worry when sounds or skills disappear.
@end

- "I'm wondering if it counts as a word, or a gesture." [sr040-0019]
- "So it’s now been 3 weeks with no words whatsoever, just alot of babbling but no more “mama” or “dada”." [sr090-0030]

**But:** a lost skill can return. "She started rolling after 7 days.." [sr028-0015]

### Parents ask whether the child looks, answers and connects enough; some fear a wider problem
@forms
Parents question their child’s attention and social responses.
Parents combine observations into wider developmental fears.
@end

- "But I can’t make noises at her and then she makes noises back." [sr099-0110]
- "I’m losing sleep and I can’t enjoy my child because all I do all day is worry and analyze his every move." [sr003-0184]

**But:** they doubt themselves too. "Or am I just overreacting?" [sr088-0135]

### Parents ask whether clinging, fears and tantrums are normal for the age
@forms
Parents question too much or too little closeness.
Parents question normal limits for tantrums and roughness.
Parents ask whether fears and sensitivities fade.
@end

- "Isn't it too early for tantrums?" [sr086-0113]
- "He is scared of trees." [sr127-0130]

**But:** some report it was a phase and passed.

### Parents cannot tell whether crying that will not stop is a phase or a sign
@forms
Parents ask whether persistent crying will pass.
Parents question crying during particular care tasks.
@end

- "What troubles me the most is that I can’t figure out what’s wrong." [sr039-0085]
- "Is this really how teething is?" [sr022-0015]

**But:** sometimes it belongs to one situation. "As soon as we take her out, she’s happy." [sr022-0068]

### Parents ask whether head shape, size, weight or late teeth need a doctor
@forms
Parents question growth, appearance and tooth development.
@end

- "My baby is now almost one year and I’m panicking because her flat spot is so so prominent and her head sort of cones at the top." [sr106-0094]

**But:** some take the doctor's word. "The pediatrician is not concerned with his weight at all, so neither am I." [sr022-0114]

### Smaller questions: what to expect at this age, loud noises, spat-out food
@small
Parents need clearer expectations of their child. => what to expect, and whether corrected age applies
Parents question loud or unfamiliar vocal sounds. => screeching and growling
A parent worries about food spitting and weight loss. => food spat out and weight lost
@end

- "However, whenever we have questions or “issues”, I’m unsure if I should consider her true age or her corrected age." [sr008-0049]

**But:** the loud child is often content. "He is a very happy guy, he’s just loud!" [sr121-0001]

### Most have already practised with the child, changed the care or asked a doctor
@across Parents have practised, changed care or sought checks.

- "I currently have an early intervention appointment to get her evaluated." [sr100-0037]

**But:** effort does not always help. "Tylenol and motrin no help at all." [sr022-0016]

### The doctor says normal, the family says not, and parents do not know whom to trust
@across Other adults leave parents unsure whom to trust.

- "I have mentioned it to our pediatrician and have been told it’s a normal behavior but my family is making me feel like it’s not." [sr027-0021]

**But:** family can be the alarm. "My sister immediately went “he probably has autism.”" [sr027-0131]

### Few mention paying; those who do name therapy, an evaluation, a walker and toys
@across Parents report paying for care, equipment and toys.

- "So I booked an evaluation 2 weeks from now, I was surprised I didn’t need a referral but I do have to pay out of pocket." [sr112-0051]

**But:** some borrow, and for some insurance pays.

### Parents say they are scared, guilty and worn out
@across Parents express fear, guilt, exhaustion, affection and curiosity.

- "I've been lacking in some areas due to PP anxiety and depression but now I feel like I completely fucked up my child's development." [sr052-0064]
- "I’m so burnt out, I feel so isolated." [sr055-0006]

**But:** pride and plain curiosity are counted here too.
