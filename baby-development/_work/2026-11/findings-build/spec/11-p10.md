
## Other people interfere
@pattern 10

### One post in forty is mainly about another adult overriding or judging the parent's care
@count pattern

- "I carry him wrong. Change him wrong. Feed him wrong." [sr089-0046]
- "Her solution is always to let her cry." [sr028-0017]

**But:** the same person is often called kind and helpful.

### Relatives ignore the parent's rules on sleep, screens, food and touch
@forms
Parents want relatives to respect tired cues.
Parents want caregivers to respect screen limits.
Parents want feeding and safety instructions followed.
Parents want the child’s refusal of contact respected.
Parents want grandparents to leave the parental role intact.
Parents want unwanted toy gifts stopped.
@end

- "We left her with my MIL and my MIL proudly told me she only used bottles while we were away." [sr071-0055]
- "My mil keeps referring to herself as mum to him and it stresses me out." [sr049-0142]

**But:** asking can work (the poster says AI helped translate this post). "I told her that we don’t do that, and she accepted it and stopped." [sr093-0021]

### Parents are told they cause the crying, or spoil the child by comforting
@forms
Parents feel blamed for crying or avoiding interaction.
Parents reject claims that comforting teaches manipulation.
Parents feel worn down defending everyday care.
Parents want separation choices respected.
@end

- "On a trip visiting overseas in-laws and my FIL said that he believes my baby cries a lot because I am clearly depressed, and that it's affecting her via my breast milk." [sr094-0099]

**But:** some stand by their choice. "I am quite content with this choice, but being made to feel like it’s abnormal." [sr062-0150]

### Others label, compare or belittle the child
@forms
Parents resent developmental labels and comparisons.
Parents want size comments stopped.
Parents reject gender restrictions on play and temperament.
Parents want affection and progress enjoyed without dismissal.
Parents leave professional feedback doubting their care.
@end

- "Tell me why this man told my 9 month old son he can’t be playing with crayons and colouring because that’s for girls." [sr109-0116]
- "Being proud of my kid doesn’t mean yours is behind, or that I’m judging your parenting, or that I think I’m better." [sr098-0046]

**But:** one criticism, of gross motor skills, is accepted as warranted.

### Smaller forms: harsh handling, and what is safe or allowed
@small
Parents want harsh treatment stopped. => a partner's shushing, isolating or spanking
Parents want agreement about toddler behaviour. => one line between the adults
Parents question others’ judgement about safe play. => what play is safe
Parents question who should intervene with other children. => who may correct another family's child
@end

- "I do not like him spanking them/slapping their hands or when he blames them for "making daddy angry'." [sr102-0101]

**But:** one doubts herself. "And yes, it’s possible I swooped in too soon." [sr018-0067]

### Most have already explained, sent guidelines or set a boundary
@across Parents have tried care changes, explanations and boundaries.

- "I then sent her guidelines from our government health service that state why this carrot in this form is a choking hazard." [sr125-0091]

**But:** it keeps failing. "She said I’m wrong and continued to let my baby chew on it." [sr125-0089]

### Every one of these posts reports what the other adult said or did
@across Parents report others overriding their judgement.

- "People will literally acknowledge that i will be mad if they let him watch the tv yet they do it anyway because they think it’s funny." [sr093-0095]

**But:** not all of it is hostile. "Unfortunately for her mom and I, they express their affection by constantly bringing presents over." [sr121-0028]

### What is bought is mostly bought by relatives; one mother bought three parenting books
@across Parents report purchases supporting or conflicting with care.

- "I just bought three books -- the Montessori Toddler, The Whole Brain Child, and How to Talk so Little Kids Will Listen." [sr019-0044]

**But:** a relative's purchase can go against the parent's wishes, as with pacifiers.

### Parents say they feel angry, ashamed, guilty and alone

- "We both feel like shit parents who aren’t looking after our boy properly." [sr098-0064]
