
## The child prefers someone else
@pattern 06

### About one post in twenty is about a child who wants one parent, or someone else, and not the other
@count pattern

- "But I am literally STARVED for my baby to show me the tiniest crumb of affection." [sr046-0061]
- "I used to be his center now I am not." [sr003-0110]

**But:** some report it was a phase that passed.

### The parent who is not chosen feels rejected, incompetent and afraid of loving the child less
@forms
Parents feel rejected when the other parent receives the affection they want.
Parents feel unable to comfort a baby whom others can soothe.
Parents feel overlooked when their baby smiles or performs skills for others.
Nursing parents feel wanted for milk while missing other affection.
Parents worry about their own connection to a child who prefers others.
Parents find affection or soothing success after feeling rejected.
@end

- "Sometimes she will even go down the list of all the people she loves, but not me." [sr091-0081]
- "i’m also afraid of my own feelings, i feel like i love him less and less." [sr088-0100]

**But:** for three the affection came later. "Since she’s turned one, I see how much she loves her mama." [sr004-0159]

### The parent who is chosen cannot leave the room, hand over bedtime or go out
@forms
Parents cannot get a break when the child demands their presence.
Parents want to share bedtime or night waking again.
Parents say care goes well until the preferred parent appears.
Parents fear leaving because separations bring prolonged distress.
@end

- "Having a great time playing with dad or grandparents then the second LO sees or hears mom, starts crying until in her arms." [sr001-0062]
- "Middle of the night, apparently dad is a wild animal coming to hurt him." [sr089-0052]

**But:** it comes and goes without a pattern.

### Parents want the child to accept the other parent's comfort
@forms
Parents want to support a partner who feels rejected.
Parents want their baby to accept the other parent’s comfort.
Parents are unsure when to intervene in the other parent’s soothing.
@end

- "Anyone have any suggestions on how to get him to let me comfort him, even if it’s once every 5 times?" [sr013-0087]
- "Does anyone know any resources to help teach dad to be a little more nurturing?" [sr016-0142]

**But:** one doubts that preference is the problem. "This feels more like self-doubt than an actual issue." [sr065-0046]

### Parents feel displaced when the child prefers a grandmother, a cousin or the nanny
@forms
Caregivers feel displaced or caught between adults when a relative is preferred.
Caregivers want the child to accept a familiar relative they reject.
Parents feel displaced when their child appears to prefer childcare staff.
A parent questions whether a new nanny can bond while family intervene.
@end

- "He probably thinks I'm just the bitch that lives with him." [sr032-0057]
- "Yesterday, he just went up to the nanny and gave her a leg hug." [sr121-0049]

**But:** alone with the parents, the child is affectionate too.

### Parents fear the bond was damaged by the birth, by not breastfeeding or by time away
@forms
Parents fear that a difficult start or shared care damaged attachment.
Parents want to preserve or rebuild their bond across absences.
@end

These are causes the posters fear, not causes shown.

- "I had a c-section and I couldn’t breastfeed and I feel like I killed that pivotal part of bonding with a newborn." [sr087-0044]

**But:** the upset can end with the handover. "Once his mom is gone and he settles down, he is completely normal with me." [sr112-0030]

### Most have already tried handing over routines, sharing care and more time together
@across Parents try shared care, soothing and repeated contact to build comfort.

- "We’ve tried a lot to help — having him do bedtime routines, diaper changes, baths, even wrapping her in one of my shirts." [sr017-0013]

**But:** it often changes nothing. "Same result." [sr112-0029]

### The other adult is in almost every one of these posts: helping, offering, or gloating
@across Other adults’ help and comments shape parents’ experience of preference.

- "I could also handle it if he wasn’t a fucking dick and gloated about it…" [sr038-0115]

**But:** some say both parents do everything and it still happens.

### Almost none mention paying: a nanny, and purses bought for comfort
@across Parents mention nanny care, purses or unspecified purchases.

- "so now i guess i’m just gonna doomscroll Depop and buy my 5th secondhand Coach purse this week bc I deserve dopamine too goddamnit!!!n!!" [sr059-0005]

**But:** that fifth purse is a plan, and two nanny payments are inferred from the role.

### Parents say they feel rejected, embarrassed, resentful and ashamed of it
@across Parents describe rejection, resentment or exhaustion alongside love and pride.

- "I feel embarrassed and unappreciated, then I feel ashamed for allowing a toddler’s whims make me feel this way." [sr053-0014]
- "If I died tomorrow he would be none the wiser." [sr032-0059]

**But:** success brings pride. "I feel so damn proud of myself." [sr063-0013]
