
## Delight or pride in what the child now does
@pattern 08

### About one post in twenty is written out of delight or pride in something the child now does
@count pattern

- "I feel like I just won the Super Bowl." [sr075-0116]
- "She’s a perfectly normal toddler, doing all of the things they said she’d never do." [sr132-0086]

**But:** a new ability is also new work.

### Parents celebrate a first word, smile, step or tooth, most of all one they feared would not come
@forms
They celebrate and decode new words and signs.
They celebrate receiving first smiles and laughs.
They marvel at new physical milestones.
They celebrate skills they feared might never come.
They want to understand and support remarkable abilities.
@end

- "Not mama or dada, but “egg” 😂." [sr089-0010]
- "It may seem like nothing for most 1-year-olds, but to any other parents dealing with a speech delay this is a huge win!" [sr052-0003]

**But:** some doubt a first word is understood yet.

### Parents enjoy the child's jokes, favourites and will
@forms

@end

### Parents celebrate the skill that gives them time back
@forms

@end

**But:** the child still has moments of wanting to be held.
