
## Am I harming the child's development by what I do?
@pattern 05

### One post in fourteen asks whether the parent's own choices are harming the child
@count pattern

- "Have I messed it up for him? I feel like I failed him." [sr126-0085]
- "Is it possible that i've given him too much attention?" [sr069-0025]

**But:** some answer themselves. "The truth is that we don't control our children's development, we support it." [sr041-0054]

### Parents fear they do too little: enrichment, attention, talk, time with other children
@forms
They doubt their daily activities and routines are enough.
They fear competing demands leave too little attention.
They feel guilty letting their baby play or wait alone.
They worry they talk or read too little.
They fear home care or limited outings impair sociability.
@end

- "I feel like I’m not providing him developmental enrichment, but I also feel lost in what that looks like." [sr060-0007]
- "I have a 1 year old and try to be present but am paranoid I’m on my phone too much!" [sr080-0102]

**But:** the same posts often say the child is happy and meeting milestones.

### Parents fear that a missed cry, a lost temper or time apart leaves lasting damage
@forms
They fear crying, scares or anger leave lasting harm.
They fear separation or changed care damages their bond.
@end

- "Is his nervous system going to remember me not being there for him when he cries?" [sr118-0011]
- "I just beat him on his bum pretty hard and I shook him for a few seconds." [sr094-0002]
- "But I also worry that she'll stop loving me back." [sr030-0159]

**But:** after an accidental scare, one baby was laughing again within five minutes.

### Parents weigh screens against the rest they give
@forms
They weigh screen risks against learning and respite.
@end

- "I know the NHS says no screen time before two but how is that actually viable for modern mothers?" [sr094-0039]

**But:** one father swapped fast shows for old DVDs. "This has made an absolute difference in every possible way." [sr005-0036]

### Parents blame themselves when a skill is slow to come
@forms
They blame their parenting for slower milestones.
They fear too little movement holds their baby back.
They blame themselves for temperament or changed responses.
@end

- "I feel like it’s all my fault." [sr041-0041]
- "DUHHH, SHE NEVER NEEDED TO WALK!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! WHAT A FUCKING IDIOT I AM!!!!" [sr108-0089]

**But:** a second child with a different temperament changes the view.

### Parents fear a seat, a carrier, a feed or a vaccine has done hidden damage
@forms
They fear positioning or soothing harms physical development.
They fear feeding or health choices could damage development.
@end

The vaccine fears are beliefs of the posters or their relatives, not shown links.

- "To the point thinking of the shots makes me want to throw up." [sr130-0041]

**But:** the follow-up reports no change. "So far, everything seems normal still." [sr023-0114]

### Smaller forms: spoiling, bad example, two languages
@small
They fear comforting their child has created dependence. => comforting has made the child clingy
They fear their words and example teach unwanted habits. => their words and example teach bad habits
They fear multiple languages confuse or delay speech. => two languages delay speech
@end

- "Our boy is extremely clingy, and I think it’s my own fault." [sr009-0077]

**But:** some settle it. "You’re right, you can’t love a baby too much." [sr018-0164]

### Nearly all already read, play, soothe and change routines
@across They already try activities, soothing and care changes.

- "I do read him books and I explain what we’re going to do everyday and give him a play by play." [sr073-0117]

**But:** trying is not success. "We sleep trained, didn't help." [sr073-0041]

### Relatives, partners and reading tell parents they are spoiling the child or holding the child back
@across They hear warnings and criticism about their choices.

- "I've been told several times that I'm spoiling my 6-month-old because I let her nap in a baby carrier instead of putting her down in her crib." [sr087-0113]

**But:** a husband's "she will be fine" does not settle it.

### A few name what they bought: sensory things, carriers, old DVDs, an app
@across They report purchases for care, activities and family life.

- "I’ve bought loads of cool sensory stuff and I sort of do my own sensory classes at home with him." [sr027-0242]

**But:** a purchase can make it worse. "We elongated WW, shortened WW, said fuck you to WW, said unfuck you to WW, bought the Huckleberry app, only got depression and anxiety from it." [sr073-0041]

### Parents say they feel guilty, afraid and tired of being "switched on"
@across They describe guilt, fear and exhaustion.

- "I’m proud of my daughter but I’m disappointed in myself because I think I’m not teaching her properly." [sr003-0176]
- "But I’m so tired of being switched on constantly." [sr057-0061]

**But:** looking back, one mother says it eased with her second child.
