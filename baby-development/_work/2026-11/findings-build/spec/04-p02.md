
## What do I do with the child all day?
@pattern 02

### About one post in ten asks how to fill the day with the child
@count pattern

- "I’m a full-time dad now and it's the *filling the day* part that gets me." [sr020-0071]
- "Is it possible to play with him yet or is he too young?" [sr016-0043]

**But:** some days are easy. "My son is now 14 months and life lately has been… too easy." [sr038-0007]

### Parents want outings and occasions the child can enjoy, and a plan for days indoors
@forms
Parents struggle to make outings possible.

Parents need alternatives when going outside is difficult.
Parents need entertainment during journeys and ceremonies.
Parents want activities that match their child’s energy.
Parents want celebrations and memories suited to young children.
@end

Outings and days indoors are 58 of these; birthdays and holidays are 21.

- "When did you first start taking baby out and about and how did you even know how??" [sr124-0069]
- "Give her a day she’ll enjoy and have fun." [sr087-0087]

**But:** energy runs out. "And honestly I don’t have the energy for it now." [sr093-0087]

### Parents run out of ideas for an ordinary day at home and want to be told what to do
@forms

Parents want reading to fit their child.

@end

- "I wake up thinking I've got a plan and then somehow it’s 10:30 and I'm out of ideas" [sr020-0071]
- "I’d like to follow just one account or purchase one “program” or something that would just tell me what to do!" [sr036-0107]

**But:** one child's favourite is ignored by another.

### Parents cannot keep the child occupied long enough to work, wash or eat
@forms

@end

**But:** it depends who is there. "Yet when I’m working, I can see on our cameras she plays independently when my nanny or husband watch her" [sr119-0084]

### Most already fill the day with household objects, songs, books and walks
@across Parents try varied play and ways to share time.

- "I give him the salad spinner on the kitchen floor." [sr008-0164]

**But:** for some nothing holds the child's attention for long.

### Some pay for toys, outings and childcare to fill the day

### Parents say they feel guilty, bored and trapped
@across Parents describe guilt, boredom and exhaustion alongside love.

- "I feel like I'm not stimulating his mind enough and that it could be bad for his development." [sr019-0049]
