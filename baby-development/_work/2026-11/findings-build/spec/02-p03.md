
## Hard behaviour
@pattern 03

### One post in six is about hard behaviour: hitting, biting, screaming, tantrums, fights over care
@count pattern

- "I would do anything for a gentle touch." [sr079-0026]
- "We have literally never had a break." [sr106-0107]

**But:** many say the child means no harm. "He doesn’t hurt me because he’s angry, he just can’t be gentle yet." [sr079-0023]

### Parents want the child to stop hitting, biting and grabbing, at home and among other children
@forms
They want painful rough touch to stop.
They want children to share space and toys safely.
@end

- "My 21 month old keeps bitting kids at daycare and its not out of anger or anything he just goes up and bites them." [sr028-0022]
- "She will come home with large bruises on her arms." [sr110-0053]

**But:** with adults the same child may share happily.

### Parents want crying and screaming to ease, and to be able to put the child down
@forms
They want relief and explanations for persistent crying.
They need to put their child down.
They need relief from screaming.
They need help with difficult behaviour before sleep.
@end

- "I have developed tinnitus in my right ear from all the high pitched screaming." [sr097-0004]
- "Or at least eating with both hands and going to the bathroom by myself." [sr002-0080]

**But:** some nights it is only a quick cry.

### Parents want nappy changes, washing, dressing, teeth and meals without a fight
@forms
They want care routines without a fight.
They want meals without battles.
They want clean teeth without forcing the brush.
@end

- "I just feel like I’m waterboarding her every night." [sr131-0077]
- "The only way to do it properly is to pin him down and do it myself." [sr022-0029]

**But:** at daycare the same child may not resist.

### Parents want a way through tantrums, and tie many to what the child cannot yet do or say
@forms
They want to handle tantrums without worsening them.
They connect frustration with emerging skills.
They want head-banging and hair-pulling to stop.
@end

- "Do they actually stop eventually?" [sr012-0172]
- "he knows how to gesture for help or whatever he needs, but I feel like those skill go out the window the second he’s frustrated????? SOS!!!!" [sr008-0146]

**But:** a new skill can ease it. "We’re working on it, and using sign language has helped tremendously." [sr046-0124]

### Parents ask what limit a child this young can take in, and how to keep a climber safe
@forms
They want limits their young child can understand.
They need to keep an exploring child safe.
@end

- "I don’t want to raise him spoiled but I also don’t know if it’s realistic to discipline this early." [sr059-0054]
- "My 1 year old eats everything… foam, plastic, paper, silicone…" [sr061-0079]

**But:** one reports that early boundaries made things easier.

### Parents cannot get through an errand or a car journey
@forms
They want manageable outings and car journeys.
@end

- "I’m a single mum I can’t sit in the back to comfort him." [sr109-0143]

**But:** for one the stopping is the problem. "Driving is totally fine but better not stop." [sr090-0044]

### Parents are worn down by the daily battles and frightened by their own reactions
@forms
They need relief from battles and their reactions.
They question managing another baby.
@end

- "I crossed the line." [sr030-0020]
- "I am feeling so low, and I’m not enjoying being a mother." [sr014-0065]

**But:** love for the child is said in the same posts.

### Smaller forms: after a big change, and what toddlers are "supposed" to be like
@small
They link harder behaviour to a major change. => after a move, a sibling, daycare or weaning
They question stories about toddler temperament. => doubting what others say toddlers are like
They ask whether behaviour will worsen. => whether it will get worse
Other parents describe swearing or moving a diaper aside. => swearing, and a nappy pushed aside
@end

- "Since our move a month ago I’ve noticed more and more tantrums." [sr024-0017]

**But:** nothing found against it.

### Nearly all have already tried something: soothing, redirecting, time out, holding the child down, and some a slap
@across They have tried changing responses and routines.

- "Knowing that I would have to give him a shower while in tight schedule for work, I lost it and slapped his hand." [sr129-0036]

**But:** some of it works for a while.

### The advice parents get conflicts, and some of it is to hit or bite back
@across They receive conflicting advice and help.

- "I am hoping to discourage the behavior without doing anything weird or traumatizing (when I asked my dad he said "bite her back," which is insane)." [sr022-0002]

**But:** some have support. "Our daycare has been wonderful throughout all of this." [sr044-0097]

### A few mention buying something for it: nappy covers, a stroller, a book, entry to play places
@across They report buying practical items, meals and outings.

- "We bought a book called "teeth are not for biting" but he refuses to read that one book in particular." [sr133-0026]

**But:** bought is not used. "Haven’t used it yet." [sr052-0027]

### Parents say they are depleted, touched out, guilty and at times enraged
@across They feel depleted, guilty or afraid.

- "I don’t feel like a person anymore sometimes." [sr029-0026]
- "I get so touched out and triggered that I feel rage building inside me as if I’m being physically/ sexually attacked and’s need to protect myself." [sr090-0042]

**But:** some hold their temper. "And I’ve stayed patient." [sr112-0014]
