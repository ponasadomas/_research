### The caregiver worries that the baby is late to roll or struggles with tummy time.

10 of 198 posts.

These caregivers describe babies who stay on their backs, turn only onto a side, or cry rather than push up during tummy time. Ages run from two months to just over six months; some mention prematurity, body size, head shape or other babies rolling earlier. They describe floor time, assisted rolls and toys placed to encourage movement, and ask whether practice or professional help is needed.

- "I have tried everything. We practice every single day." [sr017-0004]

- "His doc is not concerned with either the not rolling thing and the head flat spot. But I find him really dismissive and I cannot get an indication to Physiotherapie." [sr041-0030]

- "He also tries to turn onto his side, but he hasn’t rolled over yet. A few babies around his age in my neighborhood have already started rolling, so I’ll admit I’m feeling a bit anxious." [sr083-0054]
