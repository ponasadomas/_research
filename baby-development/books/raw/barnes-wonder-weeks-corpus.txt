TITLE: Helpful, easy to understand, educational
My mother got me the 5th edition and I have the app. I HIGHLY recommend both. The book is easy to understand and the explanations are put in away that we can sympathize with baby when he/she might be going through a ""leap"". The illustrations and little footnotes are helpful and informational. The app is lovely, a easy way I can get info on the fly.
