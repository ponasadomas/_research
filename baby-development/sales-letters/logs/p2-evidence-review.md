# Phase 2 — local evidence review

Reviewed 2026-11-03. This review reads saved first-party archive captures and their helper-generated sidecars only. It makes no network requests and does not alter raw files. Archive presence establishes that a page was captured, not that an offer made sales or that its claims are true.

## Qualification checks

The helper's `WORDS` total includes navigation, footer, repeated hidden/mobile elements, testimonials and catalogue entries. A page needs at least 1,200 words of substantial selling body, a headline and a price or purchase CTA; a long catalogue, blog or free-registration page does not qualify. Captures of the same product in different years are variants of one letter, not separate letters. The saved HTTP header `memento-datetime` is checked against the requested timestamp when available.

Every saved `memento-datetime` matches the requested timestamp. The audit contains **136 request events** (53 CDX invocations and 83 fetch invocations), 135 response events and one transport-error event, with no redirect events. This local verification makes no claim to independently reconstruct request timing from the audit, which does not contain event timestamps.
