 If your baby or toddler just won’t sleep, it’s likely you’re exhausted, too. You’re in the right place! At The Baby Sleep Site™, a good night’s sleep, for your entire family, is just a few clicks away.

 I’m Nicole Johnson, baby sleep coach & owner of The Baby Sleep Site™. Here, you’ll find trusted support and community, powerful tools like our Members Area, comprehensive e-books and ongoing articles about every aspect of baby sleep. 

 I started The Baby Sleep Site™ after having helped my own sleepless baby get the rest he needed. I made it my mission to help tired parents, like you, solve your baby’s sleep challenges in a way that fits with your parenting style. 

 My site is filled with invaluable resources to help you and your baby or toddler get on the right sleep track, whether it’s helping your child sleep through the night, getting him on a regular nap schedule, weaning her off the breast or bottle or learning to set limits that reinforce good sleep habits. 

 I know that one solution doesn’t work for everyone. That’s why I offer many services, from my e-books, articles and blog, to customized sleep consulting that addresses your special issues. Feel free to explore my website all that you want! 

 Living without sleep and feeling uncertain about what to do can be so stressful. Just know, you are not alone! I’ve been there, as have lots of other parents. We’ll work together to develop a plan that will help your baby or toddler become a good sleeper and give you back your confidence!
