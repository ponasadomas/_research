Hi, we’re Lisa Hudson and Julia Eggles. Our passion is children and their families. Since you’ve found your way here, you’ve probably heard about our unique program – Your Child Development System.

As a loving parent we know that you want to recognise and develop your children‘s special gifts and talents. You also want to strengthen the skills that might not be so strong. You’ve already done so much to give them the very best – and   even so, you wonder – is it enough?

As child development clinicians we have over 50 years of experience to share with you.

  • And we can help you realize your child’s true potential.
  • We have developed a step-by-step tailored system for applying clinically based strategies to daily parenting challenges and to early childhood learning and development.
  • We have answered frustrated parents questions and cut through the mass of information “out there” to bring it all together.

Ease of Use and Understanding

  • We have designed our system for ease of use and understanding so you can start seeing results very quickly.
  • Our system integrates the knowledge and experience of today’s clinical and early childhood practitioners from around the world, and we have figured out how to tailor it to the specific needs of your child.
