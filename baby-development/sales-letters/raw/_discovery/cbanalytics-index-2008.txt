 What is ClickBank Analytics about?

 CB-Analytics is a powerful affiliate tool for researching products sold through 
 www.clickbank.com from affiliate perspecive. 
 Information available on clickbank is just a static snapshot of what the product looks like now. There is 
 no way to tell were the product was a where is it going. Increasing gravity indicates that more and more 
 affiliates are advertising it. This may mean more profits... and more competition. Declining gravity means 
 that affiliates are ditching the product. There may be a reason for that as well... Earnings per sale 
 is another important factor to keep in mind. If this number declines - customers are requesting refunds on the product. 
 EPS graph with sharp ups and downs indicates low sales volume as each individual sale has considerable effect on the graph. 

 How does ClickBank Analytics work?

 ClickBank kindly provides XML marketplace feed that is updated daily. That is some 15 megabytes of raw XML with thousands of 
 individual products each listed in multiple categories. Two months worth of data gives you almost full gigabyte. www.CB-Analytics.com 
 downloads this feed every day, stores all of the information in database and makes it available online. As you can see there is no magic here - 
 just lots and lots of information and some code to glue it together. 

 How to use ClickBank Analytics?

 Just enter the ID of the product you want to research or a search query (affiliate, ebook, etc) in the textbox in the header and hit "enter". All major product 
 statistics and charts are there on single page easy to see and interpret. You can bookmark or link to that page for easy access too. 
 You can also browse products by category, list them ordered by gravity, EPS or gravity change through the appropriate links in the header above. 
 Direct URL for product information page is:

 http://www.cb-analytics.com/product.php?id= {clickbank product id goes here}
