ONLINE WORKSHOP: Montessori for Babies

Montessori for Babies

This 2-hour online workshop is especially for people who are expecting a baby or have a child under 6 months old.

Are you looking for a gentle way to parent your baby?
Want to know how your baby will learn best?
Would you like to learn how to apply Montessori principles from birth?

In this session, you’ll learn:
1. how to set up your home for your baby with less items than you think
2. to understand your baby’s movement and language development
3. how to prepare yourself as a parent to raise an independent, caring child
4. and how to guide your baby through activities to support their development
