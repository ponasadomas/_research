The Tummy Time Masterclass

This class will cover how to master tummy time from day 1, including the importance, the anatomy of tummy time & how to best support your little one as they progress & gain strength in tummy time. Learn why tummy time is the foundation for the pyramid of motor development. We will touch on reflux and how to modify, how to keep them engaged when on their belly. Best for most kids up to 4 months of age. Hand outs & printable summary of topics is included as well.

The Roll & Sit Masterclass

This masterclass is geared towards helping your little one move from tummy time mini-stones to rolling belly to back & back to belly, sitting independently (along with fine motor progressions and protective reactions in sitting), up through transitioning in and out of sit and working towards crawling! Videos with demonstrations of activities to encourage mini-stones and milestones, along with a corresponding PDF will give you all you need to get your little one moving!

This class is geared towards approximately 3+ months of age

The Crawling Masterclass

This masterclass is designed for any parents of a child not yet crawling. It can be taken at any age prior to crawling, & covers everything you need to know to get there!

In this class, we will cover the milestones leading up to crawling, why those matter, why crawling is important for development, how to encourage crawling & setting your environment up for success, trouble shooting for hiccups you may find along the way (like army crawling, 3 legged crawling, and butt scooting, & what to do if your child skipped crawling).

It’s designed to take you step by step through it all, with research backed information and a PDF for reference. Don’t fear crawling anymore!

Purchase Crawling Masterclass

The Walking Masterclass

The walking masterclass covers from pulling to stand with hand support up through independent step taking, improving balance, first shoes & everything in between. Videos are within each section specifically demonstrating techniques to encourage environmental set up, see actual children performing the variations in skills & handling to encourage all of the mini-stones that lead up to walking!
