 About the Baby Sleep System

 What's In The Book?

 The book is written in a clear and concise manner and will immediately help you 
start the journey to a better night's sleep.

 The book includes:

 Help to find the real source of your baby's sleep problem.

 How to establish sleep routines with examples for different aged children.

 A choice of proven strategies, recommended by health professionals, to manage 
bedtimes and deal with night time waking.

 Separate advice for parents of newborns, older babies and toddlers.

 How to handle specific sleep issues such as: 

 When and how to stop feeding in the night

 Managing early waking 

 Lengthening day time naps

 Inappropriate sleep associations

 Advice for parents of twins, triplets and more .
