What is Brainy Baby?

Over a decade ago, The Brainy Baby Company created one of the first videos that introduced preschool basics for young children. Today The Brainy Baby Company remains an award-winning leader in videos for children ages 5 and under.

The Brainy Baby Company produces videos, books, music, toys and games for babies, toddlers and preschoolers. ABC's, 123's, Animals, Shapes & Colors, Art, Music, Spanish and French, are all introduced through fun, gentle, and always entertaining lessons.

What Parents Want – What Children Need

There's nothing like a parent's joy when your child recites the alphabet or counts to 10 for the first time. You feel an overwhelming sense of satisfaction as your child points out the "triangle", or correctly chooses the red puzzle piece. And what is more satisfying than asking, "What does a doggie say?" and hearing a proud little "Woof, woof!"

Brainy Baby® products always work best when adults use them interactively with their child. When using a DVD, for example, watch it with your child, talk about what you see, and repeat the names of new things, just as you do when reading a book together. Interaction between parent and child is a key ingredient to getting the most out of our Brainy Baby® products.

We welcome you to this site, and hope you will find everything you need to help your little one establish a love of learning for a lifetime.®
