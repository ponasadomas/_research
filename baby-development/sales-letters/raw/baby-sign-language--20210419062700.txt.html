Quickstart Guide

This guide will have you ready to start teaching your child Baby Sign Language in just 5 minutes. You will learn:

  1. Starter Signs: Learn mom, dad, eat, milk, and dog
  2. Teaching Signing: How to teach your child the signs
  3. Signing Kit: guaranteed faster results with the Baby Sign Language Kit

1. Your First Five Signs

Top Five Starter Signs (Mom, Dad, Eat, Milk, Dog)

Lets start with your first five signs: mom, dad, eat, milk, and dog.

These make great starter signs because they are words that your child is motivated to learn and they are words that can be frequently used throughout the day.

(If those signs don’t make sense for your family, there are more good starter sign options on the Top 10 Starter Signs page.)

2. Teaching Your Baby their First Signs

Baby Sign Language F.R.E.E. Method

These four principles show you how to teach sign language to your baby. Make it Fun. Provide lots of Repetition. Encourage all progress. And Expand your signing vocabulary.

3. Get Faster Results, Guaranteed

Our award winning Baby Sign Language Kits gets your baby signing faster. Kit covers advanced teaching methods for faster results, and topics such as potty training, and the transition to speaking.

Use the DVDs to teach your baby to learn to sign while being entertained. Use the flash cards to learn new words and expand your vocabulary.
