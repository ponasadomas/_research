Do you know? 75% of your child's brain mass is developed post-birth!

Research has proven that early learning methods like flashcard methodology help children's brain development in their early years. Their first 6 years is their golden window of learning! This is the chief purpose of our early learning programs.

 

WINK to LEARN provides one of the most comprehensive range of language learning children DVDs helping your child to acquire multiple languages from young. Our programs are inspired by proven teaching methods pioneered by Dr. Glenn Doman (USA) & Dr. Makato Shichida (Japan).

 

Speak & Read DVD series is a flashcard-based early childhood language learning system which helps your child to recognize more than 600 words and also to construct proper phrases and sentences. This series covers the following languages:

 

North American / European: English (US), English (UK), French *, Spanish (Continental) *, Italian *, German *, Portuguese *, Russian *, Greek *

Asian: Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Filipino *, Thai *, Vietnamese *, Bahasa Indonesia *

Middle Eastern: Hebrew *, Arabic *

South American: Spanish (Latin America) *, Portuguese (Brazilian) *

[Note: Languages annoted with an " * " will be launched by June 2011.]

 

Our Encyclopedic DVDs, supported by Singapore Zoo and Bird Park, help your child to learn more than 600 encyclopedic facts about animals and birds and hence acquire more than 900 English and Chinese words & phrases. These bits of intelligence is a great companion for your children in their learning journey.
