Episode 1 Tummy Time

This first episode is a fantastic start to the ActiveBabiesSmartKids TV series. It begins with BabyROO’s Bit More on Tummy Time, sharing with you information on tummy time and why it’s crucially important to your baby’s brain and body development.

Episode 10 Baby’s Reflexes

Episode 10 Baby’s Reflexes

Episode 10 of our Active Babies Smart Kids TV series features BabyROO’s Bit more on Baby Reflexes. In this episode Dr Jane helps us look into what primitive reflexes are, why it’s important for parents to know about them and how they affect your baby’s development. 

Primitive reflexes are of major importance to your baby’s brain development, the development of their balance, muscle tone, head control, vision and even the development of how well they use their hands and feet!

There are some absolute favourites included in the fun of our Let’s Do BabyROO session, including Old McDonald, Incy Wincy, Jack be Nimble, and a great dance where we “put the beat in our feet”!
