Mommy Jingles
Easy musical techniques to calm, communicate and connect with babies.
The Mommy Jingles Program shows mothers the same techniques used by early childhood educators and professional day care providers.  It consists of 8 videos delivered on line.  These videos include:
+ Baby Games
+ Waking Up and Getting Dressed
+ Meal Time
+ In The Car
+ Bath Time
+ Transition Times and
+ Challenging Times
Each video is accompanied by reminder cards and lyrics sheets and is supported by an audio center so that members can quickly listen to the jingles they'd like to learn.  By learning how to deal effectively with common mothering dilemmas, new mothers are empowered and gain confidence in their abilities.
