## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

The closest English-language analogue of the Pirmi model that is actually advertising: an age-organized home-activity system sold one-time, with a low tier ($49) and a "done-for-you, updates as they grow" tier ($247). It shows that "what to do with my baby today" plus "10 minutes a day" is a live, paid hook, and that a cheap static guide is being used as the entry to a personalized upgrade. Its proof stack (named OTs, citations, safety page) is the bar a Mamivio guide will be compared against. Its strongest page leans on container guilt and delay fear, which Mamivio must not copy; the home lander shows the same product can be sold without it (milestones "on their own", the gain is confidence and capability). The seller is a founder in "founding member" stage with ~1,000 claimed parents — small, not proven at scale.
