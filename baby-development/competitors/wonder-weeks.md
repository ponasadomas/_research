## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

The leap idea already gives parents an age-in-weeks schedule keyed to the due date — the same input a Pirmi-style selector asks for — and it sells relief ("this phase ends") more than activities. A Mamivio guide by month/week will be read against the leap calendar many mothers already follow; using "week" timing invites the comparison. Low price points ($4.99/month, $34.99 for 24 months) set a cheap reference for "a schedule of what is happening to my baby".
