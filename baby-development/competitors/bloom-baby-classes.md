- **Mechanism (≤8 words):** Weekly themed sensory class, age-grouped, with music.
- **Promise:** activities that "promote the natural development of infants" and bonding; "tools and knowledge to continue supporting their baby's development" at home; socialising for baby and parent.
- **Proof:** "multi award-winning", "best baby class voted by parents", founder Victoria Jennings "early years expert and mum of three"; follows the EYFS; DBS-checked leaders; parent testimonials.
- **Price:** not displayed on franchisor or franchisee pages (booking is in a franchisee shop). A third-party listing indicated £34 for 4 weeks / £9 single in Sheffield, but that page returned 403 and is not verified.
- **Format:** in-person 45-minute classes in blocks/terms; trial sessions at some venues; gift vouchers; original music on Spotify; online shop.
- **Personalization:** age group only — Caterpillar Club 0–6 months, Busy Bees 6–15 months (also "6 months to walking"), toddler classes.
- **Guarantee:** none; booking terms page not captured.
- **Angles and hooks:** "award-winning", "sensory", "development" as the reason to attend; "best baby classes near me" SEO; franchise recruitment ("Join the Franchise") on every page.
- **Objections answered:** age suitability, whether classes are sensory, trial before a block.
