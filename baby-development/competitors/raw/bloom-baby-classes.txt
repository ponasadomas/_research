# Bloom Baby Classes

[img alt: Bloom Baby Class Franchise]

Multi-award-winning baby development classes. Created by early years expert and mum of three, Victoria Jennings. Best baby class voted by parents.

[iframe: https://www.youtube.com/embed/WumlpF5ICJI?autoplay=1&mute=1&controls=1]

Bloom Baby Classes are multi award-winning sensory and baby development classes for infants aged 0–14 months, offered across the UK. Designed by early years expert and mum of three, Victoria Jennings, these classes follow the Early Years Foundation Stages of Learning, helping babies learn about the world around them in a fun, safe, and inclusive environment.

Every activity in our classes is carefully crafted to encourage the natural development of infants while strengthening the bond between caregiver and baby. Using original music and bespoke props, our activities also offer parents the tools and knowledge to continue supporting their baby’s development at home.

Parents can expect a warm, welcoming environment led by highly trained and DBS-checked class leaders. Winners of best baby class year and best franchise opportunity. Our sessions also provide a space for babies to socialize and meet other children, all while enjoying a clean, hygienic setting filled with bright and engaging materials.

## Why We Started

When Victoria became a mum for the first time in 2008, she struggled to adapt to motherhood and experienced challenges bonding with her baby. These struggles, paired with postnatal depression, inspired her to use her expertise in early years education to create a programme that would support both parents and their babies.

Victoria’s goal was to design a baby class that would aid in child development and improve the well-being of caregivers. She envisioned a community where parents could feel included, supported, and empowered while fostering a strong bond with their children.

Launched in 2017, Bloom Baby Classes reflects this mission by combining activities rooted in extensive research and aligned with the Early Years Foundation Stages of Learning. Today, the programme has expanded across the UK, and thousands of families enjoy the weekly classes.
