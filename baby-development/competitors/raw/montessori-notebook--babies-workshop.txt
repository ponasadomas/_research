#### What the workshop will cover

People often tell me, I wish I'd known about Montessori earlier. So this workshop is for you!In this session, you’ll learn:

- how to set up your home for your baby with less items than you think

- to understand your baby’s movement and language development

- how to prepare yourself as a parent to raise an independent, caring child

- and how to guide your baby through activities to support their development
