# Baby Classes from Newborn to Walking

With activities tailored to different developmental stages, Bloom Baby Classes are split into two age-specific groups: Caterpillar Club and Busy Bees.

[iframe: https://www.youtube.com/embed/VrXbNumVK8c?autoplay=1&mute=1&controls=1]

### 🌸 Bloom Baby Classes – More Than Just a Baby Class

At Bloom Baby Classes, we’ve created award-winning baby development sessions designed to nurture little ones and support parents too. Every class is carefully structured to boost your baby’s development, build confidence, and create joyful memories together. Created by Mum of three and early years qualified Victoria Jennings.

🐛 Caterpillar Club (0–6 months)
Our gentle baby development class designed especially for newborns to early sitters. With soothing music, sensory play and bonding time, Caterpillar Club helps your baby feel safe, secure and ready to explore the world.

🐝 Busy Bees (6 months to walking)
Once your little one is on the move, Busy Bees is the perfect class. With upbeat action songs, music, movement and sensory fun, your baby will build confidence, coordination and social skills while making friends along the way.

## Caterpillar Club

The perfect class for babies aged 0–6 months.

Caterpillar Club is a gentle baby development class designed for babies aged 0–6 months. Our carefully crafted activities focus on promoting natural physical development, such as strengthening neck and tummy muscles, encouraging first rolls, and enhancing core strength through tummy time. These activities also help with colic, reflux, and constipation while strengthening the bond between parent and baby.

Each class incorporates sensory experiences using bespoke bright props and features weekly themes to create cherished memories during your baby's important first year. Activities are accompanied by music specifically designed to aid language development and body awareness. Every session concludes with a relaxing Bloom Baby Massage to soothe your baby, leaving them calm and ready for a peaceful nap.

## Busy Bees

For babies aged from 6 months to mobile.

Busy Bees is a highly interactive musical class designed for babies aged 6 months to walking (or 14 months). The class focuses on building your baby’s confidence in exploring the big wide world while encouraging both fine and gross motor skills, teaching body awareness, and stimulating their perception of sounds.

During theme time, babies explore a variety of bright props and exciting heuristic treasure baskets, all while developing social skills alongside fellow Bloom babies.

## Birthday Parties

Bloom Baby Classes offer a magical way to celebrate your little one's first birthday with a fun, engaging, and sensory-rich party experience. Designed especially for babies, their birthday parties are packed with music, movement, and interactive play, creating a truly special atmosphere for your child and their little friends. With vibrant props, catchy songs, and plenty of opportunities for laughter and connection, Bloom makes sure every moment is memorable. Their friendly and experienced team takes care of the entertainment, so you can relax and enjoy the celebrations with family and friends. To find out more about reserving your babies first birthday party contact your local franchise.

## Nursery Sessions

Bloom Baby Classes bring their multi-award-winning baby sessions directly to private day nurseries, offering a unique and enriching experience for little ones. Designed to support early development through sensory play, music, and movement, our classes provide a stimulating and engaging environment that complements nursery routines. With vibrant props, interactive activities, and a focus on learning through play, we help babies and toddlers build confidence, coordination, and social skills. Our experienced and passionate instructors ensure that every session is fun, engaging, and beneficial, giving nurseries a wonderful way to enhance their offering for families.

## Bloom Toddler Classes

Finally after years of demand for a follow on programme for our Bloom Baby Classes Bloom Toddler Classes is here. Fun and engaging activiies for toddlers and preschoolers.

A fresh new take on a toddler and preschool class for children from walking to four years of age. At our classes for toddlers and preschoolers children have fun learning new skills to prepare them for nursery and reception.

With our own bespoke music including a phonics song, numbers song and calming zen track our back to back activities for toddlers aid development and focus on first early learning goals.
