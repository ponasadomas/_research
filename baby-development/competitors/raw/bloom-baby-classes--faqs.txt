# Bloom Baby Classes – Frequently Asked Questions

## What age are Bloom Baby Classes for?

Bloom Baby Classes are designed from birth to 3 years. We offer:

- Caterpillar Club: gentle multi-sensory classes for newborns to 6 months.

- Busy Bees: lively, sensory music and movement for babies 6–15 months.

- Toddler Classes: engaging sessions for confident walkers up to 4 years.

## Are Bloom Baby Classes sensory classes?

Yes — all of our classes are award-winning sensory experiences. Every session includes music, movement, props and play designed to stimulate your little one’s senses and support their development.

## How do I find a class near me?

Simply use our Class Finder tool — enter your postcode and you’ll see all available Bloom Baby and Toddler Classes near you. You can book online in just a few clicks.

## Can I book a trial class before a full term?

Yes. Many of our franchisees offer trial sessions so you can experience Bloom with your baby before booking a block of classes.

## Do Bloom Baby Classes support child development?

Absolutely. Every class has been created with early years specialists to support:

- Bonding between parent and child

- Social skills and interaction

- Language development

- Fine and gross motor skills

- Emotional confidence

## Do I need to bring anything to class?

You may want to bring a blanket or muslin for baby to lie on during the class.

## Can I bring another adult with me?

Yes of couse just let you local leader know ahead of the class, we welcome all caregivers and family members along to enjoy our baby development classes.

## Do you offer a twin discount?

Yes we offer a discount for families with twins please contact your local franchise leader for more information

## Do you offer gift vouchers?

Yes we offer gift vouchers, a bloom baby class gift voucher makes the perfect present for a new mum or work colleague going on maternity leave.
