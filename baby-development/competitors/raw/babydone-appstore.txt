Running out of baby activity ideas? Track the day. Know what to do next.

BabyDone is a baby care tracker and an activity library in one app, for babies from birth to 2 years. Log sleep, feeds, diapers and growth — and find something to do with your baby when you have no idea what that should be. Mom, dad, grandparents and the nanny all see the same task board.

CARE — sleep, feeds and everything else in one place

Sleep, breastfeeding, bottle, diapers, pumping and growth
One-tap timers for sleep and nursing, with Live Activity on the Lock Screen
The whole day at a glance: clock dial, 24-hour ring or timeline — pick the view you like
"Next feed in about 40 minutes" — based on your baby's own rhythm, not a generic table
Stats for 7 / 14 / 30 days: day and night sleep, feeds, diapers, and the hours your baby usually sleeps
Growth charts with WHO percentiles — weight, length, head circumference
Rhythm assistant: gentle "it's time" reminders built from your own intervals (Pro)

TODAY — one shared tracker for the whole family

A single day plan everyone can see: who already did what, and what's still open
Reminders for vitamin D, bath, walk — the small things that slip your mind
Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets: tick things off without opening the app
Day templates and a day plan generator — massage, gym and play picked for your mood (Pro)
Daily stars for finished days: unlock app accents, the widget and templates

LIBRARY — activities that fit right now

Activities by month up to a year, then a shelf for 1 year and older
Tummy time, sensory play, songs and finger games
Newborn care, bonding and simple daily routines
Activities using everyday household items
A new tip every day for your baby's current age
Your own cards: save what works for your baby and reuse it

Everything is stored on your device and works offline. With Pro, the family shares one code and everything syncs across phones.

No planning, no overthinking — open the app, start a timer or pick an activity, and get on with your day.

Download BabyDone and never wonder "what do I do with my baby right now" again.

BabyDone is not a medical device and does not replace advice from your pediatrician.

BabyDone Pro

A subscription unlocks family sharing for up to 5 people (mom, dad, grandparents, nanny), cloud sync via your family code, the full activity library with tag filters, up to 30 tasks per day instead of 10, the day plan generator, the care rhythm assistant, favorites, unlimited custom cards, and a direct line to the developer. Monthly and yearly subscriptions are available with a 7-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID upon confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage your subscription and turn off auto-renewal anytime in your Apple ID settings.

# Version History

-

What’s new in BabyDone 1.7

The whole day on one dial
Naps on the outer ring, night sleep on the inner ring. Dashed arcs show when your baby’s next sleep or feed is likely. Tap any entry to see its time and duration.

Reminders that follow your baby’s rhythm
BabyDone learns from your sleep and feeding logs and reminds you before your baby is likely to need the next sleep or feed. Set how early you want the reminder and choose quiet hours.

Easier logging in Care
• Forgot to start the timer? Tap “Still sleeping” and the timer runs from the real start time
• New options: expressed milk, wet and dirty diapers, pumping
• Rearrange, resize, add or remove cards on the Care screen
• Units: ml/kg/cm or oz/lb/in, switch any time

New widgets
New Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for Feeding, Sleep, Diaper, Today and Care. On the Lock Screen, the ring fills up as the next feed or nap gets closer. Settings → Widgets shows how to add them.

Make it yours
7 app icons, 6 color themes, three background styles and the tab to open on launch. You can also rename tracker categories and turn haptics, sounds and the “day done” fireworks on or off.

More
• A day plan that matches how you feel: “So-so”, “Okay” or “Full of energy”
• A refreshed Library with a new hint for your baby’s age every day
• A new welcome flow and a short tour of the app
• Clearer iCloud backups and lots of small fixes

Thanks for being with us!
