Ashleigh

Hello, I’m Ashleigh – proud owner of Bloom Baby Classes West Lothian!

With over 13 years’ experience as a primary school teacher and now a mum to my beautiful daughter Mylah, I’m so excited to bring Bloom’s award-winning baby and toddler development classes to families across Armadale, Broxburn, Larbert, Linlithgow, East Calder and surrounding areas.

My own Bloom journey began when I was searching for enriching baby classes for Mylah. After travelling to Northumberland to experience Bloom sessions, I knew I had to bring this magic to West Lothian. The classes were engaging, sensory-rich, and full of joy—not just for babies, but for parents too.

At Bloom West Lothian, we offer baby and toddler classes that combine sensory play, baby massage, music, and themed fun in a warm and welcoming space. Whether you're bonding with a newborn or exploring with your toddler, you’ll find friendship, support, and development-led fun.

I can’t wait to welcome you and your little one to class and create special Bloom memories together!

See you soon, Ashleigh x
