Montessori for adolescents

Saturday 24 October 2026, 2 hour workshop including Q&A, US$29

It’s time to see teenagers in a new way! Montessori offers such a beautiful lens for understanding what our adolescents need from us.

In this workshop, Tony Evans (founder of Dundas Montessori School and Strata Montessori) and Marzieh Darling-Donnelly (graduate of Strata Montessori) will explore who the Montessori adolescent is, from their search for purpose and belonging to their growing need for independence and meaningful work. We’ll look at practical ways to bring these principles into everyday life at home, whether or not your teenager attends a Montessori school.
