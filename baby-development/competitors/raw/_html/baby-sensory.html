Support your baby’s development with research-backed classes parents trust and babies love

Baby Sensory® classes provide ideas for creative play, massage, tummy time, movement, visual development, textures, scents and music in simple practical ways that can be easily repeated at home. We also use a combination of original and traditional songs and rhymes to develop early speech and language skills, and sensory signing activities to help you and your baby communicate from birth.

Will a light help my child sleep better at night?

With the clocks changing, sleep can suddenly become a big topic for families. Changes to daylight can make us think more about bedtime routines, darker evenings and how we can help our little ones settle comfortably at night.

For some babies and children, a softly glowing night light might seem like the perfect solution. It can feel comforting and reassuring, particularly for a child who does not like being in complete darkness.

But could that little light actually be affecting their sleep?

How does your baby communicate before they can talk?

Long before babies learn to speak, they are already communicating with the people around them. From birth, babies use gestures, sounds, facial expressions and body movements to attract attention and show how they are feeling.

Why Baby Sensory® Is Beneficial From Birth

Many new parents wonder whether their baby is too young to join a class. The answer is simple: no.

Baby Sensory® was designed exclusively for babies from birth because learning begins long before your baby takes their first steps. In fact, learning starts in the womb, brain growth is most rapid during the first three months of life, and those early sensory experiences play an important role in your baby's overall development.

Our birth to 6 month classes are carefully designed to help you enjoy every stage of your baby's exciting development journey while giving you the confidence to learn and grow together.
