GET 24/7 PARENTING SUPPORT THROUGHOUT YOUR PARENTING JOURNEY Join 10 million+ parents who turn to BabySparks for expert parenting guidance. BabySparks offers 3 invaluable services to parents: For parents of children ages 0-3, we offer a personalized daily activity program. Each parent-led activity comes with a video tutorial and targets a critical area of development, helping you create the meaningful interactions that drive optimal development. Our proprietary smart adaptive technology learns and adapts to each child’s specific needs. Along with your activities, you get a daily feed with stage-based tips, access to our library of hundreds of development and parenting articles, tools to track milestones, progress and growth, and real-time reports. For parents of children of all ages, we offer hundreds of expert-led online classes, covering every area of parenting. With our premium subscription, you get unlimited access to all of our classes. Also for parents of children of all ages, our AI powered parenting expert, Ava, is always online, ready to answer any question you have about parenting. Chat for free for a limited time, or get unlimited access to Ava with our premium subscription. Whether you’re the parent of a baby or toddler who wants to set your child up to thrive, or a parent of an older child looking for support on your parenting journey, BabySparks has your back!   • Ok but not for preemies
    11/29/2020
    Keen ennobled

    Updating my review: I’m not sure if they went in and fixed the issue but I went back into the app and saw it has my sons adjusted age. So I give it a 5 star because it is a great app to help with your child’s development! Our son is 10 weeks old but he was born 34 weeks (5 weeks early). For preemies, you take how many weeks they have been born subtract that by how many weeks they were early and that’s their developmental/gestational age. So he is at 5 weeks right now. I used the app a few times and it only gave me videos for 2 months old and he wasn’t responding at all because he’s too young. He’s basically acting like he is only 1 month old. He isn’t cooing, following objects or responding to sounds. The app is great if the baby is born at full term. It does not change it up for preemies, even though it has the option to check the premature box and add his original delivery date.

    Developer Response

    Hello Keen, Thank you for your feedback, please write to us at support@babysparks.com we can review your account and see options together that can work for you and your baby.

  • Entertaining and helpful!
    11/29/2019
    TweetyBird0510

    BabySparks is great! No new parent needs to pressure themselves too much because most of these activities simulate normal day-to-day interaction and play that most great parents do with their baby. However, I’m one of those people who will get stuck in a rut and end up doing the same 5 activities with baby over and over every day. This app provides a wide range of activities to vary your playtime. I love that the activity descriptions are clear, the link to the various developmental milestones is described right there in the activity and every activity has an accompanying video. The only reason I didn’t give 5 stars is because of a very slight misstep in the UI. It is mildly annoying that when you look at an activity it stops you on your way back to the main list to ask how the activity was. I like to look at the daily activities first thing in the morning and get an idea of what we’re going to try that day and again would be nice not to have to click extra times to get back to the main list. It’s an unnecessary step. It’s a small thing but over time feels cumbersome. Slightly more thought on how much button pushing is required in this app would make it a 5-star treat for every new parent!

  • Really?
    11/17/2022
    LaCoconutGirl

    I’ve just created my children’s profiles and clicked on a milestone to view it, and I was prompted to subscribe. I then went to view a potty training video and it again prompted me to purchase the lesson or subscribe. I am a pediatric nurse, thus my purpose in downloading this app is to find an easy tool that I can refer my patient’s parents use. I was attracted to this app because of it’s high 5-star rating and because of the activities and lessons that they suggest parents to do with their children. It seemed to serve as an easy guide for parents who want to help their children but don’t know how. There are many resources out there that cost little to nothing — if you have the means, why not purchase? But if you don’t, like many of us, then it’s not feasible to spend money on something like this. I’ll refer my parents to CDC Milestone Tracker, which is by the way a free service. I do appreciate the thought behind this app and I know the developers mean well; I’ve seen them reply to most, if not all, reviews. Kudos to people who keep our children in the best interests because it seems to be a shortage in pediatric services nationwide.Best wishes to all.

    Developer Response

    Hi. Our purpose is in fact to help parents or caregivers who want some guidance on raising their babies. Thanks for bringing this out, for people who can't afford it, we have a free option which gives access to child’s growth and development in real-time, all articles, tips, milestones, and 2 activities per day. Thanks again for your valuable opinion and we hope you have an amazing day!

  • Needs more settings and customization
    06/04/2022
    MsSugarCookie

    The short animations when you complete/rate an activity and when you are a certain percentage towards being done with activities for the day are all very annoying and unskippable. You are unable to exit out of the activity while the animation is happening. The same type of thing happens when you want to see what an activity is and just want to watch the video before deciding to do it. After clicking on the activity and then go to exit out, a pop up automatically shows up for you to rate the difficulty of the activity. These hindrances would be all fine and well if there was a setting to turn them off BUT there is absolutely no settings menu or customizable options. A simple “turn off animations” and “turn off reminders to rate activities” would be a huge help!Otherwise, the activities are usually day to day type of things that are easy to add to normal play. I like to view the app at the beginning of the day and see how I can work in the activities during our daily routine. It gets a little repetitive sometimes but that’s fixed by having quite a few activities available to pick and choose.

    Developer Response

    Hey there! Thank you so much for your feedback. We truly appreciate it and our team will take it into account for future app updates. In the meantime, we recommend you close the difficulty survey whenever you are checking the daily program at the beginning of the day. Please remember if you want further assistance on the activities your baby is getting on the daily routine, you can always reach us at support@babysparks.com Have a wonderful day!
