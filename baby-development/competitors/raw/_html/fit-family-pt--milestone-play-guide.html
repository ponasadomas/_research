Floor to Explore: Early Developmental Milestone Play Guide (0-4 months)

A weekly guide to achieving early milestones through play, positioning, and tummy time! Pictures, videos, troubleshooting, warning signs, & more!   

 

This guide was created by pediatric physical therapists, experts in pediatric gross motor milestones! It is separated into one-month chapters, each featuring a motor milestone list, tummy time tips & troubleshooting, and an activity list broken into two-week time frames.  Take the guess work, or let's face it, googling, out of your baby's early development!

 

This jam-packed guide walks you through the first four months and has everything you need to start using play time to achieve milestones -- even in those early weeks when you're wondering if all they do is sleep!
