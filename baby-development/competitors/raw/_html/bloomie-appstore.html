Bloomie grows with your baby from birth through age two — with a daily play plan matched to their exact age, the real CDC milestone checklists, simple trackers with WHO growth percentiles, care reminders, memories, and warm health guidance. Your baby's data stays on your device. A FRESH PLAY PLAN EVERY DAY - 180+ development activities for 0–24 months, matched to your baby's age — corrected age for premature babies - Sized to fit the play time YOU have, and gently shaped by your goals and your baby's milestones - Clear steps, skills and safety notes — no special toys needed MILESTONES, MINUS THE ANXIETY - CDC “Learn the Signs. Act Early.” checklists at 2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18 and 24 months - Calm progress by area — never compared to other children - Honest guidance on when to check in with your pediatrician - Celebrate meaningful moments with photos and notes in Memories EVERY FEED, NAP AND GROWTH SPURT - Feeding, sleep, diaper and growth logs in two taps - WHO growth percentile curves for weight and length - Sleep rhythm: how long your baby has been awake compared with the typical window for their age - Optional care reminders for the things tired brains can forget - Export your tracking data to JSON or CSV whenever you like HEALTH ANSWERS AT 3AM - 100 illustrated guides on feeding, sleep, illness, safety and development — searchable in seconds - Vaccine and check-up schedules through age two, with optional reminders - Support for you too: postpartum recovery and wellbeing guides for mom PRIVATE BY DESIGN Your baby's profiles, logs, notes and photos stay on your device and are excluded from automatic backups. You can explicitly export or share records, or save a password-protected recovery file. No accounts. No ads. Anonymous product analytics help us improve onboarding and feature usefulness, but contain no baby-entered information. Bloomie does not access the advertising identifier or track you across apps or websites. Bloomie is educational and not a substitute for professional medical advice — always consult your pediatrician about your child's health and development. Bloomie requires an auto-renewing subscription. A free trial may be available. Prices and offers vary by region. Family Sharing is supported on the annual plan. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase or when the free trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store settings.
