Running out of baby activity ideas? Track the day. Know what to do next. BabyDone is a baby care tracker and an activity library in one app, for babies from birth to 2 years. Log sleep, feeds, diapers and growth — and find something to do with your baby when you have no idea what that should be. Mom, dad, grandparents and the nanny all see the same task board. CARE — sleep, feeds and everything else in one place Sleep, breastfeeding, bottle, diapers, pumping and growth One-tap timers for sleep and nursing, with Live Activity on the Lock Screen The whole day at a glance: clock dial, 24-hour ring or timeline — pick the view you like "Next feed in about 40 minutes" — based on your baby's own rhythm, not a generic table Stats for 7 / 14 / 30 days: day and night sleep, feeds, diapers, and the hours your baby usually sleeps Growth charts with WHO percentiles — weight, length, head circumference Rhythm assistant: gentle "it's time" reminders built from your own intervals (Pro) TODAY — one shared tracker for the whole family A single day plan everyone can see: who already did what, and what's still open Reminders for vitamin D, bath, walk — the small things that slip your mind Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets: tick things off without opening the app Day templates and a day plan generator — massage, gym and play picked for your mood (Pro) Daily stars for finished days: unlock app accents, the widget and templates LIBRARY — activities that fit right now Activities by month up to a year, then a shelf for 1 year and older Tummy time, sensory play, songs and finger games Newborn care, bonding and simple daily routines Activities using everyday household items A new tip every day for your baby's current age Your own cards: save what works for your baby and reuse it Everything is stored on your device and works offline. With Pro, the family shares one code and everything syncs across phones. No planning, no overthinking — open the app, start a timer or pick an activity, and get on with your day. Download BabyDone and never wonder "what do I do with my baby right now" again. BabyDone is not a medical device and does not replace advice from your pediatrician. BabyDone Pro A subscription unlocks family sharing for up to 5 people (mom, dad, grandparents, nanny), cloud sync via your family code, the full activity library with tag filters, up to 30 tasks per day instead of 10, the day plan generator, the care rhythm assistant, favorites, unlimited custom cards, and a direct line to the developer. Monthly and yearly subscriptions are available with a 7-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID upon confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage your subscription and turn off auto-renewal anytime in your Apple ID settings.
