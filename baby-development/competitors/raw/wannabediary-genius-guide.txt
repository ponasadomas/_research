# FAQs

###

Is this gentle and baby-friendly?

Absolutely. Every activity in the guide is designed to follow your baby’s natural pace — no overstimulation, no pressure, no unrealistic expectations. Just simple, nurturing ways to support development through love, touch, and play.

###

Do I need to buy special stuff?

Nope. Most activities use what you already have at home — your face, voice, hands, and a few household items. I’ve added links to the things we personally use, because moms keep asking for them, but they’re all completely optional. Use what works for you.

###

What if I don’t have time every day?

That’s the beauty of it, you don’t need to. These moments fit naturally into daily routines like diaper changes, feeding, or cuddles. It’s about connection, not perfection.

###

What if my baby is “behind” or “ahead” on milestones?

Then you’re exactly where you’re supposed to be. Every baby develops at their own rhythm. This guide helps you notice, support, and celebrate progress — not stress over timelines.

###

Can I start this right away?

Yes! You can open the guide today and try your first activity within minutes. Most take less than 10 minutes and happen naturally through play or daily care.
