## Questions you probably have

What is Kinedu, exactly?

It's a baby development app built by child development experts and grounded in early childhood science. It gives you a personalized daily plan of activities, tracks your baby's milestones, and connects you with real experts through live classes and courses. Think of it as having a pediatric development specialist in your pocket.

How do I actually get the app?

Sign up here, then check your email. You'll get a temporary password and download links for iPhone and Android. It takes about 2 minutes to be up and running.

What if I have questions or need help?

Just email us at hello@kinedu.com. A real person will get back to you. No digging through a help center to find a contact form.

Is any part of it actually free?

Yes. You get a full 7-day free trial with access to everything. After that, it's $7/month, or less if you go yearly. Cancel anytime. A lot of parents tell us it's cheaper than one pediatrician visit and they use it every single day.

What age is Kinedu for?

Kinedu covers pregnancy through age 6. Every activity, milestone, and piece of guidance is tailored to your baby's exact age and developmental stage, so the app grows with your child.

Do I need special toys?

Nope. Activities use everyday things you already have at home: spoons, towels, boxes, your own voice. No special equipment or fancy toys required.

Can I use it with multiple babies?

Yes! You can add multiple children to your account. Each one gets their own personalized plan based on their age and development. If you have a newborn and a toddler, both get exactly what they need.
