Hey, I’m Rachel

I’m a pediatric Occupational Therapist, writer, mom of 3 kiddos, teacher, Albert Schweitzer Fellow, early childhood advocate and total child development nerd.

My OT training and 14+ years of experience have fostered my passion for supporting whole family development - bodies, minds and relationships. I help parents rise above “doing it right” parenting pressures to learn, play and grow right alongside their kiddos.
