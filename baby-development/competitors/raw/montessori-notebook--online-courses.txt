Saturday 24 October 2026, 2 hour workshop including Q&A, US$29

It’s time to see teenagers in a new way! Montessori offers such a beautiful lens for understanding what our adolescents need from us.

In this workshop, Tony Evans (founder of Dundas Montessori School and Strata Montessori) and Marzieh Darling-Donnelly (graduate of Strata Montessori) will explore who the Montessori adolescent is, from their search for purpose and belonging to their growing need for independence and meaningful work. ## 52 weeks of Montessori

### US$49, Self-study online course, A (digital) Montessori card deck, delivered to your inbox one a week for 52 weeks

I’m excited to introduce a new Montessori card deck I’ve created just for you! Each card focuses on one aspect of the Montessori method, designed to help you implement it in your home each week.

These digital cards will be delivered by email weekly, allowing you to take it step by step without feeling overwhelmed. We’ll dive into one small topic each week, giving you time to practice and build on each skill progressively.

By the end of the year, your Montessori practice at home will feel more automatic, integrated, authentic, and easier to maintain.

## FAQ – online courses

###

I’m not sure which course to follow. Can you help me?

I have put together a helpful overview here to help you find the course you need right now.

###

I’d love the staff / families in my school to have access to your courses. Do you have school pricing?

Yes. On each course you’ll find two options for school pricing:

1. access for your staff (lifetime access for unlimited staff members)

2. access for families in your school (lifetime access for unlimited families)

###

Are your courses available in other languages? Or translated captions?

The courses are all in English. All videos are possible to watch with captions in many languages (including English, Dutch, Spanish, Arabic, Hindi). Contact me if you have a language request.
