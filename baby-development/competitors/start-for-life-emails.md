# Best Start in Life / Start for Life (UK Government) — free personalised weekly emails

Captured 2026-10-25. URLs: https://www.beststartinlife.gov.uk/ (home), https://beststartinlife.gov.uk/weekly-email/. Raw files: `_raw/competitors/start-for-life-emails.txt`, `start-for-life-emails--weekly.txt`. Sign-up not submitted (personal data).

- **Buyer/trigger:** any UK parent, from pregnancy on.
- **Mechanism (≤8 words):** Emails timed to pregnancy week or baby's age.
- **Promise:** "practical, age-appropriate support" on development, feeding, tiredness, weaning, wellbeing.
- **Proof:** UK Government/NHS; "96% subscribers said they would recommend" (YouGov).
- **Price:** free.
- **Format:** weekly emails for the first 12 weeks after birth, then monthly.
- **Personalization:** due date or baby's date of birth.
- **Guarantee:** n/a.
- **Angles and hooks:** official trust; "questions big & small".
- **Objections answered:** what to expect in the emails.

Verbatim:

> Weekly emails tailored to your stage of pregnancy or your child’s age, with personalised tips and activities to support your child’s health and development.

> We’ll send you weekly emails for the first 12 weeks of your baby’s life, then monthly emails after that.

> 96% subscribers said they would recommend the Start for Life emails to new parents (according to a YouGov survey).

## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

A free, government-backed, age-timed email programme already reaches UK parents.
