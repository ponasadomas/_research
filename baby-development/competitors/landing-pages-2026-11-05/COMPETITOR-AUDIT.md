### Synthesis for the landing-page designer

These are observations from the readable subset, not prevalence estimates for the entire niche.

1. **Sell a specific result in the first screen.** Current pages usually name the benefit before explaining the product in detail: easier sleep, confidence, movement capability or reading. MamiVio’s version should name getting through the day with less guessing, show the relevant baby age, then explain what is purchased. Sources: [Baby Sleep Answers](https://www.babysleepanswers.com/), [Baby Acrobatics](https://www.babyacrobatics.com/), [Brightest Beginning](https://brightestbeginning.com/courses/play-develop-thrive/).
2. **Show what the customer receives.** Curricula, kit components, reference sheets, videos and member-area previews make intangible purchases legible. MamiVio’s strongest equivalent is a readable sample day, with its sequence and permission to adapt visibly built into the sample. This is a proposed translation of the observed product-demonstration pattern. Sources: [signing kit](https://babysignlanguage.com/product/deluxe-kit/), [Baby Tour Guide](https://babytourguide.teachable.com/p/the-montessori-babies-course-promo?coupon_code=MONTESSORIBABIES&product_id=3768385).
3. **Place reassurance at the decision point.** Refund language and suitability/access answers recur on course and information-product pages. For MamiVio show the actual price, one-time-payment wording, included age/version, delivery format and real refund terms beside purchase controls. Sources: [Brightest Beginning](https://brightestbeginning.com/courses/play-develop-thrive/), [Baby Sleep Solution](https://www.babysleepsolution.com/).
4. **Use age and human specificity as trust cues.** Age-labeled parent accounts, clearly named authors and stated reviewer roles are stronger models for this offer than generic decorative approval badges. MamiVio must supply its own genuine evidence. Sources: [Baby Acrobatics](https://www.babyacrobatics.com/), [Baby Sleep Site](https://www.babysleepsite.com/).
5. **Do not infer a universal winning palette.** The three additional visually inspected pages here range from pale/teal to starry navy/yellow to historical green/grey. Only the contemporary Baby Sleep Answers treatment is close to a reassuring practical resource. Its muted CTA color should not be copied without checking text contrast. Palette choice needs to serve MamiVio’s tone and usability, then be tested on MamiVio traffic.

The most relevant references in this half of the list are Brightest Beginning for the reassurance and deliverables structure; Baby Sleep Answers for tone and age relevance; Baby Acrobatics for a tangible digital offer and specific review roles; and the Baby Sleep Site for age-oriented organization. Recovered original sales pages are compared as a separate commercial cohort in [HISTORICAL-DESIGN-EVIDENCE.md](HISTORICAL-DESIGN-EVIDENCE.md); their relevance depends on offer and audience fit rather than age alone.

### Evidence and coverage

All 21 original URLs were attempted using the web reader. Browser navigation and DOM inspection then resolved access gaps. The layout findings below concern content order and page components. No palette, typography, responsive behavior or conversion performance is inferred from text extraction. ### Implications for the MamiVio brief

1. **Lead with relief from decisions.** BabyPillars makes organization and prioritization central; Healthiest Baby names guilt and uncertainty; Milestones & Motherhood repeatedly emphasizes confident interpretation. This supports a positioning hypothesis centered on getting through the day with confidence. It does not prove which headline converts best.
2. **Show current-age relevance early.** BabyPillars, The Sleep Lady and Milestones & Motherhood make age or developmental stage a navigation decision. A short age selector or plainly labeled current-age preview can adapt that convention within one landing page.
3. **Make the purchase concrete.** Healthiest Baby and The Montessori Notebook identify format, age and price directly. MamiVio needs an actual plan preview, clear delivery and visible one-time payment language.
4. **Treat authority as specific evidence.** A named relevant author/reviewer, visible credentials and a precise account of what they did are stronger design inputs than a generic expert badge. This is an inference from the professional-led sources, not a measured comparison.
5. **Separate reassurance from overpromising.** Reduced uncertainty fits the target; guaranteed developmental outcomes, fear about future failure and exceptional achievement examples risk shifting the promise away from an easier day.
6. **Use one offer.** Catalogs, multiple courses, subscriptions and consultation routes serve broader businesses. They provide components to study, but their information architecture should not dictate MamiVio's single current-age plan page.

The [expanded commercial evidence search](CONVERSION-EVIDENCE.md) found seller-reported split-test improvements at Reading Head Start and an agency-reported sales improvement for The Baby Sleep Site. Their disclosures omit important experiment details and do not supply a color-only winner. Use these reports alongside the visual comparison, rather than treating page appearance as performance evidence.
