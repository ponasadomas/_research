# Published conversion evidence for the supplied competitor brands

Researched 5 November 2026. This report investigates public commercial evidence behind the supplied mother-and-baby websites, beyond observations of their current sales pages. It supports the comparative visual audit and the revised MamiVio description.

**The earlier statement that no reviewed source supplied conversion results was too broad.** Targeted searches found quantified seller or agency reports for **two supplied brands**: Reading Head Start and The Baby Sleep Site. They support specific lessons about mobile purchase usability, video presentation and a broader website redesign. They do not establish a winning color for MamiVio.

## Evidence found for supplied brands

### Reading Head Start

The seller's [affiliate resources page](https://www.readingheadstart.com/affiliate-tools/) reports the following changes:

| Change described by seller | Reported result | Evidence type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New mobile-oriented order form | 36.8% increase in conversions | Explicitly described as a split test. |
| Animated introduction to the video sales letter | 37.4% increase in conversions | Explicitly described as a split test. |
| More advanced custom order form | 79% increase in conversions | Before/after improvement claimed; experiment method unspecified. |
| First upsell offer | 368% improvement and approximately 40% acceptance of an $89 upgrade | Funnel monetization claim; not the initial landing-page conversion. |

The entries supply month labels but no confirmed test year, control/variant screenshots, baseline conversion rates, sample sizes, traffic sources, duration, allocation or statistical confidence. Nearby 2020 link labels cannot date each result. The statements are seller-reported, commercially motivated and not independently validated.

**Applicable lesson:** prioritize mobile purchase usability and test presentation changes. Neither a color-only comparison nor the winning CTA color is disclosed. The upsell result should not be used to predict quiz starts or initial purchases.

### The Baby Sleep Site

The agency's [client case study](https://www.cazarin.com/baby-sleep-site-case-study/) reports a **10% sales increase** following work on the website. Its partnership began in 2022. Work included homepage usability, graphics and accessibility, a logo redesign, three short videos, marketing strategy and blog promotion. The agency also reports increased consultation inquiries immediately after launch.

The case provides [before](https://www.cazarin.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/before.jpg) and [after](https://www.cazarin.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/after.jpg) images. The image paths contain March 2023, but that does not establish the measurement period. It gives no baseline sales total, traffic normalization, duration, control group or experiment allocation.

**Applicable lesson:** an intentional homepage and coherent brand presentation accompanied a reported commercial improvement for a supplied competitor. The result concerns a combined project and sales, not an isolated button, color or visit-to-purchase rate. Attribute it to the agency and do not decompose the 10% into unsupported component effects.

### Baby Sleep Miracle

The seller's [affiliate resource center](https://affiliates.babysleepmiracle.com/) advertises a direct-response video sales funnel and strong conversion performance, but gives no numerical conversion result or controlled comparison. It offers alternative landing-page arrangements, including text, text/video and autoplay/exit behavior variants.

**Applicable lesson:** the seller actively supports different funnel presentations. Their availability is evidence of alternatives, not proof of which version wins. Third-party affiliate directories repeat numerical claims, but those were not accepted as verified primary evidence.

### Baby Sign Language

Co-founder Gajan Retnasaba's [first-person project account](https://gajan.com/project/bsl/) reports approximately **1.2 million visits per year**, historical growth to roughly **4,000 daily visitors**, and the later introduction of physical kits alongside free resources. It does not give sales conversion, revenue, test variants or color results.

**Applicable lesson:** this is evidence of seller-reported reach and product development. Traffic volume cannot identify a converting visual treatment, particularly because the founder notes that a substantial audience consisted of ASL students as well as parents.

## Supporting qualitative evidence

GetResponse's [ecommerce email marketing article](https://www.getresponse.com/ru/blog/email-marketing-dlya-internet-magazinov) includes direct comments from Baby Sleep Site founder Nicole Johnson about free-content sequences, explaining the service and asking checkout abandoners for feedback. The article is dated 19 May 2021. It provides useful evidence of the brand's funnel practice, but no Baby Sleep Site-specific uplift is disclosed. Its unrelated general email averages and another brand's open rate must not be attributed to Baby Sleep Site.

The search also found a first-person service-provider account concerning The Sleep Lady's professional-training presale. Its reported interest list and profit concern a different offer and audience. It was excluded from consumer landing-page/color conclusions. [Interview source](https://mixergy.com/interviews/collins-interactive-offer-interview/)

## What the evidence justifies for MamiVio

1. **Put mobile clarity and the purchase path ahead of palette speculation.** A supplied seller explicitly reports improvements after changing mobile and custom order forms. This is closer evidence for reducing interaction friction than simply observing a green website.
2. **Distinguish the quiz entry from the sales and checkout stages.** None of the supplied-brand results above isolates the current task's headline/subhead/start-button screen. Transfer the practical lesson about clear interaction, then evaluate quiz starts, completions and eventual purchases separately.
3. **Use repeated visual patterns as a reasoned starting point.** Actual design choices should come from the separate rendered-page comparison, with comparable offers grouped together. The published figures strengthen the case for intentional presentation; they do not rank every current page.
4. **Do not label a palette as proven.** No color-only experiment attributable to a supplied brand surfaced in this search. This is a bounded search finding, not a claim that no private tests exist.
5. **Keep evidence strength visible.** The strongest item found is a seller's stated split test with missing methodology. A 10% overall sales report, a large traffic claim, funnel variants and a reassuring visual style are different kinds of evidence.

## Adjacent evidence outside the supplied list

For context only, FunnelFox's [Reading.com case study](https://funnelfox.com/cases/revenue-and-cr-uplift-after-funnel-redesign/), dated 17 February 2026, reports a redesigned screen sequence/paywall and added Apple Pay: 50% higher conversion in testing, 40% at scale and 30% higher revenue. Reading.com is **not Children Learning Reading or Reading Head Start** and was not in the supplied list. This combined-flow case is not included in supplied-brand counts or color conclusions.
