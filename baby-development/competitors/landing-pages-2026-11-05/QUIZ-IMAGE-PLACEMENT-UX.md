# MamiVio quiz image placement and UX evidence

Reviewed 5 November 2026. **Putting the photograph below the CTA is a mobile space-management choice, not a demonstrated universal conversion rule.** The previous description made that recommendation too categorical. An image above the headline or beside the copy can also be appropriate.

## What the research supports

Nielsen Norman Group's [Big Pictures on Small Screens](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/big-pictures-small-screens/) explains how top-of-page images can push useful content down on mobile. It recommends judging the image's information value, then removing, cropping, resizing or reorganizing it as appropriate. It includes examples of moving associated text above an image. This is responsive-design guidance, not a controlled test proving that a CTA should always precede an image.

NN/g's [Photos as Web Content](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/photos-as-web-content/) distinguishes images relevant to the visitor's task from decorative imagery. Its eye-tracking examples show that attention depends on what an image communicates. A mother/baby lifestyle image can establish audience and context; it does not demonstrate what the report contains or validate the assessment. The research does not establish that stock-style photographs necessarily increase conversion.

NN/g's [Scrolling and Attention](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolling-and-attention/) supports giving important information and actions early exposure. Its 2018 study used desktop eye tracking across varied tasks on 1920 × 1080 screens. It does not directly measure mobile baby-quiz conversions or compare top, right and bottom photo placements.

## What the supplied competitors show

The [current visual audits](CORRECTIVE-VISUAL-AUDIT-A.md) contain viable image-first mobile arrangements: Brightest Beginning's action starts around y703 and Baby Sleep Answers' around y548 in a 390 × 844 viewport. Both fit despite placing the photo before the copy. [BabyPillars](CORRECTIVE-VISUAL-AUDIT-B.md) puts text and its action before the large photo, with its action around y719. These observations establish alternative arrangements, not a winning order.

## Recommended starting arrangement

**Desktop:** headline, subhead and action on the left, photograph on the right. This can expose the emotional image, offer and action together without requiring a sequential image-first page. Treat it as a fit-to-task recommendation, not a performance result inferred from prevalence.

**Mobile:** compare a compact image above the headline with the earlier text-and-action-first version. A top crop around 140–180 px high is a practical design hypothesis; check the complete headline, explanation and button on representative short screens. Avoid automatically stacking a desktop-sized portrait above the text.

Direct mail's attention→interest→action logic still applies. The online difference is a variable viewport, responsive reflow and interactive next step. The useful goal is understanding and motivation before action, rather than a fixed order of rectangles. Evaluate qualified quiz starts, quiz completion and eventual purchases; an eye-tracking pattern or start-click increase alone does not establish the best commercial layout.
