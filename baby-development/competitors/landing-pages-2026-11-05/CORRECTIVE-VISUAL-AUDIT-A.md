# Competitor visual comparison for the first source group

Observed 5 November 2026. This corrects the earlier assumption that a cream and forest-green editorial treatment should be the default for MamiVio. The supplied pages show several distinct commercial design approaches. Green is one observed action color among others.

## Method and coverage

The assigned group contains nine current brands across ten supplied URLs, plus the user-specified 2016 archive. Seven current brands and the archive yielded rendered browser DOM and computed styles. Baby Sleep Solution was blocked by an expired certificate; both Baby Sign Language paths timed out, including a retry of the WWW version. Their earlier readable text remains useful for offer analysis, but they contribute no new color or visual-layout counts here.

Desktop measurements used 1440 × 1000. Mobile measurements used 390 × 844 after resizing and a font/resize settling delay. The archive instead produced a 980-pixel layout viewport, consistent with an old fixed-width page. Baby Sleep Miracle's mobile inspection was interrupted by a navigation/beforeunload dialog and is unverified.

The [machine-readable observations](CORRECTIVE-VISUAL-AUDIT-A.json) retain per-brand source URLs, exact colors, CTA positions and evidence status. A fresh [Tour Guide screenshot](raw/corrective-screenshots/tour-desktop.jpg) was saved and inspected. The existing [Brightest screenshot](raw/screenshots/brightest-desktop.jpg) corroborates that page's rendered layout and palette. Other fresh observations below are computed-style/geometry evidence; no new screenshot file was obtained for those pages because screenshot calls stalled. Colors were not inferred from reader text.

“Primary CTA” means the principal purchase or acquisition action, excluding login, menu navigation, newsletter forms and FAQs. Viewport visibility means the entire measured control falls within the target height. Video-player overlays were not inspected inside third-party embeds. The pages disclose no performance results through these observations.

## What recurs in this group

Use these denominators when combining with the second source group. These are counts of observed brands, not experiments or independent conversions.

| Pattern | Count and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Orange or yellow main CTA | **3 of 6** current brands with an identified primary action color: Tour Guide, Brightest, Children Learning Reading. |
| Teal main CTA | **2 of 6**: Baby Sleep Answers and The Baby Sleep Site. |
| Green main CTA | **1 of 6**: Baby Acrobatics. Its later CTAs also vary. |
| Filled, rounded main action | **6 of 6** identified controls; corner treatments range from 5–8px to pill-shaped. |
| Sans-serif principal page headline | **5 of 6** current brands with a page-level headline that could be classified; The Baby Sleep Site uses serif. The VSL page's embedded title is unclassified. |
| Centered headline-led hero | **4 of 6** classified page-level heroes: Tour Guide, Children Learning Reading, Acrobatics and Sleep Site. |
| Side-by-side image and copy | **2 of 6**: Brightest and Sleep Answers. Their image precedes the copy on the measured mobile layout. |
| Entire main action in the first viewport | **3 of 6** in both desktop and mobile measurements. The VSL page is excluded because its player behavior is unverified. |

This group does not support treating a forest-green button, serif headline or cream background as a default convention. The clearer repeated visual feature is a distinct filled action against the surrounding content, with a rounded shape. The split between saturated orange/yellow, teal and green needs to remain visible in any recommendation.

Action colors and overall brand palettes must be coded separately. Tour Guide has a green header but a yellow enrollment button. Brightest has blue information styling but orange enrollment. Counting both as simply “green” or “blue” would conceal the conversion interface being studied.

## Direct response and course pages serve different journeys

Children Learning Reading and Baby Sleep Miracle prioritize a promise and demonstration video before the paid offer. Brightest and Tour Guide expose enrollment earlier. This is a meaningful difference in persuasion sequence; it should not be dismissed as modern versus outdated or good versus bad from appearance alone.

For a quiz entry page consisting of a headline, subhead and start button, enrollment/start-style heroes are closer structural references than a long product explanation or video-led sales letter. That is a fit-to-task inference. It does not prove that an early action or a particular color improves conversion.

## Historical comparison

The exact [Help Your Child Speak archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20160110055329/http://helpyourchildspeak.com/) uses a white page, green `#6A9730` title masthead, white Georgia serif title and gray multi-link navigation. Purchase is a navigation destination rather than a prominent standalone hero button. Its fixed-width brochure structure remains distinguishable on mobile emulation. Keep this as historical sales-design evidence; do not mix it into counts of currently rendered acquisition controls.
