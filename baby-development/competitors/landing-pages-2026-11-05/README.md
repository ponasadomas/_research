# Landing pages: how other sellers' pages look, and what is reported to convert

Question 4: who is selling to her, and what is working?

- **Collected:** 5 November 2026, by an agent with a browser, for the design of the mamiVio
  quiz landing page and sales page. Moved here on 2026-11-17 from the mamiVio site repo.
- **Start with `COMPETITOR-AUDIT.md`:** all 43 seller URLs, where each leads today, what is
  offered, and links to recovered older versions.
- **`CORRECTIVE-VISUAL-AUDIT-A.md` and `-B.md`** (with `.json`): measured colours, type, main
  action and desktop/mobile composition of the current pages.
- **`COMPARISON-DATA.json`:** 14 current brands, 11 identifiable main-action colours, with the
  counting denominators.
- **`HISTORICAL-DESIGN-EVIDENCE.md`:** 12 URL records across 11 brands with recovered styling.
- **`CONVERSION-EVIDENCE.md`:** published conversion reports from sellers and agencies, their
  limits, and the search log.
- **`VISUAL-REFERENCES.md`:** four captured reference pages with contrast calculations.
- **`QUIZ-IMAGE-PLACEMENT-UX.md`:** how quiz pages order and place imagery, and the limits of
  that evidence.
- **`raw/`:** `screenshots/` (4), `corrective-screenshots/` (1), `raw-visual/` (15 measurement
  files, one per page and screen size).
- **Not here:** what we designed from this (design brief, design findings, quiz landing
  description, desktop review, photos) is our own work and stays with the site, in
  `~/GitHub/mamiVio.com/mamivio.com-dev/design-reference/landing-page-design-2026-11-05/`.
  Links to those files were turned into plain paths when the folder was split, and links to
  the screenshots gained the `raw/` prefix. Nothing else in the files was changed.
