## Reading this market — interpretation

- Development money on Meta is apps (Kinedu 208 ads) and local classes (Bloom since 2023, Gymboree, Tots Play). In the 2026-10-25 samples the only advertised paid guide/course in English is Baby Acrobatics and the only document-shaped seller with ads is Pirmi's HU clone. None of the PDFs found (US$9.99–27, £11.99) appeared in those samples; the sweep did not search "baby activities", "tummy time" or "montessori baby", so this is not proof they never advertise.
- The shared promise is "know exactly what to do with your baby today, for this age" (Kinedu H1; Baby Acrobatics "Stop Googling…"; Little Steps "instead of searching the internet"). Personalization by exact age — even corrected age — is standard in apps at $4–13/month.
- Tummy time is a paid topic of its own ($20–40 courses from Pathways, CanDo Kiddo, Lovevery).
- The behind/delay/diagnosis door is used by Baby Acrobatics' second lander, First Step Method, Harkla and Kinedu's "ahead of pace" readings — recorded, outside Mamivio's lines.
