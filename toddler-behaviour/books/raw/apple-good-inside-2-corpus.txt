TITLE: A positive, hopeful lesson in parenting.
Dr. Becky guides the reader through many of the classic parental challenges providing clear and pragmatic tools along the way. She outlines the typical approach to each that simply doesn’t work and I’m embarrassed to admit to seeing myself in each example. When offered a more effective strategy it feels obvious in hindsight, but somehow never occurred to me in the moment. I only wish that Dr. Becky dedicated a section of the book to discuss parenting teenagers. I hadn’t realized when I started reading “Good inside” that it would be geared for small children. If only I had this book when my kids were younger.

TITLE: i didn't like her parenting advices.
i gave 2 points for the effort spent on the production of the book. before reading the book, i was listening to the author's podcasts and her approaches to raising children seemed very logical. however, when i read the book, i realized that the advice given is always the same: to make children feel that they are always with them verbally as parents in their problems. there was no need to write a whole book to express the same idea. anyway, after reading the book, even the author's voice started to sound repulsive. we cannot always be child-focused and we cannot be around them at all times as if they were our gods.
