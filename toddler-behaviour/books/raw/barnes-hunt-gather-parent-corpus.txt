TITLE: Excellent!
I wish this book had been available when I was raising children. I bought it for my daughter who is a new parent and read it before passing it along. It provides simple, sensible advice based on anthropological/sociological evidence. After reading the examples is obvious that everything she suggests is simple and common sense -- but often so different from how we raise our children here and now. I love how she uses her own child and parenting challenges as examples as well. I will be buying this for every expectant parent moving forward!
