
## 5. The other side

### 5.1 Daycare and preschool

Daycare is the deadline, the reporter and the judge, for potty and for behaviour. Every reader found this; TR04–TR09 confirmed it in their own windows.

- *Potty rules that set the date or undo home training:* "daycare will not even offer the potty until he is consistently self initiating." [tb04-0113]; "later she said that’s against policy since he can’t self-initiate." [tb04-0114]; "My kid’s teachers seem irritated I even asked." [tb04-0115]; "They also won’t have him out of nappies until he is consistently using the toilet at home for five full days" [tb09-0286]; "So I have to keep taking him out of preschool, which I’m paying £600 a month for, for a week at a time" [tb09-0287]; "we had to resume sending her in pull-ups because they could no longer continue being out of ratio to clean her up." [tb08-0191]; "daycare has asked us NOT to use one explicitly because it can cause setbacks at school" (a toddler potty) [tb08-0264]; "I dont control the diapers, daycare makes that call" [tb08-0112].
- *Behaviour reports and expulsion:* "90% of the time the communication report complains about something that I think is developmentally normal. I worry that they think he’s a problem child." [tb08-0180]; "they can’t work with him through his emotions because of the class size." [tb08-0114]; "my child is being too disruptive to the other kids." [tb07-0273]; "Then I had to pull him out of his preschool because of his behavior." [tb05-0048]; "they decided to give my son an immediate suspension for." [tb06-0055].
- *Nowhere else to go:* "daycare options are incredibly limited where we are" [tb08-0076]; "everywhere else is full" [tb07-0308]; "We’ve been waiting for a spot to open at a few daycares close to home, but nothing yet." [tb07-0127]; and behaviour that only happens at school cannot be worked on at home: "There's no way we can modify the behavior" [tb05-0234].
- *Staff blame the parent:* "heavily implying that it's my fault she's like that." [tb03-0140]; "it feels like they’re chastising ME every time." [tb07-0102]; "You must hold him all the time, huh?" [tb01-0150].
- *A different child at daycare:* dry or calm there, not at home [tb04-0175] [tb09-0289] [tb09-0232], or the reverse [tb02-0156] [tb09-0033].
- *Daycare as the ally:* "It finally clicked when he started at a new daycare and was required to come to school in underwear." [tb06-0253]; "Our daycare provider decided it was time to potty train all 3 boys" [tb06-0271]; "Thankfully daycare is very supportive and willing to keep trying." [tb07-0312]; "they said they tend to support what the parents want" [tb09-0212]; a parent who does not want daycare "to do the heavy lifting" [tb06-0034]. In Denmark there is "very little pressure to have children potty trained before they start kindergarten at age 3." [tb08-0222] (one quote).
- *A nanny's view of the parents:* "Then as i'm leaving they put her in a diaper." [tb01-0175]; "please don't make us do it until YOU are ready." [tb01-0176]. *A daycare teacher's view:* "I see y’all’s kids meltdown." [tb09-0247].

### 5.2 Partners and fathers

`partner_disagreement` is tagged on 125 quotes. On potty, the partner is often the one who wants to stop or refuses the naked method; on tantrums, he is as often the stricter one (interpretation from the quotes below).

- *Softer or opting out on potty:* "he was like absolutely the fuck not...so that was that." [tb01-0165]; "He's a hard NO on naked" [tb03-0130]; "My husband wants to give up but man I'm so done with diapers!" [tb04-0302]; "he’s the one that desperately wants to quit" [tb06-0003]; "my partner is against this method." [tb07-0041]; "my partner says we should just let the preschool sort out the preschool issues themselves" [tb01-0169].
- *The partner who adds pressure:* "my husband put a lot of pressure on not peeing on the floor" [tb08-0052].
- *Stricter on behaviour:* "My husband is starting to think we need to be harsher and utilize punishments." [tb07-0315]; a father: "I can be a bit harsh. Trying to not be like my parents!" [tb03-0227]. The reverse household exists too: "I am more short and strict when hes acting out" [tb08-0067].
- *What mothers hear from partners:* "“we have a difficult child” and “it is not about you”." [tb04-0002]; "she'll get over it." [tb06-0018]; "My husband told me I’m not managing my stress well" [tb06-0230]; "My husband says I’m exaggerating" [tb09-0291]; "it always come down he pays the bills" [tb05-0070].
- *A list to hand him:* "I need a list of suggestions for my husband (and myself) on techniques and to-dos during the terrible twos meltdowns." [tb02-0251]; "He does well with a do this list rather than just this is wrong." [tb02-0252]; "Going to share it with my husband too so we can learn some tips and both stay consistent." [tb03-0246]. Three quotes, from two readers.
- *Fathers in their own words:* rejected — "he will only ask my wife, even if I'm the one next to him because he doesn't trust it when I say no." [tb04-0291]; "2 hours of screaming at 3am can break my resolve quite effectively" [tb08-0217]; worried for the mother — "my wife is in tears because she feels like my daughter is doing this on purpose to mess with her." [tb03-0224]; the 3-day weekend ending — "feel bad that my wife now has to try to push through it on her own" [tb07-0299]; wanting firmer potty rules over his wife's and mother's objections [tb05-0231]; "We decided we don’t want to spank him, but I also don’t want to do this overly gentle parenting thing either." [tb07-0150].
- *The child who rejects one parent, both ways.* Mothers passed over: "she'll say \"No, Daddy read book\"" is not quoted; she reports "go away, Daddy sit" [tb08-0081]; "I feel like my husband secretly enjoys being the favorite parent and part of me is starting to resent him for it." [tb08-0083]; "But knowing that intellectually doesn’t make it hurt any less" [tb08-0286]. Fathers passed over: "she just rarely wants to be with him at all." [tb05-0024]; a father who has stopped trying to win [tb05-0256]. TR06 found mothers rejected for fathers about as often as the reverse.
- *Separated parents:* "it feels like I can never build sustained momentum." [tb01-0144]; "A lot of the regressions started during the separation." [tb05-0093]; custody handovers [tb05-0219].

### 5.3 Grandparents and relatives

Readers disagreed on the direction. TR01, TR06 and TR07 found relatives pushing harder discipline more often; TR05 and TR09 found them more often undoing training by being softer. Both are well supported.

- *Harder:* a mother whose parents do not want them to be gentle parents (tb06-0041, restricted); "My family keeps telling me I’m too gentle on her" [tb07-0279]; "They’re saying how we need to be more structured" [tb07-0075]; "I’m being told by some others that I need to be more firm." [tb04-0191].
- *Softer, undoing potty:* "My mother in law put her back in a nappy yesterday because she had 5 accidents." [tb04-0295]; "won’t let me put her in underwear or pull-ups at her house" [tb05-0012]; "One of the grandmas ended up putting her in pull-ups for a day" [tb09-0156]; a grandmother "who immediately caved" [tb05-0138].
- *Pressure by attention:* "asking him allll the time if he had to pee" [tb08-0218].
- *Help that costs:* "I need my mom’s help because I have a baby and a toddler" [tb02-0006]; "Like I can’t keep it together for 3.5 days a week" [tb08-0036].
- *A father to his own parents:* "either help me or mind" [tb06-0054].

### 5.4 Parents who reject gentle parenting

Fatigue and doubt, rarely outright rejection: "I’ve tried gentle parenting, I’ve tried not gentle parenting" [tb04-0029]; "my kiddo I am finding cannot be gentle parented" [tb08-0033]; "I fundamentally disagree with don’t let your toddler see you mad or frustrated." [tb06-0225]; "I cannot always redirect or get on his level or be an instagram parent." [tb06-0227]; "Everything parenting wise seems like it’s “bad” now." [tb08-0032]; "actual gentle parenting, not permissive parenting." [tb03-0285]. `gentle_parenting_backlash` is tagged on 36 quotes.

### 5.5 Buyers who cancelled

Almost absent. No reader found a Good Inside or BLF cancellation. The nearest are a BLF course dropped on day two [tb01-0008] [tb01-0010] and a mother who loves Good Inside but worries that how much repair she needs means she is getting too much wrong (tb09-0141, restricted). This side of the door is unharvested (section 13).

## 6. What they ask, want and fear

### 6.1 The exact asks for a plan

- **"What do I do first?"** "If you’ve been through a regression like this, what would you actually do first? Would you rerun the blocks? Add stickers? Back off prompting? Use pull-ups temporarily?" [tb05-0162].
- **The full spec, in one question.** "Did you do pants off, or underwear, or something else? At what intervals did you take them to the bathroom? What worked well and what didn’t? What did regression look like? How many days do you try before you take a break" [tb09-0206].
- **With daycare built in.** "And how did you coordinate your approach with daycare, especially around poop refusal?" [tb07-0109]; "What do i do? Send him commando and tell them to prompt every hour?" [tb04-0101].
- **For the long run, not the first days.** "Any resources focused on the long term and maintaining it? Everything I've found is about the first few days." [tb01-0043]; "a long game approach rather than smashing it into one weekend but what does that look like?" [tb04-0035].
- **For this specific pattern.** "I’m just trying to figure out how to handle this specific pattern of regression." [tb09-0005]; "Did you also have a child that’s not easily influenced by rewards?" [tb05-0183].
- **Words to say.** "Just looking for tips on what to say at drop off" [tb03-0086]; "How do you handle these? How do you speak to them?" [tb06-0228]; "how do you know when to intervene?" [tb07-0120].
- **Fast, for a solo morning.** "In the mornings I'm solo and we don't have time to wait around so I need solutions that can help get me out the door avoid/dispel the tantrum quickly." [tb02-0239].
- **For the parent too.** "also need tips for us so we don't all lose our minds over here!" [tb05-0254]; "How do you regulate YOURSELF as an overwhelmed and disregulated parent?" [tb04-0254]; "Hoping for some patience techniques i can use" [tb04-0013].
- **A person, not a document.** "Is there someone I can talk to about our habits and how my toddler behaves and have someone tell me if there are changes I can make to be a better parent?" [tb01-0036]; "Is there someone who I can pay to come to my house and help us?" [tb07-0027].
- **A route.** "I need a clear route to improvement." [tb02-0108].

Against the asks: "Tips and tricks are helpful, but sometimes you just need to hear from other parents who just GET IT!" [tb03-0113]; "I’m not looking for advice. We will just keep soldiering on" [tb07-0064]; "I’m just really, really struggling, and I need to know I’m not alone." [tb09-0265]. `personalization` is tagged on 58 quotes; `information_overload` on 87.

### 6.2 What they want

To leave the house: "I just want to take my kid to the park" [tb03-0028]. A child who is not afraid [tb09-0195]. To be the parent they meant to be [tb07-0173]: "I really want to teach him to listen, respect boundaries, and handle his emotions better without constantly yelling or punishing him." [tb09-0236]. For some, a slow potty route: "I want to do this very gently and low pressure. I don’t care how long it takes and not in any rush." [tb09-0273]. Honesty: "I don’t want sugarcoating or just to hear that it’s a phase." [tb09-0292].

### 6.3 What they fear

- **Losing the place.** "I don’t want him to be kicked out of the" class over biting [tb02-0124]; "I just hope he learns before we have to find a new school" [tb05-0247]; "I’m so worried the daycare might give up on her" [tb05-0133].
- **A label.** "he will just be seen as 'the naughty baby.'" [tb07-0055]; "I don’t want him labeled as a problem child." [tb07-0107]; "I’m afraid I am raising a mean kid." [tb04-0190].
- **Harming the child.** "I don't want to traumatize her." [tb07-0085]; "I am worried I've done this completely wrong and traumatised the poor soul" [tb05-0216].
- **Too late or too early.** "I know she's too old. I know. I know I know I know." [tb08-0277]; "when we know now it was too early." [tb01-0039].
- **Isolation.** Holding a boundary for months while everyone thinks the child is unsafe to be around [tb04-0202].
- **The next baby.** "this is just going to be another baby I carry who loves dad more and puts up with me." [tb08-0084]; "will the second one reject me as well ?" [tb03-0274].
- **That it never ends.** "How am I supposed to survive 2 or 3 more years of this season" [tb02-0061].
- **A diagnosis.** `diagnosis_fear` is tagged on 38 quotes. All sit in restricted units and none is quoted (section 12).
