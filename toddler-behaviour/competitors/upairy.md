## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

UpAiry is the biggest spender in the whole Mamivio map (838 active ads, 22 months), and it sells a product, not information, using information-product copy: advertorials, a named "mechanism" (interoceptive delay, Feel & Learn), a quiz, and a free ebook as a bundle sweetener. Its ~110 landing pages are a live map of which potty angles are being tested: deadline (preschool/kindergarten/daycare), "tried everything", stubborn child, first-time mom, twins, bedwetting, authority (nurse, OT), news (school boards, California bill). These include timeline promises, "on the spectrum", and a quasi-diagnostic "interoceptive delay" that "pediatricians miss". Research records every subject in people's own words. What is usable in copy is the owner's decision, made later. Market interpretation: the potty buyer responds to a deadline and to "it's not your fault", and is used to being asked quiz questions about age and what was tried. A personalized plan could sit beside the underwear (it does not compete with a physical product) or answer the parent who already bought pants and still has accidents. Their 8-question quiz is the closest analogue to Mamivio's intake in this niche and should be walked.

## Quiz walk, 2026-10-25 (owner watching) — interpretation

Full screen text: `../_raw/competitors/upairy-quiz-walk/walk-2026-10-25.md` (15 screens; no email asked).

- **Shape:** 8 questions (age, why you're here, what they wear, readiness signs, what accidents look like, complications, what you've tried, temperament), two interstitials that teach the mechanism, a "Potty Signal Score", a 3-step plan, then the product with a "quiz reward" discount. The answers ride into the landing page URL (score, age, temperament) and are shown back there.
- **Personalisation is thin and partly inconsistent:** the plan paragraph names three answers (strong-willed, 2–3, pull-ups); the score read **1/10** on the score screen and **3/10** two screens later for the same answers. The "plan" is the same three stages for everyone, with a timeline ("2–3 weeks").
- **The questions are good intake design and match the Reddit material:** "what have you already tried" with the 3-day method, sticker charts and M&Ms named; "they were trained… then regressed"; "daycare or preschool deadline is coming"; temperament (strong-willed / cautious / busy). These are the same branches the toddler findings call for.
- **Reassurance before the sale:** "that’s not defiance"; "It’s not you. It’s not your child." — the "not your fault" line again, then blame moves to disposables.
- **Claims Mamivio cannot use:** "92% of children were out of diapers by 18 months" in 1957 (unsourced on the page), "interoception" as the missing skill, timeline promises, "1,000,000+ pairs"/"350,000+ parents" (unverified), "discount gone once your session ends" urgency.
- **Prices in the walk (EUR):** 1 pair €19,95; 5 pairs €26,00; 10 pairs €54,00, with an ebook and a printable pack thrown in as "FREE $25/$20" gifts. So the information is given away as a sweetener; the money is in the gear (fits `../synthesis/findings.md`: parents buy gear, not courses).
- **For Mamivio:** a potty plan does not compete with this; it is what the parent needs when the pants are on and there are still accidents. The intake questions above are a ready checklist; the inconsistent score is the kind of thing that makes "personalised" look fake.
