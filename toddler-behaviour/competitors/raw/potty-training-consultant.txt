## Meet the Instructor

###

Owner &amp; Founder, Potty Training Consultant

Allison Jandu

When it came time to potty train her first child, Allison was quickly overwhelmed by all the conflicting information available online and was really missing the support and science-based guidance she was after. Toddlerhood is challenging enough -- she didn't think was fair for parents to have to guess their way through potty training!

So after 5,000+ hours of research, she created resources that are all based on proven facts. And now she has an entire community where parents can come together without judgement, ask the hard questions, and get evidence-based facts that drive potty success.
