## Meet Allison

When it came time to potty train my first child, I was quickly overwhelmed by all the conflicting information available online and was really missing the support and science-based guidance I was after. Toddlerhood is challenging enough -- I didn't think was fair for parents to have to guess their way through potty training!

So after mannnnny hours of research (5,000+ to be exact), I created resources that are evidence-based. And now we have an entire community where parents can come together without judgement, ask the hard questions, and get evidence-based facts that drive potty success.

# Meet the Team

## Lauren Gehringer

Master Consultant

‍

As a certified potty training consultant, Lauren specializes in helping families overcome some of the most challenging potty training obstacles with a particular expertise in poop withholding and chronic constipation. Her approach is informed by over five years of hands-on experience working directly with families, consulting with schools to improve bathroom policies, and collaborating with experts across pediatric health, education, and child development. She is also the author of From Chewing to Pooing: Food’s Journey Through Your Body to the Potty, a popular educational resource that has helped thousands of children better understand their bodies and reduce anxiety around pooping on the potty. With her warm, evidence-based approach, Lauren continues to empower parents and professionals alike with proven strategies, no matter how stuck things might feel, with a near perfect success rate.

Rates start at $225.00

## Sasha Romary

Associate Consultant

‍

Sasha Romary is a certified potty training and parenting coach who has helped thousands of families worldwide navigate early childhood challenges with confidence and clarity. With certifications in potty training, sleep, childbirth, and parent coaching, as well as real-life experience as a mom of two, Sasha offers individualized, evidence-based support to address all aspects of potty training with her extensive experience and expertise. Known for her calm, empathetic approach, Sasha empowers parents with practical strategies tailored to each child’s unique needs, turning frustrating milestones into meaningful progress and lasting peace at home.‍

Rates start at $99.00

## Elizabeth Roy

Associate Consultant

‍

Elizabeth Roy is a certified potty training consultant with a doctorate in psychology. She specializes in supporting parents with tips, strategies, and encouragement throughout the potty training journey. With a deep understanding of human behavior, Elizabeth customizes her approach to meet each child’s unique needs, making the process more effective and less stressful. Her passion lies in turning potty training into a positive and enjoyable experience for both parents and children. Having helped numerous families overcome common challenges and uncertainties, Elizabeth is dedicated to guiding children toward potty training success, one milestone at a time.

Rates Start at $99.00

## Rachel Weiss

Master Consultant

‍

After discovering Allison’s courses while potty training her first child, Rachel became a certified potty training consultant in December 2023. Rachel is committed to providing personalized, judgment-free support to families, tailoring her approach to meet each child's unique, individual needs. As a former competitive athlete with a background in chemical engineering, Rachel has always been passionate about problem-solving and helping others achieve their goals. She considers herself a "personal trainer for potty training," using her analytical skills and extensive knowledge to create customized plans that make a real difference for families. Some of her most rewarding experiences involve helping children overcome prolonged constipation or withholding issues. She looks forward to helping make potty training a positive and successful experience for you and your child and taking on some of the mental and emotional load, so you don't have to do it alone.

Rates Start at $225.00
