Our Potty by Design resource is your must-have digital guide for potty training success. It’s based on your child’s unique behavior profile (as determined by our Toddler by Design Quiz). With 2 proven methods and plenty of encouragement for parents and kids, you’ll know what to expect and how to confidently teach this new skill.

Potty by Design is a digital product you can access immediately! Upon purchase, you will receive a link via email to download Potty by Design.

-
Why it Works

-
Key Features

- You’ll learn a proven, no-nonsense approach to toddler potty training based on their unique personality, motivations, and signals

- Our strategies help parents embrace where and when your child expresses interest in using the potty

- Our system eliminates shame, judgment and fear so that parents can teach and potty train successfully

- Toddler Potty by Design E-Guide is an easy-to-access, 30-page PDF guide that’s available for immediate download upon purchase so you can get started from the word “go!”

- We provide genuine encouragement and support for toddler potty trainers at every step in the process so they feel empowered at the potty

- Our teaching principles are rooted in the Moms on Call SMART paradigm, which helps identify how your child learns new skills and processes new information

- Lifetime access to the printable PDF guide for quick reference to take when they need to go on-the-go

- A ‘Getting Started’ guide to set the stage for success and provide realistic expectations before you embark on the potty training journey

- Recommendations for best toddler training potty supplies and products to have at home, for travel, and everything in between

- Two effective potty training methods (Triple P and Booty Camp) to assist parents in finding a method that works for their family’s lifestyle

- Tips by design – Practical tools for assisting your toddler with potty training based on their unique motivators according to the Toddler by Design SMART paradigm

- Sample schedules for both Triple P and Booty Camp methods

- Encouragement for potty trainers (that’s you!) and answers to common questions and potty training myths

- Check out our Toddler Potty by Design highlight on Instagram @momsoncall to see this resource being used in real-life!

- Complements other Moms on Call digital products like the Toddler by Design E-Guide and Toddler Online Course
