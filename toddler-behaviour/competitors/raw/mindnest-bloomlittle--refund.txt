Return Policy – Digital Products

Return Policy
Due to the nature of digital products, returns or exchanges are not available once the e‑book has been delivered. Please review the product details carefully before completing your purchase.

Refunds (within 60 days)

If you are not satisfied with your e‑book, you may request a 50% refund within 60 days of the purchase date. To be eligible, your order must be verifiable and you must provide a brief explanation of the issue you experienced with the product.

Where a refund is approved, the refundable portion will be processed back to your original payment method within 2–3 business days. Please note that any digital processing or payment gateway fees charged at checkout are non‑refundable and will be deducted from the refund amount.

For any additional questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at:
weatbloomlittle@gmail.com
