### Hey! I’m Jamie

#### I'm Jamie, and I spent most of my life being whoever you needed me to be.

I grew up hearing "don't get bigger than your britches," so I didn't. I learned to read a room before I walked into it and turn into whatever version of me would keep the peace. I was easy to be around and nice enough that nobody ever had to hear what I actually thought.

#### Then life got hard in ways I'm not going to pretty up for a website. I lived through real harm and real poverty while I raised my kid on my own. Single motherhood sharpened me. It also meant bending all the time, because when you're the only one holding everything up, bending is how you keep it from snapping.

####

Every time something knocked me down, I had two stories to choose from. One said therefore I can't. The other said okay, now what. I picked the second one more times than I can count, and I usually swore my way through it.

What happened to me was real, and I still didn't let it run my house.

For years I was the sensible expert. I handed parents good, beige advice and saved my real opinions for the comments section. Then menopause took the performance clean off me. I'm two and a half years in, my kids are grown, and my body flat out refuses to keep doing agreeable. The woman I kept quiet for decades has a lot to say, mostly about an industry that convinced you a stranger with a ring light knows your kid better than you do.

I'm furious about that, on your behalf.

I teach as the woman who bent for decades and found her way back. I know exactly where you're standing because I stood there for a long time, and I know the way out because I walked it with no map.

Okay, now what is the whole method. It's a muscle, and every class I teach breaks it down into something you can actually use on a Tuesday night when the spaghetti's on the floor.

So hi. I'm the one with a tin foil hat. Pull up a chair.

- Jamie
