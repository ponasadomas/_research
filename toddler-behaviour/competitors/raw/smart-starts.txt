## Welcome to Smart Starts!

Smart Starts for Parents equips families with the tools and encouragement to feel confident from day one. Founded by Dr. Bob Bucknam, voted one of America's Top Pediatricians, and his wife Gayle, a licensed counselor, Smart Starts combines medical expertise, counseling insight, and real-life wisdom from raising four sons and twelve grandchildren to help parents build routines that work and strengthen connections at every stage.
