## Know What a Boundary Is - And What It Isn't

A boundary is not a request or a threat. It is what you tell your kid you will do, and it requires nothing of them. That shift puts you back in charge of what you can control, so you can act with confidence and stay connected even when your kid is upset.

### Is this program right for me?

If you are exhausted by time outs, empty threats, and repeating yourself without seeing behavior change, this program is for you. It focuses on parents of younger kids and does not address teen-specific behavior challenges.

### What's included in the Boundaries for Behavior Change Program?

You will get 10 on-demand video lessons, 3 scripts, a 7-Day Connection Challenge Guide, and a growing library of resources you can use right away. Your purchase also includes Dr. Becky's live Boundaries workshop on September 23 at 11am ET and the recording afterward.

### What if I can't attend the September 23 workshop live?

You will still get the full workshop. The recording will be added after the live event so you can watch when it works for you and revisit it anytime.

### How is a boundary different from a request or a rule?

A request asks your kid to do something. A boundary tells your kid what you will do, and it requires nothing of them. You set up the environment and take action so the limit exists whether your kid likes it or not.
