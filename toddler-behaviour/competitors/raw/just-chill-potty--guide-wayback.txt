## Course Topics Preview:

1. Welcome & Getting Started

All about how you can use the course and get started with potty training.

2. When to start potty training

This section guides you through the signs that your child is ready to start potty training, and how to know that you’re ready too! We also talk through when it might not be the right time for your child, so you can plan to potty training at a time that works for your family.

3. Preparing to Start Potty Training

Everything you need to know to prepare you and your child to start potty training, including:

- Things you’ll need

- Whether to use a potty or go straight to using the toilet

- How to talk to your child to help them feel prepared

- Diet and hygiene

- Deciding on a start date

4. Potty Training Plan

A step-by-step guide taking you from day 1 of potty training and through the next few days of putting your plan in place. We also guide you through leaving the house and talk about night training too.

5. Troubleshooting and Commonly Asked Questions

We share answers to common potty training scenarios, including:

- How to handle accidents

- If your child is reluctant to use the potty

- Poo problems

- Regressions

- And more…
