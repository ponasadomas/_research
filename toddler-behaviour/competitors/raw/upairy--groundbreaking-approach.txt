How do I wash UpAiry Training Underwear?

##
1. Rinse immediately after accidents to prevent staining.

2. Machine wash in cold or warm water with your regular laundry detergent.

3. Tumble dry on low heat or air dry for best results.

4. Avoid fabric softeners as they can reduce absorbency.

Can I use UpAiry for overnight potty training

##
UpAiry Training Underwear is designed primarily for daytime training when toddlers are awake and learning to recognize their body's signals.

For overnight protection, we recommend using our extra absorbent inserts that add extra layers while still maintaining some awareness.

Remember, nighttime dryness often comes months after daytime success.
