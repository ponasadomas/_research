### All Ages

A groundbreaking course for highly sensitive and strong-willed kids. Generic advice fails "Big Feelers." Get the specialized tools you need for the explosive behavior, sensory battles, and defiance that other courses don't cover.

### 8 Course Topics

1

Understand What's Really Going On

Decode your child’s intense behavior and learn how their wiring affects emotions and reactions.

2

Stay Cool When Your Kid Explodes

Handle even the loudest, messiest, ugliest meltdowns with calm and confidence.

3

Transform Behavior Through Connection

Encourage cooperation and growth while protecting your relationship and your child’s self-worth.

4

Prevent the Next Explosion

Spot early warning signs, set your child up for success, and reduce the number of daily blowups.

5

Teach Your Kid How to Self-Regulate

6

Build Long-Term Resilience

Help your child bounce back from challenges and grow into a more confident version of themselves.

7

Supporting Your Whole Family

Explore how your own emotions affect your parenting, how to make sure siblings feel seen, and how to support your Big Feeler through big transitions like becoming an older sibling.

8

Send-Off

This work is powerful, legacy-level stuff, and the impact will ripple through your family for years to come. So take a breath, take a bow, and never, ever forget how lucky your child is to have you.

Made Simple
