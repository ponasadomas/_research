Help children towards potty training success with this digital visual schedule that you print out on your own printer at home or school. Eliminate nagging and constant reminders with visuals for children that prompt them to use the bathroom at the appropriate time.

Kit includes:

- Potty Chart Visual Schedule

- Potty Training Cards

- Potty Training Book

This visual schedule is a great asset to a classroom or home. Children can place cue cards on the card mat to help reinforce positive potty training habits. Cue cards can be attached with velcro, sticky dots, or even just tape. Laminate for extra durability.

Visual cards are included to be used on binder clips which are great to have around or for on the go.

We've also included a potty training book to help establish positive potty training habits and routines.

This is a digital item that is sent to your e-mail for you to print out. No physical product will be mailed to you.
