Toby the turtle makes potty training gentle and fun: track wins, earn rewards, follow weekly expert guides, and keep both parents perfectly in sync.

Meet Toby the turtle, your toddler's cheerful companion through every step of potty training. POTTR turns a stressful milestone into a warm, encouraging routine you and your little one can actually enjoy.

GENTLE TRACKING
Log every potty visit, accident, and success in seconds. POTTR quietly builds a picture of your child's rhythm, so you can spot patterns and celebrate progress. No pressure, no judgment.

REWARDS THEY'LL LOVE
Stars, badges, and playful milestones keep your toddler motivated and proud. Watching their collection grow makes them want to try again, and that's half the battle won.

WEEKLY EXPERT GUIDES
The Learn tab delivers fresh, expert-written guides every week, plus quick quizzes to help it all stick. From readiness signs to nighttime training, you'll always know your next step.

TIPS FROM REAL PARENTS
Get practical, been-there advice from a community of parents going through the exact same thing. Real tips for real days.

BOTH PARENTS, IN SYNC
Co-parent sync keeps everyone on the same page. Whoever's on duty, progress updates instantly across both phones. No more "did you already go?" texts.

IN 13 LANGUAGES
POTTR speaks your family's language, fully localized so every guide, reward, and tip feels natural.

LIVE ACTIVITY TIMER
A handy Live Activity timer sits right on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, so you never lose track of the next scheduled try.

PRIVATE AND KID-SAFE
Your family's data stays yours. POTTR is built to be safe for little hands and private by design. No ads, no clutter, just gentle progress.

POTTR PREMIUM (AUTO-RENEWABLE SUBSCRIPTION)
POTTR requires a Premium subscription to unlock its full feature set — including unlimited tracking, the rewards library, weekly expert guides, community tips, co-parent sync, and the potty timer. Monthly and yearly plans are available (the yearly plan includes a free trial); prices are shown in the app before purchase and may vary by region. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings.

Start your calm, confident potty-training journey today, with Toby cheering you on every step of the way.
