## Potty Training Regressions: How to Deal

This mini-course covers all things potty training regressions: what a regression is, what causes them, how to prevent them, and how to correct one if it occurs.

GET LIFETIME ACCESS NOW!

## Do I Need This Course?

If your child is about to start school for the first time, then YES.

Regressions (potty accidents) are most likely to happen around times of change. When your kiddo starts school/nursery/daycare for the first time, that is one of the biggest changes of their childhood!

This course will help you set your child up for success when starting school, help you prevent regressions, AND help you learn how to handle them if they do still occur. Full of expert tips and tricks, you will walk away feeling more confident in both your child's potty abilities and yourself as their main potty teacher and coach!
