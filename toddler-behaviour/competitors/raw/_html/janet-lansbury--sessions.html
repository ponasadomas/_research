Listen to private consultations with Janet

Sessions is the exclusive audio collection of Janet’s private phone consultations with parents (with their permission, of course!) seeking insight and support around a personal infant, toddler or preschooler dilemma.

In each episode of Sessions, Janet explores in depth a myriad of common parenting topics which will resonate with parents and early childhood professionals.

The conversations are personal, candid, sometimes emotional, always authentic.

If you’re a fan of Janet’s "Unruffled" podcasts and value her opinions and point-of-view, you’ll love the interaction and depth of Sessions.

This is Janet at her best, connecting with parents in their moment of struggle with calm, compassion, insight and answers.
