Meet Allison

When it came time to potty train my first child, I was quickly overwhelmed by all the conflicting information available online and was really missing the support and science-based guidance I was after. Toddlerhood is challenging enough -- I didn't think was fair for parents to have to guess their way through potty training!

So after mannnnny hours of research (5,000+ to be exact), I created resources that are evidence-based. And now we have an entire community where parents can come together without judgement, ask the hard questions, and get evidence-based facts that drive potty success.

Meet the Team

Lauren Gehringer

Master Consultant
‍
As a certified potty training consultant, Lauren specializes in helping families overcome some of the most challenging potty training obstacles with a particular expertise in poop withholding and chronic constipation. Her approach is informed by over five years of hands-on experience working directly with families, consulting with schools to improve bathroom policies, and collaborating with experts across pediatric health, education, and child development. She is also the author of From Chewing to Pooing: Food’s Journey Through Your Body to the Potty, a popular educational resource that has helped thousands of children better understand their bodies and reduce anxiety around pooping on the potty. With her warm, evidence-based approach, Lauren continues to empower parents and professionals alike with proven strategies, no matter how stuck things might feel, with a near perfect success rate.
