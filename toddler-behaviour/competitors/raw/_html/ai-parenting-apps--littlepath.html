Calm the hard parenting moments with personalized behavior scripts, Montessori-inspired activities, smart planning, and helpful insights now completely free. Parenting a toddler is full of moments no book fully prepares you for the meltdown at pickup, the bedtime that stretches an hour, or the hitting that seems to start out of nowhere. LittlePath gives you thoughtful, Montessori-informed responses for those moments, personalized around what has worked for your child before. Most parenting apps track milestones or suggest activities. LittlePath does something different: it adapts to your child. Behavior scripts are ranked using their temperament, developmental stage, fatigue window, and your feedback history. The more you use LittlePath, the more personalized its suggestions become. WHAT LITTLEPATH DOES · Behavior scripts tailored to your child’s temperament, energy level, and time of day · Age-appropriate activity library with smart suggestions · Weekly planning with varied activity recommendations · Behavior logging with pattern detection and helpful insights · Multiple child profiles, each with individual personalization · Child Mode with large, kid-friendly controls for guided activities · Streak tracking to encourage daily consistency · High-fatigue window alerts based on schedules and routines · Personalized guidance that adapts to your parenting approach BUILT FOR REAL PARENTS LittlePath supports Montessori-strict, Montessori-inspired, and open or eclectic parenting styles. Add your child’s temperament, energy level, sensory notes, school schedule, and nap routine, and LittlePath adjusts its recommendations accordingly. FREE FOR EVERY FAMILY Every LittlePath feature is included for free. There are no subscriptions, locked features, or advertisements. PRIVATE BY DESIGN No account is required. Your family information stays private and under your control. LittlePath is an educational parenting tool. It is not a medical or developmental service and does not replace professional care.
