Know What a Boundary Is - And What It Isn't

A boundary is not a request or a threat. It is what you tell your kid you will do, and it requires nothing of them. That shift puts you back in charge of what you can control, so you can act with confidence and stay connected even when your kid is upset.

How is a boundary different from a request or a rule?

A request asks your kid to do something. A boundary tells your kid what you will do, and it requires nothing of them. You set up the environment and take action so the limit exists whether your kid likes it or not.
