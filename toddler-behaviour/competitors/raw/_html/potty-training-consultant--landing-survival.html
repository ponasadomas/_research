What happened to the 10-Day Challenge?

The 10-Day Challenge was great – we had over 3,000 families successfully potty train their child using that course. But it was time for a change. There has been new research since that course first launched and it is my duty as an expert to bring you that new knowledge.

We are the only company out there that is always learning, researching, and adapting our educational content to make your potty training experience as easy and efficient as possible.

There were also two things I no longer liked about the name “10-Day Potty Training Challenge”. First, it implies that you need a solid 10 days to dedicate to the process. That’s simply not true, and I felt like it was discouraging parents who are busy and have other commitments. Secondly, it implies that it is a challenge. With my courses, you will find potty training to be the opposite of a challenge. In fact, you might even end up enjoying it! 

I created the Potty Training Survival Guide in place of the 10-Day Potty Training Challenge and am so excited for you to finally see it, because it truly is my best and most comprehensive course yet. I really took time to predict what questions you would ask about the process and answered them inside this course. You’re going to feel so confident by the time you’re done that you won’t only SURVIVE potty training, but you’ll THRIVE while doing it.

What if I want to add on the community support later?

Even though community support is BRAND NEW, we have been offering expert support from our team of consultants for years! You can always add the community support to any of our courses. The first month is $29.99 but after that it’s $9.99/month. However it is FREE if you buy the course before 12/1!

Can I still schedule a customized consultation?

Of course! Our consultations have been increasingly popular and have been getting a 99.5% success rate! Every child is unique and we are proud to offer specific customization to families that need it! However, we have taken a lot of our process and put it into this course.
