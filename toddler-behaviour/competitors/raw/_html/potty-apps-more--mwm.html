Turn potty chaos into a calm routine. Potty training your toddler does not have to feel overwhelming, stressful, or unpredictable. Welcome to the potty training app designed to help parents build a consistent routine, reduce daily accidents, and track progress with total confidence. Say goodbye to constantly watching the clock or trying to remember the last time your child went to the bathroom. Our smart reminder system and seamless tracking tools do the heavy lifting for you, so you can focus on celebrating every milestone. Whether you are just starting out, practicing regularly, or struggling with consistency, this app adapts to your child’s unique stage and helps you stay on track. KEY FEATURES SMART POTTY TIMERS & REMINDERS Set custom dry intervals and get gentle reminders when it is time for your toddler to try the potty. Eliminate the guesswork and prevent accidents before they happen. FAST & SIMPLE LOGGING Track potty successes, accidents, and fluid intake in seconds. The interface is built for busy parents, allowing you to log activities effortlessly without breaking your daily flow. SEE DAILY PROGRESS & PATTERNS Understand your child's natural rhythm. View clear daily summaries and visual trends to spot patterns, notice improvements, and see exactly what is working. EXPERT TRAINING GUIDES Access practical, step-by-step guides covering everything from early readiness signs and establishing routines to handling potty refusal and reducing daytime accidents. Make potty training easier, build healthy bathroom habits, and celebrate your child’s progress every step of the way. --- DISCLAIMER This application is designed for informational and tracking purposes only. It is not intended to be a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your pediatrician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding your child's health or development. SUBSCRIPTION PRICING AND TERMS The app offers an auto-renewing Premium subscription to unlock unlimited timers, unlimited activity logging, and full access to all expert training guides. Payment will be charged to your Apple ID account at the confirmation of purchase. The subscription automatically renews unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel your subscriptions by going to your App Store account settings after purchase.
