Little Bunny Bear Products

The Go Potty™ clothing and accessories range was created by Nurse Rebecca Mottram to help parents to work in partnership with their children to reach toilet independence. Designed for both Baby Potty Learning and Potty Training, the range consists of:

– Potty Learning-friendly nappy systems
– Nappy cloths
– Split crotch trousers
– Natural Mattress protectors and changing pads

These eco-conscious hand-made items are available to buy from our Etsy shop, Little Bunny Bear or you can download our Sew Potty™ patterns to make your own versions of our Go Potty™ products.

Split Pants

Split crotch clothing makes pottying quick and easy. When you hold your baby over a potty, the clothing opens up at the crotch seam, allowing your baby to eliminate straight away without fussing with buttons, poppers or the need to remove any clothes. When they are not squatting, the discreet overlapping design keeps your baby warm and covered.  Shop now.

Nappy Belt system

A soft, flexible belt which fits from birth to 3 years, Simply tuck a cloth into the front and back and the belt keeps the cloth in place. When you need to offer the potty, you can simply untuck the cloth from the front or back. If you need to change the cloth, you can do this without removing the belt. Shop now.

Nappy Cloths

Nappies are designed to wick away moisture, making it hard for your child to develop awareness of what their body is doing. Nappy Cloths are little soft cloths which are designed to be inserted into a nappy to help prepare your child for potty training. Shop now.

Puddle Pads

A puddle pad is a thick layer of specially prepared wool used to protect a mattress, floor, changing mat, or sling from urine or faeces. Wool helps babies to fall asleep faster, deeper, stay asleep longer and wake less frequently. It helps babies to regulate their body temperature and protects from mattress offgas. Shop now.
