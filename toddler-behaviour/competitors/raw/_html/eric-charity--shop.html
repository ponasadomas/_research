    New vibrating watches

    A stylish and modern wristwatch with a vibrating alarm is used in case of urine leakage during the day as a reminder to go to the toilet. The alarm can be set up to 8 alarm intervals per day. vibrating alarm clock is a good and discreet aid, for both children and adults, when you need to be reminded to visit the bathroom during the day. The watch can also be used to remember times during the day for, for example, drinking.
