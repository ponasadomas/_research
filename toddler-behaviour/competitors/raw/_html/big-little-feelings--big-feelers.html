Big Feelers  
are built different.

  • They don’t so much feel their emotions as they’re flooded by them. They don’t just have preferences; they dig in. They don’t just get upset; they explode.  
  • No matter what parenting strategies you've tried, your kid's behavior seems to get worse. So people (maybe even you) label them "defiant." "Out of control." "Over-sensitive."  
  • It's not your fault—or your kid's fault. Big Feelers' nervous systems are wired differently. They need support to manage their internal world—and the Big Feelers Course will give you the tools to do exactly that.

Is your kid a
Big Feeler?

All kids have big feelings sometimes.
But some kids are way more reactive than others.

The smallest "no" can trigger a storm. Transitions, disappointments, even the wrong color cup—it all feels like too much. Your strong-willed, big-feeling kid pushes back hard, and you're left wondering, "How did it all go sideways so fast?"

If you’re nodding your head right now, take our quick quiz to figure out if your child is a Big Feeler—and what kind of support could actually help.
