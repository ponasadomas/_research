Common Questions

Is this program right for me?

If charts, timers, consequences, and repeated reminders are not creating lasting change, this program can help. It is designed for parents of kids ages 3 to 11 who want practical support for ADHD-related challenges at home and at school.

What's included in the Parenting Kids with ADHD Program?

You will get on-demand video lessons, scripts, practical guides, the ADHD School Support Guide, and access to the live October 29 workshop with Dr. Becky and Dr. Alex Reed. The workshop replay will be included too.

What if I can't attend the October 29 workshop live?

You will still get the full workshop. The replay will be added after the live event so you can watch when it works for you and revisit it anytime.

Does my child need an ADHD diagnosis?

No. This program is for parents who recognize these patterns and want more effective ways to support their kid. It offers education and parenting tools and is not a substitute for medical evaluation or treatment.
