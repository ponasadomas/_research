A calmer, simpler way to potty train: a personalized plan, playful rewards, and gentle reminders to help your toddler go from diapers to confident. Potty training made calm, clear, and fun. Potty Path gives you a personalized day-by-day plan, playful rewards your child will love, and gentle reminders — so you can ditch the guesswork and celebrate every win together. Meet Pip, your child's friendly potty buddy who cheers them on at every step. KEY FEATURES: • Guided Program — a personalized, day-by-day plan that adapts to your child's readiness • Reward Stickers & Kid Mode — celebrate wins with a sticker board and a kid-friendly screen they can tap themselves • Smart Reminders — gentle prompts and timers so accidents happen less • Progress Tracking — see streaks, milestones, and dry days at a glance • Troubleshooting & Night Training — practical help for regressions, holding, and staying dry overnight • Readiness Quiz — find out if your little one is ready to start WHY POTTY PATH? Built with parents in mind: no clutter, no judgment — just a clear path from diapers to independence. Your child's data stays private on your device. WHAT'S INCLUDED: Free: • Readiness quiz and the first days of your plan • Daily progress and streaks • Pip, the friendly potty buddy Premium: • The complete guided program • Full reward sticker board & Kid Mode • Smart reminders & night training • Detailed progress charts • Troubleshooting library Privacy-first: your child's information is stored on your device.
