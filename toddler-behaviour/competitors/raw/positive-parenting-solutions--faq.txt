## Yes, yes, and yes. The All-In-One Parenting Success System is for kids from age 2 to 17 — and every age and stage in between. It’s truly as effective and valuable for toddlers as it is for teens.

The #1 comment we hear from new members is: “I only wish I started this earlier!”

And even if you’re only starting in the tween or teen years, you’ll find that it’s simple and straightforward to get started and get massive value from the program.

How long is this going to take?

## How much time do you spend every day arguing with your kids about chores or behavior or bedtime or homework? How much time do you spend fighting with your partner about how to handle those issues? And how much time do you spend worrying about all of it?

If you can give me ONE HOUR, I’ll introduce you to what parents call The Secret to Better Behavior. It will change EVERYTHING for you. Putting it into practice will take you ten minutes a day. Once you do that, you’ll free up ALL the time you used to spend fighting, arguing, reminding, worrying, and stressing out, and you’ll be able to do the rest of the program at your own pace.

## If you and your partner aren’t on the same page, that’s OK. The All-In-One Parenting Success System tools will work for your family even if your partner isn’t involved — and even if you’re co-parenting across households.
