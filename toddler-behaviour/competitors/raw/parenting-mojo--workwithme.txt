“I want individualized support for a very difficult/private situation.”

Most people make great progress in the Parenting Membership and Taming Your Triggers - real learning happens when you process new information with others. But if you’re not comfortable sharing your situation in front of others, or you’ve already tried a lot and you want specific guidance, private coaching could be a fit.

Three coaches support you with approaches aligned with Your Parenting Mojo’s resources:

###

Jen

Individual and couples coaching drawing on Nonviolent Communication, the Gottman Method, Emotion-Focused Therapy, and training in supporting neurodivergent adults and kids.

###

Denise

Individual coaching focused on reconnecting with your child and yourself during stressful moments, understanding what drives your child's behavior, and building your own groundedness as a parent.

###

Selena

Individual coaching with a specific focus on supporting family constellations involving ADHD, whether that’s you, your partner, and/or your kids.
