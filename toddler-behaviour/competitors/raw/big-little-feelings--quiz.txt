# Is Your Child a Big Feeler?

All kids have big feelings sometimes. But some kids? 

They seem to feel everything more.

Thanks for taking our quiz!

Take this quick, judgement-free quiz designed to help you figure out if your child is a Big Feeler—and, if so, what kind of support could actually help.
