## Personalised 'Toilet Troubles' Plans & Support

This consultation package is designed to provide the expert support you need to address challenging toilet issues, helping your child feel confident and comfortable in the bathroom again. We’ll work with you to create a tailored plan that guides you through the process, helping your child overcome any fears or anxieties, build trust in their body’s signals, and develop healthy bathroom habits.

Consultations take place with Dr Cleo Williamson – a Paediatric Clinical Psychologist working within the NHS and private sector.

## Got questions about our 1:1 Potty Training consultations?

Who will my consultation be with?

Once we have received your consultation request form a member of the team will get in touch to talk to you about scheduling in your consultation with Dr Cleo.

How long is the waiting list to get a consultation?

We offer consultations on a first come first served basis. We strive to offer an appointment as soon as possible after you make your request.

Do you offer consultations for clients outside the UK?

Yes, our telephone consultations are available worldwide. As long as we can reach you on the telephone then we can offer you an appointment.
