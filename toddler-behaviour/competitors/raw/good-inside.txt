## Common Questions

### Is this gentle parenting?

Nope, this is not gentle parenting. Good Inside parenting is sturdy parenting—we show parents how to effectively set boundaries while staying connected to their kid. Yes, both are possible and no, it's not magic, we’ll show you exactly how.

### How quickly does Good Inside work?

If there were some universal, magic amount of time, trust us - we’d be shouting it from the rooftop. What we can say is that from the moment you subscribe, you will start to see and understand the problems in your home in a totally new way. In 5 minutes a day, we can deliver you the exact scripts and strategies you need to tackle any issue you’re facing.

### Will this take a lot of time?

Rest assured, this app was made for busy parents, by busy parents. Tell our chatbot what’s on your mind and gain instant access to exactly what you need in the moment you need it. Not only will you find answers quickly, most of our content takes less than 5 minutes. That’s right - we’ll help you transform your home in as little as 5 minutes a day.

### What if my partner isn’t on board?

You’re not alone - this is very common in our new members. We say “new” members because so many parents tell us that Good Inside has helped them communicate in more effective ways with their partner and that their partner really responds to the sturdy parenting approach, as it feels more substantial and logical than other things they’ve tried.

### I worry that it’s just too late…

If there’s one thing we know for certain it’s this: It’s not too late. We know that when things feel hard in your home, it’s easy to feel stuck and lose hope. Hear us out: We are holding hope for you. We know that we can help you make the change you’re looking for.
