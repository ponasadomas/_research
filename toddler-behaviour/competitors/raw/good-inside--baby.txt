## Common Questions

### How is this different from the Good Inside book?

The book teaches the “why”.

The app helps you live it with personalized tools, expert guidance, and real-time support, to feel calm, confident, and connected in tough moments - no reading required.

### Is this based in science?

Yes! Everything at Good Inside is rooted in behavioral psychology and years of research.

We then turn those insights into practical, science-backed tools for the messy, unpredictable moments of parenting.

### Is this gentle parenting?

Not gentle - sturdy.

Good Inside helps you stay calm, connected, and confident while setting boundaries that stick.

Yes, both are possible, and no, it's not magic; we’ll show you exactly how.

### When will I see a difference?

There’s no magic timeline - if there were, we’d shout it from the rooftops.

But from the moment you join, you’ll start to see things differently, and that shift changes everything.

Good Inside gives busy parents instant access to personalized tools and real-life guidance that fit naturally into your day.

### What does a membership cost?

Plans start at $23.25/month and include unlimited access to everything on the Good Inside platform. Your membership is fully HSA/FSA eligible, so your benefits can go toward investing in your family’s emotional health.
