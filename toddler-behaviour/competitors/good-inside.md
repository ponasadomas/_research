## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

Good Inside is the category's most-funded brand and has moved from courses to an app with an AI chatbot: "answers for your situation" now exists, but as a subscription conversation, not a document. A Mamivio plan is the opposite shape: one purchase, one written plan for one child, no app to keep open. The $69 single-topic program is the closest like-for-like (short videos + 3 scripts + a challenge) and sits above Mamivio's whole ladder; Mamivio's US$49.95 tier undercuts it while being specific to the child. The "not gentle, sturdy" positioning tells copywriters that "gentle parenting" is now a word the leader runs away from. Good Inside also sells a $69 ADHD program and runs an ADHD workshop; Research records every subject in people's own words. What is usable in copy is the owner's decision, made later.
