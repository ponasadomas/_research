## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

BLF is the reference price and voice of this niche: US$34 for potty, US$99 for everything else, sold as "one course, it's all here", by two named moms with a therapist's licence. The reader who has seen BLF ads has already been told that potty takes "3 days" at home and that tantrums have a named 7-step method. Mamivio cannot match the named-expert trust or the video, while the "N days" and "is your child a Big Feeler / neurodivergent" framing remain part of the market evidence. Research records every subject in people's own words. What is usable in copy is the owner's decision, made later. What BLF does not do is adapt anything to the child: the buyer watches 3.5–6 hours and picks the module herself. A plan built from her answers (age, what was tried, daycare, the specific behaviour) is a different product, not a cheaper BLF. Mamivio's US$17.95 entry sits under BLF's potty course; the US$49.95 tier sits under the toddler course. Whether parents value "made for my child" over "the famous course" is untested.
