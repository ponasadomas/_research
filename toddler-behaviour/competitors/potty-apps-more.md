## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

POTTR is the only offer in this sweep running Mamivio's funnel shape — readiness questionnaire first, payment after — and its one review punishes exactly that: long quiz, then a surprise subscription with no trial. The lesson for Mamivio's quiz is to keep it short, say the price (one-time, US$17.95–49.95) before or early in the quiz, and make the result visibly built from the answers; POTTR's answers seem to unlock a generic tracker, which is why the paywall feels like a trick. The apps also show parents already accept paying US$20–40 a year for potty help, but for tools during training, not for the plan before it; a Mamivio plan plus a printable tracker covers the "what do I do" job these apps leave to unnamed weekly guides.
