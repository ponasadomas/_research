# Potty apps with personalized plans — Potty Path (iOS) and Potty Whiz (free app + web generators)

Captured 2026-10-25. Public App Store listing and public website; nothing installed or bought. Grouped note: two small app sellers whose deliverable is the closest functional match to a personalized potty plan.

Active Meta ads: neither in the owner's sweep. Potty Path has too few ratings to show an overview (released 2026-06-30).

- **Buyer/trigger:** Parent about to start or mid-training who wants a schedule, reminders and a tracker on the phone.
- **Mechanism (≤8 words):** Readiness quiz, then adaptive day-by-day plan.
- **Promise:** Potty Path: "a personalized, day-by-day plan that adapts to your child's readiness"; Potty Whiz: "Turn your child into a potty trained whiz in no time!"
- **Proof:** User reviews only (Potty Whiz site; none on Potty Path).
- **Price (exact, USD, App Store):** Potty Path free download with in-app purchases Monthly Plan $4.99, Yearly Plan $29.99, Lifetime Access $14.99 (lifetime is cheaper than yearly). Potty Whiz app and web tools free (Kidplay Technologies LLC).
- **Format:** Potty Path: 28-day guided program with a "consolidation phase", sticker board and Kid Mode, reminders, troubleshooting library of 20 situations (constipation, withholding, flush fears, travel, illness), night training, a "Caregiver Share Card" one-page plan for daycare/grandparents/partner, graduation certificate. Potty Whiz: logging (sits, successes, accidents, fluids), timers, shared caregivers, plus web generators: readiness quiz, printable reward chart, schedule generator from wake and nap times, daycare plan letter, "How Long Will Potty Training Take?" timeline, supplies checklist.
- **Personalization:** Potty Path: readiness quiz, plan adapts to readiness (inputs not visible on the listing). Potty Whiz: schedule from the child's daily routine; timeline from age, readiness and preferred approach; checklist by age; letter for the daycare.
- **Guarantee:** None (App Store subscription terms).
- **Angles and hooks:** "calm, clear, and fun", "ditch the guesswork", privacy ("stored on your device"), team effort with partner and daycare.
- **Objections answered:** readiness (quiz), consistency across caregivers (share card, invites), regressions and holding (troubleshooting), nights.

## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

These two show that "a personalized, day-by-day potty plan" is already a feature, offered for free (Potty Whiz web tools) or for $14.99 lifetime (Potty Path). The Mamivio candidate's core claim is therefore not unique in words; it competes on depth, trust and the parent's preference for a finished document over an app to operate daily. The "share with daycare / grandparents / partner" one-pager appears in both and answers the research question about who else is in the decision; a Mamivio plan would need the same. Their "How long will it take" timeline generator is a version of the N-days promise Mamivio avoids. Neither advertises, so neither has proved the format sells at scale.
