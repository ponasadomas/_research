## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

The top of the price range for a toddler-behaviour course ($177) and the opposite pole to BLF's "do this, say this": a buyer praises her precisely because she does not give scripts. That marks a real split in this audience (scripts vs. perspective) that Mamivio's script-led plan would land on one side of. Like Jamie Glowacki and Allison Jandu, the founder has stopped 1:1 consults and routes demand to a course: the personal tier is scarce at the top of the market, which is the space a personalized plan claims. Her listeners are a reachable audience through podcasts, not Meta ads.
