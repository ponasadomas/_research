## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

Go Potty is the UK clinical-authority voice: a nurse with a Bloomsbury book and an NHS/ERIC policy story, selling cheap printed guides and (until recently) consultations. Its message — start earlier, skills before nappy-off, no deadline — provides a contrasting position on readiness and timing. Two warnings: she publicly questions AI-generated potty plans, so a UK-facing Mamivio ad that leans on "AI-made plan" invites that critique; and the "earlier is better" frame conflicts with US "wait for readiness" advice, so Mamivio's readiness step should present both as legitimate and let the child's signs decide. Her consultations being closed leaves the "plan tailored to your child" slot in this corner of the UK market empty.
