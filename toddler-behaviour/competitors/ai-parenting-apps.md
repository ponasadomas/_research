## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

These apps already do, on paper, what the Mamivio behaviour plan proposes (scripts for the specific situation, adapted to age and temperament) and do it for free or $49.99 a year, but with no visible traction (1–2 ratings) and a graveyard of 2023 AI coaches with no updates. Read together with BLF's 76 ads and US$99 course, the market evidence so far is that parents pay for a trusted named voice, not for personalization alone. A Mamivio offer that leads with "personalized" risks sounding like these; the research should test whether the buyer's words point to "made for my child" or to "someone who knows". The "what do I say right now" hook (RootWise) is a strong trigger line that BLF also uses ("do this, say this").
