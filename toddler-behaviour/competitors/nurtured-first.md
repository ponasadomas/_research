## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

This is the closest competitor to Mamivio's positioning: it already says out loud two positions recorded in this market (no deadline, medical issues go to a professional) and turns them into the hook against BLF-style "3 days". It also already has the inputs Mamivio would ask for (readiness, temperament, daycare, what was tried, poop holding) — but as a checklist and a self-scored quiz the parent reads and applies herself, inside 37 videos. Mamivio's difference is the output: the same diagnostic questions, answered once, return a written plan for this child (readiness verdict, method, day-by-day) instead of a library to navigate. Price-wise Mamivio's US$29.95 tier sits between the Toolkit (US$23.59) and the course (US$44.79); the US$49.95 tier is just above the course and would need the plan to feel more specific than a video course does. The "three-week standoff" and "follows your child, not a clock" frames are recorded competitor positioning. Research records every subject in people's own words.
