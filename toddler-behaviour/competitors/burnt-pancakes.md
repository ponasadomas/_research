## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

A one-woman "professional potty trainer" course at BLF's toddler-course price (US$99) rather than its potty price (US$34). What justifies the price is access to Katie (group, monthly live Q&A), not content depth. "In days not months" is a softened N-days promise Mamivio must not echo. Mamivio has no human access to sell, so it competes on specificity and price: a US$29.95 plan built from the child's history is a third of this price and does not ask the parent to watch 20 videos and pick the right troubleshooting clip. The creator's "1:1 with thousands of parents" claim shows parents do pay for individual answers; Mamivio's quiz is the scalable imitation of that consult.
