## Reading this market (interpretation)

- **Two price bands with a gap.** Self-serve methods cost US$20–49 for potty (Oh Crap $20, BLF $34, PTC $49, Just Chill £36, Nurtured First $44.79) and US$79–177 for behaviour (BLF $79/$99, Nurtured First $132, Lansbury $177), up to $399 (PPS). Anything "about my child" is sold as a human service at US$99–975 (PTC $99–125 calls, Just Chill £245, Oh Crap $975). Mamivio's US$17.95–49.95 personalized plan sits in the self-serve band with a service-band promise.
- **Quizzes are everywhere, real personalization almost nowhere.** BLF, UpAiry, PTC, Moms on Call, Parenting Mojo, Potty Path, POTTR and Pull-Ups all quiz the parent; outputs seen are a type label, an emailed result or a lead into a generic product. Free generators (Potty Whiz) and cheap apps (Potty Path US$14.99 lifetime) already offer "personalized day-by-day" potty plans in words, with no traction visible.
- **The money is in potty, and in UpAiry's style of copy.** 838 + 76 + 35 active ads; deadline hooks (preschool, kindergarten, daycare), "tried everything", "it's not your fault", authority advertorials.
- **The stuck point is the product.** Sellers unbundle by problem: poop withholding (Oh Crap $97, PTC $37, Unconstipated $45, ERIC), nights, regressions, daycare. Medical framing (encopresis, OTC protocols, "interoceptive delay") is common and is where Mamivio must stop and refer.
- **Claims and framing seen live:** day counts (BLF "3 days", UpAiry "weeks, not months", PPS "48 hours", Gumroad "Just 3 Days"), ADHD/autism hooks (Saphire, The Calm Anchor, UpAiry autism advertorial, Mary Barbera, BLF Big Feelers' neurodivergence FAQ). PTC dropped its "10-Day Challenge" name for discouraging parents.
