TITLE: Gave it a try
It’s 4/1/26. This app is new to the App Store, but I decided to give it a try. I will say it is a very lengthy process just to get set up. 16 steps. Answering yes or no questions. Answering questions about your child’s height, weight, and an overall assessment of PR readiness. Not terrible. But after question # 9, you start to look up and realize that you’ve still got 7 more to go. Then after you do all of that, they hit you with the paywall. $10 a month. No trial. I feel like for this to be a brand new app with no other reviews, they need to give a 3 day trial or something. Sorry… not willing to pay that much for an app with zero reviews or historical info.
