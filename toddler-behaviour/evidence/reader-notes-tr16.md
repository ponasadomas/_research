### 1.6 Backing off vs holding firm — both directions, no verdict

- **Backing off worked:** diaper back on, "it faded away when she is not stressed about it" [tb16-0267]; paused, "glad we took a break" [tb16-0092]; back in diapers "Not as a punishment" [tb16-0278]; gave up, tried weeks later "and it instantly clicked" [tb16-0405].
- **Holding firm worked:** "I just stuck firm to the rule and tried to be as un-emotive as possible" [tb16-0138]; back to basics with boring clean-up, "Going from radio silence to this has felt incredible" [tb16-0145] [tb16-0146]; "If I'm firm in my mind, I'm a much better parent" [tb16-0394]; "no going back now" [tb16-0044]; one poop success came on its own a day after posting [tb16-0239].
- **Backing off did not help:** "We took the pressure off for several months and it didn’t help" [tb16-0422].

- **Regret at going early:** "I wish we waited longer to train but now we are too far in to go back" [tb16-0433]; regret at pull-ups kept "for too long" [tb16-0211].
