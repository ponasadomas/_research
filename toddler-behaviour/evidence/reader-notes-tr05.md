## Contrary evidence

- Oh Crap success with a first child [tb05-0101]; a parent planning to rerun the Oh Crap blocks during a regression, alongside the daycare [tb05-0159 tb05-0162].
- Daycare is as often the help as the threat: Montessori teachers prompted readiness [tb05-0202]; momentum from peers training [tb05-0223]; teachers patient and "clear they don't want to force him" [tb05-0159].
- Several posters reject scripts *because* they already use them; the gap is in-the-moment and at-school transfer, not knowledge [tb05-0136 tb05-0198 tb05-0229].
