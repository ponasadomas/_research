## Contrary evidence

- Consequence-led parents also report failure, not only gentle ones: toy removal cycles [tb04-0085, tb04-0086], time-outs that don't work [tb04-0127, tb04-0171], rewards ignored [tb04-0203]. "I’ve tried gentle parenting, I’ve tried not gentle parenting" [tb04-0029].
- Some reject softer advice: "Why would I throw in the towel after six months" [tb04-0026]; one parent defends not-isolating time-outs [tb04-0226]; another defends moving away from an authoritarian upbringing [tb04-0054].
- A few quick or painless potty moments: fear passed within hours [tb04-0094]; a child asking for the potty too often [tb04-0095].
- Not all daycares are obstacles: some prompt every 30 minutes or hourly [tb04-0069], or are "on board" [tb04-0219].
- One mother's fix was not a method but quitting work [tb04-0100].
