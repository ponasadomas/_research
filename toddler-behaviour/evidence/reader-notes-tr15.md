## 1. Wave-1 findings (section 10) checked against this window

### 10.1 Daycare/preschool policy as the hard deadline — confirmed, strongly
- Named dates and conditions: "He starts preschool where he has to be potty trained on Sept 9." [tb15-0042]; "He starts preschool on Sept 1 and he must be potty trained to go to his class." [tb15-0267]; 3-K "has asked for us to potty train her" [tb15-0026]; preschool in 6 weeks [tb15-0085], in 1.5 weeks [tb15-0186].
- The policy spelled out as a checklist: "knowing when he has to go, telling a teacher, pulling his pants down, and wiping" [tb15-0104], with a "$500 non refundable deposit" due in 24 hours [tb15-0106].
- New angle: licensing. "because of licensing the daycare cannot use pull-ups at all" [tb15-0211]; a pre-K meeting about "supervision laws in classrooms when they have to clean him up" [tb15-0023]; a school with a "total hands off policy" that will not change a child [tb15-0225]; a 2-year-old room that "accepted her … specifically because she was already potty trained", then a regression days before the start [tb15-0209].
- Every local preschool requires it, so a child at home has no route in (in an excluded unit; not quoted).
- Daycares that slow training: the old daycare "was not helpful, and he pretty much immediately regressed" [tb15-0020]; nursery said "he just isn’t getting it" on day one and "recommended pull ups" [tb15-0065]; staff "offered the potty several times but she wouldn't use it" [tb15-0038]. One daycare is "being very kind" [tb15-0023]; one nursery will "mimic" whatever the parent does at home [tb15-0066].
- Daycares that rate behaviour or are blamed: a biting policy that reached "a 5 day suspension" [tb15-0055] [tb15-0056]; a friend phoning after drop-off to say the child was "kicking and screaming" [tb15-0067]; "daycare says he’s completely lovely there" while he screams at home [tb15-0123]; a parent blaming a daycare teacher for her son's swearing and wanting to move daycare [tb15-0152] [tb15-0154].
- The other deadline is the baby: "I’m pregnant and we’re running out of time" [tb15-0138]; "want him trained before the next baby is here" [tb15-0176]; "I cannot afford 3 in diapers, she has to train!" [tb15-0098]; [tb15-0188] [tb15-0236] [tb15-0261].

- **Not found:** no teacher, therapist or other trained person saying scripts fail on their own child.

### 10.4 Oh Crap followed rule by rule, and its add-ons — confirmed as rule-following; tone complaints absent
- Parents speak the book's own terms: "roughly in block two, more in the half-and-half area" [tb15-0032]; "day 2 of block 2" [tb15-0173]; "The book specifically said if you have 2 accidents in pants, revert to naked" [tb15-0118]; "the Oh Crap book says 18-30 months is the ideal age and that "readiness" isn't really a thing" vs "People keep telling me she's too young" [tb15-0037].
- Guilt at breaking a rule: "I know oh crap says not to use pull ups, but…" [tb15-0036]; "I know i might get downvoted but … I don’t have any regrets" [tb15-0160].
- **Counter-case:** the block-1/block-2 loop ended by *breaking* the reset rule; twelve days later "only one pee accident in days" [tb15-0119].
- Worked once, not twice: "It took three days using the oh crap method … my second … does. not. fucking. care." [tb15-0097]; [tb15-0264] (twins). Its timeline is remembered as a trap: "I have no more delusions about "three day methods"" [tb15-0260]; "3-day method. Yeah right. 😂" [tb15-0285, restricted]; "WE were clueless about how long it would really take" [tb15-0258].
- A gradual-method mother questions the fast-method convention: "why do we need to potty train them in 1 month max otherwise we fail?" [tb15-0094] [tb15-0095] [tb15-0096].
- **Not found:** no Oh Crap paid add-on (consult, class, group) is named. No tone complaint about the book.
