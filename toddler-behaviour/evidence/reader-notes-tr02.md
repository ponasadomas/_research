## Patterns

1. **Daycare and preschool are a deadline, a judge, and a reporter at the same time.** Potty rules framed as eviction: "if a child isn't potty trained by 3, they are asked to leave" [tb02-0032], a handbook demanding full bladder and bowel control for newcomers [tb02-0016, 0017], "one more strike" over nap time [tb02-0121], a 23-month-old dismissed after four days [tb02-0035, 0036], a 2.5-year-old asked to leave a preschool over separation [tb02-0038–0041], fear of being "kicked out" over biting [tb02-0124]. Notes home are small and repeated: "listening ears" three days running [tb02-0046, 0047]; daily pickup reports that grind the parent down [tb02-0122, 0179]. Parents can't act on what they can't see [tb02-0024] and bribe with donuts as a workaround [tb02-0023]. Staff are also blamed back: turnover, "no boundaries", unqualified staff [tb02-0042, 0043, 0022].
2. **Grandparents: a verdict, then guilt for needing them.** "this isn't normal, he's a difficult child" said every morning [tb02-0018, 0020] makes the parent panic during tantrums [tb02-0019]; "rude"/"shouldn't comfort them" [tb02-0001, 0002]; "you're being unappreciative" when a safety concern is raised [tb02-0008, 0009]; "Oh its not that bad, you can handle it" from in-laws who promised help [tb02-0230]. The other direction: the toddler behaves worse when Nana visits and the mother considers giving up the help [tb02-0005, 0006]. Coined word: "gramnesia" [tb02-0232].
3. **Oh Crap! is the default potty method and its tone is the complaint, not its steps.** Bought for $3 after seeing it on the sub, felt like "a failure parent within the first several pages" [tb02-0103, 0102]; "keep going" read as "aggressive" [tb02-0083]; tone "obnoxious... dismissive" [tb02-0085]; method worked, then regression months later [tb02-0053]. Positive signals too: day-one tally pride [tb02-0204], commando was "a game changer" (not branded Oh Crap) [tb02-0077]. A visible backlash post against "militant" potty culture got 262 replies [tb02-0027–0031].
4. **Poop is the stuck point.** Pee comes, poop doesn't: fear after a mid-poop transfer [tb02-0130], terror at seeing it in the potty [tb02-0126], poop only in underwear at school, 12 times in 7 days [tb02-0156], holds until the nap pull-up [tb02-0208], daily poo accidents with no shame, "intentional" [tb02-0245]. Rewards are flatly reported as not working [tb02-0086, 0157, 0186, 0210, 0226, 0243].
5. **The scripts are known; parents say they fail with *their* child.** "I won't let you hit", gentle hands, time-outs, choices, timers, validating, mirroring, staying close silently — each listed as tried [tb02-0011, 0012, 0087, 0092, 0160, 0216, 0228, 0142, 0212]. Recurring line: the advice was written by someone who hasn't had *this* toddler [tb02-0227, 0160], "please don't say redirect" [tb02-0161]. Books and podcasts named and judged not to work: How to Talk So Little Kids Will Listen [tb02-0100, 0181], Hunt Gather Parent and Good Inside podcast [tb02-0181].
6. **Advice that ignores the household is rejected.** No time to read books [tb02-0095]; can't lock yourself in a room to potty train with a baby around [tb02-0175, 0176]; working parents can't keep a sitting schedule [tb02-0169]; solo mornings need a fix in minutes [tb02-0238, 0239]; a cold house for Oh Crap [tb02-0188, 0189]; looked-up snack ideas need appliances she doesn't have [tb02-0064]. This is the clearest evidence in my chunks for **personalization by situation** (not by age).
7. **Parent's state: dread of the morning, rage, and "it's only with me".** Dread on waking [tb02-0058, 0068–0070, 0134], "pure rage" at whining [tb02-0138], fight-or-flight 24/7 [tb02-0139], wanting to run away [tb02-0059, 0200], crying in the car after drop-off [tb02-0108].
