## Patterns

1. **Daycare/preschool policy is the hard deadline, for potty and now also for its reverse (daycare forcing nappies back).** Trained-only entry or room moves: a day program "would not take him" [tb09-0062], a room move depends on stopping nap accidents [tb09-0003], peers moved up to the preschool room [tb09-0079], a childcare place next August "will require being potty trained" [tb09-0111], school "require toilet training" for a 21-month-old [tb09-0211], pre-K fear [tb09-0046, tb09-0051]. New this batch: daycare rules that block progress — "can't send her without a diaper unless she is fully potty trained" [tb09-0128], pull-ups required "until he is able to be accident free for two weeks" [tb09-0249], pull-up after any poo accident "just their policy" [tb09-0132], nappies put back on at nap and left on [tb09-0193], "asked us to put him back in diapers" [tb09-0200], and the sharpest: a £600/month nursery won't remove nappies until five consistent days at home and won't prompt, so the parent pulls the child from £600/month preschool for home weeks, three times [tb09-0286, tb09-0287]. Daycare also delivers the "not ready" verdict [tb09-0013]. No expulsion-with-waiting-list story in this batch.
2. **Oh Crap is the default method, and the follow-up is confusion about its rules, not praise.** Blocks read "over and over" are why she is struggling [tb09-0022]; "the author says to move back to Block 1" [tb09-0020]; Block 2 brings all-day holding [tb09-0099]; Block 1 day-6 diary [tb09-0210]; a failed attempt and no courage to restart [tb09-0272]; 18 months of on-off since [tb09-0173]; the "past 30 month window" makes a parent "feel like a bad mom" [tb09-0064]. "Pantsless house arrest" confirmed in other words: "trained only naked" fear [tb09-0022, tb09-0026], "at what cost — my sanity?" [tb09-0188], "multiple days cleaning up messes for no progress" [tb09-0082], and a mother calling the commenters' bottomless push "a bit creepy" and "so insistent that this is the way it has to be done" [tb09-0259, tb09-0260]. One asks for "methods ... not the Oh Crap method" (34 replies) [tb09-0094]. No one calls it "gospel" here.
3. **Scripts the child turns into a game or a challenge.** "If you scream ... mom will leave the room" → child says "bye bye mom!" and screams, smiling [tb09-0293]; "You're okay, try to calm your body" makes her louder [tb09-0123]; the scooter boundary script → "He just laughs at me" [tb09-0219]; "last one" at the slide, agreed and repeated, then the meltdown [tb09-0261]; a warned, prepared transition still ends in 20 minutes of screaming [tb09-0183]; "Daddy can put your pajamas on" → 45-minute tantrum [tb09-0269]; morning affirmations, breathing, then tantrums all day [tb09-0113]. The one success line in the batch is a pivot away from asking: "Fine, I'll get dressed and go by myself" [tb09-0297].
4. **Parents who know the gentle toolkit say it fails on their child.** "validate her emotions, stay calm, stay consistent but ... it's only getting worse" [tb09-0077]; "I try really hard to gentle parent ... She can name her feelings" [tb09-0182]; "acknowledging his feelings, doesn't do anything" [tb09-0191]; name-the-feeling and deep breaths with 16-month-old twins [tb09-0012]; "Is constant operant conditioning ... the only answer??" [tb09-0101]; Good Inside / How to Talk named as books being "integrated" while the child laughs [tb09-0217, tb09-0219]. A trained person's own child: a daycare teacher whose 2.5-year-old had "the world's biggest meltdown" in public came home and cried [tb09-0247, tb09-0248].
5. **"Only with me" as its own complaint, both directions.** Fine for nanny / daycare / dad / dayhome, impossible with mum: nanny calls her "one of the most emotionally regulated toddlers" [tb09-0076]; daycare "one of the best behaved" then home "completely unmanageable" [tb09-0289]; dayhome "zero issues ... shocked" [tb09-0232]; happy at dad's pickup, "tantrum-central" at home [tb09-0279]; eats lasagne at daycare, nothing at home [tb09-0137]; father's son pees anywhere a parent is present, holds all day at school [tb09-0035]. And the reverse — child rejects the mother: daycare drop-offs "nightmare for me", fine for daddy, and she is "not buying into" the safest-person line [tb09-0207, tb09-0208]; "she dislikes me so much" [tb09-0245, tb09-0246]; listens to husband only [tb09-0223]; only wants mum while she breastfeeds the baby [tb09-0229]. Mother back from work trips gets the meltdown [tb09-0133].
6. **Advice that ignores the household is named and rejected.** "step away and take deep breaths ... what if it's not safe" [tb09-0103]; "might work in theory, it doesn't work well ... to these kids specifically" [tb09-0106]; "advice that assumes I can simply put him in his own bedroom" in a one-bedroom home [tb09-0228]; "his best doesn't include an unscheduled 3 day weekend" (against the 3-day method) [tb09-0258]; "Get a sitter ... just isn't available to me" [tb09-0264]; "Before anyone suggests she's simply too young" [tb09-0005]; "Please dont be mean" [tb09-0161]; "I don't want sugarcoating or just to hear that it's a phase" [tb09-0292].
7. **Partners and grandparents in the decision.** Husband won't do timed trips "because she'll tell us" [tb09-0008]; dad "stuck her back in a diaper" [tb09-0052]; a "huge fight" over giving a diaper for poop [tb09-0097]; husband gives in to a 30-minute cry [tb09-0152]; "My husband says I'm exaggerating" [tb09-0291]; husband questions why she needs time alone [tb09-0277]. 8. **Public shame is specific and outward.** A stranger "taking pictures", security passing the van, a confrontation, "embarrassed to be out in public with them alone now" [tb09-0240 to tb09-0243]; neighbours might "report us" [tb09-0177]; "people around us would find it weird/disgusting" (public toilet) [tb09-0149]; "people who call parents lazy if their kid isn't potty trained by 3" [tb09-0047]; "so ashamed of him still being in nappies" [tb09-0288]; a daycare teacher reassures herself with what she sees parents do at pickup [tb09-0247].
9. **Poop is the stuck point; pee usually is not.** Pee fine, poop panic for 1–2 hours a day [tb09-0154]; "never successfully pooped in the toilet, not even once" [tb09-0239]; "I don't even care about the potty anymore. I just want him to stop being so afraid to poop!" [tb09-0195]; corner and "acts like I'm torturing her" [tb09-0069]; withholding research "terrified" [tb09-0074].

## Contrary evidence

- Daycare is sometimes the ally, not the deadline: staff "support what the parents want" [tb09-0212], teachers kept trying for post-nap accidents [tb09-0004], the teacher suggested training panties [tb09-0186].
- Not everyone resists pushing: a parent restarts gently "not in any rush" [tb09-0273]; another accepts "potty limbo for months" and won't go back to diapers [tb09-0018].
- "Success after the parent backed off" is thin here: the only clear version came from a unit excluded for the parent's yelling (not quotable). The leave-alone pivot [tb09-0297] and "Sometimes I feel like we should just let him have them" in 1x75joo are the nearest.
- Early-training regret is present once [tb09-0275, tb09-0276]; the opposite view (paediatrician and peers pushing 18–24 months) also [tb09-0071, tb09-0086].
