## Contrary evidence

- The trained-person strand is thin: one father on his wife, a behavioural therapist [tb25-0497]; no trained person says a named method failed.
- Oh Crap worked for two 22-month-olds who kept going [tb25-0125] [tb25-0491].
- Daycares helped as often as they blocked (see 10.1 above).
- Holding firm is defended [tb25-0495] and backing off failed for some [tb25-0293].
- No course or plan price, no refund, no "ad for a course" complaint.
