# Reader notes — TR12 (wave 2, keep_core 007–014)

134 units read, posts dated 2026-07-01 → 07-09 (the US July 4 weekend falls inside, so many posts are "we took the long weekend to potty train"). 284 quotes, `tb12-0001` … `tb12-0284`. Counts below are counts of quotes I chose, never of people. Ledger: `sources/reddit-reading-tr12.md`.

## Wave-1 conclusions tested (findings §10 and "What's missing")

### Daycare / preschool policy as the hard deadline — confirmed, strongly
The deadline is the school's rule in most potty posts: fully trained for preschool in the fall [tb12-0031] [tb12-0077] [tb12-0097] (restricted) [tb12-0132] [tb12-0277]; "trained by 3" [tb12-0255]; kindy with no nappies in 8 months [tb12-0246]; going back to daycare Monday or Wednesday after the long weekend [tb12-0067] [tb12-0089] [tb12-0090]; whether to quit and let daycare take over [tb12-0019]. A former daycare teacher is ready to cancel school and buy diapers again [tb12-0278].

### Daycares that slow training or blame parents — both directions found, more of the first
- **Slowing or blocking:** teachers will put him back in pull-ups until he is "ready" [tb12-0059]; the room "isn't set up" for training kids [tb12-0239]; the daycare will not let the child use the adult toilets one floor away [tb12-0263] and puts a diaper on against the parent's plan [tb12-0264]; "more than 2 accidents, she has to go back to pull ups" [tb12-0189]; a vague "we'll do whatever is working for you" when asked for a plan [tb12-0228]; "Eating popsicles bottomless all day isn't an option at daycare." [tb12-0229]. One parent blames daycare for late training in general [tb12-0054].
- **Pushing the child out:** kicked out of a nanny share [tb12-0024] and out of an in-home daycare [tb12-0037]; teachers suggest shorter days [tb12-0247]; fear of removal after one bad day [tb12-0265].
- **Daycare as the source of behaviour:** [tb12-0042] [tb12-0234].
- **Contrary:** the child behaves or toilets better at daycare than at home [tb12-0043] [tb12-0075] [tb12-0139] [tb12-0243]. No post in my window says daycare blamed the parent outright.

### Oh Crap as "gospel" and its paid add-ons — method dominant, tone complaints fewer, no add-ons
Oh Crap is the default method in about 30 quotes. What hurts here is less its tone (wave 1) than its **logistics and its threats**: "The book says there is no such thing as a 2 y/o 'not being ready'" plus stories that quitting makes it worse [tb12-0091]; fear she "won" if they stop [tb12-0093] [tb12-0251]; self-blame for moving through the blocks too fast [tb12-0082]; seven days off that a working parent does not have [tb12-0254]; "we cannot just do permanent 'block 1'" [tb12-0092]; "watching the monitor like a hawk" with twins [tb12-0143]. One outright rejection: "I kind of think it's BS and causes too much anxiety, I'd be done now If I just gave stickers months ago." [tb12-0226]; daycare parents "really buy into" it [tb12-0225]. In its favour: pee solved by day 4 [tb12-0015] [tb12-0027]. **No Oh Crap paid add-on (course, consult, Facebook group) is named in these 134 units.**

### Trained people saying scripts fail on their own child — confirmed, one strong case
"As a former daycare teacher and nanny who has potty trained so many kids. I have literally tried everything and nothing works." [tb12-0276]. Behaviour scripts used and failing: the No Bad Kids line "I won't let you hit" for a month [tb12-0068], with the parent asking what to do when the child goes straight back [tb12-0069]; "hands are not for hitting" [tb12-0006] (restricted); validation at pickup [tb12-0047]; "talk her through these moments, redirect" [tb12-0056]; calm "hitting is bad" met with laughter [tb12-0208]; the in-the-moment gap stated outright [tb12-0002].

### Scripts wearing the parent down — confirmed
Overstimulated while staying calm [tb12-0048]; "In the world of gentle parenting and co-regulation (which I am all for), I feel like a failure for not being able to regulate myself better." [tb12-0164]; husband takes bedtime "because I just respond in anger now" [tb12-0165]; used to stay calm and redirect, now raises her voice [tb12-0219]; "keep my voice regulated, but NOTHING is working" [tb12-0167]; the calming thought comes only after the freak-out [tb12-0253]; asks how to manage her own overwhelm [tb12-0232] [tb12-0242] [tb12-0252]. **Contrary:** one father's success from a book a commenter named, naming the feeling and waiting [tb12-0123].

### "Only with me" — confirmed in both directions
- **Worse with me:** "I go upstairs and she's fine… I'm the problem" [tb12-0094] [tb12-0095] [tb12-0096]; hits only mum, fine with dad, grandparents and nursery [tb12-0125]; accidents only at home [tb12-0075]; listens at daycare, not home [tb12-0139] [tb12-0243]; fine with a teacher, not her parents [tb12-0088]; grandma's bedtime "always goes so smoothly" [tb12-0217]; restricted case [tb12-0169] (restricted).
- **Wants only me / rejects the other parent:** "no daddy" for every task [tb12-0012] [tb12-0013] [tb12-0014]; cries for mum on dad's bedtime night [tb12-0195]; only daddy at bedtime and she hits mum [tb12-0064]; husband settles her faster [tb12-0106]; "my kid only wants me but I also can't make anything better" [tb12-0102].

### Advice that ignores the household — confirmed, many new specifics
State law forbids waking a napping child at daycare, so "cap the nap" is impossible [tb12-0066]; no backyard and a 15-minute drive to anything in Los Angeles [tb12-0158] [tb12-0159]; carpets and a 6-month-old, so "pants off" is out [tb12-0151]; a small shared bathroom [tb12-0103]; a baby, so no eyes on her 24/7 [tb12-0199]; both parents working shifts with a thin network [tb12-0157]; full-time job plus a 13-month-old [tb12-0188]; a pool rule that bans swim diapers past age 2 [tb12-0117]; one-on-one time already given [tb12-0003] [tb12-0008]; a working mum's "I really wish there was a working mom method" [tb12-0254].

### Success after backing off vs after pushing — backing off wins in this window
- **Backing off helped:** "reducing pressure around it to eliminate the power struggle… We stopped letting it rule our days" (six weeks later) [tb12-0137] [tb12-0138]; "I chose peace today… no accidents" [tb12-0198]; holding off two months [tb12-0112]; "I'm definitely in a power struggle I don't want to be in." [tb12-0148].
- **Pushing failed:** "the more we tried… the more she hated the potty" [tb12-0183]; extra sits become a clamp-down [tb12-0022]; delaying the nap diaper [tb12-0028]; hiding the nappies led to a sore stomach [tb12-0245]; a big celebration followed by fear [tb12-0099].
- **Contrary (the fear of backing off):** [tb12-0091] [tb12-0093] [tb12-0251]. No pushed-through success story in my units.

### Grandparents softer or harsher — both
- **Harsher or critical:** "you need to 'fix this'" [tb12-0258]; relatives' "expelled from preschool" and "convict" jokes [tb12-0038] [tb12-0039]; relatives' dismissive answers — the part I trimmed is their physical-retaliation advice, refused by the mother [tb12-0127]; "you can't palm him off" [tb12-0169] (restricted).
- **Softer or undermining:** grandma's pull-up undid training [tb12-0131]; grandparents default to screens [tb12-0173]; grandparents absent [tb12-0203] (restricted). A mother says her own mother's "don't touch" approach disrupted hers, and she wants "a modern perspective" [tb12-0261] [tb12-0262].

## Surprises

- Backing off is the only route with a reported success in my window; no "pushed through and it worked" story.
- Several daycares actively block training (adult toilets refused, diapers put back on) rather than demanding it.
- Wave 1's Oh Crap tone complaints barely appear; the complaints are about the book's threats ("no such thing as not ready") and days off the parent does not have.
