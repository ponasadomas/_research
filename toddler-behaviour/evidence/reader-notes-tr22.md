## 14. Contrary evidence

- Daycare often helps or does better than home [tb22-0260] [tb22-0338] [tb22-0054] [tb22-0199]; some daycare-start posts are about the parent's relief, not the child [tb22-0081].
- Oh Crap is not attacked for tone in most of the 29 quotes; the complaints are about stages and duration. One mother's prompting-less version inside the method is working [tb22-0367].
- Backing off failed about as often as it worked (section 5).
- Several posters want company, not a plan: "I just want to feel validated" [tb22-0258]; "Some kids are just on hard mode" [tb22-0014]; "comparison is certainly the thief of joy" [tb22-0324].
- Some successes came from tiny tactics, not a method: a jellybean [tb22-0291], fruit-only snacks tb22-0431 (restricted), training pants instead of underwear [tb22-0317], pointing replacing shrieking with age [tb22-0129].
- A grandmother's home is sometimes where training works [tb22-0286] [tb22-0259].
