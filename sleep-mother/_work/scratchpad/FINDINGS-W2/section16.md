### 16.5 Method notes

- **Restriction was judged again, quote by quote.** The readers flagged 425 wave-2 quotes as restricted, many because of the unit they came from (`scratchpad/RREV-W2/before4.txt`, `before5.txt`, `before6.txt`: 235, 134, 56; MR04's notes say 147, but its flag list has 235). A review then judged each quote on its own words (`synthesis/restricted-review-mr04-mr06.tsv`). It left 103 restricted (MR04 41, MR05 29, MR06 33), mostly co-sleeping and bedsharing, sleep aids and medication, named conditions. That review overrides the readers' lists, and the merged `restricted` flag follows it. **Consequence:** wave 2 is 12% restricted (103 of 869), against 31% for wave 1 (262 of 834), and the difference comes from the rule, not the material.
