TITLE: Disappointed Reader
I never leave reviews, but I had to leave a review for this book. Do not waste your money. The book has good bits of information. However, the same bits of information are repeated throughout the entire book. I keep waiting to learn something more, but it’s just the same information repeated over and over in a different format. This “manual” could have been a 100 page book and the remainder, the recipes. The tone and language are also very juvenile and the advice: common sense. I’m trying super hard to finish the book, but keep falling asleep because of the tone and the repetitive content.
