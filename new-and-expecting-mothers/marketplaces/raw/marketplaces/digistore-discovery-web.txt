# Find products to promote

The Digistore24 marketplace is where you'll find products that have been listed by vendors. You can promote these products and receive commissions for doing so.

## Open the marketplace

  1. Log into your Digistore24 account.
  2. Open the Marketplace menu item in the affiliate view.

## Marketplace entries

When a vendor posts a product to the marketplace, a marketplace entry is created. The marketplace entries are displayed in a list.

A marketplace entry includes various details:

  * Headline, description, and product image
  * Various KPIs and product criteria
  * Links to the sales page as well as to the vendor's affiliate support page
  * The Promote now button, which you can click to request an affiliate partnership and the promolink for promoting the product
  * The marking NEW! if the marketplace entry was created within the last 90 days

## Request promolink

You have discovered a marketplace entry with an appealing product. Now you want to promote the product. 
## Search for marketplace entries

You can quickly locate marketplace entries by searching for the name of a product or vendor.

How to search for marketplace entries:

  1. Open the Marketplace menu item in the affiliate view.
  2. Enter a name in the search field above the list.


## Filter marketplace entries

Vendors classify their products into one or more product categories, e.g. personal development. You can filter according to these product categories. You also have other filter options at your disposal, e.g. payment methods, payment options, product price, commission type, or whether affiliate partnerships are automatically accepted.

How to filter marketplace entries:

Small display (e.g. smartphone)

Overview of KPIs

  * Price
  * Commission
Your share of the vendor's earnings.
  * Net earnings/sale
Your average earnings per sale including upsells.
  * Earnings/order form visitor
Your average earnings per order form visitor.
  * Vendor
  * Online since
The date the marketplace entry was created.

Payment frequency (single payment, subscription, installment).

Share of visitors who access the order form and subsequently make a purchase.

Share of returns and chargebacks.

# Add Products to the Marketplace

You can add your products to the Digistore24 marketplace so that affiliates can promote them. To do this, you create a marketplace entry.

> Note
> 
> Every marketplace entry is reviewed by Digistore24 and will only be approved and published after a successful review. This ensures the quality and credibility of marketplace entries.
> 
> Please also note the criteria for marketplace entries.

How to add a product to the marketplace:

Create a marketplace entry

  1. Log into your Digistore24 account.
  2. Go to the Vendor view and select Affiliates & partners > Marketplace.
  3. Click Create marketplace entry.

Select product categories

  1. Select a Product category.
  2. Optional: In the Additional product categories field, select one or two additional categories.

> Tip
> 
> If your product fits multiple categories, select them here. This helps your product appear when affiliates filter by categories in the marketplace.

> Note
> 
> In Analytics, only the category you selected in the Product category field is taken into account.

Enter product details for affiliates

Headline and description are displayed to affiliates in the marketplace.

  1. Enter a Headline.
  2. Enter a Description that explains why your product is worth promoting.
  3. Optional: Add a Product image by clicking the image icon.

     * Select an image that has already been uploaded.
     * Click Upload image to upload a new image.

> Tip
> 
> Use a product image to make your marketplace entry more visually appealing and help affiliates quickly understand what your product is.

Define products

  1. Select a Product for which you are creating the marketplace entry.

> Attention
> 
> The same product can only be listed on the marketplace once. This also applies if multiple vendors have a sales license for the same product.
> 
> This avoids duplicate entries in the marketplace.

  2. If you sell variants or very similar/related products, select them in the Similar products field.

> Attention
> 
> Use Similar products to avoid duplicate entries in the marketplace, e.g.:
> 
>      * Several separate entries for product/package variants (Basic/Plus/Premium)
>      * Several separate entries for products from the same product family offered together on the same sales page
> 
> Duplicate entries will not be approved. The reason is to keep the marketplace product range as appealing and varied as possible for affiliates. Learn more here.
> 
> Select the related products here instead. This groups them into one marketplace entry and combines their KPIs, which can also help improve the entry's ranking power.

  3. Optional: In the Additional affiliation products field, select the products that affiliates are allowed to promote in addition to the Product.

> Note
> 
> As soon as an affiliate requests a promo link for the product selected in the Product field and the request is approved (automatically or manually), the selected additional products will be automatically enabled for that affiliate as well.

* * *

> Note
> 
> Similar products vs. Additional affiliation products
> 
>   * Similar products: Use this for variants/closely related products to group them in one marketplace entry and combine their KPIs.
>   * Additional affiliation products: Use this to automatically enable additional products for affiliates once their promo link request for the main Product is approved. This does not combine KPIs.

Adjust further settings

  1. Optional: Enter the link to your Affiliate support page.

> Note
> 
> On the affiliate support page, you provide information affiliates can use to promote your products. You can find additional ways to support affiliates here.

  2. Optional: If you want to display a specific sales price, select Yes in Enter sales price manually.

> Note
> 
> If you have multiple payment plans, the marketplace will otherwise display an average price automatically.

  3. If you selected Yes in Enter sales price manually, enter the price in Price to display.
  4. Select the Language for the marketplace entry.
  5. In the Publish field, select whether the marketplace entry should be published after approval.
  6. Optional: In Notify in case of errors, enter one or more email addresses. If the marketplace entry is automatically deactivated due to errors (e.g., because the Digistore24 order form is no longer linked), you will be notified via these email addresses.

Finish and request approval

  1. Click Save.

> Note
> 
> Optional: Click Save and preview to view the marketplace entry from an affiliate’s perspective.

The marketplace entry will be created. You can now edit the Approval status field.

  2. In the Approval status field, select whether you want to request approval for the marketplace entry.
  3. Click Save.

If you selected Request approval now in the field Approval status, the marketplace entry will be submitted for approval.--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# What can you sell at Digistore24?

Digital products are the main products sold via Digistore24. In addition to a digital product, you can also sell physical products, e-tickets and webinars. The requirement for setting up a product on Digistore24 is that the product itself is already finished and that you are ready to sell it.

Below we give you an initial insight into product sales on Digistore24. We will show you which products are suitable to sell via Digistore24, what elements a product set up on Digistore24 will consist of and how to set up a product. At the end, you will find an overview of all the follow-up articles explaining in detail how to set up digital products, physical products, e-tickets and webinars on Digistore24.

## 1. Suitable products to sell via Digistore24

If you’re new to Digistore24, we recommend that you only sell digital products such as e-books, videos or audio files. These can be easily downloaded by the customer after the purchase and require minimum effort to set them up in our system. They are therefore ideal for starting out. Once you’ve gained a little experience, you can also offer online courses and memberships, webinars, tickets for live seminars, online services, license keys for your software, as well as physical products such as CDs, DVDs or USB drives.

a. Suitable products:

  * Download products such as e-books, video or audio files
  * Digital content with physical add-on products such as DVDs, CDs, USB drives, brochures
  * Membership sections/online video courses
  * Webinars
  * Online services of every kind (e.g. server hosting)
  * Software (with/without automatic provision of license codes)
  * e-tickets for live seminars
  * Individual or subscription products

b. Unsuitable products:

  * Physical products such as furniture, food, electronics etc.
  * Erotic content of every kind
  * Pornographic content of every kind
  * Violent content of every kind
  * Illegal content of every kind
  * Racist content of every kind
  * Content that we consider to be dubious, especially topics to do with “earning money”
  * Business and trading robots
  * Content that violates the terms and conditions of platforms such as Google, Facebook, YouTube, XING etc.

Every product you sell should also be created as a Digistore24 product. This applies to products that are only free gadgets as well as to larger product packages. You can learn more about this here.

## 2. Elements of a product set up on Digistore24

A product that you set up on Digistore24 consists of several elements:

  * Product file (digital product) or entitlement to the product (access to an event/webinar or a license key)
  * Product image(s)
  * Sales page (The link to the order form is placed here.)
  * Order form (You yourself don’t have to set this up, but you can individualize it)
  * Order confirmation page from Digistore24 (Optional with the download vault from Digistore24 for the secure download of your digital product) or your own thank you page (Your customer lands on the order confirmation page or the thank you page via a link in the order confirmation email. Here you will find the download link or a guide on how to access the product.)

You can adjust the settings for the following specifications:

  * Product variants (e.g. a product that can be purchased in different colors)
  * Product price/cost plan (single payment, installment or subscription payment, discounts)
  * Payment process (Payment processor through which the customer can pay)
  * Right of cancellation and refund guarantee (refund possible up to 60 days)

# Approval criteria for marketplace entries

On the Digistore24 marketplace, you as a vendor list your products for affiliates to find and promote.

Each marketplace entry is reviewed by Digistore24 and only approved and published after successful review. This ensures the quality and seriousness of the marketplace entries. The review is based on various approval criteria.

## Approval criteria for marketplace entries

Criteria  | Explanation
Headline and description  | Headline and description should correspond to each other. Description: 1) Create a short description of the product. Note that the description is aimed at affiliates, not end customers. 2) Explain in detail why it is worthwhile for affiliates to promote this product.
Accessibility  | The sales page of the product should be accessible. A buy button is not required. However, if you use a buy button, it should link to our Digistore24 order form. Please refer to these approval criteria for your sales page.
One marketplace entry per product*  | Only one marketplace entry can be approved per product per language.
