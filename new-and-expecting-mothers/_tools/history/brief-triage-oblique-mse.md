# MSE card triage — common rules and output schema

All 480 candidate cards came from all 68 pages of the two complete public category listings (1,356 unique discussions). Agent owns cards01–02; parent owns03–04. Read every assigned card, not just its title. Output separate UTF-8 TSVs under `_raw/oblique-deep/`, never overwrite another reader's file.

Schema: `discussion_id\tdecision\tlabel\treason`, header included. `discussion_id` is digits without `MSE-`. `decision`: `keep` or `drop`. Exactly one primary keep label: `gear`, `budget`, `childcare`, `household`, `multiples`, `food`, `sleep`, `other`. Dropped label: `excluded`. `reason` is a short neutral explanation without names. Label is only routing; do not use label counts as prevalence.

Keep pregnancy/new-baby/under-two parent practical planning, equipment/registry/small-home constraints, leave/return-work budgeting and cashflow, childcare handoffs/selection, older-child transitions surrounding a baby, twins/multiples logistics, family food preparation, routine management, explicit free/DIY alternatives, retrospective purchases/mistakes, and suitable counterevidence to report ideas. Do not drop solely because old, free, low-income, inconvenient to a thesis or not first-time-mother language. Historical benefits/financial rules must remain audience context, not current guidance.

Drop unrelated adult/older-child-only issues, empty administrative links, commercial spam/campaign notices, and title/excerpt false matches. Research records what people actually say and do, in their own words, whatever the subject; sensitive, clinical, crisis and other material is kept and quoted like any other evidence. The owner decides later what to use in copy; research never makes that decision. Record exclusion as card triage, never as a full-thread read. Do not infer crisis or minor status merely from a parent's financial difficulty or a reference to their children.

When uncertain whether a practical parent need is present, keep for complete-thread inspection. The next step fetches every kept thread and all public pagination, includes OP followups, and freezes 35–45 KB corpus chunks with an exact thread/page/post manifest. There is no hidden top-N cut. Full-read claims begin only after those complete units are read. Read long and clinical threads fully; account for every assigned unit. Per-post quotations later must total <=25 words per individual non-Reddit post/author work, be exact and source-traceable, and exclude copied quotations of another poster.
