# Evidence

What the quotes show.

- **`findings.md`:** the conclusions, about 5,500 words. Start here. Its last section lists
  what the 2026-11-17 re-reading changed.
- **`niche-opportunities.md`:** the ranking of niches and why.
- **`purchase-evidence.md`:** what parents say they bought, tried or considered.
- **`advertiser-money-map.md`:** where the advertising money is.
- **`niche-map/sub-niche-map.md`:** the map of sub-niches, with Reddit mention shares.
- **`evidence.md`:** every quote under its theme, 694,000 words. For looking things up,
  not for reading through.
- **`unfilter-digest-NN.md`:** four theme digests of the re-read quotes.
- **`reader-notes-*.md`:** what each reader noticed. `reader-notes-audience.md` is the only
  material on who she is.
- **`unfilter-report.md`:** the record of the subject filters removed on 2026-11-17, and
  what is still unread.
- **The rest:** counts, indexes and audit output from October and November 2026.
