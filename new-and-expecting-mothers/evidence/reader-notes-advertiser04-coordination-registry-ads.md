## Interpretation and evidence limits

The retained household category competes through ongoing software, assistance and shared hardware: this is an inference from the copy and the separately frozen official offer map, not evidence that a new one-time written report would sell. Within this assignment, the clearest explicit advice prices in ad text belong to Nugget's live webinar and subscription bundle. Individual leave support is advertised by Master without a fee. The closest personalized registry decision ads explicitly lead with free recommendations. Babylist monetizes a commerce/registry ecosystem; MyRegistry's inspected copy targets weddings. None of those observations proves purchase of customized written advice.

Daily organization, shared household responsibilities and reduced coordination burden are supported by some actual copy. A specialized return-to-work handoff product is not explicitly advertised in these 141 texts. Do not convert brand-level result totals into niche-specific ad counts or treat all calendar claims as newborn/caregiver logistics.

Page identity is preserved at both advertiser and snapshot levels. There are eight snapshot-identity differences: seven Skylight creator ads and one Hearth creator ad. Ten IDs contain branded-content metadata when the two Babylist/Motif records are included. These are not ten independent buyers or ten separate advertisers. Partner endorsements and seller-hosted testimonial fragments remain commercial claims.

Repeated text was still read per native ID. Cozi has one unique body/text bundle across six IDs; FamilyWall has seven unique bodies and fifteen full text bundles across thirty; Hearth has two bodies and five bundles across eleven. Native IDs, connection edges, card variants, unique text hashes and independent creative works are different measures. Media may carry additional text or context that was not viewed. Dynamic top-level placeholders were resolved only where concrete supplied cards existed; no meaning was inferred from a placeholder alone.
