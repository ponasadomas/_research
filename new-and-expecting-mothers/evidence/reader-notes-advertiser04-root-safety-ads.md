## Safetots: retail, free personalization and unrelated pet gates

The 30-ID split is **27 physical-retail promotions, two free personalized-consultation leads and one free gate-finder retail funnel**. Six physical promotions explicitly concern puppy/pet gates; one fireguard advertisement is French. Those cannot all be counted as English babyproofing campaigns. Product advertisements cover gates, play pens, fireguards and room dividers. Reported delivery/returns/review/safety claims were not independently validated.

Ads [1079293854792431](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1079293854792431) and [1613140786911185](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1613140786911185) explicitly offer a **free babyproofing consultation** with home-tailored advice, product recommendations and step-by-step guidance. They lead to `https://www.safetots.co.uk/talk-to-the-baby-safety-expert`. The ad is evidence of an advertised free offer; no consultation, qualification or delivered output was inspected. It is not an observed sale of a report. This is direct competition for personalized decision help inside a retail acquisition funnel.

Ad [1630118862070178](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1630118862070178) leads to `https://www.safetots.co.uk/stair-gates.html?finder=open` and advertises a brief gate-selection process. Its promised speed and recommendations were not tested. The remaining product CTAs establish commerce intent rather than a paid information fee.

Safetots' oldest observed active start in these 30 is August 24, 2026. Product names, gate dimensions and discounts are not individualized assessment prices. The full 57-result library, other variants and creative media remain uninspected beyond the retained 30 text records.
