## The same criteria for every candidate

For each candidate ask: (1) what was actually paid for; (2) what triggered that purchase or request; (3) what changed because of the buyer's inputs; (4) whether the written deliverable alone can perform the job; (5) whether live help or someone doing the work is central; (6) whether free/owned substitutes already suffice; (7) how quickly a plan becomes obsolete; and (8) what local or qualified expertise is required. Missing evidence stays missing. More selected quotes do not raise a niche's prevalence or independent-buyer count. The dedicated sleep-source extension deliberately enriches sleep evidence; this ranks the strength of observed evidence, not comparative national demand or payment rates across niches.

Evidence is separated into **paid standalone report**, **paid tailored bundle**, **paid adjacent information**, **seller offer only** and **need/request only**. A personalized app subscription belongs in the second category when the account describes a delivered tailored plan; a payment for prediction features alone does not. Neither a seller's price nor hardware spending is report willingness to pay. The detailed account-by-account audit is [purchase evidence](purchase-evidence.md).

## Geography and the original filter

The prepared country packet is a finite July 2025 B-tier comparison, not a national consumer sample. All 847 cards were read before 584 keeps received full-original reading. It broadens the artifact-oriented A filter: needs can be concrete without saying PDF/report, while words such as “meal” can mean something irrelevant. Country-specific questions include nursery funding/county boundaries, Australian subsidy/work activity administration, referral processes and local gear availability; these are historical peer accounts, not current entitlement guidance. [rcb01-0024] [rcb02-0001] [rcb02-0008] [rcb02-0029]

Country-community membership or an App Store storefront does not authenticate residence. Prospective gear budgets, salaries, quoted care fees and paid flights cannot rank English-speaking markets for report purchasing. No launch-market winner follows from these counts. The source ledgers preserve exact IDs, missing comment sources and historical exclusions; the recovery ledgers now account for the assigned excluded units. The original artifact/tool lists omitted the literal word report, and proximity rules could miss book/paid-service requests. The independent transaction censuses and full B-card comparison address specific recall gaps without rewriting the frozen original filter. [Detailed country/filter comparison](country-comparison-wave03.md).

The country evidence also includes paid-but-lost care capacity and a wish for structure without a belief in developmental deficit. These strengthen the handoff and play distinctions; they do not validate a report purchase.

## Recommendation and open research questions

Keep **sleep, capability-matched play and specialist leave planning** as the best-supported contenders, with their different evidence levels explicit. Keep food, registry and return coordination in the comparison; the newly read contrary accounts prevent dismissing or promoting them merely because they sound practical. There is currently no justified product-building recommendation or validated report price.

The unresolved commercial questions are narrow: which exact report was intentionally bought, what triggered it, what was paid, how the written output changed for the buyer, what they actually used, what disappointed them, and whether live support or someone performing the task created most of the value. An independently attributable post-use account and a seller-hosted testimonial answer different questions. [Purchase audit](purchase-evidence.md) and the [source coverage ledger](session-report.md) specify what is known and missing; later research should close those evidence gaps before choosing a business.
