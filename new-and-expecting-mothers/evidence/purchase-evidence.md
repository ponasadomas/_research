Paid access is not always a positive value signal. Existing rad02-0071 is a general rant about successive PDFs and shared logins; its hundreds-of-dollars figure is guessed. New rba01-0001/0002 concern gear choices and expensive equipment, not paid advice. Existing rbc01-0027 acquired an Etsy chart but never establishes that payment occurred or what it cost. Free/trial/gift/“got”/“used” and “would pay” are not completed transactions.

The closest report-format seller offers currently observed are MyMatLeave's reviewed $249 twelve-page leave PDF (mkt03-0001), BedtimeCoach's $19 printable personalized sleep plan (mkt05-0001), and Dawnly's $67 profile/playbook/fourteen-day plan (mkt05-0002). These establish advertised supply. BedtimeCoach expressly says it is new without customer reviews/success counts; Dawnly/MyMatLeave testimonials are seller-hosted and unverified. No independent buyer has yet linked one of those exact purchases to a delivered artifact, actual paid price, substantive personalization and post-use satisfaction in the audited scopes above.

The repeated gap is therefore specific: **standalone price plus materially personalized written deliverable plus independent post-use buyer account**. Neither broad distress nor adjacent subscriptions close that gap. The recommendation must carry it as unresolved, rather than declare the format validated or infer that parents never buy it.

**2026-11-16 unfilter:** Research records what people actually say and do, in their own words, whatever the subject. The owner decides later what to use in copy; research never makes that decision. The [themed evidence](evidence.md) retains every marked payment, offer, alleged incentive and format account.

The actual wording matters: reported payment can be concrete while the written format, independent authenticity or clinical effect stays unknown.
