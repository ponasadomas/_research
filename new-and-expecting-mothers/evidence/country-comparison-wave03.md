## What the original filter missed

The country wave intentionally read **all847JulyBcards across topics**, rather than treating B as irrelevant.

Several useful needs therefore sat outside A. A paid nanny/payroll question lacks the listed app/book/course/coach-type tool (`rcb01-0010`). Local referral uncertainty, a delivery-format preference and transit/bundle constraints can be expressed without the required A-word pair (`rcb02-0018`, `rcb03-0019`, `rcb02-0041`). An advice request is not weaker evidence merely because it lacks those words; it is also not a buyer.

Two design gaps remain relevant to task1: **report is absent from the literal ARTIFACT/TOOL lists**, and the proximity rules can omit commercial statements expressed in other words or farther apart. This is a code-inspection finding, not proof of hidden report customers. Even an explicit request for books can remain B without a listed decision word: the child-facing story request in `rcb03-0029` is a practical example. Its weaning task is breastfeeding transition, not solids recipes. Human full reads and deliverable/payment distinctions are necessary. This comparison does not rerun or expand a filter, relabel old corpora, or claim broader B/C queues are complete.
