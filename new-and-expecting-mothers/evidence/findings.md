## 1. The reader has a specific job, not a universal deficit

Parents often already own information, equipment or support. The remaining job may be fit, access, understanding, consent, recovery, grief or usable relief: a sleep plan that respects the family, activities possible for both child and adult, care someone can actually obtain, or a feeding transition that preserves choice. A calendar or document cannot stand in for all of these. “New mother” alone does not identify the job or establish willingness to buy a report. Many authors identify only as parents; first-child status, gender and country are not inferred. [rjfc01-0090] [rvw03-0010] [rcb02-0029] [rcb01-0029] [rad01-0095]

The strongest contenders and their contrary evidence are compared using the same criteria in [niche opportunities](niche-opportunities.md). That ranking is an interpretation, not measured market performance.

The wish for structure need not mean a belief that the child is behind. A developmental or emotional context is part of the research, rather than a condition for hiding the words. [Themed play and tracking evidence](evidence.md#play-structure-tracking-and-data).

## 3. What others assume

Advice can assume the household cooks suitable family meals, another adult will follow instructions, visitors make care easier, or a standard car-seat bundle is useful. These assumptions fail in particular accounts. A Trello board and repeated checklists did not create initiative; helpful nonintrusive visitors could make routines harder; a nondriving parent explicitly did not need the bundle. [rjfc01-0003] [rjpg01-0005] [rjpg01-0006] [rcb02-0032] [rcb02-0041]

A successful contrast is a self-made reusable packing list that let a partner pack independently. It is evidence that an agreed task can be easier to execute, not that every household needs another paid checklist. [rjpg01-0004]

## 7. What they ask, want and fear

Requests are concrete: an advance lunch, a compatible part, a realistic morning sequence, a provider's actual service scope, a locally suitable class or an answer to the correct funding scenario. A parent can afford gear yet prefer secondhand; another cannot afford childcare backup. Need, affordability, household spending and report willingness to pay are separate observations. [rad01-0095] [rcb01-0004] [rcb02-0030] [rcb01-0017] [rcb03-0010] [rcb02-0029] [rcb01-0013] [rcb02-0033]

A request for sympathy, fewer obligations or live help can be sincere without implying information demand. The desire to stop a party, tracking routine or unused tool is substantive contrary evidence. [rcb02-0009] [rad05-0032] [rad01-0085]

Pumping and food experiences cannot be reduced to a document-shaped logistics job. Milk feeding includes refusal, pressure, transfer, preparation and understandable follow-up after extensive help; food includes allergy protocols, texture skills, appetite reversals and the parent’s own cooking capacity. A paid bottle-aversion course is followed by distress and continued refusal on day three, not an established success or final failure. [glu13-0181] [glu13-0182] [glu13-0183] [Milk feeding](evidence.md#reread-milk) · [Solids and meals](evidence.md#reread-food).

The opposite format preference must also remain visible: some explicitly want short written references, a paper cookbook, explanations or a flowchart. These requests weaken any inference that paper itself is unwanted; they still do not establish willingness to pay. [glu06-0251] [glu06-0328] [References and requests](evidence.md#reread-references).

## Care, recovery, loss and autonomy are substantive jobs

Parents want to understand investigations and complications, preserve consent and privacy, obtain an accountable response, and recover the ability to sit, move, hold the baby or have sex. A healthy baby and routine clearance do not establish maternal recovery. Paid clinical sessions with a PDF can still leave the action unintelligible; others find observed, respectful care useful. Some want strength or a familiar body, while others reject pressure to change it. [glu11-0126] [glu11-0127] [glu12-0309] [glu12-0311] [Recovery](evidence.md#reread-recovery) · [Birth and consent](evidence.md#reread-birth) · [Care access](evidence.md#reread-access).

[glu04-0368]
> She also said maybe I need to look up a guide online to figure out how to do it...is this not what im paying her $200 a session for?!

Loss changes preparation, desired contact, leave, memorials and later parenting. An earlier pump-purchase request ends with the update that the second twin died; keeping only the opening would invent an ongoing shopping job. Reproductive decisions include wanting, delaying, ending or continuing a pregnancy, contraception, adoption and surrogacy under very different resources and pressures. These are expressed needs, without established report demand. [glu01-0030] [glu01-0031] [glu01-0008] [Loss](evidence.md#reread-loss) · [Family formation](evidence.md#reread-reproduction).

Love, joy, regret, numbness, anger, suicidal thoughts and fears of harming a baby can coexist with wanted parenthood and substantial support, including in fathers. Fear of disclosure, cost, privacy, repeated assessment and lack of time can make offered help unusable. The child is not a universal protective factor; one source’s reason to keep living does not explain another’s suicidality. [glu10-0351] Recognition, treatment, connection and protected time are different needs, not automatic demand for another plan. ## 10. Does the evidence support Mamivio's method?

It supports a need to distinguish actual constraints and avoid generic output. ## 13. Scope and attribution boundaries

Research records what people actually say and do, in their own words, whatever the subject. The owner decides later what to use in copy; research never makes that decision. All stored records are included; the 3,807-unit recovery assignment is complete to the extent of saved text, with missing originals, endings, comments and media explicitly unresolved. No earlier-milestone, guaranteed-sleep, feeding adequacy, clinical recovery or current statutory-entitlement conclusion is supported by consumer posts. Official guidance remains separate from buyer demand. [guide01-003]

Author hashes flag dependence, not authenticated identity or independent purchasers. Seller-hosted testimonials, affiliate access, clinical/provider credentials, receipts, reported refunds and causal outcomes remain unverified unless explicitly documented otherwise. The same author's later post or reply can change the reading of an opening. [rad01-0001] [rad01-0002] [rpa04-0001] [rpa04-0006]
