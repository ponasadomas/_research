Observed October 25, 2026, Europe/Vilnius; native browser captures are dated October 24 UTC. **The clearest visible advertising activity is around recurring apps, courses, physical subscriptions and retail ecosystems. Paid standalone customized reports do not emerge as the dominant advertised format in this inspected set.** That is an interpretation of observed offers, not a revenue estimate.

## The most useful commercial comparisons for Mamivio

**Huckleberry, Kinedu, Happy Eaters and Good Inside are the strongest observed comparisons for selling tailored guidance.** Their models attach personalization to continuing software or a human session. Happy Eaters already advertises ingredient-on-hand, age/stage, household food habits and meal-swap personalization; its official app description also names time and cultural preferences. Those inputs are not empty competitive territory. Claims are untested. [Happy Eaters exact ads and offer](reader-notes-advertiser04-meal-guides.md), [sleep/play ad reading](reader-notes-advertiser04-sleep-play-ads.md).

**Taking Cara Babies, Feeding Littles and My Little Eater show paid general education/curation offers**, with public prices and active acquisition. My Little Eater is the closest newly verified downloadable food-plan format: USD 39.99 standalone, or USD 85 with course/support content. It is a fixed plan without an inspected individual intake. Its actual ads sell a free workshop, not that download directly. [Food/safety ads](reader-notes-advertiser04-food-safety-ads.md), [meal-guide follow-up](reader-notes-advertiser04-meal-guides.md).

**The Starter Set and Safetots advertise free personalized decision help.** They advertise free family-tailored picks or home-tailored advice, with retail or a human-service tier elsewhere. A price on an independent consultant's website does not by itself show that this is the dominant paid acquisition model. [Registry ad evidence](reader-notes-advertiser04-coordination-registry-ads.md), [root safety reading](reader-notes-advertiser04-root-safety-ads.md).

The largest observed brand libraries—Skylight 878, Babylist 534, eMeals 401 and Lovevery 323—span hardware, commerce, broader household software and physical subscriptions. **Their size cannot be transferred into demand for a one-time baby report.** Conversely, smaller libraries can advertise higher-price services. This map ranks neither actual spending nor profitability.
