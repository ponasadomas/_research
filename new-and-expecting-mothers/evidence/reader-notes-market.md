## What changes the niche choice

**Age-based development information is a crowded starting point.** Pathways' free app supplies daily age/milestone recommendations, activity videos and caregiver sharing. CDC's free app includes corrected-age checklists and a shareable PDF summary. Kinedu has both an activity app and a separately priced assessment/profile product. The proposed differentiator therefore cannot merely be a child's age printed on a report. [mkt01-0003] [mkt01-0004] [mkt01-0006] [mkt01-0008]

**The commercial unit should be a decision removed from the household.** This is interpretation, not an observed buyer finding. Existing providers bundle meaningful inputs and outputs: Honey & Milk adapts pumping to work routines and supplies caregiver/handling instructions; Modern Motherhood turns a planning conversation into a postpartum support plan; 101 Before One supplies menus with shopping lists. The useful object is something a parent can hand to another caregiver or execute with available time and food. [mkt01-0014] [mkt01-0016] [mkt01-0017] [mkt01-0023] [mkt01-0024]

**A report is not automatically a cheaper equivalent of professional help.** Pumping offers include assessment, fit/setup, questions and adjustments. Postpartum planning services include a human conversation and some offer actual in-home support. Their advertised fees do not prove that customers will pay for an unsupported questionnaire-generated file. [mkt01-0015] [mkt01-0018] [mkt01-0023] [mkt01-0024] [mkt01-0025]

**First solids has visible guide price anchors, alongside strong competition.** 101 Before One advertises a $29 first-month meal plan. Solid Starts' US listing already describes tailored food/meal progression, and BLW Meals has diet-filtered age menus. A stronger custom report would have to reflect family food culture, actual ingredients, budget, cooking equipment/time, caregiver arrangements and the parent's chosen feeding approach; medical or allergy decisions need separate qualified oversight. Those additional inputs are product hypotheses, not proven unmet demand. [mkt01-0014] [mkt01-0026] [mkt01-0027]

**Sleep is a recurring-data problem.** Huckleberry and Napper offer predictions/adaptation based on logged sleep; Taking Cara Babies teaches parents to customize routines for changing circumstances. A one-time generic age schedule offers little demonstrated advantage. Pampers' Smart Sleep Coach is in sunset, but the closure notice gives no reason from which to infer category profitability. [mkt01-0009] [mkt01-0010] [mkt01-0011] [mkt01-0012]

**Pregnancy exercise is not a low-expertise entry category.** Expect, Every Mother and Expecting & Empowered supply reviewed or specialist-positioned demonstration programs. The product work includes appropriate exercise selection, execution cues and safety boundaries, not simply presenting a trimester calendar. Seller health claims must not be copied as clinical evidence. [mkt01-0020] [mkt01-0021] [mkt01-0022]

## Candidate comparison — interpretation requiring audience validation

| Candidate buyer/trigger | Observable competing value | Plausible customized-report job | Main unresolved issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-time parent of a baby roughly 6–16 weeks, unsure how to use ordinary awake time | Free Pathways activities; Kinedu/BabySparks paid daily plans | A small set of feasible play choices matched to space, time, available objects and what baby comfortably enjoys | Why pay instead of using a good free app? Avoid implying a delay or forcing activities. |
| Parent approaching first solids around six months | Free preparation databases, paid first-month plans and tailored apps | Household-specific shopping/cooking and caregiver serving plan | Existing products already cover many diet options; qualification and review needed for health-sensitive content. |
| Lactating parent with a fixed return-to-work date | Professional workday pumping plans and follow-up | Logistics-only work/commute/caregiving handoff checklist built around an established clinician-approved routine | Parents may need assessment rather than scheduling; employer/country details differ. |
| Expecting parent/couple in late pregnancy with limited nearby practical help | Doula planning sessions and postpartum memberships | A feasible first-weeks roster: who does which task, meals, helper availability, backup arrangements and contact sheet | Does a purchased document motivate real participation? A PDF cannot create missing people or time. |

Tentative validation order from market evidence alone: test postpartum household coordination and a tightly bounded first-solids planning job first; retain low-distress ordinary play as the closest extension of Mamivio, and work-return handoff as a more specific but clinically entangled option. This is an editorial judgment about report format, not a demand ranking. The audience corpus may reverse it.

## Freshness and interpretation traps

Boob to Food served euro-localized prices. BLW Meals' US listing contains several monthly/annual purchase SKUs; the applicable checkout offer is not resolved.
