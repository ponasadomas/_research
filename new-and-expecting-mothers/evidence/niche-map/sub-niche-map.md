# Sub-niche map — new and expecting parents

Updated 2026-11-17 after the four recovery digests. This map describes expressed jobs and compares the evidence for information products. It uses the same conditional commercial ranking as [global niche opportunities](../../global/synthesis/niche-opportunities.md); the earlier separate order is withdrawn. **Sleep leads on observed paid tailoring; no standalone customized-report business or price is validated.** Every previous quote id is retained. Source and capture dates remain historical, and cited consumer claims are not clinical or current legal findings.

The global set now contains **6,991 quote records**, including all **4,829 recovered quotes** from 3,807 assigned units. The [evidence file](../../global/synthesis/evidence.md) reproduces all new quotes under themes and preserves all money items and requests; the [closing report](../../global/synthesis/unfilter-report.md#re-reading-complete--2026-11-17) records what remains unavailable. Counts are excerpts, not independent people, buyers, prevalence or sales. Subject never determines research inclusion; the owner decides product and copy use later.

## 1. What the reference model establishes

The captured Pirmi Žingsniai baby offer uses age/month/week selection, an emailed PDF within 48 hours, one payment and a claimed 30+ exercises, advertised at €17.95. Its pregnancy guide uses first/second/third+ pregnancy selection and advertises a 40-week PDF at €29.95. Mamivio’s observed age-in-weeks offer lists 1/3/6-month plans at $17.95/$29.95/$49.95. These are seller promises and observed prices; claimed customer counts do not verify sales, successful use or the value of thin personalization. [mkt01-0001] [mkt01-0002]

The local October advertising snapshots remain useful supply evidence: [Pirmi and the Hungarian clone](../ads-running/pirmi-zingsniai-and-elsolepesekmama.md), [cross-niche advertiser sweep](../ads-running/spend-footprint-by-subniche-2026-10-25.md) and the [43-Page global money map](../../global/synthesis/advertiser-money-map.md). They show development/pregnancy advertising for the reference model, sleep course/program advertising in the English-language sweep, development-app advertising and a potty-course cluster. They do not supply spend, profit, conversion or a reason to infer that pregnancy information products are absent everywhere. No new network research was performed.

The earlier ClickBank snapshots compare affiliate offers across unrelated topics and older women’s pelvic-floor products. They cannot establish cold-traffic conversion for this buyer, postpartum demand or a winning delivery format. An offered price, affiliate metric, public ad, adjacent purchase and independent report buyer answer different questions.

## 2. How evidence is compared

For every job ask what was actually paid for, what inputs materially changed it, what the buyer used, what happened afterward, whether the written artifact can perform the task, what requires observation or execution, which free/owned substitutes work, and how quickly circumstances change. A selector is a hypothesis about relevant inputs, not proof that the output helps. Disability, symptoms, values, desired contact, care trust and actual available hours can change the task itself.

The [historical regex table](width-reddit-mention-share.md) and [external width proxies](../sources/width-proxies-external.md) remain discovery aids. In the archived general communities, sleep words matched 25.2% of r/NewParents and 17.0% of r/beyondthebump bodies; pregnancy symptom words matched 28.7% of r/BabyBumps. These are source-specific mention matches with overlaps and loose patterns, not shares of parents or market demand. The baby-heavy collection leaves toddler/potty/speech undercovered; low matches and subscriber/Ad Library result counts cannot rank commercial opportunity. Current theme counts live in [global counts](../../global/synthesis/counts.md), replacing the older small quote totals in this map.

## 4. Jobs by stage, including evidence that changes the job

**A1. Pregnancy weeks, symptoms and uncertain results.** Generic week-by-week content has strong free substitutes. Parents also explicitly seek locally credible complication/procedure education, interpretation after screening and access to care, which the generic comparison missed. A week selector does not encode symptoms, disability, treatment or prior loss. [guide01-004] [fdeep01-0009] [glu11-0126] [glu11-0127] [Evidence theme](../../global/synthesis/evidence.md#reread-pregnancy).

**A2. Birth preparation, consent and the hospital.** A plan can support orientation and questions; it cannot guarantee consent, pain relief, a hospital slot or the desired route. Changed plans and interventions can be welcomed or mourned. The earlier “good paper fit” and seller-price-as-payment-proof claims are withdrawn. ## 5. Other jobs with substantive evidence

Loss and bereavement can end an earlier shopping request and change later pregnancy, contact, leave and memorial decisions. Reproductive autonomy includes pregnancy continuation or abortion, contraception, fertility, adoption and surrogacy. These cannot be fitted into one universal expectant-parent journey. [glu01-0030] [glu01-0031] [glu01-0008] [Loss](../../global/synthesis/evidence.md#reread-loss) · [Family formation](../../global/synthesis/evidence.md#reread-reproduction).

Gear/registry fit, clothing, naming, memory/photo workflows and family events remain distinct jobs. A meaningful inherited book can cost nothing; a paid party or clothes order is not information spending. The original editable-baby-book request is retained. [rjnp01-0033] [glu13-0332] [Memory](../../global/synthesis/evidence.md#reread-memory) · [Clothing](../../global/synthesis/evidence.md#reread-clothing) · [Celebrations](../../global/synthesis/evidence.md#reread-events).

Consent, violence, reproductive/financial control, child-protection fears, unsafe-care allegations and institutional recourse have their own evidence. Records and written protection plans can serve accountability rather than everyday choreography; their allegations and second-hand reports remain attributed. ## 6. What remains unresolved

There is no owner quiz/order/support dataset, inspected paid Mamivio output, costed fulfillment analysis, search-volume/CPC/conversion/CAC dataset or validated launch-country choice. Observed prices and historical ads do not establish profitability or sales; independent self-reports establish reported purchases in their actual formats, without receipts. The global archive already includes a sleep-specific extension, so the previous “no sleep-specific community harvested” statement is withdrawn. Toddler, breastfeeding and mental-health coverage remain uneven; missing originals, comment sources and unavailable forum text remain in the [completion report](../../global/synthesis/unfilter-report.md#re-reading-complete--2026-11-17).

Future work, if separately requested, should identify intentional purchases, the delivered artifact, actual charge, material changes based on inputs, use, disappointment, support/revision needs and total delivery cost. Keep whole-source reading, duplicate/dependence review and exact-source checks. No collection, pilot, product work or advertising action follows from this map.
