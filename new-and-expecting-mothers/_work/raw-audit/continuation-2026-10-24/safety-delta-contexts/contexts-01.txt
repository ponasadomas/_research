I knew before becoming a parent that I probably wouldn’t be getting the same amount of sleep for a very long time, and that babies are notoriously shit at sleeping, but I guess I thought it eventually got better? My daughter is almost one, and still wakes multiple times a night, my entire post history is mostly about her sleep issues. She’s been sleep trained since six months, when I was at my breaking point waking every hour, and it did help but she still won’t do longer than a four, sometimes 4.5 hour stretch, it’s mostly two and three hour stretches. 

When she wakes she only wants to be nursed, I’ve tried just rocking her, having her dad go in, but she becomes hysterical, and if I’m up with her for longer than half an hour I find it really hard to go back to sleep even when she goes back down. So, all the night wakings are on me and have been since she was born. 

I’m at the point where I cannot talk about sleep with other parents, I get too jealous. Everyone I know who has a baby of similar age has been sleeping through the night for a while now, hell I know newborns that sleep better than my daughter. My family are constantly asking me if she’s sleeping better yet and I have to still keep saying no, not yet, and then they’ll of course love to tell me how all their kids were at this age. 

I’m a SAHM right now so time to myself is very limited, my husband works long hours and she doesn’t really like to be away from me. If I go into another room and leave her with him she screams. I used to nap when she napped but her naps are getting shorter and she’s starting to fight them so I have a feeling we’ll be cutting down to one soon, so that’s even less time on my own. At bedtime I give myself max one hour after she’s gone down because I know I need all the sleep I can get. That gives me less than two hours to myself a day, every day. 

I wouldn’t mind if I could just get decent SLEEP. I miss it so much. I honestly have no idea when it will get better. Everyone’s suggestions are to cosleep or sleep train, she is sleep trained but it’s not a miracle, I’ve tried co sleeping and she sleeps even worse that way. 

I plan to wean at a year so I have no idea how that’s gonna go for night time, but I know she doesn’t need to feed during the night, it’s purely habit at this point. It could make things better, or worse, and even with how shit they are now I know it can still get a lot worse.

All this to say, I’m exhausted, and out of my mind jealous of parents with good sleepers. I heard someone say recently their kid was an awful sleeper because they wouldn’t sleep longer than six hour stretches until nine months then started sleeping through the night, and I just thought if you think that’s an awful sleeper, then I have no idea what you’d call my daughter. I can’t even go into my bump group anymore because I feel like such an odd one out who’s still deep in shitty sleep trenches. It’s by far the hardest aspect of parenting for me so far.
