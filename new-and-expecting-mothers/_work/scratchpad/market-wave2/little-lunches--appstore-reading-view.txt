### Meals on easy mode
08/11/2022
NefariousRich
I don’t ever write reviews, but little lunches deserves it. It seems like a lesser known app, so I hope they stay in business. They’ve made meal planning and meal preparation so much easier. Every other meal planning app, such as tasty, mealime or allrecipes just over complicates the whole process. Those other apps only give you one piece if the meal and that recipe always takes forever. Little lunches takes what you have in your house into consideration and then builds meals for breakfast, lunch, and dinner all into one app. You click a button and it adds all of the stuff you need to walmart checkout. The meals are all well balanced and healthy. My wife and I have struggled for years to feed our family more veggies and eat healthier, but it’s just a lot of effort. Little lunches makes it easy by using different grains and vegetables and integrating them into your meals effortlessly. The meals are quick and uncomplicated and I’ve enjoyed every single one over the last two weeks. So have my five boys! Overall, fantastic app.
