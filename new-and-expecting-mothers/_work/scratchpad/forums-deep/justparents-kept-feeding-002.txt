Child lead weaning doesn't include giving the child food, otherwise you are pushing them to eat, child lead weaning is allowing the child to nurse for as long as nature intended, normally at 12-18 months. None of our children have ever had pureed food, it hinders the development of the gag reflex and they are far more likely to choke than on chunks of food.
With Pippa she sits at the table with us while we eat and if she wants to she is able to grab something on our plate to eat it, now she will sometimes chew the odd thing, normally she spits it out but she has swallowed the odd bit of carrot.
