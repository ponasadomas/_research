Was overdue by 1 week so visited DR, she took 1 look at my urine sample & BP & immediately rang the Maternity Hospital.  They called me in for an assessment the next day. Was put on a drip as had not eaten/drunk anything & was dehydrated. Could not leave my room, & everyone who came in had to wear gloves/apron etc.  Was finally given induction on Saturday night(very painful) & started getting cramps etc. Sunday AM, still just cramps but no sign of labour. another exam, lead midwife to think baby was breach, so another scan done - all ok.  Another pessary (induction) attempted & told to take a long walk (in the car park). At lunch time (12.00), i was examined & had dilated a tiny amount (just enough to break waters). As results not good, monitor was fixed to babies head.  At 12.00pm i was finally 10 cms dilated, so could start pushing. Epidural was wearing off.  Midwife said she would give me 2 hrs, if i could not deliver on my own, i may need C section.  At 2.30pm, was almost there (head showing), but Dr not happy for me to continue pushing, so they decided Ventouse could be used. Baby arrived 2.38pm. Healthy. It was a wonderful experience & all the midwives & staff were wonderful & very caring & attentive.  I was very happy with the birth overall, even though it was not how i had imagined. but you can't always get what you want.X

Ok heres my birth story
I had a relatively straight forward pregnancy everything was fine. I was due on the 27th August 2006 (which was bank holiday saturday and I live and was booked into a hospital in a very busy seaside resort. So I was very much hoping I wouldnt go into labour then as getting to the hospital would be a nightmare.
I woke up at 6am on Tuesday 22nd August with these pains across my stomach I didnt pay much attention to them as it wasnt my due date (stupid but anyway) I eventually I woke up my OH and said that I was getting these stomach cramps and that I'd probably eaten something bad and that I wanted to go the shops and get some pink babygrows as cos I was having a girl and wanted something pink to put her in (every mothers thing when they know what they are having) so we went across to weston to mothercare and I got some baby grows and I was moaning that pains where getting worse and my OH was like I think your in labour and I was like I cant be its my due date. Turned out I was and at about 4pm on the tuesday afternoon I went to the hospital and they gave me an examination and said that I had only just started to dilate and I'd be better off at home.
So back off home we went my mum was the emergency taxi run to the hospital and dropped us back off at out flat where I had a nice hot bath like the hospital said which helped until I got out the bath.. 😬 I couldnt do anything I struggled to get comfy etc and in the end my OH rung the hospital and I went back across at about 3 or 4am and got checked and was told I was 5cm and taken to the delivery room unfortunately as it was a small midwife based unit there was only 2 delivery suites and the one I was in was needed for someone who was alot further on than me so I was moved into a small side room at about 10am I got moved back into a delivery suite checked and I was now 7cms and the pain was getting so bad I opted for the gas and air (amazing stuff) and I had a student midwife who stayed with me from 11am right through until after I'd given birth (she actually phoned her daughter and cancelled their plans for the afternoon as she really wanted to stay with me which I thought was quite sweet)
At 2pm I got checked again and in 4 hours I'd gone from 7cm to 8cm  😠 as you can imagine so they decied then they were going to break my waters for me and to help speed me along.
I dont remember when time wise it was I said I was ready to push, I just remember having to be checked twice to make sure there was no cervix (downside of having a student midwife every check was done twice) and at 1818 on wednesday 23rd August after just over 36 hours of contractions and no sleep her head finally came out though I had to stop pushing then as they had to use some suction on her as she has pooed on her way out so when they done that I had finally given birth to my beautiful Izzabella Natalya who weighed 7lb 6oz.
I had the injection which helped bring on my placenta and that was all delivered fine and I was very lucky and didnt need any stitches I just had some minor grazes (which were painful enough when going to the bathroom)
I stayed in the hospital until Saturday afternoon the first night the night midwife looked after izzy for me in the nursery and the 2nd night I looked after her though they did have to wake me up because she was crying and the 3rd night the day I was due to go home after several times of getting up with her the night midwife said that she would look after her and wake me up if they were any problems.
I guess I had quite a straight forward pregnancy and labour and I had a great little girl who started sleeping through the night from 6 weeks old I was very lucky baby wise unfortunately for myself I started to suffer from PND which I recognised and got help for.
