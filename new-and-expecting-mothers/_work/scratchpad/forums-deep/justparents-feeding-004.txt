Why don't you spoon feed? All babies will take a spoon, it does take them a little while to learn how to swallow without swallowing air though. If she has any teeth, use a plastic or rubber spoon so she doesn't hurt her teeth if she decides to bite down. There is cup feeding as well, for this you have to cradle the baby, preferably swaddled to restrict movement, then you tip the cup slightly so the milk touches her lips, when she opens her mouth leave it to allow her to taste the milk, then if she accepts tip it a tiny bit more so that when she sucks she can just about bring milk into her mouth. You cannot tip any milk in her mouth though otherwise she will choke and splutter.
