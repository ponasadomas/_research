I think that the time you save not preparing bottles and washing etc, might balance it out.
It will take effort, and the first 6 weeks are the hardest, but that is the case for everyone with a new baby.
I
strongly
recommend that you should phone your health centre, contact your Health Visitors and get them to give you the number of the local breastfeeding support group.  Get down there as soon as poss,
before
the baby is born.  go and chat to them, and find out who lives local to you.  Get a phone number or two, if you can.  They will be more than up for helping you through every stage of your breastfeeding, and it's nice to know there's someone on the end of the phone who could also pop round if need be.
Please, try it.  Give it your best.  If it's not working for you then it won't be working for your baby - no one needs a stressed mummy!!!  But give it your best shot.
