We’ve done London a couple of times with kids and the main thing I’d say is look for somewhere with a bit of space rather than a standard hotel room - it makes a huge difference with three children.
Aparthotels or family rooms tend to work really well so you’re not all squeezed in.
Location-wise, somewhere central but slightly quieter (like around South Bank or near Kensington) can be a good balance - easy to get around but not too hectic at night.
Also worth checking if they include breakfast - saves a lot of hassle in the mornings with kids.
