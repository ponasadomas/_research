There are plenty of internet providers out there to sell you web space and provide free software to setup the site.  They can also provide a domain name and secure shopping facilities.  Paypal is probably a good idea as it offers buyer and seller protection plus means that you don't have to handle credit card payments which opens you up to a lot of legal requirements.
I used 1&1 Internet for a website that I produced for a charity cycle ride last year, but if you are not technically up to the challenge of creating one then there are plenty of people out there that you can pay for this sort of thing.  If you want it done cheaper then it may be worth talking to a local college to see if any of their students would create the site for you in exchange for being a reference when they start looking for work.
