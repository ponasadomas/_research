Child lead weaning doesn't include giving the child food, otherwise you are pushing them to eat, child lead weaning is allowing the child to nurse for as long as nature intended, normally at 12-18 months. None of our children have ever had pureed food, it hinders the development of the gag reflex and they are far more likely to choke than on chunks of food.

Your boobs only replace milk that has been removed generally, so as less is being suckled from your left breast that may be why, you can get these very soft little tubes that attach to a bottle which you fill with milk, then the tubes attach to your nipples. Then you get the baby to suckle the breast, so she is getting milk but at the same time stimulating the nipple to encourage let down which then allows milk to flow from the breast until the amount gets larger and larger, until you don't need the tubes anymore. They are designed for giving medicine for breast feeders.  I so should of been born with boobs.
