Freddie went through this phase & got over it, but i think its starting up again.  my HV advised to put them somewhere safe, tell them your going to go to "hang the washing, i'll be back soon" - when you do got back, make big point of it " here i am back again".  also if you do go off, go back when he's least expecting it & say "i 'm back", "see you in a minute etc".  she told me its because they don't know that you are ever going to come back, as far as they know you have vanished forever,  he need to constantly remind them that you will come back & therefore eventually they will realise that although you do go, you will always come back.  Whilst your away, put on some music or childs TV/DVD to distract, them give them some toys to play with etc.  Hang in there.  i think too boys are particularly clingy (so i've heard). xx
