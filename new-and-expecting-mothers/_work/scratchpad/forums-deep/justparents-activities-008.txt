We got back yesterday from blue dolphin and had a great time  😀   I'd read tons of bad reviews online but we all really enjoyed it and i suppose the great weather helped.  Its a lovely area too.
The site was fine - busy and some things were expensive but only as expected.  If you want to get a table in the evening entertainment (galaxy room) then you need to be there early, doors open at 6pm and there's usually a queue.  Bingo was a rip off with cheap tacky prizes - don't waste your money! We only went round for one night on wednesday it was the circus and was great, our two little ones were just too tierd most nights.
Plough inn pub just down the roud (next roundabout towards scarborough) had some good value family food.
thats all i can think of - hope you have a great time and get some good weather.
