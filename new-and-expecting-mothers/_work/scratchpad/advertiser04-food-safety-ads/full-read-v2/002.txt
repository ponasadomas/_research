But here’s the thing: when it comes to starting solids, it’s not about winning or losing. 

Purées, finger foods, or a little bit of both? There’s room for all of it!

Every baby learns at their own pace, every family finds their own rhythm, and no two feeding journeys look exactly alike.

🥄 Okay, so what’s wrong with spoonfeeding? Around here we talk about the many benefits of baby-led weaning, which means: letting baby lead. The research tells us that the risk of choking is minimized when a baby brings food to their own mouth and that placing food into their mouth increases risk. Spoonfeeding, while perfectly acceptable as a method for feeding baby, does not allow baby to self-feed. And our goal here is to offer the safest approach backed by research. 

🥣 But why no purees? To be clear, we love purées! There’s nothing wrong with offering purées, but offer them in combination with chewable foods to give baby the safest start. Learning to chew is important between 6-9 mos and babies can’t do that with only textureless food. 

The real win here is helping your little one explore new foods, build confidence, and enjoy the adventure (mess and all). The goal is let baby lead the way.🤍
