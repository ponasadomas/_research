TITLE Association stacking is saving me
COMPLETE BODY
I had been having a really hard time getting my LO to nap during the day. I was having a hard time picking up on his sleep cues and in general he was just really hard to get him to sleep.

I decided to start implementing scheduled naps/ww. Now his schedule is  roughly 2/1.3/2 and he’ll nap for around 3 hours.

Now here’s what’s changed my life. In general we could easily spend 1-2 hours bopping him around trying to sooth him before he would go to sleep. Now, he using my “new technique” he falls asleep within 10 mins and normally takes between 2-5 mins.

I decided to combine association stacking and to me what feels like a modified CIO. I do 3 things - put on some gentle white noise, pat his back gently and rock in the rocking chair while I hold him to my chest.

The first couple of days I worked on establishing those sleep associations. When I would try to put him to sleep I would do the above and just let him fight sleep and “cry it out” whilst I held him. He would eventually just get bored/tired and fall asleep. The first 3 days it would take anywhere from 15-50mins for him to fall asleep. But the jet was to not give up and let him get out that energy.

During the day I’m just letting him contact nap but eventually I will work on putting him in his cot.

I know this might not seem that ground breaking to most but I’m so relieved that I have found something that works for me. And now he isn’t grumpy in the day because he’s no longer over tired!! I actually get to enjoy our we together🥹
