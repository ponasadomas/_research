# Services

Overwhelmed with Your Baby Registry? Find Clarity and Build the Perfect List!

Creating your baby registry should be exciting, not overwhelming! I specialize in stress-free baby registry planning, helping you focus on must-have essentials while avoiding unnecessary clutter. Together, we’ll set clear goals, carefully curate your list, and ensure every item fits your unique lifestyle and parenting needs.

Did you know your registry can include more than just baby gear? You can also add essential support services like birth and postpartum doulas, midwives, lactation consultants, pelvic floor therapy, chiropractic care, acupuncture, and more—many of which aren’t covered by insurance. I’ll help you secure the funds you need so you can invest in the expert care and support you truly deserve.

Let’s create a personalized baby registry that works for you! Book a FREE 20 minute consultation today.

*While I will do my best to provide your family with products and services that are tailored to your needs, I cannot guarantee any specific outcome of said products or services, as every person’s experience and every baby are different. I will give you the best information that I know today to guide you on an optimal journey with your new baby.
