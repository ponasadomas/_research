## A Closer Look at Our App for Parents 👀

Ask Lovevery

Introducing Ask Lovevery—your personal child development expert available anytime, anywhere. This new feature draws from Lovevery’s extensive database of content, including our research-backed courses and thousands of answers to real questions from parents just like you. Whether you’re dealing with a 3 a.m. sleep struggle or wondering if your toddler’s behavior is typical, Ask Lovevery is like having an expert in your pocket 24/7. Submit your own questions or explore our FAQ library organized by topics like Sleep, Feeding, and Brain Development.

Activities

Hundreds of them! Our Activities section serves as your own Digital Play Guide and features helpful videos with experts to show you how to make the most of your Play Kits. We include easy-to-read instructions and plenty of DIY ideas for more at-home learning.

Play Finder

Play ideas don’t get easier than this. From the Activities tab, tap on the magnifying glass icon in the top right corner to open our Play Finder. Simply point your camera at any Lovevery plaything to immediately see ways to play with that plaything, plus how it supports development. Tap on the age below the name of the plaything to see play at different ages.

My Child

We’ve all wondered, “Should my child be able to do _____ by now?” The Lovevery App’s “My Child” feature breaks it all down by developmental stage, so you can learn when certain cognitive, motor, social-emotional, and communication skills may emerge.

Home

The Home tab greets you when you open the app. Each week up to age 3, you’ll find a new, curated selection of plaything activity videos, articles, and expert advice related to a key aspect of your child’s development.

## FAQ

How much does the app cost?

Play Kits subscribers get free access to The Lovevery App! If you aren’t a Play Kits subscriber, you can try The Lovevery App free for 28 days 🙌🏻 If you love it, you can subscribe to our Play Kits for free app access or to the app only for $12 per month.

The Ask Lovevery feature looks so cool. How does it work?

Ask Lovevery gives you instant responses to your parenting questions 24/7. Submit your questions for quick, expert-sourced responses, or browse FAQs in categories like Sleep, Feeding, and Brain Development.

Does the app replace the printed Play Guide that came with my Play Kit?

The Lovevery App “Activities” tab was inspired by The Play Guide. This section of the app goes deeper into developmental play, guided by experts. Both the printed Play Guide and The Lovevery App will help you make the most of each stage.

Is The Lovevery App for my child?

The Lovevery App is for parents and caregivers, not your child 💕 What you learn through the app can help you connect with your child during playtime.

Is the app available on iOS (iPhone) and Android?

The Lovevery App is available on iOS (iPhone) and Android right now.

Can you tell me more about the topics you cover in The Lovevery App?

- Cognitive development

- Includes: “serve and return,” problem-solving, executive function

- Feeding

- Includes: drinking from a cup, introducing solids

- Language & communication

- Includes: babbles, receptive vocabulary, first words, signs of comprehension

- Motor Skills

- Includes: rolling, sitting, walking, grasping, stacking, fine motor skills

- Play

- Includes: floor time, independent play, play activities, developmental activities

- Sleep

- Includes: sleep training, bedtime routines, nap schedules

- Social-Emotional

- Includes: stranger anxiety, separation anxiety, managing feelings, tantrums, turn taking

- Tummy Time

- Includes: stages, positions, activities

How can I tell you about other topics I’d like to see in the app?

We’d love to hear what topics you’d most like to learn about. DM our Cofounder and CEO Jessica Rolph on Instagram at @jessicarolph.

Can I access the Lovevery App for free?

Our app is available for free to our Play Kits subscribers because together, the app and The Play Kits create a better, deeper play experience. The app includes activities and how-tos to help you make the most of The Play Kits and demonstrate ways to play with each plaything. If you aren’t a Play Kits subscriber, you can try the app free for 28 days.

How long will I have access to the app?

While The Lovevery App covers birth to age 3, you’ll have access to the app as your child grows. We hope you’ll continue to be inspired by the activity ideas and Ask & Learn feature as your child revisits and repurposes their Play Kits 🧠

I’m not a Play Kits subscriber, but I’m interested in the activities and app. How can I have access?

Easy! Download the app to try it free for 28 days.

I was a Play Kits subscriber for my first child and kept The Play Kits for my new baby. Can I access the app?

We’re so glad your family’s been enjoying—and growing with—The Play Kits. Please contact our Customer Experience Team, and we’ll be happy to consider your request for access. Get in touch here.

Can other people in my child’s life—a partner, caregiver, or family member—have access to the app, too?

Yes, they can. We hope The Lovevery App supports your child’s playtime with any lucky someone who’s a part of their life 🧡 They won’t have a unique login—please use your same login info for access.

I have a Play Kits subscription, but I don’t have a Lovevery account. How can I login to the app?

No problem. You can access the app by making an account using the email you used when purchasing your subscription 👍

What happens if I cancel my Play Kits subscription?

The app is for active Play Kit subscribers because the app and The Play Kits work together to help you make the most of every stage.
