# Pathways.org Baby Milestones App

## Play With Purpose. Track With Confidence.

The Pathways.org Baby Milestones App supports your baby’s development by showing what your baby should be doing and when, including important milestones like sitting, rolling, walking, and first words. Track your baby’s development with a personalized dashboard, and see hundreds of fun expert-recommended activities to help them grow and thrive. Available in 21 languages.

## Why the Early Years Matter

From birth to age three, a baby’s brain is like a sponge, soaking up new skills and building new neural Pathways (that’s where we get our name!), through a process called neuroplasticity. Because their brain is so flexible, this is a great time to guide their development.
