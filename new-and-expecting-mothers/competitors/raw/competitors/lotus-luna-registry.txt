The process of building a baby registry and knowing which products to buy can seem daunting with the overwhelming amount of options available for all things baby. As your baby gear expert, I take care of all the legwork for you, saving you time, money, and energy so you can focus more on nurturing your needs as you prepare for your baby’s arrival. I use my expertise in infant care, postpartum doula experience working alongside families using a wide range of products, rigorous research, and personal experience paired with your specific wishes to help select products and present options that best align with your family’s lifestyle, budget, and overall needs.

I specialize in helping families choose safe, non-toxic options, but above all, I work closely with you to understand your priorities and preferences through the process. It’s an incredibly exciting time as you look toward your baby’s arrival and think about all their needs, but it’s also important for me to bring your needs to the forefront, especially when it comes to postpartum healing and recovery. My services are designed to leave you feeling confident about your product choices and prepared to welcome your baby into the world.

I offer a 10% discount on the below packages for my birth and/or postpartum clients. I am always open to discussing payment plan options that would best support your family.

-

## If you have already created a registry, but would like an expert eye to ensure you’re on the right track…

1 hr. virtual session following a thorough review of your registry with specific suggestions & product recommendations

Delivery of completed registry checklist with “must haves” & “nice to haves” for reference of any additional needs or wishes

Text/phone/email support 1 week following virtual session for any questions that arise

$350

Questionnaire to understand your specific baby gear needs (up to 10 products), lifestyle, budget, & overall preferences

1-1.5 hr. virtual session to present & discuss select options for each product, specifically chosen for your family

Delivery of custom curated list with product recommendations & shopping links

Text/phone/email support 1 week following virtual session for any questions that arise

$450

Questionnaire to understand your lifestyle, budget, preferences, & overall needs

1 hr. final virtual session to review completed registry & answer any questions

Delivery of completed registry checklist
