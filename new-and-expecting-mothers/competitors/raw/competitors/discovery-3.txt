##### Is it safe to exercise during pregnancy?

Staying active during pregnancy is not only safe for most women, but it is also one of the best things you can do for both mother and baby. The Empowered Motherhood Program (EMP) was created to make this easier. It combines aerobic and resistance training with clear trimester-specific modifications and education, allowing women to move confidently throughout their pregnancy (Prevett et al., 2024; RANZCOG, 2023).

If you were active before becoming pregnant, then it is safe for you to maintain higher weekly training volumes, as long as symptoms are monitored and loads are adjusted as needed (Bains et al., 2025).   *  Lactation & long-term: Vigorous exercise does not reduce milk supply or quality and is linked to healthier perinatal growth and a lower risk of childhood obesity (Lovelady et al., 1990; Dewey & McCrory, 1994; Chen et al., 2021).

##### Do I need exercise experience to join?

Not at all. Our workouts are suitable for all fitness levels - from beginners to experienced exercisers. The EMP week by week programs offer workouts for all fitness levels with ability to progress or regress your workouts to suit your fitness and energy levels.

##### What types of workouts are included?

Because we love to move in a variety of ways, we've ensured that the EMP Pregnancy Week by Week Programs include a huge variety of workouts, including pilates, strength, cardio, yoga, stretch classes, fascial release, core and pelvic floor and more! 

##### How does EMP support pelvic floor and core health?

Definitely. As a physio-led program, it is really important to us that you learn how to stregnthen and protect your pelvic floor during pregnancy, and prepare it for birth.

Our physiotherapist-designed programs include dedicated pelvic floor and deep core exercises. These help you reduce pregnancy-related complications such as lower back pain, pelvic girdle pain and incontinence, help you to prepare for birth, and support postnatal recovery.

##### Can I do these workouts at home?

Yes! All workouts are on-demand, so you can access them anytime, anywhere, no special equipment required (just a mat and light weights if you choose).

##### What if I’m in my first/second/third trimester, can I still start?

Absolutely. EMP has week-by-week workouts tailored to each stage of pregnancy. You can safely begin at any trimester.

When you join the EMP, we recommend you complete our Personalised Where to Start quiz so that we can guide you to the best programs and workouts. 

 

##### Is there support beyond exercise?

Yes. Alongside workouts, EMP includes personalised support from our Founders, expert education, live Q+As, and a supportive global community of mothers and health professionals, so you’re never alone on your journey.

## FAQ

Is it safe for me to exercise during my pregnancy?

According to leading global expert on maternal health the American College of Obstetrics and Gynecologists (ACOG): Concerns that regular physical activity during pregnancy may cause miscarriage, poor fetal growth, musculoskeletal injury, or premature delivery have not been substantiated for women with uncomplicated pregnancies. Also, "physical activity and exercise in pregnancy are associated with minimal risks and have been shown to benefit most women, although some modification to exercise routines may be necessary because of normal anatomic and physiologic changes and fetal requirements." It is important that you consult your doctor before you start this or any other fitness program.

How can I get Expect?

Expect is 100% digital—just download our app to join. You’ll unlock OB/GYN-approved workouts anytime, anywhere. iPhone users will also have access to Pelvic Floor Rescue, our audio-only pelvic floor training and strengthening program, designed by a board certified physical-therapist and approved by a board-certified urogynecologist to treat incontinence without any special equipment.
