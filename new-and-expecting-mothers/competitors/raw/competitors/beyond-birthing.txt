## Doula in Your Pocket

This service gives you direct access to a trained Postpartum Doula. Through it, you can
obtain evidence-based information for all things postpartum. This type of support is
primarily done through text messaging.

#### Includes:

- initial in-person, video or telephone visit to discuss needs

- daytime text support between 9am and 6pm

- for the first two weeks, evening text support between 6pm and 9pm

- access to information and resources provided by the doula

#### Price:

- $100 per month

Contact me for further details

## Virtual Doula

Virtual services are helpful when you live somewhere that isn't within my range of
travel. If you have regular in-person support but would like to have someone to answer
questions or check in on you to see how you are feeling, then Virtual Support might be
for you.

#### Includes:

- video support (roughly 8 hours of video meetings accessed throughout the month)

- phone support between 9am and 5pm

- text support

#### Price:

- $250 per month

- this service can also be arranged on an hourly rate

## Postpartum Planning

Are you wondering what the first weeks and months will look like when your baby arrives?
A postpartum planning session is helpful to discuss what your hopes are and how to best
implement them.

#### Includes:

- Two in-person consultations:

- 1st consultation is to discuss what your hopes and wishes are

- 2nd consultation is to go over the proposed plans

- The final postpartum plan will be e-mailed to you

#### Price:

- $100.00

## Overnight Services

A restful sleep is important during the postpartum period. Is baby keeping you up more
than you had hoped? Do you have other children to get ready in the morning?

In person services available in your home between the hours of 9pm and 7am

#### Includes:

- getting up with baby as needed during the night

- giving bottles or bringing baby to breastfeeding parent

- light housework if needed (depending on the layout of your home)

- help with the preparation of items needed for the morning routine

#### Price:

- $40 per hour

- minimum of 8 hours

Contact me for further details

## Day Services

Whether
directly after the baby is born or in the months after, daytime support helps ease the
task load for new families.

In person services available in your home between the hours of 9am and 5pm.

#### Includes:

- light housekeeping

- meal preparation

- support for parents, infants, and other children

- access to resources for parents on topics such as infant feeding, infant sleep, safe
sleep and postpartum concerns

#### Price:

- $25 per hour (minimum 2 hours per visit)

## In-Hospital Services

In-hospital immediate postpartum support at the hospital once baby has arrived. Assistance in answering questions about the transition from hospital to home, help supporting early feeding and baby care.

#### Includes:

- up to two hours of support immediately after birth including (but not limited to):

- assisting with inital feeding

- information and support on how to care for baby

- information and support on how to care for birthing parent after delivery

#### Price:

- $70
