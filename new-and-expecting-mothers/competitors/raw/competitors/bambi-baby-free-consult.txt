Build Your Baby Registry With Us

##
Baby Registry Building With Expert Guidance

Starting a baby registry can quickly turn into a very long list of
products you’re not sure you need. During your virtual consultation,
a Bambi Baby expert can help you build a new registry or work through
one you’ve already started—thinking through the gear that makes the
most sense for your family, space, and lifestyle.

Whether you want help creating the whole registry or just need
another set of expert eyes before you call it finished, we can work
through it together.

Build Your Registry With an Expert

Stroller & Travel-System Planning
Think through daily routines, travel, newborn use, and how your stroller and infant car seat may work together.

Car-Seat Stages
Understand what you need now, what comes next, and which questions are worth considering before you register.

Nursery Essentials
Plan the major pieces first so cribs, dressers, gliders, mattresses, and room needs make sense together.

Everyday Baby Gear
Work through the practical items that support feeding, sleep, travel, outings, and your family's routine.

##

Lightweight strollers can look surprisingly similar online. Seeing
several models together makes it much easier to understand the
differences. We can line up the travel strollers you’re considering
and walk through what separates them—from folded size and carryability
to seat space, recline, canopy coverage, storage, and travel-system use.

Compare cabin-approved and overhead-bin-compatible travel strollers

Understand stroller weight differences and what they mean for carrying and travel

Explore newborn-ready travel stroller options

Get guidance building a travel system that fits the way your family travels

Car Seat Guidance

##
Understand Fit, Installation & the Next Stage

Car-seat shopping is different because safety, fit, and correct use
have to stay at the center of the conversation. Your virtual
consultation can include Certified Child Passenger Safety Technician
expertise to help you better understand infant seats, convertible
seats, rotating designs, installation considerations, child and
vehicle fit, and when it may be time to move into the next stage.

Infant, convertible, rotating, and next-stage options

Installation and ease-of-use considerations

Child fit and vehicle-fit questions

Travel-system compatibility when relevant

Virtual Consultation FAQ

##
Questions About Bambi Baby Virtual Consultations

Here are the details families ask about most before requesting
one-on-one virtual baby gear help.

Is a Bambi Baby virtual consultation free?

Yes. Bambi Baby virtual consultations are complimentary. There is no deposit or consultation fee.

How do I schedule a Bambi Baby virtual consultation?

Complete the consultation request form on this page. A member of our team will reach out to coordinate your virtual appointment.

Can Bambi Baby help me build my baby registry during a virtual consultation?

Yes. Registry building can be part of your virtual consultation. Whether you are starting from scratch or already have a registry underway, your Bambi Baby expert can help you work through the baby gear you are considering and build a registry around your family’s needs and lifestyle.

What products can I see during a virtual consultation?

Virtual consultations can cover strollers, car seats, playards, travel cribs, baby loungers, baby carriers, and nursery furniture including cribs, dressers, gliders, recliners, and more.

Can I compare strollers during a virtual consultation?

Yes. Your expert can demonstrate stroller features and place models side by side so you can better understand differences in size, fold, configurations, seating, storage, handling, and travel-system compatibility.

Can I get car-seat guidance during a virtual consultation?

Yes. Virtual consultations can include Certified Child Passenger Safety Technician expertise to help you understand car-seat stages, installation considerations, child and vehicle fit, ease of use, and travel-system compatibility.

Can Bambi Baby help me plan nursery furniture virtually?

Yes. Your expert can walk you through cribs, dressers, gliders, recliners, mattresses, finishes, and furniture collections so you can better understand scale, style, and the pieces you may want for your nursery.

What video app do I need for a virtual consultation?

We accommodate your video chat preference. Common choices include FaceTime, Zoom, and WhatsApp.

Can I use a virtual consultation before visiting a Bambi Baby store?

Yes. A virtual consultation can help you narrow down the products you want to see in person so you can arrive at the store with a more focused short list.

What happens after I submit the virtual consultation form?

After you submit the form, a member of the Bambi Baby team will reach out to coordinate your consultation and the topics you would like to cover.
