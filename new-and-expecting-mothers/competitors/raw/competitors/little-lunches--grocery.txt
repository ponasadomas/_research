#
Grocery Delivery with Little Lunches

###
Healthy meals planned and delivered — all in one app

Making family meals doesn’t have to mean long grocery lists and endless store trips. With Little Lunches, you get a personalized meal plan, an automatically generated grocery list, and direct connections to grocery delivery services like

Instacart

and

Walmart Grocery Delivery

. Publix shoppers in the Southeast can also enjoy

Publix delivery and pickup through Instacart.

##
Why Parents Love Grocery Delivery with Little Lunches

Automatic grocery lists

Your weekly meal plan instantly becomes a shopping list, sorted and ready to use.

Same-day delivery or pickup

Order directly through Instacart or Walmart Grocery Delivery and get everything you need delivered to your door — or choose fast pickup.

Time-saving meal planning

Spend less time planning and shopping, and more time enjoying meals with your family.

Smart ingredient reuse

We help you reduce waste (and save money) by reusing ingredients across your weekly plan.
