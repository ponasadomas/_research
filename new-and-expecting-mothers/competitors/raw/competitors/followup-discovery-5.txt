## Recipe #1: Chicken and Vegetable Rice

Image

### Ingredients

#### For the chicken:

  * 1 tablespoon of olive oil and more for cooking
  * 5 chicken thighs or drumsticks
  * ¼ teaspoon of salt
  * ¼ teaspoon of garlic powder
  * ¼ teaspoon of cumin
  * ¼ teaspoon of paprika

####  For the sofrito and rice:

  * ½ onion, finely chopped
  * ½ red bell pepper, finely chopped
  * 2 cloves of garlic, finely chopped
  * ¼ cup of cilantro, finely chopped
  * ¼ teaspoon of salt
  * ¼ teaspoon of garlic powder
  * ¼ teaspoon of paprika
  * ¼ teaspoon of cumin
  * ¼ teaspoon of curry
  * ¼ teaspoon of chili powder
  * 1 cup of marinara sauce
  * 1 ¼ cups of vegetable broth
  * 1 cup of white basmati rice
  * ⅔ cup of frozen peas

### Procedure

Step 1:
Add the chicken to a large bowl and add olive oil, salt, garlic powder, paprika, and cumin. Use clean hands to mix the chicken in the mixture.
Step 2:
Add olive oil to a large skillet and place it over medium-high heat. Once the oil is hot, add the chicken and cook until it's browned, about 4-5 minutes, then flip and cook for another 4-5 minutes. Remove from the skillet and transfer to a plate.

Step 3:
In the same skillet, you'll make your sofrito by adding the finely chopped onion and red bell pepper. Sauté for 2-3 minutes, then add the garlic and sauté for 30 seconds. Next, add your spices: salt, garlic powder, paprika, cumin, curry, and chili powder. Add the rice and mix it to incorporate with the spices. Cook for 30 seconds. Then, add the marinara sauce and vegetable broth, the peas, and the cilantro. Stir well to combine.

Step 4:
Place the previously browned chicken on top. Reduce the heat to low, immediately cover the skillet, and cook for 20 minutes. After 20 minutes, remove the skillet from the heat and let it rest covered for 10 minutes. Serve immediately. Garnish with cilantro and serve with a squeeze of fresh lemon juice. Enjoy!

## Recipe #2: Rice with Lentils

Image

### Ingredients:

  * 2 tablespoons of olive oil
  * 1 ½ finely chopped onions
  * ⅓ cup of green lentils
  * 4 cups of water for the lentils
  * 2 cups of water for the rice and more for the onions to taste
  * 1 cup of white rice
  * 1 teaspoon of cumin
  * Salt to taste

### Procedure

Step 1:
We begin by caramelizing the onion: add olive oil and onion to a skillet and cook for 6 minutes, stirring constantly. Add 3 tablespoons of water to the onion and continue cooking while stirring constantly.

Step 2:
While the onion is cooking, add the lentils and 4 cups of water to a pot. Cover the pot and cook for 40 minutes. If it needs more water while cooking, add it.

Step 3:
Continue cooking the onion until it is completely golden brown, stirring constantly. If you see it sticking to the pan, add a little water and stir constantly until you achieve golden, caramelized onions.

Step 4:
When the onion is ready, add the rice, a pinch of salt, and cumin. Cook for 1 minute. Add two cups of water. Stir, cover, and cook on low heat for 20 minutes. Remove it from the pot and let it rest for 10 minutes.

Step 5:
When the lentils are soft and the liquid has been absorbed, add a pinch of salt and stir to incorporate. Add the cooked lentils to the prepared rice with onions. Stir to combine the lentils with the rice and onions and serve.

# Reducing Food Waste and Saving Costs: How Little Lunches Meal Planning App is Transforming Family Mealtime

Food waste is a pressing global issue that not only impacts the environment but also burdens household budgets. According to the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO), around one-third of all food produced for human consumption is wasted every year. Individual American households waste around $2,000 worth of food annually. As families strive to reduce their environmental footprint and save money, innovative solutions like the Little Lunches meal planning app are playing a vital role. This article delves into the impact of food waste, explores practical household strategies to minimize waste, and showcases how Little Lunches is revolutionizing meal planning to achieve these goals.

## The Impact of Food Waste

Food waste has far-reaching consequences on the environment and economy. When food is discarded, it leads to greenhouse gas emissions, squandering valuable resources such as water and land, and exacerbating hunger and poverty. In addition, the financial burden of wasted food can strain household budgets, especially for families on tight incomes. By reducing food waste, families can take positive steps towards sustainability and create a more responsible approach to food consumption.

## Household Strategies to Save

Minimizing food waste begins at home, and families can adopt several practical strategies to reduce their wastage:

Plan Meals Wisely: Planning meals in advance enables families to buy only what they need, reducing the likelihood of perishable items going unused. 

Store Food Properly: Proper storage of groceries and leftovers can extend their shelf life, preventing spoilage. 

Practice FIFO (First In, First Out): Rotate groceries in the pantry and refrigerator, using older items first to avoid expiration. 

Get Creative with Leftovers: Transform leftovers into new meals or freeze them for later use. 

## How Little Lunches Helps Reduce Food Waste

### 1. Reuse Ingredients Already at Home

Little Lunches meal planning app takes inventory of ingredients already available in the kitchen and incorporates them into the meal plan. This feature prevents unused items from piling up and ensures that family favorites are regularly included in meal planning. By repurposing existing ingredients, families not only save money but also reduce their environmental impact.

### 2. Utilize Ingredients from Prior Week's Plan

The app smartly incorporates leftovers from the previous week into new recipes, minimizing waste and maximizing the utility of food items. This feature promotes mindful meal planning, making sure that no edible food goes to waste.

### 3. Meal Plan Recipes Sized for Nutrient Intake

Little Lunches takes into account the nutrient requirements of each family member when creating meal plans. The portion sizes are optimized to fulfill nutritional needs, avoiding over-purchasing and unnecessary leftovers. By customizing portion sizes, the app ensures that no food is wasted while also supporting the health and well-being of all family members.

### 4. Auto-Generated Grocery Shopping List

With Little Lunches, grocery shopping becomes a breeze. The app automatically generates a tailored shopping list based on the weekly meal plan, ensuring that families buy only what they need. By following the app's precise shopping list, families can reduce impulse purchases and avoid buying items they already have, leading to more efficient and cost-effective grocery trips.

## Conclusion

The impact of food waste on the environment and household budgets is significant, but with the right tools and strategies, families can make a difference. The Little Lunches meal planning app empowers households to reduce food waste, save costs, and enjoy delicious and nutritious meals. By reusing ingredients, optimizing meal plans, and generating precise grocery lists, the app is transforming the way families approach mealtime, making it more sustainable, efficient, and budget-friendly.

## The $11 Billion Diet: How Kids Are Surrounded by Junk Food Ads Every Day

Food and beverage companies pour roughly $11 billion into advertising each year, and a significant share is aimed at children and teenagers. Much of that budget promotes fast food, sugary drinks, candy, and salty snacks—familiar staples of the “junk food” category.

These ads saturate kids' daily lives, making it tough for families to prioritize nutritious choices. They're engineered to grab attention through colorful designs, cheerful characters, and catchy jingles, creating a world where less nutritious foods are constantly front and center.

This environment makes it more challenging for families to promote a healthy lifestyle, especially when marketing messages are so persistent.

Early exposures shape preferences. When highly processed foods come first, fresh foods can seem unfamiliar—even unappealing.

Support long-term change through a balanced food relationship that celebrates natural flavors.

Quick Tip: Offer familiar favorites alongside new foods to help build trust in flavor variety.

## Turning the Tables: What Parents Can Do

### Manage Media and Screen Time

Consider ad-free platforms and co-viewing strategies to decrease exposure. Ask simple questions like:

  * “Who’s that character talking to?”
  * “Is that food more fun or actually good for us?”

These moments build lifelong awareness.

### Create a Positive Food Environment at Home

You're your child's most important food influence. Keep familiar, nourishing options visible and available.

Use the Division of Responsibility to clarify roles:

  1. Parent: Decides what, when, and where
  2. Child: Chooses whether to eat and how much

Parent Tip: Include your child in food prep—it boosts curiosity and pride in trying new things.

### Push for Broader Change

Parental choices matter—but big shifts also happen at policy levels. Advocating for restrictions on child-targeted marketing helps everyone.

Support efforts that create a fairer food space—one where your choices aren’t drowned out by billion-dollar budgets.
