## I'm Aly

I’ve spent the last decade behind the scenes of growing companies helping them operate more efficiently and profitably. When I got pregnant with my first son, I had no idea what to do for maternity leave. So I wung it and ended up answering emails from my hospital bed. 10/10 don’t recommend. When we found out we were pregnant just 9 months later, I decided not to make the same excuses. I planned a wonderful maternity leave, fully funded and took another month off later that year to be with my family. Since then, I’ve been helping business owners plan their maternity leaves and create a better business to come back to. Four years later I have an amazing system that’s helped thousands of women take better time off without feeling pulled in two directions.

Most moms that come to me already have an idea of what they want, my job is to help find the resources and provide the frameworks that take this season from “just getting through” to “woah, this was an amazing shift for my entire business.”

## You Might Be Wondering

How Early Should I Start?
As soon as you can. The more time you give yourself, the more options you have for saving, state funding, and handing off work.

What if My Due Date Is Soon?
Planned moves the tasks you've missed to the top so you can catch up this week.

I'm Not Pregnant Yet. Can I Use It?
Yes. Enter the due date you're hoping for and update it when you know.

How Do I Get It?
You'll get an email with instant access right after checkout. Inside the members area you'll have everything saved for you to return to.

I Don't Have a Team. Will It Work for Me?
Yes, I've helped hundreds of solo business owners. You'll have guidance on how to approach leaving if you work on your own.

How Do I Know This Isn't Generic?
Like anything, what you get is what you give. Be as specific as possible entering your information, your desired timeline, and any other details about your business to get the best snapshot of your leave plan.

What if I Need More Support?
