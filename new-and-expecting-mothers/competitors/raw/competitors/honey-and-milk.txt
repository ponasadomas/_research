##
What to Expect

Successfully continue breastfeeding while returning to work with expert guidance on pumping, storage, and scheduling. This 60-90 minute consultation covers breast pump selection and optimization, pumping schedules that work with your job, proper milk storage and handling, maintaining milk supply while working, transitioning baby to bottle feeding, and troubleshooting common work-related challenges. Perfect for mothers planning to return to work while continuing their breastfeeding journey.
