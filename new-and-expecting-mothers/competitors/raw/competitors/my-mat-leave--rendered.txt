I'm Cassie. For years, I was the person inside companies designing parental leave programs — shaping policies, overseeing how they were implemented, and supporting teams who managed the day-to-day details.

I understood how the system worked on paper.

But not always how it played out in real life.

And then I went on leave myself.

I came back earlier than I needed to. Not because I wanted to — because I didn't fully apply my own expertise to my own situation. I'd helped hundreds of employees navigate leave, and I still left time on the table.

"I gave those weeks away. I didn't know I was doing it. That's the part that stays with me."

My second leave was different. Partway through, I was laid off.

Because I understood the sequencing — how benefits layer when your employment status changes — I was able to protect myself in a way most parents don't know is possible.

I healed. I bonded with my baby. And I received paid benefits for nearly a full year. Not because I was lucky — because I knew what I was entitled to. I didn't have to choose between my recovery and my mortgage.

"Most parents assume that losing their job means losing their leave. It doesn't have to."

Friends started asking for help. Then strangers. Every conversation confirmed the same thing: the benefits exist — but nobody is putting it together for you.

That's why I built MyMatLeave. What I know shouldn't only belong to me.

WHY STRATEGY MATTERS

Why Most Parents Don't Get Their Full Leave

The gap isn't benefits — it's strategy.

The information is scattered

EDD guides, employer handbooks, Reddit threads — most parents end up with gaps and conflicting answers.

Timing changes the outcome

When you file and how you layer benefits directly affects your total paid time off.

Five programs, one strategy

SDI, PFL, CFRA, and your employer's policy each have different rules — and for many California employees, FMLA doesn't apply. The value is in knowing which ones apply to you and how they work together.

AI gets the numbers wrong

California leave law is specific and changes regularly. Every MyMatLeave plan is reviewed by a certified leave strategist — not generated by a chatbot.

What is CA SDI? State Disability Insurance — the deduction on your paycheck labeled "CA SDI" or "CASDI." If you've been employed in California, you've likely been paying into it.
