# How to Improve your Marketplace Search Results

Sometimes you may not receive the results you anticipate from your search. If your search results are either too slim or too broad, you can improve your results by using the following techniques:

Change Your Terms

  * Enter the keywords in quotes. If you want results that include a specific phrase, entering it in quotation marks will reduce the results to that phrase only, not results for each individual word.
​

  * If you enter the keywords with “AND” in between them, the results will only include listings that have both terms.
​

  * If you enter the keywords with “OR” in between them, the results will include listings that have one or more of the terms in them.
​

  * If you enter the keywords with “NOT” in between them, the results will include only listings that exclude the second term but contain the first term.

  * If you enter “+” before a keyword, the results will only include listings that contain keyword listed after the “+”.

  * If you enter “-“ before a keyword, the results will exclude any listings listed after the “-“.

  * If you use parentheses with your keywords, you can do more complicated searches. For example, if you type (golf OR swing) AND help, you will get results that include either golf or swing combined with the word help, such as golf help or swing help.

  * Using the character ? will return listings that use keywords where the ? is replaced with a single letter. For example, if you type gol?, you would get results that include golf or gold.
​

  * Using the character * will return listings that use keywords where the * is replaced with multiple characters. For example, if you type gol*, you would get results that include golf, golfing, golfer, gold, golden, etc. This can be very useful for returning listings with related keywords, like the golf/golfer/golfing example.
​

  * Using the character ~ after a keyword will return listings that include keywords that are spelled similarly to the keyword. For example, if you type roam~, it would return listings with keywords such as roams, foam, etc.

Sort Your Results

Once you have completed a search, you can sort your search results by specific search parameters. You can sort results by:

  * Rank:
​Rank helps affiliates find the best offers based on a combination of factors, including overall sales volume, affiliate-generated sales, and affiliate commissions.
​

  * Avg $/Conversion:
​This number represents the average commission an affiliate earns for each conversion to the seller's offer, including the earnings from all sales (initial sales, upsells, and rebills). You can specify a minimum and/or a maximum Avg $/Conversion value.
​

  * Initial $/Conversion:
​This number represents the average commission an affiliate initially earns for each conversion to the seller's offer, not including rebills. You can specify a minimum and/or a maximum Initial $/Conversion value.
​

  * Future $/Conversion:
​This number represents the average commission an affiliate earns for each rebill payment of the seller's product(s). You can specify a minimum and/or a maximum Recurring $/Conversion.
​

  * Gravity:
​The gravity performance statistic is an approximate, relative measure of how many affiliates have successfully promoted products from an account, with a higher number meaning more conversions. You can specify a minimum and/or a maximum gravity value.

  * EPC (Earnings Per Click)

This number represents the average amount of net commission earned per click in the last 30 days.

  * CVR (Conversion Rate)

This number represents the average rate at which clicks from an affiliate link resulted in an initial sale in the last 30 days.

For more information on these parameters, check out the “How to Use the ClickBank Marketplace Advanced Search Function” article.

To sort your results, select the parameter you wish to sort by from the drop-down menu near the top of the returned results list after completing a search in the ClickBank Marketplace.
​


Filter Your Results

Once you have completed your search and have a returned list of results, you can filter the results using the following filters:

  * Gravity

  * Avg $/Conversion

  * Initial $/Conversion

  * Future $/Conversion

  * Earnings Per Click

  * Conversion Rate

Use Attributes

You can also narrow your results list by selecting for particular attributes. You can select what attributes to use by checking locating the “Attributes” box on the left side of the listing results page. You can filter your list by checking the following attributes:

  * Physical:
​Selecting this option displays only accounts with physical products.
​

  * Upsell(s):
​Selecting this option displays only accounts with upsell flows.
​

  * Required Language(s):
​You can select one or more languages to display only accounts with products in the selected languages.
​

  * One-Time:
​Selecting this option displays accounts with one-time products.
​

  * Recurring:
​Selecting this option displays accounts with recurring billing products.
​

  * $1 Trial:
​Selecting this option displays accounts with products with a $1 trial.
​

  * Must Have Affiliate Tools Page:
​Display only seller accounts with an Affiliate Tools page. An Affiliate Tools page contains resources such as images, tips, keywords, testimonials, and FAQs that can help you promote the account's products more effectively.
​

  * Support for Mobile Traffic:
​Display only seller accounts with a mobile HopLink target URL.
​

  * Recently Promoted:
​Display only seller accounts that you have recently promoted.
​

  * Affiliates Require Approval:
​Display only sellers that require affiliates to be approved to promote their products.

# How do I add or update my ClickBank Affiliate Marketplace listing?

Learn about how to update your ClickBank Marketplace listing

July 27, 2025

## Overview

An important step in attracting affiliates to promote your product is adding a listing to the ClickBank Affiliate Marketplace. The ClickBank Affiliate Marketplace is an online marketplace that displays active accounts with offers ready for promotion.

When affiliates are looking for products to promote, they often check the marketplace first. It’s up to the ClickBank Seller to create a compelling description to list in order to interest potential affiliates.
​Related: What is the ClickBank Marketplace?
​

There is one marketplace listing per ClickBank Nickname. If you have different offers in different categories, consider creating an additional nickname from your primary ClickBank account.

After the first 90 days following seller activation, a seller must meet one of the following criteria to be eligible to be included in the marketplace:

  * Have an affiliate generated sale or rebill transaction within the past 90 days that have not been subsequently returned.

  * Have a marketplace listing that has been updated in the last 90 days.

NOTE: If your marketplace listing is no longer appearing in the marketplace, try adding an affiliate tools page, or updating your listing to be more descriptive and compelling.

Follow these steps to add or update your ClickBank Affiliate Marketplace Listing.

Step 1: Log in to your ClickBank account.

Step 2: Click the Accounts tab.
​

Step 3: Click the nickname with the marketplace listing you want to update.
​

Step 4: Click the Vendor Settings tab and then click My Site.
​

Step 5: Locate the Marketplace Information box and click Manage Marketplace Listing.

Step 6: Select the Category and Subcategory that best fit your marketplace listing.

Step 7: Enter the listing details. The Offer Title and Offer Description are mandatory fields.

​​Step 8: Enter up to 10 keywords that describe the offer.

### Question 3: What Is the Fastest Useful Format?

Format follows the problem.

If the customer needs to remember five steps, create a checklist.

If she needs to repeat a process, create a template.

If she needs examples and explanation, create a short guide.

If she needs to watch a task being performed, create a short tutorial or mini-course.

If she needs to organize information over time, create a planner, tracker, calendar, or workbook.

Choose the smallest format that can create the promised result. A twelve-module course is not automatically more valuable than a one-page checklist.

It is definitely harder to finish.

### Question 4: Is There a Demand Signal?

A demand signal is evidence that people care about the problem.

It is not a guarantee that they will buy your version.

For Kids Activities Blog, search activity helped me choose which coloring pages to create. If parents were already searching for a particular topic, that told me the need was not imaginary.

You can also look for repeated customer questions, comments in relevant communities, competing products, popular videos, bestselling books, or related offers in an affiliate marketplace.

Digistore24 currently describes its marketplace as containing more than 10,000 offers across 44 niches. That makes it useful for exploring categories, language, prices, and problems that sellers and affiliates are already working with.

It does not mean every similar product will sell.

Use the marketplace as one signal. Then look for the same problem in at least one other place.


## The One-Hour Digital Product Idea Check

You have an idea. Before you spend a week building it, spend an hour looking for evidence.

This is not scientific market research. It is a quick check designed to catch obvious problems and help you ask better questions.

Time  | Check  | What to look for  | What it can tell you
10 minutes  | Search activity  | The exact language people use and related questions  | Whether people are actively looking for help
10 minutes  | Digistore24 marketplace  | Relevant categories, offers, prices, positioning, and affiliate activity  | Whether sellers are investing in the broader problem area
15 minutes  | Communities and comments  | Repeated questions, frustration, workarounds, and missing resources  | How real people describe the problem
15 minutes  | Competitors  | Similar products, reviews, pricing, strengths, and gaps  | What buyers can already choose and what may be missing
10 minutes  | Interest check  | Direct replies, waitlist signups, or conversations with likely users  | Whether your specific promise earns attention

### Start With Search Activity

Search the exact problem, not just the product name.

For my coloring pages, I did not begin with “What PDF should I create?” I looked at what parents were searching for and created resources around that demand.

Read the related searches and questions. Notice the nouns people use. Notice whether they want instructions, examples, a template, a printable, or a recommendation.

This can also uncover a better format. People may not want a twenty-page guide. They may want one printable page they can use in ten minutes.

### Browse the Digistore24 Marketplace

Search the broader category and look at how existing offers describe the customer, problem, and result.

Competition is not automatically bad. It can show that sellers, affiliates, and buyers are active in the category.

No competition is not automatically a secret opportunity. It may mean the product uses different language, lives in another category, or has very little demand.

Look for patterns. Do not copy another product.

### Read Real Questions

Search communities where the one person already gathers. Read public discussions, comments, reviews, and questions.

You are looking for repeated language, not one dramatic complaint.

Save the phrases people use. Those phrases may help you clarify the product, title, sales page, and examples.

If you participate in a community, follow its rules. Do not drop a sales pitch into a group that prohibits promotion.

### Test the Specific Promise

Talk to a few people who resemble the intended customer.

Describe the product plainly:

I am creating a [format] for [person] who needs to [result]. It will help with [specific problem]. Would that be useful to you? What would it need to include?

You can invite people to a waitlist or offer early access. If you accept payment before the product is complete, clearly explain what the buyer will receive and when it will be delivered.

There is no magic number of replies that guarantees a winner.

## Deconstructing the Course-Flipping Trap: Why Selling "The Dream" Fails

Many beginner affiliate marketing opportunities promoted to parents are actually master resell rights (MRR) programs. These programs charge up to $1,000 for training that contains little actual business value, instruction, or physical product utility. Instead, they teach you how to sell the exact same course to other tired parents.

This creates an empty loop that leads to extreme moral fatigue and jadedness. Many parents enter these programs hoping for financial relief, only to realize they are participating in a system that feels like a cult. Recommending physical utility goods that you actually use is a much more stable and ethical business model. You build long-term, compounding trust by helping parents find the right baby monitor, stroller, or sleep routine.
