# The Best Apps for Starting Solids and Baby-Led Weaning in 2026

By Berkay Yenilmez

Published on October 12, 2026 • 7 min read

Sito is our app, and it is the one we recommend for starting solids and baby-led weaning. We read the App Store listings of six other apps on October 8, 2026, and set them side by side with Sito below.

TL;DR

  * For starting solids, we recommend Sito (our app): how to serve 583 foods at your baby's age, a log of every taste, and 9 allergens one at a time with a check-in after each.
  * Solid Starts, BLW Meals, Tummi and three more apps sit beside Sito in the table, each as its own App Store listing describes it.
  * Sito keeps going at 12 months, when meals and 18 nutrients open on their own, and it makes one PDF for the doctor in six languages.

## The best app for starting solids: Sito

Sito changes with your baby's age. From 4 months the home screen becomes Starting Solids.

Before the first taste, Sito shows the NHS's three signs of readiness and a short safety sheet: stay with your baby at every meal, sit them upright, and learn infant first aid for choking.

## Solid Starts, BLW Meals, Tummi and the other starting solids apps

### Solid Starts

Solid Starts covers solids "with baby led weaning, BLW, or transitioning from spoon feeding or purées to finger foods" (App Store, updated October 7, 2026). Its listing names 400+ foods with how to cut and serve them by age, and a food log whose reactions you can download for doctors. We compare the two apps on our Solid Starts alternative page.

### BLW Meals

BLW Meals describes a baby-led weaning guide from 6 to 24 months with allergen guidance (App Store, updated September 18, 2026). The listing names 100+ foods with how to cut and prepare them, recipes and meal plans, then guides for 2 to 7 years.

### Tummi

Tummi lists 400+ foods with prep by stage, a log of foods, reactions and milestones, and an AI chat for feeding questions (App Store, updated August 27, 2026).

### 101 before one

101 before one is a baby-led weaning and family-meals program for 6 to 12 months (App Store, updated October 8, 2026). The listing names 101 foods, 300+ recipes and meal ideas, and the top 9 allergens.

### Starting Solids: BLW & Purees

Starting Solids: BLW & Purees shows 200+ foods at 6, 9 and 12 months with their choking risks (App Store, updated October 4, 2026). Allergen exposures and reactions go in a diary, and its guides run from 6 to 24 months.

### Baby Led Weaning App

Baby Led Weaning App by Drama Labs lists 1000+ baby-led weaning foods and recipes together, a food diary, and allergen introductions and reactions tracked (App Store, updated February 7, 2026).
