Make mealtimes easier with free personalized meal plans, smart grocery lists & healthy recipes for babies, toddlers & families, plus tailored AI feeding support.

Little Lunches helps families save time, reduce mealtime stress, and feed their children with confidence through personalized meal planning, kid-friendly recipes, lunchbox ideas, and feeding guidance designed by pediatric experts.

TOP FEATURES

Smart Meal Planning
• Personalized weekly meal plans tailored to your family’s preferences, allergies, and nutrition goals
• Plan meals for breakfast, lunch, lunchbox, dinner, and snacks
• Built-in grocery lists and pantry tools to simplify shopping
• Grocery delivery integration with Instacart and Walmart in supported regions
• Add another parent or caregiver to plan together

Healthy Recipes for Kids & Families
• 500+ healthy, kid-approved recipes for babies, toddlers, kids, and parents
• Baby-led weaning (BLW), toddler meals, lunchbox ideas, family dinners, snacks, and more
• Allergy-friendly filters and nutrition facts for every recipe
• Step-by-step cooking tutorials to help you cook with confidence
• Save favorite recipes and create your own custom recipes

Baby Food & Feeding Support
• Discover age-appropriate foods and recipes from 6 to 24 months
• Learn how to prepare, serve, and introduce new foods with confidence

• Practical guidance designed to support every stage of your child’s feeding journey

### Meals on easy mode

08/11/2022
NefariousRich

I don’t ever write reviews, but little lunches deserves it. It seems like a lesser known app, so I hope they stay in business. They’ve made meal planning and meal preparation so much easier. Every other meal planning app, such as tasty, mealime or allrecipes just over complicates the whole process. Those other apps only give you one piece if the meal and that recipe always takes forever. Little lunches takes what you have in your house into consideration and then builds meals for breakfast, lunch, and dinner all into one app. You click a button and it adds all of the stuff you need to walmart checkout. The meals are all well balanced and healthy. My wife and I have struggled for years to feed our family more veggies and eat healthier, but it’s just a lot of effort. Little lunches makes it easy by using different grains and vegetables and integrating them into your meals effortlessly. The meals are quick and uncomplicated and I’ve enjoyed every single one over the last two weeks. So have my five boys! Overall, fantastic app.
