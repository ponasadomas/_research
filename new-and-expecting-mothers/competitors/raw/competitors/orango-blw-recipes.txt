New: share your baby's WhatsApp stickers on social media and get a month of Pro free. Plus 500+ first foods and 600+ baby-led weaning recipes.

BLW Recipes takes the guesswork out of starting solids. Explore 500+ first foods with age guidance, serving ideas, choking and allergen notes — and 600+ baby-led weaning recipes the whole family can share.

TRACK EVERY FIRST BITE
• Mark foods as tried and log any reactions
• Allergy notes stay attached to the exact food
• See what's still left to discover

SMART MEAL PLANS (PRO)
• Generate 1 day to 100 days in one tap
• Automatically skips your baby's allergens and fits your family's diet
• Build your own plan, meal by meal
• Turn any date range into a grocery buy list

MADE FOR REAL FAMILIES
• Filter by age, meal, skill level, diet and allergens
• Works in 10 languages
• No account needed — your data stays on your device

Includes a 3-day free trial of Pro. Not medical advice: always supervise meals and talk to your pediatrician about allergens.
