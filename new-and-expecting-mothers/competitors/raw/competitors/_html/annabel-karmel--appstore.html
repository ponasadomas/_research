Make feeding your baby, toddler and family easier with Annabel Karmel's multi award-winning recipe app. From first tastes and baby-led weaning to family meals, you'll find expert guidance, practical tools and over 1,500 nutritious recipes all in one place. Millions of families rely on Annabel's recipes and advice to raise healthy, happy babies. With over 35 years of baby and children’s food expertise, Annabel’s app is the leading resource for making mealtimes simple - and tasty. Start your 7-day FREE TRIAL today! OVER 1500 SIMPLE RECIPES It’s easy to search for, filter and save over 1500 delicious, nutritious recipes for every age and stage. You’ll NEVER be stuck for mealtime inspiration again. Plus, enjoy a fresh batch of recipes from Annabel’s kitchen every week. 100+ FIRST FOODS This is your ultimate guide to safely introducing 100+ first foods to your baby. Learn how to safely prepare each food at every age and stage, whether you are spoon feeding or baby-led weaning. You'll also find allergy advice, preparation tips, and recipe inspiration all in one place. FIRST FOODS TRACKER Keep track of foods your little one has tried and loved, celebrate feeding milestones and build confidence as you introduce more variety. You can also log allergic reactions and create a helpful record to share with healthcare professionals if needed. ADVICE HUB Got questions? Visit our new Advice Hub, where Annabel shares expert answers to common feeding concerns, from milk feeds and first tastes to teething and baby-led weaning. RECIPE COLLECTIONS Save and store your favorite recipes into personalized collections to make planning, shopping and cooking for your family easy. THUMBS UP? Mark recipes as ‘It’s a hit!’ or ‘Try again’ to help you keep track of your little one’s favorites, and those you might need to give another go. It can take lots of tries for babies and tots to accept new flavors! MEAL PLANNERS Plan your family’s mealtimes with custom Meal Planners, so you’ll always have an answer when they ask, ‘what’s for dinner?’! https://www.annabelkarmel.com/terms-conditions/
