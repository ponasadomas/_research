Grocery Delivery with Little Lunches

Healthy meals planned and delivered — all in one app

Making family meals doesn’t have to mean long grocery lists and endless store trips. With Little Lunches, you get a personalized meal plan, an automatically generated grocery list, and direct connections to grocery delivery services like Instacart and Walmart Grocery Delivery . Publix shoppers in the Southeast can also enjoy Publix delivery and pickup through Instacart.

Why Parents Love Grocery Delivery with Little Lunches

Automatic grocery lists

Your weekly meal plan instantly becomes a shopping list, sorted and ready to use.

Same-day delivery or pickup

Order directly through Instacart or Walmart Grocery Delivery and get everything you need delivered to your door — or choose fast pickup.

Time-saving meal planning

Spend less time planning and shopping, and more time enjoying meals with your family.

Smart ingredient reuse

We help you reduce waste (and save money) by reusing ingredients across your weekly plan.

How It Works

  1. Plan your meals → Choose from hundreds of kid-approved recipes.
  2. Get your grocery list → Automatically generated and customizable.
  3. Order groceries online → One click to Instacart or Walmart for same-day delivery or pickup. Publix shoppers can also get groceries via Instacart.

Grocery Partners

Little Lunches works with trusted grocery services nationwide

  • Instacart Grocery Delivery → Same-day delivery from stores like Publix, Costco, Kroger, Target, and more.
  • Walmart Grocery Delivery → Budget-friendly groceries, delivered or ready for pickup.
  • Publix Grocery Delivery → Available in Florida and the Southeast through Instacart delivery and pickup.

Make Mealtimes Easier

Join thousands of families already using Little Lunches to simplify mealtime. With our integrated grocery delivery, your family eats better while you save time, money, and stress.
