How we work together: Virtual Sessions

1. Virtual Prenatal Sessions

During these virtual appointments, we’ll meet together via video or phone—your choice! Together we will discuss your birth preferences, create a tailored plan together, and discuss in detail any questions, concerns, or fears you have around your birth. We’ll also develop strategies to help eliminate potential fears with knowledge and intentional goal-setting and build trust in the birthing process with education and information. I’ll also explain the stages of labor, what to expect during each stage, and demonstrate comfort measures for you and a partner, if applicable, for early labor relief.

2. Virtual Postpartum Planning

You plan for all the big events in your life—a wedding, a new job, a vacation, a birth. Why would welcoming a new baby home be any different? Whether you’re a seasoned mom or a first time parent, you always benefit from personalized support in your tender fourth trimester period. With expertise and care, I help you feel peace of mind and confidence by creating a tailored plan alongside you that prepares you for your postpartum period. In this plan, I walk you through what to expect during the first 6 weeks postpartum, how to actually take care of your healing body, basic newborn care including feeding, baby-wearing, sleep basics and bathing, inform you on postnatal mood disorders, and teach you how to implement a detailed, tangible plan for creating your ideal circle of support and much more.

3. One-on-One Consultation

Do you have a bebe or mama question, need extra informational or emotional support, or would just like to process allll the things with a modern, caring, professionally trained practitioner? You’ve come to just the place! When you book a one-on-one consultation, you get an in-depth personalized consultation based on your unique needs. In your initial booking message, let me know what you’d like to discuss or a general overview of your questions/concerns. I’ll reach out to you to schedule a time for our session that can be done over video or phone. During this session, you’ll receive tailored, evidenced-based, and compassionate support that meets you where you’re at. No judgment, no shame, no bias. I understand the importance of being able to be heard, to be understood, and to be tenderly cared for as an expecting and new mother. You can rest assured that each session is secure and confidential. In an hour, we can typically effectively cover 1 to 2 topics in-depth, but if you’d like to schedule an extended session, you’re more than welcome to indicate so in your initial message. If you have a lot of little questions or unsure of how much time you’ll need, let me know in your booking message and we will work together to meet your needs!

How we work together:
Virtual Birth & Postpartum Packages

1. Complimentary Consultation

This complimentary meeting is a chance for us to meet, for you to learn more about my support services, to answer any questions you may have, and to determine if we are a compatible fit for each other. I recognize that inviting someone into your birth space requires mutual comfortability and trust for such a sacred event. At this meeting, the goal is to ensure that working together would be a wonderful addition to your birth experience.

2. Birth Preferences Session

At this session, we will go over your options for labor and the choices you have to create the best birth experience as determined by you! As the birthing person, how you feel about your birth is just as important as the outcome of your birth. You are much more inclined to feel empowered and supported after the birth process when you’re able to set intentions for your birth in a way that feels good to you. During this session I’ll help walk you through your options, create a birth preferences sheet with you, and provide tools to help you feel empowered and confident to advocate for your wishes in your birth. I’ll also explain the stages of labor, what to expect during each stage, and demonstrate comfort measures for you and a partner, if applicable, for early labor relief.

3. Postpartum Planning Session

Let’s move beyond the old adage of “sleep when baby sleeps.” You need someone to keep it real with you. You plan for all the big events in your life—a wedding, a new job, a vacation, a birth. Why would welcoming a new baby home be any different? Whether you’re a seasoned mom or a first time parent, you always benefit from personalized support in your tender fourth trimester period. With expertise and care, I help you feel peace of mind and confidence by creating a tailored plan alongside you that prepares you for your postpartum period. In this plan, I walk you through what to expect during the first 6 weeks postpartum, how to actually take care of your healing body, basic newborn care including feeding, baby-wearing, sleep basics and bathing, inform you on postnatal mood disorders, and teach you how to implement a detailed, tangible plan for creating your ideal circle of support and much more.

4. Communication

When you hire me as your birth doula, you will have text, e-mail, and call support throughout your pregnancy during normal business hours. You will receive 24 hour on-call phone and text support starting at 38 weeks until the birth of your baby. If you’ve hired me as your postpartum doula, you will have text, e-mail, and call support immediately following the birth of your baby through 6 weeks postpartum.

5. During labor & birth

Once you've hired me, I am on call for your labor starting at 38 weeks. I attend your birth virtually providing immediate support via text, phone, or video depending on your desired preference. I will stay with you virtually immediately postpartum until feeding and bonding has been established.

6. Postpartum check-in

Following your labor, I will check in with you the next few days to answer any questions you may have, help you process your birth, and assist you now that baby is home! You’re welcome to reach out to me during this time for added informational and emotional support. Those first days home are a huge transition! At one week postpartum, we will schedule a 1 hour video check-in to explore in-depth your recovery, your emotional well-being, and to cover any topics you’d like to discuss or process. If you’ve hired me as your postpartum doula, we will have weekly video check-ins through 6 weeks postpartum.
