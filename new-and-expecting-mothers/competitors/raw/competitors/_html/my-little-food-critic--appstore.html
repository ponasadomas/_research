The BEST SELLING My Little Food Critic App is your “go to” for feeding babies, toddlers, kids and the whole family! NEW RECIPES EVERY WEEK Fresh NEW recipes landing every week! Step by step recipe instructions, images and instructional recipe VIDEOS for selected recipes. Easily change cooking units between metric & imperial units. EASY TO MAKE NUTRITIOUS & DELICIOUS RECIPES Enhanced search functionality, making it easy to search for recipes by dietary requirements (nut free, egg free, dairy free, gluten free, vegan & vegetarian), ingredients or recipe name, so you’ll never run out of inspiring meals for your family! MEAL PLANNER, SHOPPING LIST & FAVOURITES Automatically add recipe ingredients to create a shopping list. Create your customised weekly meal plan and use the favourites button to save your ‘go to’ recipes . FIRST FOODS GUIDE Learn how to safely prepare first foods for introducing solids to your baby (whether you are BLW or puree feeding). LIFESTYLE & PLAY Articles and tips from acclaimed experts as well as unique ‘play & learn’ activities, created from a teaching background to learn through play. Why not follow us @my.little.food.critic with a community of over 470,000 followers offering meal time inspiration rooted in wholesome ingredients and rich flavours. Say goodbye to bland, processed baby food and embrace a diet packed with fresh produce and enticing flavours!     I’m a first time mom and was having a hard time finding meals that me and my baby could both eat. I found this account on ig and decided to buy the app! My baby is nine months old so i was curious if the recipes would be suitable for her. Almost all of the recipes work perfect for me and baby! She lovesss every meal i make her and i like that we are able to enjoy them together. It’s super easy to use and i usually just put the recipes that she likes under the favorites tab so that i can easily go back to them. Most of the meals are super quick to make which is perfect when i have limited time. I also like that a lot of the recipes call for different spices which give some of the traditional recipes a new flavor for the baby to try. She loves the cardamom French toast!!! Highly recommend this app!

    Developer Response

    Hi Rielebell, It is clear in the description of the app that it is a month by month subscription. We have worked hard to price the app very reasonably to make it accessible for everyone. It compares favourably with the cost of a cookbook, considering that the app gets new content weekly yet a cookbook does not. The app subscriptions allow us to produce the free content that you have seen and enjoyed on the instagram page and website.
