# Services that support you — your way

Most families start with an in-home consult — it gives us the fullest picture of how feeding is going. The more we know, the sooner we can help make things better.

## In-Home Consults
Most families start here

What's included

We come to you, and we take our time. 90 minutes in the room where you actually feed your baby.

- Time to talk through whatever is worrying you

- Weighing your baby, including a weighted feed

- Full assessment, including an infant oral exam

- Guidance on breastfeeding, bottle feeding and pumping

- A personalized plan built around your feeding goals

Payments

## How payment works

### Self-pay practice

Wellspring does not bill insurance directly. Payment is due at the time of your visit.

### Superbill provided

After every consult you receive an itemized superbill to submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement.

### HSA and FSA accepted

Along with credit and debit cards. Lactation care is an eligible expense for most HSA and FSA plans.

Read more on payments — why self-pay means faster, more flexible care, and how superbills work.
