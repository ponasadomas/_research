# Calm, practical guidance for the years that go by fastest

mamivio is a small team that writes for mothers. We turn what pediatricians, sleep researchers and experienced parents already know into something you can actually use tonight — short, kind and free of judgment.

No trend chasing, no shaming, no thousand-page manuals. Just the next sensible step for the stage your child is in right now.

## Three things we hold to

### Mothers are not a problem to be fixed

You know your child better than any article does. Our job is to hand you good information and then get out of the way.

### Every child grows on their own clock

We write in ranges, not deadlines, and we say plainly when something is worth mentioning to your doctor.

### Short beats thorough at 3am

Everything we publish is written to be read one-handed, on a phone, while someone small needs you.
