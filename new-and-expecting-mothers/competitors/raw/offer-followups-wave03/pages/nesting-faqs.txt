Frequently Asked Questions
Do you work with first-time parents only?
Not at all. I support both first-time and experienced parents. Even seasoned parents often appreciate updated product guidance, gear guidance to accommodate multiple children, help organizing their home, and hands-on support preparing for a new baby — especially when balancing a busy work and family life.
When is the best time to work with Nesting Notes Co.?
Most clients begin between the second and early third trimester, but support is available at any stage — including postpartum. If you’re feeling overwhelmed or unsure where to start, it’s a good time to reach out. We can figure out the best timeline for you, together.
Can you help even if I already have a registry?
Absolutely. I can review and refine existing registries, suggest additions or swaps, and ensure everything aligns with your needs, space, and parenting goals. I can offer additional home nesting services to ensure you're ready and confident to welcome baby home!
Do you offer in-home support?
Yes. In-home nesting support is a core part of my services. This can include unboxing and setting up products, laundering and organizing baby clothing, preparing the nursery and home, and demonstrating the use of your gear and products.
I'm going to be a grandparent! How can I best support my child with your services?
I'd recommend purchasing the Nest Easy Gift Card(s) from the "Gifting" tab. There will be no better gift for them than a completely tailored nesting experience and peace of mind as they prepare to welcome your grandbaby!
I have a Babylist Registry, can I add your services to ask friends and family to contribute?
The best way to do this will be to request a gift card for my services. This will enable you to choose the best services to suit your nesting dreams!
Install the Babylist browser button (available for Chrome, Safari, or Firefox).
Once the button is installed, browse the "Gifting" page and click the button anytime you want to add a gift card.
A pop-up will let you confirm the product details and quantity.
Click “Add to Babylist”—and done!
(This works on other universal registries too, like Amazon or MyRegistry — just use their “Add a Link” option.)
