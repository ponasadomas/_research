Our In Home Services
-Village Nesting Visit-
Starting at $150
The final weeks before baby arrives can feel overwhelming.
During this visit, I'll help prepare the essential spaces in your home so those first days with a newborn feel calmer and more manageable.
Together we'll organize newborn essentials, prepare feeding and diaper stations , and set up key baby items.
I will also do light kitchen cleaning, reset common areas, wash your sheets, blankets, and anything else you'd like ready for being at home with baby.
This service can also be used for any period in your life where you just need an extra set of helping hands around the home ❤
This service can also be booked during any period of overwhelm not just before baby comes if you need.a helping hand.
Available for families in Northwest Indiana and the Chicagoland area.
Schedule your Visit
-The Village Home Visit-
Starting at $85
Sometimes you don't need a specific service.
You just need someone to show up.
Whether your house feels like it's closing in, the laundry has taken over, the newborn haze is real, or you're just a parent who needs an extra set of hands for a few hours, this is that service.
I'll come to you, assess what needs the most attention, and just help.
No judgment, no agenda, no unrealistic expectations.
Just me showing up and making your home feel a little more manageable so you can breathe again.
This could look like light cleaning and organizing, prepping meals, holding the baby while you shower, tackling a pile that's been staring at you for weeks, or just being a calm presence in your space while you figure out your next breath.
Every visit is different because every family is different. This one is completely tailored to whatever you need most that day.
Available for families in Northwest Indiana and the Chicagoland area.
Schedule your visit
-Post Baby Shower Reset-
Starting at $150
Baby showers are wonderful.... until all the boxes come home.
During this visit, I'll help unpack, sort and organize all your new baby gear so your nursery and essentials are ready before baby arrives.
Together we'll unbox all the new gear you just got,
sterilize and set up feeding supplies, organize diapering stations, wash and organize baby clothes by size,
and get your most important items ready to use.
If you have questions about how to use your new stuff I can show you as well!
This service turns post- shower chaos into a calm functional space for your growing family.
Schedule your Visit
- Baby Gear Assembly -
Price Varies
Skip the confusing instructions and let us handle the setup.
This service focuses on safely assembling baby furniture and equipment such as cribs, bassinets, dressers, changing tables, and other nursery essentials.
We'll make sure everything is properly assembled and ready for everyday use.
Perfect for parents who want peace of mind knowing their baby gear is built correctly and safely.
We offer individual pricing per item, or bundles for multiple items.
