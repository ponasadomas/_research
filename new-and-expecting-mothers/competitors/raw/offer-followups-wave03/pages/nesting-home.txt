Hi, I'm Emilie.
Founder and Principal Nesting Consultant
Nesting season is my favorite season—and I want it to be yours, too.
​
I created Nesting Notes Co. to bring peace, clarity, and joy to the often overwhelming process of preparing for a baby. With a background in developmental psychology and years of hands on experience as a Quality & Safety Improvement Consultant and "Auntie Em" to 5, I bring thoughtful expertise, practical insight, and a love for all things baby to every family I work with.
​
My goal is simple: to help you curate a nest that feels beautiful, intentional, and uniquely yours—so you can spend less time researching and second-guessing, and more time enjoying this special season.
