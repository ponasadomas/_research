### Personalized Sleep Plan

Our personalised sleep plan is designed to help families improve their baby’s sleep in a clear, structured and supportive way.

We analyse your baby’s current sleep habits, routines and environment to create a plan adapted to your child’s age, temperament and family lifestyle.

Our approach is gentle, realistic and adapted to families living in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and across the UAE, where routines may vary due to travel, visits or work schedules.

We focus on sustainable improvements that help babies develop healthy sleep habits while parents feel confident and supported.

### What is included in the personalised sleep plan

#### A detailed analysis of your situation

Before writing a single recommendation, we need to understand you. You’ll receive a comprehensive questionnaire covering your baby’s sleep habits, routines, environment, feeding, and your parenting style. Nothing is assumed.

#### A clear, step-by-step written plan

A detailed document tailored to your baby’s age and temperament — from newborns to age 6. With specific guidance for nights, naps, and transition routines. No vagueness, no guesswork.

#### Compatible with your parenting approach

Breastfeeding? Co-sleeping? Prefer no crying? The plan adapts to you — not the other way around. Our approach is always respectful, gradual, and free from extinction methods.

#### Daily support during implementation

We don’t leave you alone with a document. During the first days of implementation, you’ll have direct WhatsApp support to answer questions, adjust the plan if needed, and keep you on track when the nights get hard.

#### Plan review and adjustment

Babies change. If something isn’t working as expected, we adjust. The plan isn’t rigid — it evolves with you.
