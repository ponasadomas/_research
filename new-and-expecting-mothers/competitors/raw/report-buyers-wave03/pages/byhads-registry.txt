Questions

## Frequently asked

### How far along should I be to book?
+

Ideally, book between 20–28 weeks. That gives us time for the intake call, build window, and any revisions before your baby shower. But I've worked with parents at every stage, including postpartum, so if you're further along, just reach out.

### Which registry platforms do you use?
+

I work with all the major ones, Babylist, Amazon, Target, Pottery Barn Kids, Walmart, Buy Buy Baby, and more. Babylist is my personal favorite because it lets you pull from anywhere, but I'll build on whichever platform works best for you.

### Do you work within a specific budget?
+

Absolutely. We'll talk numbers during the intake call. I curate across price points, and I'm just as comfortable sourcing heirloom-quality pieces as I am finding budget-friendly alternatives that actually perform.

### What if my partner and I disagree on things?
+

Totally normal, and I actually love when both partners join the intake call. Part of my job is helping you find products that honor both styles and preferences. We'll work it out together.

### What if I already started a registry?
+

No problem! I can audit what you have, keep what's working, and replace what's not for a smaller fee than a custom curation.

### Is the fee refundable?
+

The fee is non-refundable, as it secures your spot and covers the time and care that goes into your personalized registry.
