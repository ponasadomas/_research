The Process

How it works

01

Book + Intake Call

Fill out the contact form and I'll personally reach out to schedule a 45-minute call. We'll talk through your pregnancy, goals, budget, priorities, aesthetic, and what matters most to you.

02

I Build Your Registry

Over the next three weeks, I build your fully personalized registry on your platform of choice (Babylist, Amazon, Target, Pottery Barn Kids, etc.).

03

Walkthrough + Revisions

We hop on a second call so I can walk you through every category, explain my choices, and swap anything that doesn't feel right.

04

Share With Confidence

You get a registry you're actually excited to send to friends and family, and you never have to second-guess another stroller review again.
