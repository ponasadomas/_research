Registry Review:
Perfecting Your Baby Registry

Already have your baby registry set up but want to ensure it’s absolutely perfect? Nina’s Registry Review service is here to help you feel confident and prepared.

With her years of expertise and deep knowledge of baby products, Nina will provide a thorough evaluation of your registry, identifying any gaps, recommending must-haves you might have missed, and offering alternatives to better suit your family’s unique lifestyle and needs.

Sometimes, all it takes is a fresh perspective to turn a good registry into a great one!

What's Included:

  • Nina's Complete Registry Form: A personalized questionnaire to better understand your family's needs and preferences.
  • 60-Minute Phone Consultation: A one-on-one discussion with Nina to discuss your current registry and address your concerns so that she can tailor her recommendations.
  • Registry Review: Nina will do a detailed analysis of your registry and will prepare her feedback, notes, and insights.
  • Custom Recommendations: Nina will provide a written summary of her notes with any suggested additions, changes, or alternative products to help you finalize your ultimate baby registry.

Feel confident knowing your registry has been expertly reviewed and curated for your growing family!
