## Baby Registry Review FAQs

#### Do I need to have a completed baby registry for this service?

I recommend that your baby registry be mostly complete before you book a review. I totally understand that selecting baby products can be really overwhelming, and sometimes, you just hit a stopping point and can’t continue. I’m here to help! If you're early in the process and want advice on a specific product or two, you can book a lighter/shorter session here: 1:1 Consultations

#### What site should I use to build my baby registry?

I recommend Babylist because it is the easiest for you as a parent to navigate and you can add items from any online retailer.

#### Who will be doing my review?

Katie, our founder, does all of the reviews personally to ensure the best quality assessment tailored uniquely to your family.

#### Can you save me money?

If saving money is your goal, and especially if you have a cluttered registry, we can often save you more than the cost of this service! Sometimes, if a registry is really bare, we may need to add cost to our recommendations to make sure you have the basics covered. However, we also offer options and advice for buying items secondhand, which can really save you money.

#### Can you build me a baby registry from scratch?

Absolutely! However, that is a different service to this one. This service requires an existing baby registry. If you’d like a “Done-For-You” baby registry you can find more information about that and all of our premium maternity concierge services here.
