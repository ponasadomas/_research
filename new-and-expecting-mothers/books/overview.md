## What was not collected

Goodreads explicitly disallows GPTBot in its robots policy; no original review pages were harvested. Amazon US review archives remain uncollected after the follow-up policy probe found explicit GPTBot and ChatGPT-User bans; see `sources/reviews-followups.md`. Apple Books public product pages expose a few selected reviews, with no permitted review-history pagination discovered. Publisher endorsements and syndicated editorial reviews were kept out of the buyer quote bank. No full books were read.

Additional titles surfaced in recommendation carousels (for example Baby Food Bible, How to Wean Your Baby, The Family Firm, The Big Book of Organic Baby Food and Nine Golden Months) were not torn down. Their listing is a continuation queue, not evidence of popularity or an exhaustive market boundary.

Continue: use permitted original retailer review lists and publisher category catalogs; prioritize low-rating reasons and book-to-report switching. Read actual licensed books if evaluating their methods. Do not use a retailer synopsis to validate a clinical mechanism.
