Is Your Home Ready for What's Coming?

Whether you're expecting, have a crawler on your hands, or a toddler who gets into absolutely everything. I'll help you see your home through fresh eyes and create a safety plan that actually makes sense for your family.

Book Single Room AssessmentBook Whole Home Assessment

Why Virtual?

You don't need someone in your home to get expert safety guidance. 

With a few photos and a conversation, I can identify hazards, create a personalized safety plan, and recommend exactly what you need , all without disrupting your schedule or your space. 

And because it's virtual, I can help families anywhere.

Whats Included

✓ Photo Review of Your Home Spaces 

You send me photos of your main living areas and I do the rest. I'll look at everything through the eyes of a crawler, a new walker, and a curious toddler, because they see things we don't.

✓ Personalized Written Safety Plan 

You'll receive a clear, easy to follow safety plan specific to your home, not a generic checklist you could find on Google. Every recommendation is based on your actual space, your child's age, and your family's lifestyle.

✓ Product Recommendations 

No more falling down the Amazon rabbit hole at midnight. I'll tell you exactly what to get, where to get it, and why, so you're only buying what you actually need.

✓ Follow Up Support 

Have questions after you receive your plan? Want to talk through a specific hazard or product? I'm here. This doesn't end when I send your safety plan.

Who this is for

This is for you if...

You're expecting and you're already lying awake thinking about outlet covers and stair gates and whether your furniture is anchored to the wall.

You have a crawler or new walker and your home suddenly looks like an obstacle course designed specifically to give you a heart attack.

You have a toddler. I don't need to say more. You know.

You just want someone to look at your space with fresh eyes and tell you honestly what needs to change, without judgment, without overwhelm, and without a sales pitch.

How it works

Book Your Assessment

Pick your package and fill out a quick intake form. Tell me about your home, your kid, and what's keeping you up at night.

Send Your Photos

I'll send you a simple guide on exactly what to photograph. Your phone camera is perfect. No special setup needed.

I Get to Work

I go through everything carefully and build your personalized safety plan from scratch. No copy paste, no generic answers.

I've Got You After

Your plan arrives and I'm still here. Questions, concerns, "wait is this thing actually dangerous" texts, I'm not going anywhere until you feel good.

A Note Before We Begin

I'll always be straight with you, it's just how I'm built. 

My virtual baby proofing service is based on the photos and information you share with me. I will be thorough, thoughtful, and genuinely invested in your family's safety. But I am not a licensed safety inspector or contractor, and no virtual assessment can catch every single hazard in every home.

My recommendations are here to guide and support you, not to replace professional installation or medical advice. You know your home and your child better than anyone. I'm just here to give you the tools to make confident decisions.

By booking this service you acknowledge that The Village Parent Community provides guidance and educational recommendations only, and that putting those recommendations into action is your responsibility as the parent or guardian.

I'm currently working toward my certification from the International Association for Child Safety (IAFCS) — because showing up with both heart and credentials matters to me.
