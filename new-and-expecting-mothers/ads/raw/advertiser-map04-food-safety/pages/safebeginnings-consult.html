For those times you just want an expert opinion.

Personalized safety consultations and recommendations for car seat safety, babyproofing and injury prevention from a leading safety expert.

Is your car seat giving you grief? Stuck on which car seat will work best for your family and child's needs?
 Have a tricky babyproofing situation that you'd like advice or product recommendations for?
 Not sure how to best set up your child's nursery, bedroom or play space to keep them safe?
 Holly can help.

Holly Choi is an internationally-recognized baby and toddler safety expert.

Her extensive experience and knowledge across multiple areas of baby and toddler safety (first aid, car seat safety, childproofing and injury prevention), in addition to being a mom of two, gives her a well-rounded and unique, yet relatable perspective.

Holly is known for her deep understanding of baby/juvenile consumer product safety in both the US and Canada; and is often called on by US and Canadian media for expert commentary. Holly has been a featured safety expert for Good Morning America, Forbes, Global News (Canada), and CTV (Canada), among others.

After seeing a lack of credible baby and toddler safety information on social media, Holly began posting accessible educational content for parents and caregivers. Her social media content now reaches parents and caregivers all across the US, Canada, and beyond.

what's included

$145 USD

video call - 30 minutes

  • At time of booking, you will be asked to fill out a brief intake form regarding your most pressing baby/toddler safety concerns and/or gear needs that you would like to address during your consultation.
  • Holly's scope of practice includes questions related to car seat safety, babyproofing, childhood injury prevention (preventing falls, burns, drowning, bleeding, choking etc.)
  • Consultations are currently only open to residents of the US and Canada, as Holly's product knowledge and certifications are valid in the US and Canada.

recommendations / summary report

  • Using the information gathered during the safety consultation, Holly will email you a summary report and any product recommendations within a week of the consultation.
  • Holly may ask for additional photos of a concern (such as tricky area that requires a baby gate). If required, she will let you know during the consultation.
