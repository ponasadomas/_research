    Questions before you buy

    Frequently Asked Questions

    A few details about what is included, how online infant CPR education works, when to start, and how families use Infant Safety & CPR for choking response, safe sleep, car seats, babyproofing, and emergency readiness.

    What is included in Infant Safety & CPR?

    Infant Safety & CPR gives you practical online parent education for baby safety, including infant CPR, choking response, breathing emergencies, safe sleep, crib safety, car seat basics, baby gear safety, feeding safety, medication safety, water safety, fire safety, babyproofing, first aid kits, and emergency planning.

    The goal is not to scare you. It is to help you understand what to do, what to watch for, and how to feel more prepared if something happens.

    Will I get certified in infant CPR after taking this course?

    No. This course teaches life-saving skills and safety education, but it is not an official CPR certification course.

    For CPR certification, you need hands-on skills testing with a certified instructor. This course is designed to help parents and caregivers learn the concepts, see clear demonstrations, practice at home, and feel more prepared in an emergency.

    Can I really learn infant CPR online?

    Yes, online learning can help you understand the steps, see demonstrations, learn proper positioning, and practice at your own pace.

    Many parents find it less intimidating to learn first at home, pause, rewind, and revisit the videos. If you need formal certification or hands-on feedback, we recommend pairing this course with an in-person CPR certification class.

    What age range does this safety course cover?

    This course focuses on infants from 0–12 months, with safety guidance designed for babies during the first year.

    It covers common first-year safety concerns like safe sleep, choking prevention and response, feeding safety, car seats, baby gear, home safety, water safety, and emergency readiness.

    When should I take this course — during pregnancy or after baby arrives?

    Either works. Many parents like taking it during pregnancy so they can learn and practice before baby arrives.

    If your baby is already here, you can jump straight to the safety topics that feel most urgent, like choking response, safe sleep, car seats, feeding safety, or babyproofing.

    Can my partner, babysitter, or grandparents watch too?

    Yes. One purchase can be used by your household, and many families watch key lessons with partners, grandparents, babysitters, or other trusted caregivers.

    This can be especially helpful when multiple people care for your baby and you want everyone on the same page about choking response, safe sleep, car seats, feeding safety, and emergency plans.

    Does this cover choking and starting solids?

    Yes. The course includes choking response and feeding safety, which can be especially helpful before or during the starting-solids stage.

    You will learn what to watch for, how to respond if your baby is choking, and how to think through feeding safety with more confidence and less panic.

    Does this cover safe sleep?

    Yes. Infant Safety & CPR includes safe sleep guidance, crib safety, sleep space safety, and common questions parents have about reducing sleep-related risks.

    The goal is to help you understand the recommendations and make safer sleep decisions without turning every nap into a panic spiral.

    Does this replace my pediatrician or emergency care?

    No. This course is online parent education. It does not replace medical care, emergency care, an individualized safety evaluation, or an in-person CPR certification course.

    For urgent health or safety concerns, call 911 or seek emergency care. For medical questions about your baby, contact your pediatrician or healthcare provider.

    How long do I have access?

    You get 12 months of unlimited access to Infant Safety & CPR.

    That gives you time to learn before baby arrives, revisit lessons as your baby grows, and review key skills with your partner or caregivers when safety questions come up.

    Do I have to watch everything in order?

    No. You can watch the course in order, or you can use it like a baby safety library and jump to the topic you need most.

    Many families start with infant CPR, choking response, and safe sleep, then return later for car seats, babyproofing, water safety, medication safety, and emergency planning.

    Is Infant Safety & CPR HSA/FSA eligible?

    Yes. You will receive a receipt that you can submit to your HSA or FSA plan administrator.

    Plan rules can vary, so we recommend checking with your individual plan if you have questions about reimbursement.

    Do you offer a money-back guarantee?

    Yes. We offer a 15-day money-back guarantee.

    If the course is not the right fit, reach out to our team within 15 days of purchase. We want you to feel comfortable getting started.

    Do you offer financial assistance?

    Yes. We offer reduced-cost options for families who need financial assistance, and your healthcare provider may also be able to help cover the cost.
