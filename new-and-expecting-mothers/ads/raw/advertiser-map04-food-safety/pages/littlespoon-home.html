Little Spoon delivers across the continental U.S. While we’d love to reach Alaska and Hawaii, we’re currently limited to the mainland.

If you live on a military base, some locations don’t allow outside delivery drivers on site. If that applies to your address, your delivery experience may look a little different—but our Care Team is here to help find a solution for you and your family.

We’re also unable to deliver to PO Boxes. If you need help updating your address or figuring out the best option, just reach out to our Care Team and we’ll find a solution together.

We take packing seriously so your meals arrive fresh tasting and ready to go. Every order is insulated and shipped with coolant to keep things properly chilled throughout transit.

Our packaging is also designed with sustainability in mind. Most materials are curbside recyclable and we use upcycled insulation and reusable gel packs wherever possible to help reduce waste.

When your box arrives, just transfer everything to the fridge or freezer. This keeps your meals at peak freshness until you’re ready for them. Your kiddo gets the best—and we’re here to make that part easy.

If you place your order by Saturday at 5 PM ET, your first delivery will arrive the following week.

After that, your subscription ships every two weeks on a consistent schedule. Need an order in between? You can adjust your delivery cadence directly in your account.

You can also update your delivery date, menu, or address anytime—just make sure any changes are submitted before Saturday at 5 PM ET for the following week’s delivery.

We know plans shift so we’re here to keep things flexible and easy.

Formula
Store unopened cans in a cool, dry place. Once opened, keep the can tightly covered, use a clean and dry scoop, and use within 1 month for best quality. Prepared formula should be refrigerated immediately and used within 24 hours.

Babyblends, Smoothies + YoGos
Store in the fridge for up to 14 days or freeze for up to 2 months. Our packaging keeps everything fresh without unnecessary additives.

Biteables + Plates
Refrigerate for up to 7 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.

Lunchers
Keep refrigerated for up to 14 days. These are designed to be enjoyed fresh, so freezing isn’t recommended.

Snacks, Puffs + Baby Cereal
These are all pantry friendly + shelf-stable. Store in a cool, dry pantry or cupboard and reseal after each use.

Superstars Multivitamins
Store in a cool, dry place, out of direct sunlight. No need to refrigerate.

Opened Meals
Once opened, refrigerate and serve within 48 hours for the best taste and safety.
