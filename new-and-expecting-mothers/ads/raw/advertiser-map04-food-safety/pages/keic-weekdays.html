About Real Easy Weekdays

  • Complete meal system for easy weekday breakfasts, lunches, dinners, and snacks – including our community’s favorite, Banana Lentil Muffins!
  • Designed for busy families and their children
  • Vegetarian options for all meals and snacks
  • Mostly homemade food with some convenience foods as well
  • Includes a comprehensive Allergy-Friendly Companion Guide is free from the Top 9 U.S. Allergens, Top 14 European Allergens, and Top 11 Canadian Allergens, and is gluten-free.

Dinner time is getting closer and you feel that dread starting to build. You’re exhausted from meal planning and having to come up with three meals and all the snacks for your family. Your brain hurts. What are you going to make for lunch tomorrow? And the next day? How do you create a system that will help you get meals ready quickly without your family becoming bored?

I’ve been there. This is the system that I created to help relieve the meal planning burden for myself. 

Real Easy Weekdays is a system of meals that is flexible. You can use seasonal fruits and veggies, you can use convenience foods, or you always have the option of taking extra time to your own meatballs. This has the plan and the recipes for both. This system teaches you how to save time. The best part? You don’t have to think about meals as much, which gives you some of your mental energy back! You’ll get 60 meal and snack recipes, strategies for introducing new meals to your family, and step-by-step instructions for making the meal system your own! Plus time savers and ideas for making meals more affordable. Families all over the world are using it.

-Jennifer Anderson, MSPH, RDN
