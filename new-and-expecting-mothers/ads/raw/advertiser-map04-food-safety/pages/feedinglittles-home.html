At Feeding Littles
we believe...

That you know your child better than anyone.

We’re here to help you make the best decisions for your family.

Picky eating is normal.

Most kids go through a picky phase, sometimes lasting years. How you respond is what matters most.

Feeding babies should be fun.

We love Baby-led Weaning, but we know there's no one-size-fits-all approach to feeding. What matters most is that you feel confident and your baby is learning to love food on their terms.

How your child eats is not a measure of your parenting.

Feeding kids is harder than most parents expect.
