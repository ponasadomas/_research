Your questions, answered.

How does Little Spoon ensure food safety for babies and toddlers?

Food safety is our #1 priority. Our process includes:

✔️ Sourcing clean ingredients from trusted suppliers.

✔️ Routine testing for heavy metals, pesticides, and plasticizers.

✔️ Strict quality control before shipping any product.

✔️ Exceeding U.S. regulatory standards by following or surpassing the stricter EU benchmarks.

We never compromise when it comes to your little one’s health.

How does Little Spoon ensure the quality of its ingredients?

We source our certified organic ingredients for both Babyblends and Babyblends+ from trusted farms and suppliers who adhere to sustainable and safe farming practices. Every ingredient is fully traceable to its source, ensuring transparency and accountability at every step. We rigorously test to ensure our blends meet our strict safety standards, so you can feel confident that what you're feeding your little one is both safe and of the highest quality.

You can check out many of our farm partners on Our Standards page.

How do you test your food?

At Little Spoon, safety and transparency come first. That’s why we align our testing standards with California’s Proposition 65 and strict European Union regulations — some of the toughest in the world.

We rigorously test our Babyblends, Biteables, Puffs, Cereal, Smoothies, and YoGos for heavy metals, pesticides, and BPA/BPS at certified third-party labs. No product is sold unless it meets our strict internal limits, which surpass U.S. standards.

While the Baby Food Safety Act aims to establish federal limits for heavy metals, those regulations wouldn’t be finalized until 2027 — and we don’t think parents should have to wait. That’s why we’ve already lowered our lead threshold to ≤10ppb, aligning with new FDA guidance and surpassing U.S. regulations today. Rather than waiting for change, we’re leading the way in food safety, ensuring your little one’s meals meet best-in-class standards now.

How do I know my baby is ready to start solids?

Typically, your baby is ready to start solids between 4-6 months. We always recommend talking to your pediatrician to discuss your baby’s needs when it comes to starting solids. There are a few telltale signs that your baby may be ready:
Sitting up with little to no support

Strong head control

Has outgrown their tongue thrust reflex

Is starting to show interest in food and what those around are eating

What makes Little Spoon different from the baby food sold in grocery stores?

Most grocery store baby food? Heavily processed, heat-pasteurized, and packed with preservatives to last for years. A congressional report even raised concerns about heavy metals and lack of traceability in popular brands — not exactly reassuring for parents.

At Little Spoon, we do things differently.

Industry-leading safety standards — We test all our baby and toddler products, including Blends, Biteables, Puffs, Cereal, Smoothies, and YoGos, for heavy metals, pesticides, and BPA/BPS — exceeding U.S. regulations.

Stricter limits — Our standards align with EU benchmarks, with heavy metal limits set at:

Lead: ≤10 ppb

Arsenic: ≤20 ppb (≤40 ppb for cereal)

Cadmium: ≤40 ppb

Mercury: ≤3 ppb

✔️ USDA Certified Organic, non-GMO meals — We skip the rice (a common source of arsenic), fortifiers, and artificial additives, prioritizing nutrient diversity with high-quality ingredients sourced from trusted farmers.

✔️ Cold-pressure processing (HPP) — An FDA-approved process that keeps our food fresh without heat pasteurization, locking in nutrients, taste, and texture — for fridge-fresh meals that last 14 days (or up to 2 months in the freezer).

✔️ Designed to grow with your little one — From first bites to adventurous textures, our meals support every stage of eating — whether you’re spoon-feeding or diving into baby-led weaning.

Industry-leading safety. Real food. No shortcuts.

What’s the difference between Babyblends and Babyblends+?

Think of Babyblends as your go-to mealtime MVP, made for spoon-feeding or pouch feeding and packed with real, organic goodness.

Babyblends+ are our functional recipes, designed for real life and real development. They’re loaded with ingredients that support brain, belly, and immune health and come in a mess-free pouch made for those out-the-door, snack-on-the-go, hands-full moments.

Same real food, just a new way to feed with benefits built in. Mix, match and build your dream box with both Babyblends (in cups + pouches) and Babyblends+ (pouches).

However you feed your mini, we’ve got you.

What’s Little Spoon’s take on pouches vs. spoon-feeding?

As you can imagine, we’re big fans of the spoon. Experts agree it helps with motor skills, builds routines, and creates sweet bonding moments. That’s why our original baby meals come in cups meant to be spoon-fed.

But let’s be real, life with a baby isn’t always a sit-down meal. That’s where our baby pouches comes in. Babyblends and Babyblends+ pouches have the same real, organic ingredients, just packed in a pouch for in-the-car-eats, stroller snack attacks, and everything in between. And yes, you can totally use a spoon with our pouches too.

Spoon or pouch, you and your baby deserve organic purees you can trust. Because feeding your baby should feel good AND fit your life.

What makes Babyblends+ functional?

Each blend is tailored to support a specific area of development: Brain Support (DHA from algal oil and choline from whole foods), Immune Support (Vitamin C from acerola cherry and zinc from real food sources), and Probiotic Support (Clinically studied BC30™ probiotics).

Every Babyblends+ pouch is USDA organic, non-GMO, and made with real ingredients like fruits, veggies, legumes, and seeds. No weird stuff, just the good stuff.

How do I store my Babyblends?

Little Spoon Babyblends and Babyblends+ can be stored in the fridge for up to 14 days, or in your freezer for up to 2 months.

Our purees can be eaten at a range of temperatures, whatever works best for your baby! To warm up your blend, we recommend putting the container in a shallow bowl of warm water and stirring until heated. If you wish to microwave, simply pour the puree into a microwave safe dish and stir to ensure it's been evenly warmed.

Can I choose my own purees?

You bet! After you sign-up you have full control of your little one’s menu. If you have questions about what blends are best for your baby, you can reach out to us at [email protected] or use the chat feature to talk with one of our Child Nutrition certified Care Team members.

Can I use Babyblends for baby-led weaning?

Yes! We’re big fans of baby-led weaning (BLW) and of giving parents options that work for real life. Whether you’re going full BLW or doing a mix of self-feeding and spoon-feeding, Little Spoon has you covered.

Babyblends are a great companion to BLW, offering smooth, organic purees you can spoon-feed or mix into meals. Babyblends+ take it a step further. These functional pouches are packed with nutrients for brain, belly, and immune support and can be squeezed onto a spoon, stirred into foods like yogurt or oatmeal, or self-fed right from the pouch.

And when your mini’s ready for the next step, Biteables are the perfect soft, pinchable meals for practicing independent eating.

As always, chat with your pediatrician to decide what feeding approach works best for your family.

What if my baby doesn’t like the food?

We know how frustrating it can be to see your baby refusing food. But be patient before you give up on the food - the average baby needs to taste a food up to 15 times before recognizing it and developing a taste for it.

It’s all part of palate training and perfectly normal. Pro tip? Try mixing a new flavor in with a familiar one to help ease your baby into trying new things! You can read more tips here.

Is Little Spoon kosher?

All of our Babyblends are kosher Pareve (no dairy or meat) and OU Kosher certified, the world’s largest and most recognized kosher certification agency.
