Babyproofing Your Home: Protecting Baby from Hazards & Hidden Dangers

Babyproofing your home helps keep your little one safe as they grow and explore. Learn which areas in your home need attention, how to secure them, and simple lifestyle changes that will help keep your baby safe. Avoid the most common babyproofing mistakes and give yourself the peace of mind you deserve.
Covered in this class
  • The 10 most common hazards in every home
  • Essential babyproofing gear and how to use it
  • Lifestyle changes to make at home to ensure your baby’s safety
  • The most common babyproofing mistakes, and how to avoid them
