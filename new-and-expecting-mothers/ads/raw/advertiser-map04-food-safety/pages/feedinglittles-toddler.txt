Common questions

 My child is 7. Is it too late to change their eating habits?

Not at all. This course was designed to help parents of children all the way through elementary school. The strategies shift with age, and we cover that specifically. Many parents of older kids see significant progress when they change the dynamics around mealtime, even after years of struggling.

 How is this different from what I can find for free online?

Free content gives you fragments. Our Toddler and Kid Course gives you a complete, sequenced game plan built by a dietitian and multiple feeding therapists, tested with thousands of real families. Our Feeding Therapy Strategies eBook contains dozens of techniques to try that you won’t find anywhere online. Most parents tell us they wish they'd done this instead of spending months searching with no concrete results.

 What if my child has sensory issues?

We include a bonus module specifically on sensory and oral-motor challenges, including how to identify if your child needs more help. While this course doesn't replace working with a specialist, many families with sensory-sensitive kids have found it enormously helpful for understanding what's happening and what to try at home.

 How do I access the course? Do I need to watch it all at once?

You get immediate access after purchase, from any device. The course is broken into short lessons - each one starts with a summary so you can choose what to watch. You can also listen podcast-style or read the full script. Watch it in 20-minute chunks during nap time. You'll have lifetime access so you can revisit as your child grows. Login here.

 Will this course help me?

Many of our clients report success when taking the course and implementing its strategies. Sometimes making a few small changes makes all the difference. In other cases, clients have to employ many of the techniques used in the course over time to see improvement. If your child does not improve with the techniques in this course or is exhibiting any of the red flags mentioned in it, we recommend seeking a feeding evaluation with an OT or SLP who specializes in feeding. This course does not place individualized therapy when it’s warranted.

 What if it doesn't work for us?

We offer a full 30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked. We are that confident it will work for your family.

 How long is the Toddler & Kid Course?

At 2.5 hours, the course is fully self-paced: work through it in bite-sized chunks or jump straight to the section you need.

 Can I go back and watch this course as my child gets older?

Yes, our courses are yours once you buy them, as long as we are in the business of hosting them! Feel free to watch it again as your child grows and changes.

 Does this course include meal ideas and recipes?

Yes, our Toddler & Kid course has 96 meal ideas and 36 snack ideas. We have included some recipes in the course, but if you want even more please check out our cookbook Feeding Littles and Beyond and our free meal planning app, Feeding Littles App!

 I am a feeding professional. Do you offer CEUs for your courses?

At this time, our courses are not approved for CEUs for many health professionals. However, many childcare professionals or allied health professionals have been able to use our courses toward CEU hours by submitting a request to their certifying agency. If you need a certificate of completion for taking our courses, please email hello@feedinglittles.com
