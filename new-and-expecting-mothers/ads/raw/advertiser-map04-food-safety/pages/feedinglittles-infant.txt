Common questions

 What age is this course for?

The course is designed for babies 5-12 months. We recommend taking it around 5 months so you're ready before solids begin, but it's helpful at any point. If your baby is already over 10 months and self-feeding, the Toddler & Kid Course is a better fit.

 We already started with purees - is it too late?

Not at all. The course has a full section on transitioning from purees to finger foods, including a Texture Graduation Protocol for babies who are resistant to the change. It's never too late!

 Is this only baby-led weaning, or does it cover spoon-feeding too?

The course focuses on self-feeding and BLW, but it also covers how to offer purees in a way your baby can feed themselves. It also goes over how to transition from spoon-feeding when you're ready. We meet you where you are.

 My baby was premature. Will this course work for us?

Many preemie families have taken the course successfully. We recommend discussing readiness cues with your pediatrician first, as premature babies may be ready at a different adjusted age. The course content itself is still highly applicable.

 How long do I have access?

Forever - well, as long as we’re in the business of selling courses! Our courses don’t expire, so you can revisit it whenever you need a refresher, hit a new challenge or have another baby!

 What if I don't have time to watch videos?

Every lesson can be watched as a video, read as an article or listened to podcast-style. Most parents finish the core content in 2 hours, taken at their own pace.

 How long is the Infant Course?

At 2 hours, the course is fully self-paced: work through it in bite-sized chunks or jump straight to the section you need.

 I am a feeding professional. Do you offer CEUs for your courses?

At this time, our courses are not approved for CEUs for many health professionals. However, many childcare professionals or allied health professionals have been able to use our courses toward CEU hours by submitting a request to their certifying agency. If you need a certificate of completion for taking our courses, please email hello@feedinglittles.com
