How does Little Spoon work?

Life with kids is unpredictable, so we make your subscription flexible. Once your first box is on its way, you’re in full control.

You can skip an order, pause your subscription, cancel, or adjust your delivery schedule anytime from your account. You can also update your menu, add products, or change your shipping address whenever you need—just be sure to make changes before your weekly cutoff.

We’re here to keep things easy and stress-free so you can focus on what matters most.

What makes Little Spoon different?

At Little Spoon, quality isn’t just about taste — it’s about trust. Our USDA-certified organic Formula, Babyblends, Biteables and pouches are rigorously tested for toxins, heavy metals, pesticides, and plasticizers. Our full product portfolio bans over 100 commonly used ingredients and never includes artificial flavors, sugars and dyes.

When it comes to your baby, we go beyond U.S. regulations and meet or exceed some of the strictest E.U. standards in the world.

We’re also the first U.S. baby food brand to set strict limits on 500+ potential toxins, including industry-leading benchmarks for lead, arsenic and cadmium. No product leaves our hands unless it meets our safety standards—period.

All of our recipes are packed with organic fruits, hidden veggies and superfoods—and all are made with high-quality ingredients sourced from trusted partners across the US. From the very first bottle to big-kid meals, we’re committed to making every stage safer, better-for-your-kiddo.

We’ll never stop raising the bar on safety, transparency and quality. Feeding your little one shouldn’t be a guessing game—it should be simple, trustworthy and rooted in the highest standards.

What are Little Spoon’s transparency standards?

At Little Spoon, we’ve always prioritized food safety and transparency — and now, we’re raising the bar even higher. We’ve strengthened our industry-leading testing standards for heavy metals, pesticides, and plasticizers across all baby and toddler products — raising the bar for food safety and transparency.

Here’s what that looks like:

✔️ Stricter limits on heavy metals — aligned with or exceeding EU benchmarks.

✔️ Expanded safety testing for babies and toddlers — Formula, Blends, Biteables, Puffs, Cereal, Smoothies and YoGos.

✔️ A commitment to full transparency — no product hits your doorstep unless it meets our strict safety standards.

Parents shouldn’t have to hope their baby’s food is safe. We believe in proving it.

How do you ensure safety and quality?

Food safety and quality are the foundation of everything we make. Little Spoon products—including Formula, Babyblends, Biteables, Puffs, Cereal, Smoothies and YoGos—are rigorously tested for heavy metals, pesticides, and BPA/BPS. Our standards go beyond U.S. regulations and align with best-in-class E.U. limits.

We also use cold-pressure technology and proprietary packaging to help protect nutrients and keep food fresh without artificial additives.

Little Spoon partners with the Clean Label Project to set strict testing limits across our entire baby and toddler portfolio, reinforcing our commitment to raising the bar on safety and transparency.

When you choose Little Spoon, you’re choosing products developed with expert oversight, modern food-safety practices and a deep commitment to your child’s well-being.

Where does Little Spoon deliver?

Little Spoon delivers across the continental U.S. While we’d love to reach Alaska and Hawaii, we’re currently limited to the mainland.

If you live on a military base, some locations don’t allow outside delivery drivers on site. If that applies to your address, your delivery experience may look a little different—but our Care Team is here to help find a solution for you and your family.

We’re also unable to deliver to PO Boxes. If you need help updating your address or figuring out the best option, just reach out to our Care Team and we’ll find a solution together.

How are Little Spoon orders packed?

We take packing seriously so your meals arrive fresh tasting and ready to go. Every order is insulated and shipped with coolant to keep things properly chilled throughout transit.

Our packaging is also designed with sustainability in mind. Most materials are curbside recyclable and we use upcycled insulation and reusable gel packs wherever possible to help reduce waste.

When your box arrives, just transfer everything to the fridge or freezer. This keeps your meals at peak freshness until you’re ready for them. Your kiddo gets the best—and we’re here to make that part easy.

When will my Little Spoon order arrive?

If you place your order by Saturday at 5 PM ET, your first delivery will arrive the following week.

After that, your subscription ships every two weeks on a consistent schedule. Need an order in between? You can adjust your delivery cadence directly in your account.

You can also update your delivery date, menu, or address anytime—just make sure any changes are submitted before Saturday at 5 PM ET for the following week’s delivery.

We know plans shift so we’re here to keep things flexible and easy.

How should I store Little Spoon products?

Formula
Store unopened cans in a cool, dry place. Once opened, keep the can tightly covered, use a clean and dry scoop, and use within 1 month for best quality. Prepared formula should be refrigerated immediately and used within 24 hours.

Babyblends, Smoothies + YoGos
Store in the fridge for up to 14 days or freeze for up to 2 months. Our packaging keeps everything fresh without unnecessary additives.

Biteables + Plates
Refrigerate for up to 7 days, or freeze for up to 2 months.

Lunchers
Keep refrigerated for up to 14 days. These are designed to be enjoyed fresh, so freezing isn’t recommended.

Snacks, Puffs + Baby Cereal
These are all pantry friendly + shelf-stable. Store in a cool, dry pantry or cupboard and reseal after each use.

Superstars Multivitamins
Store in a cool, dry place, out of direct sunlight. No need to refrigerate.

Opened Meals
Once opened, refrigerate and serve within 48 hours for the best taste and safety.

If you have questions about storage or what’s best for your little one’s age or stage, our Care Team is always here to help.

Ingredient and allergen information varies by product. Please review each product’s ingredient list and allergen details to determine what works best for your family.

Some Little Spoon products are made in facilities that also handle common allergens. Our manufacturing partners follow strict allergen control procedures designed to prevent unintended cross-contact.

Can I choose the products and quantity myself?

You can always view our menus before checkout to see exactly what’s available. After checkout, you’ll be able to select the products you want or go with our pre-selected menu.

Before every order, we’ll send you an email reminder. From there, you can log in to your account to choose the exact meals and snacks you want for that delivery. If you prefer consistency, you can also set a menu that repeats for all future orders.

Short on time? No problem. If you don’t make updates before your cutoff window, we’ll fill your box with a balanced mix of our most-loved options.

Whether you want familiar favorites or something new, you’re in control of what arrives at your door. And if you ever need help or menu guidance, our Care Team is always here to support you.

How do you ensure the safety and quality of Little Spoon formula?

Not only is our Little Spoon formula US organic certified, certified to the EU Organic Equivalency Arrangement, Clean Label Project verified + Certified Pesticide-Free, but every batch is tested again and again—500+ checks for toxins, including heavy metals, plus screenings for pesticides and harmful bacteria. Our test results are publicly available for any parent to view. When it comes to our safety standards, if anything raises an eyebrow, it doesn’t make the can.

Our manufacturing partner has over 25 years of expertise. Each batch undergoes 2,000 safety checks before making its way to our third party lab for testing.

We also keep a strict No-No List: 100+ ingredients you’ll never find in any of our recipes, and you’ll never find corn syrup, palm oil, maltodextrin or other fillers in our formula. Just complete nutrition with everything your baby needs (and nothing they don’t) to grow strong through their first year of life.

What makes Little Spoon Formula different from other infant formulas?

Our formula recipe starts with organic, grass-fed whole milk from New Zealand cows raised with space to roam year round and graze in pastures free of synthetic pesticides. We chose a whole milk base, so babies get more naturally occurring milk fats and fewer added oils—closer to the natural composition of breast milk. Every batch is tested for 500+ toxins, including heavy metals, and held to US organic standards and certified to the EU Organic Equivalency Arrangement, plus our own strict EU-aligned safety limits. Learn more about our commitment to transparency and our publicly available testing limits.
