FAQ

 How do I download the Feeding Littles App?

Go to the App Store to download our app for free!

 Do I have to pay for the Feeding Littles App?

Feeding Littles app is free to download. Once you create an account, you get access to all 75+ recipes, 50+ quick meal ideas, and the weekly meal planner at no cost. You can also save a limited number of your own imported recipes.

A Feeding Littles Premium subscription unlocks unlimited recipe saves, so you can import recipes from social media, blogs, screenshots, photos, and even handwritten recipe cards (yes, it reads cursive!).

 Is your app available for both iOS and Android devices?

Right now, the Feeding Littles App is only available on iOS for Apple devices.

 Is the Feeding Littles App available worldwide?

Yes, our app is available worldwide.

 Can I share my account with a family member?

Yes, there are two ways families can use the app together:

Sharing a subscription: Feeding Littles purchases and subscriptions support Apple’s Family Sharing, so members of the same Apple Family can share a single subscription.

Sharing your meal plan, grocery list, and recipes: Feeding Littles Premium includes a household feature. You can create a household and invite family members so everyone works from the same weekly meal plan and grocery list, and can see each other's saved recipes. Everyone stays signed in to their own account.

Creating a household requires Premium, but joining is free, so the people you invite don't each need a subscription. A household can include up to 6 people. To set one up, go to Settings > General > Household Management, then tap Create a Household and Invite a Member.

 I’m a monthly app subscriber. How do I switch to Feeding Littles Premium (annual subscription)?

You can manage and change your subscription through your Apple ID:

On your iPhone, open Settings.

Tap your name at the top.

Tap Subscriptions.

Choose the Feeding Littles subscription and switch to the annual plan if it’s available.

For more detailed instructions, you can also visit Apple’s support page about managing subscriptions.

 Can I use the Feeding Littles App on my computer or iPad?

You can download the iOS app on your iPad from the App Store, but the experience is currently optimized for iPhone screens. We do not have a desktop or web version of the app at this time.

 I own one of your Feeding Littles online courses. Is the course material included in the Feeding Littles App?

No, the Feeding Littles App does not include the same material as our online courses and is meant for meal planning and recipe importing and organization.

 Are the recipes in the Feeding Littles App the same as the ones in your book Feeding Littles and Beyond?

No, the recipes in the Feeding Littles App were created in collaboration with Chef Stacey Weber and include many of Megan’s favorite family recipes. Each recipe contains additional tips and includes a photo of how to serve that recipe safely to a baby.

 Why should I use the Feeding Littles App over any other meal planning and recipe-saving app?

The Feeding Littles App was created by experts, for parents. Unlike most recipe-saving apps available, it includes 75+ unique family-friendly recipes that we know kids love. Our app also includes expert tips for picky eating, Baby-led Weaning, adding more nutrition to your child’s diet and more.

 How do I contact support?

Please contact us at app@feedinglittles.com so we can assist you.
