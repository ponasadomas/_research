This is not your average baby food, this is family food.

If you’re tired of cooking two separate dinners every night, that ends here.

The 101 Before One App is built around realistic weeknight family meals. Inside, you’ll find meals handcrafted so your baby can eat the same foods as the rest of the family with a few simple modifications.

Spend less time making separate meals and more time creating memories around the table as a family.

Sweet potato and banana on repeat? Not anymore..

Research shows that the 6–8 month window is the most powerful time to build food acceptance. The 101 Foods Checklist makes sure you don't waste it.

Many parents end up serving the same few “safe” foods over and over again - simply because they don't know what else to serve! 

But the 6 to 12 month window is a powerful opportunity to introduce your baby to a wide variety of foods, flavors, and textures.

Research shows that babies who experience more textures between 6 and 8 months are less likely to develop picky eating later on. This early exposure helps babies build familiarity with different foods and develop their own palate before your baby’s 1st birthday.

That is exactly why we created the 101 Foods Checklist.

Instead of getting stuck rotating the same few foods at every meal, the checklist lives on your fridge so you always know what foods your baby still needs to try!

Created by a working mom, for working parents

When I became a first-time mom (while working full time), our pediatrician told me to  “feed your baby what you eat,” but I needed more guidance to actually make that work.

As I learned more about baby-led weaning, I started modifying our family’s favorite meals so my baby could safely eat the same food we were eating. Those recipes eventually became the start of 101 before one®.

We created the app to make feeding your baby even easier by giving parents instant access to recipes, meal plans, and expert guidance whenever they need it.

Today, more than 100,000 families use 101 before one® to confidently introduce solid foods and enjoy family meals together.

The 101 before one® app is built by a team of pediatric specialists—a pediatrician, pediatric allergist, pediatric feeding therapist, registered dietitian, lactation consultant, and infant feeding specialist. Between us, we've raised 12 children and helped 100,000+ families

 What’s the difference between 101 before one and all the other baby-led weaning programs out there?

1️⃣ 101 Before One was not created by just one expert in their field, we are a team of pediatric experts across a variety of fields.

This means you have a program made by a pediatric allergist, feeding specialist, registered dietician, feeding therapist, and chef!

So you get the best baby led weaning practices and meals from a well-rounded perspective.

2️⃣ These are not recipes that only your baby can eat.

These are family-style recipes with modifications and serving instructions for feeding your baby what you’re eating.

You are feeding your family and your baby, together. Your baby will eat family meals with you from the start and eat what you do, not the other way around.

3️⃣ You get access to weekly meal plans and shopping lists. Our families love our meal plans so much they continue using them after their children are toddlers and older.

We give you and your baby nutritious meals during their first year and for the rest of your lives.

 When is the best time to start baby led weaning and this program?

We recommend joining this program when your baby is between 4-6 months old.

All research shows that starting solids at around 6 months is best. You’ll learn how to spot the signs that your baby is ready for solids in the program.

With that being said, if your baby is 6 -10 months, you are not too late and we still recommend you join!

You can start baby led weaning with 101 before one even if you’ve already tried introducing solids following another approach.

You’ll get so much out of this program and set yourself up for feeding your toddler with success when you join when your baby is older. Most of our families use the recipes and meal plans they gain in 101 before one into toddlerhood and well beyond!

 Are there photos of the meals?

Every single meal and recipe has a full-color photo showing you exactly how to serve it to your baby. We know how important visuals are when you're learning to feed your baby safely.

 Is this right for me if my child is older than six months?

Yes! All research shows that starting solids at around 6 months is best. You’ll learn how to spot the signs that your baby is ready for solids in the program.

With that being said, if your baby is 6 -10 months, you are not too late and we still recommend you join!

You can start baby led weaning with 101 before one even if you’ve already tried introducing solids following another approach.

You’ll get so much out of this program and set yourself up for feeding your toddler with success when you join when your baby is older. Most of our families use the recipes and meal plans they gain in 101 before one into toddlerhood and well beyond!

 Is this right for me if we have special diets or food restrictions in our home?

Absolutely. All of our recipes are clearly marked to show if they are gluten free, egg free, soy free, dairy free, peanut free, etc.

We also include substitutes and omissions for dietary needs in our recipes.

Our recipes are searchable. Simply type in what you’re looking for, such as ‘gluten-free dinner recipes’ and you'll have a list of options right away.

We also have a specific collection of vegan and plant-based recipes.

We have families with a wide variety of diets and preferences who have loved 101 before one because this program makes it easy to feed their entire family and stay within their dietary guidelines.
