Live On-line Classes

Infant CPR

($60 per couple)

This essential live online class is designed for expectant parents, new parents, birth partners, and caregivers to build confidence in responding to emergencies during infancy. You will learn life-saving skills including infant CPR and choking relief of infants from birth to 1 year. Taught by skilled American Heart Associations certified instructors in a supportive, judgment-free environment, this course combines expert guidance through video, demonstration, and practice (you provide a doll or stuffed toy). Whether you are preparing for your first baby or refreshing your skills, you will leave feeling empowered to protect your growing family.

This 75 minute live online class covers the basics of babyproofing, when to begin and how to avoid being overwhelmed by the number of available products. We will also cover safe sleep, fire safety and touch on emergency preparedness. This class cover safety from newborns though general home safety for all.

Grandparents Guide to Success

($60 per session for two participants)

Whether you are first-time grandparent or just need a refresher, this live online class is designed to help you confidently support your growing family. We will cover current perinatal safety practices, including safe sleep guidelines, infant CPR awareness, feeding support, and how your role can evolve during pregnancy, birth, and postpartum. Learn how things may have changed since you were a new parent and how to be a calming, informed presence as your loved ones welcome a new baby. This class empowers grandparents with up-to-date knowledge and meaningful ways to help.

Infant Safety Seat Basics

($60 per couple)

Keeping your little one safe starts with the right car seat—and knowing how to use it correctly. In this essential live online class, caregivers will learn basic overall car seat safety as well as how use the specific car seat that they have chosen to use. Primary learning points will be familiarization with your car seats components, safety mechanisms and how to correctly secure baby for every ride. We will cover common mistakes, and current safety guidelines, with time for hands-on practice and personalized guidance. Please chose a class date that ensures that you will have your car seat for this class. Whether you are expecting your first or need a refresher, this class will help you feel confident and prepared every time you hit the road

Prenatal Breastfeeding Basics

($60 per couple)

This two hour class for expectant mothers and their partner or family member is taught by our International Board Certified Lactation Consultants (IBCLC). We will discuss the practical information you need to get breastfeeding off to a good start, grow your milk supply, and reach your feeding goals. Some of the many topics covered are; Breastfeeding techniques for comfortable and effective feeds, how to know baby is getting enough milk, nutrition for the breastfeeding parent, bottle feeding breastmilk.
