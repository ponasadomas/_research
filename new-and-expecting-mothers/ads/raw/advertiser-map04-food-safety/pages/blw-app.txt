About BLW Meals app

The BLW Meals app was created in 2020 with the goal of simplifying baby food introduction. BLW Meals app provides information that focuses on helping parents and caregivers understand and apply the Baby-Led Weaning (BLW) approach in an easy and safe way.

Introducing solid foods can be healthy, respectful, and fun.

With the BLW method, your baby gets the chance to feed independently from the start. This establishes a positive relationship with food for life.

We guide you through every phase, from 6 months to 2 years old.

In the BLW Meals app, you’ll find a complete guide with tips, tasty and easy-to-make recipes, nutritionist-approved meal plans, quizzes, and more!
