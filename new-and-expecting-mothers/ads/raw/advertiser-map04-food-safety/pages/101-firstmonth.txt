Your Baby's First Month of Solids Shapes Their Relationship With Food

The foods your baby is exposed to during this window help determine their comfort with new textures, their willingness to try new things, and their eating habits for years to come. Research shows that early and repeated exposure to a variety of foods and allergens supports healthy eating habits and may help reduce picky eating.

But most parents start solids without a clear plan — and end up repeating the same few foods or feeling unsure what to serve next. This guide makes sure you start strong.

Frequently Asked Questions

 What's included in the meal plan?

A 54-page digital PDF with 20 easy starter meals for baby, 20 simple family-style dinners, Top 9 allergen introduction guidance, and 40 total recipes and meal ideas — all with full-color photos showing exactly how to serve each meal.

 Does this follow baby-led feeding?

Yes! We recommend allowing your baby to self-feed from day one — whether you start with purées, finger foods, or a combination of both. This guide supports all three approaches.

 Are there photos of the meals?

Every single meal and recipe has a full-color photo showing you exactly how to serve it to your baby. We know how important visuals are when you're learning to feed your baby safely.

 Is this digital or printed?

This is a 54-page digital PDF with lifetime access. It's sized to 8.5x11 landscape and designed to print easily in black and white or color — so you can keep it right in your kitchen.

 How do I receive the guide?

Yes! We will email you a link to download the guide after you purchase it. If you run into any issues or can’t find the email, please contact info@101beforeone.com
