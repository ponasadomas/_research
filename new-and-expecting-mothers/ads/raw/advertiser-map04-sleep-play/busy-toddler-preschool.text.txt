Author:

Thank you so much for your purchase of Playing Preschool Year 1. I am delighted to have you here and can’t wait to for the upcoming year of learning to unfold for your family.
A Reminder About Books
While the books in Playing Preschool have been carefully selected and vetted for developmental appropriateness and adherence to the unit’s theme,
they are suggestions only.
Only a few times in Playing Preschool does an activity align exactly with a book. The vast majority of book titles can be exchanged for any book that aligns with the topic. For example, any children’s book on apples can be used. I have merely offered a few recommendations.
Thoughts on Collecting Books
Do not feel any pressure to buy every book used in the program. Check out books from the library, look online, borrow from neighbors, ask friends on Facebook – see what your community has to offer.
After finishing a unit, if a book became a treasured favorite, that’s a better reason to buy a book now that the relationship with the text has been established.
