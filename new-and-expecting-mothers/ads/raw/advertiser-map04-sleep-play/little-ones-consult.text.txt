A Consultation with the Sleep Team at Little Ones®
This is as close to having us in your home as it gets.
The experts behind the guidance that has transformed sleep for over 1 million families across 90 countries are now available for limited, private, face-to-face consultations.
If you've followed Little Ones, used the app, or found us at 3am when nothing else was working, this is your chance to sit down with us directly.
What's included:
A 1-hour private online video consultation with one of the Little Ones sleep team (all certified and highly experienced sleep consultants)
A fully personalised sleep plan tailored to your baby or toddler
Your Sleep Prescription - a detailed, documented follow-up of our personalised recommendations, sent directly to you after your session
1 month free membership to Little Ones Sleep App
Who this is for:
Families with babies and toddlers aged 0-3 who want expert, personalised support and the confidence of knowing they're getting it straight from the source.
Our approach is responsive and science-backed, with solutions tailored to your child and your family.
International award-winning guidance, endorsed by Dr Kate Johnson, Ph.D. in Sleep Physiology and Postdoctoral Fellow at Harvard Medical School and Stanford Research International.
Spots are strictly limited - we only open up a handful of consultations each week. Book yours below now!

You can trust us with your little one's sleep
"I am blown away by your comprehensive work. What I particularly love about the Little Ones strategies are that they are a more gentle alternative and can be tailored to your family so that you really see positive change to your baby's sleep."
- Dr Kate Johnson, Harvard Medical School
Let us give you a plan for:
Night Waking
We'll get to the bottom of your little one's night waking and provide a personalised plan to achieve better nights, quickly.
Short Naps
After analysing your little one's sleep information, we'll clearly see what's causing their short naps and give you detailed guidance about how to achieve longer, more restorative daytime sleep.
Early Rising
5am starts are tough on everyone! We will discover what's causing that early wake and provide solutions you can implement right away.
Settling or Self-Settling
If your little one struggles to settle to sleep, or if you're needing to work on self-settling, we have many methods we will talk you through step by step. No cry it out here!
Optimum Sleep Routine
Good sleep is made or broken in the day; the daytime routine is the most important sleep element. We will guide you through the best daily routine for your little one, based on their age, development, sleep science, their circadian rhythm and your family's daily patterns.
Bedtime Battles
Bedtime can go wrong for many reasons. We will dissect your little one's bedtime struggles and give you clear, structured guidance to get bedtime happening in a positive way in your home.
Spaces are limited!
We only open a few Sleep Consult appointments each week. Book now to secure your spot.
Refund & Rescheduling Policy:
Rescheduling
- If you need to reschedule your call, please let us know at least 24 hours prior to the call. Calls can be rescheduled a maximum of three times. After the third reschedule, the refund policy below will apply.
No-Shows
- If you fail to show up to your scheduled call you will no longer be able to reschedule the call. A refund will not be given.
Refund
- Cancellations made 7+ days in advance of your scheduled consult will be fully refunded. Cancellations made within 7 days and more than 48 hours in advance of your scheduled consult will receive a 50% refund. Cancellations made within 48 hours of your scheduled consult will not be eligible for a refund.
Refunds will not be given on calls that have taken place.
