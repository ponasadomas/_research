Get to Know Myriam
Myriam Sandler is the creator of Mothercould, a kids activity and family lifestyle community. She shares unique short video tutorials of sensory and food recipes, easy play activities and educational tools using recycled household items. Myriam is also the author of Playful by Design: Your Stress-Free Guide to Raising Confident, Creative Kids through Independent Play. Born in Venezuela and raised in Miami, FL, the bilingual mother of three offers parents, educators and caregivers simple and accessible play solutions, parenting hacks and more.
