A personalized baby sleep schedule that adapts when naps change, helping you know what comes next throughout your baby’s day.

PERSONALIZED BABY SLEEP, MADE SIMPLE
Little Ones is a baby sleep app that gives you a personalized daily schedule, adapts when naps change and helps you know what comes next.

A nap runs short, another runs long, or your baby wakes earlier than usual. Our adaptive Sleep-o-Rhythm updates your baby’s day using their age, stage and actual sleep, without you having to calculate wake windows or force a schedule that no longer works.

Open Little Ones. Know what’s next.

A BABY SLEEP SCHEDULE THAT ADAPTS
Sleep-o-Rhythm gives you a personalized sleep and feeding plan based on your baby’s age and stage.

Log what actually happens and the plan adjusts as your baby’s day unfolds. If a nap is shorter or longer than expected, you can see what to aim for next and keep the rest of the day on track.

No more trying to calculate wake windows or wondering what one unexpected nap means for the rest of the day.

TRACK SLEEP, NAPS AND FEEDS
Keep your baby’s sleep and feeding information together in one convenient place.

• Log naps, night sleep and feeds
• See your baby’s next suggested sleep time
• Follow a personalized daily rhythm
• Identify patterns as your baby grows
• Adapt the day when sleep does not go to plan
• Understand your baby’s changing sleep needs

HELP WHEN SLEEP GETS TRICKY
Baby sleep changes constantly, but you do not have to navigate it alone.

Find practical, age-appropriate guidance for:
• Short naps
• Frequent night waking
• Early rising
• Nap refusal
• Sleep regressions
• Settling struggles
• Bedtime battles
• Nap transitions
• Newborn sleep
• Toddler sleep

We help you understand what may be happening and what you can do next.

REAL SLEEP EXPERTS, IN YOUR POCKET
Sometimes you need more than an article.

Get personalized support from our certified sleep consultants through private in-app chat. Talk through what is happening with your baby and receive practical guidance tailored to your family’s situation.

A VILLAGE IN YOUR POCKET
Parenting was never meant to happen alone.

Inside our Parenting Village, you can connect with other parents, ask questions, share experiences and find support from people navigating the same wonderfully unpredictable stages.

BUILT AROUND YOUR BABY
Little Ones combines sleep biology, child development and your baby’s actual day to provide guidance that evolves as they grow.

You will also find a range of responsive settling and self-settling approaches, allowing you to choose what feels right for your baby and your family. Our focus is not a perfect schedule -it is helping you better understand your baby and feel confident about what comes next.

INDEPENDENTLY ENDORSED
Our sleep guidance is independently endorsed by Dr Kate Johnson, Ph.D. in Sleep Physiology and postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Sleep Medicine at Harvard Medical School and Stanford Research Institute.

“What I particularly love about the Little Ones strategies is that they can be tailored to your family, so that you really see positive change to your baby’s sleep.”

TRUSTED BY MORE THAN 1 MILLION FAMILIES
Little Ones has helped more than one million families around the world navigate baby and toddler sleep.

From newborn sleep and changing nap needs to regressions, night waking, early rising, nap transitions and toddler sleep, Little Ones is there as your baby grows.

No more wondering when the next nap should be, how long your baby should sleep, or what today’s unexpected nap means for the rest of the day.

Open Little Ones. Know what’s next.

For our first kid we tried multiple sleep training apps before finding Little Ones. The schedule made sense (we didn’t closely follow the feeding schedule but stuck to the sleeping recommendations) and the guides on gentle sleep training methods based on our comfort level worked when nothing else would! Saved my sanity
01/20/2022
reviewfrog
I found this program one sleepless night with my 4 month old. I would spend my nights searching for help for my baby who didn’t sleep. The only way I could get him asleep was to rock him asleep, then hold him for 30-60 minutes until I could put him down. Otherwise , he woke right up when I put him down. That was for every nap, and multiple times per night. Two family members told me about Healthy Sleep, Happy Baby, and how their husbands had to stop them from going into their baby’s room when the baby was crying inconsolably. My aunt even gave me the book. First of all, as a sleep deprived mom, I did not have the time or mental energy to read such a large book. Secondly, we refused to let our baby cry like that.  So finding this program was just what I needed. The app makes it so easy to know when to feed your baby and when he needs sleep. I was able to get started immediately, I didn’t have to read extensively before implementing it, which is what you need when you are desperate for sleep. I started out just trying to get my baby on the sleep schedule. After a few weeks of that, one night my baby woke up every two hours and I said it was time to try self settling. When the program said that the goal was to put your baby down wide awake, and letting him put himself asleep, I thought that was a wild fantasy! And now, several weeks later, my baby usually will put himself to sleep. I am amazed! I loved the gentle approach to sleep training. I didn’t feel like I was punishing or neglecting my baby. It definitely took persistence, and my baby still isn’t perfectly in sync with the program, but I now have a schedule for the day, and we are all getting more sleep and are all happier. I can’t thank you enough for this program! I would recommend it to anyone! I only wish I knew about it sooner!

Save yourself the heartache
07/26/2020
missaprilkat
Buying this program was the biggest mistake that I made in my baby’s first year. My baby had a very difficult time syncing with their schedule, and it often turned me into a stressed out mama trying to force sleep on her baby when it was the last thing in the world she wanted. We both ended up in tears several times. In addition I personally found their expert feedback to be somewhat lazy. If you’re having an issue and you’re not on their schedule to the T, they deem your baby as overtired. I can’t tell you how many times I’ve been told that my baby is overtired- how is a baby that gets 10 more minutes of daytime sleep than they recommend overtired? I feel that they’re belief that the schedule is the be all end all prevents their sleep consultants from thinking more critically about what is causing issues- if your baby is not perfectly on the schedule then they assume that’s the problem, and they don’t go much deeper than that. I also was completely unprepared for the 4 month regression when it hit. Had I known you can begin teaching a baby to sleep independently before that, then I could have avoided a lot of that pain. I found the courses and sleep consultants from Taking Cara Babies much more helpful.
