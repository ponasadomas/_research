Your baby's sleep, made simpler
Baby sleep can feel confusing, unpredictable and exhausting.
Our smart sleep app acts like a
sleep expert in your pocket
, helping guide your baby’s day and respond to challenges as they happen.
No endless Googling. No conflicting advice.
Just clear, personalised sleep support that
adapts to your baby.
Smart on the outside. Adaptive on the inside.
Our app uses adaptive sleep technology to understand your baby’s needs and adjust their rhythm throughout the day.
We call this Sleep O Rhythm and it is the heart of our app.
It continuously adjusts your baby’s sleep rhythm based on their real sleep and feeding patterns, helping prevent overtiredness, short naps and night wakings.
No generic schedules. No rigid rules.
Just smarter sleep that evolves with your baby.
Sleep o Rhythm takes into account:
✔︎ Your baby’s age and development
✔︎ Real sleep and feed tracking
✔︎ Changing sleep needs over time
✔︎ The latest sleep science
So instead of trying to figure it all out, your baby’s day is guided for you.
