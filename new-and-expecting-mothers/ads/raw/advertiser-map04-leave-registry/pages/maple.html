Thank you for being a part of Maple.

Maple began with a simple idea: help families stay organized and connected. Thanks to thousands of families across the world, like yours, that idea became something real.

Together, we helped move the conversation around the mental load at home forward and showed that family life works better when everyone can share in it.

Thank you for your trust, support, and belief in what we were building. We’re proud of what Maple became, and grateful to have shared this journey with you.

Maple has been acquired by Wander and will sunset on December 31, 2026. Between now and then, we’ll provide guidance, resources, and recommendations to help your family transition smoothly.

Your account will remain active until December 31, 2026. After that, the account will be closed and inaccessible.

Your data will remain fully accessible until December 31, 2026.

We highly recommend taking this opportunity to export or transfer your data to other apps or services before Maple shuts down.

All data will be deleted and there will be no way to recover it once Maple is officially shut down. This includes all connections to any external services Maple offered such as Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook and TeamSnap.

Annual Maple subscriptions will no longer renew. Monthly subscriptions will remain active until December 31, 2026.
