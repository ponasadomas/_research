Get Started Now
It only takes a few minutes to onboard your new assistant.
STEP 1
Create your profile
Set up your account in minutes. Add your name, choose your preferences, and connect your Google, Apple, or Outlook calendars. Join or create your Circle to keep your household synced.
STEP 2
Add what you need
Text Ohai, forward emails, or upload screenshots, PDFs, and photos. Anything you share becomes organized information like events, reminders, tasks, or notes.
STEP 3
Get organized automatically
Ohai turns your updates into a clear and actionable plan. Events go to the right calendar, tasks are tracked, reminders are created, and your day stays organized without extra effort.

Frequently Asked Questions
Why do I need an AI household assistant?
Modern families juggle calendars, emails, school portals, sports apps, and constant updates. Ohai reduces the mental load by turning all of that noise into one organized, unified plan, saving time, reducing stress, and preventing things from slipping through the cracks.

What does O scan and sync?
O can process and organize calendars, emails, PDFs, photos, school updates, sports schedules, newsletters, and other household information, turning all of it into structured events, tasks, and reminders.
How much does it cost?
Ohai offers flexible plans depending on how many people need access:
• Individual Premium: $9.99 per month
• Premium Plus (Duo): $19.99 per month
• Premium Group: $29.99 per month
A free trial is available for new users.
Is my data private?
Yes. You choose exactly what to connect. Ohai only accesses sources you authorize and never sells or shares your data.
How does Ohai help me manage my household calendar?
Ohai syncs every connected calendar, scans events for important details, resolves conflicts, and keeps everything updated in one place. It sends reminders, handles schedule changes, and keeps your day organized automatically.
How does Ohai help with school and sports coordination?
O scans emails, PDFs, district notices, sports apps, and newsletters to pull out schedules, closures, and updates, then adds everything to your family calendar without manual entry.
How does Ohai use AI for meal planning?
Ohai recommends meals based on your preferences, fits them into your schedule, creates a grocery list, and connects with Instacart so ingredients can be delivered when you need them.
How does Ohai keep tasks and to-dos organized?
Text, upload, or forward anything, Ohai turns notes, lists, and reminders into structured tasks with due dates, assignments, and smart follow-ups.
How does Ohai help families stay aligned?
Ohai centralizes events, tasks, messages, and reminders in one shared system. Everyone stays on the same page without constant texting or checking multiple apps.
How does Ohai help with multiple caregivers or co-parents?
Yes. Shared circles allow each adult to connect their calendars, receive reminders, and coordinate plans seamlessly, even across households.
How does Ohai handle travel and logistics planning?
You can ask O to plan trips, create checklists, track confirmations, find available times, and send reminders leading up to travel.
How does Ohai work with AI search?
You can ask O for instructions, recommendations, hours, directions, or quick information. Ohai finds the answer and helps you act on it, whether that means adding to your calendar, creating a list, or sending a link.
