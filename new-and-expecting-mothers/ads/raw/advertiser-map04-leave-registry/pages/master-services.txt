Frequently Asked Questions
I don’t have a team—can this still work for me?
Yes. Many of our clients are solo business owners with no team, and the Solo Maternity Leave Method planning path was created specifically for you. Whether you’re doing it all yourself or thinking about bringing on support, I’ll help you build a plan that fits your exact business model (and energy levels).
What if I’m due really soon—do I still have time?
Absolutely. While more planning time is always great, I’ve worked with plenty of moms who are just weeks (or even days) out. We’ll prioritize the most urgent pieces and give you a realistic, streamlined plan you can put into action quickly. If you’re in a time crunch, Private Coaching or a Planning Intensive might be your best next step.
I’m not pregnant yet, but I want to plan ahead. Should I start now?
Yes! If you’re TTC or thinking about growing your family in the future, starting now will give you the most options and the smoothest experience possible. The earlier you start, the more empowered and prepared you’ll feel—and that readiness will ripple through everything you build.
I’m already overwhelmed—how much time will this take?
I get it. That’s why each offer is designed to meet you where you are. The Solo Method is completely self-paced (bite-sized lessons you can do in short bursts). BOND gives you a step-by-step weekly structure over 6 weeks, with support built in. And private coaching can be as hands-on or streamlined as you need. You don’t need to overhaul your business in a week—we’ll take it one intentional step at a time.
I’ve seen free checklists online—how is this different?
Great question. Those free resources are one-size-fits-all—and usually built for corporate employees. What I offer is customized for entrepreneurs—women whose businesses depend on them, who can’t hand off to HR, and who need both strategic systems and personal support to plan a leave that actually works. This isn’t a checklist. It’s a transformation.
What if I don’t know which service is right for me?
You’re not alone—and I’m here to help. If you’re unsure where to start, take the free Maternity Leave Readiness Assessment. It will show you exactly where you stand and recommend the best next step based on your business, timeline, and support needs. Still have questions? Reach out—I’m happy to help you figure out what makes the most sense for you.
