From One Mom to Another

Hi, I’m Michelle, proud mom of two and founder of Safe in the Seat.

When I brought my first baby home, I was completely overwhelmed. I just wanted to do everything right, but the more I researched, the more confused I became.

Then, during a newborn photo session, I found out my son’s car seat straps weren’t tight enough or in the right spot. My heart sank. I thought I had done everything right, but I hadn’t.

That moment changed everything. I wished someone would just make car seat safety simple with no guilt, no overwhelm, just clear answers.

That’s exactly why I created Safe in the Seat. Here, you’ll find expert help, trusted tools, and judgment-free guidance from a mom who’s been in your shoes… and just so happens to be a Certified Child Passenger Safety Technician too.

So take a deep breath. You’ve found your go-to resource for keeping your little ones safe.
