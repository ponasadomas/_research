FAQs
How does Cocoon’s pricing work?
Cocoon charges an upfront annual per employee per year fee based on company size plus a one-time implementation fee, giving employers transparency and predictability into their annual costs. All leave types are included in Cocoon’s standard pricing. Check out
our pricing page
for more details.
What is the ROI of Cocoon?
When employees don’t file for all the benefits they’re owed from their state and/or private insurance, employers that offer a fully paid policy end up paying more to keep an employee’s salary at 100%. Cocoon’s Insights feature automatically calculates how much you save when employees can easily file claims and receive the benefits they’re due. On average, Cocoon saves employers $12,515 per leave taken. Calculate how much you could save with our
free tool
.
How long does it take to implement Cocoon?
Employers can be live with Cocoon in 4-6 weeks. The Cocoon Customer Success team will guide you through key implementation steps of account configuration, inputting your federal and state coverage, and setting up your census and payroll.
Does Cocoon offer preferred partner pricing?
