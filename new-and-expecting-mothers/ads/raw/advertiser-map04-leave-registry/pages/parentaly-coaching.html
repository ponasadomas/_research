Our Proven Method

Pre-leave planning

You’ll get

  • Three 30-minute sessions with a Parentaly coach as you prepare for leave

  • Guided exercises based on your specific needs and circumstances

  • Access to a library of templates and resources to save time in leave planning

You’ll leave with
  • Coverage plan

    How your work should be managed while you’re gone

  • Communication plan

    How your team should communicate with you during leave

  • Re-onboarding plan

    How you’ll successfully re-onboard when you’re back

(optional)

On-leave coaching

You’ll get

  • Three optional 1:1 coaching sessions (recommended for new parents taking 6+ months of leave)

  • Individualized discussion on topics like working parent identity, career confidence, and effective home systems

  • Tailored support to think through your personal and professional goals

You’ll leave with
  • Tailored guidance

    Get support on individual needs during your time off

  • Home systems

    Develop strong home systems that will facilitate your success as a working parent

  • Return-to-work prep

    Feel prepared to return to work following your leave

Return-to-work support

You’ll get

  • Three 30-minute sessions with a Parentaly coach to guide your return-to-work experience

  • Access to templates and resources to streamline your re-onboarding

  • Continued professional development to support your career goals

You’ll leave with
  • Successful reintegration

    Establishing work-life rhythms and catching up once you're back

  • Productivity tools

    Routines and mindsets to help you work smarter as a new parent

  • Career vision

    Revisiting your career goals and prioritizing your work to get you there

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens in a career coaching session?

Your Parentaly career coach will customize your sessions to your specific needs, and will share frameworks and materials to support those needs. These sessions typically involve sharing personal experiences and challenges, and receiving feedback and tactical recommendations.

What is Parentaly, and where can I learn more about your services?

Parentaly is a benefit offering that combines expert career coaching and resources to improve the experience for expecting parents before, during and after their leave. Parentaly's goal is to empower working parents to successfully re-onboard and fulfill their career goals.

By registering for the program, you have access to up to 9 career coaching sessions throughout your parental leave. Visit our website to learn more.

What is the cost of the Parentaly program, and do I have to pay for it?

Your company is offering the Parentaly program as an employee benefit, which means they cover 100% of the cost! Employees who enroll will be taking advantage of a premium career coaching program at no cost to them.

Is the parental leave coaching program only for (pregnant) women?

No! The parental leave coaching program is designed for any expecting parent, including non-birthing parents! Hundreds of alumni graduating from the Parentaly program are non-birthing parents who have found it invaluable in helping them prepare for their leave and think about their career goals moving forward.

I haven't been coached before. What exactly is a career coach?

A Parentaly career coach is trained to ask questions about you and your work, use that information to guide you toward concrete actions to achieve your goals, and hold you accountable to your plan.

What is the ideal timing to start the Parentaly program?

To get the full benefit of the program, we recommend starting at least 3 months before your leave begins, but we accept sign-ups at any point in your leave journey, including when you’re returning to work.
