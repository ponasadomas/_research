Here’s How I Can Help You Master Your Maternity Leave

Work your way through our proven 4-phase framework —so your business keeps growing while you step away.

Phase 1

PHASE ONE

Build the Plan

We start by creating a clear, customized maternity leave plan that aligns with your business goals, your timeline, and your personal values. This isn’t a plug-and-play template—it’s your personalized roadmap to a leave that actually works.

Phase 2

PHASE TWO

“Operationalize” the Business

Next, we streamline your systems, document your workflows, and identify what needs to run without you. From automations to delegation, we help you press pause without panic.

Phase 3

PHASE THREE

Nurture the Team

We ensure your team (or future team!) is empowered to take the wheel while you’re away. You’ll set expectations, strengthen communication, and feel confident in their ability to hold things down.

Phase 4

PHASE four

Design the Re-Entry

Your return doesn’t have to feel like a crash landing. We help you map out a phased, intentional comeback that supports your energy, priorities, and long-term vision—so you can step back in with clarity and calm.
