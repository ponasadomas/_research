Everything You Need to Know About Childcare
£4.99

There are two main schemes designed to help you manage childcare costs in the UK: funded childcare hours and Tax-Free Childcare.

In England, working parents can access up to 30 hours of funded childcare starting from when their child is just 9 months old, a benefit that can save families up to £8,000 a year. When combined with the Tax-Free Childcare scheme, these initiatives can slash your monthly expenses by hundreds of pounds.

However, getting there isn't always simple. There are strict eligibility criteria to navigate, crucial application deadlines to meet, and varying rules depending on whether you live in England, Scotland, Wales, or Northern Ireland.

What We’ll Cover in This 1-Hour Masterclass:

  • Who qualifies for funded hours and Tax-Free Childcare in the UK.

  • What adjusted net income means and how to calculate it.

  • How and when to apply for both schemes to avoid missing deadlines.

  • What happens to your childcare benefits during maternity leave or redundancy.

  • How Universal Credit interacts with funded hours.

  • The logistics of hiring a nanny and transitioning your child into care.

  • How to use the Workplace Nursery Benefit to slash your fees by 40%.

⚠️Is this session right for you? Please note that this masterclass is tailored for those earning under £100k due to specific scheme caps. If your income is £100k+, this particular session won't be suitable—but we’ve got you covered! You might find our previous session with wealth manager & financial coach Philly Ponniah much more relevant.
