Billing questions

Do I need a credit card to start my free trial?

No. Your 14-day trial is free and doesn't ask for any payment information, so there's nothing to remember to cancel.

What happens when my trial ends?

Choose monthly ($5.95) or yearly ($49) to keep going. Everything you added during your trial stays in your account. If you decide not to subscribe, you won't be charged.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. We have a 60-day, no-questions-asked refund policy.

Will my yearly subscription renew automatically?

Not by default. We don't automatically bill you when your yearly subscription ends unless you choose to turn on auto-renew.

Why isn't there a free plan?

If you're not paying for the product, you're the product. Plan to Eat is fully customer-funded, so we can focus on you instead of selling your data or showing ads.

Can my family share one account?

Yes. Use one Plan to Eat account on as many devices as you like and share it with the people in your household.
