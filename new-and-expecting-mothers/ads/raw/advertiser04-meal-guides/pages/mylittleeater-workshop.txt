Hey there, I’m Edwena.

Mama, Pediatric Dietitian and Feeding Expert with Over 14 Years in The Field, Founder of My Little Eater and Creator of The Texture Timeline™.

And I get it.

I know what it’s like to be staring at your baby, food in hand, heart racing, thinking “I can’t believe my baby is ready for solids! Now what??”.

The mix of emotions is real, and yet your commitment to doing best for your baby is top priority. 

The thing is… even though I had the best of intentions…I didn’t know what I didn’t know. 

I didn’t realize the “mistakes” I was making that could have ended up taking me down a road of picky eating/sensory issues/limited diet/allergies/and oral motor challenges. 

But here’s the good news—you don’t have to figure this all out on your own like I had to.

That’s why I created this workshop. My goal is to take the stress out of starting solids, give you clear, practical guidance, and most importantly, help you feel confident. Because at the end of the day, feeding your baby should be a fun and joyful experience—not something that keeps you up at night.
