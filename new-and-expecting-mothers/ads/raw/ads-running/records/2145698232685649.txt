Bring on baby’s first foods! 🍐🍓🥑🥚

If you thought your baby needed a purée for their first food… guess again. 

Babies can start with almost any food as long as it is appropriately prepared according to their age.

Let’s be clear: Purées aren’t bad—and there’s nothing wrong with serving them. They’re just not a required first step before finger foods. In fact, forget everything you’ve heard about “steps” or “stages”. They aren’t required. 

The goal? Give baby safe opportunities to explore different tastes, textures, shapes, and smells while learning the skills needed to eat.
