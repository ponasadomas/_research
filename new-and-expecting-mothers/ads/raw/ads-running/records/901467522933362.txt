Your baby doesn’t need teeth to eat real food! 🍍

Wondering what foods your 6-month-old baby can eat? You might be surprised by how many options you have. 

From steak and chicken to pasta, broccoli, and pineapple, babies can explore a wide variety of flavours and textures from around 6 months old. 

The key isn’t how many teeth your baby has — it’s whether they’re showing signs of readiness for starting solids and whether food is prepared safely for their age and developmental stage.

5 foods your baby can explore from 6 months:

🥩 Steak
🍝 Pasta
🍗 Chicken
🥦 Broccoli
🍍 Pineapple

Remember: Every baby develops at their own pace. Always check for readiness signs, offer appropriately prepared foods, and supervise your baby while eating.
