Yes, that’s chicken liver, but stay with us… 

Chicken liver is SO nutritious for babies when starting solids but often overlooked. It’s rich in fat and protein, in addition to choline, iron, folate, selenium, zinc, and vitamins A, B12, and other B vitamins. Together, these nutrients support baby’s energy to play and explore, brain development, growth, strength, red blood cells, hormones, immune function, taste perception, vision, and metabolic processes! 

Whew! 😅

Additionally, introducing your baby to a wide variety of foods and flavors, even ones you may not love yourself, helps them to accept a variety of foods later on! 

⚠️ Note: Chicken liver has high levels of vitamin A. Vitamin A is an essential nutrient, but can be toxic when consumed in excess. For this reason, take care to limit the serving size and frequency; 1 to 2 tablespoons of chicken liver once a week is plenty.
