Starting solids doesn’t mean your baby is suddenly going to start eating full meals overnight 🥩🥦🥔🍝

Social media can make it seem like every baby is sitting down and eating huge plates of food from day one, but the reality is that this isn’t typical for most babies. 🤍

Some babies take to solids quickly, while others need more time to explore, touch, squish, taste, and get comfortable with new foods. And that’s completely normal. 

In the beginning, it’s not about how much food your baby actually eats. Prior to 12 months, breastmilk and/or formula should be your baby’s primary source of nutrition. Introducing solid food is all about helping them explore, learn, and build exposure to new foods and  allergens. Success looks different for everyone, but the goal is building familiarity, developing skills, and creating positive experiences around food.

✨ If your baby is showing interest, exploring different foods, and gradually becoming more comfortable at mealtimes, you’re on the right track!

Try not to compare your baby’s plate to what you see on Instagram. Every baby learns at their own pace, and progress doesn’t always look like an empty plate. 🤍
