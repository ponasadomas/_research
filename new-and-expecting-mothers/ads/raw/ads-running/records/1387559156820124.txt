The reason so many toddlers prefer bland foods is because many parents assume that’s all they can have when they start solids (think: rice cereal & oatmeal), so of course babies develop a preference for bland foods. 

But you can actually begin introducing flavor, texture, and different ingredients (even spice!) as soon as your baby is ready to start solids. This is typically around 6 months. 

⚠️ Remember: Egg is one of THE most common food allergens and should be introduced separately from other foods so you can rule out potential allergies. This video shows pesto (which often contains nuts) as well as Parmesan cheese. Before offering or mixing allergens, be sure you’ve introduced the common ones separately from other foods first.
