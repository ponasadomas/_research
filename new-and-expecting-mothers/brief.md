# Mamivio product brief

Captured 2026-10-24. This is an inventory and interpretation of the live offer, not independent verification of its claims. Source: https://www.mamivio.com/ and https://www.mamivio.com/baby-development-guide/. Saved text/HTML: `competitors/raw/product/home.txt`, `guide.txt`, `guide.html`.

## Observed offer

The home page describes practical, supportive information for mothers and directs readers to a baby-development guide. The guide sells a digital plan selected for a baby's age in weeks, with weekly milestones and daily activities described as taking 10–15 minutes. The page states email delivery within 48 hours, a single payment, no subscription, and a 14-day money-back guarantee. Rendered English-page prices were $17.95 / $29.95 / $49.95 for one / three / six months; three months was preselected. See `funnel.md` for source captures, add-ons and limits.

Mechanism, interpretation: organize age-appropriate activities into a short weekly plan. Age selection alone does not establish personalization to observed abilities, preferences, prematurity, household constraints or medical history. The proposed research should test what additional inputs create useful differences in the delivered report.

Audience, interpretation: parents of babies, especially first-time mothers uncertain what to do during waking time. Expecting mothers are in the owner's brief but the observed main offer centers on a baby already born. The final niche is not predetermined by this page.

## Claims requiring evidence before reuse

The guide claims historical changes in development, a 6–8 week gap, a ninefold flat-head increase, a 22% motor-delay figure and historical versus modern milestone ages. These numbers have no visible supporting references in the saved sales text. Its comparison of historical tummy time with modern practice must not imply stomach sleeping is safe. CDC screening checklist changes cannot by themselves demonstrate population-wide developmental decline.

The page names a pediatric physiotherapist and states experience and baby counts. The captured page does not establish the person's identity, professional registration, authorship or review of the actual report. Testimonials and a 120-plus trust claim are seller-controlled and unverified. Home-page emphasis on a small writing team and the guide's specialist-origin account need reconciling. Do not use these as proof of report effectiveness.

Starting every plan two weeks earlier is not a validated assessment of individual development. This evidence establishes no PDF diagnostic, treatment or delay-prevention effect. Research records what people actually say and do, in their own words, whatever the subject. The owner decides later what to use in copy; research never makes that decision. Retain ordinary activity planning and clinical-assessment experiences with their actual provenance.

## Working hypotheses to test

Compare: ordinary baby play/tummy-time planning; first solids and allergens; return-to-work pumping logistics; postpartum household/support preparation; baby sleep routines; pregnancy exercise; higher-risk clinical needs such as recovery or infant symptoms. Evaluate urgency, visible spending, useful personalization, free substitutes, competition, acquisition feasibility and clinical burden separately.

Questions carried into validation: launch country/language, fulfillment qualifications, source and reviewer accountability, actual customization inputs, acquisition budget, revision/support policy and customer willingness to pay. None prevents public research now.
