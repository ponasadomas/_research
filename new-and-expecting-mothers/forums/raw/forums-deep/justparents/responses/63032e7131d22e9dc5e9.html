Charlotte's birth:

I was 11 days overdue when I went in the hospital to be induced. My DH and my mum came with me and we went in for around 7.30pm. I was monitored and the at about 9ish I was given some gel and was monitored again for about 30 mins, the mw told me I would probably need more gel in the morning. I started with a few pains but just told that they were staring pains and given some paracetamol. My DH and my mum went home as visiting time was over so I thought I would get my head down for some sleep. I started to get really sharp pains approx every 5 mins so walked up and down the ward but the other ladies started to settle down for the night so I didn't want to disturb them.

I kept feeling like I needed the loo so I was up and down like a yoyo and by this time my pains were really strong and went upto the desk and told the mw I was really struggling, I was told I was having starting pains and to get a bath, which did nothing I had to get out of the bath and go back to the front desk as I was doubled over and wasn't able to move when I had a contraction. The mw examined me and said I was actually 3cms dialated and strapped me to a monitor again. I asked for pain relief and got told I could have pethidine as the delivery suite was too busy and the anethatist was not there at the moment for epidurals. It worked almost immediately and I went off to sleep. I woke up at about 2am needing a wee so trotted off to the toilet where my waters went and had a show. I told the mw and he examined me a realised I was fully dialated! He rushed off to phone my DH and then rushed me down to the delivery suite. I was handed over to another mw, she started asking me questions and I just shouted I needed to push, she quickly examined me and said ok. My DH turned up 10 mins later, Charlotte pooed on the way out and was suctioned but was fine, she was born at 3.03am weighing 7lbs 7oz.

George's birth:

I wanted a homebirth this time and I was a little scared that I could only have Gas and Air for pain relief so I hired a TENs machine. I was 4 days overdue and wasn't expecting anything to happen yet as Charlotte was really overdue. We had been told by my mw that he had expected him to be born over the weekend so me and DH had joked that he was wrong as it was now Monday.

I had just settled Charlotte in bed and flopped into the armchair to watch a bit of TV and almost immediately jumped back up with a sharp pain in my side. I didn't think much of it and sat back down. 20 mins later I had another one this time I realised it was a contraction. My DH wasn't too bothered so I just leant on a gym ball and eventually put on my tens. They started coming at really odd intervals every four mins to one on top of the other I was hoping they would get in some sort of rythm but suddenly just came every minute or so. I was just breathing through a contraction when I felt a trickling and a huge urge to push! I made DH get onto the delivery suite and send out a mw and then onto my mum. My mum turned up to find me climbing the stairs to go to the loo where I had a show then the mw came flying up the stairs to find me having a huge contraction. She made me go downstairs to the living room where I'd planned to give birth, DH hadn't even put anything down on the floor yet! He was running around and got the plastic down just as my waters went! They were pea soup green, the mw wanted to get the monitor on me to check George his heart beat was only 60 a minute so she sent Martin off to ring the delivery suite to send an ambulance and find out where the 2nd mw was as she hadn't showed up. He was out in the hallway when he was born and missed it all. George was out in 2 pushes but was really pale and wasn't breathing properly, we found a tight knot in his cord. I remember the paramedics turning up and then the 2nd mw, we all laughed when a taxi turned up with the gas and air, a little bit late! We went to the hospital where George was taken to Special care as his temperature was also very low and his breathing needed to be stablised, he was ok by the morning and we were allowed to go home.

I managed to do it all with no pain relief at all but boy I felt every milimetre of his head coming out! George was born at 9.52pm weighing 7lbs 5oz, less than 3 hrs from feeling the first contraction!

here's mine

I had a rare pregnancy related disorder called accute fatty liver pregnancy and presented no symptoms of it except high blood pressure. This occurs in something like 1 in 18,000 pregnancies and with no symptoms to know what to test for, I was lucky that another woman had the same thing a few months before at the same hospital so they decided to check me for that (after ruling out several other things). Luckily they did and called me to the hospital because with in an hour of my arrival, my daughter's heart rate had dropped dramatically due to the stress my illness was causing her. Had they not checked for this, I would not be here to tell this story as my liver was over 50 percent fat when I delivered (delivery is the cure). Aaralyn was born 5 1/2 weeks before my due date and was 4 lbs but a feisty little you know what right from the get go. I didn't get to see her until 2 oclock the day after she was born (10:02 pm) and that really sucked!! You'd never know to look at her or hear her talk that she had ever suffered any trauma at all.
