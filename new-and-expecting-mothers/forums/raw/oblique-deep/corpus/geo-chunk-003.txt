I also use the disposable nappy inserts in addition to these. They are biodegradable and flushable thin cloths made from bamboo. I line the washable nappy with them. I have a pump-in-style that I bought in the US. It was about 1/4 of the price that it is in Hong Kong. We bought a small transformer that was actually meant for a camera to run it in HK--cost us less than $100 HKD. I've used the pump-in-style for two babies.
