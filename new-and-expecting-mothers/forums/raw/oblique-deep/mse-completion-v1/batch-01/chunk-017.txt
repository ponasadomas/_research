The kiddicare just for bums ones are probably worth a mention too if you've got the space to buy them (they come in boxes of 4 standard nappy packs) and store them (which is why we quit using them in the end - two in nappies meant we were running out of storage space).

I tend to just go with wherever's on offer really these days - if Tesco are doing one of their regular two big packs for a tenner we go there - otherwise Aldi.

Aldi don't seem to do the 5+ size though (at least in our local stores) which is worth bearing in mind if you've got kids like mine who end up in a higher nappy size than for their weight because the "right" size doesn't fit well on them (my kids are really tall and the youngest is really long-bodied so we've often got to go up a size to get the fit right).
