You could look into a SPOC (simple piece of cloth) wrap. I've tried a ring sling (gave me shoulder/neck ache), and a Patapum toddler carrier (thought it wasn't secure enough) and another (I forget which), none of which I really liked. Now I just have a long, hemmed piece of cotton that I use for my youngest (just turned 1). Wraps are a good choice from a budget point of view and they're very versatile - lots of ways to wear them. It can take practice to learn how to wrap, but once you've got it, it's really quick.

I would recommend reading http://www.wrapyourbaby.com/ , http://www.babywearing.co.uk/ , http://www.thebabywearer.com/ and http://wearyourbaby.com/Default.aspx?tabid=36
