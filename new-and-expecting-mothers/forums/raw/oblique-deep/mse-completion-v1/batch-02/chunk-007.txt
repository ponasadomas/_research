From 6 weeks to week 20 50% or SMP whichever is greater

Which would be 39 weeks/9 months.

Also the goverment paying SMP, I think what is meant by that is HMRC refund 92% of SMP to the employer (more for a small employer). Employer pays you, HMRC refunds 92%.

Be careful about the qualifying weeks. Your SMP is usually calculated by taking the % calculated from the average of the 8 weeks to the qualifying week (15 weeks before due date). In your case this would be Sunday, May 26, 2013 to Saturday, July 20, 2013. If your OH is only getting SMP in this period, the 50% of salary they talk about could be 50% of SMP, not 50% of her normal wage. You need to clarify this.
