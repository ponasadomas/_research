POST Comment_75545565 | reply | author_id=2271331 | 2019-03-05T15:10:08+00:00
https://forums.moneysavingexpert.com/discussion/5973406/kidstart-anyone-used-it#Comment_75545565
I got on really well with it, we got £25 in a year without really trying. I mainly used it for Amazon and eBay, when I remembered. We did get a couple of big items too, which helped (about £10). We don't do masses of shopping, but I haven't had any issues with it tracking, unlike TopCashback (total farce, nothing I did tracked and clearing cookies didn't help!).

As far as I see it, it's free money for nothing extra. It's been a nice little starter for his savings account- I got an American express credit card and put the cashback from that in his savings account too.

I have heard other people say the amounts didn't track- not sure if it's that, or they were put off by how long it takes for the money to appear as available (it stays as pending for a while for some retailers). If you set the account it goes to up then it transfers automatically, so no need to worry to much if you just earmark it as savings for the distant future.

Hope that helps!
