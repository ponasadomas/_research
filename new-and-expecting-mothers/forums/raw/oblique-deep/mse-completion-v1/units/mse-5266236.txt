The maximum you can claim per month in vouchers is £243 (this is dependent on your salary). This would be deducted monthly from your gross salary and paid into your childcare voucher account.

The benefit of this is that you don't pay tax or NI on this amount. You also don't miss it as it is taken straight out your salary if you see what I mean. Because it is taken before tax and NI it also means that you pay a little less tax and NI each month than you otherwise would.

Hope that this is helpful
