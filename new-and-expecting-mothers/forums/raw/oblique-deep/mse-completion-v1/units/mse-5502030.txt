I've had a look at a cot bunk and though the upper cot is lower than a normal upper bunk bed, there is a huge drop from the top of the railings to the floor and babies/toddlers do climb out.

This is especially worrying when, a few years ago, a 4 year old boy died after falling from a top bunk while asleep and this was not the only sad tale. Older children have died, too.

Some children get out of cots at a very early age, as did my granddaughter, who wanted a bed like her brother, so she was allowed to sleep in a low toddler bed.

Even at just 4 and nearly 3, when sleeping in a regular, but not high, bed, away from home,they have a thick duvet by the bedside to prevent harm from a fall.
