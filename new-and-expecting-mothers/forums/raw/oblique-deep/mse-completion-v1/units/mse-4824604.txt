It's still serving good duty underneath our jumperoo cushioning the base of the frame in case the littlest one rolls onto it and bumps her head and it's survived a LOT of washing and puking on thanks to the neverending reflux queen! Was planning on using it as a rug in the summer house once we'd finished with it as well as when you take the arches off it'll quite easily pass for one.
