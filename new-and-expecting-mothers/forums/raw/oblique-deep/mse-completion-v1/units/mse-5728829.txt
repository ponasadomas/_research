For you and you alone, the vouchers won't be available to you as you need to have earned and paid PAYE tax to make it beneficial, so that only leaves the TFC account as your only option. That does have some issues, such as proving you income, but is your only option on your own.

However, if you have a TFC, it means that no one in your household can have the vouchers, so if your other half has vouchers now, they couldn't if you opened up a TFC account, which means you will lose out.

Personally, I would have thought the TFC account is more beneficial to you, but without knowing all the details it is difficult to be sure.
