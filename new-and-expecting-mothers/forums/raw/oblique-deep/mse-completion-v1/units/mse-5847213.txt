minimum voucher amount is £20 very few employers let it go lower.

But you can raise or lower your voucher amount each month as long as you do it before their cut off (they can tell your or the voucher company can).

October, you just need to be taking a voucher each month, no stopping.

You may be aware that your vouchers are valid until the September after your youngest 15th Birthday (16 if disabled)

buy now save them for later, they are not just for nursery / childminders.
