Good find, I always fancied a BabyDan one myself! I would say, as long as you have a table you can use, dont bother with a tray as its only one more thing to clean (baby led weaning is so much fun!) I would also say do you really need the cushions as they are only small for such a short time. I stuffed a cushion off the sofa behind DS1 in the beginning!

It would be quite easy to make those wrap around cushions with an old duvet and pillow cases cut up, some stick on velcro to attach the ends together, some Copydex glue and a bit of bravery. Alternatively, you could consider treating yourself to a proper set now then selling them at the end for a bit of return?
