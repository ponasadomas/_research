Their decision is correct, you allowed the pharmacy to sign on your behalf and you were aware that you had not paid. Responsibility for payment remains with you.

The next stage of appeal would be when they take you to court. This is likely to be a civil case but they also have the option to prosecute (these cases are rare). Either way, you won't win and you'll end up paying far more, at least double when the penalty and court costs are added.
