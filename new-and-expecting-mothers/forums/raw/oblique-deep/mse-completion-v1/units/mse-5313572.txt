From what I understand, SMP means that your company pays you 90% for 6 weeks and then you drop to the ~£140 a week for 33 weeks. You should also qualify for child benefit which is ~£80/month.

Don't forget to factor in child care costs when returning to work.

Have you done a statement of affairs to work out exactly what's coming in and going out? Doing a budget really stopped me freaking out about SMP!

http://www.stoozing.com/calculator/soa.php

You can phone ACAS for advice http://www.acas.org.uk/maternity

Depending on how things go you may not feel well enough to work after just 8 weeks, never mind work longer days. Even if the labour is fine and you bounce back quickly most babies are still feeding every 3-4 hours, including through the night, at 8 weeks so you'll be exhausted. As the months go on you don't get a chance to catchup on sleep so you'll become more exhausted than thought was possible. There's a reason most people take months off and although you can return early you won't be able to change your mind and get SMP again.

Do you definitely only get SMP from your employer and no enhanced package? Have you factored in that you'll get child benefit? Your spending is likely to decrease as you'll be going out less and won't be paying your commuting costs? Could you save now in order to subsidise a longer time off? It might be best to plan the full maternity leave and give your employer the required notice if you want to return early (2 months I think).
