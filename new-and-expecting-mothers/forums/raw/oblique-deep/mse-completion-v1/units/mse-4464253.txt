Mothercare are doing free Maxi Cosi Cabriofix car seat with the bee ATM if you are thinking of buying, think the offer is on until the 6th of March.

Don't know about the car but the bee is one of the most compact prams you can get unless you just go for a stroller. I used our carrycot instead of a Moses basket and he seemed to like it, we had a crib but we used the carrycot with a sleep mattress.

The only thing you can do with the carrycot is sit it on the back seat or on the floor behind the front seats providing you don't have anyone sitting in the back.

I loved our carrycot on the pram, he always looked comfy laying completely flat but I know others barely used theres.
