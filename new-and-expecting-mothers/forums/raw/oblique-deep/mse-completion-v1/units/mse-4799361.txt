My 21 month old eats the same as us every night.

Tends to be things like Cottage Pie, lasagne, curry (tikka masala or bhuna usually) home made pizza, lamb hot pot, pasta bake, corned beef hash, chicken fajitas, stir fry (we're having pork tonight) roast dinners, (beef, chicken, pork, gammon) bolognese, bread crumbed fish with home made oven chips and peas, chicken wrapped in bacon with cheese in the middle, casseroles (beef or chicken usually) vegetable crumble, risottos, stuffed peppers/mushrooms, omelettes/frittatas, baked potatoes with different fillings, sweet potato and spinach curry, quiche.

As you can see I'm not a fancy cook or anything. I tend to do a meal plan for the week on a Thursday night and plan Fri-Thu, I try to do different meals at least for a fortnight. I go my shopping on a Friday and buy all the ingredients for the meals I need if they're not in the cupboard.

Lunch wise, we do rice cakes with different toppings, tortilla/wrap pizzas, omelettes, toasties, savoury muffins, savoury flapjacks, french toast, scrambled egg, dippy egg with soldiers, small 'picnic' so breadsticks with cream cheese for dipping, peppers, cucumber, sliced meat etc.
