It may not be so much the fit, as the weight: do think about that for whatever system you buy.

It's a while ago now: we had a Silver Cross pram and separate pushchair: both were hand-me-downs and will have seen a few more adventures. BUT I pretty much never put the pram frame in the boot (Vauxhall Astra at the time, I think), and only rarely the pushchair. It fitted, but it weighed a ton and took up a lot of space. So they lived in the hall and were used for walking outings.

Then I had a succession of umbrella fold buggies which were light and easy to fold, there was much more space around them for shopping, change bags, snacks and drinks. Even the double buggy I borrowed for a while didn't take up too much space. They went in and out of the car as needed.
