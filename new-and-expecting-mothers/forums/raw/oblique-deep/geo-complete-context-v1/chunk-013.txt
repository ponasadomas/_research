I bought my Phil & Ted's Double (Sport) brand new in 2007 for about HKD8000. I sold it a couple of months ago for HKD4000 - and got lots of interest at that price. It was in excellent condition and I think if you can get a second-hand one, it might be worth it for you. It's a pretty robust stroller, so as long as it's been gently used, you should be fine with a second-hand one.

I used it with my newborn and his older brother (21 mths difference) for 2 years. In the beginning, the newborn was happy in the bassinet bit underneath. I have to admit though it's not very easy to slip the newborn in and out. But one positive thing is that as the bassinet is suspended and sways slightly with the movement of the stroller, the baby fell asleep almost immediately every time. When he got to about 4 mths old, he wasn't so happy in the little cocoon-like bassinet - not much of a view.

It was good to switch over at about 5mths to the little one sitting in the back seat (as reclined as possible) and the elder one in the front seat. They were happy with that arrangement for nearly 2 yrs, though the little one was getting a bit big and heavy for the back by the end.

The Phil & Ted's is bulky - I'm also about 5' 3" and while I could put it in and out of the car boot, I rarely took it out and about - just used it around where I live, going to the supermarket, dropping the elder one at nursery, etc. Never attempted to use it on public transport, that's for sure.

I don't think it's the perfect stroller - but I think it's about the best bet for a double stroller that you can get here and works particularly well with kids who are close in age. I did really like mine.

If you think you might get some use out of one, do buy second-hand and then re-sell on. You won't lose much.

HTH
