https://archive.geoclicks.com/threads/what-to-prepare-before-the-delivery.127563/#post-896643
first off, what one person loves, another might find useless.

a lot will depend on breast vs bottle feeding but generally:

onesies

crib/cot/bassinet (whatever it is you want the babies to sleep in)

bedding

nappies

those are the ONLY ESSENTIALS.

however, you may also wish to get:

breastfeeding pillow

bottles

steriliser

pacifiers

baby bath (you can actually bathe babies in the regular bath or even the kitchen sink, you don't really need a bath for htem)

change table (a bed, sofa, or even the floor can work just as well)

bouncy chair/swing

baby powder

soft sponge for cleaning

thermometer (better to have one before you need it)

cotton balls

wet wipes

nursing tops (for you)

nursing bras (for you)

bra pads 

maternity pads

some kind of blankets/sheets/wraps

sling

carseats

stroller (that lay flat for babies)

newborns don't really need toys... they may find something interesting by the time they get to 3 months or so.
