Hi

My baby was quite small at birth and was too small for the Baby Bjorn so I bought a HuggaBaby sling (it was the only sling they had at B2B at the time). It was great for the first few weeks and I found it easy to use and when she grew bigger, I moved to the Baby Bjorn. Funnily, she is now outgrowing the Bjorn so I have just started using the sling again, carrying her on my hip, and she seems to enjoy it. 

It's a good sling and serves its purpose well, but not having tried any others, I can't really compare. The fabric isn't too heavy and the baby seems very comfortable in it.
