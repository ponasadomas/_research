Hi e-mum. I used to breastfeed them both at the same time at night at first, however, we switched to one on expressed milk (my husband did the feed) and the other on breastfeed on our midwife's advise. She also said every once in a while, it is OK to have both of them fed by someone else and you get some rest. Although I never did that and it didn't make that much easier for me to have one on breast and the other on bottle, it was great to have my husband feed one of them with me (although this was very difficult for him as he had to go to work the next day) as we were able to have very good talks on those quiet hours And yes, we used to wake up both of them for feeding at the same time. It worked for us and the kids are now 2 years old and they are on really great schedule still.
