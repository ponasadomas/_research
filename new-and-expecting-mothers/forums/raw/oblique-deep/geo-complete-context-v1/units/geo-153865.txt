being close to Matilda would be you are far away from Hung Hom as that is on the Kowloon side...the closest service apartment I can think of is Macdonald #2 which is 20-30mins from Matilda depending on the traffic. there's a YWCA opposite it so there might be some activities your daughter can join - otherwise you could take a 5mins taxi ride to HK park and there is a lot to do there. also, it is near central so the ride back from Hung Hom shouldn't be too long - if he takes the bus from Hung Hom to central and then a minibus up - he should be back within an hour (45mins depending on the time of day). hope this helps!

If I were you, I think that being close to Hung Hom is probably more important than being close to Matilda. You'll be at the hospital either 3-5 days most likely, but will need to be in Hong Kong for at least a month or two if you add up being there before the baby comes and then waiting for a passport so you can go back to China. I would just go close to Hung Hom for the convenience for your husband. Many people commute for an hour or more in labour if they are in the US or other countries and as labour generally takes many more hours than that, it's no big deal most of the time. 

That said, I don't know Hung Hom at all... but I do know it will be cheaper than anywhere on Hong Kong side!!
