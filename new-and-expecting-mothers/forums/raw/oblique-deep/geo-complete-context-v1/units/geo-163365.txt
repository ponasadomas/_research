I was told by a few of my friends regarding their horrible experience giving birth in Macau, so I think it is a right choice to give birth in Hong Kong. If this is your first birth, you should have enough time (don't wait until water bursts, just start to come to HK when there is a sign of labor, e.g. bleeding or contraction in 15-30min gap) . Good luck!
