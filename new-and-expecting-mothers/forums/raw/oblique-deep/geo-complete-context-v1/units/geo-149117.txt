Hey thanka,

We have the Black ergo sport. The material is a bit lighter than the original, but I know that people are happy with either. There's a new one called the performance and my friends who have that one are also very happy with it. You can get most at Bumps and the performance is available at a shop called Little Barefeet in Central on Jubilee street, just off Queens Rd. The performance is made with cooler material and looks a bit sleeker than the other ones. If we didn't have one already, I'd try the performance as HK is soooo hot most of the year.
