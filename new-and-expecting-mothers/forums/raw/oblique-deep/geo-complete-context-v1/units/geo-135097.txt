My conclusion is that some of them are quite sweet and others taste like soy milk, but the one I found best is "Snow Brand" Pure & Touch (Yellow can for 0-9months). To us, it's definately the worst tasting & not sweet but for babies, it might be the closest to breast milk.

I know for a fact that the HK Sanatorium & Hospital in Happy Valley uses "Snow Brand" formula as well but you have to request it. I know that this one definatly has legit distribution channels to HK and the product should not be fake if you purchase it at the big chain grocery stores or Mannings or Watsons.
