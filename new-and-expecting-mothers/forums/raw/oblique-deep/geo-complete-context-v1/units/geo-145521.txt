POST post-1028861 | reply | author_id=13388 | 2010-09-30T12:48:28+0800
https://archive.geoclicks.com/threads/baby-shopping-recommendation-in-shenzen.145521/#post-1028861
Luohu Commercial Centre doesn't have great baby clothes, and all the stalls are the same, but good for convenience.

Dongmen (Lao Jie MTR) has tons of shops, most of the ground level ones are Chinese fashions, but there's an inside local mall (cannot describe though, sorry) that has many small baby stalls with decent clothes.

Hua Qiang Bei (MTR station) has a market near there (again, cannot give you an address), it's English translation name is called something like the Foreign Clothing market. It's all the factory seconds, many well known brands, lots and lots of different shops. If you are with Chinese people, just get them to ask shop people in the area and they should be able to tell you - it's pretty well known there. I wouldn't go there heavily pregnant unless you have nerves of steel. It's pretty crowded and cramped there. But so cheap, plus you can bargain. I highly recommend it there.

I got all my baby clothes in SZ (was living there) for 0-6 months and saved a BUNDLE.
