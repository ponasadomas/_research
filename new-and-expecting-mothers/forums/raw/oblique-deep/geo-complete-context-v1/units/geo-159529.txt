I was booked at Matilda for a regular delivery in a private room. Ended up with an emergency csection, some complications and a couple days in ICU. Bill quickly exceeded 200k.

In addition to standard delivery/ room charges you will pay doctors fees (pediatrician, OB, anesthetist), epidural if you have one, for certain tests and consumables. There are some fairly recent posts with more detailed breakdowns. For an uncomplicated delivery would budget around HK$100k.
