I'm using Hestia. For the most part, very good video quality and sound quality. We live in a 1900sq ft apartment and sometimes the signal can be a bit weak although it supposedly has a 300sqm range (3000sqft approx). It happens only on the other side of the apartment, especially when I turn a corner. Still highly recommend it though. Easy to use as well!
