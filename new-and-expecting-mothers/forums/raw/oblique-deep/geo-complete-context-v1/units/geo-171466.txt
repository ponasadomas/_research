I have a yoyo for the supposed extra convenience, but it is way easier using a carrier since i live in midlevels. Mind you, now bub is almost one, my back is killing me, and when we take the stroller away with us and try to use it with bub, she only has a small tolerance for it, and definitely won't sleep in it since she's not used to that. So, in hindsight, i wish i had've used the stroller more. The bugaboo comes with a car seat, doesn't it? That's good for transporting bub in the car, but makes it very heavy to lift up and down stairs etc in HK. Also, you may find bub will sleep in the stroller if rocked when nothing else works as a youngster - something else on the plus side of getting a stroller....

I didn't use a stroller when the baby was very young. I used it when they were older, and I'd recommend getting a lightweight that's easy to fold and carry on stairs and even a secondhand one. My kids stopped using it fairly early so there's no point buying an expensive one. But it does help in the in-between stage. I'm HK it's pretty easy to buy anything as you need it, so you could wait until the need actually arises.
