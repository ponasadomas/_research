We use the babyzen yoyo. It folds up very compactly and is 5.8kgs. To close it, you need to bend down, but it can be done with one hand still. If you need something that closes without bending down, then I suggest combi wellcomfort. It closes with one hand from the handle, and is also much cheaper than babyzen (but not so compact).
