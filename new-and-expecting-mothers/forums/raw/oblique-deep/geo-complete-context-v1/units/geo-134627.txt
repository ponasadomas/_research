I think you can get away with a lot more non-pregnancy clothing, as flowy shirts are still in, as are oversized shirts which will look actually nice with a belly. H&M also has sales too in the maternity section and you might be able to find some items for under $200 - I found stuff for $80-100 when I was pregnant.
