Moms of Multiples is still very much active and I am involved myself. We still do bi-weekly playgroups, special coffee mornings and lunches, parties for Halloween, Xmas and Easter. Lunches at the beginning of the year (Sept) and one at the end (June). Membership fees are $350 a year (or $175 until the end of the membership year). We've also done a pregnant with multiples lunch for quite some time but all moms just delivered. However I had 2 other moms mail me (both pregnant) so with 3 or more we'll start them again ... usually on a monthly basis, so please join. You'll find the details on the website, or pm me!
