gpjapan said:

 Why not baby bjorn?

Click to expand...

The Baby Bjorn--at least the model we had--is not very supportive of the baby's hips which can cause mis-development of the back and hips in the baby--not only that the way it's structured can cause back pain for the person carrying the baby. Now that we have an Ergo, any time we try to use the Baby Bjorn we are shocked at how awkward and uncomfortable it is.
