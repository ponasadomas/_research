my boys are 16mths apart, when the 2nd was born i found it easiest to wear the little one and stroller the older one. when the little one got too heavy (around 6 or 7mths), then we started using the phil and teds double stroller which was really good for us, especially for travel - we still use it and our boys are 2yrs and 3.5yrs now. But it really depends where you live and how you get around (by car, taxi, public transport) bc it's really heavy and pain to collapse if you are alone with 2 kids...better now that they are older but when the little one is unable to stand and you have a toddler, I found the phil and teds hard to use but loved it more as the kids got older.

we ended up buying a car when the 2nd was about 1yrs old because we moved away from the city/midlevels and taking the minibus/taxi with 2 toddlers and a stroller was incredibly difficult for me...

good luck!!! : )

My kids are 2 year apart, I use Peg Perego for our 4 month old son and Mclaren for 2.3 year old daughter. 

The baby is usually in the Peg Perego, carry Baby Bijorn for places I can't use Peg Perego so I fold that and carry it in my hand. My daughter is in the Mclaren, when she wants to walk I just fold the Mclaren and hang it on my shoulder or use to carry stuff. When we are out longer and my daughter is sleepy we lay her down in the Peg Perego and put our son in the Baby Bijorn. Use different combinations depending on where we are going
