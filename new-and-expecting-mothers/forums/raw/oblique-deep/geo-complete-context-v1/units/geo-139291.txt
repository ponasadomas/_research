yeah - they're all prefolds, with either press-studs or velcro to do them up. super easy! the one size fits most are slightly 'trickier' as they need a liner & a cover, but they are really cost effective as you just change the size by folding them over once to make them smaller. there are also a variety of press studs to choose from to make them looser or tighter. i've been using the same nappies since she was born (19mths). 

as far as i know, the fuzzibunz only have the all in ones, which are even easier to use, but may require you to buy different sizes. i'm not entirely sure, though.

hope you find some you like.

I recommend the Bum Genius reuseable nappies. Not sure if you can buy in HK but can have a look at the site below:

bumGenius cloth diapers by Cotton Babies

I chose these ones because they are one size adjustable (so you don't have to buy full sets in various different sizes as you have to for some brands). And they have less "moving parts" - only the outer and a washable pad to insert (some brands have 3 pieces which all too hard!)

Our son is 2.3 years and still using the same Bum Genius nappies we bought when he was 6 weeks old!
