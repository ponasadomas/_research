I know of 2 ways to enjoy member prices without paying the membership fee at Eugene Club.

1. If you spend over a certain amount, they give you a 1-year membership for free which you can use immediately. I don't remember the amount. I think it was about 3k. But ask the salespeople about it.

2. If you spend over $300 and use an HSBC credit card, you get 10% off which is often the VIP member price.
