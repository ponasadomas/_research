For strollers you just have to work out how you'll use it.

If you'll be mostly using buses, you need something that folds easily- ideally one handed. 

If you plan to use taxis you need one which can take a car seat.

If you want to use it from birth it needs to recline completely flat.

If you're going to be walking on bumpy streets you should look at ones with good suspension. 

If you will need to go upstairs you should get one that's as light as possible. 

Is your husband going to be pushing it? If so he may have strong opinions about getting a suitably 'manly' pushchair (mine did!) And if you're diferent heights you may need one with adjustable handles.

Once you have your priorities and have set a budget go to a few baby shops and browse. Select a few that seem good for you then go home and read reviews of them. Buggy spotting will take over your life for a while.

For beds it's a matter of what will fit in the space, how much do you want to spend and do you want to go straight to a cot or use a bassinet first.
