The only thing I'd say is, how long do you want baby to be facing you for? My LO was out of his infant car seat pretty early, same with carry cot, so if that was say 5 months or so, would you be completely happy with baby facing out the way? With that budget I'd got for M&P Sola or M&P Urbo.

Baby Jogger are brilliant prams though

Agree with Gilly. I have the Baby Jogger City Mini and it's absolutely fab, especially love the one-handed fold. However, I didn't put my daughter in that till she was 2.

Previous to that I had only recently started having her forward-facing in her pram.

Other Urbo and Sola are both nice prams. I must say I prefer the Skate though. You can get some fantastic offers on those if you can get to an outlet store.
