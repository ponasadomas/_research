The school has very few assets (it turns out the school grounds were leased) so I'm not very optimistic about getting any money back from the liquidators.

We paid the term's fees up front with a significant proportion from childcare vouchers. I was just wondering if anyone knows whether any special conditions apply to childcare vouchers in these circumstances? (a bit like credit cards where you can claim payments back if you feel you haven't been given the service/product you paid for).

I assume this is an unusual situation and couldn't see anything about it or on our childcare provider's website so I'm probably clutching at straws in hoping there might be special circumstances around childcare vouchers but just thought I'd ask!

Thanks in advance.
