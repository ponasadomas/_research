Yes, I would say that it does, as otherwise there is the (unlikely) potential of her being put down to work the weekend prior to MAT leave starting. The MAT B1 gives the EDD which qualifying weeks etc are calculated on. The actual date of delivery will of course will vary from this in the vast majority of cases, but the EDD will not alter by the time in pregnancy when the Mat B1 is issued.
