Some taxi firms will provide a car seat if you request it in advance. You would then need to book your taxi home in advance too of course. Alternatively some small taxi firms will agree to assign you the same driver there and back (again you need to book your taxi home in advance though) and then you take your own car seat and the taxi driver hangs on to it until the homeward journey.

If you are only going to the reception, the other option is to take your own car seat with you and stick it in the cloakroom until you are ready to go home again.
