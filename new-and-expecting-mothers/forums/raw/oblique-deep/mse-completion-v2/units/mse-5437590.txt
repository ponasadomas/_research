Do you mean not enough adult seat belt to hold the child seat in place? That would surprise me, as the adult seat belt needs to be large enough to hold in a substantially tummied adult (well, it does if it's me ...)

We don't have the Graco but have a similar class of seat. On ours the belt goes 'through' the back of the seat (behind the cloth but in front of the frame), out the opposite side, and into the buckle. I do generally have to pull out the belt to its fullest extent to have sufficient 'play' to feed it through; I then kneel on the base of the child seat for weight and to push it back, feeding the slack of the belt back through so that it retracts.

eta. Just a thought - Youtube is often good for fitting videos
