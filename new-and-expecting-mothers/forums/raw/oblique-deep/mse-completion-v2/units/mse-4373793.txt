Pop in a travel mug - you know the insulated ones with the lids? Amazing - you can make a coffee, feed, wind and change baby, and it'll still be warm when you come back to it. They're great for during feeding too, no chance of baby knocking cup over, you can pop cold drinks in them too, and the one I was given was one of my best presents ever. Just make sure the lids are the screw on type, and not the press on ones.

Pop in a few posh tea bags, some nice coffee, or even a bottle of diet coke, and mums happy!

I would scrap the baby shampoo (I had already bought it and it goes a hell of a long way and I didn't get round to using any of the baby toiletries in the hamper a friend did me.

Nappies...to be honest there are so many freebies that in newborn size I had more than enough.

Useful things that got used in my hamper were - baby toothbrush, cotton wool, weaning spoons, bath thermometer, nightlight (still use that nearly two years on!), bath toys, muslins, basic babygrows.

I've done baby hampers before.

Honestly- I would steer clear of bath products, wipes and nappies. Its up to a Mum to choose what brands they want to use- and if you get them something they don't want to use then it will either sit gathering dust or go in the bin.

I think your best bet is to get babygrows and vests, some bibs (funky giraffe do some lovely funky bandana bibs) and mittens/hats. Chances are they both will be inundated with things for newborn and 0-3 so get some bits for when baby is older.

Maybe do a little one for Mum too- some lovely bath bits, favourite chocolate and maybe a gift voucher?

POST Comment_58715961 | reply | author_id=804865 | 2013-01-18T20:24:56+00:00
https://forums.moneysavingexpert.com/discussion/4373793/help-with-a-baby-hamper/p3#Comment_58715961
If you want a cheap idea that looks expensive, muslins take dye very easily - you could do a rainbow set, or just a couple of co-ordinated colours, and tie them with a pretty ribbon.

I wouldn't bother with toiletries other than maybe travel-sized samples, as most last a long time and you don't know if the baby will have sensitive skin etc... Likewise with nappies, some brands might not suit, and you have no idea if the baby will be in newborn size for a couple of months, or like mine, go through the first two sizes in a couple of weeks....

The insulated mug would be great, one that feels nice to drink from would be my idea of heaven. Hand cream for mum, you wash your hands an awful lot with a new baby around, and hands get sore. A lovely colleague of mine gave me a couple of pretty dispensers (you can pick these up very cheaply) filled with some cheap hand cream to put by each sink in the house, I thought that was a lovely, thoughtful and useful gift.

Maybe a couple of baby books? You can usually get nice ones quite cheaply if you keep your eyes open.
