This reply may well be too late now, but I recently did hampers for my friend and her new twins, also one for the father (not so serious... Ear plugs, condoms and bottle of wine!!!)

I got memory boxes for each twin, I knew she was having a boy and girl so I got a pink one and a blue one, put their names on when born and a newspaper from the day they were born plus a magazine from that week (cars for boy as their dad is mechanic, and fashion mag for girl as their mum is very trendy!), also a bag of essentials that I found useful when mine were babies, muslins, vests, snufflebabe, kamillison cream.

I made a hamper for my friend with a travel mug with lid to keep her cuppa warm, hand cream, bubble bath, chocolate. Having said all this I think now the twins are born she would most appreciate an extra pair of hands or a few hours sleep!!!!!

HTH

Big Bright Box with jar of Coffee (& sundae spoon to reach the bottom), assorted teabags (builders, herbal, etc) & several bright mugs - pound shop affordable, bright colours for easy location by visitors. Suggest gently this be sited by the kettle & have a map of "Where to find Milk" taped on one side.

Big calendar to scribble appointments onto

Gift vouchers for local takeaways (include up to date menus)

"IOU"s for two hours twin-minding (in pushchair, out of house, at a soothing pace), a hot meal with spouse (your dining area doubles as a restaurant for them!), "spa session" (your bathroom doubles as a place to have an hour soak & preen...), & (my favourite) "laundry service" - offer to collect, wash, dry & iron a big bag of clothes?

Bottle of Calpol, (with cork & syringe for tiny doses) with poundshop A4 whiteboard & pen so you can jot who had what, when...
