Very quick reply but...do not wait until your Child Benefit has been awarded. Child Tax Credit can now only be backdated for one month so do not delay or else you will loose entitlement.

Ideally you should ring the tax credits helpline now and request a claim form and then as soon as the baby is born, get it filled in and sent off.

There may be a small delay in awarding the claim whilst they verify the child's existence but at least your claim will be in the system and will be backdated to the date of birth.
