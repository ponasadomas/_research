I always found the Asda deals the best. They have £1 delivery slots for late-ish at night. Asda are always doing the two massive boxes for £20-£25 (depending on size of nappies). I usually do this once a month, so essentially it costs me £12 per year to have them delivered. I also bung their own brand wipes in (which are v.good) which takes me over the £25 minimum order.

I don't shop in Asda but the drivers are quite used to just dropping off two big boxes of nappies and 8 packs of wipes!

If the nappies are over £10, isn't delivery from Amazon free anyway?

They are always pushing the Amazon prime- lots of threads on here about it.
