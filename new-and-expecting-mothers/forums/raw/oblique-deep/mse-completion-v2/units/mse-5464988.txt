I used to have a ford focus c-max. Very similar to drive as the standard focus, just a lot more room, a big boot and 5 full doors. I really liked mine, but decided to get a 7 seater once my 3 kids got too big. Now it's time to trade up again, I'm going back to having a Ford. I'm going for a focus grand c-max, which is also 7 seats. I really like the reliability and affordability of Fords.

If you're wanting a newer car, the c-max might be a bit out of your budget. But you can get about an 06-08 plate for around £5k.
