I thought almost all car seats could be used with either ISOFIX or seatbelt? However I understand the ISOFIX fixings aren't the same in every car, so what may fit in a VW Golf, for example, may not fit in a Ford Fiesta using ISOFIX.

:huh:

Isofix is a european standard fixing. Any Isofix seat will fit any isofix car. That's the point of isofix.

Certain seats may be better in certain cars - most extended rear facing seats are very snug in ford focuses and the like. But the fitting is universal. Not all seats have isofix, and not all cars do either.

I would suggest looking into extended rear facing too from birth, such as The Joie as already recommended. Look into extended rear facing, you should keep your baby/child rear facing until at least 4, it is 5 times safer! The Joie seat will last until 4 which saves buying another seat when the baby one is outgrown.

This whole appeal of putting a car seat on a buggy is IMO a terrible idea, a new born baby should be laying flat rather than sitting in a car seat, a baby should not be in a car seat for any longer than 90mins at a time, the curve is not natural for their backs as a newborn.

If you have a local In Car Safety Centre please go there and ask for advice, nothing should be second hand, it may be your most expensive purchase but being in the car is the biggest risk you will put your baby at, make sure they are safe.
