TITLE: How do you explain your strong desire to have the baby sleep with you to your wife?
TAGS: newborn


QUESTION 47011 | owner_id=17171 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
I have the strong desire to house our infants sleeping arrangements in the master bedroom where me and my wife sleep. I'm not talking about in the bed, in a cot next to the bed. I don't exactly know how to explain the reasons why into words. I just have the strong desire to have the baby close to me when I sleep.

Off course I'm not talking about teenagers. I'm Ok for the child to go to his own space as he grows into a toddler, but for the first little while I have the need to have the baby close to me when I sleep. Is this desire natural?

I hope I'm not being selfish and I hope I could discuss this with my wife in an easy way. I keep on seeing people talk about baby monitors for when the baby start crying, but I cannot do it like that. If the baby cries I need to know about it because he should be close to me. I don't know if other people have felt like this?

This has awaken something quite visceral or primal in me. I feel the strong urge to have the baby close to me for about the first year of it's life. I have spent my life in a series of rational academic pursuits but now that parenting is possible I seem to have awaken the lizard part of my brain. I hope some people could relate. I feel like I'm a Vulcan experiencing Pon Farr for the first time.

COMMENT 96662 | owner_id=9327
This may be normal, or it may be hypervigilance. My first spent about 3 months in our bedroom, which meant three months of nearly sleepless nights. For me, as the mother, I awoke at the faintest sound (this is well documented in new moms.) I love the idea of an immediate response to an infant’s needs, but the reality is not every sound needs to be addressed. How to explain it to your wife depends on your wife.
