COMMENT 95180 | owner_id=9327
Have you asked your pediatrician if what you're doing is good for the child? The pediatrician knows the child and knows if the child has special requirements. Not knowing the child's past medical history and possible problems, it's hard to suggest anything different than what your pediatrician has suggested. Why are you not using formula as the base for a smoothie? Also, if you disagree with the pediatrician, is it possible to get a seconf opinion? All these questions matter.
