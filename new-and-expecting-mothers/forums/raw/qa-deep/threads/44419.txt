QUESTION 44419 | owner_id=52487 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
So I have a 3 week old, born 7 days before term, 3.5kg on birth, lost almost 400g in the hospital, then returned to 3.5kg in about 10 days. Since then he has gained an additional 200g, which I fear is not enough.

We are struggling with his eating.
He wakes up about every 3 hours, and I first let him breastfeed for about 30 mins (which is a struggle in itself since he just falls asleep at the boob). After that (because I don't think he eats enough just breastfeeding) I either give him formula or breastmilk that I pumped. That's where the really annoying part begins, because most of the time he just pretends to eat, he will be sucking for 20 mins and when I check the bottle, I see that he has not eaten a single drop. He then eithet falls asleep, or is just nervous and refuses to eat/sleep, which is frustrating both because I worry about his weight gain, bit aldo because most of my pumped milk just goes to the trash.

Please help if you have any advice.

ANSWER 44690 | owner_id=19888 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
He's not pretending to eat. Kids have basically no capacity for deception until around 3-4 years old, so it's very developmentally inappropriate to ascribe such motivations to a newborn.

Instead, he's likely either trying and failing to get milk, or intending to do non-nutritove sucking for comfort. The main way to tell the difference is to observe his reaction, since trying and failing to nurse is really upsetting and frustrating for a baby, while non-nutritive sucking is soothing.

If he's doing non-nutritive sucking, I'd stop offering the bottle and give him a soother instead. If he's trying and failing to get milk, that's more concerning. I'd recommend getting him checked out for a tongue tie or other issue that makes it harder for him to nurse, and also try out different kinds of nipples on the bottles to hopefully find something that works better.
