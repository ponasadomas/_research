TITLE: If I take frozen breast milk and thaw in the fridge, then take it out to warm up on the counter, how long will it last?
TAGS: parents,breastfeeding,milk


QUESTION 42782 | owner_id=43280 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
I know once breast milk is warmed up you need to use it within 2 hours but also know that is with you actually warming it up in hot water. So far I can't find anything about letting it gradually warm up to room temperature without using hot water.


ANSWER 42783 | owner_id=43210 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
The CDC has some useful guidelines on this. If you thaw breast milk in the fridge, you can use it within the next 24 hours. Once it is warmed or brought up to room temperature (no matter the method), you should use it within 2 hours. I hope that helps!
