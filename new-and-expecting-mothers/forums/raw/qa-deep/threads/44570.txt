ANSWER 44581 | owner_id=45224 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
Forget about basic life skills. Sometimes I feel like my children are trying to thwart me in just keeping them alive.

Why, why, why would you stick your hand into a drawer just as I'm closing it?

Why why why would you pick up a thorn bush vine?

Pretty sure that kids have special angels appointed to watch over them because they are just so, so reckless.

There is always some risk in life. Dale Carnegie has a chapter in his book on worrying devoted to looking at the statistics. What are the odds that a child is going to die from giggling with food in her mouth? Has she ever giggled while eating and lived?

There are some kids who are super defiant and hold their breath until they pass out. It's really scary, but the funny thing is - once they're passed out, their body takes over and they start breathing normally.

Worst case, that's what would happen if there was really a giggle attack where she couldn't catch her breath. The situation would resolve on its own.

If your child actually does black out and your husband doesn't seem concerned, that might be an actual problem, but I kind of doubt that you have that kind of issue.
