ANSWER 42297 | owner_id=9825 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
So I did figure something out - it seems like he gets bored when I'm changing him, and begins to fuss as I'm strapping him in.

Handing him a soft, safe toy to play with seems to help - so I've taken to doing that with each changing.


ANSWER 42301 | owner_id=14484 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
I'm a little confused about the "strapping it onto his body". The diaper tabs don't attach to the child, they attach to the front of the diaper.

You may need to check that you're putting it on correctly. The velcro/sticky tabs should not be touching his skin at all, and if they are, I can understand how this would likely be very uncomfortable.

Also check that you're not making it too tight. It just needs to be tight enough that it doesn't fall off.

You could also perhaps try a different brand. We tried various brands until we found one that worked well.

COMMENT 90255 | owner_id=14484
@Zibbobz I'd suggest you check carefully all around the edges of the diaper to ensure there's no rough, plastic type material rubbing uncomfortably against his skin. If a 4 month old is getting distressed by something like this, it seems most likely that something on the diaper is causing physical discomfort. If there's nothing obvious, then I'd suggest you try different diaper brands, to see if this may help. They vary quite a lot, and we found that even some of the most common brands didn't work well at all.
