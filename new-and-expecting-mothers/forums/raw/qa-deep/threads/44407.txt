ANSWER 44478 | owner_id=45689 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
This sounds extremely challenging. You are a super-parent for your patience.

In the short term, I would keep toddler and baby apart as much as possible. Even if that means having another family help you out, or a babysitter, or having them sleeping at different hours. This gives time for your toddler to develop and mature, while keeping your baby safe, and not overburdening your toddler with expectations that are currently above his level. This will also give you time to give him plenty of personal undivided attention which he likely craves.
