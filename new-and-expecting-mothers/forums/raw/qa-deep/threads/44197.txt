ANSWER 44198 | owner_id=42067 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
Make sure your child is drinking plenty in the day, offering milk (breast or otherwise) and water often and refilling their cup/bottle often.

Also make sure that your child's pre-bedtime meal is going to keep them going all night, including protein (animal or plant based), fat and fibre. Maybe swap to brown rice/bread or add a nice thick yoghurt for dessert.

If they do wake up thirsty, and well, we all do sometimes if it's hot or we have a cold for example. A few sips of water and then cuddle back to sleep. I'd keep a suitable sippy cup of water close and keep any wakeups quiet and calm.

I often found we'd get a bad patch of sleep which would last a week or two, maybe coinciding with a growth spurt or a new development in my child, and he'd go back to sleeping better once he was done developing.

COMMENT 94834 | owner_id=39405
I would also consider the timing of the pre-bedtime meal. If dinner is say 2 hours before bed-time plus 10 hours of sleep, then 12 hours without food is a long time for a 14-months old. Maybe add another snack just before bed-time.
