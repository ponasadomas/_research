ANSWER 44531 | owner_id=31496 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
You are faced with a set of conflicting needs. On the one hand, you have a 6yo who wants a certain level of independence. On the other hand, your child's physical stature is such that it matches best with car seats that are developed for babies.

I don't know if there is a car seat that meets all your needs, but I would try to find a shop that are know to have a good knowledge about children's car seats and have a very wide selection. Then go visit them (with Clara) and see what possibilities they offer.

Although I think they are typically aimed at larger children (regular children of about age 3), you might look at a car seat that can be rotated towards the door for getting into. If one of those is an option, given Clara's size, then you could reach a kind of compromise on the freedom part. The seat will have a built-in 5-point harness, so you probably still have to buckle her in, but with the seat rotated (and maybe a couple of step ups) she could get into and out of the seat by herself.

But in the end, do not compromise Clara's safety by putting her into a car seat that is not certified for her posture (length and/or weight).
