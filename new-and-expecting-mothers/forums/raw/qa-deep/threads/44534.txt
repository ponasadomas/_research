We were thinking of taking a family trip to the zoo. Our 6 YO daughter is 1‘5“ tall and 18 pounds due to dwarfism. typically when we are out with her, we carry her around in her car seat when she isn’t walking with us. I was wondering if anyone had any suggestions for when we are at the zoo with her?

Typically when you’re at the zoo you are looking all around, so I wanted to ask what others thought would be better given this. We could bring her car seat, which does have a stroller attachment. We also have a traditional stroller that she can sit in, and we also do have a baby carrier that we can wear. We don’t typically use the baby carrier just because of her age, but this would bring her up a lot higher so she will be able to see over the fences. She would be in sort of swaddled so to speak, in the baby carrier facing us, while I’m supporting. right around our chest level.

I just worry if she is in the stroller or in her car seat that she won’t be able to see above any of the fences. Even in the baby carrier I’m not sure if that would be very easy for her to be moving around in looking left and right etc.

Again typically we carry her in the car seat, with her walking around a little bit when she has the energy and isn’t very crowded. But given that we are at the zoo and she needs to be up a lot higher and also able to move around to look in all different directions what would y’all recommend?

ANSWER 44537 | owner_id=39405 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
I would expect a stroller plus walking when she feels like it plus carrying when needed should work fine. I don't know the particular zoo you plan to go to but any zoo I've been to is designed with small children in mind. For some animals there are fences that you need to be almost adult size to comfortably look over but that is not the typical setup. The vast majority of animals can be seen from the height of a typical child barely big enough to walk. They are a significant part of the target audience of a zoo and so most zoos are build for them.

So I would recommend to just bring the stroller and try it out.
