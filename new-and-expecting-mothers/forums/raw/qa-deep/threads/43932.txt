QUESTION 43932 | owner_id=45472 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
I am a mother with 4.5 year old daughter. I am also expecting a second child and am in the second trimester.
Ever since we shared the news with the daughter, she became much more needy. We talk a lot and treat with respect but nevertheless she needs me more. My husband had to stop reading books to her before sleep because she started rejecting this previously standard practice.

Recently she, who is really calm, started to have anger issues that results in her hitting us with her fists. For example, when we say no to cartoons, she becomes visibly angry and becomes aggressive. We don't punish her for such actions, but ask her to stop hitting us and maybe consider going and drawing her feelings. She most often agrees and after drawing flowers or colouring something, says that she does not know what happened.

A week back she did it in public for the first time leaving us startled.

We think her feelings has something to do with the new baby, however we also cannot dismiss generic anger issues which my husband has (albeit, he dealt with it and my daughter has never seen him angry)

The ask:

What resources (books, studies) could I use to understand what is going on?
How to deal with it? We are against punishment apart from showing that we are upset because it is normal that she has a lot of emotions

ANSWER 44272 | owner_id=25615 | license=CC BY-SA 4.0
She is afraid in a few areas that are all critical to her. Fear of abandonment, fear about how this new stranger will affect her relations with "MY" Mom and Dad. Sibling rivalry may start from point that only child knows there is a new child coming soon. Too young for the 'marketeers' to install the thought; old is old, new is what you want, need, would like or many other thought patterns. Keep as involved as you can with her, let her know how much she is loved and do what you can to allay her fears. The now scary thoughts and especially after new sis or bro comes home. Wrinkly l'il blind thing... Hopefully she'll become the little mom to her new sibling. Andrea (oldest) took to her 'twinzers' very well.
