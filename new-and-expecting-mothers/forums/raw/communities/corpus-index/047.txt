Pregnancy is all about adaptation. It is a temporary state and it's helpful to remember that sometimes. I was completely out of the gym for my entire first trimester and I know I'm going to have to do more modifications now that my belly is getting in the way. I can't lift half of what I used to, and I can't walk as far or as fast. But this won't last forever.
Do what you can and your baby will be just fine! You have set yourself up for success by having healthy habits before pregnancy. Walk when you can, sleep when you need to, eat what works. First trimester is hard because there's no visible change but your body really is going through the wringer. Take it easy on yourself and know that you will come out of this phase eventually.
