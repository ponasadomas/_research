For me fatigue hit HARD first trimester. That coupled with getting out of breath quicker had me scale back on my workouts pretty quickly. Drop the weights down as needed, focus on form over pounds/kilograms. Slow the movements down if you want to make it more difficult. You're not going to set any PRs. But keep it up!!
    And some days, just showing up to the gym is a win 🫠 that's ok
