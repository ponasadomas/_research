I found an app called "Playfully" really helpful to give me ideas. They give 5 simple interactive activities to choose from each day using common things you have around the house (ex: cotton balls), or with just parent and baby. The activities are age-dependent and have really matched where my LO is developmentally. It does cost a few dollars per month, but I've found it worthwhile and money that I'm not spending on toys.

I have a 6 weeks old - partner plays guitar to her which she loves, I walk her around the garden and put leaves, grass ect in her hand to feel, or put a basil leaf under her nose to smell, we play peek a boo, baby massage, float her around in the bath (supporting whole body), we make up songs for her and she spends a lot of time on her baby gym.
I don’t think she really needs that many activities but it’s nice to bond with her!
