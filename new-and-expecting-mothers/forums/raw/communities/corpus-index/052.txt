I’m returning to work next week. I’m feeling both ready for it and nervous about being away from my baby. One thing that has me nervous is that I’ve been exclusively pumping and will continual pump at work.

At work, I will have a lot of flexibility for pumping, but won’t have official pumping/lactation space (I’ll be using a private office). I’m worried about protecting my supply, which has been difficult to build.

What tips, suggestions, things you wish you’d known do you have? Any products or must haves?

[Saturday November 02 2024] [+6 votes]
How long were you on leave? If your supply is established I wouldn’t make yourself sick over your supply. That being said, set a schedule and stick to it.
I had a lot of success pumping in the car on the way to work for my first pump of the day. I would keep spare parts or a manual pump in your desk in case of emergencies. The microwave steam bags work great and Medela makes cleaning wipes.
It will go great!

  [Saturday November 02 2024] [+4 votes]
I was distraught. My husband bought a cheap pump for the night and then like two weeks later I just stoped completely. My supply was miserable from day one so it was a huge loss.
Based on office and horror stories I would also get a lock for whatever you’re storing your milk in if there’s not a dedicated pumping station.
Logistics wise book your calendar and the office space on day 1 of your return for how many weeks out as possible. I had many people book over my time and I was up front that I could be on a call but they would hear pumping sounds in the background. Most stopped double booking me.

[Saturday November 02 2024] [+4 votes]
If you’re in a calendar-centric workplace BLOCK THE TIME! I have 30 minute sessions blocked every 3 hours (3 sessions per day) and stick to them. Nothing / no meeting / no work is more important than feeding your baby.
