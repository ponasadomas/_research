To preface, I just started back at work (healthcare) this week. Also I don't exclusively pump, I also nurse. But I think this could still help so I'll comment.
I feed baby before I leave in the morning, around 6 am. I work 12 hour shifts. I've been pumping at 10 am, 2 pm, and 6 pm. I get home around 9:30 and feed baby when I get home.
Idk if this will be enough to sustain my supply, since I just started this, but so far it seems okay. Oh and I'm at 12 weeks postpartum and haven't had any supply issues or problems so far. I have been lucky.
Edit: I wipe the desk I use with bleach wipes before and after pumping. I immediately put the milk in bags and in the freezer at work. I put the bags in a container though because the freezer surface is kind of gross. If it is several bags, due to the container it doesn't freeze as well, but it stays cool. My pump parts are in a vented plastic container in the fridge. I put it in the vegetable drawer because it gets less use and is out of sight.

I am 15wpp. I work in outpatient healthcare and have my own schedule of patients all day. When I went back to work I told my managers that I would be pumping at 9am, 12pm over lunch and 3pm. So far I’ve stuck to that schedule.
My pumping schedule right now is 30 mins at 5-5:15am, 15-20 mins at 9am, 12pm and 3pm, 20 mins at 6pm and 9:30pm. I’ve recently dropped my MOTN. But I used to try to do the MOTN at 1am, but that kept getting later and later to about 4am, which is why I’ve decided to drop it altogether.
Previous to going back to work though I was never super consistent, I just pumped whenever I remembered - shooting more for at least 6ppd rather than a schedule. And on weekends I still just kind of wing it. I do have an oversupply though, usually pumping around 50 oz a day.
