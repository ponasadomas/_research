After you have the crib together everything else can be done in one weekend.
1. Wash baby clothes/organize baby stuff
2. Sterilize bottles/nipples
3. Prep hospital bag
4. Discuss if you want to split shifts
5. Set up any equipment (sterilizer, baby camera)
