Some weeks I’m working out in some capacity daily, some weeks I’m on the couch 🙃 I’ve definitely slowed my pace, dropped my weight max, and cut out certain exercises (especially core work at this stage). My OB is fine with what I’m doing, the rule (as long as you’re not high risk) is basically anything you were doing before. Join r/fitpregnancy and search for pregnancy fitness routines/coaches on IG if you’re not familiar enough with a variety of movement to build your own program.

I also used to work out 2-3 times a week and first trimester knocked me out for the past 6 weeks. I made it back to the gym yesterday and did a scaled down version of my usual workout (lighter weights, more breaks). I’m super super sore today so my plan is to do what I did yesterday just once a week for a few weeks until my body gets back in the swing of things and then add a second and eventually a third dedicated work out day.
I also did a yoga class a few weeks ago when I wasn’t feeling up to a full workout and it was definitely better than nothing.
It’s good to get back to where you were pre-pregnancy gradually. You can also take up new activity vs pre-pregnancy baseline, just ramp up gradually and don’t push yourself to any extremes.
I’ve found r/fitpregnancy to be a little inspirational. I’ve never been a runner or an elite athlete, but I like the vibes in the community of making a commitment to try to stay fit.

I haven't changed my routine from pre-pregnancy. I weightlift 4 days a week, sometimes 5, and I lift as heavy as I can, gradually increasing the weight when possible. I do back and shoulders 2 days, arms and lower body 2 days. When I have time, I'll do 10 minutes of incline walking on the treadmill at the end of a workout.
I also train martial arts 4 days a week, for 2-3 hours per day.
I've mostly had the energy to keep going--and stuck with it even when I was fatigued in the first trimester--and as long as that's true, I won't stop or scale back.
You should do what you actually enjoy or feel interested in.
