3. My mum did our washing for the first two weeks. She would come every day, take the washing home and return it the next day:
4. A postpartum plan - we sat down two weeks before my ceaser and spoke about what life postpartum would look like while my husband was home with me. Some of the things on there included who would be allowed to visit in the first month, examples of “quick breaks” we could take each (eg. Short shower, walk the dog, cup of tea outside), our priorities (eg. One shared meal a day, if someone else was holding the baby we take the opportunity to hug, husband still goes to football training once a week).
5. We put the house to bed every night - everything gets tidied up and set up for the night shift. Likewise, every morning all the bottles are sterilised, all the nappies are taken out and all the washing is put out.
6. Split the night into shifts. My husband takes the first feed and I take the second feed (we are mixed feeding). This allows both of us to get at least a 4 hour stretch each of sleep at night.
