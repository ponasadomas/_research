[Sunday March 20 2022] [+14 votes]
1SE (1 second every day) app - takes 1 second worth of videos/live photos/ regular photos from each day to make a continuous video diary.  You can also add captions and journal a little too.  It is so easy, you can choose the clip you want or have the app autopopulate it for you.  It’s free, with some in app features you can pay for but I’ve never felt I need to make an additional purchase.  I have a growing video of every day of my baby’s life and I love to watch it every day.  I wish I had known about it earlier so I could have documented my growing belly this way when I was pregnant.

[Friday October 03 2025] [+1 votes]
I’ve been using Qeepsake and honestly love it. It’s kind of like a baby journal but way easier because it texts you prompts and you just reply with a memory, photo, or little milestone. I’m terrible at keeping up with traditional baby books, so this has been the only way I’ve actually kept track of the funny things my kid says and does. It’s nice to look back at everything without feeling guilty about forgetting stuff.
