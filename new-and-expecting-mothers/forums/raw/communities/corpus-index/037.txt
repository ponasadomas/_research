My OB encouraged me to keep up my same routine if that felt okay. I was working out 4-5x week at the gym and peloton cycling before pregnancy. First trimester totally knocked me out but since entering the second trimester I’ve felt much better but much weaker and get taxed much quicker - feel like this could also be due to increased heart rate. I’ve been aiming to do a mile walk at least 5x a week and peloton strength training with prenatal core 3-4x week - this is what’s really working for me right now. I did kickboxing and Muay Thai before being pregnant and I did my first class again on the first day of my 2nd tri. It was great! Definitely modified (no sparring or technical drills where I’m getting hit). I plan to go back 1-2 times per week, instead of my usual 3-5 classes per week. Then also add long walks in between. Hoping to also do some lifting as well and birthing exercises.
