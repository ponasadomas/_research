We were on Huckleberry but all the data it tracks… it proved stressful and ultimately not that helpful for us. We did it for a month then stopped and felt much better afterwards.
Instead  of a specific sleep tracking app we have been using Wonder Weeks which gives you the “gist” ie what you might expect to be happening in terms of developments, sleeping etc for that particular stretch. It’s more context than nitty gritty and it’s been good :-)
