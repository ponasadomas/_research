The kindle version is 99p on Amazon and it gives you a plan of what to feed for the first 30 days. It’s a mix of finger foods and pureed/mashed foods every day (but you could serve however you like!).

Weve just started a couple days ago and im overwhelmed too. But im just trying to take it slow. For the first week im doing one food one meal a day, and this will increase in time to i think 3 meals in a couple months or so. So yesterday she had some carrot purree and day before it was brocoli. Today she will have butternut squash - we had it last night for our supper and i just saved a bit and mashed it up. For the purees i steamed a load of veg, wizzed it down and put in ice cube trays so i dont have to keep making tiny amounts. Our plan is to take bits from what we cook. Of the meal times she doesnt get food but we are eating, i get one of her spoons and dip it into the sauce of our meal so she gets a taster and gets to practice with sittinf up and using the spoon. We got a little baby cup so she can start practising (with alot of help from us of course) to drink water out of that. But majority of her calories is milk still. Feed them to full before you give solids at first.

Totally fair to feel overwhelmed - glad you're posting this! We always like to encourage thinking of what's important to you and your culture and going from there - serving foods that have meaning to you, that you eat often, that you like to share with your family. No need to over-think it too much! You're just working on skill building at first - baby will get the majority of their nutrition and hydration from breastmilk and/or formula until 1 y/o, so you have plenty of time to "get the hang of it" so to speak.
I'd also like to invite you to join our r/SolidStartsCommunity as we aim to help parents on their journeys with starting solids, and I chime in when I can on posts to help answer questions along the way as well! 🤍Marisa (OT/Feeding Therapist/Mama of 3)

Solid Starts has plenty of access while still being free - it can be helpful on how to appropriately serve a specific food.
I was SO overwhelmed starting solids, and I still am at times with mine being 8 months now. My best advice is to not set any expectations - not for you or the baby. I was dead set on BLW, but didn’t feel totally ready so I did a lot more homemade purées but let him feed himself with a spoon and that felt good for me, then we eased into included some handheld foods, and now we rarely do a puree and are pretty strictly BLW. Some days I do two good solid meals for him, other days it’s so hectic we don’t get around to solids - I can’t sweat it or I’ll go crazy.

I’m just here to say you’re not alone, we’ve started this week, and to be honest it’s not as hard as I thought it’ll be. I just serve what we eat, but baby safe of course. I use my hand blender I had at home (kitchenaid) to make a puree or a fork, and serve a wedge/stick as part of BLW.
I downloaded and subscribed for a 7 day trial of Solid Starts and while I’d encourage to have a look and use your own opinion on the app, I wouldn’t be paying for it. I find it useful to have a look how to serve foods, but you can check it out for free on their site (you don’t even need the app). If you’re someone that loves tracking things it’s for you. Their monthly cost is the same as a pro versions of most used AIs out there who can create a download version of the meals and ingredients for you anyway.  Im also not in the US and a lot of foods I just won’t find in Australia. Personal opinion.
Good luck! Have fun! Take photos🥰😅
