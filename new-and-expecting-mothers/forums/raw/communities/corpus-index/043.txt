I think its going to be a bit of a mindset shift. You don't need to go full on. You're not training for another ironman right now. Focus on moving every day. Go for a walk/ light jog, a swim, do some strength training, yoga, etc. Just because you need to keep your heart rate low(er) doesnt mean that you need to stop.
I find that I never get my heart rate up super high when swimming, and I love it, so that would be my choice.

Run slower!! I usually run between 9-10 min pace on easy runs and 7-8 min pace for intervals, tempo, etc and I’ve been doing a lot of 11-13 min miles recently. I’m 12w for context. (A lot being 3-7 miles per week, when I was also doing 20-30 miles a week including trails/elevation pre pregnancy lol)
Also from what I’ve heard, avoiding elevated HR in pregnancy doesn’t have good data behind it, but you being told that by your specific cardiologist definitely gives it more weight.
I’ve also been loving swimming, I feel much more “normal” when swimming than I do running. Most recent swim was average HR of 120 and it absolutely still felt like a good workout.
