For context, I’m a single mom to an 8 month-old boy. We’ve been eating solids (purées) for a good 2 months now, one meal per day. His main source of nutrition is formula. He LOVES everything I give him.

Where I feel overwhelmed is when it’ll come time to start preparing meals for him. And this is where I ask for kind comments because I’m embarrassed to admit how much I hate cooking and meal prepping. I guess social media has me believing that everyone feeds their babies a full plate of food with a variety of food to pick from.

I’m more of a “keep it simple” and “grab and go” kind of person. I hated cooking as a child, never had interest in it, always took the “easy” way out, though I made sure to eat relatively healthy. I’m a huge fan of veggies and salads and grabbing an apple on the go.

But I’m so overwhelmed with feeding my son meals. I don’t really eat meals myself. I don’t sit down 3 times a day and eat a meal. So how do I expect him to? I'll cook big portions of like meat & veg. Then we just decide what side each night and it's a bit easier.

Starting solids and having to do meal planning for your baby is definitely overwhelming. My son is nearing 9 months and I know I'm going to have to go from 2 meals a day to 3, and I don't look forward to it lol. Fortunately, his daycare handles one of the meals, though I still have to prepare and pack it.
I try to do one lighter meal and one heavier meal a day. Here are some lighter meal ideas. Usually I pick a couple together:
1. some kind of mashed/chopped up fruit mixed with plain greek yogurt
2. some kind of mashed/chopped up fruit mixed with oatmeal
2. toast with peanut or almond butter, cut into strips
3. whole fruit like half a kiwi, mango cut into long strips, whole strawberries
4. avocado cut into long pieces (this is hit or miss with my son lol)
5. well done egg cut into long strips (also hit or miss lol)
6. silken tofu (don't have to cook) with a splash of soy sauce + sesame seeds
