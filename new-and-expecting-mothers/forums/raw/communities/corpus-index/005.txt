I’m a 29-year-old first-time mum, and my baby is 6 months old. Saying that the last six months have been hard would be a huge understatement.    
I always knew motherhood would change my life and relationships, but I don’t think I was prepared for just how different everything would feel. I feel like I’ve lost a lot of the people I thought would be around me, and I’m finding the lack of support incredibly difficult.    
\*\*My in-laws\*\*    
I’ve never been particularly close to my in-laws, but our relationship became significantly worse after I gave birth.    
I nearly died after developing sepsis and spent two weeks in intensive care. During that time, while I was literally lying in a hospital bed, my MIL and FIL had an argument with me. My BIL and SIL then sided with them, which made an already horrific situation even worse for both me and my husband.    
They all live together, and my BIL and SIL had their own baby one month after I had mine. I can’t help feeling that, because they had another grandchild living with them, they simply didn’t feel the need to make much effort with my daughter as we don’t live with them.
