[Thursday February 08 2024] [+48 votes]
Check to see if your local library has story time! I try to fill those afternoons with trips to the grocery store, meal preps, or going for a long walk (weather permitting)
Some of those afternoons and on Fridays we like to go to the local rec center’s indoor pool. Kids under 4 are free and an adult membership is only $25/month so even if we don’t go all of the time, it seems worth it.
I try to plan all doctors appointments and social meet-ups in the afternoons to maximize time spent out of the house. This way when we are at home reading books or playing with blocks/toys, it doesn’t feel like all we do.
This has really helped me and hope it helps you too!

> We read books, sing songs, and I try to play little games with him. He has a lot of independent play and doesn’t like being held at all.
Yeah, that’s pretty much it.
We do try to get out of the house once a day for a walk, an errand, or something fun like the library playroom. But it’s a lot of just going from room to room, me watching him play, or him watching me clean.
Sometimes I let her sit in the high chair while I do chores for 5-15 min (sometimes I need to give her puffs to keep her content lol). Sometimes we sit outside on the porch too.
I think overall the key is to mix up activities and try to get out of the house it at all possible!
ETA: I look up “DIY activities [age]” to get ideas sometimes. Like yesterday I just put a bunch of post it notes up for her to pull off. Curious Baby brand also has card books with activities for each age.

We eat breakfast together (or he stares at me while I eat lol), play on the floor together, wash our dishes in the kitchen (he watches/plays with a ladle), more floor play, nap, then we try to go somewhere or something.
