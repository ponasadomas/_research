  So my admin dropped my 7a morning duty so I can pump before the kids come, and a staff member will cover a 30 minute pump break during my 3rd period at 9:30a, and I will pump during prep at 12:00. I can pump hands free and lesson plan at my desk still during prep. My coworker will make copies if I need them.  My admin was extremely supportive thankfully! I was given a mini fridge for my room because a coworker had an extra one.
