I understand a routine might be helpful, but I really wouldn't worry if your baby does not "want" to do whatever is on the routine.
My kid hated tummy time and would really only do it on my chest for 3-4 months. She walked before 1. Other than that, talk to your baby a bunch, sing and read if you want and show them high-contrast objects/pictures. When she was a few months old, watching a helium balloon reflect light and move in the airflow kept her entertained for hours.
I somewhat miss when appropriately stimulating them was so easy!

Kinedu is a great app with daily activities. I paid for the subscription but there's a free version. There's also BabySparks, but I preferred Kinedu. Also, Lullabright is fun, but more expensive. They do a video a week that you watch and learn the songs/activities and then repeat to baby. Each video is tailored to babies corresponding age every week. I think you can get a month free with code 1MONTH
