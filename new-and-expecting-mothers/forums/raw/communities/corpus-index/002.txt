For me, my husband was key. I got help as an aunt and uncle came to stay for a month. But they only took care (by our own decision) of laundry, cooking and taking the trash out. It was a good help, but not a deal breaker.
My husband took his firsts 6 weeks of mandatory paternity leave, and while I would take care of the baby, he would take care of me. He woke up with me every single feed to support me, change baby diaper etc. breastfeeding is HARD and he was key to make it a success. After 6 weeks it was already established so once he got back to work I could start dealing with night feeds alone.
My husband works from home and when he had to travel I just had a friend to come by a few hours so I could have a shower and cook.
If your partner can stay with you and help, the only thing I would highly recommend is a cleaner. You got this!
