I returned to work after having twins. My twins are 9months, not 12 weeks, I shouldn’t complain, but it’s still hard. I work in healthcare as a medical provider and most days I have a very busy schedule seeing patients. I’m supposed to get time to pump, which is scheduled, but I often miss it due to being behind in my schedule. When I get home, I have so much to do, make dinner, eat, feed babies, bath babies, make bottles for the next day, pump again, shower, ect. I never get to bed on time, then the babies inevitably wake up, giving me less sleep. I have a very supportive partner who helps a lot, but I still feel overwhelmed.
Last night I didn’t get much sleep. Today at work I had a breakdown because I didn’t feel like my time was being respected and I felt like I was told to just deal with it. I missed my pumping time, again, because of my schedule. 
I don’t like crying at work or having breakdowns. I really didn’t want to be like this upon my return to work, but I’m just go overwhelmed and tired.
