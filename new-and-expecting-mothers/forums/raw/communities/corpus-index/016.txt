We did purées for a while-starting at 4.5 months. Gradually introduced new things, like cottage cheese and eggs. Daycare started feeding him “real” food around 10 months and that helped us figure out what to feed him at home. Now he eats a lot more, but it took us a while to figure it out. Pasta, hotdishes, cookies veggies, berries, and supplemented with some gerber snacks. We still do some fruit/veggie pouches from Costco and the yogurt pouches. Don’t let anyone shame you/make you feel guilty you aren’t doing enough. It takes time and it’s a process to figure out what food/textures/tastes they like!

I was very overwhelmed at first too. I feel like I felt pressured to start when lo was 6 months bc doctor gave us the go ahead, but tbh she wasn’t really sitting up unassisted yet so starting solids felt forced. Wasn’t until closer to 7 months that it felt right. Now baby is 10 months and we have 3 “meals” a day, but I think it’s more so just getting her used to the routine of sitting in her high chair and eating something. Mornings is yogurt, lunch is whatever I can find, and dinner is usually pasta + some kind of blended up veggie sauce. Yogurt takes no prep time, and dinner I make one pasta meal for her per week so I have it on hand. We just started giving her some of those puffs (breaking it into half or smaller) so she can practice picking stuff up with her fingers.  Idk if this is right as a ftm and I’m not on social media so we’re going for it lol. She was fine at her 9m dr appt so must be doing ok. You’ll get into the swing of things in no time!
