I was just like you when my baby was 6 months old- I felt like overnight we had to suddenly get “real food” into this baby and it all felt so overwhelming; the different foods to try, allergens, purées versus solids… and the thought of having to come up with the meals every day! But guess what- at 6 months they don’t need gourmet Michelin meals three times a day. You can give the same foods over and over again! Baby playing with the food is still progress and learning.
My baby hasn’t also been a big eater. He’s been EBF and hasn’t really taken to a lot of solids yet even now at almost 8 months. But I can see his progress from no interest in any purées, to a few spoonfuls at each meal. To just playing with the food on his tray to actually bringing it to his mouth and trying it. These small steps are still big wins! Sounds like your baby is already doing this with trying the banana and apple!
What I do now is just give baby the same thing several times throughout the week. For example- I bought a cantaloupe and cut it up according to the solid starts app guidelines and he’s gotten cantaloupe every day until I ran out. I also ate with him so he could see me enjoying it! I made up Greek yogurt with blueberries cooked down until they burst and he got that also until I ran out of yogurt and berries! You can just go slowly and it’s OK to give baby the same thing several times over. You’re doing great!

Oh goodness, I’m sorry you’re feeling so anxious. It would feel hard, especially after feeding difficulties previously!
At six months, it really is okay to take it so slow. I actually didn’t even start my daughter on solids till closer to seven months because we were moving and I was too busy to think of feeding her (I also have a toddler). She’s very happy at 8 months with food now.
At six months, you don’t need to think about meals as much yet. You can offer single ingredients things that are soft enough to squish between your fingers or purred. The solid starts app is really helpful for safe sizes per age. Think banana, strawberry, steamed veg, sweet potato, avocado.
