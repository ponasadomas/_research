After just a few days of using the Huckleberry app to track feeds and naps, my 9 week old is sleeping through the night (5-7 hour stretches) when he was previously only doing 2.5-3 hour stretches.

I use the app solely to track and don’t pay for any monthly membership. I quickly realized my baby was not getting enough daytime sleep and adjusting that made all the difference. 10/10 app. Very convenient.

I completely recognize this is not a one size fits all for newborn sleep improvements, but having the visual for when baby last ate/slept/and for how long has made it much easier to address. Sharing in case it helps any other sleep deprived parents out there lol
