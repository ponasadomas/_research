Have you pumped before? Do you know how much baby takes in a bottle around those times? You only want to pump as much as baby actually eats (you can find tables on daily averages per age range online). Maybe you want to pump a little more to start building up a "stash". I would start with pumping when baby normally eats at that time. You're hitting pumping prime time with the early morning pumps, so your supply should be pretty good! See what you get with a "MOTN" pump on your way to work / just before your commute, and again when baby takes their first bottle/ feed of the day. Then you can adjust how often from there. Low output? Pump more often. High output? Stretch it out longer. Just right? Stick to baby's schedule!

It depends on a few factors- can you keep pumping while working (are you in your own office/wearing a wearable pump, etc.) or do you need to schedule time and place to pump? I started with 3 pumps during an 8 hour shift. I made more than my baby needed so I went down to 2 pumps in an 8 hour shift. Lately, my supply dipped so I added the third pump back in and it came back. It's ok to play around! If you're stressed, tired, sick that may effect supply also so you can just adjust accordingly. Going back to work and thinking about making milk feels SO daunting until you get the hang of it. Now it's no biggie for me!
