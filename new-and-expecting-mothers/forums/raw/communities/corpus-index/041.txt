Seconding other commenter who said you can probably do whatever you did pre-pregnancy but would add: don’t feel limited to “pregnancy” versions of things.
As you your belly grows, look into the various ab workouts that are better than others - for example, I think planks can put more pressure on your pelvic floor if not done/modified correctly.
