My midwife actively recommended staying active. It’s good for you and it’s good for the baby. If you used to hit the gym before, she really encouraged to keep it going.
If starting from scratch, she recommended some lower impact sport that would still be super good for you: walking, maternity yoga, maternity pilates, and swimming as much as I wanted.
Parents want the best for us, but many times they have outdated advice. Just say that the advice has changed unless there is a condition that requires you to slow down, and move on.

There has been updated guidance published showing the benefits of exercising in pregnancy! As long as you have no medical conditions then you’re fine to stay or even become more active - you can lift weights, do classes or whatever you feel is correct for your fitness level.
