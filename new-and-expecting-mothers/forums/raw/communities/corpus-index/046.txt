Unless you have extenuating circumstances, most OBs are good with you continuing what you were doing pre-pregnancy. Don't become a marathon runner if you've never run before, but if you've been consistently lifting weights you should be good to continue until you physically feel uncomfortable doing so - or if a health issue occurs. As long as you do not have any complications you can do anything you did before. The only limitations I am aware of is contact sports, fall risks, using the valsalva maneuver during heavy lifts, and lying flat in your back once your uterus puts too much pressure on your arteries.
