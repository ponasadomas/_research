Recently returned to work and need some advice...

I do not work specific hours, it is VERY flexible - start time varies from 5am-9am, it is very dependent on my project work. But with pumping at work, this has made it very difficult to figure out a more consistent schedule. Currently, I am pumping 2 hours after arriving at work, 3 hours after and then one more session 3 hours after that before I head home. The non-shared pumping room is in another building, which requires \~5 minute walk, then I pump \~20m (typically closer to 30m for the 1st one of the day) and then it takes another 5-10m to clean up/get decent and then another 5m walk back to my office area. So I end up spending 35-40m for every pumping session. We do have a room in my building, but it is currently shared part time with a site nurse.

My return to work has already been stressful enough, with very little understanding from manager of ramping back up to full-time and have essentially been thrown back into all projects and playing catch up (different issue).
