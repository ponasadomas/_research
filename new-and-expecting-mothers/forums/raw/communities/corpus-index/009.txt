All the milestones guidelines are calculated to be reached by a baby born at 40 weeks.
So yes, there is a difference if your baby was born before that.
My baby was born at 41+6 and he reached all his milestones 2-4 weeks earlier than the “expected date”.
Think tummy time as a work out, your baby will build strength while doing it, and over time. 2 months in the gym is not enough to be strong, but he is definitely stronger.
And there is a huge difference in comfort from mommy’s chest to the hard wood floor. The floor is difficult, it’s painful on their limbs, on their tummy. Everything hurts.
He is struggling because it’s difficult, not because you did anything wrong.
Get yourself now, down on the floor in tummy time position and see how horrible it is ( I’m serious, try it, it’s super uncomfortable!!!)
I never did tummy time on the floor, my baby was always so uncomfortable, and I was uncomfortable too. Instead he was always on my chest, my bed or on the sofa ( I have a very big, safe and firm flat sofa, those that can turn into a king size bed).
Really don’t worry.

Tummy time is very much over pushed. And plus we don’t realize how much time actually is passive tummy time - being in the carrier, sleeping on your chest. It counts. 30 seconds counts. You still have a tiny baby and lots of time to see them grow. Please don’t be hard on yourself.
