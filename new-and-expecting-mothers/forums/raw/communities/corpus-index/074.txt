I had planned to continue pumping back at work as a teacher and I lasted a week before starting the weaning process. It felt impossible with my schedule and I was spending the little free time I had pumping instead of getting work done and was falling behind on my job. The best decision I made was stopping. Not the advice you’re looking for but just reminding you that it’s ok if you can’t keep up with it at work. Switched to formula and my baby was perfectly

Pump on the way to work, at the 4 and 8 hour mark and on the way home have her feed when she gets home and when she gets up for the day. I have two pumps, my spectra and a wearable, you can use the wearable out on the floor if it’s too busy to sit and pump. Try to get at least one good pump in. Pump wipes if she doesn’t have time to wash parts, spare parts if she doesn’t have time to wash in between. I’ve had a couple times where I only got to pump towards the end of my shift and it really sucks but supply isn’t affected much by a 1 off as long as you keep going after. I have an off brand Stanley and try to refill it 3 times but I’ve drank all of half of it on a busy night and been fine.
