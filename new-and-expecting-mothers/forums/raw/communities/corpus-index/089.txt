  We did something like this too for the first five weeks while my husband was off. Sometimes it was like 10-4 (him) and then 4-9:30 (me).
  We kept the bassinet out in the family room. Whoever was on duty could sleep/doze on the couch while the baby was sleeping and the person in the bedroom got uninterrupted sleep.
  We were combo feeding breastmilk / formula so while I was sleeping, my husband would feed the baby whatever milk was available. My LC recommended not going more than 4ish hours without expressing so I would wake up and hand express and/or use my haakaa for 10 minutes and then go back to sleep.

  Same (but reversed)! Although he can survive on less sleep, so it would often be 10:30-3. Once he went back to work after 4.5 weeks, we were getting longer stretch cycles so he could sleep through about half of his shift (3-9). For example, the parent on shift stayed in the bedroom or nursery with the baby while the other stayed in another room for undisturbed rest. It helped us both feel less overwhelmed and more rested during our shifts. Also, don't forget to plan in some downtime for both of you, even if it’s just a quiet coffee break, it makes a huge difference!
