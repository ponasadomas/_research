Bigger is better at that age. Don't do small pieces until they can pincer grasp around 8 or 9 months old. Just start with soft foods like bananas and boiled veggies. Sweet potatoes. Purees and oatmeal.

  [Monday October 12 2026] [+1 votes]
  People truly don't talk enough about how awesome it is to give large solid "teething" food items at this age.
  My son's first food was a big ol mango pit to gnaw on. He also liked chicken drumsticks with most of the flesh removed. Too big to choke on, big enough that he can maneuver it around his mouth and experience new tastes and textures.
