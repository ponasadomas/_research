It’s only my partner and I for the most part. A good and supportive partner is really all you need.It’s difficult, not gonna sugar coat it. You got this though. Remind yourself that It’s also not going to stay static. Don’t be afraid to hire some help if it gets to be too much while he’s at work…And do your best to take care of yourself the best you can. For me, it’s getting enough sustenance , getting at least five hours of sleep ( work in shifts if you can w your partner at night  ) going for walks and taking my vitamin regimen. Therapy also if needed-/  if it gets to be too much.
You can do this ! Newborn phase is not forever.

Use friends for support if they offer. Maybe someone can meal prep or come over to wash dishes or fold laundry. Just having someone do a chore or watch the baby for an hour or 2 can be so helpful. If it's possible also look into a postpartum doula. They can help with caring for the baby, and supporting you and the household.
