Hi y’all! My baby is about to turn 7 months old and I’m feeling pretty lost and overwhelmed when it comes to solids.

At her 6 month checkup she hadn’t gained any weight in a month, so the pediatrician recommended we start supplementing with formula. I was totally fine with that because I was ready to stop pumping at work, but when I asked her about starting solids she said that we really needed to focus on getting her weight up and not to worry too much about solids.

I’ve tried a few things (oatmeal, applesauce, avocado, and banana) but she hasn’t liked any of them, and we haven’t really kept up with it since we are so focused on her gaining weight. In addition, I’ve been feeling that parental overwhelm of how messy solids are and how little of our evenings we have as is- when we start doing more BLW I feel like our entire night after she goes to sleep will be chores and then bed.

I have time off work for our holiday break and I really want to use it to start solids more seriously, provided she’s doing well at her weight check next Monday (definitely planning on asking her pediatrician more too, just trying to get perspectives from other parents).
