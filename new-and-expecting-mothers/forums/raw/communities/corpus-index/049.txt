I did not use a wearable pump, I think it would have been difficult to get a consistent seal given large (but changing, depending on engorgement) breasts. I found a tip to keep the pumping set up in a clean plastic bag in the fridge (or a lunch box with ice packs, just needs to stay cold) between sessions to avoid having to clean it every time. You have to be really careful about keeping it clean and then of course clean it when home but that lowered the amount of time my pumping breaks took. Also recommend having another set of equipment/extra bags or bottles in case there's an accident. Even being careful, milk is slippery and it's unpleasantly easy to drop stuff.
I hope everything goes well for you! You've got a good set up.

Leave pump at work to not have to drag it around. Im a litigator too so I hated to wash pump parts since I’d rather be billing so I had multiple sets to get through the day and just do one big wash of parts at night. It made pumping at work not so time consuming. Make sure your assistant knows when scheduling meets / calls to not run up to your pump time so you don’t leak or have to be running to pump - mine gives me a 30 min grace period on each side of my pump time.

  My year back on the whole has been great!
  We are still nursing morning and night, but she’s weaned from bottles during the day completely (and only demands a daytime nurse when we are together when she’s teething or sick). I have decent recall of how I got where we are now (without any scary/too fast supply drops or mastitis!). This is probably a TL;DR for some but since you asked:
  I ended up modifying the above plan and started the first few weeks as follows:
  - 6:30/7 am nurse
  - 7:30 am pump at home
  - 10:30 am pump at desk
  - 1:30 pm pump at desk
  - 4:30 pm pump at desk
  - 6:30/7:30 pm bedtime nurse
    -10:30/11 dream feed
  I found after a few weeks I had a pretty significant oversupply, so I was able to drop the first morning pump getting ready at home. I want to say I was able to do that at around 5 months pp/1 month back to work.
