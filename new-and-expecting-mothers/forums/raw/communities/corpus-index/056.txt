[Saturday May 02 2026] [+1 votes]
I’m currently back at work 4 month pp and I pump 3 times a day. Once time in the morning as soon as I get to work and settled at my desk, once during my lunch, and once at the end of day around 4pm when there are no meetings and I’m winding down for the day. I have my own office where I can lock my door.
I actually find that closing my door and pumping gives me some time to catch up on emails and administrative tasks in peace. Some days depending on what’s happening, I may not be able to keep to this exact schedule, but I try my best. But ya, I’m definitely considering combo feeding when I have to go back to work

[Saturday May 02 2026] [+1 votes]
I scheduled pumping breaks on my work calendar and made them appear OOO. I scheduled them as recurring meetings, so when I went back from leave they were basically scheduled for 6 months. They were private, so people couldn’t tell they were for pumping, but the idea was this is a hard conflict and i am unavailable. I did one in the morning, one over noon (also functioned as my lunch), and one in the afternoon.
