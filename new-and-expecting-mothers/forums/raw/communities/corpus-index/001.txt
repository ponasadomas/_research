Give yourself grace, be okay with a mess for a little while, try to prep meals or plan out meals, do grocery pick up or delivery.
You can do it! It can be chaotic the first couple of weeks but then you get into a groove.

The day I got home from the hospital I was already having to get back into the groove of things. I was already ordering groceries, meal planning for the week.
You will be surprised how much you can handle! A village and help is nice but you can do it, I promise.
