I recommend you stay consistent on all days because your body will regulate to the new schedule and as long as you're maintaining your supply even dropping one pump might make a big difference for you. In terms of dropping one of the pumps, extending the time by 15 min should work well and do that every 3-4 days. It doesn't have to be a full week. Try to be flexible so it's not exactly 15 min.
