You are 100% not a failure and wanting to do this and well, is the first (and hardest) step! Be gentle on yourself. You can introduce things everyday, with common allergens it’s easier to do those one at a time to narrow down a reaction. Pick a few things and go! We started with big solid pieces - I think I picked like three fruits and three vegetables each week and just went down the line. (Added in other things too - eggs, yogurt, etccc). Start with one meal a day if that is easiest and ramp up to two and then three. And by meal I mean sit down and hand one piece of broccoli. After a while we moved on to two foods at a time and that turned into actual mixed foods/meals. If you’re trying something messy do it before you give them a bath — we made our first meal dinner for this reason. It’s not hugely important that they’re full on eating right now, it’s more important that they’re given the opportunity to start, to sit down for meals, to explore utensils and cups, and foods and textures! Don’t overthink it, start with one ingredient, one time a day.  Doesn’t sound so bad right? By the time you get the hang of it you’ll be adding more ingredients, combining them, and eventually adding more meals.  The best thing about starting solids for me was that it was gradual all along, it was never too much all at once. Take it one day at a time.  Combining non-allergens from the beginning is fine too. Freezing food is HIGHLY recommended. Purees are absolutely fine and absolutely nutritious!  You got this, mama.

This was so hard for me too! Mine mostly just ate Greek yogurt, oatmeal/ peanut butter, sweet potatoes, and avocado at the beginning (like months 4-8 ish). I’d let him gnaw on a whole banana or kiwi every now and then, but I was really wary of him choking and had a hard time feeling chill until he really started getting/ using his teeth. Something that helped at the beginning was timing his meals Bc it’s such a mess and they really don’t take in a ton of cals until like nine or ten months. So I’d set a timer for like 7 or 10 min and then just cap the activity when the timer went off. This made it much easier for me to tackle meals 2-3 times a day. He’s 13 months now and a really good eater. once I was comfortable with larger foods, steamed carrots, scrambled eggs, and pastas  (ravioli, tortellini) became staples. We also did a lot of toast for a while. Anyway, main take away is that a little but will go a long way. Identify some staples that are super easy and then supplement with things from your plate when it is appropriate/ feels right.
