Babysparks is a really good tool i’ve used to create a plan for activities with my NK. It provides developmentally appropriate activities based on the child’s age and milestones. I’ve also watched some Ms Rachel videos to learn some fun songs/gestures while I interact with my NK.

The Montessori Toddler by Simone Davies was super helpful when i was first starting out with babies!

Resources
Lovevery has instructions and booklets with their toy kits and on their website. They say they’ve done their research and they truly have. They also have a podcast and lots of resources on their site. I’m a fan of intentional play. I don’t work for them, lol.
Kinedu is an app or site that gives daily activities with the why, how, and a video demonstration. I think it tracks milestones as well, but I might be thinking of a different one.
There are a ton of child development classes that are free or low cost. I take some unlimited ones for $89/year. Some are geared towards a group setting, but I learn a lot and stay current with safety and best practices. Parents don’t mind if I’m doing this during down time as it’s more beneficial to the children than cleaning or something to fill the time. I do child cleaning (expected) and dishes or trash (not expected) when they need doing and I have time.
