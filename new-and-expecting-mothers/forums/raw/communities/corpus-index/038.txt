I walked, and would gradually increase the distance. I was getting up to 8km a day (broken into 2-3 walks). This would get me 10-12k steps. It definitely wasn’t easy towards the end and the walks got slower but I did what I could and never took it too seriously.
It was great because pre-pregnancy this was how I lost weight, but saw how walking transitioned for me mentally to become something my body craved for my mental health, and my baby’s health.
Coming out of pregnancy and 2.5 weeks pp I weigh the same as I did pre-pregnancy, my labor was so smooth (albeit a bit early at 35+6), i am able to go on long walks with my baby and husband. And as some one who was always considered overweight I am so proud of myself.
