## About this app

arrow_forward

Pathways.org’s expert-backed app gives parents and healthcare providers everything they need to track and support Baby’s development. Parents can explore milestones like sitting, rolling, talking, and eating, watch real-baby demonstrations, and access 300+ easy activities that help babies reach each milestone. What sets us apart is that we don’t just list milestones, we show parents how to help Baby achieve them.

Available in 21 languages | 40 years of trusted expertise
- Winner of Good Housekeeping Parenting Awards 2026 & 2025
- W3 Awards: Best Mobile App for Family & Kids + Education
- Platinum eHealthcare Award: Best Mobile App

Why Parents & Providers Love It
- Track milestones with real-baby video demos that show you exactly what to look for at every age
- Get daily activities created by experts to help Baby reach milestones as they grow
- Use the Tummy Timer to meet the daily Tummy Time goal for Baby's age
- Stay on top of Baby's routine with simple trackers for diapers and feeding
- Explore videos, checklists, and other expert-reviewed resources to help you support Baby's development
- Share progress with grandparents, caregivers, or healthcare providers

Trusted. Expert-Backed. Free.

- Guided by 70+ pediatric specialists in PT, OT, SLP, and more
- Milestones supported by American Academy of Pediatrics and CDC findings.
- Resources developed and approved by expert pediatric therapists
- A nonprofit organization supporting families since 1985

If your child is missing milestones, consult your healthcare provider. This app does not replace medical care.

Pathways.org protects your privacy and never shares personal information.

© Copyright 2026 Pathways.org – All materials including videos are provided at no cost; no fees or charges may be associated with any of the Pathways.org materials without prior written approval.
