## About this app

arrow_forward

Huckleberry is your all-in-one baby tracker and parenting partner, trusted by 5+ million families worldwide, from your first contraction to your baby's first steps. Our award-winning baby tracker app helps new parents track baby sleep, feeding, and growth, with expert guidance at every stage.

Backed by a team of pediatric experts, Huckleberry supports every family's journey, as a maternity app through the late stages of pregnancy, a newborn tracker in the early days, a baby sleep tracker, and a solid food tracker as your child grows. We turn guesswork into confidence, one stage at a time.

CONTRACTION TIMER
Time contractions with one tap. Track duration, intensity, and frequency. Built for expecting parents preparing for a newborn.

SWEETSPOT®: YOUR SLEEP TIMING COMPANION
A most-loved feature that predicts your baby's ideal nap and sleep times with remarkable accuracy. No more guessing about wake windows, short naps, or tired cues. SweetSpot® learns your child's unique rhythms to suggest optimal sleep schedules.

TRACK GROWTH & DEVELOPMENT
Our baby growth tracker helps you follow your child's development over time. From breastfeeding or bottle feeding to diapers and first foods, our baby tracker gives you peace of mind. See trends at a glance and share them easily with your pediatrician.

BERRY: 24/7 PARENTING GUIDANCE
24/7 instant parenting backup that adapts to what you need, when you need it. Using expert-vetted and contextually aware AI-powered guidance, Berry works through the messiness of parenting with you. From sleep regressions to growth questions to "is this normal?" You always have backup.

STARTING SOLIDS & BABY-LED WEANING
Our baby food tracker helps you confidently introduce first foods, whether it's purees, baby-led weaning (BLW), or both. Allergen tracker: log new foods and note any sensitivities or reactions. Food log: track foods your baby's tried, what they liked (or didn't!), and what to offer next. Serving tips: age-appropriate guidance to safely prepare common foods as purees or for BLW.

FREE APP FEATURES
• Contraction timer for labor prep
• Solid food tracker with allergens, food ideas, and serving tips
• One-touch tracker for sleep, diaper changes, feedings, pumping, growth, milestones, weight, potty training, activities, and medicine
• Complete breastfeeding timer with tracking for both sides
• Sleep summaries, history, and averages
• Track multiple children with individual profiles
• Reminders for medication, feedings, and more
• Sync with multiple caregivers across devices

PLUS MEMBERSHIP
• All free features, and
• SweetSpot®: predicts ideal wake windows and sleep times (2+ months)
• Schedule Creator: age-appropriate sleep schedules
• Insights: data-driven tips and miniplans for sleep, feeding, solids, and growth (0–18 months)
• Enhanced Reports: discover your child's growth, sleep, and feeding trends
• AI Logging: track your day through text, voice, or photo

PREMIUM MEMBERSHIP
• Everything in Plus, and
• Berry: 24/7 guidance with our expert-vetted AI chat
• Custom Sleep Plans: expert-designed sleep training plans, with regular check-ins as your baby grows

GENTLE, EVIDENCE-BASED APPROACH
Our sleep guidance never requires "crying it out." We blend trusted sleep science with gentle, family-centered solutions that respect your parenting style and fit your baby's unique wake windows, growth, and sleep regressions.

AWARD-WINNING RESULTS
Huckleberry ranks as one of the top parenting apps globally. We help families across 179 countries achieve better sleep. Up to 93% of families using our baby sleep solutions report improvement.

Whether you're expecting, tracking your baby's developmental milestones, navigating a sleep regression, starting solids, or raising a toddler, Huckleberry gives your family the tools and guidance to flourish at every stage.
