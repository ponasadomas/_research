    06/17/2021
    Gabby Ciano

    As a new mom almost two years ago, I got swept up in researching baby led weaning while pregnant and decided that’s what we were going to do when it was time for my son. Well, by the time my son was 6 months old and ready in February 2020, I got nervous. How do I cut these things for him? Any foods to avoid? Do I have to introduce 1 food at a time for a certain # of days to help show allergies? It doesn’t all have to be mushy/purée food? Even with looking into baby led weaning, I still had so many questions and though I love my pediatrician, they gave such outdated information that I wasn’t sure where to turn! Thankfully I found SolidStarts on Instagram (right when they first started!) and that was the support and guidance I needed this whole time! Save to say, my son will be turning 2 this august and is an AMAZING eater. Every time we go to a family members house, out to eat at a restaurant, or even random social media posts, everyone says how they are amazed with how well he eats and I have to give a big THANK YOU to Jenny and all of the team at Solid Starts because this mama would’ve been so lost and confused without all of their information and support! I highly recommend not only this app but checking out their Instagram page too. So many helpful tips and advice and seeing real babies with how and what they are eating. Thank you again Solid Starts! I’ve got one HAPPY eater!

    Thank you SO much for this, Gabby! Your story is exactly why we do what we do! So wonderful to hear that your son is a happy eater! xoxo

  • All mamas need this!
    08/13/2021
    MamaChelios

    As a first time mama, I had fed my son too many purees at first and when it was time to feed him solid foods, I wouldn’t feed him much of a variety because he would try something and act like he didn’t like it so I would just think to myself, “Okay. He doesn’t like that. I’m not going to serve it again.” This led to him being picky and not really willing to try new foods. I wouldn’t say that he’s extremely picky, but still.. I knew that I wanted to do something completely different the second time around. As a second time mama, I decided to go with baby led weaning. I knew that I was going to do it, even if I didn’t know how. Right as I was having my second child start solids, I found solid starts. And thank goodness because it helped me so much. I was able to serve him a variety of foods, have him eat what we ate by learning how to cut it and just make simple modifications like not adding salt or sugar. It was slow in the beginning, mostly because I was always afraid of him choking. But they teach you the difference between choking and gagging as well. And as he for better at eating I became more confident in what I was doing as well. I owe it all to solid starts. I would recommend this app to any of my mama friends or family. So great to see my son eat 3 meals a day at only 10 months old and my first son willing to try new things as well.

  • Helpful information horrible functionality
    Feb 20
    uclabecca

    The information this app provides is wonderful. The articles are helpful, the instructions on how to prepare different foods safely give me peace of mind, and the guidance on introducing allergens made it much less scary. BUT, the functionality of this app is absolute trash. When I try to add a meal to the tracker, it always logs it for the following day and I have to manually go in and individually change every ingredient to the correct day—it’s infuriating! The allergen introduction feature also just stopped working for me after pistachio. Usually when you complete an allergen, the next one pops up for you to start, but the app just stopped working once we hit pistachio. I hate the meal planning feature, and I dislike that you need to complete these other modules (like dip and bite) before it will give you instructions on the next allergen. Why can’t I just go into the allergen list, click one, and get the instructions and meals for introducing that allergen?? Logging foods is so annoying that I just stopped using the feature after a few weeks. I will also say this app is very white and has little to no information on many of the Chinese vegetables we consume. Lastly, the recipes on the app are not good and I did not enjoy any of the ones we tried that are designed to be shared by the whole family. TLDR: useful for allergen introduction (as long as the app is working) and for checking how to safely prepare a food (as long as it’s a mainstream food), and not much else.

    Developer Response

    Hi there - we sincerely appreciate you taking the time to send your thoughts. We are always working to improve the app experience and user feedback is so valuable in helping to inform our roadmap of updates. We have passed all of this onto our dev team - thank you again! If you have any other questions/feedback/suggestions, please reach out to us at solidstarts.com/contact - we’re always happy to help 🤍

    I love this app, it has been so helpful in so many ways! My son has a dairy protein allergy and so this app made it easy to keep track of what he had tried for his appointments. I did purchase the 100 dollar add on that goes over the whole plan and 100 foods in 100 days etc and I have to say I looked at it once when I bought it but it was like 200 pages and so I never referred back to it again, with a new baby who had dietary complications I just didn’t have the time to sit down and do it but others may find it helpful! The only reason I gave the app 4 stars is because of his allergy, there is no option to modify. So yes he’s tried bagels, and yes he’s had this and that, that may include dairy but I gave him non dairy options or substituted the dairy and it does not have the option to do that. So it looks like I introduced dairy when I had not. Which is fine at this time but then when I do introduce dairy it will get complicated to remember if “this bagel or that bagel” were the ones with the dairy or not. I wish they had a food allergy option. Maybe prompted questions “this item contains dairy, was a substitute made?” and the parent clicks yes and it tracks that. But overall, I use it daily, I’ve kept track of everything he has eaten since he started eating foods and I love having this tool.

  • So wonderful!!
    07/08/2022
    CarrieMPerez

    I have a 7.5 month old and we’ve been using the starting solids guide for a bout a month now. We started out with lots of resistive foods (chicken drumsticks, mango pits, pineapple cores, etc.), some mashes, and some soft foods that my son could bite pieces off of to further practice chewing and spitting. Oh goodness the gagging was so nerve racking to watch, I hated feeling like my baby was struggling and potentially could choke, but watching stories on the Solid Starts Instagram page and knowing that I had prepared his food properly for his age using this app reassured me to keep pushing through with solids and not be afraid of him gagging - Solid Starts taught me that gagging is part of learning to eat solids and is a protective mechanism (the airway actually closes itself off when baby is gagging, for protection). I have been waiting for things to “click” for my little guy to really engage his chewing skills and not gag as much and last night I gave him a piece of chicken breast (that I was quite nervous about him ripping pieces off of) and he ate it like a champ showing off his freshly learned strong chewing skills and I was so so proud of him!!! It turned out to be the dinner that he has consumed the most so far and the least stressful for me to watch (bc there was significantly less gagging), it was amazing!!! The things I’ve learned from Solid Starts have been the reason I was able to teach my son these skills and to have the confidence as a nervous parent to move forward even when it felt scary! Solid Starts is so wonderful, and this app os extremely helpful, I check it every time before feeding him a new food to make sure I have prepped the size/shape appropriately for his age so that we are as safe as we can be and he can just focus on enjoying his food vs. getting frustrated! ♥️

    Developer Response

    AMAZING! Thank you so much for sharing this! We are so thrilled that Solid Starts has helped you feel less anxious and more confident about feeding your babe! Thank you so much for your support! 😍

    I like the app, but there is room for improvement. Tracking within the app doesn’t always show up in all the different sections of the app. You might track a particular allergen on your “calendar,” showing that the allergen has been introduced, but then it’s listed as “in progress” under the list of allergens (even if you’ve already done the full 1, 2, 3 series introduction for that food). I paid for the app and feel frustrated with all these issues.

Please know we have passed your feedback along to our app team.
