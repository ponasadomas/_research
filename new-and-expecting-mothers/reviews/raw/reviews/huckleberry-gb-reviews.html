  • Truly a life changer
    04/04/2021
    Michele-29

    I’m a first time mum and for the most part, our baby slept well until he his 6 weeks old and literally started fighting all his day naps. He was constantly crying, tired but would never sleep longer than 20 minutes unless I was holding him the entire time. We were tired parents, constantly holding him, wearing him or pushing him in his pram to try get him to sleep and nothing worked he was always upset. He’d end up settling for the night at 10pm sometimes later and me and my fiancé never got time alone. This app was a last resort before giving in to an expensive sleep program and literally from day one, it’s worked.I can’t believe how easy it was but I started recording his sleep and following the suggestion as to when I should settle him for his nap - and it works wonders. I’ve learnt about my baby’s awake times and know his sleepy cues - it’s changed everything. He now naps peacefully during the day in his own crib, wakes up happy and plays happily instead of crying all the time and he consistently settled for the night at 8pm without any problems so me and my fiancé finally get some time to ourselves! This app has given us a happy, well rested baby which results in happy less stressed out parents! Can’t recommend it enough.

  • Not realistic or inline with UK standards.
    3 Jun
    CloHea

    It’s great to begin when he was about 6 months but soon as nap times and schedules really mattered it didn’t help at all. In the UK it’s stated babies at 9 months will sleep around 10 hours a night. Our schedule kept trying to push a 12 hour night. Despite how many times I’ve emailed and said my baby is incapable of 12 hours, it was always ignored and every sleep plan was based around a 12 hour night. A 6:30pm bedtime is way too early. Sleep plans are always based off of ‘a perfect picture’. For example, my baby would wake at 4:30/5am which I raised over a month and the only things suggested I found on google anyway, and didn’t help. The sleep plan is then based off the ‘6am’ rise. But this wasn’t our day…it didn’t help with what to do from 4:30am rising and how to deal with the day etc. I even made it explicit in our sleep analysis questionnaire his rough sleep needs, his wakings, his rising time our issues and it was always a schedule that just wasn’t realistic. It’s a lot of money, and it hasn’t helped us in the slightest. If anything just made things worse whilst we followed the early bedtimes and he woke even earlier! Think the app is more for babies with more predictable or ‘average’ sleep, not when sleep is a bit of a hard spot. I was an exhausted mum super excited for the schedules and advice etc, and in the near 4 months we’ve had it, it only seemed to work for about 2 weeks. Really disappointed and frustrated.

  • So many bugs! Constant lagging - not fixed after so many weeks.
    19/03/2024
    PP146LB

    This is a good app in terms of most of the features & ease of use, but unfortunately, after a few weeks of use, the app has been unbelievably slow. The app started lagging ridiculously - especially if you need to ‘edit’ an entry - it can take several minutes for the app to respond after tapping on edit. This is happening on both my phone & my husband’s phone, so it’s not an issue with the phones & we’ve tested on various wifi, same issue. We close down the app & reopen regularly - issue still persist. We’ve been hoping that the issue resolves itself and I’ve also contacted technical support, they have responded in a timely manner but have never provided any answers & just keep saying they don’t know when the issues will be fixed but their tech team are looking into it. We’ve paid an annual subscription fee to use this app - really is unacceptable for a paid for app to work this poorly. I’m sure all parents would agree that in the middle of the night, when doing a feed or change etc at 4am, the last thing we need is an app that doesn’t respond. We’ve persevered with the app for 10 weeks now (issues started around week 5), as we had hoped issues will be resolved - if it wasn’t for all the data we have already logged since day 1 of our baby’s birth, we would have demanded for a subscription refund & switched over to a different app. So disappointing.

  • Provides structure
    31 Mar
    Benchingbeckham

    I’ve been using huckleberry since the midwife came into the hospital room a couple hours after I gave birth and asked how many nappies my son had and how long I have tried to feed him for. I was so sleep deprived I had no clue. I downloaded the app and began using it immediately. My son is 7-months and I still use it. I try to see it as a tool instead of a hindrance. It helps me predict naps and set myself up for outings, track nappies, especially dirty ones as he goes a few days without pooing and make sure he is breastfeeding enough which has been important for us as he has CMPA, egg and soy allergy. If I need to go to the doctor I can confidently say exactly what has been happening or notice anything out of the ordinary. My partner is on the app too so if I can’t log something, he will which helps it not just be a “me” thing.I also love it because it gives insights to his age, what to expect and celebrates milestones with analytics, like how many hours I’ve breastfed him or how many nappies, which is a good laugh. I think I’ll stop at 1 and then I’ll have a nice gift of analytics to tell him about when he’s older.

  • Such a shame money got the better of them.
    11/06/2021
    DLHeron

    I used to love this app, as a first time mum having everything tracked was amazing. Like many new mums in covid the forced lack of support from others was extremely hard for me. In turn I suffered from PND. This app gave me piece of mind that I could follow the sweet spot settings & if I went off schedule it wasn’t an issue. I went back to work in April as a nanny & it’s been so helpful to track my little girl & the two little ones I look after. Both for my sake, my fiancé’s & the parents I work for. My fiancé is a doctor at our local hospital so is very busy, as am I with 3 children so when he comes to get Penelope there’s not much time to tell him about her day. We both relied upon the app, maybe more then we should have but regardless to that you trust things won’t be taken away from you. I’m deeply disappointed that an app I raved about to all of my friends has become so greedy. It’s one thing to charge for premium settings but to allow people to have these settings & then give a couple of months warning before taking it away. (if you check your emails). Nearly £10 a month for the setting is just greedy. So many parents rely on this & will end up sucked in to the scam. I’ve lost all trust in this app & having used it religiously for 9 months, I’ve now stopped. Who knows what they’ll charge for next. I would strongly suggest not downloading this app.

  • Not to over-exaggerate, but it’s lifechanging
    08/11/2019
    MairiandScott

    Firstly, the free part of this app is excellent for tracking naps and letting you know when baby is likely to need a nap. Our baby isn’t really one for yawning and will go from being fine to cranky in a matter of minutes so we were often leaving him to get overtired. We decided to purchase a subscription because our 3 month (nearly 4 month) old would only feed to sleep and only nap on me after feeding. This meant that as his sleep cycles developed he was waking frequently during the night and wanting to comfort feed. We’d tried the put baby down awake but drowsy thing but our baby never really seemed to be drowsy and would get so upset so we gave up. With our comprehensive plan we could indicate that we would prefer a gradual approach rather than lots of crying and we started our plan last Monday to gradually change his sleep associations at night. 10 nights later and we put baby down last night awake and he settled himself, and did not need to be picked up all night! We can’t believe it- looking forward to working on naps next! Thank you so much huckleberry!

    Developer Response

    Thank you for this review, and we're celebrating with you! Congratulations on this incredible progress! We especially appreciate your story because it dispels a couple of common myths: that babies will just sleep when they want to sleep (not true, unfortunately), and that sleep training means having to cry it out (also not true--we can improve sleep in a gentle way). Thank you for sharing!

  • Mostly fantastic app, couple of frustrating flaws
    17 Jul
    marielno

    I’ve been using Huckleberry on the premium plan for around 2 months now and have found the app so useful. In the early days it helped with midwife & health visitor questions and our own peace of mind tracking feeds and nappies. As we’ve used it more it’s helped us make sense of what to ask for from doctors and what quirks are emerging as our son grows. There are a couple of annoying flaws though! 1) often the app will bug out on my phone (iPhone 17), and the menu & navigation at the base of the screen will disappear. I have to quit and relaunch the app to fix. 2) offline use is buggggggy. I wish the app allowed for local storage of feeds, nappies, sleeps etc that then were uploaded when you reached data. On holiday recently we lost a bunch of data as events we logged without signal were not tracked when we arrived in signal areas. 3) backdating data is A PAIN. When you log an event and click save, the app takes you back to today’s set of logs, instead of the date you’ve been editing. In the holiday instance where we lost data, this meant extra taps after every nappy or sleep or feed we backdated to get back to the day in question.

    Developer Response

    Thank you for your review. We're so glad the app has been helpful! We are aware of the issues you mentioned and are actively working to fix them. We hope to resolve some of them in the upcoming update releases.

  • A good app but…
    11/12/2022
    DeeB1991

    I’m a new mum and found this app really useful to log feeds as my baby has acid reflux and had suspect cows milk allergy. It was also useful to log naps because it would remind me when his next nap would be due however… after a month or two I’ve decided to delete it because I was getting obsessed by getting him down for a nap to coincide with what the app was telling me. The sleep analysis they can do is very helpful in terms of the advice they give based on the sleep logged by you but I’d honestly say that’s as far as it goes. Babies cannot stick to schedules! We as parents go by their schedule and that’s the way it goes, or so I’ve learned. My baby is now nearly six months and we’re only just getting into a routine now but even then if can change depending on his sleep or feeding any given day. Parents, use the app if and only if you don’t see it as a crutch! Otherwise I think it’s more natural to go by your parental instincts. I feel relieved I’m no longer using it and I’m just going with the flow now! At the end of the day, that’s life! An app cannot and should not tell you what to do or how to parent.
