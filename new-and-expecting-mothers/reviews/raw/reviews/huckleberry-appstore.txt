Huckleberry is your all-in-one baby tracker and parenting partner, trusted by 5+ million families worldwide, from your first contraction to your baby's first steps. Our award-winning baby tracker app helps new parents track baby sleep, feeding, and growth with expert guidance at every stage.

Backed by a team of pediatric experts, Huckleberry supports every family's journey, as a maternity app through the late stages of pregnancy, a newborn tracker in the early days, a baby sleep tracker, and solid foods log as your child grows. We turn guesswork into confidence, one stage at a time.

CONTRACTION TIMER
Time contractions with one tap. Track duration, intensity, and frequency. The first tool in your Huckleberry journey, built for expecting parents preparing for a newborn.

SWEETSPOT®: YOUR SLEEP TIMING COMPANION
A most-loved feature that predicts your baby's ideal nap and sleep times with remarkable accuracy. No more guessing about wake windows or watching for tired cues. SweetSpot® learns your child's unique rhythms to suggest optimal sleep schedules.

TRACK GROWTH & DEVELOPMENT
Our baby growth tracker helps you follow your child's development over time. From breastfeeding or bottle feeding to diapers and first foods, our baby tracker gives you peace of mind. See trends at a glance and share them easily with your pediatrician.

BERRY: 24/7 PARENTING GUIDANCE
24/7 instant parenting backup that adapts to what you need, when you need it. Using expert-vetted and contextually aware AI-powered guidance, Berry works through the messiness of parenting with you. From sleep regressions to growth questions to "is this normal?" You always have backup.

STARTING SOLIDS
Confidently introduce new foods, whether it's purees, baby-led weaning, or both. Track allergens and note any sensitivities or reactions. Build a list of foods your baby's tried, what they liked (or didn't!), and get ideas for what to offer next. Get age-appropriate tips to safely serve common foods as purees or baby-led weaning.

FREE APP FEATURES
• Contraction timer for labor prep
• Solids tracking: allergens, reactions, food ideas, and serving tips
• One-touch tracker for sleep, diaper changes, feedings, pumping, growth, milestones, weight, potty training, activities, and medicine
• Complete breastfeeding timer with tracking for both sides
• Sleep summaries, history, and averages
• Track multiple children with individual profiles
• Reminders for medication, feedings, and more
• Sync with multiple caregivers across devices

PLUS MEMBERSHIP

• Insights: data-driven tips and miniplans for sleep, feeding, solids, and growth (0–18 months)
• Enhanced Reports: discover your child's growth, sleep, and feeding trends
• AI Logging: track your day through text, voice, or photo

PREMIUM MEMBERSHIP
• Everything in Plus, and

GENTLE, EVIDENCE-BASED APPROACH
Our sleep guidance never requires "crying it out." We blend trusted sleep science with gentle, family-centered solutions that respect your parenting style and fit your baby's unique wake windows, growth, and sleep regressions.

AWARD-WINNING RESULTS
Huckleberry ranked #1 in the iOS medical category globally. We help families across 179 countries achieve better sleep. Up to 93% of families using our baby sleep solutions report improved sleep patterns.

Whether you're expecting, tracking your newborn's development, navigating a sleep regression, starting solids, or tackling toddler milestones, Huckleberry gives your family the tools and guidance to flourish at every stage.

Lareina Star

I really love this app! I got this app after having my first baby and constantly getting questions by health care professionals. “How many wet diapers has she had?” “When is the last time she ate?” “How long did she sleep?” All very important questions and all very overwhelming after having a baby. The next doctor’s appointment after downloading this app, I simply just showed them the data. After getting past the newborn phase, I felt more confident in my answers but still unsure about other aspects (such as a sleep routine). I continued to use this app like normal after the free trial ended and then around 2 1/2 months, I decided to purchase the membership and I am so glad I did! It gives information about sleep, wake windows, and soooo many other things in an easily readable format. It provides direction and adds flexibility to our schedule. My baby is 4 months now so her wake windows are getting longer. Her sleep signs can be pretty confusing now too. With this app, I know about how much time I have before she’s going to get sleepy and need a nap again. I love that multiple caretakers can use the same account. It has made the transition from one to another super easy because the next person can just look at the data and see how long she’s been asleep or when she’s due for another nap. I also love that you can log multiple things (medicine, solids, diaper changes, etc.) This app has been so great!

### Definitely a good app.

-

-

-

-

-

10/27/2023
sunnysteph_o

This app was recommended to me as a first time Mom with a cryptic pregnancy since I had no time to prepare for my baby, and it is incredibly useful to feel like I don’t have to remember everything when it comes to when I last changed his diaper or fed him, but I will say the one downside to this app, to no fault of its own, is that as a Mom whole is already struggling to balance so many things at once, I’ve found myself obsessing over when I forget to log something immediately or if the app doesn’t layout schedules properly. For an example, it will tell me in one tab how many feedings there have been for the day. In another where I can view the week schedule, it’ll break down my entries and for some reason it has separated one feeding session into two depending on how long our session was, making me think that from the other tab I have fed him more times that I actually have. It’s a bit confusing and I found myself trying to figure out why it has these weird quirks to the point that I looked down at my baby and took a big breath, realizing that it was really not the priority 😆 Once again, an excellent app when it comes to struggling to keep track of so much, but if you find yourself being prone to over stressing about everything, it would give you some peace of mind to try it but also see how you do without it. I’ve enjoyed more just listening to and learning from my baby and his habits naturally and creating my own habits off of his!
