  • Must have app when starting BLW
    04/29/2022
    Nom nom nom59

    This app has been a lifesaver! Im a first time mom who was really interested in the idea of Baby Led Weaning. I tried to get by with whatever free resources I could find online, but still didn’t feel confident or informed enough. I’m so glad I discovered this app through their IG! Do yourself a favor and purchase/subscribe (whichever it is) asap! There is SOO much information on this app that will answer your every question during your BLW journey. And trust, I had sooo many questions! How to prepare and store certain food? How to cut it for my 6 mo? What foods can help with constipation? What’s an easy meal I can whip up with the ingredients I have on hand? This app answers it all!! The only downsides for me are that sometimes when I’m switching between tabs, like food to recipes, it can be kind of laggy. It isn’t as smooth as I’d like and I have to wait a while for the screen to load up. Also I wish there was a better way to easily access the list of foods that bind or loosen stool. Idk, but that list was super helpful to me when my babe got constipated for the first time. It was a pain having to click through so many places to find it. Also, I like the quiz feature. I found it informative, but I wish they told me the answers after! I’d like to know what the actual answers are not just what my score is. Other than those minor things this app is amazing and a must have tool on your BLW journey!

    Developer Response

    Hi! Thank you so much for your comment! We are so glad you found the filters in the free food section useful to help find foods based on nutrients, allergens or also foods that help ease constipation. We are working on making the user experience as smooth as possible with our developers and appreciate your patience with some of the loading times. We are also working on a constipation guide with our Pediatricians that will help with this list on foods that bind or loosen stools. In the meantime the best way to access this information all at once is to either use the filters (the icon with multiple lines in the search bar of the foods section), or in the "questions about the app guide" found in the Starting Solids Guide section. In terms of the quiz feature specifically for the Sugar Quiz at the end there's a button that says "show me the answers to the questions" and a new page comes up talking about the answers. We can also work on adding this feature to some of the other quizzes as well. Thank you so much for your suggestions!

  • App is overall great but food tracker could be improved
    08/30/2023
    Megn21689

    This is my favorite BLW app. Everything you need to know is all in one place. I love that I can click and get all the info I need without having to be redirected to a website or anywhere else. I love the how to serve information which is really detailed and all of the information for each food. The reason I’m giving 4 stars is because the food tracker needs improved. It would be better if all foods were listed on one page either alphabetical or in categories but easy to see together on one page. Some common foods are missing and need to be added as standard - for example, peanuts. Also, it would be nice if it would show a count of how many foods have been tried to date. I pay for this app specifically for the tracker but it’s leaving a lot of be desired so I may be switching to another app. I hope they can take my advice and make so much needed updates because with some changes this is hands down the best app!

    Developer Response

    Hi! We are so glad you are loving the app! We are happy to take this feedback to the team. We recently just added a feature at the bottom of the food tracker so you can add a food that may be culturally relevant to you. We have the food organized by their general categories such as "fruits, veggies etc." Peanut is biologically considered a legume so it is under that category. The idea about showing how many foods you have tried to date is a great idea. We are happy to take this idea to the developer. If you ever have any questions please send us a DM on instagram @blwmealsapp

  • Decent, but needs work
    11/02/2021
    Megan5613

    I appreciate the database of recipes and the tips on how to introduce foods to babies. The meal plan really misses the mark. There's a different person in charge of meal plans each month, but it seems that they just use the current bank of recipes already in the app and combine them to make a "plan". There is very, very little difference month to month and no seasonality. I cannot understand why cilantro lime shrimp and coconut mango chicken are in the November meal plan. Snacks of cucumbers and diced tomatoes. In November. It makes no sense. Same recipes and snacks over and over month to month. I subscribed for several months, but one month is really good enough. If they ever have the guests bring all their own recipes and keep seasonality in mind, it would be a great app.

    Developer Response

    Hi Megan! Thanks so much for the feedback. Our monthly guests do bring their own recipes monthly as we feature them in our "new recipes" section. In terms of seasonality, we completely get it, there are some foods that are more or less available during seasons. This also depends on where you are located in the states and what you have access too. These really are just a guide for you to get inspiration, but we will definitely work to implement your ideas!
