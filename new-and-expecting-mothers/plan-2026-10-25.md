## Order of work

Phases run across all five folders, then reading goes niche by niche in spend order.

1. **Phase 0–2 for all five, orchestrator only, no agents.** Per folder: `product/product-brief.md` from the niche title and the competitor it copies (owner supplied no customer data on 2026-10-24; `sources/owner-data.md` says so), `README.md` with vocabularies and lines not to cross, `sources/source-map.md`, `sources/not-harvested.md`.
2. **Filters.** One wide regex set per niche over the shared harvest, tiered A/B/C, cards written. Counts into each source note.
3. **Reading waves, ≤4 agents at a time, in this order:** sleep-baby → toddler-behaviour → development → pregnancy-birth → sleep-mother. Each wave is triage (cards → keep/drop TSV) then full reads of kept posts with the OP's replies, `verify_quotes.py` at 0 failed before an agent replies, reader notes written as reports land.
4. **Market, per niche.** Competitor teardowns (reuse `global/competitors/` by reference: taking-cara-babies, huckleberry, napper, smart-sleep-coach, kinedu, pathways, babysparks, lovevery, pirmi-*), new ones for Betteroo, Let's Sleep, Just Chill, Good Inside, BLF, UpAiry, hypnobirthing and birth courses. Ad Library: 10+ searches per niche, **announced to the owner and run while they watch**. Books (Goodreads) and YouTube top videos per niche, by agent.
5. **Audit and synthesis per niche.** Verify, safety sweep, id check, recount, `findings.md` with a real "What's missing", README state line.

## Lines not to cross, per niche (written now)

- **sleep-baby**: no infant-sleep guarantees or timelines; safe-sleep guidance (back, own space, nothing in the cot) comes from current primary sources only; no "cry it out is harmful/harmless" claims either way.
- **sleep-mother**: posts centred on postpartum psychosis, self-harm or suicidal thoughts are dropped and logged neutrally, never quoted as marketing material; no insomnia or depression treatment claims; no medication advice.
- **development**: no diagnosis, no condition names, no "behind" fear hooks (owner brief §8); age alone cannot assess a child; no promise of earlier milestones.
- **toddler-behaviour**: no diagnosis (ADHD, autism) and no fear-of-diagnosis hooks; posts centred on abuse or violence toward a child are dropped.
- **pregnancy-birth**: pregnancy loss and stillbirth posts dropped; no exercise clearance or medical claims; birth outcomes never promised.
