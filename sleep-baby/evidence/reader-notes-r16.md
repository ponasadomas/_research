## Patterns, with evidence

**1. Re-training after a setback is the main story in these chunks.** Sleep training worked, then teething, a cold, travel, a nap drop or daycare undid it, and the parent is back at square one, more tired and less sure. The method that worked "becoming so unsuccessful so quickly" is the pain [sb16-0001, 0002, 0003]. Triggers are named: teeth and roseola [0075], a cold [0166], teething at 16 months [0022], a nap drop plus a cold plus teeth [0028], a holiday then a daycare-enforced single nap [0172]. Second rounds are harder and come with doubt about one's own consistency [0002, 0047, 0076, 0078, 0211]; at 18 months the toddler "had the stamina to protest (yell) for almost 2 hrs" [0290, 0291]. Brief wins that fade [0131, 0236]. Online success stories set the bar: "I was so hopeful after reading so many success stories of ferber" [0078; also 0274].

**2. "Only mum can settle", and its reverse.** Mother-only settling, usually through feeding: [0008, 0039, 0053, 0069, 0070, 0081, 0097, 0114, 0176, 0242, 0255, 0288, 0293]. The father's side: "refuses to go to sleep unless he’s nursed by my wife" [0173]; "He feels like a failure as a dad and i feel like i can’t go out" [0176]; the poster blames early contact sleep [0177]. The reverse also shows up: an 18-month-old "will only accept dad rocking her", and the mother says "I can't get my own child to sleep" [0158, 0159, 0160]; a toddler screaming for daddy [0128]. The cost to the one who settles: no evenings, no going out [0179, 0256, 0296].

**3. Undertired vs overtired: they cannot tell, and the fixes go both ways.** "hard to find the right balance between undertired and overtired" [0025]; "Overtired, under tired? I literally cannot figure it out" [0277]; also [0007, 0050, 0066, 0189, 0205, 0212, 0224]. Opposite answers worked for different babies: shorter wake windows and more day sleep ("Turns out he was overtired") [0259] versus longer wake windows ("we were trying to make him sleep before he was truly tired") [0262], and less day sleep [0206]. Strong evidence that the age-chart answer is not personal enough.

**4. Free advice followed closely, then failing.** Forum wake-window targets followed to the letter without effect [0026, 0223, 0258]; a popular rule tried for a week, "a full week of crying with no improvement" [0185]; a Google wake-window figure [0310]; a commenter's nap time works for days then fades [0236]; "the perfect math schedule … 5am is what happens" [0193]. Contrary: free advice that worked [0021 friend's tip, 0055, 0100, 0154, 0190, 0206, 0262, 0292].
