## 1. How long fixes last, and what a relapse looks like (the core question)

Wave 2 said free fixes wear off within a day to two weeks. **My set confirms this for schedule tweaks.** Every follow-up after a wake-window fix shows the first relapse within about a week. Formal training lasted longer, weeks to months, and then broke on a trigger.

- **Schedule fixes from the thread, one day to a week:**
  - "got advice to lengthen wake windows and it worked very well for 3 days. Now we’re back to square one" [sb59-0084]. In the second post a good night ("put himself to sleep immediately, so I’m hoping that stays" [sb59-0094]) lasted one day: "It went to shit after that lol but we did keep the schedule" [sb59-0095].
  - "It worked for a while, we did 2/2.5/2.5/2.75 for a week … but yesterday he was up every hour again" [sb59-0348].
  - Earlier bedtimes "true for a couple of days, then we’re back to several bad nights, then that cycle repeats" [sb59-0356].
