### 7. Pain, reflux, scratching and treatment requirements can make standard settling steps impractical.

**Belongs in:** §§3, 4.5, 6.2, 10. **Relationship:** Extends. Primary evidence allocation: 39 quote records.

**Current conclusion:** «Personalisation is broader than calculating wake windows.»

**What should change:** Spell out the physical incompatibilities: holding upright after feeding can reliably put the baby to sleep before a put-down-awake step; ear pain leads to angled holding in shifts; eczema scratching leads to overnight holding; medicine can trigger gagging, choking or vomiting and require slow administration. Some children remain distressed in arms, so contact is not universally calming either. These accounts explain why superficially similar transfer failures do not necessarily have the same answer. Keep the speaker's attribution of pain, reflux, allergy or developmental difficulty; do not turn it into a verified cause.
