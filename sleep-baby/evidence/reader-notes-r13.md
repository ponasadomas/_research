## 1. The consultant purchase: what was paid, what tipped it, how it landed

**Prices seen.** Under $500 as a wish list ceiling [sb13-0061]; "close to $1,000.00" for a personalized plan, which tipped one mother into doing it herself [sb13-0064]; "$1,000 for 2 weeks of hardcore sleep help" in-home, met with "Are all these paid programs a scam?" [sb13-0170]; "expensive and no guarantee it will work" [sb13-0202]; "on a budget but I'm willing to pay at this point" [sb13-0228]; a condition charity's "sleep scholarship" paid for one (context of sb13-0034). Confirms earlier readers' $550–$1k range and adds the ~$1k "personalized plan" price point.

**What tips the decision.** Not a single bad night but the end of free options: free subreddit advice failed [sb13-0148]; one more attempt "before hiring sleep consultant" (post title; sb13-0089, then hired: sb13-0090); ads for sleep programs follow the parent after they post about sleep [sb13-0168]; "just need a coach to follow there instructions" — the buyer wants to be told, not taught [sb13-0062]; return to work [sb13-0076]. A mother who used to roll her eyes at coaches is now considering one [sb13-0200].

**How it landed — failure stories outnumber praise in these chunks.**
- Consultant gave up: "basically abandoned us after three days" [sb13-0053]; "he probably is not sleep trainable so we cut our contract" [sb13-0054]; "a professional tell me that my baby sucks and can't be helped" [sb13-0057]; "she's never seen a baby cry this long after 7 days so you can imagine how that makes us feel" [sb13-0196].
- Consultant didn't listen / plan not personal (confirms the "standard wake windows" finding): "Kept telling the consultant I thought he needed more wake time but she disagreed" [sb13-0029]; "she kept telling me to keep him awake longer and longer" [sb13-0023]; coach refused a 4th nap and "doesn't believe in overtiredness" [sb13-0121, sb13-0122, sb13-0123]; "insisting I keep waking him up" mid-feed [sb13-0031]; parent rebuilds the schedule on her own judgement after cutting the contract [sb13-0056].
- Quit mid-program: "The next morning I quit the sleep training program" [sb13-0145]; "Things are so much worse after attempting sleep training" [sb13-0146]; not comfortable continuing after the method chosen from the consultant's menu [sb13-0047, sb13-0048].
- Worked partly, expectations gap: bedtime solved but "I expected the payoff to be a little bigger" [sb13-0099, sb13-0102, sb13-0104]; consultant plan relapsed months later [sb13-0092, sb13-0204, sb13-0209, sb13-0230].
- Worked: chair method at 7 months [sb13-0008]; re-training with schedule fix in 5 days [sb13-0216]; second consultant "who listened to me about his tendencies" — sleeping through within a week [sb13-0025]. **The praised consultants are the ones described as listening or calculating from the family's own logs** [sb13-0025, sb13-0192].
- Side effect: consultant framing ("top priority") fed wake-window obsession [sb13-0009, sb13-0010].
- No refund or money-back story appears anywhere in these 125 units; "cut our contract" [sb13-0054] is the nearest.
