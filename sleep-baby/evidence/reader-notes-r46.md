## Contrary evidence
- **Not every relapse needed a method.** Early waking passed on its own (sb46-0409); "with time she just figured it out" (sb46-0441); a father's family "sorted" after a CIO round months later (sb46-0228). Time, not a plan, is the explanation some parents give.
- **Long holds exist** (sb46-0265, -0343, -0372, -0415, -0437), so "fixes wear off within days" is not universal.
- **Some want a mindset, not a fix:** "Stop hoping, just embracing" (sb46-0284); "just wanna know other parents aren't getting a magical bedtime" (sb46-0112); encouragement asks (sb46-0346, -0163).
- **Values cut against any training:** "Sleep training is not recommended and not really encouraged where I live" (sb46-0282); "if it 'works' it's because she's given up hope" (sb46-0392); a friend broke feed-to-sleep and "still has terrible nights, so is it even worth it?" (sb46-0394).
