# Reader notes — R14 (2026-10-25)

## Patterns, with ids

**1. Re-training after setbacks is the main story in this set.** A method worked, then one disruption (illness, a holiday, teething, daycare, taking the pacifier away) undid it. Parents then ask whether to restart, wait, or give up. Ferber undone by COVID [sb14-0139, 0140]; 5 days of holiday "never made a recovery" [0183]; gentle training undone by nursery [0035, 0036]; Ferber undone by daycare, back to rocking "which we haven’t had to do since she was 4 months old" [0058, 0059, 0060]; the pacifier taken away before daycare [0195, 0208]; a two-year-old sleep-trained at 4 months now needs a parent in the room [0181]; Ferber at 4 months, crying again at 8 months [0202, 0203]. Fear that one cuddle night breaks it [0049, 0233], and partners arguing over it [0047, 0192].

**2. Free advice fails parents who follow it closely (confirmed).** "followed the comments and still no luck" [0134]; a commenter's wake windows work on day one and fail on day two [0243, 0244]; wake-window advice turns into a 13-minute nap [0164]; "everything I see related to ST/Ferber says baby slept all night" [0131]; "all I ever seem to read is how low sleep needs babies transitioned with no problem" [0249]; apps' schedules make the day worse [0162]; "anything in the books but nothing changes" [0227]. Contradictory advice set out as a list [0168], and from a midwife against a feeding consultant [0221].

**3. Paid help that was not personal (confirmed, one strong case).** A well-known consultant's nap training at 13 weeks made sleep worse; she "doesn’t know why it’s not working and it must be his CMPA"; "the first one was extortionate" [0135, 0136, 0137]. Mocking the course industry: "you don’t even need to buy my online course" [0026]. A parent asks when she can start with a consultant [0246]. Fear of paying for something that might not work [0128].

## Contrary evidence

- Free advice also worked: a commenter's wake-time fix helped within days [0232]; a reply fixed one parent's bedtime [0185]; day one of new wake windows went well [0243].
- Not everyone rejects crying methods after it fails: some ask how to stand the crying so they can go on [0215].
- Some parents are content with cosleeping [0238, 0067] or contact naps [0010]; the problem there is not the sleep set-up.
- Not found in this set, contrary to earlier readers: **AI/ChatGPT as an adviser (0 units), twins (0), any numeric price (0)**. The only money words: "extortionate" [0137], SNOO "expensive" / "counting every dollar" [0127], "paying for a full day’s worth of childcare" [0075], "while I'm paying her" [0062], "mortgage overpayments" [0206], a layoff [0128].
