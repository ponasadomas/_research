# Reader notes: R12 (keep_core 071–074, 70 units, 220 quotes)

Counts here are counts of quotes I chose, not counts of people. All ids are in `voice/reddit-r12.jsonl`. This slice is late September to early October 2026, mostly r/sleeptrain, with five r/daddit threads written by fathers. It is about everyday sleep problems, not buying: only one paid service appears, and **no money amount is stated anywhere in these 70 units.**

## Patterns earlier readers flagged: confirmed or contradicted

### Free advice fails parents who follow it closely: strongly confirmed
This is the loudest pattern in the slice. Parents follow the sub's wake windows to the letter, it fails, and they come back for more numbers.
- Sub's 10-hours-awake rule for a 4-month-old: "she's miserable like 90% of the day every day" and "I feel cruel because I can't let her cuddle into me" (sb12-0078, -0079).
- "people suggested longer WW's" then "nothing makes a difference" (sb12-0155, -0156).
- Updates that come back with a no: "No😭😭😭 it's still happening" two weeks later (sb12-0112, -0111); "11 days now … still waking up around the same time" (sb12-0167); back to Ferber with longer windows: "screamed bloody murder for an hour and a half" (sb12-0202); "Worked great for a few days then got worse" (sb12-0038).
- Following an app instead: "following the Huckleberry app's timings to the minute" (sb12-0029).
- What the analysing does to them: "mentally exhausted from constantly analyzing wake windows … nothing actually sticks with this baby" (sb12-0193); "constantly googling teething vs. separation anxiety vs. growth spurt" (sb12-0074); "under-tired versus overtired confusion" (sb12-0073); "Sleep deprivation has turned my logical brain to mush" (sb12-0107); a father who doesn't know what "3/3/4" means (sb12-0103).
- Contrary: sub advice did work once for one parent, "we got her on a great two-nap routine", before separation anxiety undid it (sb12-0215). One parent kept things loose and it worked "up to this point" (sb12-0104).

### Asking for a step-by-step plan for "my situation": confirmed
Parents write out the whole day and ask strangers to do the maths. "Can you help me figure out the logistics … It seems like the math just won't work out" (sb12-0068). "How do I know which wake window to stretch?" (sb12-0125). "What wake windows do you recommend at this age?" (sb12-0154). A whole plan sent for checking (sb12-0086). "I wanted to include every piece of the puzzle as I do not know where to start" (sb12-0184). "I know the answer is to sleep train but i need help with strategy" (sb12-0129). "would like some objective advise as to what I'm doing wrong" (sb12-0174). Hour-by-hour schedules pasted in to check the arithmetic (sb12-0114).

### Paid plans that weren't personal / doing it yourself: thin here, but consistent
- The one paid hire: "We hired a sleep consultant when starting Ferber -- that period is up, and she wasn't much help with the night wakes", plus a blanket rule ("not to feed before 2:30am") that the parent breaks when the baby nurses and sleeps at once (sb12-0115, -0116). This matches earlier readers' "support ended" and "one-size rule" complaints.
- Doubt before paying: "willing to look into sleep specialist I am just nervous it won't work" (sb12-0012). The sharpest version of the question: "Did a sleep consultant actually help you when you already felt like you knew and tried all the usual advice?" (sb12-0194). This is the gap a personal plan would fill.
- Course rules that don't fit the baby: TCB's wake-to-feed "I don't even know how that's supposed to work when she's waking so frequently" (sb12-0084). TCB "worked" but they kept the feed "in survival mode" (sb12-0060). TCB Jacky and Moms on Call both in use at 15 weeks (sb12-0004).
- Won't pay for gear: "I don't want to purchase the whole tent thing" (sb12-0127).
- **No consultant prices or course prices in this slice.**
