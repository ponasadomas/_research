## 1. Patterns earlier readers flagged: confirmed or contradicted

**Paid plans that weren't personal: thin here, no direct case.** No one in this slice reports having paid for a consultant or course. What shows up is free consultant content that doesn't fit: an Instagram consultant's free 5-month schedule (The Sleep Concierge) that "seems quite low ... based on what I've read in this sub", with the parent asking Reddit to explain it [sb09-0185]. Consultants come up as a last resort: "I'm even open to hiring a sleep consultant/trainer at this point" [sb09-0042]; "Or is it worth getting a sleep consultant?" after two months of Ferber [sb09-0145].

**Free advice beating paid: confirmed in practice (free thread fixes), never set against a paid option.** Next-day wins from commenters' schedule changes: a 5-month-old's "first time ever" full night after "I adjusted her schedule yesterday like many suggested" [sb09-0182]; nap cut to 30 minutes plus a later bedtime [sb09-0256]; the chair method "tonight and it worked" [sb09-0228]; a schedule tweak on CIO night 11 [sb09-0279]; the husband's first Ferber night after a commenter's push [sb09-0156]; an early riser fixed by "anchoring the nap times and pulling bedtime earlier" [sb09-0206, sb09-0207]. *Contrary:* free advice repeats the same things: "I know I might just get suggestions I've already tried" (`beyondthebumpUK-1whr7pi`), and a parent pushes back with Taking Cara Babies' own numbers against a commenter [sb09-0102].

**A personalized plan as what got a full trial: indirect only.** No one describes a personal plan. The want is clear, though: "I just need a plan that has a reasonable chance of working because I cannot keep doing 12am–4am every single night" [sb09-0291]; "how can I know exactly how much she needs without tracking and uncapping for a week?" [sb09-0121].

**"Low sleep needs" and strangers disputing it: confirmed, with a twist: parents dispute it too.** Applied by the doctor: "perfectly normal and healthy, just extremely low sleep needs" [sb09-0134]; by the parent: "since about 6 weeks old ... an hour or so less than the lowest recommended time" [sb09-0033], "endless energy and low sleep needs" [sb09-0118], two children "both low sleep needs" [sb09-0214], "maxes out on his overnight sleep and we are expecting too much" [sb09-0225]; agreed to reluctantly [sb09-0014].
