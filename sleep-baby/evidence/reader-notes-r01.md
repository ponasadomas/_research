# Reader notes: R01 (keep_purchase 001–008, 141 units, 328 quotes)

Counts here are counts of quotes I chose, not counts of people. All ids are in `voice/reddit-r01.jsonl`. This slice was picked by triage for buying, cancelling or pricing, so it leans heavily towards people who paid for something.

## Patterns

### 1. Consultant hires: what was bought, and how it ended
- **What they bought**, roughly from cheapest to most expensive: an "advisory email" with a sample schedule (sb01-0051), a one-off home visit (sb01-0093), a schedule (sb01-0002), a two-week plan with texts (sb01-0110), multi-week support with paid extensions (sb01-0029), a "14 day boot camp" (sb01-0166, -0167), monthly check-ins to six months (sb01-0328), and a night nanny who charges more to sleep train (sb01-0080). At the top: "thousands of dollars on sleep consultants" (sb01-0239).
- **What tipped it:** not knowing where to start (sb01-0065, -0066, -0043). A health plan paying for it (sb01-0058: "If I had to pay out pocket, I wouldn't have done it", sb01-0060). Being at their limit (sb01-0080, -0269).
- **The commonest complaint is a one-size schedule that is not personal.** "These are her standard wake windows she suggests for all clients" (sb01-0028). Rigid wake windows the consultant won't change (sb01-0059, -0097, -0039, -0248, -0050, -0021). The "sample schedule" (sb01-0051). Sleep capped against the baby's own log (sb01-0054). Over and over, the parent asks the subreddit and gets told the opposite (sb01-0022, -0109, -0037, -0208, -0249).
- **Support ending or turning blaming at the worst moment** is the sharpest consultant pain in this set. "the support is over because of that, and that I basically undid everything … attachment issues" (sb01-0111, -0113). "he likely has FOMO … I can pay for an additional week" (sb01-0029). "'They're stubborn' she said. I wanted to absolutely scream" (sb01-0139). Cancelled the day before, after a four-week wait (sb01-0258). Anxiety-driving deadlines for a 10-week-old (sb01-0312, -0313, -0315).
- **Worth / not worth it:** "She basically parroted what people say on here with a few tweaks" (sb01-0124). "nothing changed" (sb01-0181, -0182). Praise exists but tends to be short-lived: "the best 2 weeks" (sb01-0306), "A good week, back to…" every time (sb01-0201), and a few clear wins (sb01-0053, -0110, -0271, -0223, -0145).
- **What they wished it had done:** fit *their* baby (low sleep needs comes up again and again: sb01-0029, -0106, -0157), stay with them through teething, illness, travel and rolling (sb01-0112, -0246, -0309, -0306), and answer follow-up questions (sb01-0093: "she isnt available for my ongoing questions").

### 2. Courses and books (Taking Cara Babies dominates)
- **Panic purchases at night:** "I panic bought in the middle of the night" (sb01-0055). "my husband panic bought Taking Cara Babies" after a 2-hour cry (sb01-0075). A bootleg copy (sb01-0147). Gifted (sb01-0090, -0178).
- **Works for nights, fails for naps or later:** sb01-0116/-0117, sb01-0213 (newborn and 4-month plans, then hourly wakes), sb01-0131 (never slept through), sb01-0321 (blames the course's "whatever it takes" nap advice for the feed-to-sleep habit), sb01-0300.
- **Course rules that clash with the parent's instinct or with life:** night weaning (sb01-0056, -0296, -0297 "I am planning to abort night weaning"), "feeding every 2–3 hours and limiting naps" at 5 weeks (sb01-0137), no travel for 14–28 days (sb01-0298).
- **Other programmes by name:** Moms on Call (sb01-0040, -0154, -0163), Precious Little Sleep (sb01-0127/-0128 "it feels like CIO with more steps", -0137, -0154), Happy Sleeper / Sleep Wave (sb01-0107), Happy Little Sleeper (sb01-0086/-0087), Possums (sb01-0114, -0187, -0188), 12 Hours by 12 Weeks (sb01-0100), The Sleep Teacher (sb01-0245), Happiest Baby on the Block for $6 (sb01-0090).

### 3. Apps: Huckleberry is both lifeline and source of anxiety
- **Relief:** "keeping me sane … too tired to remember" (sb01-0244). "I don't have to do math" (sb01-0099). Helps a partner take naps (sb01-0057).
- **Anxiety and dependence:** "glued to my house and my phone" (sb01-0168). Quitting it was "a fucking disaster" (sb01-0169). "the added layer of AI is actually adding to the pressure" (sb01-0171). "making me doubt myself" (sb01-0144). "the more exact the data is, the more anxious I get" (sb01-0282, -0279, -0280). Also sb01-0115, -0174, -0250.
- **The app gets it wrong:** glitches, flags "not enough sleep" (sb01-0046), stops working (sb01-0057, -0263, -0067), Napper "gets about things right once a day" (sb01-0071). The plan contradicts the paediatrician on dummies (sb01-0048/-0049).
- **Pricing and trust:** data-selling worries (sb01-0009). Nara Baby going to $6.99/mo or $69.99 lifetime with "ZERO premium features" (sb01-0215), "money hungry monsters" yet "probably going to pay for it" (sb01-0219, -0220), "I really don't want to pay money to track sleep and feeds" (sb01-0222, -0236).

- **Successes, and what turned it:** often a free wake-window change from the sub rather than the paid method (sb01-0005, -0109, -0180); a sudden cot night at six months (sb01-0206); first child with CIO (sb01-0186).

## Contrary evidence
- Some consultant experiences are positive and specific (sb01-0053, -0110 "I loved working with her", -0271, -0145, -0223, -0251).
- Some parents are content with apps and tracking (sb01-0030, -0218).
- The idea that gentler methods are always better is challenged from both sides. A gentle plan turned into cosleeping (sb01-0229). A gentle in-room plan failed for a month (sb01-0182). Yet one parent loved the low-key Possums-like approach with a second child (sb01-0188).
- Personalisation cuts both ways. Parents want a plan built for their baby (sb01-0028), but several also blame temperament ("it's my sons demeanor", sb01-0083, -0188) and wonder if any plan can help.
