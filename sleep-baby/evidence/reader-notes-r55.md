## Contrary evidence

- **Fixes that lasted** (section 1) argue against "free fixes wear off within days". In this set, about half of the late returners reported holds of two weeks to three months.
- **Paid help is not always the failure:** a doctor's blunt instruction worked for three months [sb55-0283] [sb55-0284].
- **Consultants were not always rejected on price:** one family paid about $1,000 and it failed [sb55-0194]. One parent says "I'd pay" with no ceiling [sb55-0425].
- **Some parents want company more than a plan:** "advice or maybe just solidarity" [sb55-0056]; "Any advice (or even just solidarity tbh)" [sb55-0029]; "Having someone to resonate with is so so reassuring" (unit 106, not quoted).
- **The AI pattern is thin here:** one post.
