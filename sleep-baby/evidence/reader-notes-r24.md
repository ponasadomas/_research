## Patterns new or stronger in wave 2

- **Daycare naps are their own problem** (`daycare_naps` 30): "they don't follow schedules period"; carers who end the nap rather than let a child cry; a fixed 1:30 nap; the director who reads tiredness as hunger even after the parent involved the paediatrician; "They don't seem stressed about it. But I am!!!" (sb24-0069, sb24-0127, sb24-0331, sb24-0063, sb24-0065, sb24-0053). Contrary: "she self settles at nursery and when my mum looks after her" (sb24-0300), so the child sleeps for others but not for the parent.
- **Grandparents' rules** (`relative_caregiver_conflict` 20): "crying builds their lungs", "well, babies cry", in-laws who won't put a baby down for naps, "WOW he sleeps *too* much", a grandmother who left her own babies crying all night (sb24-0098, sb24-0099, sb24-0031, sb24-0158, sb24-0125). One conversation fixed it (sb24-0101).
- **Schedule as social cost**: guilt asking friends to move plans around naps; loosening the schedule because "everyone around me looks at me like I'm too much" and it went "TERRIBLEEEE" (sb24-0339, sb24-0325, sb24-0084).
- **Social media guilt**: Instagram reels make it "look so easy, none of it works!!"; "a ridiculous and unnecessary wave of guilt and failure"; UK mother guilty after seeing mums keep babies in their room past 12 months (sb24-0041, sb24-0042, sb24-0296).
- **Night scenes** for copy: "It's 5:30am … I'm just sobbing" (sb24-0290); "it is 2 pm and she has been there since 8 am and has not slept at all" (sb24-0058); "nanny cam … tossing and turning with eyes open for like 2-3 hours" (sb24-0306); dad at 2am told "No. Not you. I want Mommy." (sb24-0336).
- **Content parents who want nothing fixed** (other side): cosleeping mother "I won't stop despite the fact that it means I'm usually somewhat sleep deprived" (sb24-0145); attachment-parenting view that responsive parents "don't get both unlimited cuddles AND a full night's sleep" (sb24-0131).
- **Gatekeeping**: told to post on r/sleeptrain, "they removed my post because … I said I wasn't interested in sleep training" (sb24-0182).

## Contrary evidence worth keeping

- A plan bought from a consultant is sometimes the success story, and the sticking point is the setback afterwards, not the plan (sb24-0030, sb24-0252, sb24-0253).
- Several parents fixed things with no plan at all: a strict nap schedule and longer last window (sb24-0312), a heatwave nap hack with a pram rocker (sb24-0218), a talk with grandma (sb24-0101).
- One parent wanted "success stories about just winging it" (sb24-0085), the opposite of an exact-steps ask.
