## How long fixes last, and what a relapse looks like

The core chunks are mostly relapses after a trained baby, so this set adds more on **relapse after training** than on relapse after a forum tweak.

- **Days:** a sub-style schedule fix held "a few days" [sb44-0028]; CIO gives "2-3 nights" without tears, then "back to square 1" [sb44-0320]; Ferber went well for three nights, then night 5 fell apart after the parents went in early [sb44-0351] [sb44-0352]; ten days of Ferber, fine "after a few days", then 15–30 minutes of crying [sb44-0310].
- **Weeks:** "2.5 weeks into sleep training she started crawling, cut her first tooth the next night and then got a cold" [sb44-0357]; "working beautifully for about three weeks", then one morning off schedule for an interview [sb44-0361] [sb44-0362]; CIO "about 6 weeks ago", then illness [sb44-0378].
- **Months:** trained in November, sick in February, "rough ever since" [sb44-0393]; modified Ferber "so easy" and a floor bed "easy", then relapse at 18 months [sb44-0389] [sb44-0390]; split nights lasted about three weeks and came back at almost 14 months [sb44-0257]; a 4.5-month Ferber that "worked well" eroded with inconsistency [sb44-0334].
- **What set it off:** illness most often [sb44-0378] [sb44-0393] [sb44-0357] [sb44-0096], then teeth and new motor skills [sb44-0347] [sb44-0390], one off-schedule day [sb44-0362], and a parent's one-night lapse [sb44-0396].
- **What parents changed first:** most went back to feeding or nursing to sleep [sb44-0393] [sb44-0396] [sb44-0099] [sb44-0235]. Others stayed in the room or kept a hand on the back [sb44-0358] [sb44-0395], switched to a gentler method [sb44-0352], retrained fully once told to ("I’ve basically gaslit myself into believing she’s sleep trained" [sb44-0379]), or planned to reuse the consultant's plan [sb44-0394]. The second retrain failed for one family: "ferber method didn't work, the chair method didn't work" [sb44-0391].

Against wave 2's "a day to two weeks" (16.2): my set confirms the short end and adds relapses at three weeks, six weeks and three to eight months. The pattern is less "the fix wears off" than "the next illness or milestone breaks it, and the parent's first move is the old prop". These are the few threads with a dated before and after, so none of this is a rate (interpretation).
