### 5. How long fixes last, and what the relapse looks like (the core question)

- **Months:** TCB held about a month, then illness (sb41-0001, sb41-0002); TCB held four months, then 5am wakes (sb41-0122, sb41-0123); Ferber plus a coach held until a stomach bug at about 3–4 months (sb41-0118); Precious Little Sleep held almost two years until the dummy went (sb41-0405, sb41-0406); a Happy Sleeper method held months, then bedtime escapes "6, 7, even 10 times" (sb41-0220, sb41-0221); Moms on Call schedules held until the swaddle stopped or at 16 weeks (sb41-0264, sb41-0244, sb41-0375); a toddler trained and "reliably sleeping 12 hours" until a sudden crib aversion (sb41-0412).
- **What parents changed first** when it broke: feeding back in (sb41-0123, sb41-0155, sb41-0354); rocking (sb41-0463, sb41-0413); being "more responsive" during illness, then fearing they "undid all our progress" (sb41-0154, sb41-0155); a nap retrain reset by a few responsive sick days (sb41-0111); schedule tweaks next — shorter naps, later bedtime (sb41-0124), longer windows (sb41-0421); a break from the programme (sb41-0289); then a retrain question: a parent asking if each illness and vaccination means starting over (sb41-0113, restricted); "Will he remember how to sleep 11/12 hours?" - **One clean recovery with a dated follow-up:** 12 days later, going back to their own pre-training schedule while keeping the consultant's cot settling "made a huge difference" (sb41-0219). Another: the dummy-weaned toddler settled in three nights with a firmer routine from commenters (sb41-0409, sb41-0410).
This matches §16.2: the first relapse usually comes within a day to three weeks; the long holds are courses or books that held for months and then broke at a named event (illness, travel, dummy, swaddle, nap transition).
