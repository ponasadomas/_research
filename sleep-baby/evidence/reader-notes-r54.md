## 1. How long fixes last, and what a relapse looks like (the core question)

Wave 2's "a day to two weeks" holds for *new* training. This set adds many trained babies who held for **months** and then broke, which changes the shape: a relapse is usually not "the method failed" but "it worked, then life happened".
