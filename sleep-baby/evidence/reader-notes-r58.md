## 1. How long fixes last, and what a relapse looks like (the core question)

Wave 2 said fixes from the thread wear off within a day to two weeks. **That holds for schedule tweaks. It does not hold for fixes that changed something other than the clock.** Formal training usually held for weeks to months, then broke on a trigger.

- **Schedule fixes from the thread, one or two days:**
  - A 6-hour last wake window: "Oddly enough he slept through the night last night!" [sb58-0379]. The next day, on the same window: "lol nvm … up from 1145-130 losing his damn mind" [sb58-0380]. Then: "I don't know how to read him anymore" [sb58-0381].
  - An adjusted schedule "worked for a couple days" [sb58-0291], then "I thought we were out of the woods but having a hard time tonight as well" [sb58-0293].
  - A new one-nap day: "one day good, the other day bad" [sb58-0318].
