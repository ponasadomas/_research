## Contrary evidence

- **Some don't want a plan:** "I guess I don’t really need advice cause no one else knows my kid like I do" (sb34-0370); "more to vent" (sb34-0144); "I have posted looking for encouragement in standing firm" (sb34-0113); "I feel so validated that im not doing this alone" (sb34-0162).
- **Content with feeding or contact to sleep:** "I refuse to spend our time together fighting for naps" (sb34-0383); things "started to improve naturally" without training (sb34-0384); "if it ain’t broke, don’t fix it" (sb34-0049).
- **CIO that just worked:** "30mins tops and he's asleep. It's now gotten us to have him sleep through the night." (sb34-0198); gentle training "worked really well" after the 4-month regression (sb34-0200); "the best thing we ever did" (sb34-0130), though that last one needed redoing at age 2.
- **Time, not method:** the PSA, "truly the only thing that has helped is time" (sb34-0252–0258). Against personalisation: a parent who did everything by the book (sb34-0253) and found the schedule wasn't the cause (sb34-0255).
- **Vomiting as the reason to stop: none in my set.** The nearest reasons for stopping were sound and distress ("his cries sounded different", sb34-0117), and hoarseness after 90 minutes (sb34-0259).
- **No night panic-buying.** The purchases were a sleep book for a partner (sb34-0022), carriers, swings and a SNOO before 4 weeks (sb34-0070/0071), blackout curtains during Ferber week (sb34-0309), a baby tent (sb34-0298, restricted), and an attempt to buy a sold-out swaddle (sb34-0271, restricted). One TikTok price-drop bottle post reads like seeded marketing (sb34-0288, tagged `possible_promo`).
