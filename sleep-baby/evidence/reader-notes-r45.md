## 1. How long free fixes lasted, what the relapse looked like, what parents changed first (the wave-3 core question)

Wave 2 said the first relapse usually came within a day to two weeks (findings §16.2). **My set confirms it, and the two-week mark comes up again and again.**

- **Two weeks, then relapse:** a commenter's schedule "worked amazing for 2 weeks and then this past week her sleep has declined again" [sb45-0080]; added awake time "for 2 weeks", then "for the last week, he started waking more times" [sb45-0197] [sb45-0198]; a nap drop "seemed to fix our problem! - **A day to a week:** "Wednesday and Thursday, it was like magic" then "Cut to Friday" [sb45-0325] [sb45-0326]; a 3-nap schedule "did well on it the first two days" then an "overtired spiral" [sb45-0275]; Ferber "went great for 3 or 4 nights" [sb45-0515]; Ferber "worked for about a week" [sb45-0411]; CIO "on the 7th night she started sleeping through a full 11 hours, for about a week" [sb45-0466]; "she was doing so well 4 days ago" [sb45-0241]; "She slept well for a few days and then she’s back" [sb45-0263]; "She slept amazingly last week" [sb45-0487].
- **Held longer:** a retrain at 9 months held "about 3 months" [sb45-0022]; Ferber/CIO at 11.5 months, and six months later "She’s now almost 18 months and sleeps through the night. I think our last regression was when she was 13 months" [sb45-0265]; a travel relapse cleared in two nights and held to September through consistency alone [sb45-0341] [sb45-0342]; a toddler's check-in method "after like a month or so" [sb45-0131]; a regression that passed by itself in three weeks came back "a light version" two months later [sb45-0393]; CIO still holding a month on [sb45-0366].
- **What a relapse looks like, in their words:** early-morning wake, then a 12am wake, then a false start [sb45-0080]; more wakes plus 30–45 minutes awake at 5am and crying at naps [sb45-0198]; 10pm–1am screaming wakes [sb45-0352]; crying back at bedtime after weeks of none [sb45-0090] [sb45-0023]; "I thought I was seeing the light at the end of the tunnel" [sb45-0499].
- **What they changed first: awake time, almost always.** "Yesterday I added 30 mins on to her day" [sb45-0081]; "I added more awake time : 3.5/4/4.5 and capped nights" [sb45-0199]; "we added a half hour to her overall awake time" [sb45-0353]; "Attempting a 3.25/3.5/4.25 day today" [sb45-0358]; "would you add 30 mins evenly throughout her day or just to the last wake window?" [sb45-0117]. This matches wave 2's "add about 10 to 15 minutes" father. **Then the lever runs out:** "I feel like our 2 naps schedule is maxed out with 12 hours awake time" [sb45-0200]; "It just feels like it’s so soon to be stretching them again already" [sb45-0250]; "maybe I pushed it too far" [sb45-0081]. The second change is nap capping [sb45-0319] [sb45-0456] or going back a step [sb45-0276].
- **Restart the old method or start over?** Reusing the old method mostly failed on night one: retrain CIO "up every 2 hours all night" [sb45-0024]; "the same approach we did when sleep training him at 15 months" for a month [sb45-0368]; CIO retrain at 19 months, 1h15 and awake again [sb45-0174]; restart after illness, 50 minutes [sb45-0437]. Parents ask outright: "we’ll likely need to start from scratch" [sb45-0218]; "do we need to “re-Ferber”?" [sb45-0340]; "You’re so right about having to start again, and I really don’t want to." [sb45-0453].
- **The repeating loop, named:** "Every time she cuts a tooth I end up comforting her more during the night (holding, rocking, nursing), and then we’re back to square one." [sb45-0514]; "I’m afraid that I’ve taught my daughter that I won’t stick with any sleep training methods" [sb45-0517]; "one step forward/two steps back" [sb45-0182]; "I thought I had it figured out- ha." [sb45-0457].

So: R22's 2–4 weeks is at the long end again; most first relapses here came in days to two weeks, and the first fix tried is more awake time (interpretation, few follow-ups, not a rate).
