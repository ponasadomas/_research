## Patterns

1. **Expert sources disagree and the parent ends up refereeing them.** Sleep consultants against AI [sb21-0022]. A paid sleep trainer, ChatGPT and "everyone" giving three different answers in one post [sb21-0229, sb21-0230, sb21-0231]. Family against online sources [sb21-0280]. Where a parent names a paid adviser in this set, that adviser is the one who didn't fix it [sb21-0229, sb21-0230].
2. **Free advice wins by giving exact numbers for this baby.** The same move worked several times: a stranger works out the sleep math for one child, the parent tries it that night, and it works. Counting from lights-out instead of sleep onset gave "She slept through!" [sb21-0074, sb21-0075]. 30 minutes more awake time [sb21-0121]. A shorter nap routine [sb21-0210]. "put her down earlier! - so simple" [sb21-0199]. Commenters overrode an app prompt in 15 minutes [sb21-0241, sb21-0242]. "sleep trained thanks to this sub" [sb21-0146]. "grateful for the other posts I read of just do it!" [sb21-0340]. One case of a **paid course passed on for free**: a neighbour handed a father the Taking Cara Babies "ABCs of sleep" document, he followed it "to a tee", and it worked on night one [sb21-0050, sb21-0051, sb21-0052, sb21-0053].
3. **Exact-steps asks.** Parents ask for numbers and routines, not reassurance: "**specific transitional and target schedules** based on a 7 am wake-up" [sb21-0286, sb21-0285]. "Give me practical advice: exactly what is your routine" [sb21-0293]. "Did you have a specific chart you are referencing?" [sb21-0050]. The 5/3/3 method question [sb21-0369]. "How do you start sleep training from ground -0?" [sb21-0347]. Parents log nights minute by minute, as diaries [sb21-0079 Ferber 3am log, sb21-0150 chair night one, sb21-0322, sb21-0328, sb21-0287], and one father writes his up as a bug report [sb21-0246].
4. **Overtired vs undertired, and "sleep math" fatigue.** "under tired and overtired both cause wake ups" [sb21-0019]. "I feel like she’s either under tired or overtired" [sb21-0043]. "how could he be chronically under tired when he gets so little sleep" [sb21-0132]. "globally tired" vs "locally tired" [sb21-0023]. "I’m not following and not quite sure what I’m doing wrong" [sb21-0073]. "If it was that easy I would have figured it out", after 14 months of clocking windows [sb21-0258]. "Very similar WWs … yet completely different results!" [sb21-0287]. "I feel like all the advice on here assumes naps are predictable" [sb21-0099]. Online wake-window rules feed the anxiety [sb21-0036, sb21-0042, sb21-0044].
5. **Success stories set expectations that the parent's own baby then misses.** "3 days and poof it’s great" [sb21-0102, sb21-0105].
