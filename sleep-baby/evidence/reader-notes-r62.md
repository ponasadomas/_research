## Patterns, with ids

### 1. How long fixes last, and what a relapse looks like (the core wave-3 question)
Confirms wave 2's "a day to two weeks", and adds several sleep-training relapses timed to the night:
- **Ferber relapses at a fixed point.** Twelve good nights, then "night 14 and … night 15 she has cried immediately in the cot … 45 mins" (sb62-0285, -0286); the parent looks for the cause in the numbers first: "10h59min yesterday and 10h57min today" (sb62-0287). "exactly 3 weeks after our first Ferber night, she had a horrible bedtime" (sb62-0365, -0366, -0368). Two weeks of Ferber then hourly wakes (sb62-0182). A week of training "unraveled" by the 4-month regression (sb62-0522). Modified Ferber "worked amazing for 3 nights" until illness, then vomiting on every retry (sb62-0208, -0209).
- **Free schedule fixes.** Wore off: a commenter's schedule gave "one wake up the last few days" (sb62-0420), then at 13 days "not really been having the progress I was hoping for" (sb62-0421, -0422). Early-wake fixes "give us a max of 3 days" (sb62-0061). Lengthened windows "didn't help with that for long" (sb62-0101). Cutting solids before bed: "First couple days great! Then she just reverted" (sb62-0113). Rocking-to-eyes-closed worked "for like 2-3 weeks" (sb62-0224). Held at short follow-up: last window of 3 hours "worked like magic" after three days (sb62-0185); "overnight sleep has improved massively" after five days (sb62-0288); later bedtime, no false start next night (sb62-0079); longer windows fixed bedtime but "night wakes are still atrocious" after six days (sb62-0373, -0374).
- **What parents change first when it relapses:** the wake windows and total-sleep arithmetic, almost always (sb62-0287, -0190, -0316, -0338, -0525); then they ask whether the method is wrong for this baby and switch (Ferber → camping method after "a 3 week break", sb62-0132, -0133; CIO → chair method, sb62-0404; chair → full CIO, sb62-0264). The repeating version: "We have had to redo it about once a month for a few days" (sb62-0140); "We have had to sleep train our son twice" (sb62-0239, -0240).
- Paid help relapses the same way: a 5-day residential sleep centre "went out the window when we got home" (sb62-0322); a consultant plus wake-window changes, then acceptance (sb62-0005); an attachment-based consultant's 4-hour meeting, "That night she woke up every 30 minutes" (sb62-0482).
