## Reading this market (interpretation, from the notes above)

Every price point claims personalization, from a two-dropdown tool ($37) and an AI plan (£9.99) to consultant plans ($750, 525 €). The money on Meta sits with courses and Betteroo's quiz app; the two document-only quiz sellers (Dawnly, BedtimeCoach) run no ads. Betteroo and Good Inside both define themselves against "a PDF". The plain age-band PDF already exists as a cheap side product (TCB $39 eBook, Let's Sleep $15 bump, $2.99 charts).
