**Buyer / trigger:** UK mother on maternity leave: nap battles ("the moment you sit down with a hot cup of tea"), hourly wakings, dread of the night, worry about going back to work tired, 2am Googling ("Why was that nap 27 minutes?"). Potty: parent who has "no clue where to begin".

**Mechanism (≤8 words):** Understand sleep, then a step-by-step age plan.

**Promise:** Baby settles more easily and sleeps longer; predictable naps and bedtime; "enjoy your evenings again". 0–5 months: lay foundations so "you may never feel the need to sleep train".

**Proof:** "over 45,000 families", 12–13 years as a consultant, published author, podcast host, mum of three, "award-winning course", Trustpilot badge, star-rated customer quotes in ads.

**Price (from ad copy, 2026-10-24):** Sleep courses "Usually £49", £39 with a code (START10, BABY10). Guide to Potty Training £36, shown at £18 with code POTTY18 ("50% off"). Bundles exist (2024 page); prices not seen.

**Format:** Instant-access online course: videos (captioned, "night mode"), downloads and written sections, no time limit (2024 page).

**Personalization:** None; personalization is by age band (0–5 m, 6–9 m, and others on the site).

**Guarantee:** Not observed.

**Angles and hooks:** Anti-gadget ("Tired parents are an easy target for the next 'miracle' baby product"); "You're not doing anything wrong. You just need a plan that works"; less Googling; maternity-leave clock; no rigid schedules; potty as a second product line from the same sleep brand.

**Objections answered (in ads):** is it sleep training (a customer quote says no, "gentle tips and habits"); rigid schedules (no).

## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

Just Chill is the UK proof that age-banded, generic, instant-access sleep content at £39–49 sells on Meta, and that a sleep brand can open a second product line (potty) at half price to the same audience. Its "less Googling" and "maternity leave" hooks name the same moment Mamivio would sell into.
