## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

TCB sells a 50-page age-band PDF on its own for US$39: the closest thing in this market to a Mamivio report, from the most trusted brand, at roughly Mamivio's 3-month price. It is generic for the band, not built from answers. TCB's ad budget has moved to the newborn entry ($99 bundle) and to a $64 swaddle, which suggests the cheapest acquisition is the parent of a 0–3-month-old who has not yet bought anything. A Mamivio plan aimed at the same newborn moment would compete with TCB's strongest current ads; one aimed at a band TCB leaves to a single course (e.g. 18–36 months, where only the $199 toddler class sits) meets less paid pressure.
