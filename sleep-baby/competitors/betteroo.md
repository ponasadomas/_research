**Buyer / trigger:** The exhausted parent (copy is written to mothers, "postpartum" in testimonial bylines) of a child 0–6, at the moment sleep deprivation is costing them themselves: snapping at the partner, fog, guilt. Quiz triggers listed: returning to work, childcare change, move, trip, new sibling, change of sleep space.

**Mechanism (≤8 words):** Parent-first quiz, then an adapting app plan.

**Promise:** "Get yourself back": the parent's energy and calm first, the child's sleep as the route. Paywall headlines promise "a proven plan" per goal (fewer wakeups, bedtimes, naps, early waking). Ads promise "transform sleep in just 15 minutes a day" and a 28-Day Better Sleep Challenge.

**Proof:** Counts that do not agree with each other: 83,901 parents surveyed / "join 83,901 parents" on the site, "Over 71,000 exhausted parents" in the quiz meta and ads, "10+ Million parents engaged with Betteroo content". Advisory board with credentials (pediatrician, developmental psychologist, PT, OT, LCSW, SLP). Award badges (Family Choice, Parents' Picks, HLTH quarterfinalist). Logos of AAP, ACOG, APA, PSI, NHS, Red Nose shown as "grounded in" guidance, not endorsement. Named-first-name testimonials with outcome numbers. A "State of Parent & Baby Sleep 2026" survey report as content marketing.

**Price:** "Plans start at just $1/day" (USD), anchored against "$500–2,000 that sleep consultants typically charge". Tiers not observed. The paywall code carries "1:1 Expert Human Support · Included with every plan — $150 value", a countdown ("Your discount is reserved for {time} min"), promo codes and a sales-tax line. Creator ads offer 35% and 51% off through referral links.

**Format:** Web app (no app-store download), daily "micro-supports" of 5–15 minutes, tracking, 1:1 human expert chat, unlimited caregiver sharing. Ages newborn to 6.

**Personalization (what is asked, what changes):** Quiz asks child's age (multiple children allowed), parent's own sleep and how they feel on waking, goals, what better sleep would let them do, morning wake time, naps, nap length, how baby falls asleep (feeding to sleep, etc.), time to fall asleep, bedtime, night wakings, what resettles baby, parenting status (partnered, separated, extended family), weekday routine (home full time, working), a block of parent-wellbeing items (drained, depleted, stressed, overwhelmed, guilty, phone as escape), what takes the hit, what is pushing them now, timeline (a date), "anything else" (co-sleeping, reflux, contact naps), then email and name. What changes: the paywall headline, the goal phrase and target date ("How do you feel about {phrase} by {date}?"), the event framing ("Sort sleep before returning to work"). The plan is claimed to keep adapting through the app and human experts.

**Guarantee:** 30-day money-back, "no questions asked", counted from the charge date. The paywall tells every quiz-taker "You qualify for our money-back guarantee".

**Angles and hooks:** Parent-first ("It starts with you"); anti-PDF and anti-template ("No PDF you'll never open", "Generic PDFs with your baby's name inserted aren't personalized plans"); anti-abandonment ("We don't hand you a plan and disappear"); never cry-it-out; event deadline (back to work, new baby); co-sleeping/nursing parent who thought no plan fit; partner/grandparent consistency.

**Objections answered:** tried everything; is it cry-it-out; my baby is different; I should figure this out myself (guilt); can caregivers follow it; I'm barely functioning; what if it doesn't work; how can you be confident.

## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

Betteroo is the funded version of the Mamivio model: quiz → plan → day-by-day program → guarantee, on paid Meta traffic since August 2026. It has already named Mamivio's likely format as the enemy ("PDF you'll never open", "templates as custom plans", "basic personalized apps use your quiz answers once"). A static Mamivio PDF advertised beside it will be read through that frame. Two things it does that a PDF cannot easily copy: ongoing human chat and re-planning when things change. Two things Mamivio could do that Betteroo does not show: a one-time price with no subscription, and a document the parent can hand to a partner or grandparent without an app account (Betteroo answers this with caregiver links). Its quiz spends a third of its questions on the mother's own state: that is a tested way to make the quiz feel like it is about her, and worth borrowing as structure, not wording.

## Quiz walk, 2026-10-25 (owner watching) — interpretation

Full screen-by-screen text: `../_raw/competitors/betteroo-quiz-walk/walk-2026-10-25.md` (48 screens, stopped at the email gate).

- **Structure:** about 30 questions in four sections — "My Profile" (parent's age first, number and age of children, *the parent's own* sleep and how she wakes), "Sleep Habits" (wake time, naps, sleep associations, bedtime, wakings, resettling), "Lifestyle & Habits" (parenting status, work setup, drained/depleted/stressed/overwhelmed, phone as escape, guilt, what suffers), "Motivation" (what's pushing now, what better sleep looks like, life events, a target date, baby's birth date, free text) — then a loading screen, a result, testimonials and the email gate.
- **How it makes "personalized" visible:** answers are echoed back verbatim in interstitials ("You want to reduce those endless wakeups so you can get yourself back"; "managing full-time work"); the hero image changes with the parent's age bracket from the first question; the result names the parent's own target date.
- **The parent-first angle is the spine:** roughly a third of the questions are about the mother's own sleep, mood, guilt, work, partner and social life — the Betteroo "get yourself back" angle, built into the quiz, matching `../../sleep-mother/synthesis/findings.md`.
- **It answers objections before they're raised:** "You don't need to cry it out!" right after the sleep-aids question; "The #1 reason sleep plans fail is they're too rigid for real life" during loading; "Built for your real life, not a generic sleep plan".
- **Scope beyond sleep:** asks what else to explore — baby's development, nutrition, the parent's well-being, emotions — i.e. a cross-niche programme.
- **Claims Mamivio cannot copy:** "We predict you'll hit 0 night wakeups by Nov 25" (a dated outcome promise); "Stanford Sleep Medicine Center reports … up to 70%" (unverified attribution); "77,031+ moms", award badges and "As seen on" logos (unverified).
- **Comparison with Mamivio:** the quiz mechanics Mamivio needs are all here (age, real day, household, values on crying, echoed answers, a parent section). The difference Mamivio can own is a one-time document plan without the dated promise, and with an easy refund — the two things reviewers punish most in apps (see `../synthesis/findings.md` §15).

### Past the email gate — the price (2026-10-25)

- **It is a subscription:** "€29.00 now, then €44.62 every 4 weeks from 22 Nov 2026. Cancel anytime." (Paddle checkout). The offer page shows 1-Week Trial €26.77, 4-Week Plan €29.00 ("MOST POPULAR"), 12-Week Plan €69.61, all as "35% OFF" behind a 10-minute countdown. So "from $1/day" is the discounted first period of a recurring charge, about €580 a year at the full rate.
- **What is sold is a person, not a document:** "1:1 Expert Human Support ($150 Value!)" on every tier, a seven-person expert panel, daily 5–15-minute check-ins in an app, "Unlimited caregiver sharing".
- **The offer page answers the objections the Reddit material raises:** "built for a textbook baby and a perfect parent — not real life"; "If it feels wrong, we don't do it"; "Basic “personalized” apps use your quiz answers once" (a dig at one-off plans like Mamivio's); partner/caregiver sharing.
- **Claims outside Mamivio's lines:** "Guaranteed results — A proven plan to reach 0 night wakeups", "Get real results in 4 weeks", testimonials of 11-hour nights "on night two".
- **For Mamivio:** the clearest contrast in the market. Betteroo = recurring €44.62 / 4 weeks for app + human chat with a guarantee; Mamivio = one price, once, a document. Reviewers' subscription anger (`../synthesis/findings.md` §15) is the opening; Betteroo's "quiz answers once" line is the objection Mamivio's copy must answer (the plan includes what to do when things change).
