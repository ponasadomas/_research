**Buyer / trigger:** Parent of a 0–12-month-old "waking again and again" who has already searched online and tried routines.

**Mechanism (≤8 words):** Questionnaire answers, AI-generated 7-day plan.

**Promise:** A clear plan for the next seven days, built around this baby, "not the 'average' baby". No outcome promised: "We do not guarantee particular sleep outcomes."

**Proof:** None yet (launch); "evidence-informed", "safer-sleep conscious".

**Price:** £9.99 one-off, no subscription, labelled a launch price that "may increase".

**Format:** Digital plan by email: the baby's "current sleep picture", focus areas, 7-day action plan, bedtime/settling/night-waking guidance, feeding and day-sleep notes, safer-sleep guidance and what to do after day 7.

**Personalization (what is asked, what changes):** After purchase, a questionnaire on age, sleep, settling, naps, feeding, what has been tried, and preferences (including comfort with settling or sleep-training approaches). Answers are processed with AI inside the seller's "evidence, safety and decision-making framework". The example given: a 7-month-old needing 40 minutes of rocking vs a 6-week-old waking to feed get different advice.

**Guarantee:** Refund of £9.99 if the plan is not "useful", within 14 days of receiving it; no need to prove sleep did not improve. Buyer may be asked to waive the statutory cancellation period.

**Angles and hooks:** "Tired of guessing what to try"; anti-generic; low-risk price; parent preferences respected; medical causes take priority (disclaimer).

**Objections answered (FAQ titles; answers collapsed in the capture):** age suitability; will you tell me to leave my baby to cry; will you tell me to stop night feeds; how personalised; what if not useful.

## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

Same product shape as Mamivio's candidate (questionnaire → personalised plan → day-by-day steps, one-off price, money-back), already live in the UK at £9.99, with the AI generation disclosed in the terms. It shows (1) that the format is cheap to copy, so the plan's quality and the trust around it, not the format, will separate offers; (2) a compliant way to word the guarantee without promising sleep outcomes, matching this niche's rules; (3) that UK law pushes such sellers to ask for a waiver of the cancellation period. Its questionnaire comes after payment; Mamivio's candidate (and Betteroo) put the quiz before the paywall, which is where the persuasion happens.
