## Still Not Sure?

Let's answer your questions.

Will this work for my baby's age?

- This course was designed for babies aged 3 months - 24 months sleeping in a crib or bassinet. If your baby is only a few months older than 24 months, it will likely still work! Email us to discuss and we will help you decide.

What if I've already tried sleep training?

- There are SO many sleep programs out there that are not comprehensive or thorough enough. Access to our expert community and the ability to add on a phone call with Sarah makes this course unique. With experience, Sarah is able to answer questions from all varieties of babies she has worked with - all incorporated into the course!

Is this "gentle" or "cry-it-out"?

- As an occupational therapist, Sarah does not believe crying it out is the best method. This course teaches you how to implement many changes into your baby's routines to make the transition smooth. There will likely be some crying, as this is new for your baby, but not long periods of crying at all.
