How long do I have access to the program?
Your access to the Sleep Program never expires, so it's here now and for every nap transition, regression, and bump along the way.
Is there crying involved in this program?
For newborns, not at all! Our goal is to help you build healthy sleep and protect your own rest. You'll learn to read your baby's cues, set gentle foundations, and know exactly what to do when the 4 month regression hits.With older babies working towards independent sleep (aka sleep training, the Good Inside way) there can be some crying, and we won't pretend otherwise. Anytime you change how your baby falls asleep, they'll likely be some protest, it's how they tell you something feels new. You'll learn when and how to respond, so you stay close and connected the whole way through. We will never ask you to do something you are not comfortable with, our approach is customizable to both fit your baby’s temperament and what feels right for you.
Will teaching my baby to sleep independently harm attachment?
This is our favorite question to answer. It means you care about your connection with your baby just as much as we do. Throughout the program, you stay connected and responsive. You don't have to choose between attachment and sleep. And remember, your relationship with your baby isn't defined by any single moment. Attachment is built through thousands of everyday interactions: feeding, holding, playing, and responding to your baby's needs. It's not built or broken by a few nights of learning something new.
What ages is it for?
Every stage from newborn through 24 months. It's one program that grows with your baby, from safe newborn foundations, through the regressions, to independent sleep and nap transitions.
I've already read so much. How is this different?
Free advice is written for every baby, so it contradicts itself. Here, a real person looks at your baby's sleep and tells you which advice fits and which to leave. Your plan stays responsive, so you never have to choose between more sleep and your instincts.
My baby is a newborn. Is it too early?
Your timing is perfect! There's a lot you can do right now to build healthy sleep and protect your own rest. You'll learn to read your baby's cues, set gentle foundations, know exactly what to do when the 4 month regression hits, and be set up for success if / when you're ready for your baby to sleep more independently.
What if I have more questions?
We’re happy to help! Please email us at support@goodinside.com
How is this different from other sleep programs?
Our approach is a plan rooted in connection, not rigid sleep training that ignores your instincts, and not gentle validation with no real plan. It's also the only program you'll need from 0-24 months, so you're never buying separate courses or bundles as your baby grows. And you're not doing it alone: it comes with real human support and the full Good Inside app, not just a PDF and "good luck!"
