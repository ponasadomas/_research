FAQ's

###

Is this just cry-it-out?

We don't recommend using the 'extinction method' where you put your baby in their cot, leave the room and don't go back in until they're asleep.. There are 3 settling methods to choose from in the course. There are 2 in-room methods that are more gradual and 1 method where you intermittently go in and out of your baby's room to provide reassurance as they learn to self settle. Some crying is very normal as your baby learns to self settle.

###

How long does this course take to watch?

Approximately 1 hour to watch it from start to finish! I aimed for it to be about as long as it takes me to have a consultation with a client :) So doable in one or two sittings!

###

My baby is younger than 4 months - will this work?

This program is designed for babies and toddlers 4+ months when independent sleep becomes developmentally appropriate.

###

I have your book already, will this course be useful?

YES! This course steps you through the first (crucial) 7 days teaching your baby to settle independently - it's a specific plan. The book covers everything from routines, settling methods and loads more info to help your baby sleep well from birth to one year.

###

I tried sleep training before and it failed. Why will this work?

Most parents fail because of inconsistency, giving up too soon (you need 7-14 days minimum), or information overload. This program fixes all three with a clear plan and realistic expectations.

###

I'm breastfeeding - will this work?

Yes! We're not taking away feeds. We're teaching your baby to fall asleep WITHOUT feeding. You'll still feed before bed, just not to sleep (if that's your goal).

###

Do I have to do ALL naps in the cot?

No! There is no need to be trapped at home 24/7! You can absolutely offer 1 or 2 naps out and about in the pram/car/carrier and just focus on one at home in their cot. And on 'off routine' days - all the naps can be on the go if you like!

###

My baby has a dummy, do we keep it or ditch it?

If your baby is under 8 months, usually i'd suggest ditching it as your baby will rely on your help to replace it. If over 8 months, they're usually pretty capable of finding and replacing it on their own and it's okay to keep!
