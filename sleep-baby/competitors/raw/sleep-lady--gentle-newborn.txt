## Newborn Sleep FAQs: Gentle Sleep Solutions for Babies 0–5 Months

What is the Gentle Newborn Sleep Course?

The Gentle Newborn Sleep Course is designed to help parents understand their baby’s sleep from birth through 5 months. It focuses on developmentally appropriate sleep expectations, soothing strategies, flexible routines, and gentle ways to build healthy sleep foundations without leaving your baby to cry alone.

How can I help my newborn sleep better at night?

You can support better newborn sleep by creating a calm bedtime routine, watching for your baby’s sleepy cues, and providing a safe, consistent sleep environment. Frequent waking is normal during the newborn stage, so the goal is to gradually build healthy sleep habits while continuing to respond to your baby’s feeding and comfort needs.

Can you sleep train a newborn?

Formal sleep training is generally not appropriate for newborns. During the first few months, babies need frequent feeding, comfort, and caregiver support. Instead, parents can focus on gentle, age-appropriate sleep shaping to establish healthy sleep foundations.

Why does my newborn wake up so often at night?

Newborns wake frequently because their sleep cycles are still developing and they need regular feeding and reassurance. As your baby grows and their circadian rhythm matures, longer periods of nighttime sleep may gradually develop.

Should my newborn follow a sleep schedule?

Newborns usually aren’t ready for a strict sleep schedule. Instead, follow your baby’s natural sleep and feeding patterns, watch for sleepy cues, and introduce predictable routines. More consistent sleep patterns typically develop as your baby gets older.

What is the 4-month sleep regression, and how can I help my baby through it?

Around 4 months, your baby’s sleep patterns mature, which can lead to more frequent waking, shorter naps, or difficulty settling. Maintaining consistent routines, watching wake windows and sleep cues, and using gentle sleep strategies can help your baby adjust to these developmental changes.

When can I start gentle sleep coaching with my baby?

During the newborn months, the focus should be on establishing healthy sleep foundations rather than formal sleep training. As your baby approaches 4–6 months and becomes developmentally ready, you can begin introducing more structured, gentle sleep strategies based on your baby’s individual needs.
