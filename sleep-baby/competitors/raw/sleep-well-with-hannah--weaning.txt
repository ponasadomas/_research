# Join my Free Weaning Workshop

Will your baby be between the age of 4.5-6 months on the 3rd November? If so, this workshop is for you.

This period of parenting can be tough. Not only are you navigating the 4-5 month sleep regression, there’s also a lot of change for you and your baby—not least thinking about preparing for weaning.

How on earth are you meant to even think about this often daunting stage when you’re sleep deprived and running on empty.

With my background in paediatric nursing and nutritional therapy, as well as being one of the leading baby and sleep experts, I’m uniquely positioned to tackle this. My aim to turn this into a positive stage for your baby, easing your worries and creating a smoother transition to solid foods.

During the sessions we’ll cover:

-

The best timing for starting weaning

-

Common misconceptions in the early stages of weaning

-

How common weaning advice can negatively impact your baby’s sleep

-

How weaning is much more than ‘getting your baby to eat veg’

-

Plus, join me for a live cookalong where you'll create your own food

-

And, there will be plenty of time for questions

-

Starting 3rd November 2026

## Hi, I'm Hannah

I’ve spent the last 25 years helping families with their babies and children.

In my twenties I completed three university courses – in Paediatric Nursing, Nutritional Therapy and Cranial Sacral Therapy, whilst traveling the world nannying for pro golfers.

In my thirties I raised my own 3 children (Oliver born in 2008, Ella in 2012 and Henry in 2015), founded and grew my new parenting business.

Through Sleep Well with Hannah, I’ve brought together all my experience and training to help hundreds of other families learn to juggle family life and, most importantly, sleep well.

Read More

I see myself as more than a sleep trainer – my deep knowledge in babies and children is what makes families feel confident in my hands, and is what makes my programmes, talks and courses work.

Through my CALM (Consistent, Achievable, Loving and Manageable®) approach to parenting I not only teach parents how to manage their baby’s feeding, weaning, nutrition, behaviour, and my most popular subject – sleep – I also teach confidence and how to enjoy juggling life with a baby. My greatest achievement is bringing many families out of dark, sleepless places to enjoying day to day life with their happy, sleeping baby.

I’ve helped thousands of families on their sleep journey’s through my gentle approach and I’d love to help you too.
