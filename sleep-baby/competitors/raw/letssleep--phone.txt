## 45-Minute 1:1 Sleep Consultation Call

Are you feeling exhausted, overwhelmed, and unsure how to improve your little one’s sleep? Let’s tackle it together in a focused 45-minute phone consultation designed to give you clarity and confidence.

Who is this for?
✔️ Parents struggling with their baby or toddler’s sleep (birth to five years)
✔️ Anyone feeling confused by all the conflicting advice out there
✔️ Parent's feeling frustrated with the day routine and wanting a review of their current routine
✔️ Those wanting expert guidance without committing to a full sleep program

What’s Included?

-
45-minute 1:1 phone consultation

-
Personalised sleep assessment (verbal) – We’ll discuss your little one’s sleep challenges and identify key areas for improvement.

-
Routine guide for your little one's age - You'll feel clear on how to navigate naps, feeds and bed timing

-
Time to ask us anything – Get expert advice tailored to your family’s unique situation.

-
Clear action plan (verbal) – Walk away with practical, easy-to-implement strategies to improve sleep

Let’s take you from…
😵 Feeling confused by all the sleep advice out there
🥱 Running on empty with unpredictable nights and days
❌ Wondering if you're creating "bad habits"

To…
✅ Feeling confident in your approach
😴 Getting more restful sleep for the whole family
💪 Knowing exactly when something doesn't go to plan

Book a time below or email me at hello@letssleep.com.au if you don’t see a spot that works!

Let’s get you and your little one sleeping better. 💙
