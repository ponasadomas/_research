This class includes:

- 5 modules that can be taken at your own pace including: Introduction to sleep basics, setting the foundations of sleep, nutrition and sleep, sleep teaching approaches, understanding your baby's sleep profile, routines and scheduling, regressions and common sleep habits, and managing milestones and transitions

- 40 page text booklet walking you through each section of the course

- Access to private sleep group

- 25% off our hourly consultations

This class is for you if...

- your baby is struggling to get adequate naps and night sleep

- your baby is experiencing split nights

- your baby is struggling with sleep associations

- your baby is rising early

- your baby is unable to sleep in their own environment

- you are currently co-sleeping and needing help transitioning

- is over-tired and needing to break out of the overtired cycle

- you are feeling lost and in need of support to help your baby sleep well without having to follow a cry it out approach
