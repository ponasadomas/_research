# Baby Sleep Coaching

Two Weeks

## $750

Ready to trade those sleepless nights for sweet dreams? Our Baby Sleep Coaching package is here to make that happen! This service is perfect for little ones aged 4 to 24 months who are snoozing (or not snoozing!) in cribs or bassinets.

Here's what you'll get:

-
A comprehensive questionnaire to uncover your baby's unique sleep situation

-
A customized sleep plan tailored specifically to you and your baby (because your little star deserves a plan that shines just for them!)

-
One-on-one chat with our sleep consultant, Sarah, to dig deeper into those sleep habits and routines and discuss your personal plan

-
Two full weeks of dedicated support as you implement the plan and guide your baby towards independent sleep

-
What does the support look like?

-
This support is daily text messaging with Sarah, bedtime support, on the spot problem solving, feeding/development assistance as needed, baby monitor video reviewing and so much more!

Remember, this isn't about leaving your baby to cry it out – it's about teaching them a valuable life skill with you by their side every step of the way. Get ready to say hello to restful nights and bright-eyed mornings!
