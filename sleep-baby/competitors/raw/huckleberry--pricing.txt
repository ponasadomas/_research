## FAQ

### What’s the difference between Plus and Premium?

Both include advanced features like SweetSpot nap predictions, data-driven Insights, and AI logging. Premium unlocks all of our features to help through any challenge, including instant parenting backup with Berry, and expert-designed sleep plans customized for your child.

### Is the app free?

There are 3 Huckleberry membership tiers, including a free tier which features basic logging tools and reports.

### Does my subscription automatically renew?

Yes, your subscription will auto-renew. We’ll give a heads up before your subscription period ends so you can choose whether or not to renew.

### Can I share my Huckleberry plan with my partner, or do they need their own?

To share Huckleberry across multiple devices, caregivers can sign in to the same account on each phone. This lets everyone log sleep, feedings, and diapers, with updates syncing automatically between devices.

For the best experience, make sure each device is running the latest version of the app and is connected to the internet.

### Does Huckleberry offer discount codes or promo codes?

Huckleberry offers select discount opportunities to our members throughout the year. Sign up to receive messages about our promotions.

We also give two weeks of free access so members get a chance to check out some of our advanced features before committing to a paid subscription.
