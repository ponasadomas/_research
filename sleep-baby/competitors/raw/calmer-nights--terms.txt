## 2. The Baby Sleep Rescue Plan

The Baby Sleep Rescue Plan is a personalised digital sleep-support plan created using information you provide through our questionnaire.

The plan is intended to help you understand your baby's current sleep patterns and provide practical, age-appropriate ideas that may help you work towards more manageable nights.

It does not guarantee that your baby's sleep will improve, that night waking will stop, or that your baby will sleep through the night.

## 3. How personalisation works

After purchasing, you will be asked to complete a questionnaire about matters including your baby's age, sleep, settling, naps, feeding, what you have previously tried and your preferences.

Your answers are processed using automated systems, including artificial intelligence, to help generate your personalised plan.

The usefulness of the plan depends partly on the completeness and accuracy of the information you provide.

## 4. Artificial intelligence

Calmer Nights uses artificial intelligence as part of the process used to generate personalised Baby Sleep Rescue Plans.

AI-generated content is constrained by our evidence, safety and decision-making framework. However, artificial intelligence can make mistakes and the plan should not be treated as medical or professional healthcare advice.

## 5. Not medical advice

Calmer Nights provides educational sleep information and sleep-support guidance only.

It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment and is not a substitute for advice from your GP, health visitor, paediatrician or another appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

If you are concerned about your baby's health, feeding, breathing, growth, development or wellbeing, seek advice from an appropriate healthcare professional.

Where urgent medical attention may be required, seek appropriate urgent or emergency medical assistance.

## 6. Safer sleep

The Baby Sleep Rescue Plan is designed to prioritise recognised safer-sleep principles.

You should always follow current safer-sleep guidance applicable to your baby and circumstances.

Nothing in your plan should be interpreted as overriding advice provided by an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

## 7. Sleep training and settling approaches

You remain responsible for deciding whether a particular approach is suitable for your family and should stop and seek appropriate professional advice if you have concerns about your baby's health or wellbeing.
