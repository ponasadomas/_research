## What parents ask

How does Betteroo collect reviews?

Betteroo reviews come directly from real parents through in-app feedback, customer support conversations, interviews, and written testimonials. Names are anonymized to protect parent privacy, but the quotes, sleep changes, and family circumstances are exactly as parents described them.

Betteroo does not pay for reviews or script testimonials. The reviews shown reflect real experiences from families using the platform in everyday life, including during challenging periods like regressions, feeding transitions, and early newborn weeks.

They are not a curated highlight reel, and they are not solicited in exchange for discounts. Parents share what worked, what took time, and what changed for their family.
