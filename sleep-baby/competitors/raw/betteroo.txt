FAQ

## Your real questions,
Our honest answers.

We know sleep can feel overwhelming. It doesn’t need to be.

Is Betteroo a sleep training app? +

Betteroo is a personalized sleep training app — but it’s not what most people picture when they hear “sleep training.” There are no rigid schedules, no one-size-fits-all methods, and no pressure to let your baby cry it out.

Instead, Betteroo builds a custom sleep plan based on your baby’s age, temperament, feeding method, and sleep patterns — and adapts it as things change. Whether you prefer gentle settling, gradual methods, or something in between, the app supports your approach. Think of it as a sleep training app that starts by asking how you’re doing, not just how your baby slept.

What makes Betteroo different? +

You’re not alone – and you’re not doing it wrong. Most sleep advice fails because it’s designed for a textbook baby, not YOUR baby. Generic PDFs with your baby’s name inserted aren’t personalized plans.

Betteroo is different because we actually adapt. When something stops working (teething, illness, developmental leaps), we adjust your plan in real-time. We don’t abandon you with “just follow the program” – we stay with you through the chaos.

Do you use cry-it-out? +

No. Never. If it feels wrong, we don’t do it. Period.

Betteroo offers multiple gentle approaches – you choose what aligns with your values. Whether you want gradual methods, responsive settling, or something in between, your plan reflects YOUR comfort level. We never ask you to do something that goes against your instincts as a parent.

Is the plan really built around my baby? +

Your baby IS different—that’s exactly why templates fail. A 4-month-old with reflux needs a completely different approach than a 3-year-old with bedtime battles.

Betteroo builds your plan around your specific child: their age, temperament, sleep challenges, feeding method, and your family’s schedule. And when things change (because they always do), your plan changes too.

How quickly will I see results? +

Most parents notice results within the first week. But here’s what matters more: you’ll start feeling like yourself again. The ability to think clearly. Hot coffee. Making it to the thing you kept canceling.

Your baby sleeping better is the proof – you getting yourself back is the point.

How much does Betteroo cost? +

Plans start at just $1/day – a fraction of the $500–2,000 that sleep consultants typically charge.

You get a personalized plan that adapts as your child grows (newborn through age 6), unlimited caregiver sharing, and ongoing support when things change. No hourly fees, no “package ran out” cutoffs.

Take the free quiz to see your personalized plan.

What ages does Betteroo support? +

Betteroo supports children from newborn through age 6, with age-specific guidance for each developmental stage:

Newborns (0-3 months): Building foundations before formal sleep learning

Infants (4-12 months): Navigating regressions and emerging self-soothing

Toddlers (1-3 years): Handling bedtime battles, nap transitions, and big feelings

Preschoolers (3-6 years): Addressing fears, stalling tactics, and growing independence

Your plan evolves as your child grows. What works at 6 months gets updated for 18 months, then again for age 3.

Can my partner, nanny, or grandparents follow the plan too? +

Yes – and this is one of the biggest reasons Betteroo works.

Consistency across caregivers is everything. When everyone follows the same approach, your child gets clear cues every time—whether it’s bedtime with mom, naptime with grandma, or the weekend with dad.

Your plan includes sharing with unlimited caregivers at no extra cost, and they don’t need to download anything either.

One plan, one team, real progress.

How much time does this take? +

We designed this for parents running on fumes. Daily micro-supports take 5-15 minutes, not hour-long modules. Simple tracking, not complex sleep logs. Everything assumes you’re depleted, not operating at full capacity.

Because you’re not – and that’s okay.

Do I need a special device to use Betteroo? +

Nope. If you have a phone and 5 minutes a day, you can use Betteroo.

It works on any iPhone, Android phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to download from an app store and nothing to update. It takes up almost no space on your phone, so you can save the storage for baby photos.

Does this work with my setup? +

Betteroo works with YOUR setup, not against it.

Breastfeeding, formula, combo feeding. Crib, bassinet, sidecar, room-sharing. Single parent, two parents, grandparents helping. Reflux, daycare schedules, teething, travel – your plan accounts for all of it.

We meet you where you are and help you reach YOUR sleep goals within YOUR family’s reality. There’s no “right” way to do this—there’s only what works for you.

For medical conditions, we recommend using Betteroo alongside your pediatrician’s guidance. The two work well together.
