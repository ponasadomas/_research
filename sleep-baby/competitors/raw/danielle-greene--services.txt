-

#### 30-minute Ask-Me-Anything Call ($150)

A perfect option for families with already-independent sleepers who are looking for some additional support. Families who book an Ask-Me-Anything call often come prepared with questions about schedule changes, nap transitions, sickness, travel, and other minor issues.

If your child is not yet equipped with the skill of falling asleep and/or back to sleep independently, please consider booking a two-week consultation.

Book Now

#### In-Person Sleep Training ($5,997)*

In search of more hands-on support? In-person sleep training might be the perfect option for you. Danielle comes to your home ready to be there for both you and your child. She will take the guesswork out of everything and will lead the way both at bedtime and in the middle of the night.

*In-person sleep training is available for families based in select NJ counties & NYC. Pricing is varied based on location.

## Dream Big

We all want great sleep for our family, but how we get there may look different for everyone. After committing to working together, you will have the opportunity to share more about your day-to-day routine and what sleep habits currently exist in your home. This is also a great time for you to share your goals, which Danielle will work hard to help you achieve!

-

[img alt: Untitled-4-02.png]

## Make A Plan

There is no one-size-fits-all approach to sleep training. No two children or two families are the same, which means that the sleep training method that worked for your friend may not work for you. Using the information you shared with her, Danielle will work with you to create a plan that will allow your dreams of great sleep to become a reality in a way that feels safe and comfortable, and that aligns with your parenting style.

-

[img alt: Untitled-4-03.png]

## Get to Work

Danielle is guaranteed to make you feel valued, understood and encouraged. As a mom of two little ones, Danielle recognizes that sleep is not only a want, but also a need. Regardless of the type of support you choose to receive, get ready to embark on a journey towards restful nights and rejuvenated days.
