# The Baby Sleep Rescue Plan

## Let's understand your baby's nights

Every baby is different, and night waking can be exhausting. Answer a few questions about your baby's sleep and we'll use your answers to create a personalised plan based on your individual situation.

Please answer each question as accurately as you can. The more accurate your answers are, the more personalised and useful your sleep plan can be.

How we'll use your answers

We'll use the information you provide to create your personalised Baby Sleep Rescue Plan. Your responses are processed using automated systems, including AI, to help generate recommendations based on your circumstances.

Calmer Nights provides educational sleep support, not medical advice.
