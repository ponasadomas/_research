Questions

When will the app stop working?

The app will remain available until 11:59PM EST on November 30th 2026. You will no longer be able to access the app as of Dec 1st 2026.

I have an active subscription, can I still use the app until closure day?

Yes, absolutely. You will continue to have full access to the Smart Sleep Coach app right up until its official closure date on November 30th, 2026. After that date, the app will no longer be accessible. We encourage you to use this time to revisit any favorite content or tools.

Will I be charged after the app closes?

No, you will not be charged after the Smart Sleep Coach app officially closes on November 30th, 2026. We have proactively stopped all auto-renewals, so you will not incur any new charges for app access. You will continue to have full access to the Smart Sleep Coach app through November 30th, 2026. After that date, the app will no longer be accessible and no further charges will be applied. Additionally, for any users with an active annual subscription with remaining subscription time beyond November 30th, 2026, a pro-rated refund automatically be issued. Processing time for refund may vary. More details here.

Can I keep my baby’s schedule and sleep plan?

Yes, you can. You will continue to have full access to the Smart Sleep Coach app until its official closure on November 30th, 2026. During this time, we recommend you take screenshots of your baby's personalized schedule, sleep plans, or any other content you wish to retain for your records. After November 30th, the app and its content will no longer be accessible.

I would like to keep all of my baby’s data that I’ve tracked, do you have a way to share that?

Yes, you can download your tracked data right in the app. Go to the Progress screen, open the Patterns view, and you can download your weekly and monthly data from there. We recommend saving anything you'd like to keep before November 30th, 2026, since the app and its content won't be accessible after that date.

My friend told me about the app but I can’t seem to download it anywhere?

Thank you for reaching out! Unfortunately, Smart Sleep Coach is no longer available for download.
