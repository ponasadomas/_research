Parent Support

So your baby is sleeping well now, what about everything else?!

Through my kind and gentle approach, I’ve helped thousands of families achieve better sleep with my one-to-one packages, Sleep Clubs and courses. But I noticed that even after resolving sleep issues, parents still needed guidance with daily challenges like feeding, weaning, illness and teething.

When families kept asking if they could continue getting my support for everyday parenting questions, I knew exactly what was needed – and that’s how my Parenting Club was born.

What is Parenting Club?

It’s a small community of parents at various stages of newborn, baby or toddler parenting who really just need one source of advice and support from someone who gets them, to help them through the crucial stages of their little ones’ development.

What do you get?

  • Weekly drop-in clinics where you can ask me any questions you have
  • Age appropriate WhatsApp threads for information and support
  • A private Facebook Community with club members only
  • Membership area with hours of useful training videos, downloadable guides and resources
  • Exclusive monthly training sessions from me and other experts
  • A free parenting course every month, covering a huge range of topics

How to join

To keep the Parenting Club small and full of like-minded parents, we really limit access to people who have completed my one-to-one, Sleep Club or courses. Once you’ve completed any of those, you’ll get an invite to join the club. However, if you haven’t received an invite and would like to join, click the button below

Subscription auto-renews every month, in line with your subscription date. You can cancel at anytime. Access to parenting club resources are only available to active members. If you do cancel your membership, you will lose access to those resources at the end of your latest billing period.
