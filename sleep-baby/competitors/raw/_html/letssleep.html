The book I wish I had when navigating sleep with our first baby.

Let’s Sleep: The First Year


There is a LOT of conflicting advice about baby sleep and a lot of strong opinions about what is the ‘right way’ to navigate it. It’s only natural to feel overwhelmed and a tad anxious about your baby’s sleep.

That is exactly why I (hi! Jazz here - Mum, Midwife, Nurse and Sleep Consultant) wrote this book.

Let’s Sleep The First Year is full of evidence-based and judgement-free advice, giving you all the tools and strategies you need to navigate your baby’s sleep with confidence from birth to one.

Hi! I'm Jazz

As both a midwife and a mum who's been in your shoes, I'll show you exactly how to get everyone sleeping better—so you can move from survival mode to a regulated nervous system where you actually enjoy parenting again.

I’m a Baby & Toddler Sleep Consultant, Registered Midwife and Nurse.

I have completed a Bachelor of Nursing, Postgraduate Diploma in Midwifery and Postgraduate Diploma in Nursing (Child, Family & Community) and I’m a Mum - I totally get your need for down time and a schedule!

I’m also the co-founder of Beyond Sleep School (an online training provider) where we educate and certify Sleep Consultants worldwide.

My goal is to empower you with education that allows you to enjoy your family life without sleep deprivation, anxiety about sleep and overwhelm.
