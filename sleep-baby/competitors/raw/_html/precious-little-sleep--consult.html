Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a “one size fits all” approach?

Your family and child are unique and your sleep approach should be too. No matter your situation, we can and will create a plan for you and your family. We will combine the latest research with my experience working with untold numbers of families to meet your specific needs.

What about tear-free options?

I can’t guarantee you a tear-free solution. Nobody can. There are a variety of approaches to independent sleep that are more gradual, where the parent is more heavily involved. If that is most appealing to you, we can focus our plan on those strategies. But whenever you ask your child to do things in a new way there is likely to be some complaint involved.

What if we have more than 1 kid with sleep issues?

We can discuss what is going on with both kids during our call. I often work with families of multiples, where there are older siblings, etc.

What ages do you work with?

Mostly adults. Oh you meant babies! I’ve coached families of newborns all the way up to kindergarten. I will say the first six weeks are often rough (crying peaks and sleep is at its worst at six weeks), and much of that is related to issues of being a newborn (immature circadian rhythm, colic, etc.). But if your child is 3 months old and things are still wonky chances are we can make things better. I also coach families of older children around issues like: transitioning to the big kid bed, limit testing at bedtime, nap dropping, won’t stay in bed, etc.

Which countries can you work with?

I’ve worked with families on every continent except Antarctica (if you’re in Antarctica – PLEASE CALL ME!). Let me know where you are and we’ll find a day/time that works for everybody.

What about returning families?

I adore having the opportunity to work with families again! So much so that I have special pricing just for them. If we’ve worked together in the past and you have a new baby, congratulations! All packages are available at a 20% discount to returning families.
