Meet Danielle

For more than a decade, Danielle has dedicated her life to supporting children and their families in various capacities. Whether teaching in the classroom, working overseas in Ukraine or helping families get the sleep they need, Danielle’s mission has always been - and will always be - to make a positive difference in others’ lives. 

As an expert in social emotional learning and development, Danielle understands the importance of meeting children and their parents exactly where they are. As a result, Danielle believes that there is no one-size-fits-all approach to sleep training. Educated in various training methods through the Center for Pediatric Sleep Management, Danielle supports families in ways that leave them feeling comfortable, understood and successful.

Why work together?

Sleep is one of the most precious gifts a parent can give both their child and themselves, which is something Danielle learned very early on in her parenting journey. When her oldest child turned four months old, Danielle could no longer tolerate his unpredictable naps and early morning wake-ups. She bought online courses, read all of the books and followed well-known Instagram accounts, all of which worked…until they didn’t. Danielle was at a loss. What Danielle needed was someone to support her in real time, so that’s exactly what she got. She decided to work with a sleep consultant, which allowed her to receive life-changing one-on-one support. Knowing that there was someone there to hold Danielle accountable and to encourage her to stay the course made for a nearly seamless sleep training experience. After successfully sleep training her second child and several of her friends’ children on her own, Danielle knew that the gift of sleep was something she wanted and needed to share with families everywhere.

What to expect

  • Dream Big

    We all want great sleep for our family, but how we get there may look different for everyone. After committing to working together, you will have the opportunity to share more about your day-to-day routine and what sleep habits currently exist in your home. This is also a great time for you to share your goals, which Danielle will work hard to help you achieve!

  • Make A Plan

    There is no one-size-fits-all approach to sleep training. No two children or two families are the same, which means that the sleep training method that worked for your friend may not work for you. Using the information you shared with her, Danielle will work with you to create a plan that will allow your dreams of great sleep to become a reality in a way that feels safe and comfortable, and that aligns with your parenting style.

  • Get to Work

    Danielle is guaranteed to make you feel valued, understood and encouraged. As a mom of two little ones, Danielle recognizes that sleep is not only a want, but also a need. Regardless of the type of support you choose to receive, get ready to embark on a journey towards restful nights and rejuvenated days.
