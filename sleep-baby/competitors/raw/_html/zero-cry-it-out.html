Concerns you may have...

﻿I feel like it is too late to change my baby's sleep habits.

It's never too late! Babies are highly adaptable, and your little one can learn new sleep associations at any age. With the right approach, you can gently guide them toward independent sleep in the cot.


Transitioning to the cot will be a struggle.

Transitioning may have challenges, but this course is designed to make it as smooth and gentle as possible for you both. I provide you with a step-by-step plan and supportive techniques to minimise any struggle.


I'm not sure if my baby will ever sleep independently.

Many parents have successfully transitioned their babies to independent sleep using our methods. With patience, consistency, and my guidance, you can help your little one learn to sleep in the cot.


Will this course need me to stop breastfeeding?

No, not at all! This course is entirely supportive of breastfeeding. We are focusing on transitioning away from using feeding as a sleep association only. Your baby can absolutely still continue to enjoy the bond and benefits of breastfeeding while learning to sleep independently.
