Got questions? Check out our FAQs.

Which sleep course should I choose?

Our sleep courses are tailored to different age groups; covering a range of topics to help you really understand your baby or toddler’s sleep, create a routine that works for them and support them to settle to sleep in a new way.

Choose the course which is closest to your little one’s current age. If you are between age groups then we recommend choosing the next age group up. For example, if your baby is 9.5 months old then choose the 10-18 month course.

We also have discounted course bundles where you can select different course combinations and age groups with big savings applied.

How long do I have access to the courses for?

As soon as you sign up to a course, you can access all the information straight away. There is no time limit on how long you have access to a course for – so you can go through it at your own pace and refer back to it whenever you need to.

How do I access the courses?

As soon as you purchase a course you will have access to all the material in our logged in members area of the site. You will create your own password when you sign up and can login from a computer, tablet or phone.

The courses contain a mixture of videos, written content and downloadable files to share all our advice with you. All videos are all captioned and summarized in writing so you can choose how you digest the information.

I want to buy more than one course, do you offer discounts?

Yes, we offer discounted course bundles where you can select different course combinations and age groups with big savings applied.

Do the courses include personalised support?

Our courses are designed for you to complete online at your own pace. We cover a range of topics to help you unravel your current sleep situation and to create your own sleep plan to put in place.

Each course includes a troubleshooting and FAQ section to help you as you create and put your plan into place.

If you would prefer a 1:1 consultation with one of our team you can see more details of our packages here.

Does it matter if I breastfeed or bottle feed?

This course can help you with your babies sleep whether you breastfeed, bottle feed or combi-feed your baby.

How you choose to feed your baby is exactly that, your choice. We include suggested daily routines in this course which can help shape a rhythm to feeds during the day, but you do not need to feed by the clock, withhold feeds or change your feeding method to achieve improved sleep.

Some babies need night feeds for longer than others. You can carry on night feeds for as long as they are needed, and still make improvements to sleep.

My baby was born early, do I choose a course based on actual age or corrected age?

We recommend you follow your baby’s corrected age until you feel that they are on track with age appropriate developmental milestones

Can I buy a course as a gift for someone else?

Yes, you can buy gift vouchers to send to a friend or family member by email. You simply choose the value of the gift voucher you want to send and then let us know their email address and the date you would like them to receive the voucher. All they need to do is then choose the course they want and use their gift voucher code at the checkout.

What methods do you cover in the sleep courses?

Our methods cover a range of factors that can impact on sleep, to allow families to make decisions on if and how they want to make sleep changes.

So how do we approach it? If you are ready to make a change to sleep we suggest looking at the following areas:

  • Sleep environment – is this set up to optimise sleep? Is there anything potentially disturbing or disrupting sleep?
  • Routine. Creating a routine that meets your child’s current day and night time sleep needs.
  • Looking at how your baby  currently falls asleep and deciding if this is working well for you as a family, or if there are some changes you want to make.
  • Decide on an approach to making changes to how your child falls asleep that suits you and your little one.

This course walks you through each of these areas to help you identify which sleep changes will help your baby, and how to put them in place.

We include a choices of settling methods in the course so you can choose an approach that suits your baby’s temperament and your parenting style. 

 

Will there be a lot of crying involved?

For some families, sleep can be improved simply by making changes to their routine and sleep environment only.

Others may need support in learning to fall asleep in a new way. When we make changes there can be some crying – it is how our babies communicate with us. We can respond and give support whilst changing boundaries.

The course includes different approaches you can use to settling to sleep in a new way, and supporting your baby to sleep. You can choose the method that feels right for you and your baby.
