Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to read the book first?

No having read the book is not required although it is highly recommended! The book is a great comprehensive resource however the community also has resources, office hours, and the ability to ask questions so families who don't have (or haven't had time to read) the book can be very successful in the group.

What if I'm not ready to sleep train?

The Precious Little Sleep community will help you move forward wherever you are in your journey. We are here to help bring clarity, offer ideas to improve sleep outcomes, and establish healthy sleep hygiene for your children by meeting you where you are, on your own timeline.

Will this take a lot of time?

This resource was designed for busy parents! We recommend planning to pop in for 5-10 minutes most days. It is a small investment to make transformative improvements in your family's sleep!

What if I have more than one child?

We are here to support your sleep journey for all of your children, big and small families are welcome!

What if I don't like subscriptions?

We also have a one-time option for families who prefer that simplicity and convenience! This is a popular option for a variety of reasons and it provides the option for lifetime membership!
