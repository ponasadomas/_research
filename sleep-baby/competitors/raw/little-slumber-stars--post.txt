# The Mattress Search That Changed Everything (And Why I'm Obsessed with Newton)

[img alt: Writer: Sarah Taylor]
Sarah Taylor

Jul 7, 2025

4 min read

Updated: Jul 9, 2025

Hey mama,

Can we talk about something that kept me up at night for weeks? And no, it wasn't my little one's sleep schedule this time – it was trying to figure out the safest crib mattress for my baby.

As a sleep consultant, I thought I had all the answers. I mean, I help families get better sleep every single day! But when it came to choosing the actual mattress my own baby would be sleeping on for hours each night, I found myself spiraling down a rabbit hole of confusing marketing claims and "organic" labels that made my head spin.

## The "Organic" Mattress Reality Check

Here's what I discovered that honestly shocked me: most of those "organic" crib mattresses aren't actually organic at all. I know – it sounds crazy! But here's the thing that no one talks about...

You have to use a synthetic plastic waterproof liner with all those "organic" mattresses because they can't be washed cover to core.

Think about it – what happens when your little one has their first (or fifteenth 😅) blowout? If you can't wash the actual mattress, you're stuck putting a plastic barrier between your baby and that "organic" material you paid extra for. It's like buying organic food and then wrapping it in plastic before serving it to your child.

And speaking of those organic mattresses made with coconut fibers and wool – they sound amazing in theory, right? But here's the harsh reality: those mattresses will be destroyed upon the first blowout unless you use a plastic waterproof pad on top, which makes the mattress not breathable. All that money spent on "natural" materials, and you're back to square one with plastic and compromised airflow.

## The Greenwashing Problem

As I dug deeper into my research, I started noticing something frustrating. Some crib mattress companies are greenwashing their products – they call them "organic," but they won't tell you what's really inside. We're talking plastic liners, metal springs, even chemical adhesives hidden beneath those pretty organic cotton covers.

As a mom, this made me so angry. We're already worried about everything when it comes to our babies' safety, and then we have to play detective just to figure out what's actually in their mattress?

## Finding Newton (And Finally Sleeping Better Myself!)

That's when I discovered Newton, and it was like a breath of fresh air – literally! Newton is all about transparency and safety, and their crib mattresses are fully breathable and fully washable without foam, metal springs, or toxic chemicals.

But here's what really sold me: Newton's patented Wovenaire core is designed to be both breathable and washable – it's made from 90% air and the same material as a yogurt cup!

Wait, what? A yogurt cup? I had to laugh when I first heard this, but it actually makes perfect sense. It's a food-safe material that's been proven safe for babies, and the engineering behind it is incredible.

You can check out Newton's innovative design here - it's honestly fascinating!

## Why This Matters for Sleep (The Professional Take)

Now, let me put on my sleep consultant hat for a second. When I work with families, one of the biggest concerns parents have is safe sleep. They want to follow all the guidelines while still ensuring their baby is comfortable and getting quality rest.

Breathability is huge. When a mattress allows proper airflow, it helps regulate your baby's temperature throughout the night. No more worrying about overheating or creating unsafe sleep conditions with extra blankets or positioning aids.

And the washability factor? Game-changer. I can't tell you how many families I've worked with who stress about mattress cleanliness affecting their baby's sleep environment. With Newton, you can literally wash the entire thing – cover to core – and know it's truly clean.

## The Real-Life Test

I've been recommending Newton mattresses to my clients for years now, and the feedback has been incredible. Parents love that they can maintain a clean, safe sleep environment without compromising on breathability or worrying about what's hidden inside their baby's mattress.

One mom recently told me, "I finally feel like I can trust what my baby is sleeping on every night. And when she had a massive diaper blowout at 3 AM, I didn't panic about the mattress getting damaged because it is so easy to clean!"

If you want to see what she's talking about, you can learn more about Newton mattresses here.

## The Bottom Line

As both a sleep consultant and a mom, I need products that work in the real world, not just on paper. Newton mattresses deliver on their promises – they're transparent about their materials, they're designed for actual parent life (hello, washability!), and they prioritize what matters most: your baby's safety and comfort.

The quality of the hours you spend awake are a direct reflection of how you spend your night – and that applies to your baby too. When you know your little one is sleeping safely on a truly breathable, truly clean mattress, everyone sleeps better.

Trust me, as someone who's seen it all when it comes to sleep challenges, having one less thing to worry about is priceless.
