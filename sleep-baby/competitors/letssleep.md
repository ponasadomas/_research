**Buyer / trigger:** Mother of a baby 4 months to toddler who is "the only one who can settle baby": 45-minute bouncing on an exercise ball, transfer wakes, 2-hour bedtimes, contact naps. Ad hook: "just wait it out" isn't working anymore.

**Mechanism (≤8 words):** One 7-day plan, three settling methods.

**Promise:** Baby falls asleep independently in the cot in 7 days; partner can do bedtime; evenings back. The sales page says baby is "asleep in 10-15 minutes" after being put down awake.

**Proof:** Founder credentials (midwife, maternal and child health nurse, sleep consultant, co-founder of a consultant-training school, published book). Family counts drift: "Used by 5,000+ families" in ads, "over 8,000 families" on the order page, "hundreds of families" on the home and sales pages. Client testimonials on home page.

**Price:** $37 one-time, AUD (order total line reads AUD), "no recurring fees". Order bump: 4 to 12 Month Routine PDF, $15. Back-end ladder in AUD: shop guides $14–$58 (regression guide $47, crossed $87; teething/illness guide $14, crossed $54), coaching group $197 lifetime, 45-minute phone consultation (price not on captured page), 2-week online package $990 (or 4 × $280), in-home 3-hour $1,320, one night $2,200, two nights $3,300.

**Format:** 4 video modules of 3–12 minutes (about 1 hour total), daily action plan, settling-methods PDF, environment checklist, mindset tools, fridge printable, lifetime access, SamCart delivery.

**Personalization:** None in the product. The parent chooses one of three settling methods (two in-room, one in-and-out). The copy still says families succeed when they "get a plan that actually fits their family".

**Guarantee:** None on the course. Terms: all digital products "including online courses, PDFs, workbooks, and AI tools are non-refundable". The coaching group has a 30-day full refund.

**Angles and hooks:** Short and finite (7 days, 1 hour of video); "you need 7-14 days minimum"; failure blamed on inconsistency and quitting on day 3, not on the parent; one cot nap only, the rest on the go; breastfeeding kept, just not to sleep; bedtime as a 15-minute script.

**Objections answered:** is it cry-it-out (no extinction; "some crying is very normal"); too young (<4 months, no); I have your book; tried and failed before; breastfeeding; all naps in the cot; dummy (ditch under 8 months).
