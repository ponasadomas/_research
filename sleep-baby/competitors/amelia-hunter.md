Captured 2026-10-25. Spanish-language market; outside the English-speaking launch scope. Light note for the price and format reference.

**Buyer / trigger:** Spanish parents of babies over 4 months, confused by routines, wake windows and posts.

**Mechanism (≤8 words):** Questionnaire, video call, plan, three weeks' follow-up.

**Promise:** Nights stop being a struggle with a method, a plan and a guide.

**Proof:** Team of certified consultants including a neuropediatrician, a paediatric nurse and a psychologist; testimonials.

**Price (EUR):** Plan de sueño acompañado 4–24 months 525 € (3 weeks: 1 preparation + 2 follow-up).

**Format:** Evaluation questionnaire, 1 initial + 3 follow-up video calls, daily chat, sleep log, a guide for the coming months, a crying course.

**Personalization:** Questionnaire → consultant-made plan.

**Guarantee:** Not observed.

**Angles and hooks:** Information overload ("more confused than at the start").

**Objections answered:** how long, when to start, chance of success ("muy alta").
