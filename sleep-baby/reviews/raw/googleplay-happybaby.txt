## About this app

arrow_forward

No more guessing when it’s time to nap. No more battling bedtime.

Just more sleep, less stress — and a baby who drifts off in 10 minutes. Happy Baby is the sleep and routine app built for parents like you. With our science-backed DreamTimer and soothing sounds, it helps your little one fall asleep faster and stay asleep longer — while giving you peace of mind.

PERSONALIZED NAP & SLEEP SCHEDULES

Tired of wondering when to put your baby down? Our DreamTimer predicts your baby’s naps and bedtimes based on their age, sleep cues & habits — so they fall asleep before getting overtired.
- Smart daily schedules
- Tailored wake windows
- Gentle reminders before overtiredness kicks in

“After a few days, it magically predicted Lea’s nap times. Now she’s out in 10 minutes. No tears, no fussing — total game changer.” – Laura, mom of a 4m old

RECOGNIZE LEAPS & SLEEP REGRESSIONS EARLY

We guide you through the rough patches. Get timely, expert-backed info on sleep regressions, growth spurts, and developmental leaps — right when you need it.
- Understand what your baby’s going through
- Know what to expect and how to support them
- Feel more in control during every phase

STAY AHEAD OF BEDTIME BATTLES

Our smart reminders help you wind down before your baby gets overtired — making bedtime smoother for everyone.
- Spot early sleepy cues
- Avoid cranky meltdowns
- End the day on a calm note

CALM YOUR BABY WITH SOUNDS THAT SOOTHE

Choose from 50+ proven soundscapes made for baby sleep — including white noise, lullabies, and nature sounds.
- Based on sleep science
- Designed for babies
- Gentle, rhythmic, calming

NEVER MISS A FEED AGAIN

Log every bottle, nursing session or solid meal. Know when and how much your baby last ate — at a glance.
- Track breastfeeding, bottles, and solids
- Stay ahead of hunger cues
- See patterns & get reminders

UNDERSTAND YOUR BABY’S GROWTH

Track sleep, feedings, growth, and diaper changes — all in one place.
- View daily & weekly patterns
- Get meaningful insights, not just data
- Feel confident in your parenting

WHY PARENTS LOVE HAPPY BABY

Because it was made for real life, by real parents.
- 100,000+ happy parents
- Built with baby sleep scientists
- Designed by parents who’ve been there

START YOUR CALM & CONFIDENT PARENTING JOURNEY TODAY

Get the rest you both need — and the support you deserve. Download Happy Baby today and see the difference in just a few days.
