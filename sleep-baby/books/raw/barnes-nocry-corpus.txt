TITLE: Very happy to have come across this book
After almost 1 year of sleep deprivation, this book has truly changed the night-time dynamics in our household and has allowed both my husband and I to become fully functional adults again. Our child-raising beliefs did not contemplate the option of a ""let-the-baby-cry-it-out"" method. But we still aspired to get a decent night of sleep. The ""No Cry Sleep Solution"" really worked for us. 

First of all, it provided us with background information about what a baby's sleep cycle is truly like, ridding us of unreasonable expectations and allowing us to understand our baby better. Then it went on to provide very concrete and specific tools, along with the explanation of why they are likely to work. Because every baby is different, a number of different alternatives are offered. And no, these methods are not magical - they all require the involvement and engagement of the parents or care takers. But we have found that this effort was minimal compared to the bleak outlook of not sleeping through the night for a very, very long time. 

In our case, it took us about 6 weeks for our baby to go from waking up 4-6 times a night to sleeping through the night most of the time. Of those 6 weeks, only the first two were somewhat challenging as our baby adapted to these new methods, but no more challenging than it was trying to run our lives day in and day out in a constant state of sleep deprivation. The ""final"" 4 weeks were easier and easier, as our baby showed a gradual improvement (i. e. reduction) in her night time awakenings. For example, the last two weeks were almost exclusively one awakening per night (of a 12-hour period), until now, in which she has had many nights of sleeping 12 hours all the way through.

Additionally, one of the tools proposed, the pre-bedtime routine, is not only an essential part of getting your baby to sleep through the night but also has become a very important bonding moment and highly enjoyable for all of us.

Elizabeth Pantley shares her experience of being the mother of 4 with a lot of clarity, insight and an immense amount of compassion. We highly recommend this book.
