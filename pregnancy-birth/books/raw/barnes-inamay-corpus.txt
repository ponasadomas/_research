TITLE: Such an informative, comforting read
As a first-time mom, this book has been absolutely wonderful in giving me confidence that birth is a natural, normal process, not something to be feared. The first half of the book is entirely birth stories from a huge range of moms, from young to old, high- to low-risk, long and short labors and with babies in a variety of positions. The stories are very comprehensive and helpful for any woman looking ahead to birth. The second half of the book is Ina May's discussion of birth, different birthing theories and positions. I found it especially helpful learning about how much a woman's ability to stay relaxed and comfortable impacts the speed of birthing. It makes so much sense that a woman who feels comfortable in the room and with the people around her is going to have an easier birth experience. Overall a superbly helpful book.

REVIEW barnes-inamay-904269727 | 5 stars | 2009-02-17 | verifiedBuyer=False
TITLE: Fearless, beautiful, compelling, simple, inspiring, encouraging
For ANYONE who will at some time in their life experience pregnancy, labor and birth, whether actively or as a supporter. The first half of the book shares beautiful, personal stories of labor and birth, which have taken place under the care of Ina May or one of her fellow Farm-midwives. As the author, Ina May also adds her reasonings of why certain situations occurred and how she safely helped the mothers through them. The second half reveals facts and statistics of the many procedures that often accompany labor and birth from BOTH the medical AND the midwifery world of labor and birth in America compared with other developed countries. It is a warm, loving, nurturing book that supports and informs readers of the natural process of labor and birth that a woman's and a baby's body endures, without too many big or science-based words. It shines light onto the myths about how long a woman takes to labor, how long into a pregnancy one can be before inducing, how UNNECESSARY the majority of cesarian sections are in our country, and much much more!!! It is FASCINATING, APPALLING (when you read of the unnecessary statistics-that leave moms in MORE and LONGER-lasting pain than the actual birth would have-that occur in hospitals), UPLIFTING, BEAUTIFUL, PROFOUND and AMAZING. I say it is FEARLESS because prior to reading this book, I was always afraid of the pain that was associated with giving birth. When I finished it, I was SO EMPOWERED I could have given birth that minute:) This book opened my eyes to a WHOLE NEW WORLD of childbirth that works WONDERFULLY WELL when it is approached with the utmost love, compassion, naturalness and awareness, to ensure the safety or both mother and child. It INSTILLS POWER into the mother that she is capable of having a pleasurable birthing experience, one that is potentially pain-free! It is a MUST-READ for anyone that is open and willing to accept the most AMAZING process of our existence:) I say this because I do know one person who I've bought this for and she wasn't too psyched on the idea... but she is also afraid of giving birth and wasn't open enough to let go of her fear, which the book explains is unnecessary, and to surrender to the PERFECT PHYSIOLOGICAL PROCESS that she will endure! Everyone else I've bought this book for, they have LOVED it, and felt empowered as well, and recommend it to their friends, as well! I hope you enjoy it as much as I did...
