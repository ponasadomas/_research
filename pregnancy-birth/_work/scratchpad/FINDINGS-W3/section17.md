### 17.7 Method note

- **Pressure-to-end exclusion rule.** From PR57 on, readers were told to exclude any unit that mentions pressure from anyone to end the pregnancy, even in passing; PR57, PR60–PR64, PR66, PR67 and PR69–PR72 record applying it in their ledgers, and PR63 withdrew one quote under a strict reading (its id is burned and not in the merged file). Before that the README rule covered only posts centred on the decision: PR55 kept one unit with an ex-partner's pressure mentioned in passing (pregnant-1skt4xm); its two quotes are restricted (pb55-0014, pb55-0015 (restricted)). The rule removes posts about coercive partners and families, so the partner and family picture in wave 3 is, if anything, milder than the posts were.
- **Restriction share by reader.** 900 of 7,332 wave-3 quotes are restricted (12%). It runs from 5% (PR46, purchase chunks) and 6% (PR47 purchase, PR50 other-side) to 21% (PR49, birth-story success chunks) and 19% (PR53). Core chunks sit between 8% and 19%. The share tracks what a chunk is about, so success chunks (birth stories) lose the most, and the birth itself is again thin: 88 wave-3 quotes are at stage `birth`.
- **Seasonal effects.** Wave 3 runs from early April to late June. Heat is 52 wave-3 quotes, mostly late May and June (PR64, PR68–PR72), against 188 in wave 2's July heatwaves. The stage mix is not the cause of the work finding in 17.2: first-trimester quotes are 15.1% of both wave 2 (831) and wave 3 (1,105), second-trimester 14.4% and 14.7%, third-trimester 26.4% and 26.9%. What differs is what readers were asked to test; they looked for the dated plan and many reported its absence, which is a stronger signal than a confirmation (interpretation). PR61's window is US Mother's Day week; PR46's April chunks hold almost no heat and few visitor posts.
- **Briefing and vocabulary effects.** Readers were given §16.2 and the tests in 17.2, so rises in `care_navigation` (17 → 349), `guide_request` (20 → 84), `sugar_coated` (47 → 114), `stock_lines` (234 → 346) and the new `dated_plan` (19) are mostly prompted. Tags both waves used heavily, as shares of each wave's quotes (wave 1 / 2 / 3): `work_in_pregnancy` 10.1 / 10.8 / 11.5%, `maternity_leave` 5.3 / 5.5 / 5.4%, `partner_role` 14.7 / 12.8 / 12.0%, `pregnancy_with_toddler` 7.0 / 6.7 / 6.0%, `visitors` 4.3 / 5.3 / 4.4%, `mother_in_law` 2.6 / 4.3 / 3.7%, `own_mother` 0.8 / 4.9 / 5.0%, `first_two_weeks` 9.3 / 7.3 / 6.7%, `information_overload` 4.3 / 3.6 / 3.5%, `ob_prepayment` 0 / 0.5 / 0.3%.
- **Non-independent posts.** Seeded, AI-written and rage-bait posts listed in each reader note were not quoted or were quoted as one voice; none above is counted as independent support. Nine quote pairs in wave 3 carry identical text under two ids because two readers met the same post or a crosspost (e.g. pb51-0173 = pb66-0249, pb46-0124 = pb50-0056, pb70-0316 = pb71-0015); each pair is one voice. Where a pattern above leans on one author I say so (pb48-0064 to pb48-0076; pb50-0230 to pb50-0233; pb47-0094 to pb47-0098). 'N of 27 notes' counts reader notes, not people.
