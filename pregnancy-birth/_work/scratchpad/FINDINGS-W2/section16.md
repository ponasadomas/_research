Those units were kept at triage from 4,170 tier-A cards dated 25 June – 24 August (`_raw/reddit/filter-v1/wave2/cards-index.jsonl`; chunks in `_raw/reddit/read-w2/chunks.jsonl`: 2,482 keep_core, 254 keep_purchase, 217 keep_otherside, 130 keep_success units). Every number below is a count of quotes the readers chose, never of people. Wave-1/wave-2 splits are my own count (command in 16.6). Reader notes: `synthesis/reader-notes-pr23.md` … `reader-notes-pr45.md`.

**Read this first.** The readers were given section 10's claims and asked to check them, so 'confirms' is weaker evidence than a pattern found without prompting. The places where they contradict or refine a claim are the stronger signal. Theme tags also changed between waves (16.6), so I compare waves only on tags both waves used.

### 16.5 What this changes for a Pirmi-style guide at $17.95 / $29.95 / $49.95 (interpretation, tied to the ids above)

- **Work and leave, US care deadlines, his leave, who minds the older child and the visit plan belong in the product, as one dated worksheet rather than four chapters.** In her words they are one calendar. His return-to-work date sets the visitor window [pb27-0120, pb45-0249]. The visit date is fixed by flights before the baby's is known [pb26-0067, pb35-0052]. The older child's cover depends on who is in town that week [pb40-0164, pb38-0157]. Her leave start depends on an OB's form and a tenure rule [pb37-0160, pb45-0039]. The worksheet has to start in the first trimester, not the third. The 24-week payment deadline [pb23-0162], disability cover that can't be bought once pregnant [pb42-0180], tenure cliffs [pb45-0039] and job choices made at five weeks (PR40) all fall early. What it can honestly hold: dates to find things out by, questions for HR, the benefits administrator and the OB's billing office, a leave-adding sheet for both parents, a visit calendar (arrival, length, where they sleep, who pays, what they do), an older-child plan with a plan B, and 'if you are asked to prepay' questions (itemised bill, financial assistance, what happens if you change practice). What it must not do: state an entitlement, an amount owed or a legal right. Every leave and billing line above is one woman's account. A US edition and a UK edition are the minimum.
- **Pregnancy number is supported, but as a gate into her situation, not as the personalisation itself.** Second-time content must assume she has a child to mind, less time [pb24-0124], care that may brush her off [pb37-0121] and a first birth that may not predict this one [pb30-0090]. Generic second-time content ('you know this') is what she complains about.
- **Weekly content: give ranges and plan Bs, never promises and single numbers.** The second-trimester line is the one she hears every week [pb30-0178]. Single weight targets hurt [pb37-0205].
- **The partner piece: short, task-based, audio or video, framed as his job** [pb26-0155, pb24-0004, pb42-0121], and including his leave arithmetic.
- **Care navigation**: a 'who does what, who to call' page per country [pb43-0218, pb42-0208].
- **How to answer 'everything online is an ad'** [pb24-0044, pb24-0013, pb34-0134], from what this audience says it trusts and distrusts:
  - no outcome promise, since a promised "perfect birthing experience" is what she later resents [pb41-0070];
  - nothing sold inside it;
  - neutral wording over positive spin [pb23-0083, pb23-0086], plain about what may not get better [pb45-0112];
  - say who wrote it and why;
  - a country edition;
  - its own leave and billing lines framed as questions to ask, not answers;
  - a format that isn't a feed [pb45-0053];
  - and say what free sources already do well rather than calling them scams.

  The strongest honest point (interpretation): what she asks for, everything in one place in order [pb23-0004], does not exist free. One woman looked for it and decided to write her own [pb23-0005].
- **Price.** Wave 2 doesn't change the wave-1 picture and makes the ceiling a little clearer (16.3). The 1/3/6-month ladder still doesn't map onto a pregnancy, and wave 2 makes that worse: the worksheet she needs runs from the first trimester to about six weeks after the birth.

### 16.6 Method note

- **Restriction is at quote level, and the merged file decides.** Several quotes that reader notes used freely are restricted in `voice-merged/all.jsonl` and are cited above only with the label (restricted): the UK childcare lines pb43-0234 (restricted), pb43-0235 (restricted) and pb43-0237 (restricted); pb26-0002 (restricted); pb32-0048 (restricted); pb23-0080 (restricted); pb40-0171 (restricted); pb34-0128 (restricted). Restriction follows the unit: a crisis or emergency line anywhere in a post can restrict an ordinary money quote from the same post.
- **Theme tags changed between waves.** Readers adopted new tags in wave 2, so tag counts can't show a rise. `own_mother` 42 quotes in wave 1 vs 272 in wave 2, `stock_lines` 53 vs 234, `guide_request` 2 vs 20 and `us_insurance` 173 vs 17 reflect vocabulary, not prevalence. I compare waves only on tags both used heavily: `work_in_pregnancy` 551/594, `maternity_leave` 289/303, `partner_role` 800/707, `pregnancy_with_toddler` 383/368, `visitors` 234/290, `mother_in_law` 143/240, `first_two_weeks` 508/403, `information_overload` 232/199, `week_by_week` 88/74. For tags new to wave 2 I give the wave-2 count alone.
- **Summer over-represents heat.** Wave 2 is June–August and covers the European and US heatwaves of late June and July 2026. Wave 1 is late August–October. The triage notes also flagged heat, wildfire smoke, overseas visits, OB prepayment, doula prices and free registry boxes, and readers were asked to look for them. So the 188 heat-tagged quotes are a season plus a briefing effect, not a year-round share.
- **Readers were briefed with section 10's claims.** Their 'confirmed' lines are weaker evidence than a pattern found without prompting. The refinements and contradictions above (stories can harm, a decision method beats a plan, pb41-0217 is a seller, partners who help, women who refuse warnings) are the more independent findings.
- **Non-independent posts.** Seeded, AI-written, rage-bait and engagement posts listed in each reader note were not quoted or were quoted as one voice. Crossposts and same-author reposts were quoted once. Where a pattern above leans on a single author I say so (pb28-0173, pb33-0087 to pb33-0091, pb30-0118). 'Readers report' and 'N of 23 notes' count reader notes, not people.
- **Gaps that remain.** The birth itself and the hardest first weeks are still thin, because acute medical, crisis and loss posts were excluded or restricted (PR24, PR25, PR38 and PR40 say so). Tier B and C, the safety-flagged posts, and tier A before 25 June are unread (section 13).
- **Commands.** Wave splits, theme counts, heat, smoke and AI wording: `python3 -I pregnancy-birth/scratchpad/FINDINGS-W2/w2counts.py` (run from `pregnancy-birth/`). Restricted-id check for this section: `python3 -I pregnancy-birth/scratchpad/FINDINGS-W2/check_restricted.py`.
