# Reader notes — PR25 (pregnancy-birth, wave 2: keep_success 003–012, keep_otherside 001–002)

Reader's interpretation; every pattern cites pb25 ids in `../voice/reddit-pr25.jsonl`. Counts are of quotes chosen, not of people. 128 units read, 17 excluded, 11 read with no quote, 348 quotes (42 restricted). Ledger: `../sources/reddit-reading-pr25.md`.

These chunks are mostly "I graduated" birth stories (keep_success) plus in-law and partner posts (keep_otherside). Many birth stories sit on the emergency line; I excluded the ones centred on an emergency and restricted procedure lines in the rest, so the usable material skews toward what surrounds the birth: who is there, who is told what, what preparation was worth, and the first days.

## Patterns

1. **The subreddit and other women's birth stories are the preparation she credits.** Graduation posts open or close by thanking the sub: pb25-0010, 0051, 0055, 0066, 0071, 0081, 0114, 0157, 0182, 0202, 0262. Several say they posted because one similar story would have helped them: "I know reading something like this when I was in the pregnancy trenches would have helped me a lot." [pb25-0141]; also pb25-0012, 0043, 0126, 0235, 0253, 0310. Reading also feeds fear: "I had read so many scary birth stories" [pb25-0068]; pb25-0047, 0045 (restricted), 0201 (restricted), 0222, 0140, 0180, 0208, 0162 (restricted). **Confirms wave-1 §10 Against-2** (they trust stories over documents) and For-4 (overload): the guide would compete with this habit, not replace it.

2. **Preparation that "went out the window" — and the voices that say it still paid.** Against: "Everything I prepped for: the teas, the dates, the acupuncture, the birth prep classes, the information about natural birth - thrown out the window lol." [pb25-0076]; a second-time mother dropping the classes, creams and label-reading she did the first time [pb25-0093, 0094]; "glad I didn't spend hours on my birth plan" [pb25-0213]; "the only thing that materialised from there is the vitamin k injection" [pb25-0042]; pb25-0041, 0145 (restricted), 0242. For: hypnobirthing's decision method "was absolute key" [pb25-0056, 0057]; hypnobirthing practice reused for surgical recovery [pb25-0160, restricted]; "Next baby I have I will definitely attend birthing classes and possibly find a mw or doula" [pb25-0054]; doula "so worth it as a FTM" [pb25-0074]. Mixed, as in wave 1 (§10 Against-4); new nuance: what is judged worth it is a way of deciding when plans change (BRAIN, "be flexible"), not the plan [pb25-0056, 0057, 0311, 0243, 0282].

3. **Hospital and birth-class marketing vs the night itself.** "At the classes they were so big on saying how supported mothers are with 1:1 nursing" [pb25-0147] then a nurse who refused to check her [pb25-0148] and an intercom telling her to watch TV [pb25-0149]; her husband "do everything he learned in the classes but it was clearly out of his hands" [pb25-0150]; "like a fool for thinking I'd have a better experience there" [pb25-0152]. A milder version: "be prepared to be a bit unprepared" [pb25-0102, restricted]. This is the "felt like I'd been lied to" line from paid/hospital preparation.

4. **First-time mothers not believed in labour.** "They always think FTMs are dramatic." [pb25-0266]; "I believed the professionals" [pb25-0267]; "kept being told I wasn't in active labor yet" [pb25-0264]; told her waters were urine [pb25-0265]; "Crazy advice." about being told to wait for 1–2-minute contractions [pb25-0117, 0118]; fear of being sent home [pb25-0033, 0184]; "Of course they said 'oh that's normal'" [pb25-0037, 0038].
