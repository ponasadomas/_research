# Reader notes — PR49 (wave 3, read-w3 keep_success 007–021 + keep_otherside 001–003)

153 units, 320 quotes (66 restricted). Posts 1 April – 24 June 2026. Interpretation is mine; quote ids carry the evidence. Ids marked (R) are restricted: cite the id, not the words.

**Selection effect, read first.** 109 of my 153 units are keep_success "graduation" posts: birth stories written after the birth, mostly positive. Their core is birth procedure (induction, epidural, surgery, tears), so a lot was restricted and the voice is new mothers looking back (194 of 320 quotes). The 44 otherside units are early-April partner, relative and family-conflict posts. Patterns below are about what these two piles say, not about pregnancy in general.
