## Patterns

1. **"It will be worth it" and "just wait for the second trimester" are the stock lines that land worst.** "it will all be worth it when he's in your arms" / "you'll forget the pain once he's out" do not reach birth fear [0023]; a mother answers every "I can't get out of bed" with "well he will be worth it" [0089, 0090]; "Everyone kept saying just wait until the second" [0026], "Everyone says the 2nd trimester is amazing" [0221], "I keep hearing things will get better" [0231], "no sign of this 2nd trimester energy I've heard about" [0004]. "People keep asking if I am excited but I am stressed and sad" [0247]; questions about showers, registries and the nursery make an 18-week mother angry [0220]. Others push: "just push through" [0029], "you might change my mind" about no epidural [0186, restricted], four women at work saying inductions are the worst [0102], "everyone … thinks I'm overreacting" [0121, 0191], a boss's dress code [0214]. For second babies: "the second is easier" [0135] and the "second vaginal birth … much easier" story [0110, restricted].
2. **Family as visitors: the first days are fought over before the birth.** In-laws to a couple planning three visitor-free weeks: "You guys are taking our excitement and happiness away" … "does she have something wrong with her" [0070–0072]; a mother who walks into the room the day after a section and later says "That is like telling the queen to leave. I am the grandparent!" [0114–0117]; a father's "Your milk is waste" a month in [0041]. A looking-back PSA: two in-law visits of two hours each on day one, too uncomfortable to use the bathroom, "Decide how long visits should be, too" [0182–0185]. Family also takes over the shower: a mother-in-law adds 20 out-of-town guests and the cousin sends the mother the invoices [0081–0083]; grandmothers "waiting SO long" will not skip Christmas gifts [0002].
3. **The partner gap, in both directions.** Women: he goes to events while she is home with an 11-day-old, "my entire life has changed overnight while his hasn't" [0130–0133]; "he gets to keep his body" [0222]; "so angry that he gets to experience happiness while I'm miserable" [0091]; "not fully understanding what a woman's body goes through" [0235]; "it will never be 'just him and I' again" [0234]; a thrifty, unexcited husband who dampens her bond [0076, 0077]; "I now cannot stand this man" at 7 weeks [0127]. Partners matter in labour: "he is my assigned advocate" [0188]; a section under general anaesthetic would keep him out, "I've robbed him from this experience" [0124]; a partner who still has to build the crib as labour starts [0097]. From the other side: a wife (pregnant partner's spouse) deferring, "She's the one going through all of this" [0001–0003]; a UK mother writing to r/daddit that staff never asked how her partner was and she had to request resources for him [0144, 0145]. Fathers' leave: annual leave saved for 4 weeks [0155]; parental-leave plan due before childcare is known [0139]; intermittent leave because a new job is not protected [0212].
4. **Pregnancy with a toddler is guilt plus logistics.** "I dread having to actually parent my toddler" and Uber Eats guilt at 11 weeks [0161–0164]; nightly sobbing over "2 under 2" fed by videos, and leaving him for the hospital [0168–0171]; a 3-year-old turning to her father "as I become increasingly pregnant" [0061]; a clinic that sends her home for bringing her toddler, "what the fuck am I supposed to do?" With a newborn: the "lovely bubble" with two children that didn't arrive [0136, 0137].
5. **Second pregnancy: "now I know".** "now I know how long that road really is" [0162]; the one symptom "I'm horrified to be experiencing again" [0224]; terrified of another vaginal birth after a severe tear [0108, 0109 restricted]; afraid of missing labour signs again [0051, 0052]; tired at 8 weeks as she was in the last trimester, maternity wear at 8 weeks not 16 [0036]; "My first pregnancy was so nice!"
