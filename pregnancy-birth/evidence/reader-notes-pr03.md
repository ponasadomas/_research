## Patterns

1. **"Be flexible with the plan" is what birth-success posters say helped most, more than any course.** Again and again the plan fell apart and the mother says that was fine: [pb03-0005] [pb03-0008] [pb03-0026] [pb03-0060] [pb03-0102]. Some go further and say obsessing over the plan backfires [pb03-0198] [pb03-0204]. A gap they name: they prepared for the birth they wanted, not the one they got. "Learn about c-sections anyway" [pb03-0020] [pb03-0018], and nobody prepares you for what comes right after the birth [pb03-0147].
2. **The fear before was worse than the thing itself.** Dread of the epidural, of induction, of pain, then "the anticipation was way worse" [pb03-0001] [pb03-0073] [pb03-0119] [pb03-0121] [pb03-0050] [pb03-0024]. Low pain tolerance "and if I could do it any one can" [pb03-0117]. For copy this is a *feeling* line, never a promise about outcomes.
3. **Reading other women's birth stories is the preparation they actually used** [pb03-0014] [pb03-0029] [pb03-0118]. Some read only positive stories [pb03-0104]; negative stories made them afraid [pb03-0024] [pb03-0021]. They also binge packing-list content "to feel more in control" [pb03-0089].
4. **First-time mothers, UK triage and "you'll just know".** NHS call-in rules (3–4 contractions in 10, 5-1-1), being sent home, "it could be weeks", and being afraid of looking silly: [pb03-0031] [pb03-0033] [pb03-0034] [pb03-0133] [pb03-0135] [pb03-0144] [pb03-0245] [pb03-0246]. No bed, sent 35 minutes away, 17 minutes on hold [pb03-0134] [pb03-0137]. Induction delays and not being told what was happening [pb03-0040] [pb03-0054]. The partner is often the one who rings [pb03-0032].
5. **The first two weeks come as a shock**, and it's the feeding that hits hardest. "Nobody really prepares you for this part" [pb03-0003]; "Taking care of a newborn is the hard part" [pb03-0199]; formula guilt and wishing someone had said it's fine [pb03-0006] [pb03-0076] [pb03-0078] [pb03-0100]. Fathers say breastfeeding's "true nature is disguised" [pb03-0183] and that the hospital classes didn't prepare them [pb03-0228] [pb03-0230].
6. **Later pregnancies are judged against the first.** Mother of three: pandemic first birth, a better second, a calm third [pb03-0074]–[pb03-0079]. After a frightening first postpartum, a second pregnancy spent "preparing for history repeating itself" [pb03-0087] [pb03-0088]. Pregnancy with a toddler from the father's side: leave, solo-parenting, debt, preparing the sibling [pb03-0148] [pb03-0149] [pb03-0177] [pb03-0214] [pb03-0215] [pb03-0226].

## Contrary evidence

- Preparation did help some: videos before a caesarean [pb03-0018], a birth plan easing fear [pb03-0048], hypnobirthing mindset [pb03-0146], child-development courses [pb03-0159]. Self-advocacy posts say research matters [pb03-0056] [pb03-0084].
- Hypnobirthing got mixed reports: a Positive Birth Company book technique was dropped while pushing [pb03-0143]; a heavily prepared mother still met tools she had never heard of [pb03-0053]; hospital breathing techniques "pissed me off" [pb03-0198].
- Flexibility as preparation contradicts the courses' promise of a calm unmedicated birth. Some got exactly the birth they planned and credit their mindset [pb03-0065] [pb03-0146].
- A Dutch mother's €30 kraampakket was her only purchase [pb03-0068]. Cost of birth barely comes up in these chunks.
