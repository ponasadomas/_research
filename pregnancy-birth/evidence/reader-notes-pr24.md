- Every US amount is one woman's bill or letter, not a norm; never state what a global fee or deposit "should" be. Country edition needed (US billing vs UK private-wing pricing vs NHS scheduling).
- Rule used for keep_success: units whose account includes an acute emergency or a serious scan finding were excluded; a woman's own preference for or against pain relief or an unmedicated birth (0029, 0097, 0229, 0243, 0246) is restricted; a course's bias towards one kind of birth (0074, 0136) is not. - Doula failure quotes (0119–0123) are one woman's account of a named insurer's rules, and are restricted because an OP reply in the unit describes an emergency birth.
