## About this app

arrow_forward

The Bump’s free pregnancy app is the most loved pregnancy and baby tracker for expecting and new parents, providing features you won’t find on other baby apps.

** FEATURES **

PREGNANCY APP AND BABY TRACKER
The Bump includes comprehensive tools to help you throughout conception, pregnancy, birth, and postpartum. From our ovulation tracker, due date calculator, expert advice, and post-brith tools such as breastfeeding, diaper log, and baby milestones, The Bump is here for you every step of the way.

CONTRACTION TIMER
Track your contractions as you get ready to welcome your little one into the world. With just a press of a button, you can easily time your contractions to easily help you along your birth plan.

BABY NAMES
Use our baby name game to swipe through traditional, modern, and unique names to find the perfect fit for your baby. You can also browse The Bump’s specially curated lists of baby names and search by length, country of origin, meaning and much more.

BREASTFEEDING TRACKER
Track your baby’s feeding schedule easily with The Bump. Effortlessly log breastfeeding sessions with just a few taps-record the start and end times of each feeding, along with which breast was used, ensuring you stay organized and informed. Track pumping sessions during any time of the day or night, and know how much you have on hand. Using formula? Track feeding with our bottle schedule tool.

3D INTERACTIVE BABY GROWTH TRACKER
The Bump compares baby’s size and development in utero to beautifully illustrated produce (“Baby Is As Big As a Peach”) in a way that’s fun and easy to share with friends and family. See an exciting and exclusive 3D interactive visualization of baby's week-by-week growth. Interact and learn new facts about baby like never before with the next step in baby size trackers.

KICK COUNTER
The Bump is your reliable companion for tracking your baby's kicks effortlessly. With simple features and intuitive design, it helps expecting parents monitor fetal movement and ensure their baby's well-being with ease

BABY TRACKER NEWBORN LOG
Easily track your newborn’s feeding and diaper changes with all our newborn tools to help get your infant on the right schedule.

DAILY ADVICE
Every day, the award-winning editorial staff of The Bump delivers fresh and relevant content for your specific week of pregnancy. The articles are timely and comprehensive: always know what’s safe and standard; find out how to ward off morning sickness; learn the best things to pack in your hospital bag; and discover the best prenatal workouts for you.

PLANNER+
A feature that equips every expecting mom with important information about their prenatal doctor visits. It suggests questions to ask your doctor and integrates appointments seamlessly with your mobile phone's calendar.

BABY REGISTRY
The Bump has collected the top registry products across Amazon, Target and so much more, complete with reviews from parents who’ve been where you are. This registry has only grown over time, so you can be sure you’ll find everything you want and need.

PREGNANCY AND BABY PHOTOS
Document your pregnancy by creating a weekly album of your happily growing belly. And once baby is born, the album expands to track their first amazing year in the world.

CUSTOMER SERVICE
The Bump team reads every email, answers each phone call and takes all of your reviews to heart.
