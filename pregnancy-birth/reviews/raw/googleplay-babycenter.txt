## About this app

arrow_forward

BabyCenter is the go-to pregnancy tracker, parenting resource and family planning app for expecting families at every stage of your pregnancy journey—from conception, through your baby’s infancy years. Designed for pregnancy, childbirth, and parenthood, our pregnancy & baby tracker provides daily updates, week-by-week insights, and expert backed resources to support your growing family. Connect with the BabyCenter community and access medically reviewed content to enhance your pregnancy, motherhood, and parenting experience.

Enter your due date or use our Pregnancy Due Date Calculator to personalize your newborn tracker with updates for every step. Log weekly maternity bump photos, log and track your baby’s developmental milestones, stages and growth with 3-D videos, milestones, and expert-reviewed articles. Join our free loyalty program to earn points and redeem for exclusive discounts and products.

BabyCenter’s free pregnancy and baby size tracker continues to support you after your newborn’s arrival with daily updates, tools like the Baby Growth Tracker, baby sleep log, feeding schedules and guides for your baby, twins or toddler.

All health information is written by experts and reviewed and approved by the BabyCenter Medical Advisory Board. These doctors and professionals ensure that our pregnancy and parenting information is thorough and accurate for women and their babies.

Pregnancy & Childbirth
* Take Baby Safety and childbirth classes — now free for all members
* Check your baby’s growth during pregnancy with our 3-D fetal development videos
* Find help and tips about common pregnancy symptoms and questions
* Enjoy pregnancy workouts, infant food guides and nutrition advice tailored to your trimester
* Use our Pregnancy Calendar to track appointments and symptoms like nausea and morning sickness
* Find the best pregnancy and baby products recommended by parents and editors
* Prepare for babies arrival with the Baby Registry Checklist and Builder
* Count down and prepare for the big day with our printable hospital bag checklist and birth plan

Parenthood
* Use our Baby Growth Tracker to chart your baby’s size, fetus development and big milestones
* Get ideas for fun baby and toddler games and activities to boost your child’s development
* Use the Breastfeeding, formula and Solid food Feeding Guide to solve common challenges

Starting a Family
* Track ovulation and fertility with our Ovulation Calculator
* Learn which prenatal vitamins experts recommend
* Spot the early signs of pregnancy by tracking symptoms

BabyCenter Community
* Find a supportive space and connect with moms, parents and parents-to-be
* Join your Birth Club to meet people with due dates in the same month
* Ask questions, share stories, build connections and find support from other expecting mothers, fathers and families

Pregnancy Apps & Tools
* Ovulation Calculator: Predict your fertile window while TTC
* Pregnancy Due Date Calculator: Calculate your baby’s due date
* Registry Builder: Research your favorite pregnancy and baby products
* Birth Plan Template: Document your preferences for your birth experience
* Contraction Timer: Track contractions during late pregnancy and labor
* Baby Name Generator
* Baby Kick Tracker

An Award-Winning Experience
BabyCenter is proud to be recognized by leading organizations for its excellence in delivering expert content and top-of-the-line experiences to the parents who visit our site and use our pregnancy app and baby tracker app.
