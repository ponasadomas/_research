  • Enjoyed the tracking
    07/07/2020
    xcvnncnn

    Have really enjoyed using this app. I have found the weekly updates really helpful to visualise what’s going on with baby and also to reassure me r.e. common signs and symptoms. I’m now nearly 34 weeks with first baby. I found the new symptoms every week were incredible accurate and would be reading through them thinking- oh that’s what that is called. It’s very settling to feel that your pregnancy experience is normal and allows me to bring up anything that isn’t mentioned with my midwife instead.I haven’t used much more of the app than the my pregnancy section. In community- I find some of the chat groups a bit weird and off topic- about strangers mother in law problems and a lot of it is very dramatic and I find that unhelpful, but I’m sure if you needed some support you could probably find it in the community section and join some groups. I use another app that you can visualise the baby full size and also tells you how many days your pregnant/ can upload bump pics to show you at each stage. I really like to see the number of days of pregnancy as well as weeks so maybe that is a suggestion for how to improve this app!

  • Very Informative and a lovely Support Network
    07/06/2021
    CLS@V

    In very early pregnancy we are told we shouldn’t tell people we are pregnant. This puts a huge amount of pressure and frustration on women as our bodies are changing, our hormones are raging, and most of the time we are just plain terrified of losing the pregnancy. We have so many questions and so few people to turn to. The app itself provides lots of really useful information and the community section is active 24/7 so there is always someone to talk to. This platform provides an anonymous, non-judgemental and widely experienced group of women from all over the world, sharing their feelings and experiences. I would be quite lost without it.

  • Inconsistent app. Abusive community.
    15/03/2020
    ursitsitstsyd

    Beware that once you have made an account there is no delete/deactivate option. You cannot delete your own posts in the community or delete/edit your comments on someone else’s post.I loved the app when I first started using in my first trimester. However I found that as the weeks went on the size comparisons became very inconsistent. One week baby is as big as a cucumber and then 3 weeks later a grapefruit. In my head a cucumber is longer than a grapefruit. Just one example that stuck out for me.Weekly baby updates aren’t gender neutral. One minute they’ll refer to the baby as “he” the next as “she”. This doesn’t change if you add the gender to the app.I agree that the app is very American-orientated. Most people in the community section are American and a lot of the information provided by WTE is based on the assumption that you’re living in America. If you still choose to download this app for whatever reason, avoid the community. It’s full of hateful, bitter, keyboard warriors who know better than any other mums on the board. It’s inundated with anti-vaxx advocates and crazy self-absorbed women complaining that the gift they received for their baby shower was under $100 or that the shower that was kindly thrown for them free of charge was not the perfect one they’d dreamt of their whole life. The crazy negativity and entitlement ruined the whole app for me.All in all, if you download the app, take their information with a pinch of salt as it’s inconsistent, inaccurate and just avoid the community.

  • Blessing and a curse!
    08/07/2020
    ejs1212123

    The app is great for reading bits of advice, learning about size of baby, what to expect each week etc. However, I do feel that it can exacerbate some anxieties. On more than one occasion I have gone onto a generic thread only to find posts about miscarriage. I understand that this is the experience of many and that people will want somewhere to vent, grieve etc. However, for other expectant moms, I do feel that this can only cause additional worry, especially when you have accessed a thread that is seemingly completely unrelated. This has made me use the app less and less, as I am constantly fearful that this is something that I will accidentally stumble across. Miscarriage is something that expectant moms are already fearful of and I do feel that this should be more moderated!
