REVIEW pregnancyplus-us-6695490397 | 3 stars | 2020-11-28T15:08:52.000Z
TITLE: Needs updated verbiage around breastfeeding articles
I love this app for its general informational purposes and tracking purposes, and I wish I could give it five stars. However, there’s a very evident push to persuade mothers to breastfeed, with one article even explicitly stating: “Breastfeeding is still the best.”  This type of explicit pressuring is incredibly insensitive to the mothers for whom breastfeeding isn’t an option, and disregards very recent research that suggests that, while there may be some genuine medical and emotional benefits to breast feeding, a generous portion of the difference in health and behavioral metrics for breastfed versus formula fed babies are actually due to socioeconomic conditions that would better account for the purported benefits of breastfeeding- socioeconomic factors that were never controlled for in most of the most widely cited articles by breastfeeding advocates. Even as a breastfeeding mother, I find, as do many medical professionals in the field, that taking a hardline stance is irresponsible at best. Mothers deserve a full breadth of education on both choices in order to make their own decisions.

With that aside, I’m not saying that the apps authors shouldn’t present the benefits of breastfeeding to upcoming mothers. I’m just encouraging the authors to reconsider verbiage that may be biased, insensitive and potentially misleading, as in the example above, and rather that they would take an impartial stance in presenting the pros and cons of both choices. The one thing evidence is clear on for the well-being of the mother and the baby: either choice may be a healthier fit for one mother, and not the other. Some articles within the app do better at achieving this balance. Others fall short.
