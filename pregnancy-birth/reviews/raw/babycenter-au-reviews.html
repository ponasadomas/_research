  • Highly recommend!
    14/06/2022
    Katarina12345678

    I’m a first time mum and I’m absolutely loving the app. It has awesome weekly updates about your baby during pregnancy as well as when they are a baby. The weekly updates also have links to relevant articles - most of the time the links are things I’ve literally been starting to wonder about that week and then they appear :) In addition to their articles, the forums are amazing. You are grouped into a forum with others with the same due date. This means the questions others ask are often similar to what I was experiencing/wondering at the time. It’s comforting both pregnant and as a new mum to not only know that others are experiencing the same issues/challenges/achievements but also to hear other mum’s ideas/tips/advice and/or just “I’m in the same boat too”. I have to admit that when I get up for my son’s early morning feed, one of my first things I do when I get on my phone is check my emails for the link to the latest forum updates. Thank you Baby Center for being such a valuable resource :)

  • Please add feature
    10/02/2021
    Rose_potter

    2 years ago I submitted a review and was emailed in response. I was pleased that my review had been read and I was hopeful that it would be actioned upon. Pregnant again, I am saddened to see that no changes were implemented. This app needs an option for the sad even of a stillbirth. There are 6 babies stillborn everyday in Australia. This app is an incredibly popular app amongst pregnant mothers, this means that mothers are confronted with having to select that their baby has arrived with a joyful animation of a baby and celebrations with the text ‘enjoy the snuggles’. To see this, when you have just delivered your deceased baby is horrible. Parents then have a choice- to either delete that child from the app (imagine deleting your child because they died...) or pretend that their baby was fine and enter their details and have milestone emails continue to be sent. A constant reminder that you buried your precious baby, and no they aren’t starting solids.... this impacts and adds to trauma. PLEASE add an option where you can select that your baby was born but grew it’s angel wings. Associated emails could then have targeted support, like links to Red Nose, stillbirth foundation and bears of hope. Please make the right change, for the sake of helping reduce the impacts of trauma. I truly hope to hear back from you and see changes to your app. Rose

    Developer Response

    Hi Rose_potter, We really appreciate you getting in touch with us again about this important and sensitive topic. Please be assured that we have not forgotten about this. We have added an option to our US app now for users who have sadly suffered a loss. We are looking into rolling this out to the apps in our other markets too. We’re sorry this hasn’t been as fast it would ideally have been. It is no reflection on how much we respect and support our users who have lost a baby. We appreciate you being so patient with us while we work on developing this functionality. Kind regards, The BabyCenter Team.

  • One sided and outdated info
    13/03/2020
    Renata Pacheco

    As a first time mum and not knowing much of what I was doing in the start I thought the app was helpful as some sort of guidance. Nowadays after having a quite difficult baby and reading and learning a lot about child development I can see the info provided is often outdated and limited. For example, even though they don’t push this sort of opinion down your throat they do suggest options of sleep training rather than explaining and normalizing infant “normal” sleep and explaining why babies sleep like they do. This week I got an email suggesting time outs for a 2 year old and I was shocked as I know for a fact this could never work positively for any child can you imagine for a 2yo. That’s why I’m writing this review. If you are an inexperienced mumma do your own research, and maybe just use the app as a guide. Always make your own mind up and listen to your child. Learning about their brain development and why they act the way they do was extremely important for me as a mum

    Developer Response

    Hi,Thank you for your review. We will share your feedback with the appropriate members of our team here at BabyCenter, and we welcome any additional feedback you have in the future.ThanksBabyCenter Team

  • Incredibly helpful and reassuring, just one small tweak needed!
    15 Apr
    Ichelle555

    Rating: ★★★★☆I’ve been using the BabyCenter app throughout my pregnancy and it has been an absolute lifesaver. This is my first pregnancy. I still find myself checking it daily for updates.The week-by-week development updates are spot on. It is so reassuring to see exactly how the baby is growing (the fruit comparisons are a fun touch!) and to understand what is happening with my own body. The articles are evidence-based and really help translate medical jargon into things that actually make sense. The community groups are also a great place to connect with others who are at the exact same stage as me and I can ask questions too. The only thing that prevents this from being a 5-star review is the notification system. I find that I get quite a few "alert" style notifications for articles or community threads that aren't always relevant to my specific interests. I’d love to be able to set my own interests see and have a bit more control over which types of articles l read or so I don't miss the important developmental milestones among the general lifestyle content.Overall, this is a must-have for any expecting parent. Extremely helpful.

  • Great app for sharing experiences
    14/10/2019
    EmmaWoolly

    I have found babycenter great for the chat boards. It sorts you into birth month so everyone is reasonably close together in terms of birth age. It’s good to know what stage other babies are at and get tips from other mothers, of course bearing in mind when it is best to get professional advice. I like that it tells you how many new posts there are on each subject that you haven’t read yet. The articles are reasonably good and mirror the advice in most other places eg books, raising children website. I would not use babycentre articles as the main authority, but they are generally on the mark in terms of contemporary advice.
