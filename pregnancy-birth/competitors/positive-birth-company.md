## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

This is the category leader in the UK and the closest thing to a "pregnancy guide" product: a £39 Pregnancy Pack that walks the nine months, beside a £39 birth course, bundled at £59. It sets an anchor of roughly £39 per stage-pack and shows the ladder logic (pregnancy → birth → postpartum → early years) that Mamivio's 1/3/6-month tiers echo. What it does not do is ask anything: no week, no pregnancy number, no situation. A personalised trimester guide would compete on "made for my week and my second pregnancy", not on content volume, where 6–9 hours of video plus a community is hard to beat at that price.
