## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

The original hypnobirthing brand is a franchise, not an info product: the money is in $400-plus local classes, and the only cheap self-serve items are a $41 book-and-audio bundle and a $20 relaxation track. It shows how high live birth education is priced in the US, and that the method's owners have not built a self-serve personalised product.
