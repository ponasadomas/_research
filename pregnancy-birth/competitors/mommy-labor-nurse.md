## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

The US mass-market price point: $39 for a full birth course plus newborn bonus, with HSA/FSA reimbursement as a selling point. Its strongest copy is not the course but the reader's state: Googling, TikTok, anxious, "will I know what to do". That pain is the same one a personalised trimester guide would answer. The "second time around" line confirms second-time mothers buy birth prep to make it "different", but MLN gives them nothing different. Mamivio would be selling a guide, not a course, at a price this $39 anchor caps.
