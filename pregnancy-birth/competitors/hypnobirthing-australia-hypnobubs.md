## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

The Australian market leader sells the same thing twice under two brands at two prices, and its biggest proof is an outcome statistic Mamivio may not use. Two useful patterns: it segments by birth path (standard, caesarean, "change of circumstances") rather than by week or pregnancy number, and it monetises personalisation as a human upgrade. A Mamivio guide that personalises by week and pregnancy number would occupy the gap below the private session, without outcome claims.
