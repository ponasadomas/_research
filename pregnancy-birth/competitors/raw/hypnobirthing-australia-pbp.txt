## meet

## MELISSA

[img alt: Melissa Spilsted - Hypnobirthing Australia™ Director & Founder of the Positive Birth Program (Hypnobirthing Practitioner Childbirth Educator)]

#### Melissa Spilsted is the director and founder of Hypnobirthing Australia™ and is recognised as Australia’s leading expert in hypnobirthing.

She teaches childbirth educators, caregivers and parents throughout Australia and the world. She is a passionate advocate for natural birthing but also recognises that sometimes the safest delivery for mother and baby is via c-section. As a Clinical Hypnotherapist, experienced Childbirth Educator, and hypnobirthing mum of 3; Melissa has first-hand experience in using these proven techniques for empowering birth and wants to share these secrets with you!
