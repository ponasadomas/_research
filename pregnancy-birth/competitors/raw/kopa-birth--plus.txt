## Frequently asked questions

Can I use my HSA, FSA, or HRA card to pay for the course?

Yes! Kopa Birth® Plus Membership is fully eligible for reimbursement through most HSA, FSA, and HRAs. In many cases, you can use your card to purchase the course directly on our website. If you need to purchase the course through a different payment method, your purchase receipt includes all the information you will need to send to your plan administrator to receive a reimbursement for your course. Learn more about HSA FSA eligibility.

Can you explain the 30-day, money-back guarantee?

We know that there’s a lot of hope and trust wrapped up in your choice of childbirth class and baby prep, and we want this to be a risk-free experience for you. If for some reason you’re unsatisfied with the courses, we offer a 30-day, 100% money-back guarantee. Just email us at [email protected], and we’ll process the refund.

How long will I have access to the course?

Plus Membership gives you LIFETIME access to the Kopa® Birth Course and all other Kopa® Birth online courses. This means you can return and watch the classes again in a future pregnancy.

Will I need to purchase anything else to prepare for birth and parenting?

The classes offered in Plus Membership are comprehensive and inclusive of birth, postpartum, breastfeeding, and newborn care. For most parents, Plus Membership is all you will need to embrace childbearing and early parenting with confidence.
