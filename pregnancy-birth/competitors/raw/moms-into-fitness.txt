1️⃣ When I turned 40 I changed the way I strength train.

I train my legs just 2x a week, at home, usually 4-5 exercises.

✨Squat Exercise: Usually goblet, ballerina squat or a plié.
✨Single Side Exercise: Bulgarian, deficit lunge, B–Stance or reverse lunge.
✨Glute focused: Bridge or hip thrust.
✨Targeted movement: Something like weighted abduction, donkey kick or hip extension.

That’s it.

I’m lifting heav. And by heavy, I mean the last 1-2 reps should feel hard. You should be getting close to failure with good form.

2️⃣ When I turned 40 I changed my cardio.

I stopped doing the long, exhausting cardio workouts. Cardio with weights. Constant jumping.

And I started walking outside for about 30 minutes every day.

Because changing body composition requires both sides of the equation.

>Strength training builds and maintains muscle.
>Walking…along with focusing on protein and produce…reduces body fat so we can SEE that muscle.

But how long does this take?

I don’t want you thinking in terms of another four-week challenge.

I’m 46 now. We’re doing this for the long haul.

For our body composition, yes. But also for our bones, blood sugar, joints, strength, and the ability to feel capable in our bodies.

Within about 3–4 weeks, you will see and feel it.

But around three months is when I tell women to expect that more significant turning point.

If you’re over 40 and feel stuck…whether your workouts aren’t working or you don’t know where to start…keep it simple.

I’ve been there. I thought harder workouts were what my body needed. But I kept feeling wired, tired, and frustrated.

Here are 3 things I recommend:
1️⃣ Less is more. You don’t have to strength train 5-6 days a week. Do 3x 30-minutes strength workouts at home.
2️⃣ If hard cardio leaves you feeling depleted, swap those sessions for a daily walk. That change made a world of difference for me.
3️⃣ Instead of checking off your workout and sitting at your desk for the rest of the day, add movement throughout your day. Every hour, get up and move for a few minutes (pacing on calls, chores, taking a lap after lunch).

This is what my workout week looks like at 46. Simple. Sustainable. Effective.
