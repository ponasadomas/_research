What makes us the best

Holistic, evidence-based antenatal classes

Four in depth modules that allow you to understand the key to reducing pain and intervention plus enjoy your pregnancy & birth. Presented by Internationally recocognised She Births® Founder, Birth Expert, Doula and Yoga Teacher, Nadine Richardson.

Visualisations & Strategies

Strategies for every stage of labour with our proven tools and techniques to overcome pain. More than 6 Hypnosis Visualisations to help you stay calm, let go of fear & mentally prepare for giving birth.

Videos & Cheatsheets

Print-ready PDF resources; Visual Birth Preferences sheet, Breastfeeding Resources, Acupressure Guides & Active Birthing Guides and so much more. Plus videos to help with breastfeeding, conscious parenting & even your relationship.

20+ Beautiful Birth Stories

Positive birth story videos to watch on the go. In depth interviews with families that will leave you feeling confident and empowered for birth. (Not all natural, not all perfect.)
Perfect to inspire you & your partner to create a beautiful birth, no matter what unfolds.

Expert Support

Expert support and guidance from our Educators, Doulas & Midwives in online forums with Nadine Richardson. Connect with our specialised physiotherapists, chiropractors, acupuncturists, midwives and doctors.

Prenatal Yoga

Access to all of Nadine Richardson’s internationally renowned pregnancy yoga classes. Create a more natural, gentle and joyful birth experience.

Choose a Recommended Doula

Our Doula Coordinator will understand your needs and match you with the best doula for your family. Choose a doula on our private recommended list.

Nourishing Community

Our Private Facebook community allows you to connect with an amazing the tribe of families. We create a non-judgemental space for sharing & supporting one another. Our Soul Mama Circles are a unique & special Mothers Group.
