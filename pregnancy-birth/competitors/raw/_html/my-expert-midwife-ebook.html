Our registered midwives @ My Expert Midwife are here to help you to get ahead with our instantly accessible 30 page guide for navigating through the first 20 weeks of pregnancy.

For most women, each pregnancy is different. Even if this pregnancy is like your others there may be some things that have changed; the way that your maternity care is delivered or safety information since your last baby.

If this is your first baby, you may be feeling that you’re starting on a bit of an unknown journey. This e-book is designed to gently and easily help you navigate those first 20 weeks offering you information you may not have realised you even needed.

Exclusively available at My Expert Midwife. Packed with top tips and easy to digest content.
