The Calm Birth School Hypnobirthing Guide

What Is Hypnobirthing?

Hypnobirthing is sometimes viewed as being a bit “woo woo” or “out there” but the truth is, hypnobirthing  is a holistic, evidence-based approach to preparing for childbirth that blends together 6 essential pillars; Hypnosis, Breathing, Relaxation, Affirmations, Visualisation and Birth Education. It helps you to change your perception of birth, in particular reducing your fear and equips you with coping tools to manage sensations during labour. The aim of hypnobirthing, The Calm Birth School way, is to help you create a positive birth experience no matter what type of birth you have…

Inspired by research on fear, tension and pain pathways in the body, hypnobirthing helps regulate the nervous system so you can labour with calm confidence and strength. It can be used alongside medical care whether you are planning a hospital birth, birth centre birth, or home birth, and it supports & inspires informed decision making and active participation in your birth journey.

Why Hypnobirthing Works: The Science and Physiology

Birth is driven by hormones. When fear and stress are high, the body releases adrenaline, which can slow labour progress and increase discomfort. Hypnobirthing helps shift your nervous system into a parasympathetic state so oxytocin and endorphins flow more freely, supporting:

  • Efficient cervical opening
  • Effective uterine movement
  • Pain Management
  • Emotional regulation and calm focus

Research consistently highlights that when women & birthing people feel relaxed and supported, there is a reduction in perceived pain, decreased need for analgesia, and higher satisfaction with the birth experience. Hypnobirthing does not replace medical care; it enhances your body’s natural capacities, optimises comfort, and supports well-being before, during, and after birth.

Core Hypnobirthing Techniques

Hypnobirthing uses a set of practical, evidence-based tools that you can practise throughout pregnancy:

E

Breathing

The Calm Birth School uses 3 hypnobirthing breathing techniques which can be practised during your pregnancy to build muscle memory, encourage positive hormone release and help you to feel grounded. The breathing patterns are then used at different times during your labour to promote relaxation, balance oxygen and carbon dioxide levels and to work with the body at a particular phase of labour. This also helps you stay present and connected to your body.

E

Relaxation

Relaxation includes fantastic grounding techniques to help you feel calm and safe. It also helps build muscle memory so that relaxing the body during labor becomes easier.

E

Hypnosis

This is the “hypno” part of hypnobirthing, which can often deter people. Hypnosis is deep relaxation. Relaxation allows access to the subconscious to change outdated beliefs about birth, such as the idea that it is scary, frightening, gory, or dangerous. Hypnosis is not mind control and it doesn’t mean you will do something or disclose something that you don’t want to. You are always in control!

E

Visualisation

Visualisation is a tool that can be used to prepare for birth. Imagining, picturing, and visualising your ideal birth helps your brain think you have already given birth, which helps calm and regulate your nervous system. It also encourages taking practical action to achieve the desired outcome.

E

Affirmations

Affirmations are empowering, positive statements that help to redirect attention or change feelings about giving birth. Repeating affirmations helps create new neural pathways, which can lead to noticing more positive things. They can also be a good grounding tool for staying in the moment and redirecting attention away from alarming feelings.

E

Education

Hypnobirthing is a full antenatal course that helps prepare you for giving birth. It includes education on the birth companion’s role, birth options, navigating the pregnancy and birth experience with care providers, interventions, and what to pack in your bag.

In addition to the core pillars of hypnobirthing you also learn;

Partner Support Strategies, How to ask questions of your care provider
Creating birth plans/preferences, Bonding and connecting with your baby and they develop & grow, Choosing where to give birth, How movement and exercise can support your pregnancy & birth

Benefits of Choosing Hypnobirthing

Research into hypnobirthing, hypnosis, and mindfulness-based birth preparation suggests that these approaches can support people to feel calmer, more confident, and better prepared for labour and birth.

Studies have shown that learning relaxation, breathing, and mindfulness techniques during pregnancy may help to:

  • Reduce fear and anxiety around birth
     • Increase confidence and a sense of emotional control
     • Support deeper relaxation during labour
     • Improve coping with the sensations of labour
     • Encourage supportive partner involvement
     • Increase satisfaction with the birth experience

Some research also suggests that practising relaxation and mindfulness techniques may be associated with shorter labours or lower use of certain medical interventions for some people, although results vary and depend on many individual factors.

Importantly, these approaches are not intended to control or guarantee birth outcomes, but to provide practical tools that help people feel informed, supported, and emotionally prepared for whatever path their birth may take.

Is Hypnobirthing Right For You?

Hypnobirthing is ideal if you want:

  • A calm and informed birth preparation plan
  • Practical tools to manage sensations without fear
  • Involvement of your partner in your birth experience
  • Confidence and clarity about your choices
  • A positive mindset that supports your body’s natural instincts

You can start hypnobirthing at any stage of pregnancy, but earlier practice often helps deepen confidence and skill with the techniques.

If you have had a previous traumatic birth experience, hypnobirthing can help with anxieties, and be part of your birth preparation plan. However, it’s worth noting that practising hypnobirthing does guarantee a perfect birth experience. A birth free from medical interventions or special circumstances can never be assumed, but doing a hypnobirthing course can help you to feel more positive during your pregnancy and ensure you have tools and strategies to use no matter how your birth unfolds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does hypnobirthing mean I will be unconscious or unaware during birth?

No. You remain fully aware, present, and in control. Hypnobirthing changes how you feel about birth through your practice during your pregnancy and teaches you how to enter a deeply relaxed, confident mindset during birth so your body works more effectively. This helps the mind and body to work together.

Can I still use medical pain relief if I want to?

Absolutely. Hypnobirthing complements whatever birth choices you make, including medical pain relief options. Its purpose is to give you tools, not to limit your access to care.

Is hypnobirthing evidence based?

Yes. Hypnobirthing techniques are informed by research in psychology and neuroscience. Studies suggest that fear and anxiety can increase the perception of pain during labour, while relaxation, breathing, and mindfulness techniques may help reduce stress responses and promote calmer, more effective coping during labour. By encouraging a state of relaxation, these techniques may also support the body’s natural physiological processes during labour.

Do I have to be a certain age, fitness level, or pregnancy type?

Hypnobirthing is suitable for most people regardless of age, fitness, or pregnancy type. If you have specific medical conditions or complex care plans, your hypnobirthing practice can be personalised in collaboration with your care provider. You can prepare using hypnobirthing whether you intend to have a spontaneous vaginal birth, a birth induction or another type of intervention or if you have decided to have a caesarean birth.

How long should I practice before birth?

Regular practice throughout the weeks before labour strengthens your confidence with the techniques. Many people begin around 20 weeks, but starting at any point can still be beneficial.
