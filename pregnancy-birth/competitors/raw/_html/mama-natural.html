Meet your host Genevieve

Meet your host Genevieve

Hi there! I’m Genevieve Howland, Certified Doula (CD), bestselling author of The Mama Natural Week-by-Week Guide to Pregnancy & Childbirth and creator of MamaNatural.com, the most popular natural pregnancy and parenting site online.

I’m a mom of three, and I’ve had three very different births! From a 27-hour endurance event with my first, to a wild and wonderful homebirth that happened so fast that my husband caught the baby before the midwife arrived!

Since 2010, I’ve been on a mission to help other mamas have the empowered births that they desire! This course has been a labor of love for over a decade now, and I’m so proud of all the resources and life-changing information we’ve created to help you.

Meet your Midwife Maura

Meet your Midwife Maura

Hello, I’m Maura Winkler, a Certified Nurse Midwife (CNM), Registered Nurse (RN), and Board Certified Lactation Consultant (IBCLC). I’m also a mom of four!

I started my career in medical school, but the “intervention-first” approach of hospital birth inspired me to pursue my true calling of midwifery.

I now run a midwife practice in Buffalo, NY and own a birth center there too. I’ve attended hundreds of natural births, and look forward to giving you the practical, evidence-based tools you need to have the birth you desire.

How long will it take me to complete this course?

The total course content is around 12 hours in length. Technically, you could flex the binge-watching muscles Netflix gave you and power through it in one day!

But, if you’ve got the time, it’s best to space the classes out so you can really practice and absorb everything.

 
Can I pay with my FSA, HSA, or HRA card?

Yes. Childbirth classes are eligible for reimbursement with a flexible spending account (FSA), health savings account (HSA), or a health reimbursement arrangement (HRA), and we do accept FSA / HSA / HRA cards as well. If you have additional questions about getting reimbursed with your FSA, HSA, or HRA account, contact [email protected].

 
Is there any long-term commitment?

Nope. You’ve got enough of a long-term commitment growing inside of you.

You can pay all at once or in monthly installments. The monthly installments work like this: If you enroll on July 1, the first charge will take place then, the second charge will be on August 1, and the final charge on September 1. Never anything more. We split payments up like this to make life easier for you, as we know pregnancy can be an expensive time.

How long will I have access to the course?

You have a full year to work through the course at your own pace.

 
What if I don’t like the course?

We offer a 30-day money back guarantee. If you’re not happy within the first 30 days, we will refund your tuition paid minus the $35 for the bracelet and affirmation cards that we send students in the mail.

Is this course for my partner too?

Heck yes! We designed the Mama Natural Birth Course to be informative and entertaining for mamas and their significant others as well, whether that’s a husband, boyfriend, partner, or family member.

I’m in my third trimester. Is it too late for me to take the course?

Nope. The only time it’s too late is if you’re in transition (and even then you could watch some videos about post birth). The course is designed for you to complete it as quickly or as slowly as you’d like.

The total course content is around 12 hours in length. Technically, you could power through and do it all in one day! But, if you’ve got the time, it’s best to space the classes out so you can really practice and absorb everything you need to know.

This is my second baby. Should I still take the course?

If you want a natural childbirth, yes! The course is aimed at all parents-to-be, whether this is your first or your twentieth birth. (And if it’s the latter, we can connect you to our TV talent agent as well.)

I’m planning a hospital birth. Will the course still help me?

Absolutely! In fact, it’s even more important for you to take this class! The information, strategies, practices, and tools you will learn in the Mama Natural Birth Course will help you have your best, most informed birth no matter where you choose to deliver.

I’m planning a home birth. Is this course right for me?

First, congratulations! Second, yes! You will learn so many tools and exercises that will help you with your home birth. You’ll develop an awareness of your body and the birth process that will put your mind at ease. You’ll also master comfort measures that will help you ride through the intensity of birth and help you embrace this transformative event!

I’m planning a VBAC. Is this course for me?

Yes, yes, and yes! We’ve got a special bonus class just for moms who are preparing for a vaginal birth after cesarean. As a doula, co-host Maura has helped dozens of mamas have successful VBACs, and she’ll share all her wisdom with you too.

I live internationally. Can I still take the course?

Sure! We want international mamas to get in on the love. The techniques and principles that we cover in the course are universal, and will inform and enrich your childbirth no matter where you live. You’ll have to put up with our funny accents, though!

Oh, and you’ll need to pay for the class in US dollars via your credit card. We don’t charge any pesky transaction fees for you to use a foreign credit card, but your bank or credit card holder may charge you one of these:

  • A conversion fee to put your payment into US dollars
  • A “foreign transaction” fee since our business is based outside of your country
