What is hypnobirthing?

Hypnobirthing is so much more than its name suggests. There is no hypnotising! Hypnobirthing is simply lots of valuable information to equip you with the power to keep yourself calm and relaxed during birth - any type of birth. It empowers you to advocate for yourself and achieve a positive birth experience.

Hypnobirthing is a science-based, logical approach to pregnancy and childbirth. It’s light-bulb moment after light bulb moment! Through stripping birth back to the basics of physiology, hormones and the muscles used during labour, you will fully understand the birthing body. This will help to remove any fears and negative associations surrounding childbirth.
