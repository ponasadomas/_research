Description

Help your child prepare for their new role as a big brother or sister with this interactive online course, equivalent to our Sibling in-person classes. This engaging eClass provides fun lessons for parents and children to do together, including kid-friendly videos, coloring pages, and interactive activities. There is no Mayo Clinic-specific information in this course.

What You’ll Learn:

  • How to answer tough questions about pregnancy and birth
  • Talking to your child about breastfeeding and baby care
  • Tips for a smooth introduction to the newborn
  • Handling big emotions and reducing jealousy
  • Fun ways to involve your child in caring for their new sibling

How It Works:

  • Access from any computer or mobile device
  • Takes 2–3 hours to complete
  • Available in 22 languages including: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Arabic, Japanese, and Chinese
  • Local Mayo Clinic Rochester patients receive additional course materials via email
  • Six months of access after purchase
