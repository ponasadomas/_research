Compare our She Births® Face-to-Face and Online Birth Education Courses

She Births® is a childbirth education program that offers online, face-to-face and hybrid courses. While the core content is similar, there are some differences between the formats.

Face-to-face course

  • Interactivity

    In a face-to-face course, you have the opportunity to ask questions and interact with the instructor and other participants.

  • Flexibility

    A face-to-face course has a set schedule and require you to attend in person.

  • Personalisation

    A face-to-face course offers more opportunities for personalization, as the instructor can tailor the content to the needs of the group.

  • Comfort

    Some people may feel more comfortable in a face-to-face setting, where they can meet the instructor and other participants in person.

  • Printable Resources & Bonuses

    Print-ready visual Birth Preference sheet, Breastfeeding Resources, Acupressure & Active Birthing Guides, She Births® Course Handbook, plus so many more BONUSES.

  • A 100+ page She Births® Complete Guidebook

    This She Births® Complete Guidebook helps you integrate and practice what you’ve learnt easily.

Online course

  • Interactivity

    Conducted online, it is worth noting that there will be limitations on opportunities to interact and socialize with fellow students apart from in our private Facebook group.

  • Flexibility

    The online course offers more flexibility in terms of when and where you can complete it. You can take the course at your own pace, and it's accessible from anywhere with an internet connection.

  • Personalisation

    The online course is standardised, although there may be some opportunities to ask questions or seek guidance from the instructor via email.

  • Comfort

    Some people may prefer the anonymity and privacy of an online course.

  • Printable Resources & Bonuses

    Print-ready visual Birth Preference sheet, Breastfeeding Resources, Acupressure & Active Birthing Guides, She Births® Course Handbook, plus so many more BONUSES.

  • A 100+ page She Births® Complete Guidebook

    This She Births® Complete Guidebook helps you integrate and practice what you’ve learnt easily.

Ultimately, the choice between online and face-to-face courses will depend on your preferences and circumstances. Both formats offer valuable information and resources to help prepare you for childbirth.
