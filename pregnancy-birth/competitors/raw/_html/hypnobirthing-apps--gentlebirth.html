TRANSFORM YOUR PREGNANCY JOURNEY WITH GENTLEBIRTH - THE #1 PREGNANCY APP TRANSFORM YOUR PREGNANCY JOURNEY WITH GENTLEBIRTH - THE #1 PREGNANCY APP Whether you're preparing for your first or next baby, GentleBirth has been created for you. Giving the gift of a GentleBirth can be the most memorable and impactful gift you give your baby. Our app is engineered to redefine your pregnancy journey and gear you towards a positive and confident birthing experience. Here are some potential benefits you could unlock with the GentleBirth Pregnancy App: · Experience less pain during labor · Lower anxiety and fear · Experience shorter labor duration · Reduced chances of unplanned inductions and unexpected cesareans · Prepare your partners to be more confident and equipped Our approach is to reduce fear and anxiety, and increase your confidence as you delve into the science of positive birth. Learn how the combination of positive psychology, mindfulness, meditation, CBT, and sports psychology can empower you to thrive during pregnancy, birth, and early parenting. Inclusive and non-judgmental, our app is suitable for moms planning any type of birth – medicated, unmedicated, vaginal, cesarean, singleton, or twins. With GentleBirth, you're covered! WHAT DOES GENTLEBIRTH OFFER YOU? · Daily 'brain training' sessions: Includes short meditations, hypnobirthing techniques, affirmations, breathing exercises, and mindfulness. · Curated playlists: Handpicked for sleep, anxiety, confidence during pregnancy, positive birth, and the 4th trimester. · Personalized tracking: Motivates and guides your practice. · Robust support: From our GentleBirth Team and an uplifting Community of over 10,000 members. WE VALUE YOUR VOICE We’d love to hear about your GentleBirth journey. For feedback or questions about pregnancy, birth, or the GentleBirth program, don’t hesitate to reach out to us on social media or email us at team@gentlebirth.com. UNLOCK PREMIUM ACCESS · Try out Premium, free for 7 days. · Delve deeper into mindfulness, hypnosis, affirmations, meditation, and breathing techniques. · Receive personalized daily recommendations, driven by AI. · Listen effortlessly to curated playlists for every birth journey. · Download sessions for offline practice - be prepared anywhere, anytime. Ready to redefine your pregnancy journey? Give your baby the gift of a GentleBirth today!   • Beautiful birth!
    12/26/2020
    CKYrunner

    If you only do one thing to prepare for birth, let this be it!! This was hands down the most helpful thing for my labor. I only started listening to this in the last 5 weeks of pregnancy but I wish I had found it sooner! I listened to it every night in the bathtub and sometimes falling asleep at night. By the time labor started, I thought I’d be listening to it more but my body was consumed by an outside force that only allowed me to focus inward. My husband took some videos during my labor and I heard myself speaking some of the hypnosis phrases out loud. I have no memory of this!! I unfortunately never got around to listening to the “pushing” tracks so I wonder if that would have gone a bit differently for me. I did listen to the postpartum tracks in the weeks following my birth which really helped me mentally. The nature sounds we’re also really helpful for getting my newborn to sleep. I have been recommending this app to all my pregnant friends. I will be using this again from the beginning of my next pregnancy! Thank you for helping me have the most beautiful and positive birth experience. 10 stars!!!!

  • “Easiest birth I’ve ever seen”
    07/23/2020
    Alnn18

    I downloaded this app the last month of pregnancy as a crash course in Hypnobirthing and to help me calm the anxiousness I was feeling giving birth during a global pandemic. I was pregnant with my 4th baby and had done natural birth previously, but never using Hypnobirth techniques per se. I listened to these meditations every night as I was falling asleep and throughout the day during a little nap or rest. I completely believe that the compounding benefits of using it are real! When it came time to deliver my baby I had the most controlled and peaceful, unmediated birth. I didn’t even yell out in pain once and was able to calmly direct my husband and doctor in what support I needed. After my baby was born my doctor, who has been delivering babies for 40 years, said “That was the easiest birth I’ve ever seen. It was like slow motion. It was hard to tell when you were pushing it was so quiet.” I’m grateful to have had this app as one of my birth preparation tools, it was so helpful!

    Developer Response

I'm so happy that you got to experience the incredible power of 'brain training for birth' with the GentleBirth App. Wishing your family continued health and wellness during these very challenging times. Tracy
