## FAQS

How is the Online Birth Course different to the in person Masterclass?

Our Full Online Birth Course has all the wisdom and experience Nadine has gathered and shared over 20 years. You will be able to undergo the same transformative process, but it can be done in your own time, at home.

Where can I find info on your in person Masterclass?

Click here for information on our Weekend Courses in NSW and Victoria.

Is the Online Course as effective as the in person Masterclass?

For some, it is actually more effective. They can review content as many times as they need, there are also forums with Nadine each week that will allow you to ask any questions that may arise during your pregnancy.

When is the best time to start?

We highly recommend accessing the course today. You will gain instant support in the private online community, start familiarising yourself with the resources, as well as yoga and visualisations. We recommend beginning your full program between 22-36 weeks gestation.

Do I need to attend the hospital classes too?

Most of our families only complete our program and get amazing results. Although, we do recommend taking a tour of your hospital before the big day.

How long will I have access to the Online Birth Course content for?

Course content will be available to you for 12 months. Not only can you prepare for the birth of your baby, but you’ll be able to review our breastfeeding and parenting content in those early days after birth.

I'm still undecided, can I talk to a human?

Of course you can! From Australia, call 1300 101 SHE (743) during business hours. For international calls or calls outside of business hours, email support@shebirths.com.
