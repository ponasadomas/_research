BIRTHFIT is the leading organization training coaches and practitioners to support women through the full motherhood transition — from preconception through postpartum.

## Supporting strength.
Leading with intention.

#### THE BIRTHFIT

## Certification

### Train to lead women through the motherhood transition.

Our flagship certification equips coaches and practitioners with a movement-based, nervous system-informed framework for supporting women through preconception, pregnancy, birth prep, and postpartum.

This is for practitioners and coaches who know the standard model is not enough — and want a deeper, more complete way to support mothers.

### What you’ll gain:

- A proven, seven-pillar framework to support women through preconception, pregnancy, and postpartum

- Knowledge and confidence to program and coach core, pelvic floor, and full-body training for every phase

- Live, case-based learning you can immediately apply to real clients

- A clear path to build or grow a profitable, purpose-driven practice

- The ability to teach and sell BIRTHFIT curricula in your own community

## Why BIRTHFIT

### A trusted framework for mothers and the people who serve them.

BIRTHFIT exists to raise the standard of care in the motherhood transition. We believe women deserve support that is grounded in science, rooted in fundamentals, and respectful of the intelligence of the female body.

For more than 12 years, BIRTHFIT has led the conversation in prenatal and postpartum fitness, practitioner education, and whole-person maternal support.

## Prenatal and postpartum training that meets real life.

BIRTHFIT fitness programs are designed for women who want safe, effective movement support during pregnancy, postpartum, and beyond. Whether you are preparing for birth, healing your core and pelvic floor, or rebuilding strength, there is a program for your season.

## Prenatal

Two ways to train during pregnancy: BIRTHFIT Basics or Prenatal Training. We offer options to follow a full-term program or train by trimester based on where you are in your journey.

PRENATAL PROGRAMS

## Postpartum

Five postpartum programs, from early recovery to rebuilding strength, designed to meet you exactly where you are and support your return to movement with intention.
