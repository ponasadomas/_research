## FAQs

Do you offer a trial period?

Wanting a little sneak peek of our course prior to enrolling?

Not a problem! Please CLICK HERE for your Free Course Preview and also receive a bonus ‘Pregnancy Relaxation’ MP3 so you can get started with your hypnobirthing practice today.

If you decide to purchase the course your account will automatically be upgraded so you can access all of the course materials.

How long do I have access to the online course?

You have access to the. materials 24/7 through your phone, mobile device or computer for a full 24 months with the option to extend later. We even have an app to make it super easy to access.

You can download the MP3s, eBook and handouts and keep them permanently if you wish.

When the best time to start the course?

Mum’s often ask us, when is the best time to start the course? We answer – anytime is possible! The main thing is that you have the opportunity to learn these techniques before you birth.

Having said that; the ideal time to start the course is between 20-30 weeks of pregnancy. This gives you ample time to practice the techniques leading up to birth. Although it is also fine to begin further along in pregnancy.

Will I still benefit from attending the course if I'm later if pregnancy?

Absolutely! Many parents who have attended the course later in pregnancy, have achieved excellent results.

Determination is the deciding factor when it comes to hypnobirthing success. Please feel free to contact your closest practitioner to discuss more intensive training options if needed.

How many hours is the Positive Birth Program Online Course?

The course is approx. 7 hours and includes comprehensive video tuition that you can watch over and over if you like. Melissa will guide you step-by-step on journey towards a very calm and positive birth using hypnobirthing techniques. The course is easy to follow, comprehensive and fun.

And because this is online, you can study at your own pace. It’s very flexible!

Is this course suitable for my birth partner as well?

The course is structured in such a way that the birth partner is encouraged to attend as they will learn how to best support and advocate for you during your pregnancy and birth.

Having said this, sometimes there are reasons why the birthing mother needs to attend the course solo – and this is also completely fine. You never really are alone, because your birth partner is also your baby. 😊

Am I able to access the course on multiple devices?

The course is secured on an online learning platform so you can log into the course anytime using any internet enabled device (phone, tablet, computer etc) and take back up where you left off. You can watch the lessons over again if you like.

You can login to the online learning platform as many times as you need. It’s one login, but no restrictions on devices or anything.

You also have access to an app, so you can easily access the content on your mobile device.

Can I save all of the course content?

You can save all of the PDFs, eBooks and MP3s to your computer for future access beyond the 24-month access period, however please note that there isn’t a way to save the videos.

The course is updated on a regular basis and Melissa provides online support throughout, that’s why we limit access. However, you have the option to extend your access later, once you’re enrolled.

Do you offer support?

You can leave comments and ask questions throughout the course. Melissa is there to support you every step of the way.

And – if you decide that you would like a private one-on-one session to compliment the course, this upgrade is also available.

Is the course suitable as a hypnobirthing refresher course?

Yes indeed! In fact, we regularly receive this enquiry from parents who have previously attended hypnobirthing classes and we suggest this very option.

Another option available to you is to attend a hypnobirthing Refresher with a Certified Hypnobirthing Australia™ Practitioner. The refresher course is usually run privately by the practitioner and is 3 hours in total and includes your tuition for yourself and partner. You can click here to use the map to find your closest Hypnobirthing Australia practitioner. You can contact them directly to discuss available class times and booking.

Some like to complete their refresher between 20-30 weeks and some prefer to do it after 30 weeks – that is completely up to you. One thing is for sure, we find that those parents who attend a refresher really benefit from it.

Is this course suitable if I need a caesarean?

The Positive Birth Program incorporates many aspects that can be utilised for both natural or caesarean birth, however, if you are specifically planning to birth by c-section, our Positive Caesarean Birth Online Course is the course that we recommend.

If you are aiming for a natural birth and want to be super prepared and have resources on hand should you need a cesarean, we recommend the Value Package – It covers you for either scenario.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a money back guarantee – if you don’t enjoy the course content, then please get in touch with us within 30 days of purchase. We’ll provide you with a full refund provided you have watched less than 10% of the course content, haven’t downloaded any of the resources, or completed a private session.
