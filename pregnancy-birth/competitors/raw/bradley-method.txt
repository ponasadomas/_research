# START FOR YOUR BABY?

The Bradley Method® series is designed for small classes with lots of individual attention. The standard length is 12 weekly classes covering 12 units of instruction. The Student Workbook is included with classes. It has over 130 pages with over 75 pictures and illustrations. Your local Bradley Method® Professional is an independent affiliated instructor.

By taking classes in The Bradley Method® of Natural Childbirth, you will learn about:

- Prenatal Nutrition & Exercise

- Relaxation for Easier Birth

- Husbands/Partners as Coaches

- Birth Plans and more!
