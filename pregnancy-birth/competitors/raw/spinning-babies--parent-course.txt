Course description

Whether you're weeks or months from birth, this gentle, self-paced Birth Preparation course helps you feel more grounded, physically, emotionally, and in partnership with your baby. In just 60 minutes, you'll discover how your body and baby work together during pregnancy and labor, and learn simple ways to bring more balance, ease, and confidence to the journey ahead.

Rooted in the Spinning Babies® approach, this course is ideal for first-time parents and anyone looking for a body-centered way to prepare for birth, one that prioritizes movement, connection, and the body’s innate wisdom.

Spinning Babies® Birth Preparation in 60 Minutes Online Course for Parents is now available in Spanish and German so more families can discover our gentle, body-led approach in the language that feels most natural to them. Choose your preferred language to explore simple daily activities that support comfort in pregnancy, connection with baby, and more ease in birth.

Your purchase includes lifetime access, meaning you can return to this course for as long as Spinning Babies® continues to offer it on our learning platform or a successor platform.

“Lifetime access” refers to the lifetime of the course, not the lifetime of the purchaser. Access is available for as long as Spinning Babies® continues to offer the course on its current learning platform or a successor platform. Spinning Babies® reserves the right to update, modify, replace, retire, or discontinue a course or learning platform at its discretion.

What You’ll Learn

This course is built around the idea that you don’t need to memorize every step; you just need the right tools to listen to your body and support your baby’s journey. Inside, you’ll find videos, exercises and printable resources to keep on hand covering:

- Your Birth Anatomy

Understand how your pelvis, uterus, and soft tissues work together to support physiological birth.

- Daily Activities for Comfort

Gentle stretches, balance movements, and mindful practices to bring more ease to pregnancy — and readiness for labor.

- Breathing for Connection

Try guided breathwork to center awareness and create space for calm and presence.

- The Three Balances℠

Learn foundational techniques that can restore body balance and support fetal positioning and comfort.

- Rest Smart℠

Discover how the way you sit, sleep, and rest may influence your baby’s position — and your comfort.

- Where’s Baby?

Use the Belly Mapping® Method to tune in to baby’s position and encourage more awareness and bonding.

- Your Baby’s Journey Through the Pelvis

Explore how your baby moves through the three levels of the pelvis — and how to support that journey with movement and alignment.
