### STILL GOT QUESTIONS?

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Built To Birth only for moms planning a natural or unmedicated birth?

Not at all!

Built To Birth places a strong emphasis on understanding and supporting physiologic birth, but our goal is to help you feel prepared and empowered no matter how your birth unfolds.

Whether you're planning an unmedicated, natural birth, considering an epidural, preparing for an induction, hoping for a VBAC, or navigating a cesarean birth, you'll learn about your options and how to make informed decisions along the way.

Prepare for a physiologic birth. Be ready for every scenario.

### How is Built To Birth different from other online childbirth classes?

Most childbirth classes focus primarily on labor and delivery. Built To Birth goes beyond birth preparation to support your entire journey through pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum.

Created by Lamaze Certified Childbirth Educator, DONA Certified Doula, Certified Prenatal & Postnatal Fitness Specialist, and Certified Health Coach Bridget Teyler, the Built To Birth Courses combine fitness programs and evidence-based education with practical tools you can actually use.

From understanding how your body works during labor to preparing for breastfeeding, hypnobirthing albums, newborn care, postpartum recovery, and pre/postnatal fitness programs, you'll have a complete library of resources in one place.

It's more than a just birth class.

### Is Built To Birth taught by a Lamaze Certified Childbirth Educator?

Yes! Built To Birth was created by Bridget Teyler, a Lamaze Certified Childbirth Educator (LCCE) and DONA Certified Doula.

Our childbirth education is built around Lamaze's Six Healthy Birth Practices, which emphasize supporting the natural process of labor, continuous birth support, freedom of movement, avoiding unnecessary interventions, and keeping mother and baby together.

These principles help you understand physiologic birth while preparing to make informed decisions about your care.

### I love Bridget's YouTube videos. How is Built To Birth Premium different?

Bridget's YouTube channel is a wonderful place to learn about pregnancy and birth, but it was never designed to replace a complete childbirth education program.

Built To Birth Premium goes much deeper, bringing together comprehensive courses, practical exercises, downloadable workbooks, guided meditations, and resources that aren't available in her free videos.

Instead of piecing together your preparation, you'll have one organized place to learn what you need for pregnancy, labor, breastfeeding, newborn care, and postpartum recovery.

YouTube gives you helpful tips. Built To Birth gives you the complete plan.

### When should I start the Built To Birth Program? Am I too far along?

It's never too early—or too late—to start preparing!

Starting earlier in pregnancy gives you more time to work through the lessons, practice what you're learning, and prepare your mind, body, and birth team.

But even if you're just a few weeks away from meeting your baby, there's still so much you can gain from our childbirth preparation resources.

And because Premium includes breastfeeding, newborn care, and postpartum courses, your learning doesn't stop once your baby arrives.

With lifetime access, you can revisit everything whenever you need it.

### I live outside the United States. Will Built To Birth still be helpful?

Yes! Built To Birth has resources that can be valuable to expecting parents around the world.

While some childbirth lessons discuss common U.S. hospital practices and interventions, the foundations of labor physiology, pregnancy fitness, breastfeeding, newborn care, and postpartum preparation are relevant across many different birth settings.

Healthcare practices may differ, but understanding your body and preparing for your birth journey is valuable wherever you live.
