## More than an antenatal course

Prepare for birth, feel more confident caring for your baby and connect with other expectant parents.

The NCT Antenatal course combines practical preparation, evidence-based information and supportive discussions led by an experienced NCT practitioner.

You'll have time to ask questions, explore your options and feel more confident as you prepare for birth and early parenthood.

### Understand your birth choices

Learn what to expect during labour and birth, explore your options and understand different ways your baby may be born.

### Get ready for life with your baby

Learn about feeding, newborn care, sleep, recovery after birth and the realities of early parenthood.

### Learn alongside other expectant parents

Meet parents whose babies are due around the same time, share experiences and build connections that can continue beyond your course.

### Support that continues beyond your course

Your NCT experience doesn't end when your sessions finish. You'll have access to additional resources, support and opportunities to connect with other parents.

## What is an antenatal course and when should I book?

An NCT Antenatal course helps you prepare for birth and the early days of parenthood.

You'll learn about labour and birth, feeding your baby, newborn care, recovery after birth and adjusting to life as a parent.

It’s also a great opportunity to meet other expectant parents in your local area, learn together, share experiences and build connections that can continue well beyond the course.

You can book your course at any time, although many parents book around 16 weeks of pregnancy. Courses can fill quickly, so booking early gives you the best chance of finding a course and dates that work for you. You may also have the option to spread the cost through monthly, interest-free instalments.

Each course price covers two people, so you can attend with your partner, a family member or friend.

## Frequently asked questions

You can contact us through web chat on this page or take a look at our answers to frequently asked questions.

When is the best time to attend an antenatal course?

We usually recommend choosing an antenatal course that finishes around three weeks before your baby's due date. This gives you time to reflect on what you've learned, get organised, and feel prepared for your baby's arrival. A course that finishes two weeks before your due date can also work well for many parents.

To help you choose, each course includes a target due date range, such as "For due dates from mid-October to late November". These ranges are there as a guide rather than a strict rule. For example, if you're due in early December, a course aimed at parents due from mid-October to late November could still be a good fit.

The main thing to think about is how much time you'll have between the course ending and your baby arriving. Because babies don't always wait for their due date, we recommend leaving a little space after your course where possible, just in case your baby decides to make an early appearance.

If a course finishes within two weeks of your due date, we'll highlight this with a "Finishes near due date" label so you have all the information you need when deciding which course is right for you.

Where is my nearest NCT course?

We’re proud to be the UK’s original and leading antenatal course provider, with in-person classes in over 400 locations nationwide.

Just enter your postcode and due date to see courses near you - you can also adjust the search radius to explore more options. You can also explore our antenatal course locations

If you don’t see a course that fits, it could be that local courses are fully booked right now. Don’t worry - you can join the priority waitlist or get in touch with our friendly bookings team, who’ll do their best to help you find something that works.

Who will be leading the course?

Your NCT course will be led by skilled educators who are independent from the NHS and trained at university level on all things pregnancy, birth, infant feeding and early parenthood.

Our 600+ practitioners are highly experienced in supporting new parents and all enjoy creating welcoming, informative sessions that help you feel confident, connected and prepared. They combine up-to-date, evidence-based information with practical skills and local insight, helping you make sense of what’s available in your area.

Our fantastic NCT practitioners are here to support you through this life-changing time and believe no question is too silly!

How do I book onto a course?

Booking your NCT antenatal course couldn’t be easier.

Start by thinking about what suits you best - would you prefer an in-person, online, or mixed-format class? You might also want to consider what days of the week or times work best for you and anyone else that might be attending.

When you enter your postcode and due date, we’ll match you with the courses that best fit your location and when your baby might be born. Once you’ve found the right course, simply follow the steps to book your place, add your personal details (and a second attendee if you’d like) and complete the payment. You can also pop in any additional information you think it is important we should know.

If you need a hand at any point, our friendly bookings team is always here to help you - by phone, email or live chat.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes! We offer course discounts for households earning under £35,000 a year - you can find out more and check if you’re eligible for a course discount.

Once you’ve found the course you’d like to attend, just select ‘Show payment & discount information’ and then ‘Check eligibility & apply.’ And if you need support, our helpful bookings team is always on hand.

When should I book my course?

You can book your course at any time, though most parents do so around 16 weeks of pregnancy. Our courses can fill quickly, so it’s a good idea to book early if you can.

Booking early also means you’ll have the option to spread the cost in monthly, interest-free instalments, making it easier to plan ahead.

How many people will be on the course?

Our courses usually have between 10-14 people attending, though some groups are smaller and others bigger.

We keep group sizes low so there’s time to chat, ask questions, share experiences and of course, get to know each other - often leading to friendships that continue long after the course ends!

Why does the price of a course differ?

Course prices vary depending on your location. Venue hire costs can differ across the country, so in some areas courses may be a little more expensive for us to run.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes - you’ve got a few interest-free options for spreading the cost:

- Monthly card instalments: Available if there’s enough time before your course begins.

- PayPal Pay in 3: Available right up until your course starts (if you’re eligible).

You can select your payment option at checkout.

Are antenatal courses suitable for second- or third-time parents?

Absolutely! Many parents who join our in-person antenatal courses already have children. It’s a great chance to connect with other expectant parents in your local area and at a similar stage of pregnancy. You’ll also have the chance to reflect on previous experiences and refresh your knowledge and skills.

We also offer a shorter, online-only course – NCT Antenatal Refresher. Over eight hours of live, interactive sessions, you’ll revisit key topics around pregnancy, birth and newborn care - and explore what life might look like with a new baby and older siblings.

What is Parent Hub?

Parent Hub is a digital platform exclusive for NCT course attendees. It’s full of additional videos and resources to compliment your learning before, during and after your course.

Parent Hub is where you’ll find free course extras including:

- Hypnobirthing – videos, audio clips and affirmations

- Yoga for Pregnancy – five classes suitable for all levels

- Newborn safety and first aid

- Access to Newborn Talks

- Special offers

- Further information on local activities and events

Can I change or cancel a booking?

We understand that plans can change. You can cancel your booking within 14 days of confirmation, free of charge, as long as your course hasn’t started.

There are also options if you need to change your booking - find out more in our cancellations and refunds information. As always, we’re here to help.

What happens if I miss a session?

We recommend choosing a course where you know you can come to every session, as you can’t move between groups once the course has started.

If you do miss one, your NCT course leader will usually share a summary and helpful resources covering what you missed. In some cases, we may be able to offer a live online catch-up. You can find more details in our terms and conditions.

Do you offer any support once my baby is born?

Yes! Newborn Talks are daily, live Zoom sessions led by our antenatal course practitioners, designed to support you in the early days after birth.

Each 45-minute talk focuses on key newborn topics, including sleep, crying and feeding, offering practical advice you can try right away. You’ll also have the chance to ask your specific questions and get real-time guidance.

There's absolutely no pressure to be on camera, and you're welcome to dip in and out or attend the same session again another week - whatever works for you. More information on Newborn Talks can be found in the Parent Hub.

Do you offer online-only classes?

Yes, we do – we know that in-person classes don’t suit everyone.

Our online NCT Antenatal courses are a great option if you’re living outside of the UK, in an area without a scheduled course, or simply prefer to focus on learning and preparation from home. You’ll still get the same trusted NCT information and practical guidance - just in a way that works best for you.

Find an online NCT antenatal course

What does it mean that this course is led by a Practitioner in Training?

Practitioners in Training are in the final stage of becoming fully qualified NCT practitioners. They have completed comprehensive training, have been assessed as ready to facilitate, and continue to receive support and mentoring from NCT Learning & Development Specialists. You'll receive the same trusted NCT course content and information, and our team works closely with Practitioners in Training to ensure a high-quality experience for those attending.

Can I bring my dog?

We welcome fully trained assistance dogs that support a handler with a disability. If you plan to attend with an assistance dog, please let us know when you book so we can make any necessary arrangements with the venue.

However, for the comfort and safety of all participants, it’s not possible to bring other dogs, including pets or dogs that are still in training. This is because some attendees may have allergies or phobias, and our courses take place in shared indoor venues.

I need extra support – who can I speak to?

We want every parent to feel welcome at an NCT course.

If there’s anything that might make it harder for you to join – such as access needs, communication support, or any other practical considerations we should be aware of, please let us know. You can contact us by email at booking.support@nct.org.uk, via live web chat, or by calling 0300 330 0700 (option 2).

We’ll listen, work with you to understand what you need, and do our best to find a solution that helps you get the most from your NCT course experience.
