### What is hypnobirthing?

Hypnobirthing is so much more than its name suggests. There is no hypnotising! Hypnobirthing is simply lots of valuable information to equip you with the power to keep yourself calm and relaxed during birth - any type of birth. It empowers you to advocate for yourself and achieve a positive birth experience.

Hypnobirthing is a science-based, logical approach to pregnancy and childbirth. It’s light-bulb moment after light bulb moment! Through stripping birth back to the basics of physiology, hormones and the muscles used during labour, you will fully understand the birthing body. This will help to remove any fears and negative associations surrounding childbirth.

## Frequently asked questions

-

What if I’m already doing another antenatal course?

No problem. The Hypnobirthing Pack can serve you as a take home revision of some parts of a traditional antenatal class and also adds the hypnobirthing element, which is usually a paid extra or not covered as comprehensively.

-

Do I need to do an in-person antenatal course too?

No. The Hypnobirthing Pack gives you a complete and comprehensive antenatal education. We do suggest that you also enrol in the Postpartum Pack as this will give you the all important knowledge you need when baby arrives.

-

What if I have questions on the course content?

You have the incredible Positive Birth Community on Facebook, our DMs on Instagram or please email us at hello@thepositivebirthcompany.com.

-

Do you have payment plans for the course?

Yes, you can pay for the course in 2 instalments. Just select this option here or at checkout.

-

How long does it take?

There are over 15 hours worth of birth and pregnancy education to watch split into bitesize videos.

-

When should I start the pack?

You can begin your antenatal education at any point during pregnancy. During early pregnancy, our Pregnancy Pack will help you become familiar with your changing body and all the dos and don’ts! When it comes to the Hypnobirthing Pack, the more you know and the more you practice the techniques, the better. Most people start the Hypnobirthing Pack at around 20-30 weeks. But as the pack is just over 6 hours long, it’s possible to binge watch during the last few days of your pregnancy to power up your knowledge at the last minute!

-

How long do I have access to the course for?

12 months.

-

Is the content useful outside the UK?

Yes, the course teaches physiology, science and practical techniques that are relevant for anyone across the globe. Although there are a few mentions to the NHS, this should not impact your experience or knowledge.

-

What if I don’t like the content once I get started?

We have a money back guarantee - if you don’t enjoy the content and you’ve watched less than 20% of the pack and have not used our call the midwife service, within the first two weeks after purchase, you can receive a full refund.
