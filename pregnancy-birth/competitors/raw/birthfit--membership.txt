## What You Get

4 LIVE MONTHLY CALLS

WEEK 1: LINDSEY LEADS (recorded)

Master classes on the seven pillars, clinical application, real-world coaching. Lindsey brings 10+ years of training practitioners and her lived experience as a chiropractor, doula, strength coach, and mother.

WEEK 2: GUEST SPEAKER (recorded)

Learn from experts in adjacent fields—pelvic floor PTs, midwives, functional medicine practitioners, birth workers. Expand your lens. Sharpen your tools.

WEEK 3: MINDSET + MOVEMENT (recorded)

Integration sessions that connect the mental, emotional, and physical. As a BIRTHFIT Coach or Practitioner, you're expected to have your own movement practice - and to keep refining it. Each month this session gives you a specific intention to bring to that practice. When you feel it in your body then you are able to teach it on a deeper level.

WEEK 4: OFFICE HOURS (live)

Bring your questions, your case studies, your challenges. Get real-time feedback. Collaborate with other coaches, practitioners, mothers. This is where theory becomes practice and wisdom is shared. This is LIVE.

New content delivered straight to your inbox each week — watch or listen whenever it fits your life. Once a month, we come together live for Office Hours to go deeper. You won't want to miss these!

## Frequently Asked Questions

Is this membership for me?

The BIRTHFIT Community Membership is designed for coaches, practitioners, aspiring professionals, and women who want to deepen their understanding of the motherhood transition. If you want ongoing education, real-world application of the BIRTHFIT methodology, and connection with a community that is actively doing the work — this membership is for you.

There are no requirements to join, this is open to anyone who wants to learn, grow, and be part of the movement. Many members join before pursuing certification, while others join after certification to continue developing their skills and stay connected to the professional community.

What if I can’t attend the live call?

Most calls are recorded and uploaded to the resource library so you can watch them later.

However, we strongly encourage attending live whenever possible, as this is where you can ask questions, discuss real client scenarios, and connect with other members..

Is there a contract or commitment?

No. The BIRTHFIT Community Membership is a monthly subscription with no long-term contract.

How does this membership differ from the BIRTHFIT Certification?

The BIRTHFIT Certification is a structured educational pathway that leads to the opportunity to apply for BIRTHFIT Certification. The Community Membership is ongoing education, mentorship, and collaboration. Many members use it to stay engaged after certification or to prepare for certification in the future. Or those who are looking to keep learning more.

What results can I expect from being a member?

Members often experience increased confidence supporting women through preconception, pregnancy, and postpartum, exposure to real case studies and clinical discussions, and deeper connection with like-minded professionals. The goal is long-term mastery, not just passive content consumption. We bridge the gap for you!

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

The monthly membership may be cancelled anytime. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle—no partial refunds will be issued. When the membership ends, you will lose access to calls, recordings, and resources within the community.
