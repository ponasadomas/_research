### COVID-19 Update

The health and well being of our pregnant families and Educators remains our highest priority.

Due to the uncertainty and restrictions during COVID-19 HypnoBirthing Classes are also being offered as a LIVE ONLINE CLASS.

Many of the classes listed below are running as an ONLINE COURSE. Face to Face Classes have resumed in some areas. Please contact the Educator running a class in your area to discuss the ONLINE OPTION if this is required.
