#

Online NCT Antenatal course

Experience the UK’s most trusted antenatal course from the comfort of your own home

Connect with your supportive course leader and other soon-to-be parents in live, interactive online sessions.

Book your course

Image

[img alt: Couple sitting on sofa with laptop]

## Why choose an Online NCT Antenatal course?

### All online sessions are live and interactive. Nothing pre-recorded.

### Feel confident, knowledgeable, and ready for life with your new baby.

### Join other parents-to-be whose babies are due around the same time.

### Now includes exclusive NCT x Mini First Aid newborn safety & baby health content

## What you’ll learn

From early signs to the first weeks

Five evidenced-based sessions covering labour, birth, feeding, and life with a newborn - all shaped around the choices you want to make.

Session 1: Getting ready for birth

Connect with other expectant parents from day one

What happens in late pregnancy

Birth environments

Recognising the signs that labour has started

Induction of labour

Pelvic floor health

Your baby’s arrival into the world

Session 2: Understanding birth

Stages of labour

Vaginal, assisted, and caesarean birth

Pain management options

Perineal health

Supporting yourself and/or a birthing parent

Session 3: Meeting your baby and recovery from birth

The first moments with your baby

Physical recovery after birth

Practical baby care

Early days scenarios and safer sleep

Neonatal care

Session 4: Feeding your baby

Recognising feeding cues and patterns

Breastfeeding, formula and mixed feeding

How breastmilk supply works, positioning and attachment

Paced bottle feeding

Practical feeding skills and common challenges

Session 5: Life with a new baby

What happens after birth

Sleep patterns and bonding

Baby development and responding to crying

Postnatal wellbeing and support networks

Find an online course

[img alt: Pregnant couple on video call on a tablet device]

## Same great experience as the in-person course

- Trusted, evidence-based information so you feel prepared for labour, birth and the first days with your baby.

- Our friendly, knowledgeable NCT course leaders will take the time to answer all your questions.

- You’ll connect with other parents who are expecting at a similar time as you.
