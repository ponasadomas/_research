#### Lamaze for Parents

####

#### Everyone’s journey from pregnancy through birth to parenthood is unique – and it by nature is transformative! Lamaze serves as a trusted guide along the way with all of our recommendations firmly grounded in the latest research. Our goal is simple: We want you to feel confident asking questions and making informed decisions, and comfortably taking control of your journey.

#### Preparing for birth? The Lamaze Labor, Birth, and Breastfeeding Bundle gives you the essential guidance most parents want before labor begins. This complete birth preparation program brings together Lamaze’s three most popular classes to help you feel informed, confident, and ready for birth.

Did you know that you may be able to receive insurance reimbursement or use HSA/FSA funds for Lamaze childbirth education classes? Check out a sample script for calling your insurance company and sample forms here.

# Lamaze Labor, Birth, and Breastfeeding Bundle

Most Popular

Backed by more than 60 years of evidence-based Lamaze education, this bundle helps you understand what to expect during labor, learn practical comfort techniques, and prepare for a strong start with breastfeeding.

Through these three classes you will learn how to:

• Understand the six practices that support safe, healthy births for mothers and babies

• Make informed decisions

• Use proven comfort techniques to cope with labor and increase your confidence

• Use effective positions that help labor progress naturally

• Learn the benefits of breastfeeding for both parents and babies and how to get breastfeeding off to a strong start

Estimated completion time: 4.5–6 hours
