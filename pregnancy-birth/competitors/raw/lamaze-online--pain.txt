- Overview

- Contents (3)

Worried about how you will cope with labor pain? Many expecting parents want practical ways to stay calm, comfortable, and confident during labor.

You will learn practical techniques to help you cope with labor, including how to:

• Use relaxation and breathing techniques that support early labor

• Practice comfort strategies that may help reduce discomfort during active labor

• Understand back labor and techniques that may help baby rotate into a better position

• Prepare for transition, the most intense stage of labor, with practical coping tools

• Explore effective pushing positions that open the pelvis and support smoother birth

Estimated completion time: 1.75–2.25 hours

Learn at your own pace and revisit the material anytime.
