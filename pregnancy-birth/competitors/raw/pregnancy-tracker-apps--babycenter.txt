### Helpful for all stages of pregnancy... Even loss

-

-

-

-

-

12/08/2022
monester3300

I am pretty new to the pregnancy app world, but in the past two months I definitely started using a couple for different reasons. BabyCenter has been one of my main ones from the start. From the beginning, it provided so much information and a sense of community. Unfortunately, after 10 weeks pregnancy I had a miscarriage. This where BabyCenter has shined above the rest. It actually has support groups, information and acknowledges loss of pregnancy in sensitive manner. It doesn't just restart and skips over the process like it never happened. Other apps did and instantly switched to focusing on conception versus coping with a loss. They didn't acknowledge my experience at all like BabyCenter. This is the most human and personalized app that I tried and I was extremely impressed by that. If your an expecting mother BabyCenter is a great app to have in your arsenal. It has extensive wealth of information, support groups, has been great for planning but is also conscientious of the unexpected. Pregnancy is a human experience and doesn't not always go as planned. Loss of pregnancy or an infant should not be an unaddressed subject in a pregnancy app just because it's painful. It should be treated with extreme sensitivity, and provide more for those customers that need to feel like they are not alone and address their grief like BabyCenter has. Thank you BabyCenter for acknowledging loss as unfortunate but real part pregnancy and parenthood. You all have helped me greatly 💗🙏

### Fun app but annoying inaccuracies

-

-

-

-

-

12/31/2021
No name nickname 123

It’s nice to have a place to find fairly reliable info quickly, and the due date calculator is helpful, and the weekly updates are fun. I’m currently using the app to track my pregnancy, and a couple things are starting to be annoying. First, there are ads in the mobile app that cannot be cleared but they block part of the descriptions. Not a huge deal, but annoying. Also, the size comparisons are often very strange and inaccurate- at 22 weeks I had a “spaghetti squash “ and now at 23 weeks it’s a “large” mango??? Even in Central America I never saw 11.5 inch mangoes lol. Another time it went from a banana to an avocado 🤷‍♀️ Obviously not a big deal, but it seems like it would be simple to rearrange them into an order that makes more sense. My older kids like the weekly updates and having a relatable comparison, so it’s confusing and annoying when those visuals don’t match up. Also, a simple google search can provide a more accurate diagram of what the baby looks like inside mom’s body. The summary will say the uterus is above the belly button, but the matching visual graphic still has the top of the uterus well below the belly button. I know we all carry differently and all bodies are different, but the 20+ week images all still look like around 10-12 weeks with barely a bump and uterus super low. I only took one star off for these because they really aren’t a big deal, I just don’t know why they spent the time and money to even bother with such inaccurate depictions, and the app would be more fun and useful if these were updated!
