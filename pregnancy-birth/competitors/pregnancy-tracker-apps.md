## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

"Week by week" is free and owned by apps with 4.8–4.9 ratings across hundreds of thousands of users; a paid guide cannot sell weekly baby-size content. What the free apps keep the same for everyone is everything except the due date: pregnancy number, the toddler at home, how she feels, what she has planned for the birth. That is the space a paid personalised guide would have to occupy, and it would be bought alongside these apps, not instead of them.
