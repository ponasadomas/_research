## Comparison with Mamivio — interpretation

Like NCT in the UK, Calmbirth turns the second pregnancy into its own product (refresher), live and educator-run. Two national leaders, one in each of two markets, sell "second pregnancy" as a separate course — evidence the axis matters to buyers, while no self-serve product serves it.
